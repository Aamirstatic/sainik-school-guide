Tip #1

Master NCERT First

80% questions come directly from NCERT textbooks. Read every chapter 3 times — understanding, notes, revision.

Tip #3

Take 15-20 Mock Tests

Toppers take 15-20 full mocks before the exam. Start with 1/month, increase to 2-3/week.

💡 Spend MORE time analyzing mocks than taking them. 1 hour analysis per mock.
