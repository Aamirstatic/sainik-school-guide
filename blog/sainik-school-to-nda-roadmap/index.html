    The journey from Sainik School Class 6 to Indian Armed Forces Officer is a 12-year marathon. It’s the most structured and reliable pathway to a career in the Indian Army, Navy, or Air Force.

    This roadmap gives you a year-by-year plan so your child stays on track from Day 1.

    Year 1 (Age 10-11): Sainik School Admission

    FocusDetails
    GoalClear AISSEE, get admission
    ActionStudy Class 5 syllabus thoroughly
    Key SubjectsMaths, English, GK, Intelligence
    ExamAISSEE (January)
    ResultFebruary–March
    AdmissionApril–May

    Year 2 (Age 11-12): Class 6 — Foundation Year

    FocusDetails
    AcademicsBuild strong CBSE foundation
    PhysicalAdapt to daily PT routine
    NCCJunior Division begins
    SportsTry different sports, find your strength
    Key HabitDaily study routine (2-3 hours self-study)

    FocusDetails
    AcademicsStrengthen Maths and Science
    PhysicalImprove running time, push-ups

    Key HabitReading newspapers for GK

    Year 4 (Age 13-14): Class 8 — NCC ‘A’ Certificate

    FocusDetails
    AcademicsMaintain good grades
    NCCGet ‘A’ Certificate
    PhysicalObstacle training begins
    SportsRepresent school in inter-school competitions
    Key HabitLeadership roles (class monitor, house captain)

    Year 5 (Age 14-15): Class 9 — Serious Preparation Begins

    FocusDetails
    AcademicsClass 9 CBSE — Science, Maths become critical
    NCCSenior Division begins, ‘B’ Certificate
    PhysicalAdvanced PT, swimming
    SportsCompete at cluster/state level
    Key HabitStart reading about NDA, Armed Forces

    Year 6 (Age 15-16): Class 10 — Board Exam Year

    FocusDetails
    AcademicsCBSE Class 10 Board Exam — score well (85%+)
    NCC‘B’ Certificate
    PhysicalMaintain fitness standards
    Stream ChoiceChoose PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) for Class 11
    Key HabitTime management for board exam prep

    Year 7 (Age 16-17): Class 11 — NDA Written Prep Begins

    FocusDetails
    AcademicsClass 11 CBSE — PCM focus
    NDA WrittenStart solving NDA previous year papers
    NCC‘C’ Certificate preparation
    PhysicalMeet NDA physical standards
    Key HabitSolve 1 NDA paper every weekend

    Year 8 (Age 17-18): Class 12 — NDA Exam Year

    FocusDetails
    AcademicsCBSE Class 12 Board Exam — score well
    NDA WrittenAppear for NDA exam (UPSC) — April & September
    NCCGet ‘C’ Certificate (SSB direct entry advantage)
    PhysicalPeak fitness for SSB
    Key HabitSSB interview preparation

    Year 9-11 (Age 18-21): NDA Training — Khadakwasla, Pune

    FocusDetails
    Duration3 years (6 semesters)
    AcademicB.A./B.Sc. degree from JNU
    Military TrainingWeapon training, tactics, leadership
    PhysicalRigorous daily PT and drills
    Stipend₹56,100/month as Lieutenant (after commissioning)

    Year 12 (Age 21-22): IMA/INA/AFA — Final Training

    FocusDetails
    IMA (Army)Indian Military Academy, Dehradun — 1 year
    INA (Navy)Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala — 1 year
    AFA (Air Force)Air Force Academy, Dundigal — 1 year
    OutcomeCommissioned as Lieutenant (Army), Sub-Lieutenant (Navy), or Flying Officer (Air Force)

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    How long is the journey from Sainik School to Officer?

    Approximately 12 years — from Class 6 admission at age 10-11 to commissioned officer at age 22.

    What is the NDA selection rate from Sainik Schools?

    30-40% of NDA cadets come from Sainik Schools. This is 100-150x higher than from regular schools.

    Is Sainik School necessary for NDA?

    No, but it’s the best preparation. NDA accepts candidates from all schools, but Sainik School students have a significantly higher selection rate.

    What if my child doesn’t clear NDA?

    Sainik School prepares students for multiple career paths — engineering, medical, civil services, and corporate careers.
