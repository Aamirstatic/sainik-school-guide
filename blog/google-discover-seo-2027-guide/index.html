    💡 Title Writing Best Practices for Discover

    ✅ Do:

    • Keep title between 40-70 characters
    • Include numbers (odd numbers perform better: 7, 9, 11)
    • Use emotional triggers (shock, surprise, benefit)
    • Add current year (2027 increases relevance)
    • Make it curiosity-provoking
    • Include primary keyword near the beginning

    ❌ Don’t:

    • Use clickbait that misleads
    • Exceed 70 characters (gets truncated)
    • Use all caps (looks spammy)
    • Write vague titles (“Sainik School Guide” — too generic)
    • Forget mobile readability (Discover is mobile-first)

    2. Touch-friendly elements: Minimum 48px tap target size
    3. Readable font size: No zooming required (16px minimum)
    4. Horizontal scrolling: Avoid horizontal scroll on mobile
    5. Fast loading: PageSpeed Score > 85 (Images optimized, caching enabled)
    6. Structured data: JSON-LD for articles (Reviewed by Google Rich Results Test)

    📋 Google Discover Checklist (Free Download)

    ✅ Pre-Publication Checklist

    • Title: 40-70 characters, includes number/hook/year (2027)
    • Featured Image: 1200 × 630 pixels, WebP format, original photo
    • Alt Text: Primary keyword in description
    • Introduction: 2-3 sentences, hooks reader in first 3 seconds
    • Paragraph Length: Max 1-2 sentences on mobile
    • Heading Structure: H1 (title), H2, H3 hierarchy used correctly
    • Tables: Responsive, proper formatting for mobile
    • Bullet Points: Large touch targets, concise
    • Internal Links: 3-5 links to pillar/supporting articles
    • Meta Description: 150-160 characters, includes primary keyword
    • Schema Markup: JSON-LD Article schema validated
    • Page Speed: Images optimized, caching enabled
    • Mobile Test: No horizontal scrolling, readable without zoom

    ✅ Post-Publication Checklist

    • Submit to Google Search Console: “Request Indexing”
    • Share on Social Media: Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp groups
    • Monitor Analytics: Check impressions, CTR after 3, 7, 14 days
    • Engage with Comments: Respond within 24 hours
    • Update Internal Links: Link from 2-3 old articles to new
    • Promote in Newsletter: If you have email subscribers
    • Track Rankings: Monitor position changes after 30 days
    • Refresh Content: Add new data, update statistics after 6 months
