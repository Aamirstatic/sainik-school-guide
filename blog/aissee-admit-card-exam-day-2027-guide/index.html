    📥 How to Download AISSEE 2027 Admit Card

    🖥️ Step-by-Step Admit Card Download Process

    Phase 1: Wait for Release Date

    • Admit card releases: January 2, 2027
    • Exam date: January 19, 2027
    • Gap: 17 days between admit card and exam (adequate time)

    ⚠️ Important:

    • Admit card available only on NTA official portal
    • No physical copies sent by post
    • No email attachments of admit card
    • Download only from exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE

    Phase 2: Login to Download

    1. Visit Official Website:

    • Go to: exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE
    • Look for “Admit Card” tab on homepage (appears after Jan 2)
    • Or directly go to: AISSEE Admit Card Download Page

    2. Login Using:

    OptionDetailsRequired
    Option AApplication Number + Date of BirthMost common
    Option BApplication Number + PasswordIf you remember password
    Option CApplication Number onlyRare, requires recovery

    3. Login Process:

    1. Click “Login” on admit card page
    2. Enter Application Number (15 digits, e.g., 1234000123)
    3. Enter Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY format, as in application)
    4. Click “Submit”
    5. Admit card page opens with candidate details

    Phase 3: Verify & Download

    1. Check Candidate Details:

    • ✅ Name (matches application form)
    • ✅ Date of Birth (matches birth certificate)
    • ✅ Category (General/OBC/SC/ST/Defence)
    • ✅ Class (6 or 9 as applied)
    • ✅ Exam Center (allotted city)
    • ✅ Exam Time (2:00 PM - 4:30 PM)
    • ✅ Reporting Time (1:30 PM)
    • ✅ Photograph (matches uploaded photo)

    2. If Details Are Correct:

    • Click “Download Admit Card”
    • Save PDF to your device
    • Print 2 copies (one for exam, one spare)
    • Take screenshot on phone (backup)

    3. If Details Are WRONG:

    • DO NOT PANIC
    • Note the incorrect details
    • Contact NTA Helpdesk immediately: 011-40759000
    • Correction possible during correction window (if applicable)
    • Mistakes on admit card may cause entry denial

    3. Download Issues? Try:

    • Clear browser cache + cookies
    • Try different browser (Chrome recommended)
    • Try different device (phone/laptop)
    • Use NTA helpdesk number if still not working

    🏫 Exam Center Rules & Regulations

    Entry Rules

    1. Report by 1:30 PM — No entry after 2:00 PM
    2. Gate closes at 2:00 PM sharp — no exceptions
    3. Verify identity — staff checks admit card + ID proof
    4. Photograph verification — your photo on admit card matched with you
    5. Store belongings — All bags deposited at designated area (no responsibility for loss)

    Inside the Exam Hall

    1. Sit at allotted roll number (written on admit card)
    2. Keep admit card on desk (visible to invigilator)
    3. OMR sheet — separate answer sheet, mark carefully
    4. Answer booklet — where you write answers
    5. Blue/Black ballpoint pen only for marking answers
    6. Pencil not allowed (except for rough work if permitted)
    7. No talking — complete silence maintained
    8. Raise hand if you need invigilator (raising hand only method)
    9. Leave hall only with invigilator permission (emergency only)
    10. Submit both booklet + OMR sheet before leaving

    OMR Sheet Marking Rules

    1. Use only blue or black ballpoint pen
    2. Darken the circle completely (no partial marking)
    3. Write Roll Number in the specified area on OMR
    4. Write Test Booklet Code as per instructions
    5. No overwriting or marks outside circle
    6. Unanswered questions — leave blank (no negative marking for unanswered, but -1.25 for wrong)
    7. Check OMR sheet after each section (if permitted)

    Exit Rules

    1. Leave hall only after invigilator says “Time is up”
    2. Collect your belongings from storage area
    3. Sign the attendance sheet before leaving
    4. Leave exam center premises immediately
    5. Discussing paper outside is allowed (but no leaks)
    6. No photo/video of OMR sheet or question paper

    📦 What to Carry: Complete Checklist (Print & Check)

    📋 Day Before Exam (January 18, 2027)

    ✅ Pack This Bag:

    • Admit Card (2 printouts) — A4 size, clear print
    • ID Proof (original + 2 photocopies) — Birth certificate, Aadhaar card, or School ID card
    • 2 Passport-size photos — Same as application (attach one on admit card space if provided)
    • Blue/Black ballpoint pen (2 pens backup) — Gel pens may smudge OMR
    • Transparent water bottle (1 liter max) — No label or remove label
    • Simple analog watch (optional) — No smart features, no calculator
    • Hand sanitizer (small, 50ml) — Permitted in some centers
    • Face mask (optional) — Check current guidelines
    • Pen drive/USB — Not allowed, leave at home

    📍 At Exam Center (Upon Arrival):

    • Locate your roll number on the displayed list
    • Join queue for entry verification
    • Submit bag at designated storage area
    • Keep only pockets items (admit card, pen, ID, handkerchief)
    • Wait for invigilator to guide to seat
    • Verify seat matches your roll number
    • Place admit card on desk (top-left corner)
    • Place ID proof beside admit card
    • Place pen on desk (within reach)

    📍 Exam Center Search & Location

    How to Find Your Exam Center

    1. On Admit Card:

    • Exam City written clearly (e.g., “Mumbai”, “Delhi”, “Bangalore”)
    • Center Name and Address written on admit card
    • Reporting Time (1:30 PM) mentioned

    2. If Admit Card Not Yet Downloaded:

    • Wait for January 2, 2027
    • Or check “Exam City Intimation” (released Dec 2026)
    • Usually same as chosen preference during application

    3. Center Change Requests:

    • Not allowed after application submission
    • Only genuine hardship cases considered (with documentation)
    • Contact NTA: 011-40759000 (10 AM - 5 PM)
    • Email: aissee@nta.ac.in

    🗺️ Exam Center Reachability Tips

    1. Visit Once Before (Optional but Recommended):

    • Go to center a day before (just to see location)
    • Note travel time, transport, parking
    • Plan your Jan 19 route accordingly

    2. If First-Time Visit:

    • Reach 45 mins early (even though gate closes at 2:00 PM)
    • Carry map (Google Maps on phone, saved offline)
    • Ask locals about the exact center location
    • Note landmark near the center (school building, main road)

    3. Transportation Plan:

    • Public transport (bus, train) — check peak hour crowding
    • Private vehicle (car, two-wheeler) — arrange parking spot
    • Drop by parent — ensure they reach by 1:15 PM to drop you
    • Backup transport — have alternative if primary fails

    📝 Admit Card Details Verification Checklist

    ✅ Immediately After Downloading:

    • Candidate Name matches exactly with application form and birth certificate
    • Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) matches birth certificate and application
    • Category (General/OBC/SC/ST/Defence) is correct
    • Class (6 or 9) matches what you applied for
    • Photograph is clear and matches you (recent, same as application)
    • Signature is visible (if shown on admit card)
    • Exam Center Name and Address are correct (city at least)
    • Exam Date is January 19, 2027
    • Exam Time is 2:00 PM - 4:30 PM (Sunday)
    • Reporting Time is 1:30 PM (mandatory)
    • Roll Number assigned (visible on admit card)
    • Test Booklet Code (if applicable, written on OMR sheet)
    • Exam Day Instructions listed (read and follow)

    ⚠️ If Any Detail Is Wrong:

    1. Don’t panic
    2. Note the incorrect detail
    3. Contact NTA Helpdesk: 011-40759000 (10 AM - 5 PM)
    4. Carry proof of correct detail (birth certificate, application receipt)
    5. Try to get it corrected before exam day (easier than on exam day)
    6. On exam day, carry the wrong admit card + proof of correctness; may be allowed with verification
