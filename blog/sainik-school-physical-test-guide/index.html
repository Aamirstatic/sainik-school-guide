    Quick Summary — Sainik School Physical Test at a Glance

    After clearing the AISSEE written exam, students must pass a physical fitness and medical test. This is a qualifying test — you either pass or fail. Your AISSEE score does not matter if you fail the physical test. Every year, students who score well in the written exam are rejected because of physical fitness issues.

    What Is the Sainik School Physical Test?

    The physical test is conducted after the AISSEE written exam results are declared. It is part of the counselling/admission process. The test has two components:

    1. Medical Examination — Physical fitness check by medical officers
    2. Physical Fitness Test — Basic fitness assessment (varies by school)

    Weight Requirements

    Weight must be proportionate to height. There is no fixed weight table — the medical officer assesses whether the child’s weight is within a healthy range for their height and age.

    BMI CategoryStatus
    Underweight (BMI < 18.5)May be flagged
    Normal (BMI 18.5–24.9)✅ Pass
    Overweight (BMI 25–29.9)May be flagged
    Obese (BMI ≥ 30)❌ Likely fail

    Pro Tip: If your child is underweight, increase protein intake (eggs, milk, dal, chicken) and ensure 3 meals + 2 snacks daily. If overweight, reduce sugar/junk food and increase physical activity. Start 6 months before the medical test.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    What is the minimum height for Sainik School Class 6 admission?

    For Class 6 (age 10–12), the minimum height requirement is approximately 125 cm for boys and 123 cm for girls. Height requirements increase with age. These are approximate standards — actual requirements may vary slightly by school. Always check the specific school’s medical standards.

    Can my child wear glasses and get into Sainik School?

    Most Sainik Schools require 6/6 vision without glasses. Students who rely on glasses may not pass the medical test. However, some schools may have slightly relaxed standards. Get your child’s eyes checked by an ophthalmologist at least 6 months before the medical test. If vision correction is possible through exercises or nutrition, start immediately.

    What if my child is overweight for the Sainik School physical test?

    If your child is overweight, start a structured fitness and nutrition plan immediately. Reduce sugar, junk food, and fried items. Increase physical activity — running, sports, and exercises. Ensure a balanced diet with adequate protein and vegetables. Most children can reach a healthy weight within 3–6 months with consistent effort.

    Is there a physical fitness test or just a medical examination?

    Most Sainik Schools conduct a medical examination (health check by doctors). Some schools also conduct a basic physical fitness test (running, push-ups, etc.). The medical examination is mandatory for all schools. The fitness test varies by school — check with the specific school for their requirements.

    What are the common reasons for rejection in Sainik School physical test?

    The most common reasons for rejection are: poor vision (not 6/6 without glasses — 35% of rejections), dental issues (15%), overweight/underweight (15%), flat foot (10%), and knock knees (8%). All of these can be identified and addressed early if you get a comprehensive health check-up done 6 months before the medical test.
