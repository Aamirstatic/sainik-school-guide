AISSEE 2027 Syllabus: Class 6 & Class 9 Guide

    AISSEE 2027 Syllabus: Complete Subject-Wise Guide for Class 6 and Class 9

    📢 Latest Updates — This article is updated regularly with the latest information for 2027.

    DateUpdate
    9 July 2026Article published with complete information for AISSEE 2027 session

    Key Takeaway: The AISSEE 2027 syllabus for Class 6 covers Mathematics, English, General Knowledge, and Intelligence. For Class 9, it covers Mathematics, English, General Science, Social Studies, and Intelligence. The syllabus is largely based on NCERT textbooks. Mathematics carries the highest marks weightage in both classes, making it the most critical subject to prepare thoroughly.

    The All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE) is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 in Sainik Schools across India. Understanding the complete syllabus and exam pattern is the first step toward effective preparation. This guide provides a detailed, subject-wise breakdown of the AISSEE 2027 syllabus for both Class 6 and Class 9.

    AISSEE 2027 Syllabus for Class 6

    Mathematics Syllabus — Class 6

    The Mathematics section is based on the NCERT Class 5 syllabus with some topics from Class 6.

    Number System

    • Natural numbers and whole numbers
    • Place value and face value
    • Comparison and ordering of numbers
    • Rounding off numbers
    • Roman numerals
    • Indian and International number systems

    Arithmetic

    • Four operations: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
    • Fractions: Types (proper, improper, mixed), equivalent fractions, comparison, addition, subtraction
    • Decimals: Place value, comparison, operations
    • HCF and LCM: Finding by prime factorization and division method
    • Ratio and Proportion: Simple ratios, unitary method
    • Percentage: Conversion to/from fractions and decimals, simple percentage problems
    • Profit and Loss: Cost price, selling price, profit/loss percentage
    • Simple Interest: Formula and basic problems
    • Time and Distance: Speed, distance, time calculations
    • Time and Work: Basic problems

    Geometry

    • Basic concepts: Point, line, line segment, ray
    • Angles: Types (acute, obtuse, right, straight, reflex), measurement
    • Triangles: Types (equilateral, isosceles, scalene, right-angled), properties
    • Quadrilaterals: Square, rectangle, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium
    • Circles: Radius, diameter, chord, arc, sector
    • Symmetry: Lines of symmetry in shapes
    • 3D Shapes: Cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere — faces, edges, vertices

    Mensuration

    • Perimeter of square, rectangle, triangle
    • Area of square, rectangle, triangle, circle
    • Concept of volume (basic introduction)

    Algebra (Introduction)

    • Introduction to variables
    • Simple equations
    • Patterns and generalizations

    Data Handling

    • Pictographs
    • Bar graphs
    • Pie charts
    • Mean, median, mode (basic)

    English Syllabus — Class 6

    The English section tests grammar, vocabulary, and reading comprehension.

    Reading Comprehension

    • Passage-based questions (unseen passages)
    • Answering questions based on the passage
    • Vocabulary in context

    Grammar

    • Parts of Speech: Nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions
    • Tenses: Simple present, past, future; present/past continuous
    • Articles: A, an, the — usage rules
    • Subject-Verb Agreement: Basic rules
    • Punctuation: Full stops, commas, question marks, exclamation marks, quotation marks
    • Active and Passive Voice: Basic concepts
    • Direct and Indirect Speech: Basic concepts

    Vocabulary

    • Synonyms and antonyms
    • One-word substitutions
    • Spelling corrections
    • Word meanings in context
    • Idioms and phrases (basic)

    Writing Skills

    • Paragraph writing
    • Letter writing (informal)
    • Story completion
    • Sentence rearrangement

    General Knowledge Syllabus — Class 6

    Indian History

    • Indus Valley Civilization (basic)
    • Important dynasties and rulers (overview)
    • Indian freedom struggle — major events and leaders
    • Important national dates and events

    Geography

    • India: States, capitals, and union territories
    • Major rivers, mountains, and plains of India
    • Indian neighbours
    • Continents and oceans
    • Basic map reading

    Indian Polity

    • National symbols (flag, anthem, emblem, song)
    • Fundamental duties
    • Important constitutional bodies (basic awareness)
    • Indian Presidents and Prime Ministers (recent)

    General Science

    • Human body — major organs and their functions
    • Plants — types, parts, photosynthesis (basic)
    • Animals — classification (basic)
    • Solar system — planets, sun, moon
    • Basic physics concepts — light, sound, electricity (introductory)
    • States of matter — solid, liquid, gas

    Current Affairs

    • Sports events and achievements
    • Awards and honors (national and international)
    • Important government schemes
    • Recent scientific achievements
    • Defence-related news

    Miscellaneous

    • Famous personalities
    • Books and authors
    • Inventions and discoveries
    • Abbreviations

    Intelligence / Mental Ability Syllabus — Class 6

    Verbal Reasoning

    • Analogies (word-based)
    • Classification (odd one out)
    • Series completion (alphabet and number)
    • Coding-decoding
    • Blood relations
    • Direction sense
    • Ranking and ordering

    Non-Verbal Reasoning

    • Pattern recognition in figures
    • Mirror images
    • Water images
    • Paper folding and cutting
    • Embedded figures
    • Figure completion
    • Similar and dissimilar figures

    AISSEE 2027 Syllabus for Class 9

    Mathematics Syllabus — Class 9

    The Mathematics section is based on the NCERT Class 8 syllabus.

    Number System

    • Rational and irrational numbers
    • Real numbers
    • Exponents and powers
    • Square roots and cube roots
    • Playing with numbers (divisibility rules)

    Algebra

    • Linear Equations: In one variable, word problems
    • Polynomials: Types, degree, addition, subtraction, multiplication
    • Factorization: Common factors, algebraic identities
    • Algebraic Identities: (a+b)², (a-b)², (a+b)(a-b), (a+b)³, (a-b)³
    • Exponents and Powers: Laws of exponents

    Geometry

    • Lines and Angles: Types of angles, complementary, supplementary, vertically opposite, parallel lines and transversal
    • Triangles: Congruence criteria (SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS), properties, inequality
    • Quadrilaterals: Properties of parallelogram, rectangle, square, rhombus, trapezium
    • Circles: Theorems related to chords, angles, tangents
    • Mensuration: Surface area and volume of cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere

    Ratio, Proportion, and Percentage

    • Direct and inverse proportion
    • Percentage — increase, decrease, profit/loss, discount
    • Compound interest (introduction)

    Statistics and Probability

    • Mean, median, mode
    • Bar graphs, histograms, pie charts
    • Basic probability — experimental and theoretical

    Coordinate Geometry (Basic)

    • Cartesian plane
    • Plotting points
    • Distance between points (on same axis)

    English Syllabus — Class 9

    Reading Comprehension

    • Unseen passages — factual, descriptive, literary
    • Inference-based questions
    • Vocabulary-based questions

    Grammar

    • Tenses: All 12 tenses with usage
    • Voice: Active and passive voice
    • Speech: Direct and indirect speech
    • Conditionals: Zero, first, second conditionals
    • Modals: Can, could, may, might, must, should, would, shall
    • Subject-Verb Agreement: Advanced rules
    • Determiners: Articles, quantifiers, demonstratives
    • Relative Clauses: Defining and non-defining
    • Reported Speech: Reporting verbs

    Vocabulary

    • Synonyms and antonyms
    • One-word substitutions
    • Idioms and phrases
    • Homophones and homonyms
    • Confusing words
    • Word formation (prefixes, suffixes)

    Writing Skills

    • Essay writing
    • Letter writing (formal and informal)
    • Notice writing
    • Message writing
    • Paragraph writing
    • Story writing

    General Science Syllabus — Class 9

    Physics

    • Motion: Distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration
    • Force and Laws of Motion: Newton’s laws (basic), friction
    • Gravitation: Free fall, weight, mass
    • Work and Energy: Types of energy, conservation of energy
    • Sound: Production, propagation, reflection, echo
    • Light: Reflection, refraction, lenses, dispersion

    Chemistry

    • Matter in Our Surroundings: States of matter, change of state, evaporation
    • Is Matter Around Us Pure: Mixtures, compounds, solutions, suspensions
    • Atoms and Molecules: Atomic mass, molecular mass, mole concept
    • Structure of the Atom: Electrons, protons, neutrons, atomic number, mass number
    • The Fundamental Unit of Life: Cell structure and function

    Biology

    • Tissues: Plant and animal tissues
    • Diversity in Living Organisms: Classification hierarchy
    • Why Do We Fall Ill: Diseases, health, and hygiene
    • Natural Resources: Air, water, soil — conservation
    • Food Resources: Crop production, food preservation

    How to Use This Syllabus for Preparation

    Step 1: Cross-Reference with NCERT

    The AISSEE syllabus aligns closely with NCERT textbooks. For each topic listed above:

    1. Read the corresponding NCERT chapter thoroughly
    2. Solve all NCERT exercises and examples
    3. Make notes of key formulas and concepts

    Step 2: Identify High-Weightage Topics

    Focus more preparation time on high-weightage topics:

    • Class 6: Arithmetic, Number System, and Geometry
    • Class 9: Algebra and Geometry

    Step 3: Create a Topic-Wise Study Plan

    Allocate study time based on weightage:

    • High-weightage topics: 40% of study time
    • Medium-weightage topics: 35% of study time
    • Low-weightage topics: 25% of study time

    Step 4: Practice with Previous Year Papers

    After completing each topic, solve previous year AISSEE questions from that topic. This helps you:

    • Understand the question format
    • Gauge the difficulty level
    • Identify commonly asked questions

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Is the AISSEE syllabus based on NCERT?

    Yes, the AISSEE syllabus is largely based on the NCERT curriculum. For Class 6 admission, the syllabus covers topics from NCERT Class 5 (and some introductory Class 6 topics). For Class 9 admission, the syllabus is based on NCERT Class 8 content. NTA designs the AISSEE question paper keeping NCERT textbooks as the primary reference, which is why NCERT books are considered the most important study material for AISSEE preparation.

    What is the difference between AISSEE Class 6 and Class 9 syllabus?

    The main differences are: (1) Class 6 has General Knowledge as a subject, while Class 9 has General Science and Social Studies as separate subjects. (2) Class 6 Mathematics carries 150 marks (50% weightage), while Class 9 Mathematics carries 200 marks (50% weightage). (3) The complexity level of questions is higher for Class 9. (4) Class 9 requires 3 hours for the exam while Class 6 requires 2.5 hours. (5) The Intelligence section difficulty increases from Class 6 to Class 9.

    Is there negative marking in AISSEE 2027?

    No, there is no negative marking in AISSEE. Each correct answer earns full marks, and unanswered or wrong answers do not result in any deduction. This means students should attempt all questions, even if they need to make an educated guess. However, random guessing without any elimination is not recommended as it wastes time.

    How many questions are there in AISSEE 2027?

    For Class 6, AISSEE has 125 questions carrying a total of 300 marks. For Class 9, there are 150 questions carrying a total of 400 marks. All questions are multiple-choice questions (MCQs) with four options each, and students need to mark their answers on an OMR sheet.

    What level of questions are asked in AISSEE Mathematics?

    AISSEE Mathematics questions are generally of NCERT level with moderate difficulty. For Class 6, questions are based on Class 5 NCERT Mathematics with some application-based problems. For Class 9, questions are based on Class 8 NCERT Mathematics. While most questions are straightforward, 15–20% questions may require multi-step problem solving or application of concepts in unfamiliar contexts. Speed and accuracy are crucial given the time constraints.
