    Why 5 Months Is Enough (If You Start TODAY)

    The AISSEE 2027 exam is expected in January 2027. That gives you approximately 5 months from today (August 2, 2026). Here is the truth: 5 months is more than enough to crack AISSEE — IF you follow a structured plan.

    Every year, thousands of students crack AISSEE with just 3–4 months of focused preparation. The key is not how many hours you study, but how strategically you use your time. A student who studies 2 hours daily with a clear plan will outperform a student who studies 6 hours randomly.

    This article gives you the exact month-by-month, week-by-week plan that AISSEE toppers follow. Print it out. Stick it on your wall. Follow it religiously.

    Month 1: August 2026 — Build the Foundation

    Goal: Complete NCERT basics for all subjects

    August is your foundation month. Do not jump into solving papers yet. First, make sure your basics are rock solid.

    For Class 6 Aspirants:

    WeekSubjectTopics to Cover
    Week 1MathematicsNumber System, Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division
    Week 2MathematicsFractions, Decimals, Factors & Multiples, LCM & HCF
    Week 3MathematicsGeometry (Lines, Angles, Triangles, Circles), Mensuration
    Week 4EVS/GKHuman Body, Plants, Animals, Environment, Indian Geography

    For Class 9 Aspirants:

    WeekSubjectTopics to Cover
    Week 1MathematicsNumber Systems, Polynomials, Coordinate Geometry
    Week 2MathematicsLinear Equations, Quadrilaterals, Triangles
    Week 3MathematicsMensuration (Surface Area, Volume), Statistics, Probability
    Week 4SciencePhysics (Motion, Force), Chemistry (Atoms, Elements), Biology

    Daily Timetable for August:

    TimeActivityDuration
    4:00 PMSchool homework1 hour
    5:30 PMAISSEE Math practice45 min
    6:30 PMAISSEE GK/Science30 min
    8:00 PMReasoning puzzles30 min
    Total2.75 hours

    August Checklist:

    • Complete NCERT Class 5 Math (Class 6 aspirants)
    • Complete NCERT Class 8 Math (Class 9 aspirants)
    • Read one GK book chapter daily
    • Solve 10 reasoning questions daily
    • Maintain a notebook for formulas and important facts

    Month 2: September 2026 — Start Practice Papers

    Goal: Solve previous year papers and identify weak areas

    September is when you shift from learning to practicing. Start solving AISSEE previous year papers (2021–2026) under timed conditions.

    Weekly Plan:

    WeekActivityPapers to Solve
    Week 1Solve AISSEE 2026 paper (Class 6/9)1 paper
    Week 2Solve AISSEE 2025 paper + analyze mistakes1 paper
    Week 3Solve AISSEE 2024 paper + weak topic revision1 paper
    Week 4Solve AISSEE 2023 paper + mock test1 paper

    How to Analyze a Paper:

    After solving each paper, do this:

    1. Count correct answers per subject
    2. Identify weak subjects (lowest score)
    3. Identify weak topics within each subject
    4. Make a list of formulas/concepts you forgot
    5. Re-solve wrong questions without looking at answers

    Daily Timetable for September:

    TimeActivityDuration
    4:00 PMSchool homework1 hour
    5:30 PMAISSEE paper solving (timed)1.5 hours
    7:30 PMAnalysis + weak topic revision45 min
    9:00 PMReasoning practice30 min
    Total3.75 hours

    Month 3: October 2026 — Application Form + Targeted Practice

    Goal: Fill application form correctly + strengthen weak areas

    October is critical because the AISSEE application form is expected to open this month. Simultaneously, you need to focus on your weak areas identified in September.

    Application Form Tasks (Week 1–2):

    • Gather all documents (birth certificate, domicile, category cert, photo)
    • Fill form carefully — double-check all entries
    • Upload photo and signature as per specifications
    • Pay fee and download confirmation

    Targeted Practice (Week 2–4):

    Weak AreaPractice Strategy
    MathematicsSolve 30 problems daily from weak topics
    GK/ScienceRead NCERT + Lucent GK daily (30 min)
    EnglishGrammar exercises + reading comprehension
    ReasoningPractice from R.S. Aggarwal Reasoning book

    Daily Timetable for October:

    TimeActivityDuration
    4:00 PMSchool homework1 hour
    5:30 PMWeak topic practice1.5 hours
    7:30 PMGK/Science reading45 min
    9:00 PMReasoning + English30 min
    Total3.75 hours

    Month 4: November 2026 — Mock Test Season

    Goal: Take 8–10 full-length mock tests and analyze each one

    November is mock test month. This is where you simulate real exam conditions and build your test-taking strategy.

    Mock Test Schedule:

    WeekMock TestsFocus
    Week 1Mock 1, 2Time management, question selection
    Week 2Mock 3, 4Accuracy improvement, skip strategy
    Week 3Mock 5, 6Speed improvement, weak area final push
    Week 4Mock 7, 8Final tuning, confidence building

    Mock Test Strategy:

    1. Take the test in one sitting — no breaks, no phone
    2. Time yourself strictly — 2.5 hours for Class 6, 3 hours for Class 9
    3. Attempt ALL questions — no negative marking in AISSEE
    4. Analyze immediately after — spend 1 hour reviewing each mock
    5. Track your score — aim for 5–10% improvement each week

    Target Scores:

    ClassSafe ScoreCompetitive ScoreTop Score
    Class 6180/300220/300260+/300
    Class 9240/400300/400350+/400

    Daily Timetable for November:

    TimeActivityDuration
    4:00 PMSchool homework1 hour
    5:00 PMMock test (alternate days)2.5 hours
    8:00 PMMock analysis + revision1 hour
    9:30 PMLight reading (GK)30 min
    Total4–5 hours

    Month 5: December 2026 — Final Revision

    Goal: Revise everything, no new topics, build confidence

    December is revision month. Do NOT start any new topics. Focus only on:

    1. Revising formulas and important concepts
    2. Solving your notebook of mistakes from previous mocks
    3. Taking 2–3 final mock tests
    4. Staying calm and confident

    Revision Strategy:

    WeekFocusActivity
    Week 1MathematicsRevise all formulas, solve 50 mixed problems
    Week 2GK + ScienceRevise notes, read current affairs
    Week 3English + ReasoningGrammar rules, reasoning patterns
    Week 4Full revision + Light mocks2 mock tests, casual revision

    What NOT to Do in December:

    • ❌ Do not start new topics
    • ❌ Do not panic if your score is not perfect
    • ❌ Do not change your strategy at the last minute
    • ❌ Do not skip sleep or meals
    • ❌ Do not compare your preparation with others

    Daily Timetable for December:

    TimeActivityDuration
    4:00 PMSchool homework1 hour
    5:30 PMRevision (formulas, notes)1.5 hours
    7:30 PMLight mock or topic practice1.5 hours
    9:30 PMRelaxation + early sleep—
    Total4 hours

    Exam Month: January 2027 — Stay Calm, Execute

    Last Week Before Exam:

    DayActivity
    7 days beforeLight revision only, no new topics
    5 days beforeRevise formulas, read GK notes casually
    3 days beforeRest, eat well, sleep early
    1 day beforeCheck admit card, prepare exam kit, relax
    Exam DayWake up early, eat breakfast, reach centre 1 hour early

    Exam Day Kit:

    • Admit card (printed)
    • Aadhaar card / Photo ID
    • 2 black ballpoint pens
    • 2 pencils + eraser + sharpener
    • Water bottle (transparent)
    • Watch (no smartwatch)

    Subject-Wise Tips for the Final 5 Months

    Mathematics (Highest Weightage — 150/300 for Class 6)

    High-Weightage Topics:

    • Number System & Arithmetic (30% questions)
    • Geometry (20% questions)
    • Mensuration (15% questions)
    • Fractions & Decimals (15% questions)
    • Data Handling (10% questions)

    Pro Tip: Learn shortcut methods for calculations. In AISSEE, speed matters as much as accuracy.

    General Knowledge (50/300 for Class 6)

    Focus Areas:

    • Indian History (Freedom Movement)
    • Indian Geography (Rivers, Mountains, States)
    • Indian Constitution (Fundamental Rights)
    • Current Affairs (Last 6 months)
    • Science basics (Human Body, Plants, Animals)

    Pro Tip: Read Lucent GK + current affairs magazine monthly.

    English (50/300 for Class 6)

    Focus Areas:

    • Grammar (Tenses, Articles, Prepositions)
    • Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word substitutions)
    • Reading Comprehension
    • Sentence Correction

    Pro Tip: Read a newspaper editorial daily. It improves both English and GK.

    Intelligence/Reasoning (50/300 for Class 6)

    Focus Areas:

    • Analogy (Word, Number, Letter)
    • Classification (Odd one out)
    • Series (Number, Alphabet, Mixed)
    • Coding-Decoding
    • Direction Sense
    • Mirror & Water Images

    Pro Tip: Practice 20 reasoning questions daily. It is the easiest section to score full marks.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    Is 5 months enough to crack AISSEE?

    Yes. 5 months of focused, strategic preparation is sufficient for most students. The key is consistency — studying 2–3 hours daily is better than cramming 10 hours on weekends.

    Should I join coaching or self-study?

    Both work. Coaching provides structure and expert guidance. Self-study works if you are disciplined and follow a plan like this one. Many toppers are self-studied.

    How many hours should I study daily for AISSEE?

    2–3 hours in August–September, 3–4 hours in October–November, and 4–5 hours in December. Do not exceed 5 hours — burnout is real.

    What is the best book for AISSEE preparation?

    NCERT textbooks are the foundation. For practice, use Arihant’s AISSEE guide, R.S. Aggarwal for Reasoning, and Lucent for GK. See our Best Books guide.

    Can I crack AISSEE without coaching?

    Absolutely. Many top rankers prepare through self-study. Follow this 5-month plan, solve previous year papers, and take regular mock tests. Self-discipline is the key.
