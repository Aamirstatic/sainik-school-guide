    Why Document Verification Is the Most Critical Step in AISSEE Admission

    Every year, thousands of AISSEE applicants are rejected not because of merit, but because of incomplete or incorrect documentation. The Sainik School Society (SSS) and NTA follow a strict verification process, and missing a single document can delay or disqualify your application.

    Common document-related rejection reasons:

    • Age proof mismatch with birth certificate
    • Caste certificate expired or issued by wrong authority
    • Bonafide certificate not from current school
    • Photographs not meeting specifications (size, background, pose)
    • Income certificate not valid for the financial year

    This guide ensures you submit complete, valid, and properly formatted documents in your first attempt.

    ✅ Accepted Age Proof Documents

    Hospital Birth RecordGovernment hospital✅ SupportingCross-verification only

    ❌ Not Accepted as Primary Proof

    DocumentReason
    Affidavit on stamp paperNot sufficient alone
    Ration cardNot accepted as age proof
    Mark sheet/grade cardDoes not show DOB
    PAN cardNot accepted for age verification

    🔑 Birth Certificate Rules

    • Must be issued by Municipal Corporation, Taluk office, or village panchayat
    • Must show exact date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format
    • Must be in English or Hindi (translated copy if regional language)
    • Must match other documents (Aadhaar, school records)
    • Original required — photocopies attested by gazetted officer accepted

    📅 Age Calculation Reference Date

    • Cutoff date: March 31, 2027
    • Age calculated as on this date
    • If child turns 10/13 on March 30, 2027 → ✅ Eligible
    • If child turns 10/13 on April 1, 2027 → ❌ Not eligible

    SC✅ Yes (if seeking reservation)15% seat reservation
    ST✅ Yes (if seeking reservation)7.5% seat reservation

    📄 Caste Certificate Guidelines

    RequirementSpecification
    Issuing AuthorityDistrict Magistrate, Commissioner, or Sub-Divisional Officer
    ValidityMust be valid as of AISSEE application date
    FormatCentral or state government format
    Must includeCaste name, family details, issuing authority, date
    For OBCMust have creamy layer certificate if applicable

    🚫 Common Caste Certificate Rejection Reasons

    • Certificate expired (valid for 1 year in some states)
    • Issued by unauthorized authority
    • Name mismatch with application form
    • Missing family income details (for OBC)
    • Photocopy of original (original must be produced at verification)

    💡 OBC Creamy Layer Rules

    • If parents’ income > ₹8 lakh/year → Creamy layer certificate required
    • If parents’ income ≤ ₹8 lakh/year → Non-creamy layer certificate
    • Creamy layer candidates do not get OBC reservation
    • Certificate must be issued within current financial year

    Photographs: Specifications & Guidelines

    📸 Photograph Requirements

    SpecificationRequirement
    Size3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (passport size)
    BackgroundWhite or light blue background
    PoseFull face, front view, neutral expression
    ClothingSchool uniform or formal attire
    No glassesSpectacles not allowed (if worn normally)
    No hat/CapReligious headwear permitted
    File formatJPG/JPEG (if online submission)
    File size20KB – 50KB (if uploading online)
    Print size2.5 cm × 3.5 cm on application form

    📷 Common Photography Mistakes to Avoid

    • ❌ Selfies or informal photos
    • ❌ Wearing sunglasses or dark glasses
    • ❌ Photo taken against colorful background
    • ❌ Photo edited or filtered
    • ❌ Group photos or photos with others
    • ❌ Wearing mask or covering face
    • ❌ Digital morphed or edited photos

    📋 Photograph Submission Checklist

    • 6 identical photographs
    • White background, clear face visibility
    • Taken within last 3 months
    • Name and class written lightly on back (if physical)
    • Digital copy meets size specifications
    • Spare photographs kept for interview/medical

    • Vision meets minimum standards (6/6 or 6/9 with correction)
    • Hearing within normal range
    • Physical growth appropriate for age
    • Any disability declared (if applicable)

    8. General physical examination

    FAQs: Sainik School Documents 2027

    Q1: What if my birth certificate is in a regional language?

    A: Submit the original + English translation attested by a certified translator. The translation must include all details from the original.

    Q2: Can I submit photocopies of documents initially?

    A: Yes, scanned copies for online application. Originals must be produced at document verification and admission.

    Q3: What if I don’t have a birth certificate?

    A: Apply for a late birth registration at municipal corporation immediately. Alternatively, hospital discharge certificate + Aadhaar may be accepted with SSS permission.

    Q4: How many photographs are required?

    A: 6 photographs for the application form + 2 spare photographs for interview/medical. Total: 8 photos recommended.

    Q5: Can I get age relaxation for defense category?

    A: No. Age limits are same for all categories. However, 25% seats are reserved for defence wards within the same age limit.

    Q6: What if my caste certificate is from a different state?

    A: Caste certificate must be from the state of residence or central government format. Out-of-state certificates may be accepted with additional verification.

    Q6: Is income certificate mandatory for general category?

    A: Not mandatory for admission, but required if applying for fee concession or EWS quota. General category without fee concession does not need income certificate.

    Q8: Can documents be submitted after the deadline?

    A: Generally no. Late submissions are not accepted. However, genuine medical emergencies may be considered with permission from SSS — contact the admission cell.

    Q9: What documents are needed for girl candidate admission?

    A: Same as boys plus:

    • Proof of gender (if required)
    • 25% seat reservation claim (if applicable)
    • No age relaxation for girls

    Q10: Where can I verify my documents before submission?

    A:

    • Use our Document Checklist Tool
    • Contact Sainik School Society helpdesk
    • Consult educational consultant specialized in AISSEE
    • Visit concerned Sainik School for pre-admission guidance
