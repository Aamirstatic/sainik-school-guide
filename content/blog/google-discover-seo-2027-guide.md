### 💡 Title Writing Best Practices for Discover

✅ **Do:**
- Keep title **between 40-70 characters**
- Include **numbers** (odd numbers perform better: 7, 9, 11)
- Use **emotional triggers** (shock, surprise, benefit)
- Add **current year** (2027 increases relevance)
- Make it **curiosity-provoking**
- Include **primary keyword** near the beginning

❌ **Don't:**
- Use **clickbait that misleads**
- Exceed **70 characters** (gets truncated)
- Use **all caps** (looks spammy)
- Write **vague titles** ("Sainik School Guide" — too generic)
- Forget **mobile readability** (Discover is mobile-first)

## 📱 Mobile-First Formatting: Discover Reads on Phones

80% of Google Discover traffic comes from **mobile devices**. Your content must be mobile-friendly.

### 📐 Mobile Formatting Best Practices

| Element | Desktop | Mobile/Discover | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Paragraph Length** | 2-3 sentences | **1-2 sentences** | Easier on small screens |
| **Line Length** | 60-70 characters | **45-50 characters** | Better readability |
| **Font Size** | 16px minimum | **16px minimum** | Prevents zooming |
| **Headings** | H2, H3 hierarchy | **Clear H2, H3 structure** | Scannable content |
| **Tables** | Standard formatting | **Responsive tables** | Horizontal scroll on mobile |
| **Images** | Full width | **Max-width: 100%** | Fits any screen |
| **Bullet Points** | Standard | **Large touch targets** | Easier thumb scrolling |


### ⚡ Technical Mobile Requirements

1. **Viewport meta tag**: `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">`
2. **Touch-friendly elements**: Minimum 48px tap target size
3. **Readable font size**: No zooming required (16px minimum)
4. **Horizontal scrolling**: Avoid horizontal scroll on mobile
5. **Fast loading**: PageSpeed Score > 85 (Images optimized, caching enabled)
6. **Structured data**: JSON-LD for articles (Reviewed by Google Rich Results Test)

## 🎯 Google Discover vs SEO: Key Differences

| Aspect | Google Discover | Traditional SEO |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Traffic Source** | Feed-based (passive) | Search-based (active) |
| **User Intent** | "I didn't know I needed this" | "I'm looking for this specifically" |
| **CTR Benchmark** | 5-10% (varies widely) | 2-5% (position-dependent) |
| **Content Type** | Evergreen + trending mix | Keyword-focused |
| **Update Frequency** | Regular updates help | Evergreen can rank for years |
| **Mobile Requirement** | Critical (80% traffic) | Important but not 100% |
| **Image Importance** | **Critical** (main driver) | Important but secondary |
| **Time to Traffic** | 1-4 weeks | 2-6 months |
| **Best For** | Brand awareness, massive reach | Specific query capture |

### 💡 Hybrid Strategy: Combine Both

**Best approach for Sainik School websites:**

1. **60% Discover-optimized content** (evergreen guides, list articles, visual content)
2. **40% SEO-optimized content** (specific keyword targets, application-focused posts)
3. **Cross-link both types** (SEO articles link to Discover articles and vice versa)
4. **Monitor both traffic sources** in Google Analytics 4
