## Why Age Limit Is the #1 Reason for Application Rejection

Every year, **thousands of AISSEE applications are rejected** because the candidate's age does not meet the eligibility criteria. Parents often miscalculate the age, use the wrong cutoff date, or assume there is age relaxation for their category.

**There is NO age relaxation for any category** — not for SC, not for ST, not for OBC, not for Defence wards. The age limits are **strict and non-negotiable**.

This article explains the age criteria in complete detail with real examples, so you never have to doubt your child's eligibility.

## How Age Is Calculated: Step-by-Step with Examples

Your child's age on **March 31, 2027** must be between the minimum and maximum age specified above.

**Age = March 31, 2027 − Date of Birth**

## Date of Birth Cutoff: The Critical Detail Most Parents Miss

The cutoff date is **March 31, 2027** — NOT the exam date (January 2027) and NOT the application date (October–November 2026).

**This means:**
- If your child turns 10 on **March 30, 2027** → ✅ Eligible
- If your child turns 10 on **April 1, 2027** → ❌ Not Eligible (even though it is just 1 day difference)

**Why March 31?** This is the standard academic year cutoff used by CBSE and most Indian education boards. The academic session starts in April, so March 31 is the reference date.

### Common Mistake: Using January as Cutoff

Many parents calculate age as of the exam date (January 2027). This is **wrong**. The official cutoff is **March 31, 2027**. If your child is 10 years old in January but will turn 12 before March 31, they might be over-age.

## Category-Wise Age Relaxation: The Truth

### Is there age relaxation for SC/ST candidates?

**No.** The age limits are the **same for all categories** — General, OBC, SC, ST, Defence, and any other category. There is no relaxation whatsoever.

### Is there age relaxation for Defence wards?

**No.** Defence wards get reservation in **seat allocation** (a certain percentage of seats are reserved for defence category), but the **age limits remain the same**.

### Is there age relaxation for girls?

**No.** Girls have the same age limits as boys. However, **25% of seats** in Class 6 are reserved for girl candidates.

### Summary Table

| Category | Age Limit | Relaxation | Seats Reserved |
|----------|-----------|------------|---------------|
| General | 10–12 (Class 6), 13–15 (Class 9) | None | Open merit |
| OBC | Same as General | None | 27% seats |
| SC | Same as General | None | 15% seats |
| ST | Same as General | None | 7.5% seats |
| Defence | Same as General | None | 25% seats |
| Girls | Same as General | None | 25% seats (Class 6) |

## What If My Child Is Just Outside the Age Limit?

### Scenario 1: Child is 1 month too young

Unfortunately, there is **no provision** for age relaxation. If your child does not meet the minimum age on March 31, 2027, they are not eligible. You will need to wait for AISSEE 2028.

### Scenario 2: Child is 1 month too old

Same rule applies. If your child exceeds the maximum age on March 31, 2027, they cannot apply. Consider other options like:
- Rashtriya Military School (different age limits)
- RIMC (Rashtriya Indian Military College)
- Navodaya Vidyalaya (different age limits)

### Scenario 3: Child is studying in a different class

The class requirement is based on what the child is **currently studying**, not what they have completed. For Class 6 admission, the child must be studying in Class 5 or equivalent at the time of application.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the age limit for Sainik School Class 6 in 2027?
The child must be between **10 and 12 years old** as on March 31, 2027. This means eligible date of birth is between April 1, 2015 and March 31, 2017.

### What is the age limit for Sainik School Class 9 in 2027?
The child must be between **13 and 15 years old** as on March 31, 2027. Eligible date of birth is between April 1, 2012 and March 31, 2014.

### Is there any age relaxation for SC/ST students?
No. The age limits are the same for all categories — General, OBC, SC, ST, Defence, and all other categories.

### My child is 9 years and 11 months old. Is he eligible?
No. The child must be at least **10 years old on March 31, 2027**. If they turn 10 after March 31, 2027, they are not eligible for AISSEE 2027 and must wait for AISSEE 2028.

### Can a student apply for both Class 6 and Class 9?
No. A student can apply for only **one class** per year. Choose the class based on the child's current class and age eligibility.

### What if the date of birth on the birth certificate is different from the school record?
The **birth certificate date** is considered final and authoritative. If there is a discrepancy, get it corrected in the school records before applying.

### Is there a separate age limit for girls?
No. Girls have the **same age limits** as boys. However, 25% of Class 6 seats are reserved for girl candidates.
