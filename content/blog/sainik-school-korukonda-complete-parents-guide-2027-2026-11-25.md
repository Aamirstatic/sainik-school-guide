## A palace that became a school

If you are filling the AISSEE 2027 form this week and your home state is Andhra Pradesh, start here: **Sainik School Korukonda** is the oldest Sainik School in the state, and one of the first in the country. It was formally founded on **18 January 1962** — in a building older than the school itself by half a century.

The school runs from the **Alak Appala Kondayamba Vijayaram Palace**, built in **1911** by Maharaja Vijayaram Gajapathi Raju as his country residence. In September 1961, the palace — along with 206 acres of land around it — was gifted for use as a school by the Vizianagaram royal family, at the initiative of Dr P. V. G. Raju, the Raja Saheb of Vizianagaram.

Korukonda sits about **12 km from Vizianagaram** town, in Andhra Pradesh's north coastal region. Our [Andhra Pradesh guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-andhra-pradesh-complete-guide-2027/) covers the state's second school, Kalikiri, and the full state picture.

## Seats for 2027-28: 90 in Class 6, 36 in Class 9

Here is the number that decides your child's odds. From Appendix VI-A and VI-A1 of the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin:

|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 6 — boys** | 55 | 25 | 80 |
| **Class 6 — girls** | 5 | 5 | 10 |
| **Class 6 — total** | — | — | **90** |
| **Class 9 — boys** | 23 | 11 | 34 |
| **Class 9 — girls** | — | — | 2 |
| **Class 9 — total** | — | — | **36** |

Three things jump out. First, Korukonda is one of the bigger intakes in the country — 90 Class 6 seats puts it near the top of the 33 schools, and its 36 Class 9 seats are the seventh-largest Class 9 intake anywhere. Second, the **67% home-state quota** means an Andhra Pradesh-domiciled child competes for 60 of the 90 Class 6 seats, while an out-of-state child chases 30 — our [home-state quota explainer](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-67-percent-explained-2026-10-17/) works through the arithmetic. Third, Korukonda is one of the few schools with **Class 9 seats for girls** — just 2, but 12 of the 33 schools have none at all. Our [girls' seats explainer](/news/aissee-2027-7-days-left-girls-351-class-6-seats-2026-11-25/) lists all 351 Class 6 and 69 Class 9 girls' seats.

The seat figures are tentative — the bulletin says so itself — but they are the numbers NTA published, and they are the ones to plan around.

## Admission: only through AISSEE, and the clock is ticking

There is no management quota, no donation route and no direct admission to Korukonda. Entry is to **Class 6 and Class 9 only**, through AISSEE, conducted by NTA.

For 2027-28, the form is open at **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** until **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**. The fee is Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and Rs 700 for SC and ST. A late window runs 3–6 December with an extra Rs 500 — but treat that as an emergency exit, not a plan.

The age rules are the standard AISSEE ones: **10–12 years as on 31 March 2027 for Class 6** (born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017, studying in Class 5) and **13–15 years for Class 9** (born between 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014, studying in Class 8). The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. There is no negative marking, and the qualifying bar is 25% in each section with 40% aggregate — our [worked examples of the 25% rule](/blog/aissee-2027-qualifying-marks-25-percent-rule-worked-examples/) show exactly what that means for your child's target score.

One Korukonda-specific note: the Class 6 paper is offered in **Telugu** as well as English and Hindi, among 13 language options. If your child is stronger in Telugu, choose it on the form — the paper is the same, only the language changes.

## Nine houses, and a campus built for the whole day

Korukonda started with four houses in 1962. Today it has **nine — Pallava, Kakatiya, Maurya, Pandiya, Gajapathi, Chalukya, Moghul and Gupta for boys, and Satavahana for girl cadets**. Each house holds 72 cadets, with study rooms, Wi-Fi, computers, a television and recreation facilities, and the House Master lives in the house block. The house names are a quiet history lesson in themselves: dynasties that ruled the Deccan, from the Pallavas to the Gajapathis whose palace the school occupies.

The campus is built for a cadet's whole day, not just the classroom. There is a **50-acre sports ground**, a swimming pool, tennis and squash courts, an air rifle shooting range, a 650-seat auditorium, and a **12-bed hospital** with a resident doctor, a male and a female nursing assistant, and an ambulance. The cadets' mess is called **Almeida Hall**, after the first principal. The administrative wing still sits in the Maharaja's palace.

## Fees and cutoffs: what is reported, and what is not verified

This is the section where honesty costs us a clean number — and that is the point. No verified 2027 fee figure for Korukonda is available from the school or the NTA bulletin. One coaching portal reports tuition at the older Sainik Schools at **Rs 1,28,615 a year for 2026-27**. Treat that as a reported figure, not a bill: the all-in cost — fee plus uniform, books, travel and extras — is always higher than the headline tuition figure, and scholarships exist for defence families and SC/ST cadets. Confirm the current fee from the school before you budget.

On cutoffs: NTA does not publish school-wise cutoffs in a single list, and the "expected cutoffs" floating around coaching sites are estimates built from last year's lists, not announcements. We will not quote one as fact. The sound advice: **aim for 280 or more out of 300 in Class 6**, and treat the 25%-per-section qualifying bar as a floor, not a target.

## Is Korukonda the right first choice for your child?

Ask yourself three questions before you put it at the top of your preference list:

1. **Is your child genuinely drawn to the armed forces?** Korukonda's whole system — dawn PT, NDA coaching, nine-house rivalry, a 50-acre sports ground — is built for one destination. A child who wants it thrives here.
2. **Can your family handle the distance and the boarding?** Korukonda is 12 km from Vizianagaram and a long journey from most of India, and term breaks become the rhythm of family life for six years. If you can, visit the campus before you fill the preference list — the palace alone is worth the trip.
3. **Is Class 6 the entry point?** With 90 seats against 36 in Class 9, Class 6 is the sensible target. Class 9 entry is a second chance, not a plan — though Korukonda's 36 Class 9 seats are among the largest in the country, so it is a better second chance than most.

If the answer to all three is yes, Korukonda is a school that, 64 years after it opened in a gifted palace, is still sending its cadets to the Academy. The form closes on 2 December — seven days is enough to fill it carefully, not twice.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Korukonda have for 2027-28?**

Korukonda has 90 Class 6 seats — 80 for boys and 10 for girls — and 36 Class 9 seats — 34 for boys and 2 for girls. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin.

**When was Sainik School Korukonda founded?**

It was formally founded on 18 January 1962 by its first principal, Commander Trevor De Almeida, in the Alak Appala Kondayamba Vijayaram Palace at Korukonda — a palace built in 1911 by Maharaja Vijayaram Gajapathi Raju and gifted for use as a school in September 1961.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Korukonda?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Korukonda?**

No single verified 2027 fee figure is available from the school or the NTA bulletin. One coaching portal reports tuition at the older Sainik Schools at Rs 1,28,615 a year for 2026-27, so parents should confirm the current figure from the school before budgeting.

**Does Sainik School Korukonda admit girls?**

Yes — 10 girls' seats in Class 6 and 2 in Class 9 for 2027-28, five of the Class 6 girls' seats for Andhra Pradesh girls and five for girls from other states. Girls study in Satavahana House, the school's ninth house.

**How many boarding houses does Sainik School Korukonda have?**

Nine — Pallava, Kakatiya, Maurya, Pandiya, Gajapathi, Chalukya, Moghul and Gupta for boys, and Satavahana for girl cadets. Each house holds 72 cadets, with its own House Master living in the house block.
