## The school Odisha parents mean

If you live in Odisha and your child dreams of the armed forces, there is really only one school on your list — because Odisha has only one Sainik School under the Sainik Schools Society. **Sainik School Bhubaneswar** sits in the state capital, two kilometres from the national highway, next to Utkal University. It is one of the oldest Sainik Schools in India, and for a parent filling the AISSEE 2027 form this week, it is the school that decides whether the home-state quota works for you or against you.

The school was started on **15 January 1962** by **Biju Patnaik**, then Chief Minister of Odisha, in temporary government accommodation. Two years later, on **8 January 1964**, Prime Minister **Jawaharlal Nehru** inaugurated the permanent campus. That is the kind of beginning most schools can only borrow from history books — Bhubaneswar lived it. Its motto says what it is for: **"Seva, Kartavya, Gyan, Veerta"** — service, duty, knowledge, bravery.

## The seats: 90 in Class 6, none in Class 9

Here is the number that shapes every Odisha family's strategy this year. For 2027-28, Bhubaneswar has **90 Class 6 seats and zero Class 9 seats**:

| Entry | Boys | Girls | Total (tentative) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class 6 | 80 | 10 | 90 |
| Class 9 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The Class 6 split matters, because the home-state quota is where Odisha families win. Of the 80 boys' seats, **55 go to Odisha-domicile boys and 25 to boys from other states**; of the 10 girls' seats, **five are for Odisha girls and five for girls from other states**. Inside the Odisha boys' quota, the category split is reported as SC 8, ST 4, OBC-NCL 15, Defence 6 and General 22.

Two things to read into this. First, if your child is in Class 8, Bhubaneswar is simply not an option this year — there are no Class 9 seats at all, and Bhubaneswar is one of only four of the 33 schools with a zero in that column. Your AISSEE choices should point elsewhere, or at Odisha's second Sainik School, **Sambalpur**, which has 12 Class 9 seats this year. Our [Odisha state guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-odisha-complete-guide-2027/) compares the two schools side by side.

Second, the home-state quota is your biggest advantage if you are Odisha-domiciled — 67% of seats are reserved for the home state, and that is what makes a 90-seat school a realistic target rather than a lottery. We explain the whole quota system, including the 25% defence-wives-and-wards share inside each category, in our [home-state quota guide](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-67-percent-explained-2026-10-17/).

## The NDA record — and the Harvard on the lawn

Let us be straight about the numbers everyone quotes. The school's Diamond Jubilee coverage in 2022 reports that **more than 600 of its alumni have become officers** in the armed forces, and that **ten of them rose to three-star rank** — Lieutenant General or equivalent. Those are reported figures, not an officially audited tally, but the direction is not in doubt: this is a school whose alumni sit at the top of the services. One of its younger alumni, **Captain Ashutosh Kumar**, was awarded the **Shaurya Chakra posthumously** in 2020 after dying in a counter-terror operation in Jammu and Kashmir.

And then there is the detail no other school can copy. On **12 September 1964**, Air Marshal Arjan Singh handed the school a **Harvard trainer aircraft** for display — and it still stands on the campus today. A child who joins Bhubaneswar walks past a real piece of Indian Air Force history every single day. That is the kind of thing that makes a ten-year-old decide what he wants to be.

The school is also a serious sporting school — its cadets have won **All India Sainik Schools championships in hockey and football** — and NCC is integral to cadet life, with all three wings: Army, Navy and Air Force.

## Fees: what Bhubaneswar actually costs

The latest published figure is **₹1,62,822 a year for a Class 6 cadet**, from the school's 2023-24 fee sheet. Treat that as a planning figure, not a quote: fees are revised periodically, and the 2027-28 figure will be higher. Budget around ₹1.8–1.9 lakh to be safe, and confirm the current figure from the school before you commit.

Nobody actually pays the full sticker price for long. **Defence scholarships** for wards of serving personnel and ex-servicemen, and **state government scholarships**, reduce the burden substantially, and fee concessions are income-linked. Our detailed walkthrough: [Sainik School fee concessions and income slabs — how to apply](/blog/sainik-school-fee-concession-income-slabs-how-to-apply-2026-10-16/).

## The admission path, start to finish

1. **AISSEE form (closes 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM):** one central form on the NTA portal; select Sainik School Bhubaneswar as your preference. Fee ₹850 (General/OBC/Defence) or ₹700 (SC/ST). Keep the application number safe.
2. **Written exam (Sunday, 31 January 2027):** Class 6 is 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes; Maths carries half the marks and decides more admissions than any other subject.
3. **E-counselling:** merit lists by category and state; Bhubaneswar seats allotted per the seat matrix above. Remember — no Class 9 counselling at Bhubaneswar this year.
4. **Medical examination:** mandatory and strict. Check our [medical certificate format guide](/blog/medical-certificate-format-sainik-school-admission-2026-10-16/) early, not after allotment.
5. **Document verification + first fee payment:** domicile, birth certificate, category certificates — originals.

## One thing to know before you fall in love with the campus

Bhubaneswar has been in the news over a **proposed relocation of the school for a road-widening project** — reported, and still a proposal rather than a decided move. It does not change anything about AISSEE 2027: admission, the exam and the counselling all run exactly as published. But if you are the kind of parent who visits a campus before choosing it, check the current position with the school itself rather than assuming the campus you read about is the campus your child will join.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Bhubaneswar have for 2027-28?**
Bhubaneswar has 90 Class 6 seats for 2027-28 — 80 for boys and 10 for girls — and zero Class 9 seats. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin's seat appendices.

**When was Sainik School Bhubaneswar founded?**
It was started on 15 January 1962 by Biju Patnaik, then Chief Minister of Odisha, in temporary government accommodation. The permanent campus was inaugurated by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru on 8 January 1964. Its motto is Seva, Kartavya, Gyan, Veerta.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Bhubaneswar?**
Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8 — but Bhubaneswar has no Class 9 seats this year.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Bhubaneswar?**
The latest published figure is ₹1,62,822 a year for a Class 6 cadet (2023-24 sheet). Fees are revised periodically, so treat that as a planning figure and confirm the current figure from the school before budgeting. Defence and state scholarships reduce the burden for eligible families.

**Does Sainik School Bhubaneswar admit girls?**
Yes. For 2027-28, 10 of the 90 Class 6 seats are for girls — five for Odisha-domicile girls and five for girls from other states. There are no Class 9 seats at all this year, for boys or girls.

**How strong is Bhubaneswar's NDA record?**
More than 600 of its alumni are reported to have become officers in the armed forces, and ten are reported to have risen to the rank of Lieutenant General or equivalent (three-star). The school celebrated its Diamond Jubilee — 60 years — in 2022.
