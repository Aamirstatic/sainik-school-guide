## Why Tilaiya is on so many parents' lists this year

If you are filling the AISSEE 2027 form this week, one school deserves a closer look than most: **Sainik School Tilaiya**. For the 2027-28 session it has **140 Class 6 seats — the largest intake of any Sainik School in India**. That is not a small thing when every other school is offering 50 to 120.

But the seats are only half the story. Tilaiya is also one of the oldest Sainik Schools in the country, founded in **1963**, with a record of sending about **900 cadets to the National Defence Academy**. For a parent in Jharkhand — or anywhere in eastern India — it combines the biggest door with one of the longest traditions.

## A school born out of the 1962 war

Tilaiya's foundation stone was laid on **16 September 1963**, in the shadow of the 1962 war with China. The then Defence Minister, **V. K. Krishna Menon**, and the then Chief Minister of Bihar, **K. B. Sahay**, pushed it through, and the first principal, **Lt Col L. E. G. Smith**, built it from nothing on the banks of the Tilaiya reservoir.

It was one of the **first 12 Sainik Schools** in India. The idea was simple and it hasn't changed: take bright boys (and now girls) from ordinary families, give them a public-school education with military discipline, and prepare them for the National Defence Academy and the armed forces.

Sixty-three years later, the school still lives by that idea. Its **Honour Code** — "As a Tilaiyan, I will be truthful, trustworthy, honest and forthright under all circumstances" — is recited by every cadet, and the morning assembly still begins with the pledge that the safety, honour and welfare of the country come first.

## Seats for 2027-28: the biggest Class 6 intake in India

The AISSEE 2027 bulletin lists Tilaiya's tentative vacancies as:

| Entry | Boys | Girls | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class 6 | 126 (87 Jharkhand + 39 other states) | 14 (8 Jharkhand + 6 other states) | **140** |
| Class 9 | 24 | 1 | **25** |

Two things jump out. First, the **67% home-state quota** works strongly in a Jharkhand child's favour: 87 of the 126 boys' seats and 8 of the 14 girls' seats are reserved for Jharkhand-domiciled candidates. (Our [domicile certificate guide](/blog/domicile-certificate-sainik-school-guide/) explains how the quota works and what document you need.)

Second, Class 9 is a different world: just 25 seats, and only **one** for a girl. If your child is in Class 8, Tilaiya is a long shot — apply, by all means, but keep your choice list wide. The full national picture is in our [AISSEE 2027 seat news](/news/aissee-2027-3160-class-6-seats-731-class-9-seats-2026-11-24/).

Remember the bulletin's own warning: these numbers are **tentative**. They can change if cadets withdraw, so list Tilaiya in your choices even if your category shows few seats — redistribution can open a seat later.

## How admission works

There is exactly one way in: **AISSEE**, the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination, run by NTA. For 2027-28:

- **Apply by:** 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM, at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society (late window 3–6 December with an extra Rs 500)
- **Application fee:** Rs 850 (General, OBC-NCL, defence wards) or Rs 700 (SC/ST)
- **Class 6 age:** 10 to 12 years on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017)
- **Class 9 age:** 13 to 15 years on 31 March 2027, and must have passed Class 8
- **Exam:** Sunday, 31 January 2027 — Class 6 from 2:00 to 4:30 PM (150 minutes, 300 marks), Class 9 from 2:00 to 5:00 PM (180 minutes, 400 marks)
- **Qualifying marks:** 25% in each section and 40% overall (not applicable to SC/ST)

After the written exam come the merit list, online counselling through AISSAC, document verification and a medical examination. The [Class 6 and Class 9 syllabus](/blog/aissee-2027-syllabus-class-6-class-9/) is the same for every school — there is no separate Tilaiya paper.

## What it costs — and what to check before you believe a number

Honestly, the fee is the one figure we cannot give you as a single confirmed number, and any site that does is guessing. Different portals report very different totals for Tilaiya — roughly **Rs 1.4 lakh to Rs 1.9 lakh a year** — and the school revises its fee structure regularly. One recent portal breaks the 2026-27 figure down as about Rs 1,28,615 tuition plus Rs 44,250 dietary money, with a one-time clothing charge of around Rs 38,000 at admission. Treat that as indicative.

## Life at Tilaiya: houses, sport and the NDA record

A Tilaiyan's day starts early and runs on routine: morning PT and drill, classes, games in the evening, prep at night. Cadets live in one of **12 houses** — Aryabhatta, Ashoka, Gautam, Kunwar Singh, Magadh, Maurya, Mithila, Nalanda, Patliputra, Rajgir, Vaishali and Vikram — each with its own traditions, and houses compete for trophies all year.

The school's proudest statistic is its NDA record: about **900 cadets** have joined the National Defence Academy, and Tilaiya has twice won the **Defence Minister's Trophy** for sending the maximum cadets to NDA. It also holds the Raksha Rajya Mantri merit certificate for the best Class 10 results for four consecutive years — it is academically strong, not only a defence nursery.

The alumni list backs it up: **Air Marshal Awadhesh Kumar Bharti**, Deputy Chief of the Air Staff; filmmaker **Prakash Jha**; **Justice Shiva Kirti Singh** of the Supreme Court; **Lt Gen Gyan Bhushan**; and **Shaibal Gupta**, Padma Shri. Cadets have also gone into the IAS, IPS, medicine and engineering.

## Should your child apply to Tilaiya?

Apply if your child is in Class 5 or Class 8, meets the age rule, and can handle residential life — homesickness in the first term is real, and every Tilaiyan family goes through it. Tilaiya makes particular sense if you hold a **Jharkhand domicile** (the 67% quota) or if your child is aiming for the NDA.

One last thing: the form closes on **2 December**. Tilaiya is chosen during counselling, not on the form — the form only registers your child for AISSEE. So apply now, prepare for 31 January, and decide the school list when the merit list comes out. ## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Tilaiya have for 2027-28?**
Tilaiya has 140 Class 6 seats — 126 boys and 14 girls — the largest Class 6 intake of any Sainik School this year. Class 9 has 25 seats. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin.

**When was Sainik School Tilaiya founded?**
The foundation stone was laid on 16 September 1963. It was one of the first 12 Sainik Schools in India, set up after the 1962 war to prepare boys for the armed forces. Its motto is Agre Sarat Sarvada — Forward Ever.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Tilaiya?**
Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Tilaiya?**
Different portals report between roughly Rs 1.4 lakh and Rs 1.9 lakh a year, so treat any single figure with caution — the school revises fees and the exact notice matters. Defence families can get scholarships, and SC and ST families pay less. Check the school's own fee notice before planning.

**Does Sainik School Tilaiya admit girls?**
Yes. Tilaiya was one of the first five Sainik Schools to admit girls, with the first girl cadets joining in the 2021-22 session. For 2027-28, 14 of its 140 Class 6 seats are for girls.

**Is Tilaiya a good choice for NDA preparation?**
It has one of the strongest records in the country: the school says about 900 of its cadets have joined the National Defence Academy, and it has won the Defence Minister's Trophy twice for sending the maximum cadets to NDA. But NDA selection still depends on the child — the school opens the door, it does not carry them through it.
