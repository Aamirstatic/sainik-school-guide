West Bengal has exactly one Sainik School. Just one — and it happens to be one of the oldest and most decorated in the country. Sainik School Purulia was founded in **1962**, in the second year of the Sainik Schools scheme, and its alumni include a Chief of Air Staff, a Deputy Chief of Army Staff, and — as the news reminded us this very weekend — a three-term Chief Minister of Nagaland who says the school shaped his character.

If your child is in Class 5 or Class 8 in West Bengal right now, this guide is the complete picture — the school, the quota math, the seats, the fees, and the ten days you have left to apply.

## The one school: Purulia

| | **Sainik School Purulia** |
|---|---|
| Established | 29 January 1962 — one of the oldest Sainik Schools in India |
| Location | Purulia–Ranchi road, Purulia district, West Bengal |
| Classes | VI to XII, residential, CBSE-affiliated, English medium |
| Campus | About 280 acres; 5 km from Purulia bus stand, 8 km from Purulia Junction railway station |
| Enrolment | Around 550 cadets |
| Motto | "Knowledge Through Discipline" |
| Admission | Only through the AISSEE (NTA) — no other route |

Purulia started functioning at Bongabari, near Chharra, under its first principal, Lt Col S. Mazumdar. It is a full residential school — your child lives on campus from Class 6, with 70 days of official vacations a year. Purulia town, the nearest railhead, is 8 km from the school gate.

## The quota math — and the 120 seats

This is the section that matters most. For AISSEE 2027, Purulia has **120 Class 6 seats** in the tentative seat matrix:

| Pool | Boys | Girls | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| West Bengal domicile (67%) | 74 | — | — |
| Other states (33%) | 34 | — | — |
| **All Class 6 seats** | **108** | **12** | **120** |

Two things to notice. First, the home-state advantage is real: 74 of the 108 boys' seats are reserved for West Bengal-domicile candidates. Second, girls have a real route here: 12 of the 120 seats are for girls, under the rule that at least 10% of Class 6 seats (or 10 seats, whichever is higher) are reserved for girls.

Within the seats, the reservation structure follows the Sainik Schools SOP: **SC 15%, ST 7.5%, OBC-NCL 27%**, and **25% of the remainder** for defence wards. Class 9 is a much smaller door — about **10 seats in the previous cycle** — so if your child is in Class 8, treat Class 9 as a long shot. Read our [home-state quota explainer](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-explained/) for the full 67/33 mechanism, and our [girls' quota guide](/blog/girls-quota-sainik-schools-explained-2026-10-22/) if your daughter is applying.

## The alumni record

- **Air Chief Marshal Arup Raha** — the 21st Chief of the Air Staff of the Indian Air Force.
- **Lt Gen Subrata Saha** — Deputy Chief of Army Staff, and the first Purulia alumnus to reach Lieutenant General rank; he commanded 15 Corps in Kashmir.
- **Neiphiu Rio** — three-term Chief Minister of Nagaland, who said at the Old Boys' Association meeting on 21 November 2026 that Purulia played an important role in shaping his character.
- **Vivekanand Sinha, IPS** — Inspector General of Police, Bastar, Chhattisgarh.

There is also a story every Purulia parent should know. In 2021, Class 10 cadet **Amit Raj** sacrificed his own life saving three children from a fire. It is not why you send a child to a Sainik School — but it tells you what the school drills into its cadets about putting others first.

## The fees — and the help that exists

Honestly, this is the question that stops most West Bengal families, so here it is plainly. The fees at Purulia are reported at **about ₹1.6 lakh a year** — confirm the current figure with the school before admission, because it is revised from time to time.

What many families do not know is that help exists. The **West Bengal government awards a few scholarships on a merit-cum-means basis** — full fees, clothing and money for those who qualify, though the income criteria are stringent and very few actually qualify. The **Ministry of Defence provides scholarships to the wards of serving and retired JCOs and NCOs**, and all cadets receive central assistance. If money is the reason you are hesitating, check the scholarship route before you decide the school is out of reach — and treat anyone offering a Purulia seat or a fee concession for payment as a scam. Admission is only through the AISSEE.

## Your timeline: 10 days to apply

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM** | AISSEE 2027 form closes (Class 6 and Class 9) |
| 3–6 December 2026 | Late-fee window — extra ₹500 on top of the regular fee |
| 7–9 December 2026 | Correction window — fix permitted details, one time only |
| 11 January 2027 | Exam city announced |
| 25 January 2027 | Admit cards available |
| **31 January 2027 (Sunday)** | **AISSEE 2027 exam** |

The application fee is **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and **₹700** for SC/ST. Class 6 is a 150-minute, 125-question, 300-mark paper (2:00–4:30 PM); Class 9 is 180 minutes, 150 questions, 400 marks, in English only (2:00–5:00 PM). There is **no negative marking**, and the qualifying rule is **25% in every section and 40% in aggregate** (not applicable to SC/ST) — our [qualifying-marks worked examples](/blog/aissee-2027-qualifying-marks-25-percent-rule-worked-examples/) show how the 25% rule can end a run even with a good total. Age limits: Class 6, born **1 April 2015–31 March 2017** and studying in Class 5; Class 9, born **1 April 2012–31 March 2014** and studying in Class 8. Do not wait until 2 December — the portal is always busiest on the last day. Our [10-week countdown calendar](/blog/aissee-2027-10-week-countdown-calendar-23-november-to-exam-2026-11-21/) takes you from the form to exam day, week by week.

## What to do this week: a West Bengal parent's checklist

1. **Check the date of birth** against the birth certificate — Class 6: 1 April 2015–31 March 2017; Class 9: 1 April 2012–31 March 2014.
2. **Get the domicile certificate ready.** West Bengal domicile puts your child in the 67% pool — 74 of the 108 boys' seats.
3. **Photograph and signature** to NTA specs — white background, recent photo, correct file size.
4. **Fill the form at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** and pay the fee (₹850 / ₹700) by 2 December, 11:50 PM.
5. **Save the confirmation page and application number.** You will need them for the admit card in January.

One last thing, and it is specific to this week. On **Wednesday, 25 November**, Sainik School Purulia itself is holding a **walk-in interview for four contractual Primary Teacher posts** at 8:00 AM, on a consolidated pay of ₹16,000 a month — our full notice is here: [Sainik School Purulia PRT walk-in, 25 November](/news/sainik-school-purulia-prt-walk-in-25-november-2026-11-18/).

West Bengal has one Sainik School, and it is a good one — old, decorated, and eight kilometres from Purulia Junction. The quota is built for your child, the form is open for ten more days, and the only thing standing between the two is a form filled carefully, on a calm Sunday evening, before the rush.
