> **🔄 Last verified: 26 September 2026** — Counselling ✅ **AISSAC** ke naam se **Sainik Schools Society** conduct karti hai (NTA nahi) — `pesa.ncog.gov.in` par, poori tarah **online**, **bina koi fee** ke, lagbhag **6 rounds** mein. 2027 ka schedule 🔶 result (expected Mar 2027) ke baad aayega.

Exam clear karna aadhi ladai hai. **Counselling jeetna** doosri aadhi hai. Har saal achhe marks lane wale bachche sirf isliye seat se chook jaate hain kyunki unhone choice filling mein galti ki — galat order, kam options, ya last round ka intezaar nahi kiya.

Ye article AISSAC ka **poora process** samjhata hai — taaki aap wo galti na karein.

## AISSAC kya hai? (Basic facts ✅)

- **Full form:** All India Sainik Schools Admission Counselling
- **Conducted by:** Sainik Schools Society (NTA exam leta hai, counselling Society karti hai — ye farak samajhna zaroori hai)
- **Kahan:** `pesa.ncog.gov.in` par online
- **Fee:** Koi counselling fee nahi ✅
- **Rounds:** Lagbhag 6 rounds (2025 SOP ke anusaar)

## Step-by-step process

### Step 1: Registration
Result ke baad AISSAC portal par register kijiye — AISSEE roll number aur basic details se. **Jo register nahi karega, use seat nahi milegi** — merit list mein naam hona kaafi nahi.

### Step 2: Choice filling (sabse critical step)
Schools ki preference order dijiye — traditional 33 + new PPP schools, dono ke options. **Yahi step admission tay karta hai.** Strategy neeche di hai.

### Step 3: Seat allotment
Har round mein allotment hoti hai — **aapke marks (merit) + reservation category + aapki choice order** — teeno milkar tay karte hain kaunsi seat milegi.

### Step 4: Medical test
Allotment ke baad school mein medical hota hai — eyesight, height-weight, general fitness. [Medical guide](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/) padhkar pehle se taiyar rahiye.

### Step 5: Admission & fee
Medical clear hone par school mein reporting, documents verification aur fee payment.

## Choice filling strategy (meri salah)

| Strategy | Kya karein | Kyun |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Dream + Safe mix | Upar dream schools, neeche safe options | Sirf top schools bharne par khaali haath reh sakte hain |
| Kam se kam 10–15 choices | Jitne zyada options, utna behtar chance | Har round mein naye options khul sakte hain |
| Fee check karke | PPP schools ki fee pehle dekh lein | Baad mein fee dekhkar mana karna seat waste karta hai |
| Distance realistic rakhein | Bahut door ka school soch-samajhkar chunein | 7 saal ka sawal hai |
| Har round mein participate karein | Round 1 mein seat na mile to haar mat maniye | 6 rounds hote hain — kai bachchon ko round 3–4 mein milti hai |

## Reservation kaise kaam karta hai? ✅ (SOP)

- **67%** seats home-state ke students ke liye, **33%** other states ke liye
- SC **15%**, ST **7.5%**, OBC-NCL **27%**
- Bache hue mein se **25%** defence wards ke liye
- Girls (Class 6, 33 schools): **10% ya 10 seats, jo zyada ho**

[Quota ka detailed guide](/blog/sainik-school-sc-st-defence-quota-2027/) yahan hai.

## Documents checklist (pehle se taiyar rakhiye)

- Birth certificate
- Class 5/8 pass certificate
- Domicile certificate
- Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL — valid aur latest)
- Defence service certificate (agar lagu ho)
- Photo + signature (specification ke anusaar)
- AISSEE admit card + scorecard

## Har round mein kya hota hai? (Round-wise samajh)

| Round | Kya hota hai | Aapko kya karna hai |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| Registration | Portal par sign-up | Documents taiyar rakhkar turant register kijiye |
| Choice filling | School preference order | Strategy se order lagayein (upar di gayi table) |
| Round 1 | Pehli allotment (top merit) | Seat mili to accept + medical ki taiyari; nahi mili to next round |
| Round 2–4 | Khali seats par agli merit | Choice modify kar sakte hain — realistic options add kijiye |
| Round 5–6 | Last merit + waiting | Hिम्मat mat haariye — kai seats yahin milti hain |
| Spot round | Bachi-khuchi seats | Turant decision lena hota hai — pehle se taiyar rahiye |

**Golden rule:** Beech mein counselling chhodkar mat jaiye. Har saal aise cases hote hain jahan Round 1 mein 230 marks wala select nahi hua, lekin Round 4 mein 215 marks wale ko seat mil gayi — kyunki pehle wala haar maan chuka tha.

## FAQs

**Q1. Kya counselling mein fee lagti hai?**
Nahi. AISSAC registration aur choice filling **free** hai ✅. Beware: koi bhi "counselling fee" maange to fraud hai.

**Q2. NTA counselling karti hai ya koi aur?**
Sainik Schools Society — `pesa.ncog.gov.in` par. NTA sirf exam conduct karta hai.

**Q3. Agar Round 1 mein seat na mile to?**
Aage ke rounds ka intezaar kijiye — lagbhag 6 rounds hote hain. Har round mein choice modify karne ka mauka milta hai.

**Q4. Kya choice filling mein new PPP schools bhi honge?**
Haan — 2026 mein the, 2027 mein bhi 🔶 expected hain. [PPP schools guide](/blog/new-sainik-schools-ppp-model-2027/) padhiye.

**Q5. Medical mein fail hone par seat ka kya hota hai?**
Seat agle eligible candidate ko chali jaati hai. Isliye pehle se [medical checkup](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/) karwa lijiye.

**Q6. Spot round kya hota hai?**
Aakhri rounds ke baad bachi hui seats ke liye hota hai. Kam marks wale students ke liye aakhri mauka — isliye counselling se beech mein mat hatiye.
