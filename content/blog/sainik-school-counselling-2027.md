> **🔄 Last verified: 26 September 2026** — Counselling runs under the name ✅ **AISSAC** (All India Sainik Schools Admission Counselling), conducted by the **Sainik Schools Society** — not NTA — at `pesa.ncog.gov.in`. Fully ✅ **online**, ✅ **no counselling fee**, around ✅ **6 rounds** (per the 2026 cycle). The 2027 schedule is 🔶 **expected after the result** (roughly March 2027).

Here's the worry every parent carries after the result: *"My child has qualified — is the seat confirmed now?"* I wish I could say yes. The honest answer is no. Qualifying the AISSEE only buys you a ticket to the counselling hall. The actual seat — which school, which round — is decided by **AISSAC**, and every year good scorers lose seats they should have won because they treated counselling casually. This guide to Sainik School counselling 2027 exists so that doesn't happen to you.

A mother from Patna told me her story last year, and I think about it every counselling season. Her son had scored well above the qualifying marks. In Round 1 she filled just four schools — all famous names. He got nothing in Round 1, and feeling defeated, she skipped Round 2 entirely. In Round 4, a boy with *lower* marks took a seat she could easily have had. She still says, quietly, "If only we'd stayed for one more round." Please stay for every round.

## The basics — get this straight first

- **What it is:** AISSAC — All India Sainik Schools Admission Counselling ✅
- **Who runs it:** the Sainik Schools Society (NTA only conducts the exam; the Society handles everything after) ✅
- **Where:** online, at `pesa.ncog.gov.in` ✅
- **Fee:** none — registration and choice filling are free ✅ (anyone demanding a "counselling fee" is running a scam)
- **How many rounds:** around 6, based on the 2026 cycle ✅

That first point deserves emphasis because it confuses people every year. NTA's job ends with the scorecard. From counselling onwards, you're dealing with the Sainik Schools Society.

## Step by step: what actually happens

**Step 1 — Registration.** After the result, register on the AISSAC portal with your child's AISSEE details. One registration covers the whole counselling — you do it once. And understand this clearly: *if you don't register, you don't get a seat*, no matter how good the score. A name on the merit list alone means nothing without registration.

**Step 2 — Choice filling.** You list your school preferences, in order — up to 10 school choices. This is the single most important step of the entire admission, and I've given it its own section below.

**Step 3 — Seat allotment.** Each round, the system allots seats based on five things together: AISSEE rank, domicile (home-state vs other-state), gender, category, and your preference order. All five. Not just marks.

**Step 4 — Accept, Reconsider, or Exit.** After an allotment, you choose: **Accept** the seat and move to medical/document verification, **Reconsider** (stay in the counselling for a better option in later rounds), or **Exit** the process. This decision is yours every round — use it thoughtfully.

**Step 5 — Medical fitness and document verification.** An allotted seat is confirmed only after the medical examination and document verification at the school. [Read the medical test guide](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/) well in advance — a failed medical sends the seat to the next candidate in line.

## Round-by-round: how the flow actually works

| Stage | What happens | What you should do |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| Registration | One-time sign-up on `pesa.ncog.gov.in` | Register immediately — keep documents scanned and ready |
| Choice filling | You order up to 10 school preferences | Use the strategy below; order matters enormously |
| Round 1 | First allotment, top ranks | Seat offered? Accept or Reconsider. Nothing? Don't panic — stay in |
| Rounds 2–4 | Vacant seats go to the next ranks | You can modify choices — add realistic options here |
| Rounds 5–6 | Final merit movement, waiting lists clear | Hold your nerve — many seats are won right here |
| Spot round | Leftover seats, if any | Decisions are quick — be prepared to say yes on the spot |

The pattern is consistent every year: Round 1 rewards the toppers, but Rounds 3–6 are where patient, well-prepared families win. The system rewards those who stay, not just those who score.

## Choice filling: the step that decides everything

| Strategy | What to do | Why it works |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| Mix dreams with safety | Dream schools on top, realistic options below | Filling only famous schools can leave you with nothing |
| Use all 10 preferences | Fill every slot you're allowed | More options = more chances across rounds |
| Check fees first | Look up each school's fee before listing it | Rejecting a seat later over fees wastes everyone's time |
| Be realistic about distance | Think hard before choosing a school 2,000 km away | This is a 7-year commitment for your child |
| Modify in later rounds | Add safer options if early rounds miss | The system allows it — use it |

One more thing about the new PPP-model schools: they were part of choice filling in 2026 and are 🔶 expected in 2027 as well. Don't dismiss them out of hand — read the [PPP schools guide](/blog/new-sainik-schools-ppp-model-2027/) before you decide. Some are excellent; their fees just differ from the traditional schools.

## How reservation shapes your allotment

Allotment isn't pure merit — reservation rules from the Society's SOP apply ✅:

- **67%** of seats go to students of the school's home state; **33%** to students from other states
- Within that: **SC 15%**, **ST 7.5%**, **OBC-NCL 27%**
- Of what's left, **25%** is reserved for defence wards
- No single state/UT can take more than 25% of the Other-States quota

What this means in practice: your home-state school is usually your statistically best shot, because 67% of its seats are fenced for students like your child. Our [quota guide](/blog/sainik-school-sc-st-defence-quota-2027/) breaks this down with examples.

## Documents: keep these ready before Round 1

- Birth certificate + Aadhaar
- Class 5 (or Class 8, for Class 9 entry) pass certificate
- Domicile certificate
- Category certificate — SC/ST/OBC-NCL, valid and current
- Defence service certificate, if applicable
- Photograph + signature in the specified format
- AISSEE admit card and scorecard

Document verification kills more admissions than people admit. An expired category certificate or a mismatched name spelling can cost a confirmed seat. Check everything in February, not in May. When the [result and scorecard](/blog/aissee-2027-result-scorecard-guide/) arrive, download and preserve them immediately.

## The golden rule

Don't leave the counselling midway. I know Round 1 disappointment stings. But the data of every cycle says the same thing: seats keep moving through Rounds 4, 5 and 6, and the families who stay — choices updated, documents ready, decisions quick — are the ones who convert a qualifying score into an admission letter. Be that family.

## FAQs

**Q1. Is there really no counselling fee?**
Really none. AISSAC registration and choice filling are ✅ free. If anyone asks you for money for counselling, it's fraud — report it.

**Q2. Who conducts the counselling — NTA or someone else?**
The Sainik Schools Society, at `pesa.ncog.gov.in`. NTA's role ends with conducting the exam and declaring results.

**Q3. What if my child doesn't get a seat in Round 1?**
Stay in the process. Around six rounds happen, and you can modify your choices between rounds. Many students get their seats in Rounds 3–6.

**Q4. What do Accept, Reconsider and Exit mean after allotment?**
Accept means you take the allotted seat and proceed to medical/verification. Reconsider keeps you in the counselling for a possible better allotment later. Exit withdraws you from the process entirely.

**Q5. Are the new PPP schools part of counselling?**
They were in 2026, and are 🔶 expected in 2027. They appear in the same choice-filling list.

**Q6. What happens if the medical test is failed after allotment?**
The seat passes to the next eligible candidate. This is why a [pre-counselling medical checkup](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/) is worth every rupee.
