## Quick Summary — Sainik School Counselling 2027 at a Glance

Clearing the AISSEE written exam is only **half the battle**. The other half is the **e-counselling process (AISSAC)**, where seats are actually allocated based on your child's rank, category, and school preferences. Every year, well-prepared students lose their seats because of counselling mistakes — wrong preference order, missed deadlines, or incomplete documents.

Here is the counselling process at a glance:

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Counselling Portal** | [pesa.ncog.gov.in/sainikschoolecounselling](https://pesa.ncog.gov.in/sainikschoolecounselling/landingpage) |
| **Counselling Mode** | Online (e-counselling) |
| **Expected Start** | March 2027 (after AISSEE results) |
| **Total Rounds** | 2–3 rounds + Spot Round |
| **Home State Quota** | 67% seats reserved |
| **Other State Quota** | 33% seats open to all India |
| **Reservation Categories** | SC (15%), ST (7.5%), OBC-NCL (27%), Defence (25%), Girls (25%) |
| **Document Verification** | At allotted school after seat acceptance |
| **Session Start** | April–May 2027 |

> **Bottom Line:** Fill **as many school preferences as possible**. Register for counselling **immediately** when the portal opens. Never leave any preference slot blank. A seat in a distant school is better than no seat at all.

## How Sainik School Counselling Works — The Complete Process

The [Sainik School admission](/blog/sainik-school-admission-2027-guide/) counselling is conducted by the **Sainik Schools Society** through the **AISSAC (All India Sainik Schools Admission Counselling)** portal. It is an online process with multiple rounds.

### Counselling Process Overview

```
AISSEE Result Declared
        ↓
Register on AISSAC Portal
        ↓
Fill School Preferences (Choices)
        ↓
Lock Choices Before Deadline
        ↓
Seat Allotment (Round 1)
        ↓
Accept Seat + Pay Fee + Document Verification
        ↓
(If no seat) → Round 2 Allotment
        ↓
(If still no seat) → Spot Round (Offline)
        ↓
Final Admission
```

### Counselling Timeline (Tentative for 2027)

Based on AISSEE 2025 and 2026 counselling schedules:

| Event | Expected Date | Duration |
|-------|:------------:|:--------:|
| **AISSEE 2027 Result** | February 2027 | — |
| **AISSAC Portal Opens** | March 2027 | — |
| **Registration & Choice Filling** | March 2027 | 7–10 days |
| **Choice Locking Deadline** | March 2027 | — |
| **Round 1 Seat Allotment** | March 2027 | — |
| **Round 1 Fee Payment & Reporting** | March–April 2027 | 7–10 days |
| **Round 2 Seat Allotment** | April 2027 | — |
| **Round 2 Fee Payment & Reporting** | April 2027 | 7–10 days |
| **Spot Round (if vacant seats)** | April–May 2027 | Varies by school |
| **Academic Session Begins** | April–May 2027 | — |

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Register and Participate in Counselling

### Step 1: Check AISSEE 2027 Result

1. Visit [exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/)
2. Click on **"AISSEE 2027 Result"**
3. Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. Note down your:
   - **All India Rank (AIR)**
   - **State Rank**
   - **Category Rank** (if applicable)
   - **Total Score** (out of 300 for Class 6, 400 for Class 9)
5. Download and print the **Score Card**

### Step 2: Register on the AISSAC Counselling Portal

1. Visit **[pesa.ncog.gov.in/sainikschoolecounselling](https://pesa.ncog.gov.in/sainikschoolecounselling/landingpage)**
2. Click on **"New Registration"** or **"Candidate Login"**
3. Enter:
   - AISSEE Roll Number
   - Date of Birth
   - Mobile Number (OTP verification required)
4. Create a password for the counselling portal
5. Note down your **Counselling Registration Number**

> **Important:** Use the **same mobile number** that was used during AISSEE application. If the number has changed, contact the AISSAC helpline before registering.

### Step 3: Fill School Preferences (Choice Filling)

This is the **most critical step** in the entire counselling process. Your school preferences determine which school your child will be allotted.

**How to fill preferences optimally:**

| Preference # | Strategy | Reasoning |
|:---:|----------|-----------|
| **1st** | Your **home state's** Sainik School | 67% seats reserved for state domiciled students |
| **2nd** | A strong school in a **neighboring state** | Good backup; easier for parents to visit |
| **3rd** | A school with **high NDA selection rate** | Quality of training matters more than distance |
| **4th** | A school with **good infrastructure** | Labs, library, sports facilities |
| **5th+** | **Any school** you are willing to send your child to | More choices = higher chance of getting a seat |

**Critical Rules:**
- **Fill ALL available preference slots** — more preferences = higher probability of getting a seat
- **Never leave a slot blank** — an unfilled slot is a wasted opportunity
- **Order matters** — the system allots the highest-preference school where a seat is available
- **Do not limit to 1–2 schools** — this dramatically reduces your chances

> **Author's Advice:** I have seen parents fill only 1 preference (their home state school) and then wonder why their child did not get a seat. If the home state school's seats are full, the system checks your 2nd preference, then 3rd, and so on. If you have only 1 preference and it is full, you get **nothing**. Fill every available slot.

### Step 4: Lock Your Choices

After filling preferences, you must **lock** them before the deadline:

1. Review all preferences carefully
2. Check the order — is your top choice really your top choice?
3. Click **"Lock Choices"**
4. Enter your password to confirm
5. Download the **Choice Locking Confirmation**

**After locking:**
- You **cannot change** your preferences
- The system will use these locked preferences for seat allotment
- If you do not lock before the deadline, your choices may not be considered

### Step 5: Wait for Seat Allotment

The system allocates seats based on:

| Factor | How It Affects Allotment |
|--------|------------------------|
| **AISSEE Rank** | Higher rank = higher priority |
| **Category** | SC/ST/OBC/Defence quota applies |
| **Domicile** | 67% seats reserved for home state students |
| **School Preferences** | Highest available preference is allotted |
| **Seat Availability** | Depends on number of applicants vs. seats |

**Checking Allotment Result:**
1. Login to the AISSAC portal on the allotment date
2. Check if a seat has been allotted
3. If allotted — note the school name and proceed to Step 6
4. If not allotted — wait for Round 2

### Step 6: Accept Seat and Complete Admission

If your child is allotted a seat:

1. **Accept the seat** on the portal within the deadline (usually 7–10 days)
2. **Pay the admission fee** online through the portal
3. **Download the Allotment Letter**
4. **Report to the allotted school** for document verification (within the specified dates)
5. **Carry original documents** for verification at the school

## Seat Allotment Rules — Quotas and Reservations

Understanding the reservation system is crucial for making informed preference decisions.

### Complete Reservation Structure

| Reservation Category | Percentage | Eligibility |
|---------------------|:----------:|-------------|
| **Home State Domicile** | 67% | Students from the state where the school is located |
| **Other State (All India)** | 33% | Students from any state |
| **SC (Scheduled Caste)** | 15% | Valid SC certificate from competent authority |
| **ST (Scheduled Tribe)** | 7.5% | Valid ST certificate from competent authority |
| **OBC-NCL** | 27% | OBC Non-Creamy Layer certificate |
| **Defence Ward** | 25% | Children of serving/ex-service personnel |
| **Girls Quota** | 25% | Girl students (applicable in co-ed schools) |

**Source:** Sainik Schools Society admission guidelines

### How Quotas Interact

A student can be eligible for **multiple quotas simultaneously**. For example:
- A girl student from the home state with SC category and defence background can benefit from **all applicable quotas**
- The system applies the **most beneficial combination** of reservations

### Important Notes on Domicile

- **Domicile certificate** must be from the state where the Sainik School is located
- For **home state quota (67%)**, the student must have a valid domicile certificate of that state
- If you do not have a domicile certificate, you are only eligible for the **33% All India quota**
- Some states have stricter domicile rules — check the specific school's admission guidelines

## Spot Round — The Last Chance for Admission

If seats remain **vacant** after the regular counselling rounds (Round 1 and Round 2), schools conduct **Spot Rounds**. This is offline counselling conducted directly by the school.

### When Does Spot Round Happen?

- Usually **2–4 weeks** after the last regular counselling round
- Not all schools conduct Spot Rounds — only those with vacant seats
- Dates vary by school — check individual school websites

### How Spot Round Works

| Step | Action |
|:---:|--------|
| 1 | School publishes **vacant seat list** on their website |
| 2 | Eligible candidates (who cleared AISSEE but did not get a seat) can apply |
| 3 | Applications are accepted **offline** at the school |
| 4 | Admission is based on **AISSEE merit rank** |
| 5 | For equal ranks, **first-come-first-served** may apply |
| 6 | Selected students must pay fees and complete admission **immediately** |

### Documents Required for Spot Round

| Document | Copies Needed | Notes |
|----------|:---:|-------|
| AISSEE Score Card | 3 | Download from NTA portal |
| Class 5/8 Marksheet | 3 | Original + photocopies |
| Birth Certificate | 3 | Municipal Corporation issued |
| Domicile Certificate | 3 | For home state quota |
| Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) | 3 | Valid, current financial year |
| Defence Certificate | 3 | If applicable |
| Passport-size Photographs | 6 | Same as AISSEE application |
| Aadhaar Card | 3 | Student + parent |
| Parent's ID Proof | 3 | Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport |
| Transfer Certificate | 1 | From current school |

### Spot Round Tips

1. **Be physically present** — Spot Rounds require in-person attendance at the school
2. **Carry all documents** — Missing documents mean immediate disqualification
3. **Arrive early** — First-come-first-served for equal ranks means timing matters
4. **Carry fee payment** — You may need to pay on the spot (demand draft or online)
5. **Have a backup plan** — Spot Rounds are competitive; do not rely solely on this

## What If Your Child Does Not Get Any Seat?

If your child clears AISSEE but does not get a seat in any counselling round or Spot Round:

### Option 1: Try Again Next Year

- If your child applied for Class 6, they can apply for **Class 9** in the next cycle (2 years later)
- If your child applied for Class 9, they can try for **Class 9 again** next year (if age permits)
- Use the extra year for better preparation

### Option 2: Apply to New PPP Sainik Schools

- **69 new Sainik Schools** under the PPP model have more seats available
- These schools follow the same AISSEE admission process
- Competition may be slightly lower than traditional schools
- Check the list on the [Sainik Schools Society website](https://sainikschooladmission.sainikschool.gov.in/)

### Option 3: Apply to Rashtriya Military Schools (RMS)

- 5 Rashtriya Military Schools across India
- Similar military-style residential education
- Separate entrance exam (conducted by Rashtriya Military Schools)
- [rashtriyamilitaryschools.edu.in](https://rashtriyamilitaryschools.edu.in/)

### Option 4: Apply to RIMC Dehradun

- **Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC)**, Dehradun
- Extremely selective (~50% NDA selection rate)
- Separate entrance exam
- Admission in Class 8 only
- [rimc.gov.in](https://rimc.gov.in/)

### Option 5: Consider Private Military-Style Schools

- Several private boarding schools offer similar discipline and NDA preparation
- Examples: Mayo College, Scindia School, Doon School
- Higher fees but quality education

## Common Counselling Mistakes That Cost Seats

| Mistake | Consequence | Prevention |
|---------|------------|------------|
| **Filling only 1–2 school preferences** | No seat if those schools are full | Fill ALL available preference slots |
| **Not registering before deadline** | Disqualified from counselling entirely | Set reminders; register on Day 1 |
| **Wrong category selection** | Seat cancelled during verification | Double-check category before locking |
| **Missing document verification date** | Admission cancelled | Note all dates; report on time |
| **Not paying admission fee on time** | Seat given to next candidate in line | Pay immediately after allotment |
| **Not locking choices** | Preferences may not be considered | Lock choices before the deadline |
| **Wrong domicile certificate** | Home state quota not applicable | Get domicile certificate ready before counselling |
| **Relying only on Spot Round** | Spot Round is not guaranteed | Participate in regular counselling rounds first |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### When does Sainik School counselling 2027 start?

Sainik School e-counselling (AISSAC) 2027 is expected to start in **March 2027**, approximately 2–4 weeks after the AISSEE 2027 results are declared (expected February 2027). The counselling registration portal at [pesa.ncog.gov.in](https://pesa.ncog.gov.in/sainikschoolecounselling/landingpage) will open for 7–10 days. Based on previous year patterns, the entire counselling process (including Spot Rounds) concludes by May 2027. Register immediately when the portal opens — late registrations are not accepted.

### How many school preferences should I fill during counselling?

Fill **all available preference slots** — typically 5 or more, depending on how many the portal allows. Students who fill more preferences have a **significantly higher chance** of getting a seat. Always put your home state's Sainik School as the first preference (67% state quota), then add schools in neighboring states and other locations as backups. Never leave any preference slot blank.

### What is the Spot Round in Sainik School counselling?

The Spot Round is an **offline counselling round** conducted directly by individual Sainik Schools when seats remain vacant after the regular online counselling rounds. Eligible candidates (who cleared AISSEE but did not get a seat) can apply in person at the school. Admission is based on AISSEE merit rank, and for equal ranks, first-come-first-served may apply. Spot Rounds typically happen in April–May, but not all schools conduct them — only those with vacant seats.

### Can I change my school preferences after locking them?

No, once you **lock your choices**, they cannot be changed. This is why it is critical to review your preferences carefully before locking. Consider factors like home state quota (67%), NDA selection rates, infrastructure, distance from home, and fee structure before finalizing your order. If you do not lock your choices before the deadline, the system may not consider your preferences for seat allotment.

### What documents are needed for Sainik School counselling?

The key documents required for counselling and admission are: **AISSEE Score Card**, **Class 5/8 Marksheet**, **Birth Certificate**, **Domicile Certificate** (for home state quota), **Category Certificate** (SC/ST/OBC if applicable), **Defence Certificate** (if applicable), **Aadhaar Card**, **passport-size photographs** (6 copies), and **Transfer Certificate** from the current school. Carry both **originals and 3 photocopies** of each document for verification at the allotted school.
