## The scene that repeats every admission season

Every year, the same thing happens at some Sainik School reporting desk in June or July. A child has done everything right — cleared the exam, secured a seat — and then a document officer checks the family's OBC certificate and shakes his head at the issue date. The certificate is two years old, and the admission gets held up.

Here is the rule parents consistently miss, stated up front: **for all practical purposes, your OBC-NCL certificate is a one-year document.** A certificate issued two years ago — even with no expiry date printed on it — will very likely be rejected at verification. ✅ CONFIRMED central norm (see Sources).

## What "OBC-NCL" actually means for AISSEE

**OBC** means your caste appears in the government's list of Other Backward Classes. Here is the first trap: there is a **state list** and a **Central List** of OBCs, and they are not the same. AISSEE is a central-level admission, so only castes in the **Central List of OBCs** count. A caste that is OBC in your state list but missing from the Central List gets no reservation benefit here. 🔶 EXPECTED per central norms — the AISSEE 2027 bulletin has not confirmed this wording yet, but it is the standard rule across central admissions and NTA exams. Check the Central List at ncbc.nic.in before you fill the form.

**NCL** — Non-Creamy Layer — is the second gate, and it is about income, not caste. Your family's **parental income must stay below ₹8 lakh per annum for three consecutive financial years** (salary and agricultural income excluded). ✅ CONFIRMED norm. Cross that line and your old certificate stops working — you are no longer non-creamy layer, paper or no paper.

## The one-year rule, stated plainly

Here is the norm from how central authorities and national exam bodies actually behave:

- **Central OBC-NCL certificates are treated as valid for one year.** Even with no expiry date printed, authorities routinely ask for one **issued within the last one year** — or **issued on or after 1 April of the admission financial year**. ✅ CONFIRMED — the consistent norm across central recruitment and NTA-pattern exams. (Incomes change, and so does NCL status; a 2023 certificate says nothing reliable about 2026–27.)
- **For AISSEE 2027, expect the same pattern**: hold a certificate **issued on or after 1 April 2026** when verification happens. 🔶 EXPECTED — the 2027 bulletin will give the exact wording; have a fresh certificate ready regardless. If yours was issued in 2024 or earlier, assume you need a new one.

## Central vs state certificate: know which one you hold

This distinction costs families seats every year: a state OBC certificate is issued for state-level admissions and jobs — it is **not** accepted for central or all-India applications.

| | Central (Govt of India) OBC-NCL certificate | State OBC certificate |
|---|---|---|
| **Used for** | Central jobs, central universities, national-level exams and admissions — including AISSEE 🔶 EXPECTED | State-level admissions and jobs only |
| **Caste basis** | Caste must be in the **Central List** of OBCs | Caste must be in the **state** OBC list |
| **Issuing authority** | Tehsildar / SDM / District Magistrate office ✅ | Tehsildar / SDM / DM office, state format ✅ |
| **Income basis** | Parental income below ₹8 lakh p.a. for 3 consecutive financial years (salary + agricultural income excluded) ✅ | Follows state rules |
| **Validity norm** | **1 year** — typically issued within the last year / on or after 1 April of the financial year ✅ | 1–3 years, depending on state rules ✅ |

Before you rely on your certificate, check two things: that it is in the **central format**, and that your caste is on the **Central List**.

## Your timeline: do this in October–November 2026

Certificates take **14 to 30 days** to process in most states ✅, so act now. The AISSEE 2027 notification is 🔶 expected around October–November 2026 and the exam is tentatively 31 January 2027 (per the NTA calendar) — a certificate issued now stays fresh through registration, counselling, and reporting.

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| **This week (Oct 2026)** | Check your certificate's issue date. If it is older than a year — or not in the central format — start the re-issue application now, online on your state's e-District portal or at the Tehsildar/SDM office. |
| **Before registration closes** | Have the fresh certificate in hand, so the category you select in the form matches the document you upload. |
| **Counselling (mid-2027)** | Carry the original. Officers check the issue date, the format, and the name spellings. |
| **School reporting** | Same documents again — originals. This is where old certificates get caught. |

The category you select during [registration](/blog/aissee-2027-registration-step-by-step-walkthrough) must match what your documents can prove. Filling the form as OBC-NCL and then failing to produce a valid certificate at verification can cost the seat — a wrong-category form with no supporting document is a classic way a good score dies at the reporting desk. If you cannot get a fresh certificate in time, it is far safer to apply as General. See the [counselling-day document checklist](/blog/aissee-counselling-day-document-checklist-2026-10-12).

## The certificate checklist

1. **Issue date** — within the last year, ideally on or after 1 April 2026. The most-checked field.
2. **Central format** — the central-government format for OBC, not your state's format.
3. **NCL clause** — it must explicitly state non-creamy layer status. Some old caste certificates skip this; those are not enough.
4. **Name spellings** — child's and parent's names must match the birth certificate and Aadhaar exactly. Small mismatches trigger objections; get an affidavit or correction early.
5. **Seal and signature** — Tehsildar/SDM/District Magistrate office, with a proper seal. A printout without attestation is just paper.

And scan it well: blurry phone photos of certificates get flagged during uploads. (The [defence-quota documents guide](/blog/defence-quota-documents-sainik-schools) covers the same scanning discipline for quota paperwork.)

## What getting it wrong costs

- Your category claim can be rejected at **document verification during counselling** — after months of preparation and a cleared exam.
- An admission offer can be **cancelled at the school reporting stage** if the certificate fails scrutiny.
- You cannot fix a two-year-old certificate the night before reporting. Re-issue takes weeks, and counselling rounds do not wait.

The fix is cheap and boring: one visit to the Tehsildar's office — or one online application — in October 2026. Do the boring thing now.

## FAQs

**Q1. My OBC certificate was issued in 2024. Can I use it for AISSEE 2027?**
Almost certainly not. Central OBC-NCL certificates are treated as valid for one year — get one issued on or after 1 April 2026. The AISSEE 2027 bulletin is not out yet, so confirm the exact wording when it releases.

**Q2. My caste is OBC in my state's list. Is that enough for AISSEE?**
Not by itself. AISSEE is a central-level admission, so your caste needs to appear in the Central List of OBCs (ncbc.nic.in). Check the central list before you claim the benefit.

**Q3. Who issues the OBC-NCL certificate, and how long does it take?**
The Tehsildar, SDM, or District Magistrate's office in your district — apply online through your state's e-District/e-Seva portal, or in person. Processing usually takes 14 to 30 days, so apply in October 2026, not December.

**Q4. Our family income crossed ₹8 lakh last year. What should we do?**
Then you no longer meet the non-creamy layer condition (parental income below ₹8 lakh p.a. for three consecutive financial years, excluding salary and agricultural income). Do not claim OBC-NCL — apply under the General category instead.

**Q5. The name on my certificate is spelled slightly differently from the Aadhaar. Is that a problem?**
Yes — name mismatches are one of the most common reasons documents get flagged at verification. Get the spelling corrected, or carry a legal affidavit for a minor mismatch.

**Q6. When exactly should I get the fresh certificate for AISSEE 2027?**
Now — October/November 2026, so it stays fresh through registration, counselling, and school reporting.
