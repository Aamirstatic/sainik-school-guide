## One school, one exam — and why that changes your strategy

Parents searching for "Sainik Schools in Kerala" usually expect a list. The honest answer is shorter: Kerala has **one school under the Sainik Schools Society** — **Sainik School Kazhakootam**, near Thiruvananthapuram. Every Kerala family aiming for a Society school through AISSEE is aiming at the same campus — concentrated competition, but simpler planning: one school, one campus, one preference to get right.

This is the latest in our state series — see also our [Gujarat guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-gujarat-complete-guide-2027/) and [Odisha guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-odisha-complete-guide-2027/). The exam is national; the quota maths is local.

## At a glance: Sainik School Kazhakootam

The school started functioning on 20 January 1962 in army barracks at Pangode, Thiruvananthapuram, with 120 students. The foundation stone of its own campus at Kazhakootam was laid by V. K. Krishna Menon, India's first Defence Minister, on 5 February 1962, and the school shifted there in 1964. The first principal was Lt Col B. K. Somaiah.

| Fact | Detail |

| Founded | 20 January 1962 (Pangode barracks); moved to Kazhakootam in 1964 |

| Affiliation | School profiles describe it as CBSE-affiliated |

## How admission works: AISSEE first, Kazhakootam second

A common misunderstanding, and it costs families every year: you do not apply "to Kazhakootam." You apply to AISSEE, the national entrance exam conducted by NTA. Only after the result does counselling — AISSAC, run by the Sainik Schools Society — turn your rank into a school. For 2027, the pattern to plan around (all EXPECTED until the bulletin lands) is:

| Stage | What happens | Status for 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| AISSEE written exam | Class 6: 150 minutes, 125 questions, 300 marks. Class 9: 180 minutes, 150 questions, 400 marks. No negative marking. | Exam date 31 January 2027, tentative per NTA calendar |
| Qualifying marks | 25% in each section and 40% aggregate (not applicable to SC/ST candidates) | 2026 bulletin rule, expected to repeat |
| AISSAC counselling | Online preference filling, seat allotment in rounds, then document verification and medical examination | After the result; no counselling fee in the 2026 pattern |
| Age, Class 6 | 10–12 years on 31 March 2027; studying in Class 5 | Projected window: born 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017 |
| Age, Class 9 | 13–15 years on 31 March 2027; studying in Class 8 | Projected window: born 1 April 2012 – 31 March 2014 |

Class 5 now means Class 6 entry; Class 8 means Class 9 entry — but Class 9 has far fewer seats nationally, so competition there is sharper.

## The quota maths every Kerala parent must understand

Seats at Kazhakootam are not one open pool. They are divided first by state, then by category.

| Slice | Share | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Kerala (home state) | 67% | A Kerala-domiciled child competes here first — this is your main pool |
| Other states | 33% | Everyone else, including Kerala children who miss the home-state cut, in later rounds |
| SC | 15% | Within the reservation structure |
| ST | 7.5% | Within the reservation structure |
| OBC-NCL | 27% | Within the reservation structure |
| Defence wards | 25% of the remainder | After the category slices, for children of serving and ex-service personnel |
| Girls (Class 6) | 10% of vacancies or 10 seats, whichever is more | Kazhakootam has had girl boarders since 2021 |

Two practical consequences. First, your domicile certificate is your entry ticket to the 67% pool — get it right and early. Second, preference order matters enormously: if Kazhakootam is genuinely your first choice, rank it first. Our [AISSAC preference guide](/blog/how-to-choose-school-preferences-aissac-counselling/) covers the home-state-versus-dream-school trade-off in detail.

## Fees and seats: what this guide deliberately does not quote

You will find portal pages quoting Kazhakootam seat counts and annual fees to the rupee. This guide does not repeat them, and here is why: the figures conflict across portals, none of them is the school's own 2027 notice, and a family that budgets on a wrong fee or chases a wrong seat count makes worse decisions than a family that waits for the real numbers.

What you can rely on: the AISSEE 2027 bulletin (exam fee, pattern, eligibility), the counselling seat matrix (actual vacancies by class and category), and the school's own fee notice (boarding and tuition). Until those appear, treat every portal figure as a rumour with a decimal point.

## Life at Kazhakootam: what your child is actually signing up for

Boarding at Kazhakootam is not "school with a hostel." Cadets live in houses named after leaders and heroes, follow a structured day mixing academics with physical training, sports, NCC activities and co-curricular life, and stay on campus through the term. Since 1962 the school's purpose has been to prepare boys — and now girls too — for the National Defence Academy and the officer cadre.

Weigh that honestly. Kazhakootam is a superb launchpad for a defence career, but it is also a disciplined residential life for an 11-year-old, far from home, with limited phone access and a routine that does not bend for one child. Visit the campus if you can, talk to parents of current cadets — not just coaching centres — and ask your child plainly whether they want this life, not just whether they can pass the exam.

## Kerala parent's checklist before the bulletin drops

1. **Domicile certificate** — Kerala domicile puts your child in the 67% pool; check the spelling matches the birth certificate.
2. **Birth and category certificates** — these decide age eligibility and reservation claims; one mismatch can cost a seat.
3. **Current class record** — confirm Class 5 (for Class 6 entry) or Class 8 (for Class 9 entry).
4. **Photograph and signature** — fresh, correctly sized, plain background; bad uploads are the silliest way to lose a year.
5. **Preference thinking** — decide now whether Kazhakootam is first choice; do not improvise during counselling week.
6. **Mock-test routine** — one full-length timed paper a week beats ten guidebooks read passively.
