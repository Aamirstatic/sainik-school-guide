Clearing AISSEE is only half the battle. The school your child actually gets depends on **AISSAC** — the All India Sainik Schools Admission Counselling run by the Sainik Schools Society on **pesa.ncog.gov.in**. It is online, free, and usually runs for about **six rounds**. And the order of your school preferences matters more than most parents realise.

## Understand the quotas before you order anything

For the 33 established Sainik Schools, seats are divided by two axes at once:

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Home-state quota | **67%** of seats go to candidates domiciled in the school's state/UT |
| Other-states quota | **33%** go to candidates from all other states/UTs |
| SC | 15% reservation |
| ST | 7.5% reservation |
| OBC-NCL | 27% reservation |
| Defence wards | 25% of the remaining seats (serving personnel and ex-servicemen) |
| Girls | **10% of vacancies or 10 seats**, whichever is more (Class 6) |

The practical takeaway: if you are domiciled in the state where a school sits, you are competing inside a 67% pool instead of the 33% pool. A school 50 km away in your own state is often a smarter top preference than a famous school two states away. For New Sainik Schools, the **40% AISSEE route** (covered in our explainer on the [new Sainik Schools routes](/blog/new-sainik-schools-ppp-model-2027/)) works differently — check the vacancies appendix of the bulletin before ordering.

## The 6 rules of ordering your preferences

1. **Order by genuine preference, not by cutoffs.** Within your quota and category, AISSAC allots the highest school on *your* list whose cutoff you clear. Putting a school you would never send your child to at the top is self-sabotage; putting it low harms nobody.
2. **Fill the maximum preferences allowed.** More entries mean more rounds can upgrade you. An empty lower half is a wasted safety net.
3. **Mix aspirational and realistic.** One or two dream schools at the top, then schools where your child's mock-test scores comfortably beat last year's published cutoffs. The Society publishes the marks of the *first and last* candidates called for medicals, school-wise and category-wise — use those as your reality check.
4. **Weigh home-state schools heavily.** The 67% pool is simply bigger. Two schools you like equally should be ordered with the home-state one higher.
5. **Factor in the medical.** After the initial call list comes document verification and the **medical examination** — standards are published in the bulletin appendix, and a rejection at this stage ends that round's allotment. Read the [medical test guide](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/) before finalising preferences; there is no point preferring a school your child would be medically rejected from.
6. **Do not freeze your list too early.** AISSAC allows editing during the choice-filling window. Update your order once school-wise tentative vacancies are published in the bulletin appendix.

## Three strategy templates, pick yours

| | **Safe** | **Balanced** | **Ambitious** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Mock scores near last year's cutoff | Scores 10–20 marks above cutoffs | Scores 30+ marks above cutoffs |
| Top 2 | One dream school + one home-state school at/above your score | Two dream schools | Dream schools only |
| Middle | Home-state schools within your score band | Home-state + nearby new Sainik Schools | Home-state schools at your band |
| Bottom | Maximum allowed, including new Sainik Schools with 40% route | Maximum allowed | Maximum allowed (safety) |
| Key discipline | Resist reordering dream schools down in panic | Re-check cutoffs after every round | Never leave the bottom empty "because I will definitely get round 1" |

## Round-by-round: how upgrades actually work

AISSAC allots seats round after round against your list. If round 1 gives you preference #5, rounds 2–6 can slide you up to #4, #3, and so on as higher-scoring candidates vacate seats or seats unlock. Two implications:

- **Stay in the counselling** even after an early allotment you are not thrilled about. Exiting early locks in disappointment.
- **Never game the system by withholding acceptance steps** — missing document verification or the medical deadline forfeits the allotted seat and can bar you from later rounds.

## Pre-submission checklist

- [ ] Domicile certificate matches the state you are claiming home quota in
- [ ] Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/Defence) issued by the competent authority and within validity
- [ ] Maximum preferences filled — no blanks at the bottom
- [ ] Home-state schools ranked above out-of-state schools of equal appeal
- [ ] Medical standards read and cleared mentally against the bulletin appendix
- [ ] New Sainik Schools' 40% route understood before including them
- [ ] List re-checked after tentative vacancy numbers are published

## FAQs

**What is AISSAC counselling for AISSEE?**
AISSAC is the online e-counselling conducted by the Sainik Schools Society for admission to Sainik Schools after AISSEE. It runs on pesa.ncog.gov.in, is free of cost, and usually completes in about six rounds.

**Does my domicile decide my quota in AISSAC?**
Yes. In the 33 established Sainik Schools, 67% of seats are reserved for candidates from the school's home state or UT, decided by the domicile certificate you submit — not by the city where you wrote the exam.

**Should I put only my dream Sainik School in the AISSAC preference list?**
No. Fill the maximum number of preferences allowed, ordered honestly by preference, and mix aspirational and realistic schools. Rounds keep upgrading candidates, but only among schools on your list.

**What happens if I am allotted a school in an early AISSAC round?**
You go through document verification and the medical examination. If you stay in the counselling, later rounds can upgrade you to a higher-preference school on your list as seats open up.

**Is there any fee for participating in AISSAC counselling?**
No. AISSAC counselling registration itself is free; you pay the school's admission fee only after a seat is finally allotted and you accept it.
