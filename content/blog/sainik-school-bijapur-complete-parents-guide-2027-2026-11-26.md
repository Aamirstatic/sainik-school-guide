## Karnataka's first Sainik School — and the 13th in India

If your home state is Karnataka and your child is aiming for a Sainik School, there are only two Society schools to choose from — and the older one is **Sainik School Bijapur**, in Vijayapura (the city most people still call Bijapur). It was the **13th Sainik School** set up in the country, and it has been preparing cadets for the National Defence Academy since **1963**.

The school began in the humblest possible way: on **16 September 1963**, in temporary shelters in a corner of **Vijaya College**, with just **63 cadets**. The foundation stone had been laid by Prime Minister **Indira Gandhi**. Three years later, in **November 1966**, it moved to its permanent home — a **406-acre campus on Athani Road** — and the then Vice President of India, **Dr Zakir Hussain**, formally declared the campus open on **17 December 1966**. The school celebrated its Golden Jubilee in 2013, and in November 2026 it hosted the All India Sainik Schools National Games, with 544 cadets from 43 schools competing on its grounds.

Vijayapura is the old Adil Shahi capital, famous for the Gol Gumbaz — the second-largest dome in the world.

One honesty note on the houses: the school's own website lists six houses named after Karnataka's historical dynasties — **Adil Shahi, Chalukya, Hoysala, Rashtrakoota, Vijayanagar and Wodeyar**. Some older school profiles list Ganga and Kadamba instead of Adil Shahi and Wodeyar; the names appear to have changed over the years, and the list above is the one the school publishes today.

## Seats for 2027-28: 102 in Class 6, none in Class 9

This is the part of the guide that matters most — and it contains a correction and a warning. The seat numbers below come from the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin itself — Appendix VI-A for Class 6 and Appendix VI-A1 for Class 9 — and four independent portals all reproduce the same figures. They are tentative: the bulletin says vacancies can change as cadets leave.

| Entry | Boys | Girls | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 6** | 92 (63 home state + 29 other states) | 10 (5 home state + 5 other states) | **102** |
| **Class 9** | 0 | 0 | **0** |

Two things to notice. **First, the correction:** several admission portals still list 120 Class 6 seats and 13 Class 9 seats for Bijapur. Those are last year's figures. For 2027-28 the bulletin says 102 and zero. If you are planning around the old numbers, update your expectations now.

**Second, the warning:** Bijapur has **no Class 9 seats at all** this year. A Class 9 aspirant simply cannot choose Bijapur for 2027-28 — the school will not appear as an option for them. If your child is in Class 8 and Bijapur was the dream, pick another school as first preference and save the disappointment. For Class 6, on the other hand, 102 seats is a solid intake, and the 10 girls' seats are exactly 10% of it — the standard girls' quota at work.

Within each total, the standard reservation applies: 67% for Karnataka-domicile candidates, 33% for other states; within those, 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% OBC-NCL, and 25% of the remainder for children of serving and ex-servicemen. Our [Karnataka guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-karnataka-complete-guide-2027/) explains how the 67% home-state rule plays out for a Karnataka child, and how Bijapur compares with the state's other Society school, Kodagu.

## Admission: only through AISSEE, and the clock is ticking

There is no other route into Bijapur — no management quota, no direct admission, no agent who can sell a seat. Anyone offering one is running a scam. Admission is through AISSEE alone, run by NTA:

- **Apply by:** Wednesday, **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**, at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. The fee — ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST — must be paid by the same hour.
- **Late window:** 3–6 December with an extra ₹500. Our [late-fee warning](/news/aissee-2027-6-days-left-late-fee-window-risks-2026-11-26/) explains why it is a rescue boat, not a plan — and our [last-weekend plan](/news/aissee-2027-6-days-left-last-weekend-finish-form-sunday-2026-11-26/) shows how to finish the form by Sunday 29 November instead.
- **Correction window:** 7–9 December, for permitted changes only.
- **Exam city:** announced 11 January 2027. **Admit card:** 25 January 2027. **Exam:** Sunday, **31 January 2027**.
- **Eligibility:** Class 6 — age 10–12 on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017), studying in Class 5. Class 9 — age 13–15 (born 1 April 2012 – 31 March 2014), Class 8 passed — but again, Bijapur has no Class 9 seats this year.

The exam itself is 150 minutes, 125 questions, 300 marks for Class 6 — and maths carries 150 of those 300 marks. Half the paper is maths. That is the single most useful fact in this guide for a parent planning the next two months of preparation.

## Six houses, and a day that starts at 5 AM

Bijapur's six boarding houses — Adil Shahi, Chalukya, Hoysala, Rashtrakoota, Vijayanagar and Wodeyar — are named after the dynasties that shaped Karnataka, and the house is the centre of a cadet's life: inter-house competitions, shared routines, seniors looking after juniors. Girls live in the **Rani Channamma Hostel**, named after the warrior queen of Kittur.

The daily routine, as published by the school, is the standard Sainik School rhythm — worth showing your child before the form goes in, because this is the life they are choosing:

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 5:00 AM | Reveille, then PT |
| 7:25–7:55 AM | Breakfast |
| 8:00–8:30 AM | Assembly |
| 8:30 AM–1:40 PM | Classes |
| 4:00–5:30 PM | Games and inter-house competitions |
| 6:30–8:15 PM | Evening prep |
| 10:15–10:30 PM | Roll call, lights out |

That is a long, structured day for an 11-year-old, and it is the same every day. If your child has never lived away from home, a campus visit before you put Bijapur first on the form will tell you more than any guide can.

## Fees: what is reported, and what is not verified

**Fees:** for 2026-27, the school's own fee document puts the total at **₹2,14,555** for a new Class 6 cadet — tuition ₹1,28,615, diet charges ₹53,690, and pocket money and clothing ₹32,250 — payable in two instalments through SBI Collect. The Government of Karnataka scholarship is paid directly into the cadet's bank account; parents pay the full fee regardless. The 2027-28 figure has not been published yet, and fees rise every year, so confirm the current figure from the school before budgeting.

**Cutoff:** no verified Bijapur cutoff for AISSEE 2026 is quoted here. Cut-off tables for 2024 and 2025 exist, but the published versions do not show category-wise marks in a form we can verify, and quoting a guessed number would be worse than quoting none. The qualifying bar itself is fixed by NTA: 25% in each section and 40% aggregate (not applicable to SC/ST). If you are weighing Bijapur against another boarding option, our [AISSEE vs JNVST comparison](/blog/aissee-vs-jnvst-two-national-boarding-exams-compared/) lays out how the two national boarding exams differ.

## Is Bijapur the right first choice for your child?

Ask three questions. **First, is Karnataka your home state?** If yes, Bijapur is the natural first choice — 67% of its seats are reserved for Karnataka children, and it is the state's oldest Society school. If no, remember you are competing for the 33% other-states share, and a school in your own home state may be the smarter first preference.

**Second, is your child entering Class 6?** For Class 6, Bijapur's 102 seats make it a strong, realistic first choice. For Class 9, there is nothing to apply for this year — do not waste a preference on it.

**Third, can your child live the routine?** A 5 AM start, a full day of classes, games and prep, and a dormitory far from home. For a child who thrives on structure, it is the making of them. For one who does not, no school's history will compensate.

If the answers line up, the form closes on 2 December. Six days is enough — if the documents are ready today.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Bijapur have for 2027-28?**

Bijapur has 102 Class 6 seats — 92 for boys (63 Karnataka domicile and 29 other states) and 10 for girls (5 Karnataka and 5 other states). It has no Class 9 seats at all for 2027-28, so a Class 9 aspirant cannot choose Bijapur this year. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin, Appendices VI-A and VI-A1.

**When was Sainik School Bijapur founded?**

It started on 16 September 1963 in a corner of Vijaya College, Bijapur, as the 13th Sainik School in India. The foundation stone was laid by Prime Minister Indira Gandhi, and the present 406-acre campus on Athani Road was inaugurated by Vice President Dr Zakir Hussain on 17 December 1966.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Bijapur?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8 — but Bijapur has no Class 9 seats this year.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Bijapur?**

For 2026-27, the school's own fee document puts the total at Rs 2,14,555 for a new Class 6 cadet — tuition Rs 1,28,615, diet charges Rs 53,690, and pocket money and clothing Rs 32,250. The 2027-28 figure has not been published yet, so confirm it from the school before budgeting.

**Does Sainik School Bijapur admit girls?**

Yes — 10 girls' seats in Class 6 for 2027-28, five for Karnataka girls and five for girls from other states. Girls live in the Rani Channamma Hostel, named after the warrior queen of Kittur. There are no girls' seats in Class 9 this year, because there are no Class 9 seats at all.

**What is the advantage of Karnataka domicile for Bijapur admission?**

67 percent of seats are reserved for Karnataka-domicile candidates and 33 percent for candidates from other states. A Karnataka child competes mainly on the Karnataka merit list for their category — which is why the domicile certificate matters so much.
