Every year, between the AISSEE registration window closing and the admit cards releasing, a quiet wave of panic moves through parent groups: "Application under scrutiny." "Photo flagged for re-upload." "Payment not received." Most of these panics have the same handful of causes — and most are preventable. Here are the eight real reasons AISSEE applications get rejected, and what to do about each.

## 1. The photo doesn't meet specs (the #1 rejection trigger)

NTA's photo verification is automated and unforgiving. For AISSEE 2026, the candidate's photograph had to be a **JPG between 10 KB and 200 KB**, recent, against a **plain white background**, with **about 80% face coverage and both ears visible**. No caps, no masks, no sunglasses; no Polaroid or computer-generated images. Selfies with filters, cropped family photos, old photos and hand-written names on the print are the four classic triggers.

**Fix:** If flagged, respond to the re-upload notice NTA sends to your registered email/mobile immediately — a missed notice can harden into a rejection.

## 2. Signature mismatch or invalid signature

Sign in **running hand on white paper with a black pen — no capital letters**. For AISSEE 2026 the signature had to be a **JPG between 4 KB and 30 KB**. A parent who signs for the child, a signature in block capitals, or a tiny signature zoomed into pixelation — all get flagged.

**Fix:** Same route as the photo — re-upload within the correction window, and practise one signature the child can reproduce at the centre.

## 3. Fee deducted but form shows "not paid"

This is the most stressful one, because money has left your account. Bank settlement with NTA can take up to **48 hours** to reflect. Do **not** pay a second time in a panic within those 48 hours — a duplicate payment is its own mess to untangle, and NTA treats duplicate applications harshly.

**Fix:** Wait 48 hours, re-check the application status. Keep the bank transaction reference, UTR number and every screenshot — they are your evidence if the payment is disputed. If the status still shows unpaid, re-pay once through the portal and dispute the first deduction with your bank.

## 4. Name or date of birth doesn't match the birth certificate

The form's name and DOB fields are compared **character by character** against the birth certificate. "Aarav" vs "Aaraav", a DOB off by one digit — these are rejection-grade errors, and they often originate from a birth certificate that was never corrected. Our [birth certificate correction guide](/blog/birth-certificate-corrections-before-aissee-form-2026-10-16/) walks through fixing the certificate before the form opens.

**Fix:** Name and DOB are exactly the fields most likely to be **locked** after submission. Get them right on day one.

## 5. Age eligibility failure

This one has no fix. Age eligibility is non-negotiable — 🔶 EXPECTED for AISSEE 2027 (from 2026 patterns): Class 6 candidates must be born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017**, and Class 9 candidates between **1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014**. Check the DOB against these bands **before** paying.

## 6. Category certificate problems

Claiming SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS with a certificate that is not in the prescribed format or not issued by the competent authority invalidates the category claim — and can void the application. OBC certificates also need to be in the **Central list**. Follow exactly the format the information bulletin prescribes; our [application documents checklist](/news/aissee-2027-application-documents-checklist-2026-10-06/) covers the full upload set.

**Fix:** If flagged in the correction window, re-upload the correct document. If you cannot produce a valid certificate, consider proceeding as General rather than risk rejection (your call — check the bulletin rules).

## 7. Duplicate applications

One child, **one application**. NTA treats multiple applications from the same candidate as a violation and can reject all of them — usually an accident when a parent loses the application number and starts over.

**Fix:** Never start a second application. Save the application number immediately. If a duplicate was already submitted, resolve it through the NTA helpline before the deadline — never submit a third.

## 8. Mobile number and email nobody checks

NTA sends correction notices, re-upload requests and the admit card link to the **registered mobile and email**. A number that changes after the form, or an email nobody opens, turns every fixable problem above into a fatal one.

**Fix:** Use a number and email the family will keep active through at least March 2027. Treat them as permanent on day one.

## The rejection-proof form-day checklist

- [ ] Photo: fresh studio shot, white background, 80% face, ears visible, name + date printed by studio, JPG 50–200 KB
- [ ] Signature: running hand, black pen on white paper, no capitals, JPG 4–30 KB — and a signature the child can reproduce at the centre
- [ ] Name and DOB: letter-for-letter match with the birth certificate — triple-check before submit
- [ ] Age: DOB inside the 1 Apr 2015–31 Mar 2017 (Class 6) or 1 Apr 2012–31 Mar 2014 (Class 9) band
- [ ] Category certificate: prescribed format, competent authority, Central list for OBC
- [ ] Fee: ₹850 / ₹700 — one payment, one application, save every receipt
- [ ] Mobile + email: active, checked daily, kept through March 2027
- [ ] The confirmation page: downloaded and saved the moment the form submits — it is your proof of a complete application.

Spend an hour on this checklist first, and the notification you get in February will be an admit card — not a rejection.

## FAQs

**Can I fix my AISSEE application after submitting it?**

Yes, if NTA opens a correction window. For AISSEE 2026 it ran 12–14 November 2025 and allowed edits to most fields except identity-critical ones. A similar window is expected for 2027 — but name, DOB and mobile may be locked, so get them right on day one.

**What happens if I submit two AISSEE applications for my child?**

Don't. NTA treats duplicate applications as a violation and can reject all of them. One child, one application, one fee payment. If a duplicate was submitted by mistake, resolve it through the NTA helpline before the deadline — never submit a third.

**My fee was deducted but the AISSEE form shows "payment not received". What should I do?**

Do not pay again immediately. Bank settlement can take up to 48 hours — keep the transaction reference, re-check after two days, and re-pay only if the status is still unpaid. Save every receipt.

**Will my AISSEE form be rejected if my photo doesn't match the specs?**

It can be flagged for re-upload or rejected. For AISSEE 2026: JPG 10–200 KB, white background, 80% face, ears visible. Follow the same until the 2027 bulletin says otherwise.

**What documents cause AISSEE application rejection?**

Most often: a category certificate in the wrong format or from the wrong authority, a birth certificate whose name/DOB doesn't match the form exactly, or a missing PwD certificate. Match the birth certificate letter by letter.

**What if my child fails the age eligibility for AISSEE 2027?**

There is no relaxation or waiver — age eligibility is non-negotiable. Expected bands for 2027: born 1 April 2015–31 March 2017 for Class 6, 1 April 2012–31 March 2014 for Class 9. Check before paying.
