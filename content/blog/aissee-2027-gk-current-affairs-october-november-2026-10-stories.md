## Why these ten stories, and why now

Look, the GK section is the one parents underestimate. In Class 6 it is **25 questions for 50 marks** — one-sixth of the whole paper. Your child needs at least **25% in every section**, which means 7 correct answers in GK just to stay in the race. And unlike Maths, GK cannot be "figured out" in the exam hall. Either your child has seen the fact before, or they haven't.

Here are the ten stories. [If you want the full GK method first, read our section strategy.](/blog/aissee-class-6-gk-section-strategy-50-marks/)

## 1. AISSEE 2027 is on — form closes 2 December

The National Testing Agency opened AISSEE 2027 applications on **3 November 2026**, and the window closes on **2 December at 11:50 PM**. The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with a Rs 500 extra fee. The fee is Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and Rs 700 for SC/ST.

**Likely question:** "When is AISSEE 2027 conducted?" — and increasingly, "What is the last date to apply?" Learn both dates, not just the exam day.

## 2. Assam gets its second Sainik School

On **17 November 2026**, Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma laid the foundation stone for the state's second Sainik School at **Langvoku in Karbi Anglong district**. The Rs 336-crore campus will hold **640 cadets** and take about 30 months to build. Assam's first Sainik School is at Goalpara, opened in 1964.

**Likely question:** "Where is Assam's second Sainik School being built?" The answer is Langvoku, Karbi Anglong — and the Rs 336 crore figure is the number examiners love.

## 3. The National Games closed at Bijapur on 7 November

The fourth All India Sainik Schools National Games ran from **3 to 7 November 2026** at Sainik School Bijapur, with Raksha Rajya Mantri **Sanjay Seth** as chief guest at the closing ceremony. Five titles were decided: **Ghorakhal** won junior boys' basketball, **Vikasa** won junior girls' basketball, **Amaravatinagar** won junior boys' volleyball, **Jhansi** won the debate championship and **Kapurthala** won the quiz.

**Likely question:** "Which school won the debate championship at the 2026 National Games?" — Jhansi.

## 4. Kunjpura puts 12 cadets on the NDA and TES merit lists

Sainik School Kunjpura placed **10 cadets on the NDA-157 merit list** and 2 on the TES-56 list. The best NDA rank was Cadet Vijay at **AIR 136**, followed by Arpit Siwach (168). In TES-56, Bhavishya secured AIR 40 and Mukul AIR 87.

**Likely question:** "How many Kunjpura cadets made the NDA-157 merit list?" — ten. This is the kind of proud-school fact that appears in GK because it is also a morale story.

## 5. Two Punglwa cadets crack NDA-157

Sainik School Punglwa in Nagaland added two names to the NDA-157 merit list: Cadet **Dev Dagar at AIR 61** and Cadet **Anshik Yadav at AIR 125**. Small school, big ranks.

**Likely question:** "Which Sainik School cadet secured AIR 61 in NDA-157?" — Dev Dagar of Punglwa.

**Likely question:** "How many new Sainik Schools were announced under the PPP model?" — 100.

## 9. JNVST 2027 is seven days away — and the paper has a new section

The Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test for Class 6 is on **Saturday, 28 November 2026, 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM** — one week from today. Admit cards have been out since 28 October. This year's paper has a **new Environmental Studies section** — 20 EVS questions alongside 20 Mental Ability questions in the first hour. There is no negative marking; each correct answer carries 1.25 marks. [Our EVS explainer covers what changed.](/news/jnvst-2027-class-6-exam-november-28-18-days-left-admit-card-evs-2026-11-10/)

The **63rd annual athletic meet** of Sainik School Kapurthala began on Thursday, 20 November 2026, with Major General P K Singh as chief guest. Olympians **Deepika and Reena Khokhar** attended as special guests of honour.

## How to revise these in the last 71 days

Do this instead:

1. **One story a day, ten minutes.** Read the story together at dinner. Ask your child to retell it in two sentences: what happened, and the number.
2. **Turn each story into one question.** Write it on a flashcard — question on the front, answer on the back. Ten cards by the end of next week.
3. **Sunday revision.** Every Sunday, run through all ten cards. Any card your child misses twice goes on the fridge.
4. **Stop at ten.** Current affairs is a bottomless pit, and the GK section is only 50 marks. [Our balance guide explains how much current affairs is enough — and when to stop.](/blog/aissee-gk-static-vs-current-affairs-balance-plan/)

Nobody can predict the exact GK questions. But the pattern is consistent — recent defence-school news, numbers, places and firsts. These ten stories are the closest thing to a syllabus the current-affairs section has.

## FAQs

**How many marks is the GK section in AISSEE 2027 Class 6?**

25 questions for 50 marks, in a 150-minute paper of 125 questions and 300 marks. A child needs at least 25 percent in every section, so at least 7 correct answers in GK, to stay in the race.

**Does AISSEE ask current affairs questions?**

Yes. The Class 6 GK section mixes static GK with recent events, and the Class 9 Social Science and General Science sections also carry current-affairs questions. Recent defence and education news is the highest-yield current affairs for this exam.

**Which current affairs should my child revise for AISSEE 2027?**

Start with defence-school news itself: the AISSEE 2027 notification, new Sainik Schools, the National Games results, NDA merit-list successes and the JNVST and RIMCEE dates. Then add major national events from the last six months.

**How should a Class 6 child revise current affairs in the last 71 days?**

Ten minutes a day is enough. Read one story, note the who, where and number, and turn it into one question. Revisit the list every Sunday. Do not memorise whole articles — the exam tests one fact per question.

**Is the JNVST exam also on 28 November 2026?**

Yes. The JNVST Class 6 exam is on Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM. Its admit cards have been out since 28 October, and this year's paper has a new Environmental Studies section.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 exam?**

Sunday, 6 December 2026. The exam city slip was released on 17 November, and the admit card is expected 5 to 7 days before the exam, roughly 29 November to 1 December.
