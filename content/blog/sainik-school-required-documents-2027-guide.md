## Why Document Verification Is the Most Critical Step in AISSEE Admission

Every year, **thousands of AISSEE applicants are rejected** not because of merit, but because of incomplete or incorrect documentation. The Sainik School Society (SSS) and NTA follow a strict verification process, and missing a single document can delay or disqualify your application.

**Common document-related rejection reasons:**
- Age proof mismatch with birth certificate
- Caste certificate expired or issued by wrong authority
- Bonafide certificate not from current school
- Photographs not meeting specifications (size, background, pose)
- Income certificate not valid for the financial year

This guide ensures you submit **complete, valid, and properly formatted** documents in your first attempt.

## Age Proof: Birth Certificate Guidelines

### ✅ Accepted Age Proof Documents

| Document | Issuing Authority | Accepted? | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-----------|-------|
| Birth Certificate | Municipal Corporation/ municipal council | ✅ **Primary** | Must show exact DOB |
| Hospital Birth Record | Government hospital | ✅ **Supporting** | Cross-verification only |
| Aadhaar Card | UIDAI | ✅ **Supporting** | Must match birth certificate |
| Passport | Passport office | ✅ **Supporting** | If available |
| School Leaving Certificate | Previous school | ⚠️ **Conditional** | Only with DOB mentioned |

### ❌ Not Accepted as Primary Proof

| Document | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Affidavit on stamp paper | Not sufficient alone |
| Ration card | Not accepted as age proof |
| Mark sheet/grade card | Does not show DOB |
| PAN card | Not accepted for age verification |

### 🔑 Birth Certificate Rules

- **Must be issued** by Municipal Corporation, Taluk office, or village panchayat
- **Must show exact date of birth** in DD/MM/YYYY format
- **Must be in English or Hindi** (translated copy if regional language)
- **Must match** other documents (Aadhaar, school records)
- **Original required** — photocopies attested by gazetted officer accepted

### 📅 Age Calculation Reference Date

- **Cutoff date: March 31, 2027**
- Age calculated as on this date
- If child turns 10/13 on March 30, 2027 → ✅ Eligible
- If child turns 10/13 on April 1, 2027 → ❌ Not eligible

## Bonafide Certificate: Current School Verification

### Who Must Submit This?

| Scenario | Required? | From Which School? |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Class 6 Applicant** | ✅ Yes | Current school (Class 5) |
| **Class 9 Applicant** | ✅ Yes | Current school (Class 8) |
| **Private Candidate** | ✅ Yes | Last school attended |
| **Open School** | ✅ Yes | Accredited institution |

### 📋 Bonafide Certificate Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Format** | On school letterhead |
| **Issued by** | Principal or Headmaster |
| **Must include** | Student name, class, DOB, admission date |
| **Must state** | "This certifies that [Name] is studying in Class [X]" |
| **Signature** | Principal's signature + school stamp |
| **Date** | Current academic session date |
| **Validity** | Must be issued within last 6 months |

### 🏫 How to Get Bonafide Certificate

1. Apply to your **current school principal** (written application)
2. Mention **purpose**: "AISSEE 2027 admission to Sainik School"
3. Allow **5-7 working days** for processing
4. Collect **original + 2 photocopies**
5. Get **attestation** from district education officer (if required)

## Caste Certificate: SC/ST/OBC Requirements

### ✅ When Caste Certificate Is Required

| Category | Required? | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **General** | ❌ No | No certificate needed |
| **OBC** | ✅ Yes (if seeking reservation) | 27% seat reservation |
| **SC** | ✅ Yes (if seeking reservation) | 15% seat reservation |
| **ST** | ✅ Yes (if seeking reservation) | 7.5% seat reservation |
| **Defence** | ✅ Yes (if claiming quota) | 25% seat reservation |

### 📄 Caste Certificate Guidelines

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Issuing Authority** | District Magistrate, Commissioner, or Sub-Divisional Officer |
| **Validity** | Must be valid as of AISSEE application date |
| **Format** | Central or state government format |
| **Must include** | Caste name, family details, issuing authority, date |
| **For OBC** | Must have **creamy layer certificate** if applicable |
| **Income limit** | OBC non-creamy layer certificate required |

### 🚫 Common Caste Certificate Rejection Reasons

- Certificate expired (valid for 1 year in some states)
- Issued by unauthorized authority
- Name mismatch with application form
- Missing family income details (for OBC)
- Photocopy of original (original must be produced at verification)

### 💡 OBC Creamy Layer Rules

- **If parents' income > ₹8 lakh/year** → Creamy layer certificate required
- **If parents' income ≤ ₹8 lakh/year** → Non-creamy layer certificate
- **Creamy layer candidates** do not get OBC reservation
- Certificate must be issued **within current financial year**

## Income Certificate: Fee Concession & EWS Quota

### ✅ When Income Certificate Is Required

| Scenario | Required? | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **General category** | ✅ Yes (if applying for fee concession) | Financial aid eligibility |
| **EWS category** | ✅ Yes | 10% reservation seats |
| **SC/ST** | ❌ No | Already have reservation |
| **Defence** | ⚠️ Yes (if claiming fee concession) | Additional benefit |

### 📄 Income Certificate Guidelines

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Issuing Authority** | Tehsildar, District Magistrate, or Revenue Officer |
| **Validity** | Current financial year (April 1 – March 31) |
| **Income Limit** | Varies by state/central govt norms |
| **Format** | On official letterhead with stamp |
| **Must include** | Annual income, family details, issuing authority |

### 💰 Income Limits for Fee Concession (2027)

| Category | Annual Income Limit | Reservation |
|----------|---------------------|-------------|
| **EWS** | Up to ₹8 lakh | 10% seats |
| **OBC-NCL** | Up to ₹8 lakh | 27% seats |
| **General** | Up to ₹8 lakh | Fee concession only |
| **SC/ST** | No income limit | 15% + 7.5% seats |

### 📝 How to Apply for Income Certificate

1. Visit **Tehsildar office** or **online portal** (state-specific)
2. Submit **application form** with income details
3. Attach **supporting documents** (pay slips, ITR, employer certificate)
4. **Verification** takes 7-15 days
5. Collect **original + 2 photocopies**

## Photographs: Specifications & Guidelines

### 📸 Photograph Requirements

| Specification | Requirement |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Size** | 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (passport size) |
| **Background** | White or light blue background |
| **Pose** | Full face, front view, neutral expression |
| **Clothing** | School uniform or formal attire |
| **No glasses** | Spectacles not allowed (if worn normally) |
| **No hat/Cap** | Religious headwear permitted |
| **File format** | JPG/JPEG (if online submission) |
| **File size** | 20KB – 50KB (if uploading online) |
| **Print size** | 2.5 cm × 3.5 cm on application form |

### 📷 Common Photography Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Selfies or informal photos
- ❌ Wearing sunglasses or dark glasses
- ❌ Photo taken against colorful background
- ❌ Photo edited or filtered
- ❌ Group photos or photos with others
- ❌ Wearing mask or covering face
- ❌ Digital morphed or edited photos

### 📋 Photograph Submission Checklist

- [ ] 6 identical photographs
- [ ] White background, clear face visibility
- [ ] Taken within last 3 months
- [ ] Name and class written lightly on back (if physical)
- [ ] Digital copy meets size specifications
- [ ] Spare photographs kept for interview/medical

## Medical Certificate: Physical Fitness Guidelines

### 🏥 Medical Certificate Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Issued by** | Registered medical practitioner |
| **From** | Government hospital or approved medical board |
| **Validity** | Must be current (within 3 months of application) |
| **Format** | On hospital/clinic letterhead |
| **Signature** | Doctor's registration number must be visible |

### 📋 Medical Certificate Must Certify

- [ ] Candidate is **physically fit** for Sainik School admission
- [ ] Age matches stated date of birth
- [ ] No **permanent medical conditions** that would prevent attendance
- [ ] **Vision** meets minimum standards (6/6 or 6/9 with correction)
- [ ] **Hearing** within normal range
- [ ] **Physical growth** appropriate for age
- [ ] Any **disability** declared (if applicable)

### 🏥 Approved Medical Authorities

| Authority | Accepted? | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Government hospital CMO** | ✅ Yes | Most authoritative |
| **Civil surgeon** | ✅ Yes | State government |
| **Registered MBBS doctor** | ✅ Yes | Private practice allowed |
| **Clinical officer** | ⚠️ Conditional | Must have MBBS backup |
| **Patogym** | ❌ No | Not accepted as primary |

### 📋 Medical Test Components

1. **Height and weight** measurement
2. **Chest measurement** (inspiration/expiration)
3. **Vision test** (distance and near)
4. **Hearing test**
5. **Blood pressure** reading
6. ** urine test** (routine)
7. **ECG** (if age > 14 or medical history)
8. **General physical examination**

### 📝 Medical Certificate Format (Key Points)**

```
Sainik School Medical Certificate 2027

This is to certify that Master/Miss [Name], son/daughter of [Father's/Mother's Name],
Date of Birth: [DD/MM/YYYY], studying in Class [X], has been examined on [Date].

**Physical Fitness:** The candidate is **FIT** for admission to Sainik School.

**Medical Conditions:** [None declared / Details if any]

**Vision:** [6/6 / 6/9 with correction]

**Height:** [____ cm]

**Weight:** [____ kg]

**Chest:** [____ cm (inspiration)] / [____ cm (expiration)]

**Registration No:** [Doctor's MCI/SMC registration]

Date: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Place: [City]
Signature of Medical Officer
Stamp of Hospital/Clinic
```

## Document Verification Process: Step-by-Step

### 📅 Timeline & Checklist

| Stage | Action | Timeline | Documents Required |
|-------|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **1. Registration** | Fill AISSEE application form | Oct-Nov 2026 | All listed documents (scanned) |
| **2. Document Upload** | Upload scanned copies on NTA portal | Oct-Nov 2026 | PDF/JPG format, specified size |
| **3. Fee Payment** | Pay application fee | Oct-Nov 2026 | Payment receipt |
| **4. First Verification** | SSS verifies uploaded documents | Dec 2026 | Originals for cross-verification |
| **5. Seat Allotment** | Based on merit + category | Jan 2027 | Verified documents |
| **6. Admission** | Report to selected school | Mar-Apr 2027 | Original documents + attestation |

### 📂 Document Verification Day Checklist

**Bring the following originals + 3 photocopies each:**

1. ✅ **Birth certificate** (original + 3 photocopies)
2. ✅ **Bonafide certificate** from current school (original + 3 photocopies)
3. ✅ **Domicile/residence proof** (original + 3 photocopies)
4. ✅ **Caste certificate** (if applicable, original + 3 photocopies)
5. ✅ **Income certificate** (if applicable, original + 3 photocopies)
6. ✅ **Category certificate** (if applicable, original + 3 photocopies)
7. ✅ **6 recent photographs** (plus 2 extras)
8. ✅ **Medical certificate** (original + 3 photocopies)
9. ✅ **Aadhaar card** (copy, for reference)
10. ✅ **School mark sheets** (Class 5 / Class 8, original + 3 photocopies)

| Issue | Frequency | Prevention |

## FAQs: Sainik School Documents 2027

### Q1: What if my birth certificate is in a regional language?
**A:** Submit the **original + English translation** attested by a certified translator. The translation must include all details from the original.

### Q2: Can I submit photocopies of documents initially?
**A:** Yes, scanned copies for online application. **Originals must be produced** at document verification and admission.

### Q3: What if I don't have a birth certificate?
**A:** Apply for a **late birth registration** at municipal corporation immediately. Alternatively, **hospital discharge certificate** + **Aadhaar** may be accepted with SSS permission.

### Q4: How many photographs are required?
**A:** **6 photographs** for the application form + **2 spare photographs** for interview/medical. Total: 8 photos recommended.

### Q5: Can I get age relaxation for defense category?
**A:** **No.** Age limits are same for all categories. However, **25% seats are reserved** for defence wards within the same age limit.

### Q6: What if my caste certificate is from a different state?
**A:** Caste certificate must be from the **state of residence** or **central government** format. Out-of-state certificates may be accepted with additional verification.

### Q6: Is income certificate mandatory for general category?
**A:** Not mandatory for admission, but **required if applying for fee concession** or EWS quota. General category without fee concession does not need income certificate.

### Q8: Can documents be submitted after the deadline?
**A:** Generally **no**. Late submissions are not accepted. However, **genuine medical emergencies** may be considered with permission from SSS — contact the admission cell.

### Q9: What documents are needed for girl candidate admission?
**A:** Same as boys **plus**:
- Proof of gender (if required)
- 25% seat reservation claim (if applicable)
- No age relaxation for girls

### Q10: Where can I verify my documents before submission?
**A:** 
- Use our [Document Checklist Tool](/tools/document-checklist/)
- Contact **Sainik School Society** helpdesk
- Consult **educational consultant** specialized in AISSEE
- Visit **concerned Sainik School** for pre-admission guidance
