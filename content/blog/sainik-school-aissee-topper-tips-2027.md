> **🔄 Last verified: 26 September 2026** — Exam ✅ 31 January 2027 (NTA calendar). Neeche di gayi strategy pichhle cycles ke merit patterns aur experienced educators ke observations par based hai. **Humne koi fictional "topper interview" nahi banaya** — jo sites AIR 1 ke naam par man-gadhant quotes chhaapti hain, unse savdhaan rahiye.

Topper koi alien nahi hota. AISSEE ki merit list mein aane wale bachchon mein ek cheez common hoti hai — **woh zyada der tak sahi cheezein karte hain.** Wahi 10 aadatein yahan de raha hoon. Inmein se koi bhi "secret trick" nahi hai — sab mehnat ke tareeke hain.

## Aadat 1: Maths ko roz ka pehla subject banate hain

Class 6 mein 150/300, Class 9 mein 200/400 — maths aadha paper hai. Toppers maths ko **roz subah** karte hain, jab dimaag sabse fresh hota hai. Target: roz 30 questions, bina naaga.

## Aadat 2: Previous papers ko textbook ki tarah treat karte hain

Kam se kam **pichhle 5 saal ke papers**, time limit ke saath. Questions repeat nahi hote, lekin **question-type repeat hota hai.** Jo bachcha 5 saal ke papers kar leta hai, use exam hall mein koi question "naya" nahi lagta. [Papers yahan milenge](/blog/aissee-previous-year-papers-pdf/).

## Aadat 3: Mistake notebook rakhte hain

Har galat question ek copy mein — sawal, sahi jawab, aur **galti kyun hui** (concept clear nahi tha? jaldi thi? OMR mein golat gola bhara?). Exam se pehle ye notebook 3 baar revise hoti hai. Ye aadat toppers ko repeat mistakes se bachati hai.

## Aadat 4: OMR practice karte hain

Paper OMR sheet par hota hai ✅. Pehli baar OMR bharne wala bachcha 10–15 minute waste karta hai. Toppers ghar par **print karke OMR-style mein** mock dete hain. [Free mock sets](/blog/aissee-2027-mock-test-free-practice-sets/) yahan hain.

## Aadat 5: Har section ko qualifying se upar rakhte hain

Qualifying: har section mein 25% + total 40% ✅. Lekin toppers jaante hain — **merit qualifying se nahi, competition se banti hai.** Koi section "chhod" nahi dete, kyunki ek weak section merit se bahar kar sakta hai.

## Aadat 6: GK ko roz thoda-thoda karte hain

GK ratta maarne ka subject nahi — **aadat** ka subject hai. Roz 15 minute: newspaper headlines + Lucent's ke 20 one-liners. 4 mahine mein 2000+ facts taiyar. Exam ✅ 31 Jan 2027 ko hai — Aug 2026 se Jan 2027 ki current affairs cover kijiye.

## Aadat 7: Revision ka fixed system hai

Padha hua bhoolna normal hai — isliye toppers **spaced revision** karte hain: aaj padha → 3 din baad revise → 10 din baad phir revise. Bina system ke revision "sab kuch ek baar aur padh lena" hota hai, jo kaam nahi karta.

## Aadat 8: Mock test ka analysis karte hain (sirf marks nahi dekhte)

Mock dene ke baad 1 ghanta analysis: kaunsa section slow tha? Kahan silly mistakes huin? Time kahan waste hua? **Analysis ke bina mock sirf timepass hai.**

## Aadat 9: Sehat aur neend se samjhauta nahi karte

8 ghante neend, 30 minute khel. Thaka dimaag na concept samajhta hai, na yaad rakhta hai. Exam se pehle wali raat ko **der tak padhne wale** aksar paper mein slow hote hain.

## Aadat 10: Parents ka support lete hain, pressure nahi

Ye aadat parents ke liye hai: **bachche par chillana band kijiye.** Toppers ke ghar mein aam taur par ek cheez common hoti hai — parents ne mahaul diya, pressure nahi. Mock mein kam marks aayein to "aur mehnat kar" nahi, "kahan problem hui, saath mein dekhte hain" kahiye.

## FAQs

**Q1. Bina coaching ke top rank aa sakta hai?**
Haan. AISSEE ka syllabus NCERT-based hai — disciplined self-study kaafi hai. Coaching discipline deta hai, knowledge aapko khud banana padta hai.

**Q2. Din mein kitne ghante padhna chahiye?**
Quality > quantity. 4–5 ghante focused padhai, 8 ghante distracted padhai se behtar hai.

**Q3. Kitne mock tests enough hain?**
Kam se kam 10–12 full mocks + 5 saal ke previous papers. Isse kam mein exam temperament nahi banta.

**Q4. Last month mein kya strategy honi chahiye?**
Naya kuch nahi — sirf revision aur mocks. Confidence high rakhiye.

**Q5. Agar mock mein marks kam aa rahe hain to?**
Trend dekhiye, ek test nahi. Har mock ke baad analysis kijiye aur weak topics par kaam kijiye. Marks dheere-dheere badhenge.
