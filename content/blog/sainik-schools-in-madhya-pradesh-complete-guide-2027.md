## One school, one route: Rewa

Parents searching for "Sainik Schools in Madhya Pradesh" usually expect a list. The honest answer is shorter than that: Madhya Pradesh has **one** school under the Sainik Schools Society — **Sainik School Rewa**. It is not a new school and not a franchise. Founded in **1962**, it is among the oldest Sainik Schools in the country, on a **260-acre campus** in Rewa (PIN 486001), affiliated to CBSE, teaching Classes 6 to 12 in English medium, with hostels, a library, laboratories, a gymnasium, sports facilities and NCC on campus.

That single-school fact shapes everything else in this guide. There is no second MP school to use as a backup choice inside the state. An MP family applies to Rewa through AISSEE, and — because the AISSEE counselling system lets candidates list preferences across schools — can also list schools in neighbouring states as lower preferences. But the home-state advantage, explained below, only applies at Rewa.

## How admission works: AISSEE, then counselling

Admission to Rewa is through the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE), conducted by the National Testing Agency. Class 6 entry is for children currently in Class 5, aged **10–12 years as of 31 March 2027**; Class 9 entry is for children in Class 8, aged **13–15 years as of 31 March 2027**. The written paper carries **300 marks for Class 6** and **400 marks for Class 9**, with no negative marking, followed by e-counselling, document verification and a medical examination.

The reservation pattern is where MP domicile pays off. **67% of seats go to candidates from the home state** — Madhya Pradesh, in Rewa's case — and **33% to candidates from other states**. Within that split, the standard pattern reserves 15% for SC, 7.5% for ST and 27% for OBC-NCL candidates, with a defence-category share of the remaining seats and 10% of seats for girls. That is why the domicile certificate matters so much for an MP family: it is the document that puts your child in the 67% pool rather than the far more competitive 33% pool. Our state-wise [domicile certificate guide](/blog/domicile-certificate-online-apply-state-wise-process-2026-10-11/) walks through the MP process, and our [caste certificate guide](/blog/caste-certificate-format-aissee-reservation-claims/) covers the category document that must match it.

One caution on documents: the AISSEE 2027 bulletin has not been released yet, so the dates, application fee (Rs 850 for General/OBC/Defence and Rs 700 for SC/ST in the 2026 cycle) and centre list below are EXPECTED from the last cycle, not confirmed for 2027. Re-check them the day the bulletin drops rather than trusting any coaching-site date in the meantime.

## Fees: read this before you budget

Here is where parents get tripped up, so we will be blunt: **published fee figures for Rewa do not agree with each other.** One secondary fee compilation lists Rewa at about **Rs 1,15,698** per year; Careers360's 2026 fee structure lists it at about **Rs 1,58,381**. Both cannot be the current figure, and neither is the school's own notice. Treat any single number quoted online as reported, and budget on the higher end until the school's own prospectus or fee notice confirms the figure for 2027–28.

What is not in doubt is that the fee covers tuition plus boarding, lodging, uniform, books and miscellaneous charges, and that scholarships and fee concessions exist for eligible categories — our [fee-concession guide](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) explains the income-linked slabs. If the fee is a stretch, apply for the concession in parallel with admission, not after the joining letter arrives.

## Exam centres and the road to Rewa

Earlier cycles have reported **Bhopal, Gwalior, Jabalpur and Rewa** as the AISSEE centres in Madhya Pradesh. That list is reported, not confirmed for 2027 — centre lists change between bulletins, so confirm it in the 2027 bulletin and choose the centre closest to home rather than the one nearest the school. The centre is where the exam happens; the school is where your child would live.

## MP parent's checklist before the bulletin drops

- **Domicile certificate** for Madhya Pradesh, in the child's name, from the competent authority — this is your 67% pool ticket.
- **Caste or category certificate** if claiming reservation, in the AISSEE appendix format.
- **Birth certificate** matching the school record exactly — name-spelling mismatches are the commonest form-stage headache, and our [Aadhaar-mismatch guide](/blog/aadhaar-mismatch-aissee-form-fix-before-submit/) explains the birth-certificate-wins rule.
- **School choice order** drafted in advance: Rewa first if you are an MP family, with realistic neighbouring-state options below it.
- **Fee budget** set at the higher reported figure until the school confirms 2027–28 fees.

## FAQs

**How many Sainik Schools are there in Madhya Pradesh?**

One school under the Sainik Schools Society: Sainik School Rewa, founded in 1962. Admission to it is through AISSEE, conducted by NTA.

**What is the home-state quota for Sainik School Rewa?**

67% of seats are reserved for candidates from the home state, Madhya Pradesh, and 33% for candidates from other states, under the standard Sainik School reservation pattern.

**What are the age limits for admission to Sainik School Rewa?**

For Class 6, age 10 to 12 years as of 31 March 2027, and for Class 9, age 13 to 15 years as of 31 March 2027, under the standard AISSEE eligibility pattern.

**What is the annual fee at Sainik School Rewa?**

Published secondary figures conflict, ranging from about Rs 1,15,698 to about Rs 1,58,381 per year, so treat any single figure as reported, not confirmed. Check the school's own fee notice or prospectus before budgeting.

**Can girls apply to Sainik School Rewa?**

Yes. Sainik School Rewa admits both boys and girls, and girls are admitted under the 10% girls' quota pattern that applies across Sainik Schools.

**Which exam centres in Madhya Pradesh host AISSEE?**

Bhopal, Gwalior, Jabalpur and Rewa have been reported as MP centres in earlier cycles. Confirm the 2027 centre list in the AISSEE 2027 bulletin when it is released, since centres can change.
