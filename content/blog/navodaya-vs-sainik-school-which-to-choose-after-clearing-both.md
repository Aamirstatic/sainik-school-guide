## A good problem to have — if you think about it now

Here is a situation thousands of families will face in the spring of 2027. Your child sits the JNVST on 28 November, then the AISSEE on 31 January. Both papers go well. Then the results arrive — and both schools offer a seat.

It sounds like a luxury problem. It isn't, quite. The two results do not arrive together, the joining deadlines do not wait for each other, and the two schools are genuinely different places: one free and close to home, the other built for the NDA and charging fees. Parents who decide the criteria *now*, while both exams are still ahead, make a calm choice in March. Parents who wait until the offer letters arrive make a panicked one.

So let us do the comparison properly — not the exam comparison (we covered [AISSEE vs JNVST as exams](/blog/aissee-vs-jnvst-two-national-boarding-exams-compared/) earlier), but the choice itself: two offer letters, one child.

## The comparison, side by side

| What matters | Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV) | Sainik School |
|---|---|---|
| Entry point | Class 6 only (through JNVST) | Class 6 and Class 9 (through AISSEE) |
| Exam this cycle | JNVST, 28 November 2026 | AISSEE, 31 January 2027 |
| Exam size | 80 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours | Class 6: 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes |
| Negative marking | None (1.25 marks per correct answer) | None |
| Cost | Free — education, boarding, lodging, uniform, textbooks | Fees apply and vary by school; check the prospectus |
| Seats | Maximum 80 per JNV, in your home district | About 3,160 tentative Class 6 seats across the 33 established schools, plus new schools |
| Girls | At least one-third of seats | 10% of vacancies or 10 seats, whichever is more (33 schools) |
| Location | Your district's JNV — close to home | Could be in your state or far from it, depending on your choices |
| What it is for | Free quality residential education, mainly for rural talent | Defence-oriented schooling with the NDA as its north star |
| After Class 12 | Any career; NDA possible, not the institutional focus | NDA is the designed path, with SSB-oriented grooming built in |

Read that table twice. The differences that matter most are not academic at all: money, distance, and purpose.

## The five questions that actually decide it

Forget league tables. Ask these five, in this order, and the answer usually becomes obvious.

**1. Can the family comfortably pay Sainik School fees for seven years?**
This is the first question because it is the one parents avoid. Navodaya costs nothing through Class 8. Sainik School fees recur every year, plus uniform, travel home, and the rest — and the amount differs by school. Scholarships exist, but plan on the full fee. If paying it for seven years would strain the family, that is not a failure; it is information. A free JNV seat is a gift, not a consolation prize.

**2. How far from home is the Sainik School your child would get?**
A JNV is in your district. A Sainik School could be two states away. For an 11-year-old, distance is not a number on a map — it is how often they come home, how quickly you reach them when they are ill, and how lonely the first term feels. Some children thrive far from home. Others need the first year closer. Be honest about which child you have.

**3. Whose NDA dream is it?**
This one needs courage. Sainik School is the direct road to the NDA — the drill, the physical routine, the whole culture points there. If the NDA is your child's own dream, said in their own words, Sainik School is the right bet. If the dream is yours and your child is merely obedient, seven years of military routine will not manufacture a passion. Navodaya keeps every door open, including the NDA, without making one door the whole house.

**4. Does your child want the military life, or just a good school?**
Related, but different. Some children love uniforms, parades and early mornings. Some just want good teachers and a hostel with friends. A child who wants the life will bloom in a Sainik School; a child who only wants a good school may be happier — and perform better — in a JNV, where the pressure is academic rather than regimental.

**5. What does Class 9 offer?**
Here is the safety valve most parents miss. If your child joins a JNV in Class 6, they can still attempt AISSEE for Class 9 Sainik entry later. The choice is not a life sentence. Equally, a child who starts at a Sainik School and finds it is not for them can return to a day school. Decide for the next three years, not for the next thirty.

## The timeline: when each answer actually arrives

| Event | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| JNVST 2027 exam (Class 6) | 28 November 2026 | ✅ Confirmed |
| AISSEE 2027 exam | 31 January 2027 | ✅ Confirmed |
| JNVST result | Expected by end of March 2027 | 🔶 Expected |
| AISSEE result | Not announced yet | To be announced |
| Sainik School counselling (AISSAC) | Spring 2027 | 🔶 Expected |

Notice the gap. The JNVST result is expected by the end of March, while the AISSEE result date has not even been announced. You may be asked to accept a JNV seat and complete joining formalities before you know the Sainik result. Do not assume you can hold a JNV seat indefinitely while waiting — ask the JNV about its joining deadline and what happens if you accept and later withdraw. Get the answer in writing, not from a neighbour.

And one practical warning that belongs in every guide we write this season: no agent can reserve a seat in either school. Admissions run on merit lists and counselling — [JNVST through NVS](https://navodaya.gov.in) and [AISSEE through AISSAC](/blog/how-seats-are-allotted-sainik-school-counselling/). Anyone asking for money to "confirm" a seat is running a scam.

## What to do now, while both exams are still ahead

Nothing in this guide should change how your child prepares this week. The JNVST is nine days away; the AISSEE is seventy-three. Both papers reward the same things — steady revision, one mock a week, and a calm household.

What should change is the family conversation. Sit down, together, and talk through the five questions above. Write the answers down. Then, when the results come, you will not be deciding under pressure — you will be reading back a decision you already made, calmly, when you had time to think.

Both schools are good schools. That is the whole point. The wrong choice is not picking the "lesser" one; it is picking under panic, or for someone else's dream, or for a reason you never said out loud. Decide the criteria now, and the offer letters will only confirm what you already knew.

## FAQs

**Can my child apply to both JNVST and AISSEE in the same year?**
Yes. They are separate exams run by different bodies — JNVST by Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti for Class 6, and AISSEE by NTA for Sainik Schools in Class 6 and Class 9. Many children sit both, and clearing one does not cancel the other.

**Is Navodaya Vidyalaya really free?**
Yes, for Class 6 to 8. Education, boarding, lodging, uniform and textbooks are free under the NVS prospectus. From Class 9 to 12 a Vidyalaya Vikas Nidhi of 600 rupees a month is collected, but girls, SC/ST, Divyang and BPL students are exempt.

**Do Sainik Schools charge fees?**
Yes. Sainik Schools charge annual fees, and the amount varies from school to school — check the individual school's prospectus for the exact figure. Scholarships exist, including state and defence scholarships, but the fee is real and recurring, unlike Navodaya.

**Which result comes first, JNVST or AISSEE?**
JNVST. The JNVST 2027 result is expected by the end of March 2027, while the AISSEE 2027 result date has not been announced yet. That means you may have to accept or hold a JNV seat before the Sainik result arrives — ask the JNV about its joining deadline rather than assuming you can wait.

**Is Sainik School better than Navodaya for the NDA?**
Sainik Schools are built around the NDA road — the routine, the physical training and the culture all point that way. Navodaya is an excellent academic boarding school, and its students also reach the NDA, but NDA preparation is not its institutional purpose. If the NDA is the child's own dream, Sainik School is the more direct route.

**Does choosing one school close the door on the other forever?**
No. A child who joins a JNV can still attempt AISSEE for Class 9 entry later, and a child in a Sainik School can attempt other exams as they grow. The Class 6 choice is important, but it is not a life sentence — children switch paths more often than parents fear.
