From tonight, the countdown starts: **55 days to 6 December 2026**, the day of the RIMCEE Stage-I written exam for Class 8 admission to Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun (July 2027 term). The application correction window [closes tonight](/news/rimcee-correction-window-closes-tonight-2026-10-11/) — after that, the only thing between your child and the exam hall is preparation. And RIMC is a different animal from AISSEE: its Maths and English sections are **descriptive**, not MCQ. A child who can pick the right option in AISSEE can still fail RIMC if they can't write a full solution or a clean paragraph.

Here is the 55-day battle plan, week by week.

## The 8-week battle plan

| Week | Dates | Focus | Daily drill |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | 11–17 Oct | Diagnose + fix the form tonight | One descriptive Maths solution written fully; check [correction window status](/news/rimcee-correction-window-closes-tonight-2026-10-11/) tonight |
| 2 | 18–24 Oct | Maths: arithmetic + algebra | 10 descriptive solutions/day, show every step |
| 3 | 25–31 Oct | English: grammar + comprehension | One unseen passage + 15 grammar items daily |
| 4 | 1–7 Nov | Maths: geometry + mensuration | Diagrams drawn by hand, formulae from memory |
| 5 | 8–14 Nov | English: writing + vocabulary | One essay/letter daily; 20 new words |
| 6 | 15–21 Nov | GK: current affairs + static GK | 50 MCQ questions/day from NCERT Class 6–7 books |
| 7 | 22–28 Nov | Full papers under exam conditions | One full written paper every 2 days; watch for the city intimation slip (12–15 days before) |
| 8 | 29 Nov–6 Dec | Revise mistakes, download admit card | Only revise error notebook; admit card expected 5–7 days before the exam |

Notice what's missing: no new topics in the final 10 days. Descriptive exams are won by clean presentation of what you already know, not by last-minute syllabus chasing.

## Why the descriptive sections decide everything

Most children applying for RIMC have done MCQ-style entrance exams before. RIMC's [written paper is a different test](/news/rimcee-2026-dec-6-exam-pattern-vs-aissee-2026-10-02/) — it rewards the child who writes solutions like a teacher would accept them. Three rules for the next 55 days:

1. **Show every step in Maths.** Examiners can't award method marks for a correct answer alone — but a wrong final answer with clean method can still earn most of the credit. Every practice solution should look like a board-exam answer.
2. **Write English daily.** Comprehension, grammar, and composition can't be crammed. Fifteen minutes of daily paragraph writing does more than a weekend of grammar rules.
3. **Keep GK moving, but light.** GK is MCQ, which means breadth beats depth. Read NCERT science and social science books from Class 6–7, plus the last three months of current affairs, and you'll cover most of it.

## The error notebook (your highest-ROI tool)

Get a dedicated notebook today. Every mistake — a formula slip in Maths, a tense error in English, a GK fact you missed — goes in with the date and the correct version. In Week 8, this notebook replaces all books. Children who maintain one consistently report it as the single most useful thing they carried into the exam hall.

## After 6 December: the road ahead (CONFIRMED)

The journey doesn't end at the exam hall, so pace the family for the full process:

- **City intimation slip:** 12–15 days before 6 December (late November 2026)
- **Admit card:** 5–7 days before the exam (early December 2026)
- **Stage-I result:** 20 February 2027
- **Stage-II viva voce:** tentatively April 2027, about 4–6 weeks after the written result
- **Medical examination:** follows the viva voce

Qualifying the written stage only opens the door; the viva and medical decide the seat. That is exactly why RIMC looks for all-round readiness — and why this plan starts with writing practice, not memorisation.

## FAQs

**When is the RIMC entrance exam 2026?**
Stage-I, the written exam, is on 6 December 2026 in pen-and-paper mode across 39 cities. The admit card will be available 5 to 7 days before the exam, and the city intimation slip about 12 to 15 days before.

**What is the RIMC exam pattern?**
The paper has descriptive Mathematics and English sections plus multiple-choice questions in General Knowledge. It is a written exam, not fully MCQ — so handwriting practice matters as much as concepts.

**Who can apply for RIMC admission for the July 2027 term?**
Candidates born between 2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015 who are studying in Class 7 or have passed Class 7 from a recognised school. The correction window for this cycle closes tonight, 11 October 2026.

**What happens after the RIMC written exam?**
The Stage-I result is scheduled for 20 February 2027. Qualifiers then face Stage-II, the viva voce, tentatively in April 2027 — roughly 4 to 6 weeks after the written result — followed by a medical examination.

**Can a Class 6 AISSEE student also prepare for RIMC?**
Yes, with care. The syllabi overlap in Maths, English and GK, but RIMC's descriptive sections and Class 7 level demand extra writing practice. Read our comparison of the two exam patterns to balance both.

**When will the RIMC admit card be released?**
Admit cards will be issued 5 to 7 days before the exam, which points to the end of November 2026. The city intimation slip comes earlier, 12 to 15 days before 6 December.
