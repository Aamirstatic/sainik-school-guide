## Manipur's only Sainik School — and one of the oldest

If you live in Manipur and your child dreams of a uniform, there is exactly one door in your own state: Sainik School Imphal. It was established on **7 October 1971**, which makes it one of the older Sainik Schools in India — older than most of the schools in the Northeast, and with more than five decades of cadets behind it.

The school began humbly, in war sheds near Tulihal Airport, and moved to its present campus at Pangei on **5 March 1974**. Today it sits on **268 acres on Pukhao Road, about 8 km from Imphal** — far enough from the city to feel like a world of its own, close enough that a parent can reach the gate in under half an hour.

That matters more than it sounds. A Sainik School is a full residential school: your child lives on campus from Class 6. The distance from home is not a line on a map — it is the difference between a weekend visit and a planned journey. For Manipur families, Imphal is the near option. For families from outside the state, it is a real journey, and worth planning before the form, not after the allotment.

## The seats: 70 in Class 6, and a very small door in Class 9

For 2027-28, Imphal has **70 Class 6 seats** and just **5 Class 9 seats**. Here is the split, exactly as the AISSEE 2027 bulletin lists it:

| Class 6 seats | Boys | Girls | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manipur domicile (67%) | 42 | 5 | 47 |
| Other states (33%) | 18 | 5 | 23 |
| **Total** | **60** | **10** | **70** |

Class 9 is a different story: **5 seats, all for boys** — 4 for Manipur domicile and 1 for other states, with no girls' seats this year. If your child is in Class 8, treat Class 9 at Imphal as a long shot and keep a second school preference ready. (Our [Class 9 seat guide](/news/aissee-2027-8-days-left-class-9-only-731-seats-2026-11-24/) explains why 731 seats across 33 schools makes Class 9 the harder door everywhere.)

Within the home-state boys' seats, the bulletin breaks the 42 down by category: **SC 6, ST 3, OBC-NCL 12, Defence 5, General 16**. The same reservation structure — SC 15%, ST 7.5%, OBC-NCL 27%, then 25% of the remainder for defence wards — applies across the board, under the Sainik Schools SOP.

Two things to notice. First, the home-state advantage is real but concentrated: 47 of 70 Class 6 seats go to Manipur children, but every Manipur aspirant is aiming at the same one school. Second, girls have a genuine route here — 10 of the 70 Class 6 seats, five for Manipur girls and five for girls from other states, under the rule that at least 10% of Class 6 seats (or 10 seats, whichever is higher) are reserved for girls. That is the same rule behind the [351 girls' seats across all 33 schools](/news/aissee-2027-7-days-left-girls-351-class-6-seats-2026-11-25/) this year.

## The fees: budget for the higher figure

Honestly, the fee is the one number we cannot give you as a fact. Published totals differ: one portal reproduces the school's 2026-27 sheet at **₹2,48,435 for a new Class 6 cadet**, while another directory lists about **₹2,26,881**. The gap is probably the difference between fee heads counted — tuition, diet, clothing, miscellaneous and deposits — and the year of the sheet.

So budget for the higher figure, and confirm the 2027-28 total from the school before you pay anything. State and Ministry of Defence scholarships exist for eligible families, and the income certificate that supports a scholarship claim is worth arranging now, not in April. What we can say plainly is that Sainik School fees are all-inclusive by design — tuition, food, lodging and uniform sit in one bill — which is why the number looks large next to a day school's fee and small next to a private boarding school's.

## How admission works

There is exactly one route in: **AISSEE 2027, on Sunday, 31 January 2027**, conducted by NTA. The form is open at **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society from 3 November to 2 December 2026 (11:50 PM)**, with a late-fee window of 3–6 December (₹500 extra) and a correction window of 7–9 December. The application fee is **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards and **₹700** for SC/ST.

Maths carries half the paper in both classes. That is the single most useful fact in this whole guide for a parent planning the next two months: if your child is weak in maths, that is where the marks are.

## Life at Imphal: houses, NCC and the long valley days

The school runs on the classic Sainik pattern. Five houses — **Tikendrajit, Netaji, Raman, Shivaji and Tagore** — with Tikendrajit traditionally the holding house for new Class 6 cadets. The house is your child's second family: the housemaster is guide and guardian, and inter-house competition runs through sport, academics and drill.

The **NCC Independent Company** — Junior and Senior Division, with Army, Navy and Air Force wings — is integral to school life, not an optional extra. For a child aiming at the NDA, those years of NCC drill, camps and leadership tasks are the quiet part of the preparation that no coaching centre sells.

A typical Sainik School day runs from reveille and morning PT through classes, prep, games and lights-out — the exact Imphal timetable is not published online, so ask the school for it on your visit. Football is part of the school's sporting life, as it is across Manipur, and the 268-acre campus has room for the full range of games and athletics. The school is a member of the Indian Public Schools' Conference, which brings its cadets into inter-school competition beyond the Sainik circuit.

## The honest notes — read these before you apply

**The Class 9 door is tiny.** Five seats, no girls' seats. If Class 9 is your child's only plan, have a backup school or a backup exam ready.

**Manipur is one school, one shot.** Unlike UP or Bihar, there is no second Sainik School in the state to fall back on. Your preference list should still include schools in neighbouring states — [Sainik School East Siang in Arunachal Pradesh](/blog/sainik-school-east-siang-complete-parents-guide-2027-2026-11-26/) is the nearest, with 85 Class 6 seats this year — because AISSAC counselling allots on merit and preference order, not on hope.

**Plan the journey, not just the form.** If you live outside Manipur, check current travel conditions before planning a school visit or the admission journey. The school is 8 km from Imphal on Pukhao Road, and the nearest rail and air links run through the city.

**Fees need confirming.** As above: two published totals disagree by about ₹21,000. Get the 2027-28 figure from the school in writing before you budget the year.

## What to ask the school before you apply

- What is the confirmed 2027-28 fee total, and what does it include?
- Which scholarships do Manipur-domicile cadets actually receive, and what income certificate is needed?
- How are girls housed on campus, and who supervises the girls' hostel?
- What is the daily timetable, and how much supervised prep time do Class 6 cadets get?
- What happens on the first reporting day — documents, medicals, fee payment?

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Imphal have for 2027-28?**
Imphal has 70 Class 6 seats — 60 for boys (42 Manipur domicile and 18 other states) and 10 for girls (5 Manipur and 5 other states) — and 5 Class 9 seats, all for boys. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin, Appendices VI-A and VI-A1.

**When was Sainik School Imphal founded?**
It was established on 7 October 1971, in war sheds near Tulihal Airport, and moved to its present 268-acre campus at Pangei on 5 March 1974. It is Manipur's only Sainik School and one of the older schools in the Sainik Schools Society.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Imphal?**
Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Imphal?**
Reported figures differ by source: one portal reproduces the school's 2026-27 sheet at Rs 2,48,435 for a new Class 6 cadet, while another directory lists about Rs 2,26,881. Treat either as a budgeting guide, not a bill, and confirm the 2027-28 figure from the school before paying.

**Does Sainik School Imphal admit girls?**
Yes — 10 girls' seats in Class 6 for 2027-28, five for Manipur girls and five for girls from other states. There are no girls' seats in Class 9 this year.

**What is the home-state advantage for Manipur children at Imphal?**
67 percent of seats are reserved for Manipur-domicile children. In Class 6 that is 47 of the 70 seats — 42 boys and 5 girls. A Manipur child competes first against other Manipur children for those seats, not against the whole country.
