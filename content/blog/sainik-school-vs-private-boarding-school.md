## When to Choose Sainik School

✅ Your child wants a career in the **Indian Armed Forces**
✅ You want **affordable** residential education with military discipline
✅ Your child is physically active and enjoys sports
✅ You value **discipline and structured routine**
✅ You want your child to develop **leadership and resilience**

## When to Choose Private Boarding

✅ Your child wants **flexibility** in career choice (not committed to NDA)
✅ You prefer **premium infrastructure** and smaller class sizes
✅ You want **international curriculum** (IB/IGCSE) options
✅ Budget is not a primary concern
✅ Your child's interests are in arts, music, or non-military activities

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is Sainik School better than DPS?
For **NDA preparation**, Sainik School is far better. For **overall career flexibility**, DPS and similar schools offer more options.

### Can I prepare for NDA from a private boarding school?
**Yes**, but it's harder. Sainik Schools have built-in NDA coaching, NCC training, and a 30-40% NDA selection rate. Private schools don't offer this.

### Which has better discipline — Sainik School or private boarding?
**Sainik School** — by a significant margin. Military-style discipline is the core of Sainik School education.

### Is the fee difference justified?
If your goal is NDA, Sainik School at ₹2-2.5L/year is a **better investment** than a private school at ₹5-10L/year. The NDA selection rate justifies the difference.
