# Sainik School vs Private Boarding School: Fees, Discipline Aur Future — Kaun Sa Sahi?

"Kya private boarding school Sainik School se behtar hai?" — ye sawaal un parents ka hai jinke paas budget hai aur wo options taul rahe hain. Jawab seedha hai: **dono ka maksad alag hai, isliye tulna maksad se karein, naam se nahi.**

## Seedhi Tulna

| Aadhar | Sainik School | Private Boarding School |
|---|---|---|
| **Maksad** | Defence officer taiyaar karna | All-round education (career khula) |
| **Admission** | AISSEE merit (✅ 31 Jan 2027 tentative) | Entrance/interview + fees bharne ki capacity |
| **Fees** | 🔶 ~₹1–1.5 lakh/saal | 🔶 ₹2–6 lakh/saal (school par nirbhar) |
| **Scholarship** | State govt schemes (SC/ST/defence) | Limited, merit-based (kuch schools mein) |
| **Discipline** | Military-style, strict | Strict lekin military-style nahi |
| **Physical training** | Roz compulsory | Sports hote hain, intensity kam |
| **NCC** | Strong culture | Kuch schools mein |
| **NDA advantage** | Built-in ecosystem | Nahi (khud taiyaari karni hogi) |
| **Peer group** | Merit se aaye bachche (diverse background) | Aam taur par affluent families |

## Fees: Asli Hisaab

Private boarding schools ki fees aam taur par Sainik School se **2x–4x zyada** hoti hai. Ek mid-range boarding school bhi saal ka ₹3–4 lakh le leta hai; top schools ₹6 lakh se upar chale jate hain. Aur wahan scholarship ke options limited hain.

Sainik School mein 🔶 ₹1–1.5 lakh/saal mein tuition + hostel + mess + activities sab shaamil hai — aur [scholarship](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) ke baad ye aur kam ho jata hai.

**Hisab lagayein:** Class 6 se Class 12 tak (7 saal) — Sainik School mein kul kharcha scholarship ke baad private boarding ke 1-2 saal ke barabar bhi na pade. Ye farq chhota nahi hai.

## 7 Saal Ka Kharcha: Ek Saaf Hisab

Parents aksar sirf "saal ki fees" dekhte hain. Poora hisab lagayein (🔶 indicative figures):

| | Sainik School | Private Boarding (mid-range) |
|---|---|---|
| Saalana fees (approx) | ₹1–1.5 lakh | ₹3–4 lakh |
| 7 saal ka kul (bina hike) | ~₹7–10.5 lakh | ~₹21–28 lakh |
| Scholarship ke baad (SC/ST/defence) | Kaafi kam ho sakta hai | Limited relief |
| Annual hike | Stable rehti hai | 5–10% aam hai |

7 saal mein **₹15–20 lakh ka farq** pad sakta hai — ye koi chhoti rakam nahi. Aur is rashi se bachche ki aage ki padhai (coaching, college) aaram se ho sakti hai. Isliye "mehnga = behtar" wali soch yahan kaam nahi karti.

## Discipline Aur Environment

- **Sainik School:** Subah 5:30 PT, fixed routine, seniors-juniors ka mentorship system, NCC drill. Bachcha "system" mein dhal jata hai.
- **Private boarding:** Discipline hoti hai lekin flexibility zyada. Facilities (infrastructure, labs, international exposure) aksar behtar hoti hain.

**Hamari salah:** Agar bachcha khud-disciplined hai aur aapko facilities chahiye, to achcha private boarding bura nahi. Lekin agar aap chahte hain ki bachche mein **khud-ba-khud discipline aaye**, to Sainik School ka system bejod hai.

## Career: Kahan Kaun Jeetta Hai?

- **NDA/Defence → Sainik School, hands down.** Private boarding mein ye ecosystem hi nahi hai.
- **IIT/NEET → Dono se possible.** Private boarding mein coaching ke saath balance aasaan ho sakta hai; Sainik School mein self-study culture strong hai.
- **Foreign universities → Private boarding** (IB/IGCSE wale schools) ka edge hai.
- **Overall personality → Sainik School** ka hostel + NCC + sports combination alag level ka confidence deta hai.

## Kaun Sa Chunein?

| Situation | Salah |
|---|---|
| Defence career ka sapna | Sainik School |
| Budget ₹3L+/saal hai, global exposure chahiye | Achcha private boarding |
| Merit par admission chahiye (paisa nahi, mehnat) | Sainik School |
| Bachcha ghar se door rehne ko taiyaar nahi | Dono hi boarding hain — day-school dekhein |
| Scholarship chahiye | Sainik School (state schemes) |

## Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawaal

**Q1. Kya private boarding se NDA clear ho sakta hai?**
Ho sakta hai, lekin taiyaari bahar se karni padegi. School ka ecosystem is disha mein nahi hota.

**Q2. Sainik School ki padhai private schools se kam hoti hai?**
Nahi — CBSE curriculum hai, aur board results aam taur par achche hote hain. Farq facilities ka ho sakta hai, padhai ka nahi.

**Q3. Dono mein se kahan ragging ka dar kam hai?**
Dono mein anti-ragging rules hote hain. Sainik Schools mein senior-junior mentorship structured hoti hai.

**Q4. Kya AISSEE ke bina Sainik School mein admission ho sakta hai?**
Nahi — 33 established schools mein AISSEE hi ekmatra rasta hai. Koi management quota nahi hai.

**Q5. Private boarding ki fees har saal badhti hai?**
Aam taur par haan — 5-10% annual hike common hai. Sainik School ki fees comparatively stable rehti hai.
