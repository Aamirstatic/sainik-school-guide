## One school for the whole state — and that changes the competition

Chhattisgarh has exactly one Sainik School, and every Chhattisgarh parent chasing a defence-school seat is chasing the same campus. **Sainik School Ambikapur**, in Surguja district in the state's north, was established by the Government of India on **1 September 2008** and now runs from its permanent campus of **84 acres at Mendra Kalan**. It is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), the medium of instruction is English, and it is a fully residential school for Classes 6 to 12.

That single-school fact is the first thing to understand, because it shapes everything else in this guide. Unlike states with two or three Sainik Schools, Chhattisgarh parents have no second home-state campus to fall back on in counselling. The 67% home-state quota below is therefore not a detail — it is the whole game for a Chhattisgarh-domiciled child, and the reason an out-of-state applicant faces a much steeper climb for the same seat.

## Admission: AISSEE, and only AISSEE

There is no management quota, no donation route and no direct admission to Ambikapur. Entry is to **Class 6 and Class 9 only**, through the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE), conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for the Sainik Schools Society. After the written exam come e-counselling through AISSAC, document verification and a medical fitness test.

The age rules follow the standard AISSEE pattern used in previous cycles: **10–12 years as on 31 March 2027 for Class 6** (born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017) and **13–15 years for Class 9** (born between 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014, studying in Class 8). Treat these as EXPECTED for 2027 until the official bulletin confirms them — the notification was still awaited as of checks reported on 18–24 October 2026, with the exam expected in January 2027.

One honesty note on the school's leadership: recent profiles name different Colonel-rank principals, so this guide does not assert a current principal's name. Check the school's official website, sainikschoolambikapur.org.in, for the serving principal before quoting it anywhere.

## Seats: the 80-vs-100 conflict parents must know about

This is where Chhattisgarh guides disagree, and hiding the disagreement would mislead you. The detailed 2026-27 seat matrix published by Ezyschooling breaks Ambikapur's Class 6 seats down as follows:

| Category | Home-state (Chhattisgarh) | Other states | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boys | 49 | 21 | 70 |
| Girls | 5 | 5 | 10 |
| **Total** | **54** | **26** | **80** |

Other admission guides, however, quote **100 seats for Class 6 and 30 for Class 9**. Both cannot be the 2027 figure, and neither source is the official bulletin. The practical advice is simple: plan on the matrix figure (80) as the conservative case, and confirm the 2027 seat count in the bulletin the moment it releases. The girls' figure is consistent either way — 10 seats in Class 6 matches the 10% girls' rule that applies across Sainik Schools.

## The 67% Chhattisgarh quota — and what it means for your child

Under the Sainik School reservation system, **67% of seats are reserved for candidates domiciled in the home state** — here, Chhattisgarh — and the remaining 33% are open to candidates from other states. Within the seats, the usual reservations apply: 15% for Scheduled Castes, 7.5% for Scheduled Tribes, 27% for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), 25% of the balance for children of defence personnel, and 10% of seats for girls. Our full explainer, [the 67% home-state quota, explained](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-67-percent-explained-2026-10-17/), works through the arithmetic with examples.

For a Chhattisgarh-domiciled child, that 67% share is a real advantage — the competition pool is the state's own applicants, not the whole country. For an out-of-state child, the honest picture is the reverse: roughly a third of the seats against national competition, so the target score has to be higher. Either way, domicile paperwork matters as much as marks. A Chhattisgarh domicile certificate that is expired, mismatched with the birth certificate or issued in a parent's name instead of the child's can cost a home-state seat. Sort it now, while the notification is still awaited.

## Fees: three published figures, no verified 2027 number

Do not budget from a single number you saw on one guide site. Published secondary figures for Ambikapur conflict:

| Source type | Reported annual figure | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Older admission guides | About Rs 1,69,289 | Reported, undated |
| 2027-28 admission guide | About Rs 1,96,671 | Reported, single source |
| Careers360 school-wise fee list | Rs 2,00,252 | Reported, compiled list |

All three are secondary reports, and guides also report a pattern of up to **10% annual fee increase**, which partly explains why the numbers drift apart by year. The honest budget line is therefore a range — roughly **Rs 1.7 lakh to Rs 2.0 lakh per year** — covering tuition, boarding, lodging, uniforms, books and other charges, with scholarships and fee concessions available for eligible categories. Confirm the exact 2027 figure from the school or the AISSEE 2027 bulletin before you commit; a family that budgets Rs 1.7 lakh and is billed Rs 2.0 lakh has a problem that a five-minute check would have prevented.

## The three questions Chhattisgarh parents actually ask

**What language will my child study in?** English, per the school's profile. That matters for a child coming from a Hindi-medium or Chhattisgarhi-speaking home: the AISSEE English section is not just an exam hurdle, it is a preview of classroom life. Build English reading into the daily routine now, not in December.

**How far is it, really?** The permanent campus is at Mendra Kalan, outside Ambikapur town — older profiles put it around 18 km from Ambikapur itself. For a family in Raipur, Bilaspur or Durg, that means a long journey for every visit and every vacation pickup, on top of the boarding adjustment itself. Visit once before counselling if you can; a campus that looks fine in photographs can feel very different after a six-hour journey.

**What about NCC and sports?** NCC is integral to life at Ambikapur, with an independent company covering the Army, Navy and Air Force wings for junior and senior divisions. For a child aiming at the NDA, that exposure — drill, camps, leadership roles — is part of what the school is selling, not an optional extra.
