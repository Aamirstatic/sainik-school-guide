## Quick Summary — Sainik School vs Military School at a Glance

Both Sainik Schools and Rashtriya Military Schools (RMS) are **residential military-preparatory institutions** under the Ministry of Defence. Both prepare students for the NDA and other defence careers. But they differ significantly in scale, fees, infrastructure, and accessibility.

| Parameter | Sainik School | Military School (RMS) |
|-----------|:---:|:---:|
| **Total Schools** | 33+ (+ 69 PPP) | 5 only |
| **Established** | 1961 | 1925 |
| **Annual Fee** | ₹2.0–2.5 Lakh | ₹0.5–1.5 Lakh |
| **Entrance Exam** | AISSEE (by NTA) | RMS CET |
| **Classes** | 6 to 12 | 6 to 12 |
| **Gender** | Boys + Girls | Boys only |
| **Exam Difficulty** | Moderate | Moderate–High |
| **NDA Selection Rate** | High (30–40%) | Very High (35–45%) |
| **Application Fee** | ₹850 (General) | ₹550 (General) |

> **Bottom Line:** Sainik School is the better choice for most families — more locations (33+ vs 5), girls admitted, and better infrastructure. Military School is the better choice if **fees are the primary constraint** (saves ₹1 lakh/year, ₹6 lakh over 6 years) or if you prefer a more traditional military environment with a longer heritage.

## What Is a Sainik School?

Sainik Schools are **residential schools under the Sainik Schools Society**, an autonomous body under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India. They were established with the primary objective of preparing students academically, physically, and mentally for entry into the **National Defence Academy (NDA)** and other defence institutions.

### Key Facts About Sainik Schools

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **First School Established** | 1961 (Sainik School Korukonda & Amaravathinagar) |
| **Total Traditional Schools** | 33 across India |
| **New PPP Schools** | 69 (Public-Private Partnership model) |
| **Admission Through** | AISSEE (All India Sainik School Entrance Exam) |
| **Conducted By** | NTA (National Testing Agency) |
| **Board Affiliation** | CBSE |
| **Medium of Instruction** | English |
| **Classes Offered** | Class 6 to Class 12 |
| **Gender** | Boys + Girls (girls admitted since 2021) |
| **Total Annual Fee** | ₹2,00,000 – ₹2,50,000 |

### Sainik School Locations

Sainik Schools are spread across **almost every state in India**, making them accessible to families nationwide. Major locations include:

| Region | Sainik Schools |
|--------|---------------|
| **North** | Kapurthala (Punjab), Kunjpura (Haryana), Nagrota (J&K), Ghorakhal (Uttarakhand), Chittorgarh (Rajasthan), Rewari (Haryana), Sujanpur Tira (HP) |
| **South** | Amaravathinagar (TN), Kazhakootam (Kerala), Korukonda (AP), Kalikiri (AP), Kodagu (Karnataka), Bijapur (Karnataka) |
| **East** | Purulia (WB), Bhubaneswar (Odisha), Sambalpur (Odisha), Tilaiya (Jharkhand), Gopalganj (Bihar), Nalanda (Bihar), Goalpara (Assam) |
| **West** | Balachadi (Gujarat), Satara (Maharashtra), Chandrapur (Maharashtra) |
| **Central** | Rewa (MP), Jhansi (UP), Amethi (UP), Mainpuri (UP), Lucknow (UP) |
| **Northeast** | Punglwa (Nagaland), Imphal (Manipur), Chhingchhip (Mizoram), East Siang (Arunachal Pradesh), Mansasbal (J&K) |

**Source:** Sainik Schools Society official directory

## What Is a Rashtriya Military School (RMS)?

Rashtriya Military Schools are **residential schools directly under the Ministry of Defence**. They are older than Sainik Schools (established 1925) and have a more traditional military approach.

### Key Facts About Rashtriya Military Schools

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **First School Established** | 1925 (as King George's Royal Indian Military Schools) |
| **Total Schools** | 5 |
| **Admission Through** | RMS CET (Common Entrance Test) |
| **Conducted By** | Ministry of Defence (directly) |
| **Board Affiliation** | CBSE |
| **Medium of Instruction** | English |
| **Classes Offered** | Class 6 to Class 12 |
| **Gender** | Boys only |
| **Total Annual Fee** | ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000 |

### RMS Locations (Only 5 in India)

| School | State | Established |
|--------|-------|:-----------:|
| **RMS Chail** | Himachal Pradesh | 1925 |
| **RMS Ajmer** | Rajasthan | 1930 |
| **RMS Belgaum** | Karnataka | 1946 |
| **RMS Dholpur** | Rajasthan | 1962 |
| **RMS Bangalore** | Karnataka | 1946 |

**Source:** Ministry of Defence — Rashtriya Military Schools official data

✅ You want **multiple school options** across India

✅ You prefer **better infrastructure** (larger campuses, more facilities)

✅ Your child can prepare for **both AISSEE and RMS CET** simultaneously

## Can You Apply to Both? — Strategy for Maximum Chances

Yes, and you **should**. The exams are on different dates, and the preparation overlaps significantly.

| Exam | Expected Date | Application Window |
|------|:------------:|:-----------------:|
| **RMS CET** | December 2026 – January 2027 | September–October 2026 |
| **AISSEE** | January 2027 | October–November 2026 |

**Recommended Strategy:**
1. **Prepare for both exams simultaneously** — 80% of the syllabus overlaps
2. **Take RMS CET first** (December) — it is harder, so preparing for it makes AISSEE easier
3. **Take AISSEE in January** — by now, your child has already prepared for the harder exam
4. **If selected in both** — compare the specific school allotted and choose based on your priorities

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Which is better — Sainik School or Military School?

Neither is universally "better" — it depends on your priorities. **Sainik School** is better if you want more location options, girls admission, and better infrastructure. **Military School (RMS)** is better if fees are a concern (saves ₹1 lakh/year), you prefer traditional military discipline, and you have a son. Both have excellent NDA track records. For most families, Sainik School is the more practical choice due to 33+ locations vs only 5.

### Are girls admitted to Military Schools (RMS)?

No, **Rashtriya Military Schools do not admit girls**. Only Sainik Schools have opened their doors to girls since 2021, with a 25% reservation for girl students. If you have a daughter, Sainik School is the only option between these two types of defence-preparatory schools.

### Is the Sainik School fee worth it compared to Military School?

Over 6 years (Class 6 to 12), Sainik School costs approximately ₹12–15 lakh, while Military School costs approximately ₹5–7.5 lakh — a difference of ₹6–7.5 lakh. However, Sainik Schools offer more locations (closer to home), better infrastructure, girls admission, and contribute 30% of NDA cadets (vs ~5% from RMS). If your family can afford ₹2–2.5 lakh/year, Sainik School offers better overall value. If fees are a constraint, Military School is an excellent and more affordable alternative with a strong NDA track record.

### Can I apply to both Sainik School and Military School?

Yes, absolutely. The exams are on different dates — **RMS CET in December–January** and **AISSEE in January**. The syllabus overlaps by approximately 80%. Many parents apply to both to maximize their child's chances. Preparing for the harder RMS CET first actually makes the AISSEE easier. There is no restriction on appearing for both exams.

### Which school has a higher NDA selection rate?

Both schools have **excellent NDA selection rates** — significantly higher than the national average of 5–7%. Military Schools have a **slightly higher per-student selection rate** (~35–45%) compared to Sainik Schools (~30–40%). However, Sainik Schools contribute more officers in **absolute numbers** (~30% of all NDA cadets) due to their larger scale (33+ schools vs 5). The difference in selection rate is marginal — both schools are proven pathways to NDA.
