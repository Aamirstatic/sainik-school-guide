## Kerala's only Sainik School — and one of the oldest in India

If you are a parent in Kerala filling the AISSEE 2027 form this week, there is exactly one school in your state to put first: **Sainik School Kazhakootam**. It is Kerala's only Sainik School, and it is also one of the oldest in the country — it began functioning on **20 January 1962**, barely a year after the Sainik School idea itself was born.

That matters for a practical reason, not a sentimental one. Because Kazhakootam is the only Sainik School in Kerala, a Kerala-domicile child who opts for it competes for the **home-state share of its seats — 44 of its 75 Class 6 seats** for boys. Pick a school in another state and that advantage disappears. For most Kerala families, this school is not just the obvious first choice. It is the strategically right one too.

## A school that began in army barracks

Kazhakootam started in the barracks lent by the Indian Army at **Pangode, Thiruvananthapuram**, on 20 January 1962, with 120 boys in Classes V to VIII. The foundation stone of the new campus was laid by the then Defence Minister, **V. K. Krishna Menon** — the man who had proposed the Sainik School idea itself — on 5 February 1962. The school shifted to Kazhakootam in 1964, to a campus designed by Prof. J. C. Alexander of the College of Engineering, Trivandrum: an academic block and 11 dormitories on a 170-foot laterite cliff, with the sea on one side and the hills on the other.

The first principal was **Lt Col B. K. Somaiah** of the Indian Army. Sixty-four years on, the school's own prospectus records that around **1,000 of its alumni — "Kazhaks" — have joined the armed forces**. That is the number to hold in your head when you weigh this school: it has been feeding officers to the services since before most of today's parents were born.

One thing Kerala parents will have heard about this year: the campus land made the news in November 2026, when a proposal to allot 20 acres for a waste-to-energy plant was dropped by the city corporation after protests. The plant is off the table. The campus is not changing.

## Seats for 2027-28: 75 in Class 6, 27 in Class 9

The AISSEE 2027 bulletin lists Kazhakootam's tentative vacancies like this:

| Class | Boys (home state) | Boys (outside state) | Girls (home state) | Girls (outside state) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Class 6 | 44 | 21 | 5 | 5 | **75** |
| Class 9 | 15 | 10 | 0 | 2 | **27** |

Read that table carefully if your child is a girl applying for Class 9: Kazhakootam has **no home-state girls' seats in Class 9** — both girls' seats sit in the outside-state pool. And remember the bulletin's own advice: these numbers are tentative, and it tells candidates to specify their choice of school irrespective of a zero in their category, because seats get redistributed when cadets withdraw. List the school you want; just keep one choice where the seats are real and visible.

## Admission: only through AISSEE, and the clock is ticking

There is no other way in. Admission to Kazhakootam is only through **AISSEE 2027**, run by NTA. Apply online at **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** by **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**; the fee is **₹850** (General, OBC-NCL, defence wards) or **₹700** (SC/ST). The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027, at 2:00 PM** — 150 minutes for Class 6, 180 minutes for Class 9, pen-paper OMR, no negative marking. Your child must score at least **25% in each section and 40% overall** to qualify, though admission itself runs on the merit list through e-counselling.

Two details Kerala parents should not miss. First, the Class 6 paper can be written in **13 languages, including Malayalam** — choose the medium on your child's reading strength, not on habit; the Class 9 paper is English only. Second, the home-state advantage only works if your domicile is Kerala. If your family lives in Kerala but your domicile certificate is from another state, that certificate — not your address — decides your pool. Check it before you submit, not after.

## Houses, campus life and the coastal adjustment

Eleven houses — Nehru, Prasad, Cariappa, Veluthampi, Shivaji, Vallathol, Ashoka, Rajaji, Tagore, V. K. Krishna Menon and Patel — divided into senior, junior and sub-junior groups. That is the first thing a new cadet learns: the house is the family inside the school.

The campus itself is the school's quiet advantage. The facilities listed by the school include smart classrooms, separate Physics, Chemistry and Biology laboratories, a Science Park, computer and language laboratories, an automated library with more than **9,000 books**, and the **Somaiah Aquatic Complex** swimming pool, alongside playfields for football, hockey, basketball, volleyball, lawn tennis and boxing, a gymnasium and an obstacle course. For a child from a Kerala town, the adjustment is real — early mornings, physical training, a shared dormitory, food that is not home food — but the sea breeze is kinder than the winter cold of the hill schools, and Thiruvananthapuram is an 18-km bus ride away when homesickness needs a visit.

## Fees: what is reported, and what is not verified

Honesty first: there is no single verified 2027-28 fee figure for Kazhakootam. What the sources say:

- The school's published fee sheet for **2026-27 new entrants** totals **₹1,95,865** (₹1,94,365 for SC/ST Kerala-domicile cadets) — tuition ₹1,28,615, diet ₹44,250, pocket money ₹18,500, clothing ₹1,500, plus a refundable caution deposit.
- Older portals quote **₹1,66,169** as the 2025-26 lumpsum, which fits the pattern of a roughly 10% annual rise.

So budget on roughly **₹1.7–2 lakh a year**, and confirm the exact figure from the school before you plan around it. The offsets are real: Kerala government merit, income-based and SC/ST scholarships for domiciled cadets, and a defence scholarship for service families reported at up to ₹32,000 a year. Ask the school office for the scholarship forms at admission time — they are not automatic.

## Is Kazhakootam the right first choice for your child?

If your domicile is Kerala, almost certainly yes — it is your only home-state school, it is among the oldest and most established in the system, and its NDA feeder record stretches back six decades. If your domicile is elsewhere, weigh it honestly: as an outside-state candidate your child chases 21 of 75 Class 6 seats, and the Malayalam-medium option does not help a child who reads best in another language. The school suits a child who will thrive on structure and early mornings and who genuinely wants the uniform — not one being pushed into it. Visit the campus if you can; 18 km from Thiruvananthapuram is close enough to see it before you choose it.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Kazhakootam have for 2027-28?**

Kazhakootam has 75 Class 6 seats — 65 boys and 10 girls — and 27 Class 9 seats — 25 boys and 2 girls. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin.

**When was Sainik School Kazhakootam founded?**

It started functioning on 20 January 1962 in army barracks at Pangode, Thiruvananthapuram, and moved to its present 225-acre campus at Kazhakootam in 1964. The foundation stone was laid by Defence Minister V. K. Krishna Menon on 5 February 1962. Its motto is Gyan, Anushasan, Sahayog — knowledge, discipline, cooperation.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Kazhakootam?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Kazhakootam?**

No single verified 2027-28 fee figure is available. The school's published 2026-27 fee sheet for new entrants totals about Rs 1,95,865, while older portals quote about Rs 1,66,169 for 2025-26. Parents should confirm the current figure from the school before budgeting.

**Does Sainik School Kazhakootam admit girls?**

Yes, since the 2021-22 academic session. For 2027-28 the bulletin lists 10 Class 6 seats and 2 Class 9 seats for girls at Kazhakootam — both Class 9 girls' seats are in the outside-state pool.

**Can my child write the AISSEE in Malayalam for Kazhakootam?**

Yes. The Class 6 paper is available in 13 languages, including Malayalam, and you choose the medium while filling the form. The Class 9 paper is in English only.
