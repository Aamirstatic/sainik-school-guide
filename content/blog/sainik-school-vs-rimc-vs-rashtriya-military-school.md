# Sainik School vs RIMC vs Military School: Teenon Mein Kya Farq Hai?

Defence ki taiyaari wale schools mein ye **teen naam** sabse zyada liye jate hain — Sainik School, RIMC (Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun), aur RMS (Rashtriya Military Schools). Teenon ka maksad ek hai — officer banana — lekin teenon ka rasta, entry point aur scale bilkul alag hai. Is article mein teeno ko aamne-saamne rakhte hain.

## Teenon Ki Pehchaan (30 Second Mein)

| | Sainik School | RIMC | RMS |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chalata kaun hai** | Sainik Schools Society (MoD) | Directly MoD/Army | Army |
| **Schools** | 100+ (AISSEE network) | Sirf 1 (Dehradun) | Sirf 5 |
| **Entry class** | Class 6 (+ Class 9) | **Class 8** | Class 6 + Class 9 |
| **Exam** | AISSEE (NTA) | RIMC entrance exam | RMS CET |
| **Seats** | Hazaaron | Bahut kam (~25 per term) | Limited |
| **Fees** | 🔶 ~₹1–1.5L/saal | Subsidized | Subsidized (kam) |

## Entry Class: Sabse Important Farq

Ye wo point hai jahan sabse zyada confusion hota hai:

- **Sainik School:** Class 6 mein main entry (10–12 saal ke bachche) — ✅ AISSEE 31 Jan 2027 (tentative)
- **RIMC:** Class 8 mein entry — bachche ki umar 11.5–13 saal ke beech honi chahiye. Matlab RIMC ka target **Class 6 ke baad** hai
- **RMS:** Class 6 aur Class 9 dono mein entry

**Matlab:** Agar aapka bachcha abhi Class 5 mein hai, to RIMC abhi aapke liye nahi hai — pehle Sainik School (Class 6) ka target rakhein, RIMC Class 8 mein backup/future option hai.

## Exam Pattern Ka Farq

| | AISSEE (Class 6) | RIMC Entrance | RMS CET |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conduct | NTA | RIMC administration | RMS |
| Format | OMR MCQ, no negative marking | Written (descriptive + objective mix) | Objective |
| Subjects | Maths, Language, Intelligence, GK | English, Maths, GK (+ interview) | Maths, English, GK, Intelligence |
| Uske baad | Counselling (AISSAC) | Interview + medical | Interview + medical |

RIMC ka exam sabse selective mana jata hai — 1 school, ~25 seats per term, pure desh se competition. Isliye uski taiyaari mein descriptive writing aur interview skills par zyada zor hota hai.

## Fees Aur Kharcha

- **RIMC/RMS:** Army-subsidized — dono ki fees Sainik Schools se kam hoti hai. Ye inka bada plus hai.
- **Sainik School:** 🔶 ~₹1–1.5 lakh/saal, lekin [scholarship schemes](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) se rahat milti hai.

Lekin yaad rahe — **kam fees ka fayda tabhi jab admission mile**. RIMC ki 25 seats ke liye desh bhar se hazaaron bachche ladte hain.

## Kaun Sa Kab Chunein? (Decision Framework)

**Bachcha Class 5 mein hai:**
→ Pehla target: **AISSEE (Sainik School, Class 6)** — sabse zyada seats, sabse zyada options
→ Saath mein: **RMS CET (Class 6)** bhar sakte hain — taiyaari overlap karti hai

**Bachcha Class 7 mein hai:**
→ **RIMC entrance** ki taiyaari shuru karein (Class 8 entry)
→ Backup: **AISSEE Class 9** (limited seats, lekin option hai)

**Defence family hai, budget kam hai:**
→ Teenon try karein — RIMC/RMS subsidized hain, Sainik mein scholarship hai

**Hamari imandaar salah:** Teenon ka form bharne mein koi burai nahi — taiyaari ka core (Maths, English, GK, Reasoning) ek hi hai. Bas har exam ka pattern aur date alag se note kar lein.

## Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawaal

**Q1. Kya RIMC mein Class 6 mein admission ho sakta hai?**
Nahi — RIMC mein entry sirf Class 8 mein hoti hai (11.5–13 saal ki umar).

**Q2. Teenon exams ek saal mein de sakte hain?**
Haan, agar age aur class eligibility match kare. Dates alag-alag hoti hain.

**Q3. Kaun sa sabse mushkil hai?**
Seats ke hisaab se RIMC sabse selective hai. Lekin "mushkil" se zyada "taiyaari" matter karti hai.

**Q4. RIMC ke baad kya hota hai?**
RIMC Class 12 tak hai — uske baad NDA ka rasta khulta hai, jaise Sainik School ke baad.

**Q5. Girls ke liye kaun sa option hai?**
Sainik Schools mein girls quota (33 schools) confirmed hai. RIMC/RMS ki girls policy unki official notifications mein check karein.

**Q6. Teenon mein se koi ek chunein to kaun sa?**
Class 6 entry ke liye: Sainik School (options sabse zyada). Ye practical jawab hai — sapno wala jawab RIMC hai, lekin wahan seats gin-ti ki hain.
