# Sainik School Fees 2027: Kitni Fees Lagti Hai? Poora Sach, Bina Jhuth Ke

> **✅ Last verified: 26 September 2026** — Application fee ke aankde AISSEE 2026 bulletin par aadharit hain (🔶 EXPECTED: 2027 mein badal sakte hain). School ki annual fees har school mein alag hoti hai — yahan diye gaye ranges pichhle cycles ke pattern par aadharit anumaan hain, exact rakam ke liye apne chune hue school ki website dekhein.

Google par "Sainik School fees" search karo to har website ekdum exact number de deti hai — "Sainik School Korukonda: ₹1,27,345". Sach? **Ye sab andaze hain.** Har school ki fees har saal thodi badalti hai, aur 2027-28 session ki official fee abhi kisi school ne ghoshit nahi ki hai.

Is article mein main aapko wahi bataunga jo pakka hai, aur jahan andaza hai wahan saaf-saaf "andaza" likhunga. Kyunki fees ka faisla bachche ke future ka faisla hai — ismein jhuth ki gunjaish nahi.

## School Ki Annual Fees: Asli Tasveer

Admission ke baad jo fees lagti hai, wo **har school mein alag** hai. Pattern ye raha hai:

| Fee Component | Lagbhag Kitna (saal bhar) |
|---------------|---------------------------|
| Tuition fee | ₹60,000 – ₹90,000 |
| Messing (khana) charges | ₹35,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Clothing & uniform | ₹8,000 – ₹12,000 (pehle saal zyada) |
| Caution money (refundable) | ₹5,000 – ₹10,000 (ek baar) |
| Miscellaneous (exam, activities) | ₹5,000 – ₹10,000 |
| **Kul milakar (approx)** | **₹1,10,000 – ₹1,70,000 saal** |

Ye range hai, kisi ek school ka final bill nahi. Purane established schools (jaise Ghorakhal, Korukonda, Kazhakootam) mein fees thodi zyada hoti hai; naye PPP-model schools mein structure alag ho sakta hai.

**Hamari salah:** Jis school ko choice filling mein upar rakhein, uski official website par "Fee Structure 2026-27" zaroor check karein — wahan pichhle saal ki rakam se aapko sahi andaza lag jayega.

## Scholarship: Fees Kam Karne Ka Asli Rasta

Ye wo hissa hai jo kai parents miss kar dete hain. Sainik School mein scholarship ka system kaafi majboot hai:

- **Defence wards** ko kayi rajyon mein fee mein chhoot milti hai — ye usi "25% of remainder for defence wards" reservation ke saath judta hai (✅ CONFIRMED — AISSAC SOP)
- **State government scholarships** — kai rajya apne domicile students ki poori ya aadhi fees dete hain (jaise kuch states mein merit-cum-means)
- **SC/ST students** ke liye central/state schemes alag se hain
- **Merit scholarships** — school ke andar top performers ko

Matlab: sticker price dekhkar ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi. Pehle apne rajya ki scholarship policy check karo — aksar asli kharcha aadha reh jaata hai. [Scholarship ki detail guide yahan padhein](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/).

## FAQ

**Q1: Kya Sainik School ki fees private boarding schools se kam hai?**
Haan, kaafi kam. Private boarding schools mein ₹3–8 lakh saal lagta hai; Sainik School mein ₹1.1–1.7 lakh ke aas-paas (plus scholarship ke baad aur kam). [Detail comparison yahan](/blog/sainik-school-vs-private-boarding-school/).

**Q2: Kya fees har saal badhti hai?**
Aam taur par 5–10% ka annual revision hota hai. Isliye 6 saal ka budget banate waqt thoda buffer rakho.

**Q3: Kya ek saath poori fees deni hoti hai ya installment mein?**
Zyadatar schools mein term-wise (2–3 installments) payment hota hai. Exact schedule admission letter ke saath milta hai.

**Q4: Agar beech mein bachcha school chhod de to fees vapas milti hai?**
Caution money vapas milta hai. Tuition/messing ka refund school ke rules par nirbhar karta hai — admission ke waqt refund policy zaroor padh lo.

**Q5: Kya girls ke liye fees mein koi chhoot hai?**
Koi central alag policy nahi hai, lekin kai state scholarships ladkiyon ko priority deti hain. Apne rajya ki policy check karo.

**Q6: NTA application fee kab deni hoti hai?**
Online form bharte waqt hi — 🔶 EXPECTED Oct–Nov 2026 window mein, jab bulletin aayega.
