# Sainik School Fees 2027: How Much Does It Really Cost? The Honest Breakdown

> **Last verified: 26 September 2026** — The AISSEE 2027 information bulletin hasn't been released yet, so 2027 fee figures below are projections. School-wise annual fees vary and change year to year; treat every number here as indicative, not final.

Let's address the question every parent asks before anything else: *"How much will this actually cost me?"*

I respect that question. A Sainik School education is a seven-year commitment, and you deserve a straight answer — not marketing. So here's the honest picture: what the fees look like, what you're really paying for, and where the genuine financial help comes from.

## First, the Application Fee (AISSEE 2027)

Before the school fees, there's the entrance exam fee. Here's where we stand:

| Category | Fee status | Amount |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| General / OBC-NCL / Defence & Ex-Servicemen wards | 🔶 EXPECTED | **₹850** |
| SC / ST | 🔶 EXPECTED | **₹700** |

These are the AISSEE 2026 rates, and we expect them to repeat for 2027 — but the NTA bulletin hasn't confirmed them yet (the bulletin itself is 🔶 **not released as of 26 Sept 2026**). Anyone quoting the 2027 fee as final right now is guessing. We'll update this the day the bulletin drops.

## Annual School Fees: The Realistic Range

Here's where I have to be careful with you, because exact fees differ across the 33 established schools and change nearly every year. Based on recent years' patterns, annual fees at most Sainik Schools fall roughly in the **₹1 lakh to ₹1.5 lakh per year** range — but some schools charge more, some less. Treat this as a planning figure, not a promise.

What I *can* tell you with confidence is what the fee covers, because that barely changes:

- **Tuition** — CBSE curriculum, same as any good private school
- **Boarding and lodging** — dormitory, house system, housemasters
- **Mess charges** — three meals plus snacks, every day
- **Uniforms, PT kit, and basic clothing issues**
- **Games, sports, NCC activities, and hobby clubs**
- **Medical care** — schools have a medical officer and MI room

When you break it down, you're paying for a full residential education — not just classes. Compare that with a good private day school in a metro (₹2–3 lakh a year, and your child still comes home to distractions), and the value becomes clearer.

## Scholarships and Fee Concessions: The Part Nobody Tells You About

This is the genuinely good news, and it's under-reported. Multiple state governments offer scholarships to students studying in Sainik Schools — typically for domicile students, and often linked to family income or category. Defence personnel and ex-servicemen's children get additional support through defence welfare schemes.

The amounts and eligibility differ by state, so there's no single national figure I can honestly quote. But here's what to do: **once your child gets a seat, ask the school's office directly about state scholarships in your first month.** Many parents miss out simply because they never ask. We've covered this in detail in our [scholarship guide](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/).

## FAQ

**1. What is the exact Sainik School fee for 2027?**
School-wise 2027 fee structures release with each school's admission notice after AISSEE results. The indicative range is ₹1–1.5 lakh per year, varying by school. Check your allotted school's website after counselling.

**2. Is there any fee concession for defence families?**
Defence and ex-servicemen families get a separate application-fee category (₹850 in 2026, expected to repeat), and additional support exists through defence welfare schemes — confirm with the school office.

**3. Do SC/ST/OBC students get fee benefits?**
The AISSEE application fee is lower for SC/ST (₹700 in 2026, expected to repeat). State government scholarships for Sainik School students often prioritise SC/ST and income-based categories. See our [scholarship guide](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/).

**4. Are new PPP-mode Sainik Schools cheaper or costlier?**
The new schools under the partnership model set their own fee structures, which can differ significantly from the 33 established schools. Compare carefully during choice filling in [counselling](/blog/sainik-school-counselling-2027/).

**5. What happens if we can't afford the fee after getting a seat?**
Talk to the school immediately — between state scholarships, defence welfare support, and instalment options, most genuine cases find a way. Don't surrender a hard-earned seat without asking.
