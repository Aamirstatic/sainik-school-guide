## Career Path 1: NDA / Indian Armed Forces (30-40%)

This is the **primary pathway** Sainik Schools are designed for.

### Route from Sainik School to Officer

| Step | Timeline |
|------|---------|
| Sainik School (Class 6-12) | 7 years |
| NDA Exam (UPSC) | After Class 12 |
| NDA Training (Khadakwasla, Pune) | 3 years |
| IMA/INA/AFA Training | 1 year |
| Commissioned Officer | Age ~22 |

| Rank | Monthly Salary (Approx.) |

| Lieutenant | ₹56,100 + allowances |
| Captain | ₹61,300 + allowances |
| Major | ₹69,400 + allowances |
| Colonel | ₹1,30,600 + allowances |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Can Sainik School students become engineers?
**Absolutely.** 20-25% of Sainik School alumni pursue engineering through JEE Main/Advanced. The strong Maths and Science foundation is a major advantage.

### Do Sainik Schools offer Science stream for Class 11-12?
**Yes.** Most Sainik Schools offer PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) and PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) streams for Class 11-12.

### What if my child doesn't clear NDA?
NDA is just ONE option. Sainik School prepares students for engineering, medical, civil services, and corporate careers equally well.

### Is Sainik School worth it if my child doesn't want to join the Army?
**Yes.** The discipline, fitness, and academic foundation from Sainik School benefits ANY career path.
