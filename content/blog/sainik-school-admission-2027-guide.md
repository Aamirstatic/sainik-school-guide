## Eligibility: kaun apply kar sakta hai?

### Class 6 admission

- **Age** 🔶 PROJECTED: 31 March 2027 ko 10–12 saal — matlab janm **1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017** ke beech. (Basis: 2026 bulletin mein 1 Apr 2014 – 31 Mar 2016 tha; ek saal shift karke projection.)
- Class 5 mein padh raha ho ya pass ho.
- Girls eligible — 33 traditional schools mein **10% seats ya 10 seats (jo zyada ho)** girls ke liye ✅ (SOP).

### Class 9 admission

- **Age** 🔶 PROJECTED: 31 March 2027 ko 13–15 saal — matlab janm **1 April 2012 – 31 March 2014** ke beech.
- Class 8 recognised school se pass.
- Paper sirf English medium mein hota hai — iska dhyan rakhein.

## Application fee 🔶 EXPECTED

2026 ke rates par based (2027 bulletin mein confirm hoga):

- **₹850** — General / OBC / Defence wards
- **₹700** — SC / ST

Fee online pay hoti hai — debit/credit card, net banking ya UPI se.

## Application kaise bharein? (Process)

1. NTA ka AISSEE portal khulega: `exams.nta.nic.in` (🔶 expected Oct 2026)
2. Registration — mobile number aur email se
3. Form bhariye — personal details, school choice, exam city
4. Documents upload — photo, signature, birth certificate, category certificate (agar lagu ho)
5. Fee payment online
6. Confirmation page **print karke rakhiye** — ye sabse zaroori step hai

[Application form ki step-by-step guide](/blog/aissee-application-form-2027-guide/) mein har screen ka detail hai. Aur form bharne se pehle [ye 5 aam galtiyan](/blog/sainik-school-admission-mistakes/) zaroor padh lijiye — har saal hazaaron forms chhoti galtiyon se reject hote hain.

## Counselling: exam ke baad kya hota hai?

Exam clear karne ke baad **AISSAC** (All India Sainik Schools Admission Counselling) hota hai — ise NTA nahi, **Sainik Schools Society** conduct karti hai, `pesa.ncog.gov.in` par, poori tarah online, **bina koi fee** ke, lagbhag 6 rounds mein ✅.

Process: online registration → choice filling (school preference) → seat allotment (merit + reservation + choice par based) → medical test → admission. [Poora counselling guide](/blog/sainik-school-counselling-2027/) yahan hai.

Reservation (33 traditional schools) ✅: 67% home-state / 33% other-states; SC 15%, ST 7.5%, OBC-NCL 27%; bache hue mein se 25% defence wards ke liye.

## FAQs

**Q1. Kya AISSEE 2027 ka notification aa gaya hai?**
Nahi. 26 September 2026 tak NTA ne information bulletin release nahi kiya hai. Expected: October 2026. "Notification out" likhne wali sites se savdhaan rahiye.

**Q2. Exam date pakki hai?**
31 January 2027 NTA ke official exam calendar (notice 16 Sept 2026) mein hai — tentative lekin official. Final confirmation bulletin mein hogi.

**Q3. Kitni fee lagegi form bharne mein?**
🔶 Expected ₹850 (Gen/OBC/Defence) / ₹700 (SC/ST) — 2026 ke rates. Bulletin mein confirm hoga.

**Q4. Kya Class 6 mein girls apply kar sakti hain?**
Haan. 33 traditional schools mein 10% ya 10 seats (jo zyada ho) girls ke liye reserved hain. [Girls guide](/blog/sainik-school-for-girls-2027/) padhiye.

**Q5. New PPP schools ke liye alag form bharna hoga?**
Nahi. Ek hi AISSEE form, aur counselling mein choice filling ke waqt dono tarah ke schools ke options milte hain. [PPP schools guide](/blog/new-sainik-schools-ppp-model-2027/) dekhein.

**Q6. Age mein chhoot (relaxation) milti hai?**
SC/ST ko qualifying marks mein chhoot hai. Age mein aam taur par koi relaxation nahi hoti — lekin final rule bulletin mein aayega.
