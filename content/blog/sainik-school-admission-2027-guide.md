Sainik Schools are among India's most prestigious residential educational institutions. Established by the Ministry of Defence, these schools combine CBSE academics with military-style discipline, physical training, and NCC to prepare students for careers in the Indian Armed Forces and beyond. With 33+ traditional schools and 69 new schools under the Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model, the Sainik School system now offers approximately 10,000 seats annually — against approximately 4 lakh applicants.

Admission to Sainik School is through the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE), conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). The process is competitive but transparent, merit-based, and well-documented. This guide walks you through every step — from checking eligibility to securing admission.

## Step-by-Step Admission Process

### Step 1: Check Eligibility

Before applying, verify that your child meets all eligibility criteria:
- Age limit for the desired class (Class 6 or Class 9)
- Educational qualification (currently studying in Class 5 or Class 8)
- Category-specific requirements (if applicable)
- Domicile requirements for home state quota

### Step 2: Online Application

The application is submitted online through the NTA website:

**Detailed Application Process:**

1. **Visit the Official Website:** Go to [exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE](https://exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE)

2. **New Registration:** Click "Apply Online for AISSEE 2027" and then "New Registration"
   - Enter the candidate's name (as on birth certificate)
   - Provide a valid email address and mobile number
   - Create a password
   - Note down the application number generated

3. **Fill the Application Form:**
   - Personal details: Name, date of birth, gender, category
   - Parent/guardian details: Names, occupation, contact information
   - Address: Permanent and correspondence address
   - Educational details: Current school, class, board
   - School preference: Select up to three preferred Sainik Schools
   - Exam centre preference: Choose preferred city for the examination

4. **Upload Documents:**
   - Recent passport-size photograph (JPEG, 10–200 KB)
   - Scanned signature (JPEG, 4–30 KB)
   - Birth certificate (PDF, 50–200 KB)
   - Category certificate (PDF, 50–200 KB) — if applicable

5. **Pay Application Fee:**
   - General/OBC: ₹550 (approximate)
   - SC/ST: ₹400 (approximate)
   - Payment methods: Debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI

6. **Submit and Print:**
   - Review all entered information carefully before submission
   - Submit the application form
   - Download and print the confirmation page
   - Save the application number and password securely

### Step 3: Download Admit Card

- Admit cards are released 1 to 2 weeks before the exam
- Download from the NTA website using application number and date of birth
- Verify all details: Name, photograph, exam centre, date, and time
- Print the admit card on A4 size paper
- Carry the printed admit card to the exam centre — digital copies are not accepted

### Step 4: Appear for AISSEE 2027

**Exam Day Instructions:**
- Reach the exam centre at least 30 minutes before the scheduled time
- Carry the printed admit card and a valid photo ID (Aadhaar card, school ID)
- Carry required stationery: Black or blue ballpoint pen, HB pencils, eraser
- Do not carry electronic devices (phones, calculators, smartwatches)
- The exam is conducted in offline (OMR-based pen and paper) mode

**Exam Pattern:**

| Class | Subjects | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|----------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Class 6 | Mathematics, English, General Knowledge, Intelligence | 125 | 300 | 2.5 hours |
| Class 9 | Mathematics, English, General Science, Social Studies, Intelligence | 150 | 400 | 3 hours |

**Important:** There is no negative marking in AISSEE. Students should attempt all questions, even if they need to make educated guesses.

For preparation strategies, read our [AISSEE 2027 Preparation Tips](/blog/aissee-2027-preparation-tips/) and [Best Books for AISSEE 2027](/blog/best-books-aissee-2027/) guides.

### Step 5: Check Results

- Results are published on the NTA website
- Enter the application number and date of birth to check
- Download and print the scorecard
- Check the merit list for your preferred Sainik School
- Note the qualifying status and rank

### Step 6: Counselling and Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates are called for counselling at their preferred Sainik School:

**Documents to Bring:**

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Birth Certificate | Age verification |
| Class 5 or 8 Marksheet | Educational qualification verification |
| Transfer Certificate | From current school |
| Category Certificate | SC/ST/OBC verification (if applicable) |
| Domicile Certificate | State residency verification |
| Defence Certificate | Parent's military service verification (if applicable) |
| Aadhaar Card | Identity verification |
| Passport-Size Photographs | 6 to 8 recent copies |
| Medical Fitness Certificate | From a registered medical practitioner |
| AISSEE Scorecard | Result verification |

**Counselling Process:**
- Document verification by school officials
- School preference submission (if not done during application)
- Seat allotment based on merit, category, domicile, and preference
- Fee payment to confirm the seat

### Step 7: Medical Test

After counselling, candidates must pass a medical fitness test:
- Conducted at designated military hospitals or school campuses
- Comprehensive physical examination (height, weight, vision, hearing, dental, orthopedic)
- Candidates found unfit may appeal or request re-examination

For detailed medical requirements, read our [Sainik School Medical Test 2027 guide](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/).

### Step 8: Final Admission

- Based on AISSEE merit, counselling, medical fitness, and seat availability
- Pay the admission fee to confirm the seat
- Complete remaining documentation
- Join the school as per the notified schedule

## Tips to Increase Admission Chances

### Academic Preparation

1. **Start Early:** Begin preparation at least 6 to 8 months before the exam
2. **Focus on NCERT:** AISSEE questions are largely based on NCERT textbooks
3. **Practice Previous Year Papers:** Understand the exam pattern and difficulty level
4. **Take Mock Tests:** Simulate exam conditions to build confidence and speed
5. **Strengthen Mathematics:** It carries 50% weightage — make it the priority subject

### Strategic Application

6. **Apply to Multiple Schools:** Increase chances by selecting three school preferences wisely
7. **Understand the Quota System:** Apply strategically based on domicile state for the 67% home state quota
8. **Prepare Documents Early:** Collect all certificates well before the deadline
9. **Consider PPP Schools:** New PPP-mode schools may have less competition

### Physical Preparation

10. **Build Physical Fitness:** While AISSEE is a written exam, the medical test requires physical fitness
11. **Eye Test:** Get eyes checked early; address any vision issues
12. **Dental Checkup:** Ensure dental health meets medical standards
13. **Weight Management:** Maintain height-weight ratio within acceptable range

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the age limit for Sainik School Class 6 admission in 2027?

For Class 6 admission in 2027, the candidate must be between 10 and 12 years of age as on 31 March 2027. This means the student should have been born between approximately 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017. SC/ST candidates may receive age relaxation of up to 2 years as per government norms. Always verify exact dates with the official AISSEE notification.

### How many seats are available in Sainik Schools?

Across all 33+ traditional Sainik Schools and 69 PPP-mode schools, there are approximately 10,000 total seats annually. Each traditional Sainik School typically has 80 to 120 seats for Class 6 and 10 to 25 seats for Class 9, depending on the school. Competition is intense, with approximately 4 lakh students appearing for AISSEE.

### Can girls apply for Sainik School admission?

Yes. Girls can apply for Sainik School admission. A minimum of 25% of total seats are reserved for girl candidates at every Sainik School. The admission process is identical — girls take the same AISSEE examination as boys. Girls are also eligible for NDA after completing Class 12 from Sainik School.

### What happens after clearing the AISSEE exam?

After clearing AISSEE, candidates are called for counselling and document verification based on their merit rank. During counselling, candidates submit their school preferences. Seats are allotted based on merit, category, domicile, and school preference. After seat allotment, candidates must pay the admission fee, undergo a medical fitness examination, and join the school as per the notified schedule.

### What is the total fee for Sainik School per year?

The total annual fee ranges from approximately ₹2,00,000 to ₹2,50,000, covering tuition, hostel, mess, uniform, books, medical, and other charges. This is significantly lower than private boarding schools (₹5,00,000 to ₹12,00,000). Fee concessions are available for SC/ST students (up to 50%), defence personnel children (up to 50%), and through state government scholarships.

### Is there negative marking in AISSEE?

No. There is no negative marking in AISSEE. Each correct answer earns full marks, and incorrect or unanswered questions do not result in any deduction. This means students should attempt all questions, even when uncertain. However, random guessing without any elimination strategy is not recommended as it wastes valuable time.

### Which Sainik School is the best in India?

All Sainik Schools follow the same CBSE curriculum and military-style system. However, some schools have stronger NDA track records, including Sainik School Korukonda, Sainik School Chittorgarh, Sainik School Kapurthala, and Sainik School Rewa. The "best" school depends on individual preferences — location, facilities, alumni network, and proximity to home. For the complete school list, see the table above.
