There are roughly **126 days** between today and 31 January 2027. Four months feels long until you subtract school exams, Diwali, and the application-form window — then it starts to feel honest.

With the NTA bulletin not yet out, the most useful thing a family can do is run two tracks in parallel: **get the paperwork ready** for the October window, and **hit the study milestones** that actually move scores.

## Track 1: Paperwork before the October window

When the notification lands, the application window is short. In the AISSEE 2026 cycle, forms were open roughly 10–30 October 2025 — about three weeks. Families that scrambled for a birth certificate or a fresh photograph in the middle of that window know how stressful it gets.

Gather these now, so October is calm form-filling rather than a document hunt:

| Document / item | Why it matters now |
|---|---|
| Recent passport-size photograph of the child | NTA specifies photo size and recency in every bulletin; an old school photo may be rejected |
| Child's signature (on white paper, scanned) | Needed for the application and matching later stages |
| Birth certificate | Date of birth must match school records exactly — mismatches are the most common correction-window headache |
| Class 5 / Class 8 study certificate or marksheet | Proves the qualifying-class requirement |
| Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL), if claiming reservation | Issued in the child's name, in the format NTA prescribes |
| Domicile / residence proof | Decides the 67% home-state quota seat allocation |
| Defence / ex-serviceman documents, if applicable | 25% of the remainder seats are for defence wards |

One detail worth checking today: **the name and date of birth must match across every document.** Discovering a spelling mismatch in November means living inside the correction window. Fixing it in September costs an afternoon.

🔶 **EXPECTED** (2026 rates): the application fee was ₹850 for General/OBC/defence wards and ₹700 for SC/ST candidates. Keep a UPI-linked card or netbanking ready — fee payment happens online only.

## Track 2: The study milestones that move scores

Four months is enough to change an outcome, but only if the preparation has a shape. A realistic split:

- **October:** finish syllabus coverage — every topic touched at least once.
- **November:** shift to timed practice with weekly full-length mocks. ✅ **No negative marking** (confirmed NTA pattern), so train your child to attempt every question.
- **December:** weak-section surgery — two mocks a week, an error diary, re-do of every wrong question.
- **January:** light revision, admit card download (expected mid-to-late January), a calm last week. Exam: tentatively **31 January 2027**.

One habit that pays disproportionate dividends: a **daily 20-minute mental-maths block** — tables to 20, squares to 30. Maths carries half the paper, and speed there frees time for every other section.
