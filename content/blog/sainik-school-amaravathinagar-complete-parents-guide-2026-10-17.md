If you are a Tamil Nadu parent researching Sainik Schools, one name keeps coming up: Amaravathinagar. It is not just the state's best-known Sainik School — it is one of the oldest military schools in India, and its history explains why Tamil Nadu families hold it in special regard. This guide is the school-profile companion to our [Tamil Nadu language and admission guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-tamil-nadu-language-admission-guide-2027/): everything about the school itself, in one place.

## A 64-year history in 60 seconds

The school opened on **16 July 1962** — making it one of the first Sainik Schools ever established (CONFIRMED — Wikipedia, Dinamalar). It began life as **Sainik School Madras (SSM)** and was renamed Sainik School Amaravathinagar in **1975** (CONFIRMED — Wikipedia, Dinamalar). The campus sits on roughly **240 acres** at Amaravathinagar, Udumalpet Taluk, Tiruppur district, Tamil Nadu 642102 (CONFIRMED — Careers360, school travel listings), in the foothills near the Amaravati Dam.

A few identity markers parents notice on day one: the school motto is **"Can Do It"**, the houses are **Chera, Chola, Pandya and Pallava** (CONFIRMED — Wikipedia), and the alumni call themselves **Amaravians**. The school has about **700–740 cadets** across Classes 6 to 12 (CONFIRMED range — Careers360 lists 742 enrolled students; Wikipedia lists 703 cadets).

## The language question, settled plainly

This is the question Tamil Nadu parents ask us most, so let us be unambiguous: **the medium of instruction is English** (CONFIRMED — Careers360, Wikipedia, Dinamalar). The school is CBSE-affiliated and every subject is taught in English. What confuses parents is the trilingual program — students also learn **Tamil and Hindi** as languages (CONFIRMED — Dinamalar). That is exposure, not a Tamil-medium option.

Be honest with yourself here: if your child cannot yet follow English instructions comfortably, a year of English reading before Class 6 entry is not optional — it is the single highest-leverage preparation step for this school.

## Fees and quotas: what the numbers actually look like

Fee figures for Amaravathinagar disagree across public sources, so here is the honest picture:

| Item | Figure | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee (tuition + boarding + diet, 2026-27) | ≈ Rs 1,16,924 | REPORTED — aisseesainikschool.in |
| Annual fee (2025-26, all recurring charges) | ≈ Rs 1,69,374 | REPORTED — JustDial listing |
| Fee escalation | ~10% per year | REPORTED — admission trackers |

The two reported totals differ because sources bundle different charges (diet money, clothing, exam fees) in different years. **Do not budget on either number alone** — confirm the current fee schedule on the school's official website (sainikschoolamaravathinagar.edu.in) before you apply. What is consistent across sources: fees rise roughly 10% a year, and means-tested scholarships exist for eligible families — see our [home-state quota explainer](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-explained/) and the [fee-concession guide](/blog/sainik-school-fee-concession-income-slabs-how-to-apply-2026-10-16/) for how income slabs work.

On seats: **67% of seats are reserved for Tamil Nadu-domiciled children** (CONFIRMED — JustDial, matches the national home-state rule in our [quota guide](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-explained/)). That is a real structural advantage for Tamil Nadu families — but it applies to domicile, so get the [domicile certificate](/blog/domicile-certificate-online-apply-state-wise-process-2026-10-11/) sorted well before the AISSEE form opens. Girls' admissions exist too: trackers report roughly **12 Class VI seats for girls** (REPORTED — JustDial); the authoritative number is in each year's AISSEE seat matrix.

## What life there actually involves

Beyond the classroom, the campus is known for an unusually wide activity program — Dinamalar's profile lists horse riding, swimming, shooting, trekking, and even aircraft and naval-systems training exposure (CONFIRMED — Dinamalar). Streams offered at the senior level are **PCM and PCB** (CONFIRMED — Careers360), aimed squarely at the NDA and engineering/medical pathways.

Getting there: Coimbatore International Airport is about **101 km** away and Coimbatore Junction about **89 km** (REPORTED — school travel listing). If you are shortlisted for counselling or the medical, plan the travel a day early — it is a real journey from Chennai or Madurai.

## Your Amaravathinagar readiness checklist

- [ ] **Eligibility:** Class 6 — 10 to 12 years; Class 9 — 13 to 15 years (AISSEE norms). Verify against the official bulletin when it releases.
- [ ] **Domicile:** Tamil Nadu domicile certificate ready — it unlocks the 67% home-state quota.
- [ ] **English:** your child follows English instructions and reads grade-level English. If not, start now.
- [ ] **Documents:** birth certificate, caste/category certificate if applicable, Aadhaar — scanned in the required formats before the form window opens.
- [ ] **AISSEE prep:** admission is through the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (written + medical), for Class 6 and Class 9 only (CONFIRMED — Wikipedia, Careers360). No other entry route exists.
- [ ] **AISSEE notification:** the 2027 notification is not out yet as of this week — [check the latest status here](/news/aissee-2027-form-still-not-out-6-tasks-2026-10-17/).

One honest note to close: Amaravathinagar's age is an advantage (mature alumni network, settled traditions) and a check (older infrastructure in places). Visit the campus on an open day if you can — no article replaces seeing the dormitories and the parade ground yourself.

## FAQs

**What is the medium of instruction at Sainik School Amaravathinagar?**
English. All teaching is in English under the CBSE curriculum, though students also study Tamil and Hindi as languages.

**When was Sainik School Amaravathinagar established?**
16 July 1962, as Sainik School Madras. It was renamed Sainik School Amaravathinagar in 1975.

**What are the annual fees at Sainik School Amaravathinagar?**
Reported figures vary by source and year — around Rs 1.17 lakh for 2026-27 per one admission tracker, with other listings higher. Confirm the exact figure on the school's official website before applying.

**How do Tamil Nadu students get admission to Amaravathinagar?**
Only through AISSEE, for Class 6 and Class 9. Tamil Nadu-domiciled children get the 67% home-state quota in seat allotment.

**Do girls study at Sainik School Amaravathinagar?**
Yes, girls have been admitted in recent years. Trackers report roughly 12 Class VI seats for girls; the exact number is in the AISSEE seat matrix each year.

**How do I reach Sainik School Amaravathinagar?**
It is at Amaravathinagar, Udumalpet Taluk, Tiruppur district, Tamil Nadu 642102. The nearest airport is Coimbatore International Airport, about 101 km away; the nearest railway station is Coimbatore Junction, about 89 km away.
