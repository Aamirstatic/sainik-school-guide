# Sainik School Physical Fitness Guide 2027: Get Fit in 4 Months

> **Last verified: 26 September 2026** — AISSEE selection is through the written exam + counselling + medical fitness — there is no separate "physical test" with qualifying marks. This guide builds the fitness that helps in the medical examination and, more importantly, in thriving at Sainik School from day one.

Let's clear up a common confusion first: **AISSEE has no physical efficiency test.** Nobody times your child's 1.6 km run for admission marks. Selection is written exam → AISSAC counselling → [medical fitness](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/).

So why a fitness guide? Two reasons. First, the medical examination does assess general physical development — an active, healthy child simply passes with fewer questions. Second, and bigger: Sainik School life starts with 5:30 AM PT and daily games. Children who arrive fit enjoy it. Children who arrive unfit suffer for the first two months. Four months of preparation now buys a happy first term.

## The 4-Month Plan

| Month | Focus | Daily routine (30–40 min) |
|-------|-------|--------------------------|
| Month 1 (Oct) | Habit building | Brisk walking 20 min + 10 min stretching + play |
| Month 2 (Nov) | Stamina | Walk-run intervals: 2 min jog / 2 min walk, ×6 + basic exercises |
| Month 3 (Dec) | Strength | Continuous 15–20 min jog + push-ups, squats, planks (age-appropriate) |
| Month 4 (Jan) | Maintenance | Light daily activity; don't start anything new before the exam |

The golden rule: **never increase too fast.** Children's joints are growing. Soreness that lasts more than two days means back off. And always — always — warm up for 5 minutes first.

## The Core Exercises (No Gym Needed)

- **Running / walk-run intervals.** The single best exercise for overall stamina. A park, a road, a terrace — anywhere works.
- **Push-ups (knee push-ups to start).** Start with 5, add one every few days. Upper-body strength for PT.
- **Squats.** Bodyweight only. Leg strength for running and drill.
- **Plank.** Start with 20 seconds. Core strength protects the back.
- **Skipping.** Brilliant for coordination and stamina — and children actually enjoy it.
- **Stretching.** After every session, 5–10 minutes. Prevents most injuries.

## What About Diet?

Nothing exotic. Just the basics most families already know but don't enforce:

- Regular meals — no skipping breakfast, especially on school days
- Protein at every meal: dal, eggs, milk, paneer, chicken — whatever your family eats
- Less packaged junk, more home food (you don't need a lecture on this)
- Water, not colas, during activity

If your child is significantly underweight or overweight, talk to your paediatrician rather than following internet diet plans. Growing children aren't small adults.

## Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Starting too hard.** The classic: Day 1, a 3 km run, then a week of limping. Build gradually.
2. **Training through pain.** Muscle tiredness is fine; joint pain is a stop signal.
3. **Ignoring rest.** Growing bodies need rest days — 5 days a week is plenty.
4. **New shoes on day one.** Break in sports shoes over two weeks to avoid blisters.
5. **Comparing with others.** Every child has a different baseline. Compare with last week's self, nobody else.

## A Small Scene

A father from Jaipur told us his son couldn't run 500 metres without stopping in October. They followed exactly this kind of gradual plan — walk-runs in the park, father cycling alongside. ## FAQ

**1. Is there a physical test in AISSEE 2027?**
No. AISSEE selection is: written OMR exam → AISSAC counselling → medical fitness examination. Physical fitness helps with the medical and with adjusting to school life.

**2. How fit does my child need to be for the medical?**
The board assesses age-appropriate general health and development — not athletic performance. An active child with no untreated conditions is typically fine. See our [medical test guide](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/).

**3. My child has never exercised. Is 4 months enough?**
Yes — for building basic stamina and strength from zero. The plan above is designed exactly for that. Start easy.

**4. Should we join a sports academy?**
Not necessary. Daily outdoor play plus the exercises above are sufficient for admission purposes. Academies are for competitive sport, not for this.

**5. What if my child has asthma or another condition?**
Consult your doctor before starting any fitness routine, disclose the condition at the medical, and follow medical advice over any article — including this one.
