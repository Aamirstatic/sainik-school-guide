# Sainik School Physical Fitness Guide 2027: 4 Mahine Mein Fit Kaise Banein

> **✅ Last verified: 26 September 2026** — Note: AISSEE mein koi alag "physical test" exam nahi hota. Selection ke baad **medical test** hota hai (uska guide [yahan](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/)). Ye article **fitness taiyaari** ke liye hai — taaki bachcha medical mein fit nikle aur school ki PT life ke liye taiyaar rahe.

Ek galatfehmi door kar doon: AISSEE written exam mein daud nahi lagwani padti. Lekin jo bachcha 4 mahine roz daudta hai, uska **weight control mein rehta hai, stamina badhta hai, aur medical test mein "fit" nikalta hai.** Aur school jaane ke baad to roz subah PT hai hi.

To ye guide "exam" ke liye nahi, **zindagi** ke liye hai. Shuru karte hain.

## Kyon Zaroori Hai? (3 Wajahen)

1. **Medical test:** Weight age ke hisaab se proportionate hona chahiye. Bahut zyada ya bahut kam — dono par sawal uthte hain.
2. **School life:** Wahan pehle din se subah 5:45 par PT hai. Jo bachcha pehle se active hai, use shock nahi lagega.
3. **Padhai:** Research kehta hai — roz khelne wale bachchon ka focus aur memory behtar hota hai. Ye bonus hai.

## 4-Mahine Ka Fitness Plan (Sept 2026 – Jan 2027)

| Mahina | Running | Strength | Khel |
|--------|---------|----------|------|
| 1 (Sept) | Roz 10 min walk-jog | 5 push-ups, 5 sit-ups | Jo pasand ho, 30 min |
| 2 (Oct) | Roz 15 min jogging | 10 push-ups, 10 sit-ups, skipping 2 min | 45 min |
| 3 (Nov) | 20 min run (1.5–2 km) | 15 push-ups, 15 sit-ups, 1 min plank | 1 ghanta |
| 4 (Dec–Jan) | 20–25 min run (2–3 km) | 20 push-ups, 20 sit-ups, 2 min plank | 1 ghanta |

**Niyam:** Hafte mein 1 din rest. Dard ho to ruk jao — doctor ko dikhao. Ye plan average healthy bachche ke liye hai.

## Roz Ke 30 Minute: Simple Routine

Subah ya shaam — ek time pakka karo:

1. **Warm-up (5 min):** Gardan, kandhe, kamar ghumana; jumping jacks 20
2. **Running (15 min):** Apni raftaar se — tez chalna bhi chalega shuru mein
3. **Strength (7 min):** Push-ups, sit-ups, skipping — jitne ho sakein
4. **Cool-down (3 min):** Dheere chalna, gehri saans

Bas. Koi gym nahi chahiye, koi equipment nahi. Maidaan ya gali kaafi hai.

## Khana-Peena: Fitness Ka Aadha Hissa

- **Subah:** Protein — doodh/anda, daliya ya paratha
- **School tiffin:** Ghar ka khana — chips/chocolate nahi
- **Shaam:** Phal — kela, seb jo mile
- **Raat:** Halka khana, time par
- **Paani:** Din mein 8–10 glass — ye sabse sasta supplement hai

**Kam karo:** Cold drink, chips, zyada meetha, raat ko bhari khana.
**Mat karo:** Koi "dieting" nahi — bachcha badh raha hai, use poshan chahiye, kami nahi.

## Weight Ka Mamla: Kitna Hona Chahiye?

Koi ek "perfect" number nahi hai — age, height aur body type par nirbhar karta hai. General niyam:

- Bachcha **active** dikhna chahiye — saans phoolna, pet nikalna — ye red flags hain
- Agar doctor kehte hain weight theek hai, to theek hai
- 4 mahine mein 3–5 kg ka healthy adjustment sambhav hai (diet + exercise se)

Zabardasti wazan ghatana-badhana mat karo — **natural fitness** hi tikti hai.

## Medical Test Se Pehle Fitness Checklist

- [ ] Roz 20+ minute running ki aadat (kam se kam 2 mahine se)
- [ ] Push-ups/sit-ups bina haanfe kar leta hai
- [ ] Weight stable hai (upar-neeche nahi ho raha)
- [ ] Neend poori hoti hai (8–9 ghante)
- [ ] Koi purani chot/dard to nahi (ho to doctor ko dikhao)

Ye checklist poori hai to medical mein fitness ki taraf se chinta mat karo. Medical ke baaki hisson (aankh, kaan, daant) ke liye [medical test guide](/blog/sainik-school-medical-test-2027/) padhein.

## Chot Se Bachav: 5 Niyam

1. **Warm-up kabhi mat chhodo** — bina warm-up ke daudna chot ko nyota dena hai
2. **Joote sahi pehno** — ghise hue ya tight joote se ghutne/takhne mein dard hota hai
3. **Dard ko ignore mat karo** — 3 din se zyada dard hai to doctor ko dikhao, "theek ho jayega" par bharosa mat karo
4. **Ek din mein zyada mat badhao** — running time hafte mein 10% se zyada na badhayein
5. **Stretching** — daudne ke baad 3 minute pairon ki stretching, kal ka dard kam hoga

## FAQ

**Q1: Kya AISSEE mein physical exam hota hai?**
Nahi. Written exam + counselling + medical test — bas. Physical fitness medical ke liye aur school life ke liye chahiye.

**Q2: Mera bachcha mota hai — kya selection ke baad dikkat hogi?**
Medical mein weight dekha jaata hai. Abhi se roz ki activity shuru karo — 4 mahine kaafi hain healthy farq ke liye.

**Q3: Kya ladkiyon ke liye fitness standards alag hain?**
Routine sabke liye ek jaisa hai. Standards age ke hisaab se hote hain.

**Q4: Barish/sardi mein running kaise karein?**
Ghar par skipping, stairs chadhna-utarna, spot jogging — bahane nahi, vikalp dhoondo.

**Q5: Kya gym join karwana chahiye?**
Bilkul nahi — is umr mein body-weight exercises hi kaafi aur safe hain.
