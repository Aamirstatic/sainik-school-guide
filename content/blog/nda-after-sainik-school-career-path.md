Har Sainik School parent ke man mein ek tasveer hoti hai: unka beta/beti ek din uniform mein, officer ban kar. Ye article us tasveer tak pahunchne ka **poora map** hai — Class 6 mein admission se lekar commission tak, har step.

## Poora safar ek nazar mein

| Stage | Kab | Kya hota hai |
|-------|-----|--------------|
| 1. AISSEE | Class 5/8 mein | Sainik School admission (exam ✅ 31 Jan 2027) |
| 2. School life | Class 6–12 (7 saal) | Padhai + PT + NDA-oriented personality |
| 3. UPSC NDA written | 12th mein/padhai ke dauraan | Maths 300 + GAT 600 = 900 marks |
| 4. SSB Interview | Written clear karne par | 5 din, 900 marks — personality test |
| 5. Medical | SSB recommend hone par | Armed forces medical standards |
| 6. Training | Merit mein naam aane par | NDA 3 saal + IMA/INA/AFA 1 saal |
| 7. Commission | Training poori hone par | Lieutenant / Sub-Lieutenant / Flying Officer |

## Stage 1–2: Sainik School ke 7 saal (foundation)

Sainik School ka poora design NDA ke liye hai — subah ki PT, disciplined routine, house system mein leadership, current affairs ki aadat. Ye 7 saal bachche ko **SSB ke liye naturally taiyar** karte hain. Bahar ka student SSB ki taiyari 12th mein shuru karta hai; Sainik School ka bachcha Class 6 se usi mahaul mein hota hai.

Is stage par parents ka kaam: [sahi admission process follow karna](/blog/sainik-school-admission-2027-guide/) aur bachche ko pressure-free mahaul dena.

## Stage 3: UPSC NDA Written Exam

UPSC saal mein **2 baar** NDA exam conduct karta hai (NDA-I aur NDA-II). Pattern:

- **Paper 1 — Mathematics:** 300 marks, 2.5 ghante
- **Paper 2 — GAT (General Ability Test):** 600 marks, 2.5 ghante (English + GK + Science + Current Affairs)

**Eligibility (established UPSC rules):**
- Age: 16.5 se 19.5 saal (notification ke hisaab se exact dates)
- 12th pass — Army wing ke liye koi bhi stream; **Air Force aur Navy ke liye Physics, Chemistry, Maths zaroori**
- Unmarried (male/female dono ke liye)

Sainik School mein Class 11–12 mein science stream lene wale students teeno wings ke liye eligible hote hain — isliye zyadatar students PCM lete hain.

## Stage 4: SSB Interview (asli game)

Written sirf ticket hai — selection SSB mein hota hai. 5 din ka process:

1. **Screening** (PPDT + OIR)
2. **Psychology tests** (TAT, WAT, SRT)
3. **GTO tasks** (group activities, outdoor tasks)
4. **Personal Interview**
5. **Conference**

Yahan ratta kaam nahi aata — **personality** check hoti hai. Aur yehi wajah hai ki Sainik School ke students ka record achha hota hai: 7 saal ki hostel life mein leadership, teamwork aur confidence naturally develop hota hai. Koi coaching 7 saal ke character ko 7 din mein nahi bana sakti.

## Stage 5–6: Medical aur Training

SSB recommend hone ke baad medical hota hai (eyesight, height-weight, overall fitness). Phir merit list — written + SSB ke total marks par.

Training:
- **NDA, Khadakwasla (Pune):** 3 saal — graduation + military training
- **Uske baad 1 saal:** IMA Dehradun (Army), INA Ezhimala (Navy), ya AFA Hyderabad (Air Force)

Training ke dauraan stipend milta hai (7th CPC ke anusaar — exact figure waqt ke saath revise hota hai, isliye UPSC notification check kijiye).

## Stage 7: Commission — sapna poora

Training poori hone par officer commission milta hai: Army mein **Lieutenant**, Navy mein **Sub-Lieutenant**, Air Force mein **Flying Officer**. Yahan se ek izzatdaar, secure aur meaningful career shuru hota hai.

## Agar NDA na ho to? (Plan B zaroor rakhein)

Har parent ko ye samajhna chahiye: NDA highly competitive hai. Plan B taiyar rakhiye:
- **TES (Technical Entry):** 12th ke marks par direct SSB — exam nahi
- **CDS/AFCAT:** Graduation ke baad
- **JEE/NEET/UPSC:** [Life after Sainik School ke saare options](/blog/life-after-sainik-school-career-options/) yahan padhiye

Bachche par "NDA hi karna hai" ka pressure mat daliye. Pressure se SSB mein fail hone ke chances *badhte* hain, ghatte nahi.

## FAQs

**Q1. Kya Sainik School mein padhne se NDA pakka hota hai?**
Nahi. NDA ka selection UPSC exam + SSB se hota hai — school koi guarantee nahi deta. Lekin taiyari ka mahaul behtareen milta hai.

**Q2. Girls NDA ja sakti hain?**
Haan, 2021 se girls NDA mein eligible hain. [Girls admission guide](/blog/sainik-school-for-girls-2027/) dekhein.

**Q3. NDA ke liye Class 11 mein kaunsi stream lein?**
PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) — teeno wings ke liye eligible rahenge. Bina PCM ke sirf Army wing.

**Q4. SSB ki taiyari kab se shuru karein?**
Sainik School mein ye Class 6 se naturally shuru ho jaati hai. Formal SSB orientation Class 11–12 mein kaafi hai.

**Q5. NDA mein kitne attempts milte hain?**
16.5–19.5 saal ki age window mein jitne exams aayein — aam taur par 4–6 attempts mil jaate hain.
