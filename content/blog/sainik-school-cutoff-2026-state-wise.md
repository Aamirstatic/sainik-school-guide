## Understanding the AISSEE Cutoff System

The All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE) is conducted annually by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 in Sainik Schools across India. The cutoff marks represent the minimum score a candidate must achieve to qualify for the next stage of the admission process, which typically includes a medical examination and, in some cases, an interview or counselling round.

Unlike many competitive examinations in India, AISSEE does **not** employ negative marking. This means candidates can attempt every question without the fear of losing marks for incorrect answers. However, the absence of negative marking also means that cutoffs tend to be higher than they would be in a negatively-marked exam, since more students attempt all questions.

The cutoff is determined by several factors, including:

- **Total number of applicants** for each school
- **Number of available seats** in each category
- **Difficulty level** of the question paper
- **Category of the candidate** (General, OBC, SC, ST, Defence)
- **State-specific demand** and competition levels

Each Sainik School publishes its own cutoff based on the performance of candidates who applied to that specific school. This means the cutoff for Sainik School Korukonda (Andhra Pradesh) will differ from Sainik School Amaravathinagar (Tamil Nadu), even though both are in South India.

## Factors That Influence Sainik School Cutoff

Understanding the factors that drive cutoff fluctuations can help you set a realistic target for AISSEE 2027.

### 1. Number of Applicants

The single most significant factor affecting cutoffs is the number of candidates appearing for the exam at each school. Over the past three years, AISSEE applications have grown by approximately 12–15% annually. In 2026, approximately 1.8 lakh students appeared for Class 6 and 65,000 for Class 9. This growing pool of aspirants directly pushes cutoffs higher.

### 2. Available Seats

Each Sainik School has a fixed number of seats, typically ranging from 80 to 120 for Class 6 and 40 to 80 for Class 9. The introduction of 69 New Sainik Schools under the Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model has added more seats to the system, but demand continues to outpace supply. The [Sainik School Seat Matrix 2027](/blog/sainik-school-seats-2027-all-schools-matrix/) provides a complete breakdown.

### 3. Difficulty Level of the Exam

A relatively easier paper leads to higher cutoffs and vice versa. In 2026, the Class 6 Mathematics section was considered moderately difficult, while the Language section was relatively easier. This led to a balanced cutoff with no extreme spikes.

### 4. Category Reservation

Sainik Schools follow the Government of India reservation policy:
- **General (Unreserved):** 50% seats
- **OBC (Non-Creamy Layer):** 27% seats
- **SC:** 15% seats
- **ST:** 7.5% seats
- **Defence Category:** Supernumerary seats

The cutoff for reserved categories is naturally lower than for the General category. Defence category candidates (children of serving/ex-defence personnel) enjoy a separate cut-off that is typically 10–20 marks lower.

### 5. State-wise Competition

Schools in states with a high density of aspirants (like Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Bihar) tend to have higher cutoffs compared to schools in states with fewer applicants. For example, Sainik School Lucknow consistently records one of the highest cutoffs in the country.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the cutoff for Sainik School Class 6 in 2026?

**A:** The AISSEE 2026 Class 6 cutoff for the General category ranged from 200 marks (Sainik School East Siang) to 282 marks (Sainik School Lucknow) out of 300. The average cutoff across all 33 schools was approximately 243 marks.

### Q2. Is there negative marking in AISSEE?

**A:** No, there is no negative marking in AISSEE. Candidates can attempt all questions without the risk of losing marks for incorrect answers. This is one of the key factors that contributes to the relatively high cutoffs.

### Q3. How much should I score to get into Sainik School Lucknow?

**A:** For General category admission to Sainik School Lucknow, you should aim for at least 285+ marks out of 300 for Class 6 and 320+ marks out of 400 for Class 9 in AISSEE 2027. The 2026 cutoffs were 282 (Class 6) and 318 (Class 9).

### Q4. Are cutoffs different for boys and girls in Sainik Schools?

**A:** Yes, some Sainik Schools that have introduced co-education or girls' admissions may have separate cutoffs for female candidates. However, the majority of schools still primarily admit boys, and the cutoff data above reflects the general pattern.

### Q5. How are Sainik School cutoffs determined?

**A:** Cutoffs are determined based on the number of available seats, the number of applicants, the difficulty level of the exam, and the reservation policy. Each school publishes its own cutoff after the results are declared by NTA.

### Q6. Can I get admission with marks below the cutoff?

**A:** If your marks are below the official cutoff for your category and school, you will not qualify for the medical examination round. However, you may be considered for admission under the waiting list if seats remain vacant after the first round of counselling.

### Q7. What is the difference between the AISSEE cutoff and the qualifying marks?

**A:** The qualifying marks are the minimum marks set by NTA to pass the exam (typically 25% overall and 33% in each subject). The cutoff is the school-specific minimum mark required to proceed to the next admission stage. The cutoff is almost always higher than the qualifying marks.
