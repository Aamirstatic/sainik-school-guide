## The straight answer

Let me not waste your time: **there is no EWS quota in Sainik Schools.** Not a small one, not a hidden one, not one that comes "through AISSAC counselling if you know the right rule." I opened the Sainik Schools Society's own 63-page AISSAC Standard Operating Procedure and searched it end to end. The word "EWS" appears **zero times**.

That is the whole story, and it is better news than it sounds — because knowing there is no EWS lane stops you from losing time chasing a certificate nobody will ask for, and lets you plan where you actually compete.

## What the official reservation policy actually says

Here is the reservation matrix for the 33 existing Sainik Schools, exactly as the AISSAC SOP lays it out:

| Layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| Home State / UT quota | 67% of seats |
| Other States / UT quota | 33% of seats |
| SC | 15% (within each of the above) |
| ST | 7.5% (within each of the above) |
| OBC (Non-Creamy Layer, central list) | 27% (within each of the above) |
| Wards of serving/ex-serving defence personnel | 25% of the **balance** 50.5% |
| Girls (Class 6) | 10% of vacancies or 10 seats, whichever is more |

The three category reservations (SC/ST/OBC-NCL) together cover 49.5% of seats. Defence wards take 25% of the remaining 50.5% — that is roughly 12.6% of total seats. Everything else — about 37.9% — is the **unreserved pool**. That unreserved pool is where an EWS student competes. There is no separate lane, no relaxed cutoff, no document benefit attached to being economically weaker.

Two more facts worth knowing. First, the qualifying marks are the same for everyone except SC/ST: **25% per section and 40% aggregate** for general candidates (CONFIRMED from the 2026 bulletin pattern). Second, [New Sainik Schools have no reservation at all](/blog/aissac-spot-rounds-how-vacant-seats-get-filled/) — admission runs on the 40% all-India merit route and the 60% school-wise route, with no category or domicile splits. If anything, that makes them the fairest option for an EWS student with a strong rank.

## So what should an EWS family actually do?

**1. Tick "General" on the AISSEE form.** The application has no EWS option. In the 2026 cycle the categories were General, OBC-NCL, SC, ST and defence wards. Marking yourself General is not a disadvantage — it is the honest and correct box, and the form expects it. (EXPECTED for 2027: the new bulletin may tweak the form, but no EWS category has ever existed in AISSEE, and nothing suggests one is coming.)

**2. Don't spend the winter getting an EWS certificate for this admission.** An EWS certificate is not on the document list for Sainik School admission or AISSAC counselling, and counselling staff have nothing to do with it. That certificate takes time and paperwork at the tehsil — spend that time on Maths instead. (It can still be useful for *other* admissions later, like state quota seats — that is a separate decision.)

**3. Treat the general cutoff as your target.** There is no published "general cutoff" — seats fill by category-wise merit — but our guide on [using cutoff trends to set a target score](/blog/how-to-use-cutoff-trends-set-aissee-target-score-2026-10-19/) shows how families estimate a safe zone. As a rule of thumb, aim well above the 40% aggregate floor; general-pool seats at popular schools fill from the top of the merit list down.

**4. Get the domicile certificate right.** For many families this matters far more than any category question. 67% of seats are home-state seats — a correct domicile certificate for the school's state is the single biggest structural advantage available, and it costs nothing to prepare. Our [step-by-step registration walkthrough](/blog/aissee-2027-registration-step-by-step-walkthrough/) covers which documents to keep ready.

**5. Ignore coaching centres selling "EWS admission guidance."** If someone promises a reserved seat route for EWS families in Sainik Schools, they are selling something that does not exist. The same applies to any claim of management seats — Sainik School admission runs entirely through AISSEE merit plus AISSAC e-counselling. (See our [AISSAC spot rounds explainer](/blog/aissac-spot-rounds-how-vacant-seats-get-filled/) for how genuinely vacant seats are filled — also on merit, no back doors.)

## The one place EWS does matter: your own school's scholarships

A reservation quota and a scholarship are different things. Several Sainik Schools offer fee concessions, merit-cum-means scholarships, or state-government freeships for economically weaker students — but these are **school-level policies**, decided after admission, not a central admission quota. If cost is a worry (a real one — boarding schools are expensive), do this: after the seat is allotted, ask the school's administrative officer directly about freeship or scholarship applications. That is where the EWS conversation belongs — not in the AISSEE form.

## Documents you actually need (EWS edition)

A short checklist, so the "do I need the certificate" question dies quietly:

- [ ] Birth certificate (age proof — Class 6: born 1 Apr 2015–31 Mar 2017, Class 9: born 1 Apr 2012–31 Mar 2014, PROJECTED per 2027 pattern)
- [ ] Domicile certificate of the school's state/UT (for the 67% home-state quota)
- [ ] Recent passport-size photograph
- [ ] Transfer certificate / school documents per the bulletin
- [ ] Category certificate **only if** SC, ST, OBC-NCL, or defence ward
- [ ] EWS certificate — **not required** for Sainik School admission

## The bottom line

The system treats your child the same as every other general-category student. That is not a bias to fight — it is clarity to plan with. One exam, one merit list, one unreserved pool. The families who do well here are the ones who aim at the top of that pool instead of sideways at lanes that don't exist.

## FAQs

**Is there an EWS quota in Sainik Schools?**
No. The official AISSAC reservation policy lists SC 15%, ST 7.5%, OBC-NCL 27%, defence wards, the home-state split and a girls quota — there is no EWS reservation. EWS candidates compete in the unreserved (general) pool.

**Do I need an EWS certificate for Sainik School admission?**
No. An EWS certificate is not among the documents required for admission, and the AISSEE application has no EWS category option. EWS families should apply under the General category.

**What should I select as my category in the AISSEE form if my family is EWS?**
Select General (unreserved). The 2026 AISSEE application categories were General, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, and defence wards — there was no EWS option, and no separate 2027 EWS category has been announced.

**Do New Sainik Schools have an EWS quota?**
No. New Sainik Schools have no reservation of any kind — admission is through the 40% all-India merit route or the 60% school-wise route, with no category or domicile reservations.

**Can an EWS student get any fee or hostel concession at a Sainik School?**
Concessions vary by school and state; some schools offer scholarships for economically weaker students, but these are school-level policies, not a central EWS quota. Check with the allotted school after admission.
