## One school — and the most historic one in the chain

Mizoram has exactly one Sainik School, and it happens to be the most historic school in the whole chain. **Sainik School Chhingchhip**, on the outskirts of Chhingchhip village in Serchhip district, about 78 km from the state capital Aizawl, was the first Sainik School in India to admit girls — three years before girls' admission opened across all 33 schools.

That is the fact most parents in Mizoram already know. What they usually do not know is the practical side: how many seats the school has in 2027, what it costs, and how the quotas actually work for a child from Mizoram. This guide puts all of it in one place.

## The school at a glance

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| School | Sainik School Chhingchhip |
| Location | Outskirts of Chhingchhip village, Serchhip district, Mizoram — about 78 km from Aizawl |
| Established | **21 April 2017**; inaugurated 17 May 2017 by the Chief Minister of Mizoram |
| Position in the chain | The **26th** Sainik School in India |
| Type | Co-educational, fully residential, English medium, CBSE-affiliated, Classes VI to XII |
| First batch | 60 boy cadets |
| Daily routine | Roughly **5 AM to 9:30 PM** — PT, classes, games, supervised prep |

The school is young — nine years old this year — and it grew up fast. When the Governor of Mizoram visited in September 2022, the school was six years old and its pioneer cadets were already in Class XI. The campus runs on the classic Sainik School day: up before dawn, physical training, classes, games in the evening, supervised study at night.

## The girls' story: why Chhingchhip matters beyond Mizoram

Here is the part of the story that belongs in the national record, not just in a state guide. In 2018, the Ministry of Defence chose Chhingchhip for a pilot project: induct girl cadets into a Sainik School for the first time. Six girls were selected from 22 applicants and joined the second batch — 54 boys and 6 girls in Class VI. Their names were Elizabeth Malsawmtluangi, Alicia Lalmuanpuii, Malsawnthari, Lalhmunghluii, Jurisa Chakma and Zonunpuii.

The pilot worked. Seeing its success, the government opened girls' admission to all 33 Sainik Schools from the **2021-22 academic session**, and 312 girl cadets were admitted across the country that year. The Governor said it plainly during his 2022 visit: Chhingchhip is the first Sainik School in the country where girl students are admitted — and the rest of the chain followed its example.

Today the standing rule gives girls **10 percent of the vacancies, or 10 seats, whichever is more**, at each of the 33 schools. At Chhingchhip that means 12 of the 120 Class 6 seats in 2027 are for girls. If you are a parent of a daughter in Mizoram, this school is not a compromise option — it is the school where the whole system learned how to admit girls.

## 2027 seats: the numbers that decide your child's chances

The AISSEE 2027 bulletin seat matrix is tentative — it can change before e-counselling — but it is the best picture available before the 2 December form deadline:

| Entry | Seats (2027, tentative) |
|---|---|
| Class 6 — total | **120** |
| Class 6 — boys, Mizoram domicile | 75 |
| Class 6 — boys, other states | 33 |
| Class 6 — girls | 12 |
| Class 9 — boys | 10 (as reported in 2022; confirm against the 2027 bulletin) |

Two things jump out. First, Chhingchhip is one of the bigger Class 6 intakes in the chain — 120 seats puts it near the top, behind only the largest schools like Tilaiya. Second, the **67 percent home-state quota** is doing real work here: 75 of the 108 boys' seats are reserved for Mizoram-domicile boys. A child from Mizoram is not competing against the whole country for most of the seats — only for the 33 other-state seats does the competition go national. That is exactly why the [domicile certificate](/blog/domicile-certificate-sainik-school-guide/) matters so much: without it, your child falls into the smaller, harder pool.

Honestly, the Class 9 route is a different story. Ten seats, boys only as reported, and the competition for them is fierce — Class 9 has only 732 seats across all 33 schools, against 3,160 in Class 6. If your child is in Class 5 now, Class 6 is the realistic door. If your child is already in Class 8, apply for Class 9 by all means — but know the odds going in.

## Fees: the number to confirm before you apply

The annual fee was reported at **around Rs 1.85 lakh per student** in an official briefing in September 2022. Treat that as a guide, not a quote — four years have passed, and the current figure may differ. Confirm it with the school before you plan around it.

What is worth knowing now is that the fee is not the end of the story. Sainik Schools have scholarship schemes and fee concessions for eligible families — defence wards, SC/ST students and low-income families can get real help. Our [scholarship and fee-concession guide](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) explains the routes, and the school's own prospectus lists the schemes it administers. Do not let the headline fee decide the question before you have checked the concessions.

## How to apply: AISSEE 2027

Admission to Chhingchhip is only through the **All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE)**, run by NTA. There is no other route, and anyone offering a seat for a payment is running a scam.

| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Form closes | **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM** |
| Late window (+ Rs 500) | 3–6 December 2026 |
| Correction window | 7–9 December 2026 |
| Exam city announcement | 11 January 2027 |
| Admit card | 25 January 2027 |
| **Exam** | **Sunday, 31 January 2027** |

The fee is **Rs 850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and **Rs 700** for SC/ST. Class 6 applicants must be born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017**; Class 9 between **1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014**. Apply only on the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** — and read our [late-fee explainer](/news/aissee-2027-9-days-left-late-fee-window-not-extension-2026-11-23/) before you decide to wait until the last day.

## FAQs

**How many Sainik Schools are there in Mizoram?**

One — Sainik School Chhingchhip, in Serchhip district, about 78 km from Aizawl. It was established on 21 April 2017 and is the 26th Sainik School in the country.

**How many Class 6 seats does Sainik School Chhingchhip have in 2027?**

120 Class 6 seats in the tentative 2027 bulletin seat matrix — 108 for boys (75 for Mizoram-domicile boys and 33 for boys from other states) and 12 for girls. Seat numbers are tentative and can change before e-counselling.

**Can girls apply to Sainik School Chhingchhip?**

Yes. Chhingchhip was the first Sainik School in India to admit girls — six girl cadets joined in 2018 as a pilot project. Girls now get 10 percent of the Class 6 vacancies at the school, and girls have been admitted to all 33 Sainik Schools since the 2021-22 session.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Chhingchhip?**

The annual fee was reported at around Rs 1.85 lakh per student in 2022. That figure is from a 2022 official briefing and may have changed, so confirm the current fee with the school before you apply. Scholarships and fee concessions exist for eligible families.

**How do I apply to Sainik School Chhingchhip for 2027?**

Through the AISSEE 2027 entrance exam, on the NTA portal at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. The form closes on 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM, the fee is Rs 850 (Rs 700 for SC/ST), and the exam is on Sunday, 31 January 2027.

**What is the home-state quota at Sainik School Chhingchhip?**

67 percent of seats are reserved for Mizoram-domicile candidates and 33 percent for candidates from other states, with SC, ST, OBC-NCL and defence-ward reservations inside those pools. A domicile certificate is what proves the home-state claim.
