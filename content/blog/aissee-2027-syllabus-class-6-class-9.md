# AISSEE 2027 Syllabus: Class 6 Aur Class 9 Ka Poora Breakup

> **🟢 Last verified: 26 September 2026** — ✅ **CONFIRMED** = 2026 NTA bulletin se (2027 mein repeat expected). 🔶 **EXPECTED** = 2027 bulletin aane par final hoga.

Syllabus ko lekar sabse badi galatfehmi ye hai ki parents poori NCERT ratne lagte hain. Sach ye hai ki AISSEE ka paper **NCERT Class 5 (Class 6 entry) aur Class 8 (Class 9 entry) ke level** par based hota hai, lekin sawal concept samajhne par hote hain — ratte par nahi.

2027 ka bulletin abhi nahi aaya hai, lekin exam pattern kayi saalon se stable hai. Niche 2026 bulletin ka verified pattern de raha hoon — 2027 mein bhi yahi expected hai.

## Exam Pattern — Ek Nazar Mein ✅

| | Class 6 | Class 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Mode | Offline OMR, MCQ | Offline OMR, MCQ |
| Duration | **150 minutes** | **180 minutes** |
| Total questions | **125** | **150** |
| Total marks | **300** | **400** |
| Negative marking | **Nahi** | **Nahi** |
| Medium | 13 bhashayein (Hindi/English/regional) | **Sirf English** |

## Class 6 — Subject-Wise Breakup (300 Marks) ✅

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Har question |
|---------|-----------|-------|--------------|
| Mathematics | 50 | 150 | 3 marks |
| Language (Hindi/English/Regional) | 25 | 50 | 2 marks |
| Intelligence (Reasoning) | 25 | 50 | 2 marks |
| General Knowledge | 25 | 50 | 2 marks |
| **Total** | **125** | **300** | — |

**Maths sabse bada game hai** — 50% marks sirf Maths se. Jo bachcha Maths mein strong hai, uska aadha paper wahin nikal jata hai. Isliye [preparation tips](/blog/aissee-2027-preparation-tips/) mein humne Maths ko sabse zyada time diya hai.

### Class 6 — Topic-Wise Focus

**Mathematics (150 marks):** Number system, fractions-decimals, LCM-HCF, percentage, profit-loss, simple interest, ratio-proportion, geometry (basic shapes, perimeter-area), mensuration, data handling. NCERT Class 5 ki poori Maths + Class 6 ke shuruati chapters.

**Language (50 marks):** Comprehension passage, grammar (noun, pronoun, tense, preposition), vocabulary, synonyms-antonyms. Jo medium chunein, usi mein paper hoga.

**Intelligence (50 marks):** Series completion, coding-decoding, odd one out, direction sense, blood relations, analogy, figure-based questions. Ye section practice se hi improve hota hai — roz 15 questions ki aadat daaliye.

**GK (50 marks):** Current affairs (pichhle 6–8 mahine), Indian history basics, geography, science facts, sports, awards, defence-related GK (Sainik School ka paper hai — armed forces se sawal aana swabhavik hai).

## Class 9 — Subject-Wise Breakup (400 Marks) ✅

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Har question |
|---------|-----------|-------|--------------|
| Mathematics | 50 | 200 | 4 marks |
| English | 25 | 50 | 2 marks |
| Intelligence | 25 | 50 | 2 marks |
| General Science | 25 | 50 | 2 marks |
| Social Science | 25 | 50 | 2 marks |
| **Total** | **150** | **400** | — |

**Dhyaan dijiye:** Class 9 ka paper **sirf English medium** mein hota hai. Aur Maths yahan bhi aadhe marks (200/400) lekar aata hai — har question 4 marks ka.

### Class 9 — Topic-Wise Focus

**Mathematics (200 marks):** Number systems, algebra (linear equations, polynomials), geometry, mensuration, trigonometry basics, statistics. NCERT Class 8 + Class 9 ke core chapters.

**English (50 marks):** Comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, writing basics. Roz ek passage padhne ki aadat daaliye.

**Intelligence (50 marks):** Class 6 se thoda advanced — logical puzzles, statement-conclusion, syllogism basics.

**General Science (50 marks):** Physics (motion, force, light), Chemistry (matter, atoms), Biology (cell, human body) — NCERT Class 8 Science.

**Social Science (50 marks):** History (modern India), Geography, Civics (Constitution basics), Economics basics — NCERT Class 8 SST.

## Qualifying Marks — Ye Rule Samajhna Zaroori Hai ✅

2026 bulletin ke anusaar: **har section mein minimum 25% marks + total mein 40% aggregate** chahiye. Matlab:

- Class 6: har section mein 25% (Maths mein 37.5/150, baaki mein 12.5/50) + total 120/300
- Class 9: har section mein 25% + total 160/400
- **SC/ST candidates par ye rule lagu nahi hota** ✅

Iska practical matlab: sirf Maths mein top karke kaam nahi chalega. Har section ki minimum taiyaari zaroori hai. Kai bachche ek section mein fail hokar overall achche marks ke bawajood qualify nahi kar paate.

## FAQ

**Q1. AISSEE 2027 ka syllabus 2026 se alag hoga?**
2027 ka bulletin abhi nahi aaya. Pattern kayi saalon se stable hai, isliye 2026 wala pattern hi expected hai. Koi badlaav hua to bulletin mein hoga.

**Q2. Class 6 ka paper Hindi mein de sakte hain?**
Haan. Class 6 ka paper 13 mediums mein hota hai, jisme Hindi bhi shamil hai. Class 9 ka paper sirf English mein hota hai.

**Q3. Kya negative marking hai?**
Nahi. ✅ AISSEE mein koi negative marking nahi hai — isliye saare questions attempt karne chahiye.

**Q4. Maths itna important kyun hai?**
Class 6 mein 150/300 aur Class 9 mein 200/400 marks Maths se aate hain. Ye sabse bada scoring section hai.

**Q5. GK ke liye kya padhein?**
Pichhle 6–8 mahine ke current affairs, NCERT basics, aur defence/armed forces se judi jaankari. Roz 15 minute news padhne ki aadat kaafi hai.

**Q6. Qualifying marks kitne chahiye?**
Har section mein 25% + total mein 40% aggregate (SC/ST par ye rule lagu nahi).
