If you live in West Bengal and your child dreams of a Sainik School, here is the most important fact up front: **West Bengal has exactly one established Sainik School — Sainik School Purulia.** There is no second old school in the state, no shortcut, and no separate WB-run entrance for it. Everything runs through the single national exam: AISSEE.

That is not bad news. A single school means a concentrated target, and the reservation system gives West Bengal students a serious structural advantage at Purulia. Let me walk you through it.

## West Bengal's Sainik School at a glance

| Detail | Sainik School Purulia |
|--------|----------------------|
| Location | Purulia district, West Bengal (one of the 33 old Sainik Schools) |
| Entry classes | Class 6 and Class 9, through AISSEE |
| Board & medium | CBSE, English medium |
| Management | Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence |
| Campus | Residential; ~5 km from Purulia bus stand, ~8 km from Purulia Junction railway station |
| For parents to know | A helipad on campus, clubs from astronomy to NCC-style training, 70 days of official vacation |

Purulia is one of the older Sainik Schools in the country (established 1962), and it admits both boys and girls — girls' seats in Class 6 follow the Society's **10% of vacancies or 10 seats, whichever is more** rule (CONFIRMED, AISSAC SOP).

## The 67% advantage: why WB students should care

Here is the part many parents miss. In the 33 established Sainik Schools, seats are divided into two buckets (CONFIRMED, AISSAC SOP):

- **67% home-state** — reserved for candidates domiciled in the state where the school sits.
- **33% other-states** — open to everyone else in India.

For a West Bengal domicile holder applying to Purulia, this is enormous. Roughly **two out of every three seats** at the school are fenced off for WB students. A student from, say, Bihar or Odisha can only compete for the remaining third — while a Kolkata or Howrah student competes in both pools, effectively.

This is why the domicile certificate is the single most valuable document a WB applicant holds. If you haven't applied for it yet, [this state-wise domicile guide](/blog/domicile-certificate-online-apply-state-wise-process-2026-10-11/) walks through the West Bengal process.

## Reservation snapshot (33 old schools, CONFIRMED per AISSAC SOP)

| Category | Reservation |
|----------|-------------|
| Home-state domicile | 67% of seats |
| Other states | 33% of seats |
| SC | 15% |
| ST | 7.5% |
| OBC-NCL | 27% |
| Defence wards | 25% of the seats remaining after the above |
| Girls (Class 6) | 10% of vacancies or 10 seats, whichever is more |

These layers stack. A WB-domicile defence ward, for instance, benefits from the home-state pool and the defence quota together.

## The AISSEE 2027 path for a WB applicant

The exam is the same for every school in India — there is no separate Purulia paper. The pattern (2026 basis, expected to repeat):

- **Class 6:** 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes — Maths, Language, Intelligence, General Knowledge.
- **Class 9:** 150 questions, 400 marks, 180 minutes — Maths, English, Intelligence, Science, Social Studies.
- **No negative marking.** Qualifying: 25% in each section plus 40% aggregate (not applicable to SC/ST candidates).

🔶 EXPECTED timeline (pattern-based, from the 2026 cycle): application form October–November 2026; admit card mid-January 2027; **exam 31 January 2027** (CONFIRMED, tentative — NTA calendar); result February 2027; e-counselling February–May 2027; medical exam; final merit list; admission by June.

Application fee 🔶 EXPECTED: **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and Defence personnel/ex-servicemen wards; **₹700** for SC/ST (2026 rates).

## Fees: what Purulia actually costs

Purulia's annual fee is reported by education portals at roughly **₹1,65,130**, rising about 10% a year — treat that as an indicative figure and confirm against the school's official communication at admission time. The money story doesn't end there:

- **Ministry of Defence scholarships** for wards of serving and retired JCOs/NCOs (reported, Purulia's official records).
- **West Bengal state merit-cum-means scholarships** — stringent income criteria, few qualifiers, but worth applying for.
- **Central assistance** schemes apply to cadets across the system.

For the full picture of what a Sainik School seat actually costs — and how families really pay for it — read [What a Sainik School Seat Actually Costs in 2027](/news/sainik-school-fees-2027-cost-scholarships-explained-2026-10-13/).

## What other schools can WB students target?

Purulia's home-state fence works both ways: a WB student can also compete in the **33% other-states pool** at every other Sainik School in India. Popular realistic options parents actually pick: Sainik School Nalanda (Bihar), Sainik School Goalpara (Assam) and Sainik School Sambalpur (Odisha) — all within the east and northeast, all through the same AISSEE. Our [Maharashtra schools guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-maharashtra-admission-guide-2026-10-13/) shows the same structure applied to another state, if you want to compare.

## Your action checklist: October–November 2026

1. **Get the WB domicile certificate now** — it's the key to the 67% pool. Don't wait for the notification.
2. **Keep the AISSEE notification watch** — expected any day in October–November 2026; the window is usually 3–4 weeks.
3. **Fix the photo and signature specs** — rejected documents are the most common form-filling casualty.
4. **Run a syllabus audit** — with ~110 days to 31 January 2027, weak chapters must be found now, not in January.
5. **Take the first full mock** — baseline the child under real OMR timing; no negative marking means attempt strategy matters.
6. **Budget the year** — use the fee figures above to plan; apply for scholarships the moment admission is confirmed, not before.
