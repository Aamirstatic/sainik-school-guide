## The school this website is named after

This one is personal to us. Our website is named after **Sainik School East Siang** — and until today, it had no complete parents' guide of its own on this site. That changes now.

East Siang is the youngest kind of Sainik School there is. It was **raised on 27 August 2018 as the 28th Sainik School of India**, at Niglok village near Pasighat, in a state that shares a 1,129 km border with China and had no Sainik School at all until this one opened.

The first session began on 27 August 2018 with **60 students**. Eight years on, the school is still young — and that youth cuts both ways. There is no sixty-year alumni network and no decades-deep NDA record: its first Class 6 batch, admitted in 2018, reached Class 12 only in 2024–25, so its NDA story is only just beginning. But your child would join a school still being shaped — small batches, a new campus, and a culture where a cadet's batch genuinely leaves a mark.

## Seats for 2027-28: 85 in Class 6, 7 in Class 9

The seat numbers below come from the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin itself — Appendix VI-A for Class 6 and Appendix VI-A1 for Class 9 — and four independent portals reproduce the same figures. They are tentative: the bulletin says vacancies can change as cadets leave.

| Entry | Boys | Girls | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 6** | 75 (52 home state + 23 other states) | 10 (5 home state + 5 other states) | **85** |
| **Class 9** | 4 (1 home state + 3 other states) | 3 | **7** |

Within the home-state boys' seats, the bulletin appendix breaks the 52 down by category: **General 37, Defence 12, ST 3, SC 0, OBC-NCL 0** — a split that reflects Arunachal's demographics. That breakdown is as reproduced by Speedy Brains from the bulletin appendix.

Now the maths that makes East Siang special. Under the standing policy, **67% of seats go to Arunachal-domicile children first** — roughly 57 of the 85 Class 6 seats. In most states, the home-state quota is a small advantage. In Arunachal, with one school and a small applicant pool, it is the whole game. Our [home-state quota explainer](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-67-percent-explained-2026-10-17/) shows how the 67/33 split works, and our [Arunachal state guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-arunachal-pradesh-complete-2027-guide/) works the numbers for an Arunachal family in full.

Two warnings. **First, the Class 9 seats:** seven seats in the whole school, four for boys and three for girls — a lottery ticket, not a plan. **Second, the girls' seats:** 10 in Class 6, because the rule takes the higher of 10% of vacancies or 10 seats. A girl from Arunachal is competing for a protected pool, not against the whole field — and unfilled girls' seats go to boys of the same category, so the quota never goes to waste.

## Admission: only through AISSEE, and the clock is ticking

There is no other route into East Siang — no management quota, no direct admission, no agent who can sell a seat. Anyone offering one is running a scam. Admission is through AISSEE alone, run by NTA:

- **Apply by:** Wednesday, **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**, at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. The fee — ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST — must be paid by the same hour.
- **Late window:** 3–6 December with an extra ₹500. Our [late-fee warning](/news/aissee-2027-6-days-left-late-fee-window-risks-2026-11-26/) explains why it is a rescue boat, not a plan.
- **Correction window:** 7–9 December, for permitted changes only.
- **Exam city:** 11 January 2027. **Admit card:** 25 January 2027. **Exam:** Sunday, **31 January 2027**.
- **Eligibility:** Class 6 — age 10–12 on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017), studying in Class 5. Class 9 — age 13–15 (born 1 April 2012 – 31 March 2014), Class 8 passed.
- **The paper:** Class 6 — 150 minutes, 125 questions, 300 marks (Maths 50 for 150; Language, Intelligence and GK 25 for 50 each). Class 9 — 180 minutes, 150 questions, 400 marks, English only. No negative marking; 25% in each section and 40% in aggregate to qualify (not applicable to SC/ST).

Rank East Siang first in your school choices — it is your home-state school, and the quota is the strongest card you hold. Our [choice-filling guide](/blog/aissee-2027-form-which-sainik-schools-to-list-rank-order/) explains the ranking logic in full.

## Fees: ₹2,17,115 for a new Class 6 cadet

For 2026-27, the school's official fee document puts the total for a new Class 6 cadet at **₹2,17,115** (₹2,15,615 for SC/ST, because the refundable security deposit is lower):

| Item (new admission, 2026-27) | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tuition fees (non-refundable) | ₹1,28,615 |
| Diet charges | ₹68,000 |
| Clothing charges | ₹9,000 |
| Miscellaneous (telephone, haircut, laundry, medical, pocket money) | ₹8,500 |
| Security deposit (refundable, one-time) — General/Defence | ₹3,000 |
| **Total, General** | **₹2,17,115** |

Two things to know before you budget. **First, tuition rises 10% every year**, and clothing, diet and miscellaneous are charged on actuals — so treat ₹2,17,115 as the first-year figure, not the six-year cost. **Second, the fee is paid in instalments**, with a final settlement after MoD scholarships and central assistance. Scholarships and fee concessions exist for eligible families — our [scholarship guide](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) explains the timelines. The 2027-28 fee has not been published yet, so confirm the figure with the school before you plan around it.

## Life on campus: houses, NCC and a school still being built

Cadets are divided into houses, and the house is where a cadet's daily life happens — the housemaster, the inter-house competitions, the loyalties that last decades. The houses confirmed in the school's own publications are **Dibang House** and **Subansiri House** — Dibang won the Inter House Volleyball Championship in October 2025, with Subansiri as runner-up. We are not naming the full house list here because the school does not publish it in one place we can verify; ask the school for the current list when you visit.

Beyond the houses, the school runs what a young Sainik School runs: **NCC training**, a physical fitness programme, clubs, arts, a Talent Development Programme, and the quarterly newsletter *Sanguine*, published since the first year. The school publishes its academic results year-wise on its website, so a parent can check the board results before deciding.

One practical note: the school began in pre-fabricated structures at Niglok, and in 2021 the state government asked for a master plan to shift it to a permanent campus at Ledum in East Siang district. Ask the school what the campus looks like today — a 2021 report is not today's campus.

The daily routine is the standard Sainik School shape — reveille before dawn, PT, classes, games, supervised evening prep, lights out by about 10:30 PM — but the school does not publish its own routine online, so treat the timings as typical, not East Siang's own timetable.

## The honest part: what to ask before you choose

A complete guide has to say the hard thing too. In November 2025, a 12-year-old Class 7 cadet died on the campus at Niglok, and the family alleged bullying by senior students; three staff members were arrested in connection with the case, which was registered at Ruksin police station. We are not retelling the details here — they are a matter of public record, reported by PTI — but a parent choosing a residential school for a 10-year-old should know it happened, and should ask the school, directly, what changed afterwards: hostel supervision at night, the anti-ragging committee, how a junior cadet reports trouble, and who a parent calls when something feels wrong. A school that answers those questions plainly is a school you can trust with your child.

## The Sela Warriors: the Army is coaching your districts

Here is the part of the Arunachal story most parents outside the state never hear. The Indian Army runs a coaching programme for tribal youth from Arunachal's border districts — **Mission Sainik School**, locally called **Sela Warriors** after the Sela Pass in Tawang, run by the Gajraj Corps. On 28 October 2026, the Chief Minister's Office publicly commended it, putting two numbers on record: **59 students have secured Sainik School admissions through the programme, and 75 aspirants are preparing for AISSEE 2027**.

It is not the first time the model has worked. In May 2024, the Army's Spear Corps picked 33 students from remote hamlets and prepared them with 88 theory classes and 18 rounds of testing. The Hindu reported in September 2025 that **32 of the 33 qualified at the national level**, and that Milli Yabi of Sarli became the first to secure final admission — to Sainik School East Siang, the school this website is named after. Our full report is here: [Arunachal's 'Sela Warriors': 59 admissions, 75 preparing for AISSEE 2027](/news/arunachal-mission-sainik-school-sela-warriors-59-admissions-2026-10-28/).

If your child is in a remote or border district, ask your school, your district education office or your nearest Army unit whether a Mission Sainik School batch runs near you — and if one does, join it.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School East Siang have for 2027-28?**

East Siang has 85 Class 6 seats — 75 for boys (52 Arunachal Pradesh domicile and 23 other states) and 10 for girls (5 Arunachal Pradesh and 5 other states) — and 7 Class 9 seats (4 boys and 3 girls). The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin, Appendices VI-A and VI-A1.

**When was Sainik School East Siang founded?**

It was raised on 27 August 2018 as the 28th Sainik School of India, at Niglok village near Pasighat in East Siang district, Arunachal Pradesh. Chief Minister Pema Khandu inaugurated it the same day. Its first session began with 60 students.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School East Siang?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School East Siang?**

For 2026-27, the school's official fee document puts the total at Rs 2,17,115 for a new Class 6 cadet — tuition Rs 1,28,615, diet charges Rs 68,000, clothing Rs 9,000 and miscellaneous Rs 8,500, plus a refundable security deposit. The 2027-28 figure has not been published yet, so confirm it from the school before budgeting.

**Does Sainik School East Siang admit girls?**

Yes — 10 girls' seats in Class 6 for 2027-28, five for Arunachal Pradesh girls and five for girls from other states, and 3 girls' seats in Class 9. Girls live in separate hostels on the same campus.

**What is the Sela Warriors programme?**

Sela Warriors is the Indian Army Gajraj Corps' Mission Sainik School coaching programme for tribal youth from Arunachal's border districts. According to the Chief Minister's Office, 59 students have secured Sainik School admissions through it and 75 aspirants are preparing for AISSEE 2027.
