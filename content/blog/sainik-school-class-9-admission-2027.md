Sainik School Class 9 admission is a **golden opportunity** for students who missed the Class 6 entry. With limited seats (~20 per school), the competition is intense — but the reward is worth it. Class 9 entry gives students 4 years of Sainik School training before NDA.

This guide covers everything specific to Class 9 admission — eligibility, syllabus differences, exam pattern, and preparation strategy.

## Class 9 AISSEE Syllabus

### Mathematics (200 marks)

| Topic | Key Areas |
|-------|----------|
| Number Systems | Rational numbers, exponents, squares, cubes |
| Algebra | Linear equations, factorisation, polynomials |
| Geometry | Quadrilaterals, circles, constructions |
| Mensuration | Surface area, volume of 3D shapes |
| Statistics | Mean, median, mode, probability |
| Trigonometry | Basic ratios, identities |

### English (50 marks)
- Grammar: Tenses, voice, narration, articles
- Comprehension: Passage-based questions
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitutions
- Writing: Short paragraph, letter writing

### General Knowledge (50 marks)
- History: Indian freedom movement, medieval India
- Geography: Physical features, climate, resources
- Civics: Constitution, fundamental rights
- Science: Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics
- Current Affairs: Last 6 months

### Intelligence (50 marks)
- Verbal Reasoning: Analogies, series, coding-decoding
- Non-Verbal Reasoning: Pattern recognition, mirror images
- Logical Reasoning: Syllogisms, blood relations

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How many seats are available for Class 9 in Sainik Schools?
Approximately **15-20 seats per school** for Class 9. Total across all 33+ schools: ~500-600 seats.

### Is the Class 9 AISSEE tougher than Class 6?
Yes. The syllabus is at **Class 8 level** with more marks for Mathematics (200 out of 350). The competition is also higher due to fewer seats.

### Can girls apply for Class 9 admission?
**Yes.** Girls can apply for Class 9 in all Sainik Schools with 25% seats reserved.

### What if my child is in Class 7 — can they apply for Class 9?
**No.** The child must be currently studying in **Class 8** to be eligible for Class 9 AISSEE.

### Is there lateral entry for Class 11?
**No.** Sainik Schools do not offer Class 11 admission. The last entry point is Class 9.
