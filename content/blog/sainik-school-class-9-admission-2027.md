Class 6 mein admission nahi ho paya? Koi baat nahi — **Class 9 direct entry** doosra mauka hai. Har saal hazaaron students Class 9 mein Sainik School join karte hain. Lekin ek baat pehle se samajh lijiye: Class 9 ki seats Class 6 se **kam** hoti hain, aur paper **English medium** mein hota hai. Taiyari usi hisaab se honi chahiye.

✅ CONFIRMED (2026 bulletin; 2027 mein repeat 🔶 EXPECTED). **Koi negative marking nahi.** Qualifying: har section 25% + total 40% (SC/ST par lagu nahi).

**Sabse bada antar:** Maths mein **har question 4 marks** ka hai (Class 6 mein 3 marks). Matlab ek galat step aur 4 marks gaye — accuracy par double dhyan dijiye. Aur poora paper English medium mein hai — Hindi-medium background wale students ke liye ye sabse badi chunauti hai.

## Seats: sach kya hai?

- 33 traditional schools mein Class 9 ki seats **limited** hoti hain — kai schools mein Class 6 ke muqable bahut kam.
- 2026 cycle mein **19 new PPP schools** mein Class 9 admission hua tha (baseline; 2027 🔶 bulletin mein confirm hoga).
- Girls Class 9 mein eligible hain, lekin seats school-wise vary karti hain.

## Taiyari strategy (4 mahine)

**Maths (200/400):** NCERT Class 8 poori + R.S. Aggarwal practice. Algebra, geometry, mensuration — teeno par command honi chahiye. Roz 30 questions.

**English:** NCERT Class 8 (Honeydew + It So Happened) + Wren & Martin grammar. Roz ek comprehension passage — speed banaiye, kyunki paper lamba hai.

**Science:** NCERT Class 8 — physics ke numericals, chemistry ke reactions, biology ke diagrams.

**SST:** NCERT Class 8 History/Geography/Civics + maps practice.

**Intelligence:** R.S. Aggarwal reasoning, roz 20 questions.

## Class 9 entry vs Class 6 entry — kaunsa behtar?

Kai parents poochte hain: "Class 6 mein nahi hua to Class 9 mein try karein ya chhod dein?" Mera jawab: **zaroor try karein.** Lekin antar samajhkar:

| Point | Class 6 Entry | Class 9 Entry |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| Seats | Zyada | Kam (competition zyada) |
| Paper medium | Hindi/English/Regional | Sirf English |
| Taiyari ka time | 7 saal ka NDA foundation | 4 saal ka NDA foundation |
| Syllabus base | NCERT Class 5 | NCERT Class 8 |

Class 9 mein aane wala bachcha 4 saal mein bhi NDA ke liye taiyar ho sakta hai — [NDA ka rasta](/blog/nda-after-sainik-school-career-path/) dekhiye. Der aaye, durust aaye.

## FAQs

**Q1. Kya Class 9 mein admission lena Class 6 se mushkil hai?**
Seats kam hain, isliye competition zyada hai. Lekin syllabus (NCERT Class 8) clear ho to bilkul possible hai.

**Q2. Hindi medium se padhne wala bachcha de sakta hai?**
De sakta hai, lekin paper English mein hoga. Abhi se English mein padhne-likhne ki practice shuru kar dijiye — 4 mahine kaafi hain aadat banane ke liye.

**Q3. Kya Class 9 mein bhi girls apply kar sakti hain?**
Haan, eligible hain. Seats school-wise vary karti hain — bulletin mein check kijiye.

**Q4. Class 9 ke baad NDA ka rasta kitna bachta hai?**
Class 9 se 12th tak 4 saal milte hain — NDA ki taiyari ke liye kaafi hain. [NDA ka poora rasta](/blog/nda-after-sainik-school-career-path/) yahan padhiye.

**Q5. Kya new PPP schools mein Class 9 ki seats hain?**
2026 mein 19 new schools mein thi. 2027 ki final list 🔶 bulletin (expected Oct 2026) mein aayegi.
