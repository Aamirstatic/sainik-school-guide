> **Last verified: 5 October 2026.** Kit contents below come from Sainik School Korukonda's joining instructions and Sainik School Kalikiri's new-admission fee structure. Lists and charges vary by school and year — confirm against your joining letter.

The day your child gets a Sainik School seat, one question will drown all others in your WhatsApp groups: *what exactly do we need to buy?* The useful answer: the school issues the uniform itself, and what you pack is a long practical list that has almost nothing to do with stitching shirts.

![Sainik School cadets in morning assembly wearing the school uniform](/images/inbody/sainik-school-uniform-complete-guide-new-parents.webp)

## The big idea: the school issues the uniform, you pay the bill

This is the single most misunderstood point. You do not take your child to a tailor. On arrival, the school issues the complete uniform kit — from the beret and badge down to the socks and name tabs — and the cost is billed in the admission fees. That is why fee structures carry a "clothing" or "uniform" line: Kalikiri's AY 2025-26 new-admission fee structure, for example, lists a **Uniform Clothing Fee of Rs. 1,500** (CONFIRMED — sskal.ac.in). Korukonda's joining instructions list **39 uniform and clothing items issued to each cadet on arrival and subsequently** (CONFIRMED — sainikschoolkorukonda.org). Standardisation is the point — every cadet dressed identically, down to the shoulder flaps.

## What the issued kit includes

Here is what one school's official list contains (Korukonda's published list; your school's version will look similar):

| Group | Items (quantity) |
|---|---|
| Footwear & socks | Leather shoes black, white and blue running sports shoes (1 pair each); maroon, black and white socks (4 pairs each) |
| Headgear & insignia | Beret, badge, heckle, lanyard, cap, name tabs (5) |
| Belts & tie | School belt, dinner belt, tie, shoulder flaps, kamarbandh/jallar/scarf set |
| Main uniform | Khaki TC shirt + trouser sets (3), white full-sleeve TC shirts (3), grey TC trousers (3) |
| Sports & PT | White/black shorts (3 each), coloured and white t-shirts (3 each), tracksuit, roundneck t-shirt |
| Everyday & bedding | Cotton vests (3), towels (4), handkerchiefs (5), bedsheets (4), pillow covers (2), mosquito net, blanket |
| Others | School bag, swimming trunk, raincoat with cap, sweater, mattress & pillow set |

Two things to notice. First, girl cadets are covered — Korukonda's joining list explicitly includes **black skin tights, inner sportswear, and mesh hair bun/juda nets with hair pins** for girl cadets (CONFIRMED). Second, this list is from a school that has been refining it for years; expect your school's kit to be similar in spirit, with local differences.

## What it actually costs

Korukonda publishes indicative rates alongside the list:

| Component | Amount (approx) |
|---|---|
| 39 uniform & clothing items | Rs. 18,309 |
| Less: clothing fee | (-) Rs. 1,500 |
| Subtotal | Rs. 16,809 |
| Washing charges | (+) Rs. 4,000 |
| **Total (rounded)** | **Rs. 20,800** |

Costs vary by school:

- **Sainik School Kalikiri**: Uniform Clothing Fee of Rs. 1,500 in the new-admission fee structure (CONFIRMED — sskal.ac.in).
- **Sainik School Kodagu**: a Rs. 20,000 one-time uniform fee appears in its fee listing (EXPECTED — careers360; treat as indicative until the school confirms).

Some schools bill item-by-item, others fold uniform into a lump sum. Budget **Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 20,000** for clothing in the first year depending on the school, and read your joining letter line by line. ## What parents must pack themselves

Korukonda's joining list tells parents exactly what the cadet must possess at admission — and it is refreshingly practical.

**Clothing (not issued):** six or more underwear, one pair of dark (black) trousers, two civil pairs — no jeans or baggy pants.

**Stationery:** two ballpoint pens with spare refills, geometry box, colour-pencil and sketch-pen sets, an **Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary**, clipboard, gum, eraser, sharpeners, 12-inch scale, water-colour box with brush, two rolls of brown paper, two marker pens.

**Toiletries:** two combs, hair oil, shampoo, two toothbrushes, large toothpaste, three toilet soaps, two washing soaps, soap cases, Vicks Vaporub, talcum and foot powder, Dettol, Band-Aid, small scissors, nail clipper — plus hair nets and pins for girl cadets.

**Miscellaneous:** a **large black steel box (36" x 15" x 12") with the cadet's name painted in block letters**, one moulded-plastic suitcase, stainless steel glass, shoe-polish kit, slippers, plastic mug, bucket, small torch, four good-quality padlocks, six clothes hangers, naphthalene balls, a small sewing kit, twelve clothes clips, a washing brush, and sanitiser.

The school adds a lovely piece of hard-won advice: cadets lose keys, so **give combination (number) locks** instead of padlocks with keys. Also welcome: a musical instrument, which the school will look after. Explicitly banned inside the hostel: sports gear like hockey sticks, racquets and cricket bats.

Two admissions-day essentials that parents forget: **six latest passport-size colour photos of the candidate**, one family group photo (parents plus all siblings with the candidate), and stamp-size photos of the candidate, father, mother and guardian for the visitor's pass (CONFIRMED — Korukonda joining instructions).

## The rules that come with the uniform

Three rules from Korukonda's rulebook worth knowing before packing:

1. **Uniform on all days except Sundays and holidays.** It is the default dress code — not optional.
2. **No ornaments of any kind.** No sacred threads, talismans, amulets, rings, ornaments or religious symbols (CONFIRMED).
3. **Arrive smartly dressed.** The first instruction under clothing: report neatly and smartly dressed. It sets the tone.

## A quick packing checklist

Use this the weekend before reporting:

- [ ] Steel box (36"x15"x12") with name painted in block letters + moulded suitcase
- [ ] Clothing: 6+ underwear, 1 dark trouser, 2 civil pairs (no jeans); girl-cadet items if applicable
- [ ] Stationery incl. Oxford dictionary and geometry box
- [ ] Toiletry kit + first-aid basics (Vicks, Dettol, Band-Aid, foot powder)
- [ ] Combination locks, clothes hangers, naphthalene balls, sewing kit, clothes clips
- [ ] Slippers, plastic mug, bucket, small torch, shoe-polish kit
- [ ] Six passport photos, one family photo, stamp photos of parents/guardian
- [ ] Musical instrument (welcome); hockey stick, cricket bat (banned — leave home)

And one money rule: fees, including the uniform billing, are paid through the school's stated mode only — do not hand cash to anyone claiming to arrange uniforms or admission from outside. ## FAQs

**Does the school provide the uniform, or do parents buy it?**
The school issues the full uniform kit on arrival and bills it in the admission fees — parents do not stitch uniforms outside. The charge varies: Korukonda's published list totals roughly Rs. 20,800 including washing charges, while Kalikiri charges a Rs. 1,500 uniform clothing fee on top of kit billing.

**How much does the Sainik School uniform kit cost?**
Korukonda's published list prices about 39 items at roughly Rs. 18,309, totalling about Rs. 20,800 after the clothing-fee adjustment and washing charges. Other schools fold uniform into lump-sum admission fees — Rs. 20,000 is listed for Kodagu, Rs. 5,900 for Kittur Rani Channamma. Confirm your joining letter.

**What should parents send with the child for admission?**
Each school publishes a joining list. Korukonda's asks for six or more underwear, one dark trouser, two civil pairs without jeans, stationery including an Oxford dictionary, a toiletry kit, a large steel box with the cadet's name painted on it, a suitcase, padlocks, a torch and sewing items. Six passport photos plus a family photo are also required.

**Can girls wear the Sainik School uniform?**
Yes. Girls are admitted to Sainik Schools and follow the same uniform. Korukonda's joining list includes girl-specific items — black skin tights, inner sportswear, and mesh hair bun or juda nets with hair pins.
