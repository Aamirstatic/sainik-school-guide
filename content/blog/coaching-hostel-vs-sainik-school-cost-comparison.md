Last year, a mother from Kanpur was torn between two plans for her Class 5 son: Sainik School, or a Kota-style coaching hostel from Class 6. She'd heard coaching was the "serious" option and Sainik School was cheap but uncertain. Nobody had shown her both bills side by side. So let's do exactly that — with numbers you can verify yourself.

## What you're actually comparing

First, a fairness note: these are not the same product. A **Sainik School** is a complete residential school — CBSE academics, hostel, food, uniforms, sports, NDA-oriented discipline — for Classes 6 to 12. A **coaching hostel** is a hostel near a coaching institute, usually in a hub like Kota, where the child attends school somewhere and spends evenings in foundation/JEE/NEET-oriented classes.

One replaces your child's schooling entirely. The other stacks extra schooling on top of schooling. The price difference is exactly what you'd expect.

## The year-one bill, side by side

| Item | Sainik School | Coaching hostel (Kota-style) |
|---|---|---|
| Annual tuition + boarding | ~₹1–1.5 lakh (all-inclusive, indicative) | Coaching fee ₹41,000–54,000 (Class 6–8 foundation) + hostel ₹8,000–18,000/month with food |
| Hostel/food | Included | ₹96,000–2,16,000 a year |
| Regular school fees | Included (CBSE in-house) | ₹12,000–50,000 a year (dummy/private school) |
| Uniforms, books, stationery | Included or nominal | ₹10,000–30,000 a year |
| Travel home | 2–3 trips a year | 2–3 trips a year (Kota is far for most families) |
| **Realistic year-one total** | **~₹1–1.5 lakh** | **~₹2–3.5 lakh** |

The foundation coaching fee looks small on the poster — around ₹41,000 for Class 6, ₹54,000 for Class 8 at a major Kota institute — and that's where the sales pitch stops. The hostel is the second fee. Nobody posters the hostel. And for a fully residential bundled program, one Kota brand lists ₹3,30,000 to ₹3,50,000 per year outright — no stacking needed, because it's all one bill.

## The 7-year maths that changes the conversation

This is the table the Kanpur mother needed. Seven years of schooling, Class 6 to Class 12:

| Scenario | Rough 7-year total | Who pays this |
|---|---|---|
| **Sainik School, full fee** | ~₹7–10.5 lakh | Family with no scholarship eligibility |
| **Sainik School, with state scholarship/fee concession** | ~₹2–5 lakh (often less) | Most domicile families — income-based slabs cover 25–100% of tuition in several states |
| **Coaching hostel, budget setup** | ~₹14–17 lakh | Local coaching + basic hostel + school |
| **Coaching hostel, branded Kota setup** | ~₹21–28 lakh | Big-name institute + premium hostel, 7 years |

These are *ranges*, not quotations — fees move every year, and your child's run has seven of them. But the gap is not close: even at full fee, a Sainik School education costs less than *half* of a branded coaching-hostel run over the same years. With a state scholarship — which a large share of domicile cadets qualify for — the Sainik School path can cost a quarter or less. See our [scholarship and fee-concession guide](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) for how the income slabs work.

## The hidden costs checklist — both sides

Neither bill is the whole story. Run this checklist before you decide:

- [ ] **The entrance exam fee** — AISSEE itself costs ₹850 (General/OBC-NCL/Defence) or ₹700 (SC/ST) per the 2026 bulletin pattern; coaching hostels charge an admission test or direct entry.
- [ ] **The "one more year" cost** — the coaching route's biggest risk: a Class 11–12 student who doesn't crack JEE/NEET often pays for a dropper year (₹1–2 lakh more). A Sainik School student has a complete Class 12 certificate and the NDA route regardless.
- [ ] **Health and homesickness** — hostel mess food, sleep schedules, and a 12-year-old far from home carry real costs. Sainik Schools are built for young cadets — house masters, structured routine, medical care on campus. A commercial hostel is not a school; supervision quality varies wildly.
- [ ] **What the routine buys** — a Sainik School day includes PT, games, and study hours by design; the "discipline" parents pay coaching institutes to impose is the school's normal Tuesday.
- [ ] **The exit options** — Sainik School keeps every door open: NDA (the designed path), JEE, NEET, UPSC, and regular careers. The coaching-hostel path narrows early toward one exam; changing direction at 16 costs a year and a fee.

## When the coaching hostel actually makes sense

Honest comparison means admitting the other side's case. If your child has already decided on IIT or medicine with total clarity — and I mean *their* clarity — and is in Class 9 or 10, a focused coaching environment near home can be the sharper tool. And if AISSEE doesn't work out, the coaching route is a legitimate Plan B. Parents weighing the two exam routes should also read our [UP Sainik School vs AISSEE guide](/blog/up-sainik-school-vs-aissee-which-form-to-fill-2026-10-07/) — the UP exam is a second, often easier, doorway to the same kind of schooling.

But for a Class 5 or 6 child, a coaching hostel means paying double for a *less* complete education — no campus life, no sports culture, no peer group growing up together — while a Sainik School gives the whole thing for less. That's not ideology. That's the bill.

## The bottom line

A Sainik School is the cheapest full residential education in India that also happens to be one of the most disciplined; the coaching hostel is the most expensive way to get half of one. Do the maths once, on paper, with your own numbers — then decide with a clear head.

## FAQs

**Is a Sainik School cheaper than a coaching hostel?**

Yes, substantially. Sainik School annual fees sit around ₹1 to 1.5 lakh per year all-inclusive, while a Kota-style coaching hostel for a Class 6 to 8 student typically totals ₹2 to 3.5 lakh per year once coaching fees, hostel, food, and school fees are added — and Sainik Schools additionally offer state scholarships and fee concessions that coaching institutes generally do not.

**What does the 7-year total cost of a Sainik School education come to?**

Roughly ₹7 to 10.5 lakh for all seven years from Class 6 to Class 12 at current indicative rates of ₹1 to 1.5 lakh per year, before scholarships. Many families end up paying far less because of state government scholarships and income-based fee concessions.

**How much does a Kota coaching hostel cost per year for Class 6 to 8?**

Published foundation coaching fees are modest — for example, around ₹41,000 to ₹54,000 a year for Class 6 to 8 at a major Kota institute — but hostel, food, school fees, stationery and travel typically add ₹1 to 2 lakh more. Fully residential programs bundle everything at about ₹3.3 to 3.5 lakh per year.

**Does a Sainik School include coaching-type preparation?**

A Sainik School is a full CBSE residential school with a defence-oriented routine — not an exam factory. The structured routine, peer environment and physical training prepare students for the NDA over years, but if your child also wants IIT or NEET coaching later, that would be an additional cost.

**Which option has better scholarships?**

Sainik Schools have a clear edge: many state governments fund tuition scholarships and fee concessions for domicile cadets, with income-based slabs and defence-family support. Coaching institutes offer merit scholarships — typically up to 90 percent at the top Kota brands — but these are won by the highest scorers, not allocated by income.
