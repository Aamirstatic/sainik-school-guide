> **Last verified: 7 October 2026.** Day-one procedures differ slightly from school to school — your child's **joining letter is the final word** on dates, documents and kit. The specifics below are grounded in official joining instructions published by Sainik Schools (Jhansi, Chandrapur and East Siang), with ✅ **CONFIRMED** where the schools' documents state it and 🔶 **EXPECTED** where it is the usual pattern.

## The Morning Everything Changes

The result is out. The medical is done. Your name is on the merit list. And then a letter arrives — the joining instructions — telling you to report to a campus hundreds of kilometres away, on a specific date, with a long list of documents and a trunk full of kit.

Every parent I have spoken to about this day remembers two things: how proud they were driving in through the school gate, and how hard it was to drive out through it. That second part is the whole story of the first day. Let me walk you through it so nothing on that day surprises you.

## Before the First Day: The Joining Letter Is Your Bible

Once counselling and medicals are complete, the allotted school sends joining instructions — usually published on the school's website too. Read it twice. Everything below is the *usual* pattern; your school's letter overrides it.

### The documents to prepare

Schools are exacting about paperwork, and they warn you in plain words: admission is not possible without the complete set. Based on official admission documents published by Sainik School East Siang (✅ CONFIRMED) and Sainik School Jhansi's joining instructions (✅ CONFIRMED), the typical list looks like this:

- **Agreement bond** executed on non-judicial stamp paper of Rs 100 value (East Siang lists the exact proforma)
- **Birth certificate** in original, as proof of date of birth
- **Domicile / residence certificate** from the competent authority
- **Caste certificate** from the competent authority (if applicable)
- **Transfer certificate** from the previous school (Class 9 entry)
- **Medical certificate** from a government medical officer
- **Income certificate** and income affidavit (Jhansi)
- **Anti-ragging affidavits** by parent and cadet (Jhansi)
- **Six recent passport-size photographs** of the cadet, plus family photographs (East Siang)
- **Blood group certificate** and vaccination details (Jhansi)
- **Aadhaar cards** of cadet and parents
- **SBI bank account** opened in the cadet's name — East Siang's instructions specifically ask parents to open one, and many schools bank with SBI
- **Ex-servicemen:** discharge certificate copy

Start collecting these the moment the merit list is out. Stamp papers, medical certificates and bank accounts take days, not hours.

### The kit to pack

This is where parents either overpack or underpack. The schools' official kit lists are remarkably detailed — Sainik School Chandrapur's joining instructions (✅ CONFIRMED) list separate boys' and girls' items. A typical packing list:

| Category | What to pack (typical) |
|---|---|
| Clothing | Vests, underwear (4–8 sets), handkerchiefs, formal civil pairs, night dress, black and white cotton socks, personal clothing as required |
| Footwear | Bathroom slippers, black leather school shoes, white sports shoes |
| Stationery | Notebooks (ruled + plain), pens, pencils, eraser, scale, geometry box, colour pencils, sketch pens, clipboard, dictionary |
| Toiletries | Bathing soap, soap case, toothpaste, toothbrush, hair oil, shampoo, washing soap, talcum and foot powder, nail clipper |
| Miscellaneous | Bucket and mug, lock and key, towel, bedsheet, mosquito net, torch, umbrella, school bag, water bottle, black polish and shoe brush, comb, clothes hangers |
| Girls | Innerwear, slips, hair bun and hairnet, sports bra, sanitary pads |

And the things that must **not** go in the trunk (✅ CONFIRMED from East Siang's rules): junk food, mobile phones and electronic items, cash beyond Rs 300, and valuables like gold chains. Confiscated items are not returned — the rules are explicit about this.

## A Typical First Day: Hour by Hour

Every school runs its own schedule, but the shape of the day is remarkably consistent. Treat this as the pattern (🔶 EXPECTED), not a timetable:

| Time | What usually happens |
|---|---|
| Morning | Arrive at the school gate with your child and the luggage. Head to the reporting/registration point named in the joining letter. |
| Late morning | **Document verification** — counters check each document against the school's checklist. Carry originals *and* photocopies. This is the slowest part; queues are long. |
| Midday | Fee payment / demand draft submission if applicable; collection of the house allocation and dormitory/room assignment. |
| Afternoon | Kit inspection and issue (uniform pieces the school provides), dormitory settling — bed, almirah, lock and key. Hair standards are checked from day one. |
| Evening | **The goodbye.** Parents leave the campus. This is the hardest twenty minutes of the day, for you more than for them. |
| Night | First night in the house with the housemaster and new housemates. Dinner in the mess, early lights-out. The routine begins tomorrow. |

One practical tip: keep one set of clothes, water and snacks in a small hand bag — the trunk goes into a queue and may reach the dormitory hours after you do.

## The Goodbye Matters More Than You Think

Sainik Schools are residential — parents are not permitted to stay. Plan your return travel for the same day. On East Siang's official rules, parent visits are restricted to the second Saturday of each month (✅ CONFIRMED), and several schools follow a similar pattern. Check your school's visiting rules in the joining letter so you know exactly when you will see your child next.

Keep the goodbye short and confident. A long, tearful farewell at the gate sets the tone for the whole week — your child takes their emotional cue from your face. Tell them you are proud, tell them when you will see them next, and leave. The housemaster has seen a hundred first days and knows exactly what to do with a wobbly child.

## The First Week: Settling In

The first week is usually an orientation, not academics at full speed (🔶 EXPECTED). Children learn the routine — morning PT, assembly, classes, games, study hours, lights-out — and the strange new geography of house, classroom, mess and drill square. They meet their housemaster, who is part teacher, part warden, part parent for the next seven years, and their housemates, who become something closer than friends.

Homesickness peaks around day 3 to 5 in most cases, and then it fades. If it doesn't fade in a few weeks, talk to the housemaster early — read our full guide on [homesickness](/blog/homesickness-in-sainik-school-how-parents-can-help/).

## FAQs

**Can parents stay with their child on the first day at Sainik School?**
No — Sainik Schools are residential campuses and parents leave the same day after reporting and verification are done. Plan your return travel for the same day.

**When does the first day at Sainik School happen?**
After the merit list and medicals, your allotted school sends joining instructions with the exact reporting date, which falls near the start of the new session. Check the school's website and the joining letter — the date varies by school.

**What documents are needed on the first day at Sainik School?**
Typically: the agreement bond on Rs 100 stamp paper, birth certificate, domicile or residence certificate, caste certificate if applicable, transfer certificate, passport photos, Aadhaar cards and an SBI bank account in the cadet's name. Your school's joining letter is the final word — lists differ slightly.

**Can I call my child on the first day at Sainik School?**
Cadets are not allowed mobile phones — see [Can Students Keep Mobile Phones in Sainik School?](/blog/can-students-keep-mobile-phones-in-sainik-school/). Calls go through the housemaster. Note down the housemaster's and house phone numbers on reporting day — that is your lifeline for the first weeks.

**What should my child pack for the first day at Sainik School?**
Your school's joining letter carries the exact kit list. Typically it includes personal clothing, underwear, vests, handkerchiefs, black and white socks, slippers, lock and key, notebooks, geometry box, toiletries, bucket and mug, torch, umbrella and water bottle. Mobile phones, junk food, cash and valuables are prohibited — also see our [uniform kit guide](/blog/sainik-school-uniform-complete-guide-new-parents/).

**Is it normal for a child to cry on the first day at Sainik School?**
Completely normal — most children feel wobbly in the first days, and it usually passes within a week or two. Housemasters and seniors expect it; a calm goodbye from you helps more than a long emotional one.
