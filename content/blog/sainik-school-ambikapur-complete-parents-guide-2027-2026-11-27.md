## Chhattisgarh's only Sainik School

Here is the fact that shapes everything else in this guide: **Sainik School Ambikapur is the only Sainik School in Chhattisgarh.** A child from Raipur, Bilaspur, Durg or Bastar who wants a Sainik School education has exactly one in-state option — and that makes the home-state quota the whole story.

Under the Sainik Schools Society rules, **67 percent of seats go to children domiciled in the home state**. At Ambikapur that means 67 of the 100 Class 6 seats are reserved for Chhattisgarh children, competing only against each other. The remaining 33 seats are open to children from every other state in India. If your child holds a Chhattisgarh domicile, this is the most favourable seat arithmetic of any Sainik School in the country — one school, one state, no sharing. If you are applying from outside the state, the climb is steeper, and you should know that before you fill the form, not after the result.

The school sits at Mendra Kalan, about 18 km from Ambikapur town in Surguja district, in the north of the state — the tribal heartland the school was deliberately placed to serve. It was established on **1 September 2008**, which makes it one of the younger Sainik Schools: old enough to have a settled campus and a full Class 6-to-12 run, young enough that it does not carry the decades of NDA legend that schools like Kapurthala or Kazhakootam do. That is not a weakness. It is simply the honest position — and for a parent, a younger school with 100 Class 6 seats and a 67 percent home-state quota is often a better bet than a famous school with 80 seats and a queue from three states.

## A school that started in 2008 and stayed put

Ambikapur did not spend years in a temporary campus the way some younger schools did. It was established in September 2008 and now runs from its permanent 84-acre campus at Mendra Kalan — a big, open site on the Ambikapur–Bilaspur highway side of town, far enough from the city to be a proper residential campus and close enough that the town is a short drive away.

That distance matters more than it sounds. Parents visiting from Raipur face a long road journey, and the first-term homesickness conversation is harder when home is 300 km away rather than 30. On the other hand, the isolation is the point: a residential school works because the campus is the child's whole world for the term, and Mendra Kalan gives it room to be one — grounds, parade square, hostels and classrooms on a single site, with the NCC as an integral part of daily life rather than an afterthought. The school has an Independent Company of NCC covering Junior and Senior Divisions across all three wings, which is unusual even among Sainik Schools and worth asking about on a visit: it means a cadet here can realistically experience Army, Navy and Air Force training before choosing a path.

One honest note on the school's leadership: profiles name different Colonel-rank principals, so this guide does not assert a current principal's name. Check the school's official website before quoting it anywhere.

## The seats: 100 in Class 6, 18 in Class 9

This is the part parents get wrong most often, because older admission guides quote different numbers. Some still list **30 seats for Class 9** — that was the figure in earlier cycles. The AISSEE 2027 bulletin says **18**, and the bulletin is the only source that settles it. We read both appendices directly from the official NTA bulletin PDF for this guide, so these are the numbers to plan on. They are tentative — the bulletin says so — but they are the official tentative numbers.

| **Total** | **12** | **6** | **18** |

Read that Class 9 table twice before deciding. Eighteen seats, of which only 12 go to Chhattisgarh children — and within those, the category split is brutally narrow: a General-category Chhattisgarh boy is competing for 3 seats. Class 9 entry at Ambikapur is a lottery ticket, not a plan. Class 6, with 100 seats, is where this school is genuinely winnable — especially for a Chhattisgarh-domiciled child, where 62 boys' seats and 5 girls' seats are reserved for the home state alone.

For context, all 33 Sainik Schools together have 3,160 Class 6 seats and 732 Class 9 seats in 2027-28. Ambikapur's 100 Class 6 seats put it on the larger side. And the reservation math inside those seats follows the standard pattern: SC 15%, ST 7.5%, OBC-NCL 27%, with 25% of the remainder for defence wards — the same rules we explain in our [home-state quota guide](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-explained/).

## The fee: honest about what we know

Sainik School fees are the least transparent number in this whole process, and Ambikapur is no exception. Here is what we can actually stand behind:

- The **latest published figure** for a Class 6 cadet is **₹1,98,842 for 2025-26** (₹1,97,342 for SC/ST), from the school's own fee document as reproduced by The Speedy Brains. The breakdown: tuition ₹1,16,923, messing ₹45,386, uniform ₹1,500, pocket money ₹1,500, incidentals ₹30,533, plus refundable caution money of ₹3,000 (₹1,500 for SC/ST).
- **Careers360 lists ₹2,00,252** for the school — a different figure from a different year, and the two cannot both be the 2027-28 fee.
- The school's **2026-27 fee circular covers continuing cadets only** (Classes VII–XII); its Class VII total is ₹1,96,671, with tuition at ₹1,28,615. Tuition rises about 10 percent a year under Sainik Schools Society rules.

So the honest answer is: budget **around ₹2 lakh for the first year**, confirm the exact 2027-28 figure from the school before paying, and remember that the fee can be paid in lump sum or in 2 or 4 instalments (₹100 per instalment). Defence wards and SC/ST families should also ask the school directly about scholarships and fee concessions — the school's fee sheet lists different caution money by category, and concession rules are applied at admission, not after.

## A day at Ambikapur

We could not verify Ambikapur's own published daily timetable, so what follows is the standard Sainik School day — the pattern every school in the system runs, with local timings varying slightly. Do not quote these hours as the school's own.

Reveille is early, before 6 AM, and the day starts with PT and drill. Then breakfast, assembly, and a full morning of classes — six or seven periods, English medium, CBSE syllabus. Afternoons are for games and NCC, and this is where Ambikapur's three-wing NCC company shows up in the routine rather than on a brochure. Evenings are prep: supervised study where the day's lessons are revised and homework is finished, because there is no going home to do it. Lights out is early, and the next morning starts the same way.

For an 11-year-old joining Class 6, the shock is not the study — it is the routine. Waking yourself, making your bed, keeping your kit in order, eating on time, living with thirty other boys in a dormitory. Parents who prepare their child for the routine, not just the exam, have a far easier first term. Our [Chhattisgarh guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-chhattisgarh-complete-guide-2027/) covers the same ground for the state, and the school's own [profile page](/schools/sainik-school-ambikapur/) has the basic facts in one place.

## How to apply

Admission is through **AISSEE 2027 only** — there is no direct admission, no management quota, and no donation route. The form is open now on the official NTA portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**, and it closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. Our advice, published this morning, is to submit by **today, Friday 27 November** — the weekend is your buffer, not your plan. The fee is ₹850 (General/OBC-NCL/defence) or ₹700 (SC/ST); the late window runs 3–6 December with an extra ₹500, and corrections can be made from 7–9 December.

- **Class 6:** age 10–12 on 31 March 2027 — born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017 — and currently in Class 5. Boys and girls both eligible.
- **Class 9:** age 13–15 on 31 March 2027 — born between 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014 — and must have passed Class 8 from a recognised school at the time of admission. English only.
- **The exam:** Sunday, 31 January 2027. Class 6 is 150 minutes, 125 questions, 300 marks; Class 9 is 180 minutes, 150 questions, 400 marks. No negative marking; 25% per section and 40% aggregate to qualify (not applicable to SC/ST).

After the exam comes the result, then AISSAC e-counselling — online, no fee, run by the Sainik Schools Society — where your school preferences and the home-state quota decide the allotment. Choose your preferences carefully: putting Ambikapur first as a Chhattisgarh-domiciled applicant is the single highest-value decision in the whole process, and our [AISSAC choice-filling guide](/blog/how-to-choose-school-preferences-aissac-counselling/) explains why the order matters more than most parents think.

## The honest questions to ask

Before you commit a year of your child's life to this school, ask these — of the school, of parents whose children study there, and of yourself:

1. **Is my child ready to live 18 km outside a small town, 300 km from Raipur?** Distance from home is the hardest part of the first term, and it is worse when visits mean a full day's travel.
2. **Are we applying for Class 6 or Class 9?** If Class 9, understand that 18 seats is a long shot and keep a parallel plan — RMS, JNVST, or next year's Class 6 attempt for a younger sibling.
3. **Can we afford roughly ₹2 lakh a year, every year, for seven years?** The first-year fee is not the cost. The cost is seven years of it, rising about 10 percent a year.
4. **Does my child actually want this?** A Sainik School is a wonderful education and a hard life in equal measure. A child who wants to be there thrives; a child who was pushed there counts the days. Ask them, and listen to the answer.

Ambikapur is not the most famous Sainik School, and it does not pretend to be. What it is, is Chhattisgarh's only one — with 100 Class 6 seats, a 67 percent home-state quota, an 84-acre campus and an NCC company that covers all three services. For a Chhattisgarh family with a child in Class 5, that combination is genuinely hard to beat. The form closes on 2 December. Today is the day to fill it.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Ambikapur have for 2027-28?**

Ambikapur has 100 Class 6 seats — 90 for boys (62 Chhattisgarh domicile and 28 other states) and 10 for girls (5 Chhattisgarh and 5 other states) — and 18 Class 9 seats (13 boys and 5 girls). The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin, Appendices VI-A and VI-A1.

**When was Sainik School Ambikapur founded?**

It was established by the Government of India on 1 September 2008, and now runs from its permanent 84-acre campus at Mendra Kalan, about 18 km from Ambikapur town in Surguja district, Chhattisgarh.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Ambikapur?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Ambikapur?**

The latest published figure is ₹1,98,842 for a Class 6 cadet in 2025-26 (₹1,97,342 for SC/ST), from the school's own fee document. Careers360 lists ₹2,00,252. Tuition rises about 10 percent a year, so treat the 2027-28 figure as around ₹2 lakh and confirm it from the school before paying.

**Does Sainik School Ambikapur admit girls?**

Yes — girls have been admitted since the 2021-22 academic session. For 2027-28 there are 10 girls' seats in Class 6 (5 Chhattisgarh, 5 other states) and 5 in Class 9.

**Is Sainik School Ambikapur the only Sainik School in Chhattisgarh?**

Yes. It is the state's only Sainik School, which is why the 67 percent home-state quota matters so much here — 67 of the 100 Class 6 seats are reserved for Chhattisgarh-domiciled children.
