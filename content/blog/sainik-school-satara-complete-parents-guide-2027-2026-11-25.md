## The school where the whole idea began

If you live in Maharashtra and your child dreams of the armed forces, there is one school every conversation eventually reaches: **Sainik School Satara**. Not because it is the biggest or the newest, but because it is the first. The entire Sainik School idea started here, on **23 June 1961**, when Defence Minister **V. K. Krishna Menon** inaugurated the school on the vision of Maharashtra's then Chief Minister **Yashwantrao Chavan**. The aim, stated plainly at the time, was to prepare boys from financially weaker sections — academically, mentally and physically — for entry into the National Defence Academy, and to remove the regional imbalance in armed-forces recruitment. Sixty-five years later, that is still the school's job description.

Satara sits on the **Pune-Bangalore Highway**, on a **115-acre** campus in the city of Satara (Post Box 20, Sadar Bazar, Satara 415001). It is a fully residential, English-medium, CBSE-affiliated school for Classes VI to XII, run by the Sainik Schools Society under the Ministry of Defence, and a member of the Indian Public Schools' Conference, with a **Primary Sainik School** attached.

## The seats: 100 in Class 6, 21 in Class 9

Here is the number that shapes every Maharashtra family's strategy this year. For 2027-28, Satara has **100 Class 6 seats and 21 Class 9 seats** (tentative, from the bulletin):

| Entry | Boys | Girls | Total (tentative) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class 6 | 90 | 10 | 100 |
| Class 9 | 18 | 3 | 21 |

The Class 6 split is where the home-state quota does its work. Of the 90 boys' seats, **62 go to Maharashtra-domicile boys and 28 to boys from other states**; of the 10 girls' seats, **five are for Maharashtra girls and five for girls from other states**. Inside the Maharashtra boys' quota, the category split is reported as SC 9, ST 4, OBC-NCL 17, Defence 8 and General 24. For Class 9, the 18 boys' seats split **13 Maharashtra and 5 other states**, and the 3 girls' seats split **1 Maharashtra and 2 other states**.

Two things to read into this. First, Satara is one of the tougher schools to get into — its Class 6 cut-off is among the highest in the country, with Maharashtra-quota General cut-offs reported around 225–240 out of 300 in recent years. A seat here is difficult, not impossible — but your child needs to be scoring at that level in mocks before you count on it.

Second, if Satara is your dream school but the cut-off worries you, remember Maharashtra has a second classic school: **Sainik School Chandrapur**, with 105 Class 6 seats and 26 Class 9 seats this year. Same exam, same form — just a different preference in counselling. We explain the whole quota system, including the 25% defence share inside each category, in our [home-state quota guide](/blog/home-state-quota-sainik-schools-67-percent-explained-2026-10-17/).

## The NDA record — and why "Ajinkyan" means something

Let us be straight about the numbers everyone quotes. The school is reported to have produced **more than 580 NDA and UPSC selections** to date, placing it consistently among the top-performing Sainik Schools in the country. That is a reported figure, not an officially audited tally — but the names behind it are verifiable, and they are the reason Satara's reputation is what it is.

Its alumni include **Air Chief Marshal (Retd.) Pradeep Vasant Naik**, a former Chief of the Air Staff; **Vice Admiral S. V. Bhokare**; **Lt Gen Rajendra Ramrao Nimbhorkar**; and **Lt Gen Pradeep Chandran Nair**. Beyond the uniform, Satara's old boys include film director **Mukul S. Anand**, actor **Rakesh Roshan**, and journalist **Sudhir Gadgil**. The students are known as **Ajinkyans**, after the Ajinkyatara fort that watches over the city — and the school's annual magazine carries the same name.

## Fees: what Satara actually costs

The latest published figures are from the **2025-26 fee structure: about ₹1,83,879 a year** for General, OBC-NCL and defence categories, and **₹1,82,379 for SC/ST**. Treat that as a planning figure, not a quote: fees rise roughly 10% a year under Ministry of Defence rules, so budget **around ₹2 lakh for 2027-28** and confirm the current figure from the school before you commit.

Nobody actually pays the full sticker price for long. **Defence scholarships** for wards of serving personnel and ex-servicemen, and **state government scholarships**, reduce the burden substantially, and fee concessions are income-linked. ## The admission path, start to finish

1. **AISSEE form (closes 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM):** one central form on the NTA portal; select Sainik School Satara as your preference. Fee ₹850 (General/OBC/Defence) or ₹700 (SC/ST). Keep the application number safe.
2. **Written exam (Sunday, 31 January 2027):** Class 6 is 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes; Maths carries half the marks and decides more admissions than any other subject. Class 9 is 150 questions, 400 marks, 180 minutes, in English only.
3. **E-counselling:** merit lists by category and state; Satara seats allotted per the seat matrix above. Remember the girls' seats are small in number — 10 in Class 6, 3 in Class 9 — so a girl aspirant's counselling choices matter even more.
4. **Medical examination:** mandatory and strict. Check our [medical certificate format guide](/blog/medical-certificate-format-sainik-school-admission-2026-10-16/) early, not after allotment.
5. **Document verification + first fee payment:** domicile, birth certificate, category certificates — originals.

## A parent's checklist

- [ ] Confirm Maharashtra domicile (home-state quota is your biggest advantage at Satara — 62 of 90 boys' Class 6 seats)
- [ ] Birth certificate DOB falls inside the Class 6 bracket — born 1 April 2015 to 31 March 2017; Class 9 — born 1 April 2012 to 31 March 2014
- [ ] AISSEE form submitted by **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM** — aim for 27 November, not the last day
- [ ] Mock scores at Satara's level: Maharashtra-quota General cut-offs are reported around 225–240 out of 300 — if your child is not there yet, the next two months are for Maths first
- [ ] Fee budget: ~₹2 lakh/year planning number; explore defence/state scholarships now
- [ ] Medical pre-check: vision, hearing, no disqualifying conditions — before counselling, not after
- [ ] Key dates: [AISSEE 2027 exam calendar and key dates](/blog/aissee-2027-exam-date-calendar/)

One last opinion, since you are deciding: Satara is the school with the longest history and one of the hardest cut-offs in the country. That combination makes it the wrong first choice for a child who is still finding their feet in Maths — and the right one for a child who is already scoring 230-plus in mocks and wants to be pushed. If your child is in the first group, put Chandrapur higher in your counselling preferences and keep Satara as the dream, not the plan.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Satara have for 2027-28?**
Satara has 100 Class 6 seats for 2027-28 — 90 for boys and 10 for girls — and 21 Class 9 seats — 18 for boys and 3 for girls. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin's seat appendices.

**When was Sainik School Satara founded?**
It was inaugurated on 23 June 1961 by Defence Minister V. K. Krishna Menon, on the vision of Maharashtra Chief Minister Yashwantrao Chavan. It was the first Sainik School in India.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Satara?**
Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Satara?**
Published 2025-26 figures put the total at about Rs 1,83,879 a year for General, OBC-NCL and defence categories, and Rs 1,82,379 for SC and ST. Fees rise roughly 10 percent a year, so budget around Rs 2 lakh for 2027-28 and confirm the current figure from the school.

**Does Sainik School Satara admit girls?**
Yes. For 2027-28, 10 of the 100 Class 6 seats are for girls — five for Maharashtra-domicile girls and five for girls from other states. There are also 3 Class 9 seats for girls.

**How strong is Satara's NDA record?**
More than 580 NDA and UPSC selections are reported from the school, and its alumni include a former Air Chief Marshal, a Vice Admiral and several Lieutenant Generals. The figures are reported by published sources, not an officially audited tally.
