# Sainik School Interview Tips 2027: Taiyaari Ki Poori Strategy

> **✅ Last verified: 26 September 2026** — Interview AISSEE written + medical ke baad counselling stage par hota hai (school-wise). Ye guide taiyaari ki strategy par hai; sawalon ki list ke liye [50 questions ka bank yahan hai](/blog/sainik-school-interview-questions-2027/).

Question bank rat lene se interview nahi nikalta. Interview mein panel ye dekhta hai — *kya ye bachcha Sainik School ki zindagi jee payega?* Uska confidence, uski soch, uska ravaiyya.

Is article mein koi question list nahi hai. Sirf strategy — kaise taiyaar karo, kya pehno, kaise baitho, kaunsi galtiyan barbaad karti hain.

## Interview Mein Asal Mein Kya Dekha Jaata Hai?

Panel ke dimaag mein 5 cheezein chal rahi hoti hain:

| Kya dekhte hain | Kaise pata chalta hai |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Confidence | Aankhon mein dekhkar baat karna, rukna nahi |
| Communication | Saaf-shabd, sahi bhasha (Hindi/English dono chalte hain) |
| Motivation | "Sainik School kyon?" ka jawab dil se aana |
| General awareness | Aas-paas ki duniya ki khabar |
| Personality | Imaandaari, vinamrata, positive soch |

Marks ka weightage school-wise alag hai, lekin ye 5 hi faisla karte hain.

## 4-Week Preparation Plan

| Hafta | Focus | Roz ka kaam (30 min) |
|-------|-------|----------------------|
| 1 | Self-introduction | Sheeshe ke saamne apna parichay 10 baar bolo — 60 second mein |
| 2 | GK & current affairs | Roz 5 GK facts + 2 khabrein; parivaar se sawal-jawab ka khel |
| 3 | Mock interviews | Ghar par papa/mama/teacher se 3 mock interviews |
| 4 | Polish | Body language, dress rehearsal, "kyon" wale jawab pakke |

**Week 1 ka sabse important kaam:** "Apne baare mein batao" — ye pehla sawal lagbhag pakka hai. 60 second ka jawab likho, yaad karo, phir apne shabdon mein bolna seekho. Ratta nahi, flow chahiye.

## Body Language: Bina Bole Bahut Kuch Kehna

- **Entry:** Darwaze par knock, permission lekar andar, "Good morning sir/ma'am"
- **Baitna:** Seedha, aaram se — aage ko jhukna nahi, peeche ko dhansna nahi
- **Aankhein:** Bolne wale ki aankhon mein dekho — ye sabse bada confidence signal hai
- **Haath:** Table par ya god mein aaram se — ungliyan mat chatkao, pen mat ghumao
- **Muskurahat:** Halki, natural — jabardasti ki hansi se buri

Ek purana niyam hai: panel pehle 30 second mein 70% faisla kar leta hai. Entry aur pehla jawab hi game hai.

## Dress Code: Simple Aur Saaf

- School uniform (agar hai) — sabse best option
- Nahi hai to: saaf shirt, pant, polished shoes
- Baal katte hue, nakhun saaf
- Koi naya fashion experiment nahi — ye interview hai, function nahi

## 7 Galtiyan Jo Interview Barbaad Karti Hain

1. **"Papa ne kaha tha"** — apna reason na hona sabse badi galti
2. **Ratte hue jawab** — 2 minute mein pakde jaoge
3. **Jhoothi hobby** — "reading" bola aur ek kitaab ka naam na pata ho
4. **Panel se behes** — asahmat ho to vinamrata se rakho, ladna nahi
5. **"Pata nahi" par ruk jana** — nahi pata to imaandaari se kaho, aur guess karne ki koshish karo
6. **Parents ka beech mein bolna** — bachche ko khud bolne do (parents, ye aapke liye hai)
7. **Time se late pahunchna** — 30 minute pehle pahuncho, yehi pehla impression hai

## Mock Interview Ghar Par Kaise Karein?

Taiyaari ka sabse asar-daar tarika:

1. Ek kamra, 2–3 log (papa, mama, bada bhai/teacher) — panel banao
2. [Question bank](/blog/sainik-school-interview-questions-2027/) se 10 sawal chunno
3. Timer lagao — 15 minute
4. Phone par video banao — phir khud dekho: kahan ruka, kahan nazar jhuki
5. Har mock ke baad 3 cheezein note karo: achha kya tha, bura kya tha, agli baar kya sudharna hai

3 mock interviews ke baad bachche ka dar 80% kam ho jaata hai. Ye mera tajurba hai, kitaabi baat nahi.

## Parents Ke Liye Khaas Salah

- **Bachche par apna sapna mat thopo** — "beta officer banega" ka pressure interview mein dikhta hai
- **Practice mein critic mat bano, coach bano** — galti par daanto nahi, sahi karke dikhao
- **Interview wale din normal raho** — tumhara tension bachche mein transfer hota hai
- **"Best of luck" ke baad "koi baat nahi" bhi taiyaar rakho** — bachche ko pata hona chahiye ki pyaar result par nirbhar nahi

## FAQ

**Q1: Interview ki taiyaari kab se shuru karein?**
Written result ke baad bhi waqt milta hai, lekin behtar hai written exam ke saath-saath hafte mein 2 din 30 minute de do.

**Q2: Kya English mein jawab dena zaroori hai?**
Nahi. Hindi mein bhi poora interview ho sakta hai. Zaroori hai saaf aur confident bolna — bhasha nahi.

**Q3: Kya parents andar ja sakte hain?**
Aam taur par nahi — bachcha akele jaata hai. Kuch schools mein baad mein parents se chhoti baat-cheet hoti hai.

**Q4: Agar bachcha ghabra jaye to?**
Normal hai. Panel bachchon se narmi se pesh aata hai — wo dar ko samajhte hain. Paani peene ko maangna bhi theek hai.

**Q5: Kya interview mein current affairs zaroor poochhte hain?**
Aksar haan — "aaj ki khabar" type 1–2 sawal aam hain. Interview se pehle 1 hafta news follow karo.
