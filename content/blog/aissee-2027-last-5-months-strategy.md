# AISSEE 2027: September Se January Tak — Final Study Plan

> **🟢 Last verified: 26 September 2026** — ✅ **CONFIRMED** = 2026 NTA bulletin se (pattern stable). Exam date ✅ **31 January 2027** (NTA calendar, tentative).

Calendar dekhiye: aaj 26 September hai, exam 31 January ko. Beech mein **127 din** bache hain. Ye woh phase hai jahan smart planning, andhi mehnat ko hara deti hai. Niche month-by-month exact plan de raha hoon — kya padhna hai, kitna padhna hai, aur kya bilkul nahi karna hai.

## Poora Roadmap — Ek Nazar Mein

| Mahina | Focus | Hafte mein mocks |
|--------|-------|------------------|
| Oct 2026 | Syllabus completion (bache hue topics) | 1 |
| Nov 2026 | Revision round 1 + weak topics | 2 |
| Dec 2026 | Revision round 2 + speed building | 2–3 |
| Jan 2027 | Sirf polish — koi naya topic nahi | 3–4 (exam tak) |

## October — Syllabus Khatm Karne Ka Mahina

October tak syllabus **poora** ho jana chahiye. Agar kuch topics bache hain to unhe priority par khatm karein:

- **Maths:** bache hue chapters + roz 30 questions practice. Formula notebook banayein.
- **Intelligence:** roz 15–20 questions. Ye section December tak 90% accuracy par hona chahiye.
- **Language/English:** grammar basics + roz ek comprehension.
- **GK/Science/SST:** NCERT reading + current affairs notes banana shuru karein.

**October ka golden rule:** naya chapter padhne ke saath uske questions bhi usi din solve karein. "Kal practice karunga" wale chapter bhool jate hain.

## November — Pehli Revision + Weak Topics

- Har subject ki **pehli full revision** karein.
- October ke mocks/previous papers mein jo galtiyaan hui thi, un topics ko dobara padhein.
- **Galtiyon ki diary** banayein — har mock ke baad 10 minute mein note karein: kaunsa topic, kyun galat hua.
- Hafte mein 2 mock tests, proper timing ke saath.

**November mein form bhi bhara jayega** (🔶 expected Oct–Nov window). Form bharne ka kaam parents sambhalein — bachche ki padhai disturb nahi honi chahiye. Process: [application form guide](/blog/aissee-application-form-2027-guide/).

## December — Speed Ka Mahina

- **Dusri revision** — is baar tez, sirf important points.
- Hafte mein 2–3 mocks. Har mock mein **time per section** note karein.
- Maths mein speed: Class 6 mein ~1.2 min/question, Class 9 mein ~1.2 min/question ka target.
- OMR practice shuru karein — ghar par print karke ya rough sheet par bubbling ki aadat daalein.

## January — Final Polish (Exam 31 Jan ✅)

- **Koi naya topic nahi.** Jo aata hai, use pakka karein.
- Roz ek previous year paper ya mock — **exam wale time slot** mein (subah).
- Formula notebook, GK notes, vocab — sirf inka revision.
- Aakhri hafte mein mocks kam, confidence zyada. Neend poori karein.
- Exam se ek din pehle: halki revision, jaldi sona, bag pack.

[Exam day tips](/blog/aissee-exam-day-tips-2027/) alag se padhein — OMR strategy aur time management wahin hai.

## Daily Timetable — Final Months

| Slot | Duration | Kya karein |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Subah | 30 min | GK / current affairs / vocab |
| Shaam 1 | 1.5 ghante | Maths (roz, bina naaga) |
| Shaam 2 | 1 ghanta | Rotate: Language / Intelligence / Science |
| Raat | 20 min | Din ka revision + galtiyon ki diary |

**Sunday:** full mock + analysis. Ye din sabse keemti hai — skip mat kijiye.

## Subject Priority (Marks Ke Hisaab Se)

| Priority | Subject | Kyun |
|----------|---------|------|
| 1 | Mathematics | 50% marks — roz practice |
| 2 | Intelligence | Easy scoring, sirf practice se |
| 3 | Language/English | Comprehension + grammar |
| 4 | GK/Science/SST | NCERT + current affairs |

**25% qualifying rule** yaad rakhein — koi section zero nahi hona chahiye. Har section ka minimum level maintain karein.

## Aakhri Dino Mein Ye 5 Galtiyaan Mat Karna

1. Naya book ya naya material shuru karna.
2. Raat ko der tak jagkar padhna (exam subah hai, body clock set rakhein).
3. Dusre bachchon se apni taiyaari compare karna.
4. Mock mein kam marks aane par ghabrana — mock seekhne ke liye hai, darne ke liye nahi.
5. Sehat ignore karna — bimaar padkar 10 din waste karne se achcha roz 8 ghante sona hai.

## FAQ

**Q1. Abhi syllabus poora nahi hua — kya selection possible hai?**
Haan, lekin abhi se disciplined hona padega. October tak syllabus khatm karne ka target rakhein, phir 3 mahine revision ke liye milenge.

**Q2. Mock tests kab se shuru karne chahiye?**
Abhi se — hafte mein 1. December se 2–3. Mock ke bina speed aur exam temperament nahi banta.

**Q3. Current affairs kab se padhein?**
Pichhle 6–8 mahine ke (June 2026 se January 2027 tak ke). Roz 15 minute kaafi hai.

**Q4. January mein naya topic padh sakte hain?**
Bilkul nahi. January sirf revision aur practice ka mahina hai.

**Q5. School exams bhi hain — balance kaise karein?**
School syllabus aur AISSEE syllabus mein 70–80% overlap hai (NCERT based). School ki padhai ko AISSEE revision samajhkar karein.

**Q6. Kitne previous year papers solve karne chahiye?**
Kam se kam 2021–2026 ke saare papers (Class 6 ya 9, jo applicable ho). [Yahan analysis ke saath](/blog/aissee-previous-year-papers-pdf/) milega.
