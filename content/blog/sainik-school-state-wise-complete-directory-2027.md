## How to Choose the Right Sainik School During Counselling

### Factors to Consider

1. **Home State Quota (67%)** — You have the highest chance of getting into a school in your home state
2. **Fee Structure** — Traditional schools are ₹2-2.5L; PPP schools are ₹3-6L
3. **NDA Track Record** — Older schools (Kunjpura, Kapurthala, Chittorgarh) have proven records
4. **Infrastructure** — Visit if possible; check sports facilities, labs, hostel
5. **Location** — Climate, accessibility, proximity to home
6. **Special Facilities** — Swimming pool, shooting range, riding (select schools)

### Counselling Strategy

> **Pro Tip:** During counselling, fill **15-20 school choices** in this order:
> 1. Top 3-5: Your dream traditional schools
> 2. Middle 5-8: Realistic options (home state schools)
> 3. Last 5-7: Safe options (PPP schools, newer schools)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How many Sainik Schools are there in India?
There are **33+ traditional government Sainik Schools** and **69 new PPP Sainik Schools**, making a total of over **100 Sainik Schools** in India.

### Which is the oldest Sainik School in India?
**Sainik School Kunjpura (Haryana)** and **Sainik School Chittorgarh (Rajasthan)** — both established in **1961** — are among the oldest Sainik Schools.

### Which Sainik School has the best NDA record?
Schools like **Kunjpura, Kapurthala, Chittorgarh, and Amaravathinagar** consistently produce high numbers of NDA selections due to their long history and established training systems.

### What is the 67% home state quota?
**67% of seats** in each Sainik School are reserved for students from the **home state** where the school is located. The remaining 33% are open to students from other states.

### Can I apply to any Sainik School in India?
**Yes.** During AISSEE counselling, you can choose **any Sainik School** in India. However, your chances are highest for schools in your home state (67% quota).

### What is the annual fee for Sainik Schools?
Traditional Sainik Schools charge approximately **₹2,00,000 to ₹2,50,000** per year. PPP schools charge **₹3,00,000 to ₹6,00,000** per year.

### Do all Sainik Schools admit girls?
**Yes.** Since 2021, all Sainik Schools (traditional and PPP) admit girls with **25% seats reserved** for female candidates.
