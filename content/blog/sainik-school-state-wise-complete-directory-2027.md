## Kaun Sa School Chunein? Hamari Salah

1. **Apne home-state ka school pehle** — 67% home-state quota aapke favour mein hai ([quota rules](/blog/sainik-school-sc-st-defence-quota-2027/))
2. **Purane schools mein competition zyada** — Satara, Kunjpura, Kapurthala jaise naam sab bharte hain
3. **Naye schools ko halke mein na lein** — wahan cut-off kam ja sakta hai
4. **Girls ke liye** — 33 schools mein 10% ya 10 seats ka quota confirmed hai

## Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawaal

**Q1. Kya ye list mein 100 naye PPP schools shaamil hain?**
Nahi. Ye sirf 33 established schools ki verified list hai. 100 schools ka announcement (March 2026) policy-level tha — kaun se schools AISSEE 2027 mein aayenge, ye bulletin mein saaf hoga.

**Q2. Mere state mein school nahi hai to kya karun?**
33% other-state quota har school mein hota hai — aap kisi bhi state ke school mein apply kar sakte hain.

**Q3. Naye schools purane schools jaise hi hain?**
Naye schools Sainik Schools Society se affiliated hain lekin PPP/state-partnership mode mein chalte hain. Discipline aur curriculum ka framework same hai.

**Q4. School ki official website kaise milegi?**
"sainikschool.ncog.gov.in" par Society ki site hai; har school ka link wahan ya Google search se mil jayega.

**Q5. Kya 2027 mein schools ki sankhya badhegi?**
🔶 Sambhavna hai — bulletin (expected Oct 2026) mein final list aayegi.
