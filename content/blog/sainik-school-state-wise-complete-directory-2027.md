# Sainik Schools in India List: All 33 Schools State-Wise (2027 Verified Guide)

If you've searched for a "sainik schools in India list" online, you've probably noticed something strange: no two lists agree. One site says 33 schools, another says 100+, a third lists names that don't seem to exist anywhere. A parent called us last month, completely confused — he'd found three different lists with three different counts and wanted to know which one was real before filling his son's application.

Here's the straight answer. There are **33 established Sainik Schools** — directly under the Ministry of Defence — and that list is officially confirmed (✅ via PIB). Everything beyond those 33 is a different category, which is exactly where the confusion comes from. Let's clear it up.

## First, Understand Why the Lists Disagree

The phrase "Sainik School" now covers three different categories:

| Category | Count | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Established schools | 33 | The original schools under the Ministry of Defence — the verified list below |

## Fees and Seats: Not the Same Everywhere

Get this clear before you plan: **there is no single national fee.** Each school sets its own fees based on its costs, mess charges, and state policy. As a rough ballpark, most established schools land somewhere around ₹1–1.5 lakh a year including mess (🔶 EXPECTED — varies school to school, so treat this as an estimate, not a figure).

## How to Choose the Right School: Our Honest Advice

1. **Your home-state school first.** The 67% home-state quota works in your favour (✅ CONFIRMED — Sainik Schools Society SOP). Fewer outsiders competing for more seats is simple maths. Check the [quota rules](/blog/sainik-school-sc-st-defence-quota-2027/) to see exactly how this works.
2. **Famous names mean tougher competition.** Everyone applies to Satara, Kunjpura, Kapurthala — which pushes their cut-offs up.
3. **Don't dismiss the newer schools.** Less-famous schools often have lower cut-offs, and the discipline framework is the same everywhere.
4. **For daughters:** girls have a confirmed quota of 10% of vacancies (or 10 seats, whichever is more) in the 33 schools (✅ CONFIRMED). Our [guide for girls](/blog/sainik-school-for-girls-2027/) covers this in detail.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Are the 100 newly announced PPP schools on this list?**
No. This is only the verified list of 33 established schools. The 100-school announcement (March 2026, ✅ CONFIRMED) was a policy-level announcement — which schools actually join AISSEE 2027-28 will only be clear in the bulletin (🔶 expected October 2026).

**Q2. My state has no Sainik School. What should I do?**
Every school reserves 33% of seats for other-state students (✅ CONFIRMED), so you can apply to a school in any state. Just remember you'll be competing in the tougher pool.

**Q3. Are the new partnership schools as good as the established ones?**
They're affiliated with the Sainik Schools Society and follow the same discipline and curriculum framework, but they run in partnership mode with state governments or private bodies. The established 33 have decades of alumni networks and NDA track records the newer ones are still building.

**Q4. How do I find a school's official website?**
Start at the Sainik Schools Society site (sainikschool.ncog.gov.in) — individual school links are listed there — or search "Sainik School [name] official website" on Google.

**Q5. Will the number of schools increase for the 2027 cycle?**
🔶 Likely, but unconfirmed. The AISSEE 2027 bulletin (expected October 2026) will carry the final participating-school list. Anyone naming specific new schools before that is guessing.

**Q6. Can I apply to more than one school?**
The application form lets you give school preferences — the counselling process (AISSAC, ✅ run by the Sainik Schools Society, online, no fee, ~6 rounds) allots schools based on merit and preference. See our [counselling guide](/blog/sainik-school-counselling-2027/) for the full process.
