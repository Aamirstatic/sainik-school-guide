## Question Attempt Strategy

### The 3-Pass Method

**Pass 1 — Easy Questions (40-50% of time)**
- Solve all questions you can answer immediately
- Skip anything that takes more than 30 seconds to think
- Mark skipped questions on the question paper

**Pass 2 — Medium Questions (30-40% of time)**
- Return to questions you skipped
- These require some calculation or thinking
- Don't spend more than 2 minutes on any single question

**Pass 3 — Hard Questions (10-20% of time)**
- Attempt remaining questions
- If unsure, make an educated guess (no negative marking!)
- Never leave a question blank

## OMR Sheet Filling Tips

### Do's ✅

1. **Fill OMR after every section** — Don't wait until the end
2. **Use blue/black ballpoint pen** — Pencil may not be scanned
3. **Fill the bubble completely** — Half-filled bubbles may not count
4. **Double-check question numbers** — Avoid misalignment
5. **Fill name and roll number first** — Before starting the exam

### Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't use whitener/correction fluid** — OMR will be rejected
2. **Don't make stray marks** — Extra marks can cause scanning errors
3. **Don't fold the OMR sheet** — Keep it flat
4. **Don't fill multiple bubbles** — Only one answer per question

## Exam Day Do's and Don'ts

### Do's ✅

- ✅ Reach centre 1.5 hours early
- ✅ Carry extra pens (2-3)
- ✅ Read questions carefully before answering
- ✅ Stay hydrated (carry water bottle)
- ✅ Attempt ALL questions (no negative marking)
- ✅ Keep calm if a section is tough — everyone faces the same paper

### Don'ts ❌

- ❌ Don't panic if the first section is hard
- ❌ Don't spend more than 2 minutes on any question
- ❌ Don't change answers unless you're 100% sure
- ❌ Don't discuss answers with others during break
- ❌ Don't carry mobile phone to the centre

## Subject-Specific Tips

### Mathematics
- **Start with easy questions** — Arithmetic before Geometry
- **Show rough work** on the question paper — helps avoid silly mistakes
- **Verify calculations** for 2-3 mark questions

### English
- **Read the passage first**, then answer questions
- **Grammar rules** — Trust your first instinct
- **Vocabulary** — Context clues help with unknown words

### General Knowledge
- **Don't overthink** — GK questions have factual answers
- **Eliminate wrong options** — 50:50 chance if you can remove 2 options
- **Current affairs** — Focus on last 6 months

### Intelligence/Reasoning
- **Pattern recognition** — Look for number/shape sequences
- **Draw diagrams** for blood relation questions
- **Practice** — This section improves the most with practice

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is there negative marking in AISSEE?
**No.** There is NO negative marking in AISSEE. Attempt ALL questions, even if unsure.

### Which section should I attempt first?
Start with **Intelligence/Reasoning** — it's the easiest and builds confidence. Then English, GK, and Maths last.

### How many questions should I attempt?
**All 125 questions.** Since there's no negative marking, there's no penalty for wrong answers.

### What if I run out of time?
Focus on **filling OMR correctly** for questions you've already solved. It's better to have 100 correct OMR entries than 125 solved but unfilled.
