This walkthrough reconstructs the NTA registration flow from the AISSEE 2026 process, which has been stable for years. Screen-by-screen behaviour for 2027 is EXPECTED to match; dates and fees will be confirmed in the bulletin.

Every year, the same thing happens on the day the AISSEE form opens: a parent sits down at a cyber café or laptop, the NTA page loads, and within five minutes they are stuck on a screen asking for a document they have not scanned yet. This guide exists so that day never surprises you. We walk through every screen of the NTA application — what it asks, what to type, and what quietly goes wrong.

## Before you touch the keyboard: the readiness checklist

Half of registration failures are not typing errors — they are missing scans. Finish this checklist before the portal goes live.

| Item | What "ready" looks like |
|------|------------------------|
| Child's photograph | Recent, colour, passport-style (last year's specs: plain background, face clearly visible) |
| Child's signature | On white paper, scanned clearly |
| Left thumb impression | Of the child, scanned (Class 6 applicants too) |
| Birth certificate | Scanned copy, matches the date of birth you will enter |
| Domicile certificate | Scanned; determines your home-state quota |
| Category/service certificate | SC/ST/OBC-NCL or defence-ward certificate, if applicable |
| Email ID + mobile number | One valid set, belonging to the candidate or a parent — used for OTPs all year |
| Fee payment method | Debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI with the fee amount ready |

Get the OBC-NCL certificate timing right — the validity rule trips many parents. Keep scans in the format and size the bulletin specifies (the 2026 bulletin fixed these precisely; check the 2027 bulletin before uploading).

## Screen 1: The registration page

On the NTA AISSEE portal (exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society), you will see a link like "Apply Online for AISSEE 2027". Clicking it opens the registration form.

**What it asks:** the candidate's name exactly as on the birth certificate, date of birth, gender, and — this is the screen that decides your future — the **home state for domicile-based school choice**. You will select the class (6 or 9) and indicate category (General / SC / ST / OBC-NCL) and whether the child is a defence ward.

**First-timer traps here:**

- **Name mismatch:** enter the name exactly as printed on the birth certificate, including spacing. Admit cards later compare this against uploaded documents.
- **The class cannot be changed casually:** Class 6 and Class 9 are separate application tracks. Double-check which class your child qualifies for (10–12 years on 31 March 2027 for Class 6; 13–15 for Class 9 — PROJECTED, confirm in the bulletin).
- **Mobile number:** this number receives OTPs for the whole cycle — admit card, answer key, counselling. Use a number that will stay active for a year.

You receive an **application number** at the end of this step. Write it down physically. Every future login starts with it.

## Screen 2: Filling the full application form

This is the long screen — personal details, correspondence and permanent addresses, parent details, and exam details.

**What it asks:**

- **Personal details:** name, parents' names, date of birth, gender, category, Aadhaar number (optional in recent cycles — confirm the 2027 bulletin's wording), religion, nationality.
- **Addresses:** correspondence and permanent. The permanent address supports the domicile claim.
- **School choice details:** you indicate school preferences per the bulletin. In recent cycles, one form covered both Sainik Schools and approved New Sainik Schools (33 established schools for both classes; 69 new schools for Class 6, 19 for Class 9 — 2026 baseline, 2027 list EXPECTED in the bulletin).
- **Exam city choice:** you select preferred exam cities. AISSEE is an offline OMR exam at centres across India.

**First-timer traps here:**

- **Only one form per candidate.** This is the rule parents break most: submitting a second form "to be safe" leads to rejection. One candidate, one class, one form.
- **Photo and signature specs:** the bulletin specifies exact pixel sizes and file formats. A scan that looks fine to your eye gets rejected by the uploader if it breaks the spec. Re-scan rather than resize-stretch.
- **Do not close the tab mid-payment.** This is where fees go missing for a day or two while the bank confirms.

## Screen 3: Uploads and fee payment

The upload section asks for the photograph, signature, thumb impression, birth certificate, domicile certificate and category/service certificate in sequence. Upload, preview each one — NTA shows a thumbnail preview; use it — and then proceed to payment.

**Fee (EXPECTED, 2026 rates):** ₹850 for General, OBC (NCL) and Defence wards; ₹700 for SC/ST. Payment is online only — debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI. The confirmation page is the proof of payment.

## Screen 4: The confirmation page

After payment, the portal generates the **confirmation page** with your application number. **Download it and print it.** The form is incomplete without this page — NTA says so in every bulletin. Parents who skip the printout and later need proof of application have nothing to show.

## The 7 mistakes that quietly ruin AISSEE forms

1. Two forms for the same child (automatic rejection).
2. Name on the form not matching the birth certificate.
3. Uploading an old photograph from last year.
4. Skipping the thumbnail preview of uploaded documents.
5. A mobile number that gets ported or switched off mid-cycle.
6. Missing the correction window (it comes once, a few days after registration closes — last year it ran 12–14 November 2025).
7. Paying the fee but never downloading the confirmation page.

## After submission: what comes next

Correction window → admit card (EXPECTED mid-January 2027) → exam (31 January 2027, tentative) → answer key → result (late Feb/early March, EXPECTED) → AISSAC e-counselling at pesa.ncog.gov.in (online, no fee, roughly six rounds). ## FAQs

**What is the official website for AISSEE 2027 registration?**
exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. Apply only there; the form is not out as of 19 October 2026, so register only once NTA publishes the information bulletin on that portal.

**What documents do I need to upload for AISSEE registration?**
Based on the 2026 process: the child's photograph, signature, left thumb impression, birth certificate, domicile certificate, and category or service certificate if applicable. Keep them scanned and sized before the form opens.

**Can I fill two AISSEE forms for my child to be safe?**
No. Only one application per candidate is allowed, covering both Sainik Schools and approved New Sainik Schools. Multiple forms for the same class lead to rejection.

**How is the AISSEE application fee paid?**
Online only — debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI. Expected fee for 2027: Rs 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and Defence wards, Rs 700 for SC/ST, based on 2026 rates.

**What happens after I submit the AISSEE form?**
Download and print the confirmation page — the application is incomplete without it. Later come the correction window, admit card release, the 31 January 2027 exam (tentative), and then e-counselling through AISSAC.

**Can the form be filled in a language other than English?**
The online registration screens are in English, but the Class 6 question paper itself can be taken in English or one of 12 Indian languages. The Class 9 paper is in English only.
