# Sainik School Seats 2027: Kitni Seats Hain, Kahan Hain — Poori Seat Matrix Samjhiye

"Kul kitni seats hain?" — ye AISSEE ke har parent ka pehla sawaal hota hai. Seedha jawab: **2027 ki final seat matrix abhi aayi nahi hai**, kyunki NTA ka bulletin pending hai. Lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki hum andhere mein hain — 2026 cycle ka official data hamare paas hai, aur usi se 2027 ka andaza lagaya ja sakta hai.

## 2026 Cycle Ka Official Baseline (✅ Verified)

| Fact | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Established Sainik Schools | ✅ CONFIRMED (PIB) | **33 schools** |
| Naye schools — Class 6 entry | ✅ (2026 cycle, HT Oct 2025) | **69 schools** (3 naye Oct 2025 mein jude) |
| Naye schools — Class 9 entry | ✅ (2026 cycle) | **19 schools** |
| 2027-28 ki final list | 🔶 Awaited | NTA bulletin (expected Oct 2026) mein aayegi |

Matlab 2026 mein AISSEE ke through **100 se zyada schools** mein admission hua — 33 purane + 69 naye (Class 6). Ye sankhya 2027 mein aur badh sakti hai, kyunki March 2026 mein Raksha Mantri ne **100 naye PPP-mode Sainik Schools** ka announcement kiya tha (Indian Express, 22 Mar 2026). Lekin dhyaan rahe — wo announcement policy-level tha; kaun se schools individually approve honge, ye bulletin mein saaf hoga.

## Category-Wise Seats Kaise Nikalti Hain? (Worked Example)

Seat matrix ka sabse confusing hissa reservation calculation hai. Ek example se samajhiye — maan lijiye kisi established school mein Class 6 ki **120 seats** hain:

| Step | Calculation | Seats |
|---|---|---|
| Kul seats | — | 120 |
| Home-state (67%) | 120 × 0.67 | ~80 |
| Other-state (33%) | 120 × 0.33 | ~40 |
| Girls quota (10% ya 10 seats, jo zyada) | 120 × 0.10 = 12 | 12 (minimum) |
| Home-state mein SC (15%) | 80 × 0.15 | ~12 |
| Home-state mein ST (7.5%) | 80 × 0.075 | ~6 |
| Home-state mein OBC-NCL (27%) | 80 × 0.27 | ~22 |
| Defence wards (bachi hui ka 25%) | remainder × 0.25 | variable |

Ye ✅ AISSAC SOP ka formula hai. Asli counselling mein ye calculation software karta hai — aapko bas apni category sahi bharni hai. Category rules ki detail [quota guide](/blog/sainik-school-sc-st-defence-quota-2027/) mein padhein.

## Class 6 vs Class 9: Seats Mein Farq

- **Class 6:** Sabse zyada seats — 33 purane + 69 naye schools mein entry. Competition zyada, lekin options bhi zyada.
- **Class 9:** Sirf 33 purane + 19 naye schools mein limited seats. Vacancy-based hoti hain (jo bachche Class 6 se Class 9 mein nahi ja pate, unki jagah).

**Hamari salah:** Agar bachcha Class 5 mein hai, to Class 6 entry ka target rakhein — ye sabse bada aur sabse aasaan darwaza hai. Class 9 ka rasta backup ke taur par rakhein.

## 2027 Mein Kya Badal Sakta Hai?

1. **Naye schools jud sakte hain** — 100 PPP schools ke announcement ke baad kuch naye naam bulletin mein aa sakte hain
2. **Seat count badh sakta hai** — kuch schools apni capacity badha rahe hain
3. **Girls seats** — naye schools mein girls entry ki policy bulletin mein saaf hogi

🔶 Ye teeno points bulletin par nirbhar hain. Koi bhi website agar aapko "2027 ki final seat list" dikhaye bulletin se pehle, to wo purana data chipka rahi hai — savdhaan rahein.

## Choice Filling Mein Seats Ka Istemaal Kaise Karein?

Counselling (AISSAC, ✅ pesa.ncog.gov.in par online, koi fee nahi, ~6 rounds) mein choice filling ke waqt:

1. **Apne home-state ke schools pehle bharein** — 67% quota aapke favour mein hai
2. **Naye schools ko underestimate na karein** — wahan competition kam hota hai
3. **Girls ke liye** girls-quota wale schools priority par rakhein
4. **Har round mein choice update karein** — pehle round mein seat na mile to ghabrayein nahi

## Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawaal

**Q1. 2027 mein kul kitni seats hongi?**
Final number bulletin mein aayega. 2026 baseline: 33 + 69 schools (Class 6). 2027 mein isse zyada hone ki ummeed hai.

**Q2. Kya har school mein Class 6 aur Class 9 dono mein entry hai?**
Nahi. Class 6 mein 102 schools (2026), Class 9 mein sirf 52. Apne target school ki entry classes bulletin mein check karein.

**Q3. Home-state quota ka fayda kaise milega?**
Form mein domicile sahi bharein aur counselling mein domicile certificate dikhayein. Galat state bharne par quota ka fayda nahi milega.

**Q4. Kya ek se zyada school ke liye apply kar sakte hain?**
Haan — ek hi AISSEE form se aap counselling mein multiple schools ki choice bhar sakte hain.

**Q5. Seat matrix kab aayegi?**
NTA ke information bulletin ke saath — 🔶 October 2026 mein expected hai.

**Q6. Kya girls har school mein apply kar sakti hain?**
33 established schools mein girls quota confirmed hai. Naye schools ki policy bulletin mein dekhein.
