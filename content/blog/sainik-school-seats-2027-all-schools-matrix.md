# Sainik School Seats 2027: How Many Seats, Where They Are — The Complete Seat Matrix Explained

"How many seats are there in total?" — it's the first question every AISSEE parent asks. The straight answer: **the final 2027 seat matrix isn't out yet**, because NTA's bulletin is still pending. But that doesn't mean we're guessing in the dark — we have the official 2026-cycle data, and it's a solid base for understanding what 2027 will look like.

## The 2026-cycle official baseline (✅ verified)

| Fact | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Established Sainik Schools | ✅ CONFIRMED (PIB) | **33 schools** |
| New schools — Class 6 entry | ✅ (2026 cycle, HT Oct 2025) | **69 schools** (3 added in Oct 2025) |
| New schools — Class 9 entry | ✅ (2026 cycle) | **19 schools** |
| Final 2027-28 list | 🔶 Awaited | Comes with the NTA bulletin (expected Oct 2026) |

So in 2026, AISSEE admissions ran across **100+ schools** — 33 established plus 69 new ones for Class 6. That number could grow for 2027: in March 2026 the Raksha Mantri announced **100 new PPP-mode Sainik Schools** (Indian Express, 22 Mar 2026). But keep perspective — that was a policy-level announcement; which individual schools get approved and enter the admission cycle will only be clear in the bulletin.

## How category-wise seats are actually calculated (worked example)

The reservation math is the most confusing part of the seat matrix, so let's walk through it. Say an established school has **120 Class 6 seats**:

| Step | Calculation | Seats |
|---|---|---|
| Total seats | — | 120 |
| Home-state (67%) | 120 × 0.67 | ~80 |
| Other-state (33%) | 120 × 0.33 | ~40 |
| Girls quota (10% or 10 seats, whichever more) | 120 × 0.10 = 12 | 12 (minimum) |
| SC within home-state (15%) | 80 × 0.15 | ~12 |
| ST within home-state (7.5%) | 80 × 0.075 | ~6 |
| OBC-NCL within home-state (27%) | 80 × 0.27 | ~22 |
| Defence wards (25% of remainder) | remainder × 0.25 | varies |

That's the ✅ AISSAC SOP formula. In real counselling the software does this arithmetic — your job is only to claim the right category and domicile. The full reservation logic is explained in our [quota guide](/blog/sainik-school-sc-st-defence-quota-2027/).

Here's a small scene that shows why this matters more than parents think: a family I know was fixated on one famous Sainik School three states away — the name, the legacy, the NDA numbers. Their son scored well. But they'd ignored that 67% of that school's seats were locked for its home state, and in the 33% other-states pool the cutoff was brutal. He missed it. The next year, a cousin with a lower score got into their *home-state* school comfortably. **The seat matrix isn't just a number — it's a map. Read it before you fall in love with a school.**

## Class 6 vs Class 9: the seat picture is very different

- **Class 6:** the biggest door — 33 established + 69 new schools in 2026. More competition, but far more options.
- **Class 9:** a much narrower door — only 33 established + 19 new schools, and seats are vacancy-based (they open up where Class 6 batches didn't fill forward).

**My honest take:** if your child is in Class 5 right now, aim squarely at Class 6 entry. It's the largest intake and the most predictable process. Keep Class 9 as a backup plan, not the main one.

## What could change for 2027?

1. **New schools may join** — after the 100 PPP-school announcement, expect some new names in the bulletin.
2. **Seat counts may rise** — several schools are expanding capacity.
3. **Girls' entry in new schools** — the policy for newer schools will be spelled out in the bulletin.

🔶 All three depend on the bulletin. If any website shows you a "final 2027 seat list" before the bulletin is out, it's recycling old data — be cautious.

## Using the seat matrix in choice filling (AISSAC)

Counselling runs on **AISSAC** — ✅ the Sainik Schools Society's online system at `pesa.ncog.gov.in`, free of charge, roughly six rounds. When you fill choices:

1. **Put your home-state schools first** — the 67% quota works in your favour there.
2. **Don't underestimate new schools** — competition there is usually lighter.
3. **For girls,** prioritise schools where the girls' quota applies.
4. **Update choices every round** — missing a seat in round one is normal, not the end.

Our [counselling guide](/blog/sainik-school-counselling-2027/) walks through the round-by-round strategy in detail.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many total seats will there be in 2027?**
The final number comes with the bulletin. The 2026 baseline was 33 + 69 schools for Class 6 — expect 2027 to be at least that, possibly more.

**Does every school admit in both Class 6 and Class 9?**
No. Class 6 entry ran in 102 schools in 2026; Class 9 in only 52. Check your target school's entry classes in the bulletin.

**How do I get the home-state quota benefit?**
Fill the domicile correctly in the form and produce the domicile certificate at verification. A wrong state entry means losing the quota — see the [admission mistakes guide](/blog/sainik-school-admission-mistakes/).

**Can I apply to more than one school?**
Yes — one AISSEE form lets you fill choices for multiple schools during counselling.

**When will the seat matrix be released?**
With NTA's information bulletin — 🔶 expected around October 2026.

**Can girls apply to every school?**
The girls' quota is confirmed for the 33 established schools. For new schools, check the bulletin — and read our [girls' guide](/blog/sainik-school-for-girls-2027/) for the full picture.
