### Step 1: Solve Without Timer (First Attempt)
- Solve the practice set without any time pressure

| Error Type | Action |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Where can I take free AISSEE 2027 mock tests?

You can take free AISSEE mock tests on platforms like **EaseToLearn** (easetolearn.com), **Sainik Guru** (sainikguru.com/tests), and mobile apps like **Shaurya Bharat** and **Sainik School Exam App** available on Google Play Store. Additionally, download previous year papers from the official NTA website at [exams.nta.nic.in](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/) for authentic practice.

### How many mock tests should I solve before AISSEE 2027?

You should solve at least **10–15 full-length mock tests** before the AISSEE 2027 exam. Students who solve 10+ mock tests score 25–30% higher than those who only study from textbooks. Start with 1 mock test per week 3 months before the exam, and increase to 2–3 per week in the last month.

### Is there negative marking in AISSEE 2027 mock tests?

No, there is **no negative marking** in AISSEE. This applies to both the real exam and mock tests. You should **attempt every question**, even if you are unsure. An educated guess has a 25% chance of being correct (since each question has 4 options), and there is no penalty for wrong answers.

### What is a good score in AISSEE mock tests?

For Class 6, a **safe target score is 190+ out of 300** for General category students. For Class 9, aim for **260+ out of 400**. These scores put you well above the typical cutoff range. For reserved categories, the targets are lower — 150–170 for Class 6 and 200–230 for Class 9.

### Should I solve mock tests with or without a timer?

**Both.** For your first attempt, solve without a timer to understand the questions and identify weak areas. For subsequent attempts, always use a timer set to the exact exam duration (2 hours 30 minutes). This builds time management skills and simulates real exam pressure. The goal is to improve both accuracy and speed.
