# Sainik School vs Kendriya Vidyalaya: Fees, Admission Aur Career — Kaun Sa Behtar?

Ye comparison har saal hazaaron parents karte hain — aur aksar galat aadhar par. Dono "sarkari schools" hain, dono CBSE hain, lekin dono ka **maksad hi alag** hai. Ek bachche ko officer banane ke liye bana hai, dusra transferable sarkari karmachariyon ke bachchon ko achchi padhai dene ke liye. Isliye "kaun behtar" ka jawab is par nirbhar hai: **aap apne bachche se kya chahte hain?**

## Seedhi Tulna: Ek Nazar Mein

| Aadhar | Sainik School | Kendriya Vidyalaya (KV) |
|---|---|---|
| **Maksad** | Defence officer taiyaar karna | Quality school education (sabke liye) |
| **Admission** | AISSEE entrance exam (✅ 31 Jan 2027 tentative) | Lottery / priority category (Class 1); Class 6+ mein limited seats |
| **Hostel** | Compulsory residential | Day school (hostel nahi) |
| **Fees** | 🔶 ~₹1–1.5 lakh/saal (school-wise alag) | Bahut kam — subsidized (kuch sau rupaye mahina) |
| **Discipline** | Military-style, strict routine | Normal school discipline |
| **Physical training** | Roz PT + games compulsory | PT periods, lekin utna intense nahi |
| **NCC** | Strong NCC culture | Kuch KVs mein NCC |
| **NDA ki taiyaari** | Built-in (curriculum ka hissa) | Khud karni padti hai |
| **Girls admission** | 33 schools mein quota (10%/10 seats) | Co-ed, aam taur par |

## Admission: Sabse Bada Farq

- **Sainik School:** AISSEE dena padega — national-level competition. Application fee 🔶 ₹850 (Gen/OBC) / ₹700 (SC/ST) expected. Exam OMR-based, no negative marking.
- **KV:** Class 1 mein lottery + priority categories (defence/transferable employees ko preference). Class 6 mein admission transfer cases ya khaali seats par hota hai — naya admission mushkil.

**Matlab:** KV mein admission "mil jaye to" wala hai; Sainik School mein "mehnat karke haasil karo" wala.

## Fees: Jeb Par Asar

Isme koi muqabla nahi — **KV bahut sasta hai**. Sarkari subsidy ki wajah se KV ki monthly fee kuch sau rupaye hoti hai (exact fee KVS site par dekhein). Sainik School mein saal ka ₹1–1.5 lakh lag sakta hai, lekin [scholarship schemes](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) se ye bojh kaafi kam ho jata hai — khaas taur par SC/ST aur defence families ke liye.

**Hamari salah:** Agar budget hi sabse badi chinta hai, to KV behtar hai. Lekin agar bachche ka defence mein career ka sapna hai, to Sainik School ki fees ek investment hai — scholarship ke baad asal kharcha utna nahi rehta jitna lagta hai.

## Career: Kahan Kaun Aage?

Imandaar jawab:

- **Defence/NDA ka sapna hai → Sainik School.** Poora ecosystem isi ke liye bana hai — PT, NCC, SSB-oriented personality development. KV ka bachcha bhi NDA clear kar sakta hai, lekin use bahar se taiyaari karni padegi.
- **Engineering/Medical/Civil → Dono barabar.** Dono CBSE hain, dono ki padhai achchi hai. KV ka day-school format coaching ke saath balance karne mein aasaan pad sakta hai.
- **Transferable job hai → KV.** Desh bhar mein KVs hain, transfer par admission mein priority milti hai.

**Ek baat saaf kar dein:** Hum koi "X% Sainik students NDA clear karte hain" jaisa fabricated statistic nahi denge — aise numbers verify nahi ho sakte. Jo verify hai wo ye hai: Sainik Schools ka structure defence career ke liye design kiya gaya hai, aur KV ka general education ke liye.

## Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawaal

**Q1. Kya KV se NDA clear ho sakta hai?**
Bilkul ho sakta hai. Bas taiyaari khud (ya coaching se) karni padegi — school mein built-in NDA ecosystem nahi hai.

**Q2. Dono mein se kiski padhai behtar hai?**
Dono CBSE hain. Padhai ka level comparable hai; farq environment aur maksad ka hai.

**Q3. Kya Sainik School mein ladkiyan ja sakti hain?**
Haan — 33 schools mein Class 6 mein girls quota (10% ya 10 seats) confirmed hai.

**Q4. KV ki fees kitni hai?**
Subsidized — kuch sau rupaye mahina. Exact current fee kvsangathan.nic.in par check karein.

**Q5. Kya dono mein ek saath apply kar sakte hain?**
Haan — dono ki process alag hai. AISSEE alag se dena hoga.

**Q6. Transfer par Sainik School badal sakta hai?**
Aam taur par nahi — admission ek school mein hota hai. KV mein transfer par dusre KV mein admission ka provision hai.
