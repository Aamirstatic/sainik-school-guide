# Sainik School vs Kendriya Vidyalaya: Fees, Admission, Career — Which Is Better?

Every year, thousands of parents compare Sainik School vs Kendriya Vidyalaya — and most compare them on the wrong basis. Both are government schools. Both follow CBSE. But their **purposes are entirely different**. One is built to produce future defence officers; the other exists to give quality education to children of transferable government employees. So "which is better" depends on one question: **what do you actually want for your child?**

Honestly, I've seen parents agonise over this for months, then realise the schools weren't even competing for the same child. Let's make the choice clear.

## Sainik School vs Kendriya Vidyalaya: The Comparison at a Glance

| Factor | Sainik School | Kendriya Vidyalaya (KV) |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Prepare defence officers | Quality school education for all |
| **Admission** | AISSEE entrance exam (✅ 31 Jan 2027 — tentative) | Lottery / priority category (Class 1); limited seats in Class 6+ |
| **Hostel** | Compulsory residential | Day school (no hostel) |
| **Fees** | 🔶 Roughly ₹1–1.5 lakh/year, varies by school (indicative) | Very low — heavily subsidised (a few hundred rupees a month, indicative) |
| **Discipline** | Military-style, strict daily routine | Normal school discipline |
| **Physical training** | Daily PT + games, compulsory | PT periods, far less intense |
| **NCC** | Strong NCC culture | Present in some KVs |
| **NDA preparation** | Built into the ecosystem | Entirely your own responsibility |
| **Girls' admission** | ✅ Quota in the 33 schools (10% of seats or 10 seats, whichever is more) | Co-ed, as standard |

## Admission: The Biggest Difference

- **Sainik School:** You sit the AISSEE — a national-level competitive exam. The 🔶 expected application fee is ₹850 (General/OBC/Defence wards) or ₹700 (SC/ST), based on the 2026 pattern. The paper is OMR-based, multiple choice, with no negative marking. ✅ Confirmed pattern for the exam date comes from NTA's official 2026-27 exam calendar.
- **KV:** Class 1 admission happens through a lottery with priority categories (children of transferable central government/defence employees get preference). In Class 6, admission is limited to transfer cases and vacant seats — fresh admission is difficult.

In short: KV admission is "get lucky and get in"; Sainik School admission is "work hard and earn it." A parent once told me his daughter missed KV Class 1 lottery three years running for his posting city — that's the lottery reality nobody warns you about.

## Fees: What It Costs Your Pocket

There's no real contest here — **KV is far cheaper.** Government subsidies keep KV monthly fees at a few hundred rupees (indicative; check the KVS website for the current schedule). Sainik School runs roughly ₹1–1.5 lakh a year depending on the school — but [scholarship and fee-concession schemes](/blog/sainik-school-scholarship-fee-concession-2027/) can reduce that burden significantly, especially for SC/ST families and defence wards.

**My honest take:** if budget is your single biggest concern, KV is the sensible pick. But if your child dreams of the armed forces, think of Sainik School fees as an investment — after scholarships, the real cost is usually far less than parents fear.

## Career Paths: Where Does Each Lead?

The straightforward answer:

- **Defence/NDA dream → Sainik School.** The entire ecosystem — PT, NCC, the personality training, the peer group — points in one direction. A KV student can absolutely clear the NDA, but the preparation happens outside school, usually through coaching or self-study.
- **Engineering/medical/civil services → both are fine.** Both are CBSE schools with solid academics. KV's day-school format can actually make it easier to balance school with competitive-exam coaching.
- **Transferable job → KV.** There are KVs across the country, and transfer cases get admission priority in the new city.

One thing I won't do: quote some "X% of Sainik students clear NDA" statistic. Those numbers can't be verified, and any site giving them is guessing. What's verifiable is the structure — Sainik Schools are designed for defence careers, KVs for general education.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Can a KV student clear the NDA?**
Absolutely. The preparation just isn't built into the school — you'll arrange coaching or self-study on the side.

**Q2. Which has better academics?**
Both follow CBSE and both are solid. The real difference is environment and purpose, not syllabus quality.

**Q3. Can girls join Sainik Schools?**
Yes — in the 33 established schools, Class 6 has a ✅ confirmed girls' quota of 10% of vacancies or 10 seats, whichever is more.

**Q4. What are KV fees currently?**
Subsidised — roughly a few hundred rupees a month (indicative). Check the current schedule at kvsangathan.nic.in.

**Q5. Can we apply to both?**
Yes — the processes are completely separate. AISSEE is a separate application from KV admission.

**Q6. Can a child transfer between Sainik Schools?**
Generally no — admission is to one school. KVs do have a provision for admission in a new KV on transfer.
