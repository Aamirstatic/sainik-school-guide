## Assam's one Sainik School

For an Assam parent, the Sainik School map is refreshingly simple: there is exactly one school under the Sainik Schools Society in the state. [Sainik School Goalpara](https://sainikschoolgoalpara.org/about-us/) describes itself as "the only school of its kind in the State of Assam", and no other Assam school appears on the Society's list. That single-school reality shapes everything in this guide — one campus, one admission route, one home-state quota to understand.

## A 1964 school with a Brahmaputra character

Goalpara is one of the older Sainik Schools, founded on 12 November 1964 under the Sainik Schools Society of the Ministry of Defence, with Lt. Col. D. N. Sahani as its first principal. The campus sits on the southern bank of the Brahmaputra, and the school leans into that geography — mornings in CBSE classrooms, afternoons on the parade ground, playing fields and river bank. It is fully residential: every cadet lives on campus, and admission happens only in Class 6 and Class 9, never mid-stream.

The alumni record is the school's strongest card. More than 200 former Goalpara cadets — "SSGians" — serve as officers in the Armed Forces, across the NDA, IMA, OTA, AFA and NAVAC, alongside alumni who became IAS and IPS officers, doctors and engineers. The school is also a member of the Indian Public Schools' Conference (IPSC), the grouping of India's established public schools. For a parent weighing a residential school 18 km outside a small town, that six-decade record is the reassurance that matters.

## Admission: AISSEE, and only AISSEE

Goalpara admits through the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE), conducted by the National Testing Agency for the Sainik Schools Society. There is no separate school-level test and no management-quota back door — the route is the national exam, then e-counselling (AISSAC), document verification and a medical test, in that order.

Two facts about the 2027 cycle need honest labelling. First, the **AISSEE 2027 notification is still awaited** — no bulletin or registration link had appeared on the official NTA portal in the most recent checks, so every 2027 date below is EXPECTED, not confirmed. Second, the exam pattern itself follows the established AISSEE framework: Class 6 is a 300-mark paper (Mathematics, Intelligence, Language, General Knowledge) and Class 9 is a 400-mark paper (Mathematics, English, Science and Social Studies), both OMR-based, with no negative marking.

## The 67% Assam quota — and what it means for your child

Under the standard Sainik School reservation pattern, **67% of seats are reserved for candidates from the home state** — here, Assam — and 33% for candidates from other states. In practice, an Assam-domiciled child competes first within the Assam pool, which is a genuine advantage over applying to a school in another state, where the same child would fall in the smaller 33% other-state pool.

Two cautions. The home-state claim needs a valid domicile certificate, so get that document ready well before the form opens — the 2026 cycle's application window lasted only about three weeks, and a short window is possible again. And the exact 2027 seat matrix for Goalpara (how many Class 6 and Class 9 seats, and how many for girls) will only be confirmed in the AISSEE 2027 bulletin. Do not plan around a seat number quoted by a coaching site.

## Age limits and documents checklist

The age rules follow the pattern used every year:

- **Class 6:** 10 to 12 years as of 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017, EXPECTED).
- **Class 9:** 13 to 15 years as of 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014, EXPECTED), and studying in Class 8.

Keep this folder ready before the notification drops:

- [ ] Birth certificate (date of birth must match the form exactly)
- [ ] Domicile certificate for Assam (for the 67% home-state pool)
- [ ] Category certificate, if applicable
- [ ] Candidate photograph, signature and left-hand thumb impression (scanned, in the sizes the bulletin specifies)
- [ ] Defence service certificate or PPO, if claiming the defence category
- [ ] Class 5 / Class 8 study certificate from the current school

## Fees: what is reported, and what is not verified

Fee figures for Goalpara are thin on the ground, and this guide will not dress up a guess as a fact. One admission directory lists an **annual boarding fee of Rs 87,846** — treat that as reported from a single secondary source, not confirmed. No fee figure from the school's own current notice was available at the time of writing, and the AISSEE 2027 bulletin may carry its own fee schedule. Before budgeting, check the school's own fee notice and the bulletin; Sainik School fees are also partly subsidised for defence-category and scholarship cadets, so the sticker figure is rarely the final figure for every family.

## The girls' question: verify, don't assume

This is the one point where Assam parents must be careful. Older school profiles describe Goalpara as a boys' school — "only boys are eligible for admission" appears in the school's own older text — while at least one current directory lists the school as co-ed. Both cannot be the 2027 position, and neither source is the AISSEE 2027 bulletin. Until the bulletin names Goalpara's Class 6 and Class 9 seats for girls, treat girls' admission here as **unconfirmed for 2027**. Families with daughters should still prepare for AISSEE — girls are admitted to many Sainik Schools, and the bulletin will settle Goalpara's position — but do not pick Goalpara as a first preference for a daughter on the strength of a directory label alone. Our [Punjab guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-punjab-complete-guide-2027/) carries the same warning for Kapurthala, for the same reason.

## What Assam parents should do this week

With the notification still awaited and the expected exam in late January 2027, the highest-value work right now is not form-filling but preparation and paperwork:

1. **Get the domicile and birth certificates in hand** — the two documents that most often delay a form.
2. **Run a weekly mock** in the AISSEE pattern (Class 6: 125 questions, 150 minutes) and track Mathematics accuracy first; it carries the most marks.
3. **Watch only the official portal** — exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society — for the notification, and ignore "form out" forwards until a bulletin actually appears there.
4. **Plan the Goalpara visit** if you can: the campus is 18 km from Goalpara town on the Matia-Dudhnoi road, with Guwahati about 130 km away. A day visit tells you more about residential life than any brochure.

Assam families have one school, one exam and one quota to master — and that simplicity is an advantage. Get the documents ready, keep the preparation steady through the Diwali break, and the short application window, whenever it opens, will be a formality rather than a scramble.
