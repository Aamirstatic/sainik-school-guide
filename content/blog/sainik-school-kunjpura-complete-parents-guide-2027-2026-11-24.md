## One of the first five — and still the benchmark

If you are a parent in Haryana filling the AISSEE 2027 form this week, there is one school that every conversation in the state comes back to: **Sainik School Kunjpura**. It is not just Haryana's first Sainik School. It is one of the **first five Sainik Schools founded anywhere in India**, and in 65 years it has become the yardstick other schools are measured against.

The school came into being on **24 July 1961**, at the behest of **V. K. Krishna Menon**, then Defence Minister — the man who conceived the Sainik School idea itself. It stands on a property that belonged to the **last Nawab of Kunjpura, Ibrahim Ali Khan**, who had built the main mansion in 1900 to house his daughter's marriage party. Before it became a school, the same building was the home of the Punjab Police Academy. The first principal was **Lt Col E. J. Simeon**, a Corps of Signals officer who ran the school for its first seven years.

That history matters for a practical reason. Kunjpura was built, from its first day, for one purpose: preparing boys — and now girls — academically, physically and mentally for the **National Defence Academy**. Everything about the place, from its motto to its timetable, still points at that single goal.

## The NDA record — and what it actually means

Let us be straight about the number everyone quotes. Standard school histories report that **more than 800 Kunjpura cadets have qualified for the NDA** — a record 16 in 2006, rising to 33 in 2020. Treat the exact lifetime tally as reported rather than official, because no single official source publishes a running total. But the direction is not in doubt, and the recent record is documented on our own site: **45 Kunjpura cadets cleared the NDA written exam in October 2026**, and **12 made the NDA-157 and TES-56 merit lists in November 2026**.

The alumni list backs that up. Former Army Chief **General Deepak Kapoor** is a Kunjean, as is former Haryana Chief Minister **Bhupinder Singh Hooda**. The school has also produced senior officers across the services, civil servants and professionals — the network is real, and in a residential school, it starts in the dormitory.

## Seats for 2027-28: 120 and 30

For the 2027-28 session, the AISSEE 2027 bulletin lists **120 Class 6 seats** and **30 Class 9 seats** at Kunjpura. The figures are tentative, as they are for every school.

| Entry | Seats (2027-28, tentative) |
|---|---|
| Class 6 | 120 (including 12 girls' seats under the 10% rule) |
| Class 9 | 30 |

The home-state rule is where Haryana parents have the edge. **67% of seats are reserved for Haryana-domicile candidates** and 33% for other states — so a Haryana-domicile child competes for roughly two-thirds of those 120 Class 6 seats, while a child from another state chases the remaining third. We explained how the school you pick decides your home-state status in our [school-choice guide](/news/aissee-2027-8-days-left-school-choice-home-state-quota-zero-seat-rule-2026-11-24/). Kunjpura's Class 6 intake of 120 is among the largest in the country — only a handful of schools, like Tilaiya at 140, take more. Our [Haryana guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-haryana-complete-guide-2027/) compares Kunjpura with the state's second school, Rewari, which was established in 2008.

## Fees: what is reported, and what is not verified

No verified 2027-28 fee figure for Kunjpura is published. Two admission portals — ezyschooling and Careers360 — agree on a **total annual fee of about Rs 1,67,570 for 2025-26**. That agreement makes it a reasonable planning figure, but it is still reported, not official: fees are revised periodically by the Board of Governors, and some schools raise them by up to 10% a year. Do not budget to the rupee on it. The only figure worth budgeting from is the school's own fee notice, which you should ask for at the time of admission. Scholarships exist for defence wards and for state-government nominees — ask the school office which ones your child qualifies for, rather than assuming.

## Campus life: what a day actually feels like

Kunjpura sits on about **275 acres** of green, open ground west of Kunjpura village. The main building — the Nawab's old mansion — still houses the administrative block, the principal's office, the library and the sub-junior classes, while the NDA block holds the computer lab, lecture hall and multimedia room. Beyond that: the **Manekshaw Auditorium**, the Simeon Squash Court, a gymnasium, a **riding school**, a swimming pool, a synthetic tennis court, and basketball, volleyball, football and hockey grounds. **NCC training is compulsory** for all cadets from Class VI, and they receive the NCC 'B' certificate before passing out — a genuine head start for any defence aspiration.

A typical weekday runs from early-morning PT through classes, games in the evening and supervised prep at night. It is a full, physical, regimented day, and that is the point — but it is also the thing to weigh honestly. If your child hates early mornings and structured routine, no NDA record will make them happy here. If they thrive on it, Kunjpura will feel like it was built for them. It was, quite literally.

## Who Kunjpura suits — and who should think twice

Kunjpura suits a Haryana-domicile child who wants the NDA and is ready for a fully residential, disciplined life. The home-state quota, the 120-seat Class 6 intake and the 130-km distance from Delhi make it the most practical first choice for most Haryana families — and for Delhi families too, since the school is an easy drive up NH44.

Think twice if your child is set on a school closer to home in another state — the outside-state pool at Kunjpura is only a third of the seats, and the competition there is fierce. And if your daughter is applying for Class 9, check the bulletin's Appendix VI-A1 for the exact girls' figure before you decide; for Class 6 girls, the position is clearer — 12 seats under the 10% rule.

## How to apply for 2027-28

Admission is only through **AISSEE**, run by NTA. Apply online at **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** by **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2015 to 31 March 2017); Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 (born 1 April 2012 to 31 March 2014) and a passed Class 8. The fee is **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards and **₹700** for SC/ST, with a late window from 3 to 6 December at an extra ₹500. The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027, at 2:00 PM** — 150 minutes for Class 6, 180 for Class 9, no negative marking in either. If you are still weighing Kunjpura against the other founding schools, our profiles of [Tilaiya](/blog/sainik-school-tilaiya-complete-parents-guide-2027-2026-11-24/), [Ghorakhal](/blog/sainik-school-ghorakhal-complete-parents-guide-2027-2026-11-24/) and [Kazhakootam](/blog/sainik-school-kazhakootam-complete-parents-guide-2027-2026-11-24/) use the same format, so you can compare them side by side.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Kunjpura have for 2027-28?**

Kunjpura has 120 Class 6 seats and 30 Class 9 seats for 2027-28. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin. By the 10 percent girls' rule, 12 of the 120 Class 6 seats are for girls.

**When was Sainik School Kunjpura founded?**

On 24 July 1961, one of the first five Sainik Schools in India. It was set up at the behest of Defence Minister V. K. Krishna Menon, on a property that belonged to the last Nawab of Kunjpura. Its motto is Siddhim Sannaddh — Ready to Accomplish — and its cadets are known as Kunjeans.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Kunjpura?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Kunjpura?**

No verified 2027-28 fee figure is published. Two admission portals agree on a total annual fee of about Rs 1,67,570 for 2025-26, so treat that as reported and indicative — the 2027-28 figure may be higher, as fees are revised periodically. Confirm the current figure from the school before budgeting.

**Does Sainik School Kunjpura admit girls?**

Yes, since the 2021-22 academic session, when the first 10 girls joined. For 2027-28, 12 of the 120 Class 6 seats are for girls under the 10 percent rule. Class 9 girls' seats are few and listed school-wise in the bulletin.

**How strong is Kunjpura's NDA record?**

More than 800 Kunjpura cadets have qualified for the National Defence Academy, as reported in standard school histories — a record 16 in 2006 and 33 in 2020. The record continues: 45 cadets cleared the NDA written exam in October 2026, and 12 made the NDA-157 and TES-56 merit lists in November 2026.
