## A coastal campus with a wartime story

If your home state is Gujarat and your child is aiming for a Sainik School, start here — because for you, there is only one campus to aim at. **Sainik School Balachadi**, near Jamnagar, is Gujarat's only school under the Sainik Schools Society. Every Gujarat family applying through AISSEE for a Society school is, in effect, applying to the same place.

And it is a remarkable place. Balachadi sits on the Gulf of Kutch coast, 32 km from Jamnagar, in an estate that was once the seaside summer resort of the rulers of Nawanagar. The school was established in **July 1961**, in the first wave of Sainik Schools, and moved to its present campus in the Balachadi Estate. On **7 March 1965**, Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri presented the school its colours — a ceremony schools of this vintage still count as a founding honour.

But the land's story starts earlier, and it is the part of Balachadi no other Sainik School can claim. During the Second World War, a camp for Polish refugee children was built here in **1942** by K. S. Digvijaysinhji, the Jam Saheb Maharaja of Nawanagar, near his summer resort. Hundreds of Polish orphans — children uprooted by the war — found shelter, food, schooling and medical care at Balachadi. Survivors later installed a plaque in front of the school building to commemorate it. When the Sainik School was established in 1961, it inherited that ground — and that legacy of compassion. Poland still honours it: a school in Warsaw is named after the Jamsaheb.

Two small honesty notes. First, the campus figure: the school's own history says the estate comprises **426 acres** of mangrove-rich coastal land; some school profiles list **375 acres**. The estate has likely been measured differently over the years — treat either figure as reported rather than surveyed. Second, Balachadi is a coastal campus: sea wind, salt air and a beach are part of daily life. For a child from inland Gujarat, that is a real adjustment, and worth a campus visit before you put it first on the form.

## Seats for 2027-28: 120 in Class 6, 27 in Class 9

The seat numbers below come from the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin itself — Appendix VI-A for Class 6 and Appendix VI-A1 for Class 9. They are tentative: the bulletin says vacancies are based on current strength and are subject to change as cadets leave. But they are the official numbers, not a portal's guess — and they correct the older "around 100 seats" figure that some portals still carry.

| Entry | Boys | Girls | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 6** | 108 (74 home state + 34 other states) | 12 (7 home state + 5 other states) | **120** |
| **Class 9** | 26 (23 home state + 3 other states) | 1 (home state) | **27** |

Read that Class 6 number carefully: **120 seats makes Balachadi one of the larger Class 6 intakes** among the 33 schools. And the 12 girls' seats are exactly 10% of the intake — the standard girls' quota at work. For Class 9, the picture is the usual one: only 27 seats, and a single girls' seat. A Class 9 aspirant needs to know that before choosing Balachadi first — the competition is far sharper than in Class 6.

Within each total, the standard reservation applies: 67% of seats for Gujarat-domicile candidates, 33% for other states; within those, 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% OBC-NCL, and 25% of the remainder for children of serving and ex-servicemen. One seat per category is allotted for girls, per the bulletin's note. Our [Gujarat guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-gujarat-complete-guide-2027/) explains how the 67% home-state rule plays out for a Gujarat child in practice.

## Admission: only through AISSEE, and the clock is ticking

There is no other route into Balachadi — no management quota, no direct admission, no agent who can sell a seat. Anyone offering one is running a scam. Admission is through AISSEE alone, run by NTA:

- **Apply by:** Wednesday, **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**, at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. The fee — ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST — must be paid by the same hour.
- **Late window:** 3–6 December with an extra ₹500. Our [late-fee warning](/news/aissee-2027-6-days-left-late-fee-window-risks-2026-11-26/) explains why it is a rescue boat, not a plan.
- **Correction window:** 7–9 December, for permitted changes only.
- **Exam city:** announced 11 January 2027. **Admit card:** 25 January 2027. **Exam:** Sunday, **31 January 2027**.
- **Eligibility:** Class 6 — age 10–12 on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017), studying in Class 5. Class 9 — age 13–15 (born 1 April 2012 – 31 March 2014), Class 8 passed.

## Eight houses, and a campus built for the whole day

Balachadi has eight boarding houses — **Pratap, Garuda, Nehru, Angre, Sardar Patel, Shastri, Shivaji and Tagore** — and the house is the centre of a cadet's life: inter-house competitions, shared routines, seniors looking after juniors. The campus is fully residential, Classes VI to XII, under CBSE, with NCC training and a military routine from morning PT to supervised evening prep.

The coastal setting shapes the training in a way no inland school can copy: the beach, the open ground and the maritime climate are part of the physical training environment, and the school has a particular strength in naval careers among its alumni. The campus also has a hospital and SAI-deputed sports coaches for boxing, shooting, hockey and athletics, according to school profiles — worth confirming on a visit, as facilities change.

The qualifying bar itself is fixed by NTA: 25% in each section and 40% aggregate (not applicable to SC/ST).

## Is Balachadi the right first choice for your child?

Ask three questions. **First, is Gujarat your home state?** If yes, Balachadi is the natural first choice — 67% of its seats are reserved for Gujarat children, and it is the state's only Society school. If no, remember you are competing for the 33% other-states share, and a school in your own home state may be the smarter first preference.

**Second, can your child live on the coast, far from home?** Balachadi is 32 km from Jamnagar town, on a coastal estate. It is residential, disciplined and a long way from home for an 11-year-old. A campus visit — the beach wind, the dormitories, the routine — will tell you more than any guide can.

**Third, is Class 6 or Class 9 the entry?** For Class 6, Balachadi's 120 seats make it a strong, realistic first choice. For Class 9, with 27 seats and one girls' seat, it is a reach for almost everyone — put it first only if your child is genuinely strong in maths, which carries 200 of the 400 Class 9 marks.

If the answers line up, the form closes on 2 December. Six days is enough — if the documents are ready today.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Balachadi have for 2027-28?**

Balachadi has 120 Class 6 seats — 108 for boys and 12 for girls — and 27 Class 9 seats — 26 for boys and 1 for a girl. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin, Appendices VI-A and VI-A1.

**When was Sainik School Balachadi founded?**

It was established in July 1961 in Jamnagar, Gujarat, and later moved to its present campus in the Balachadi Estate, 32 km from Jamnagar on the Gulf of Kutch coast. Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri presented the school its colours on 7 March 1965.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Balachadi?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Balachadi?**

No single verified 2027 fee figure is available from the school or the NTA bulletin. Careers360's 2026 school-wise fee list puts Balachadi at Rs 1,65,600 as a lumpsum figure, while another admission portal quotes around Rs 1,54,900 — the two conflict, so confirm the current figure from the school before budgeting.

**Does Sainik School Balachadi admit girls?**

Yes — 12 girls' seats in Class 6 and 1 in Class 9 for 2027-28, under the 10 percent girls' quota that applies across Sainik Schools. Girls were admitted to Balachadi's Class 6 in recent cycles.

**What is the advantage of Gujarat domicile for Balachadi admission?**

67 percent of seats are reserved for Gujarat-domicile candidates and 33 percent for candidates from other states. A Gujarat child competes mainly on the Gujarat merit list for their category — which is why the domicile certificate matters so much.
