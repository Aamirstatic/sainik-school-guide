## Understanding the AISSEE Timeline

The **All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE)** is conducted annually by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)** for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 at India's 33+ Sainik Schools and 69 PPP-mode schools. The entire admission cycle — from notification to school reporting — spans approximately **7–8 months**.

Each year, around **4 lakh students** compete for roughly **10,000 seats**, making it one of India's most competitive school-level entrance exams. Understanding the timeline is not just about knowing dates; it is about building a preparation strategy that aligns with each phase of the process.

This article provides a **comprehensive, month-by-month breakdown** of the AISSEE 2027 calendar, including expected dates based on historical patterns, previous year comparisons, and actionable preparation milestones.

## Month-by-Month AISSEE 2027 Timeline

### Phase 1: Pre-Notification Period (August–September 2026)

**What happens:** Before the official notification is released, NTA begins internal preparations — finalising exam centres, coordinating with Sainik School administrations, and drafting the information bulletin.

**What you should do:**

- Begin your AISSEE preparation if you have not already started
- Review the previous year's notification and exam pattern
- Gather essential documents (birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificates)
- Start solving previous year question papers

**Historical Context:**

| Year | Notification Month |
|---|---|
| AISSEE 2024 | September 2023 |
| AISSEE 2025 | September–October 2024 |
| AISSEE 2026 | September–October 2025 |
| AISSEE 2027 (Expected) | September–October 2026 |

### Phase 2: Official Notification Release (September–October 2026)

**What happens:** NTA releases the official AISSEE 2027 notification on their website (exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE). This notification includes:

- Eligibility criteria (age, education qualification)
- Exam pattern and syllabus
- Application process and fee details
- Important dates (application deadline, exam date, result date)
- Reservation policy details
- List of participating schools

**What you should do:**

- Read the official notification thoroughly
- Verify your eligibility (age, class qualification)
- Note all important dates and deadlines
- Understand the exam pattern for your target class (Class 6 or Class 9)

**Notification Details to Watch For:**

The notification typically contains the following critical information:

1. **Age eligibility:** Class 6 candidates must be between 10–12 years; Class 9 candidates must be between 13–15 years as on March 31 of the admission year
2. **Educational qualification:** For Class 6 — studying in Class 5 or equivalent; for Class 9 — studying in Class 8 or equivalent
3. **Application fee:** General/OBC — approximately ₹550–650; SC/ST — approximately ₹400–500
4. **Exam pattern:** Multiple choice questions (MCQs) with no negative marking

### Phase 3: Application Form Period (October–November 2026)

**What happens:** The AISSEE 2027 application form window opens on the NTA portal. This is the most critical administrative phase — missing the deadline means waiting an entire year.

**Application Process:**

1. **Registration:** Create an account on the NTA AISSEE portal using a valid email ID and mobile number
2. **Fill Application Form:** Enter personal details, academic information, school preferences, category details
3. **Upload Documents:**
   - Recent passport-size photograph (as per specifications)
   - Scanned signature
   - Birth certificate
   - Domicile certificate
   - Category certificate (if applicable)
   - Defence certificate (if applicable)
4. **Pay Application Fee:** Online payment via debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI
5. **Submit and Print:** Submit the form and take a printout for your records

**Important Application Tips:**

- **Do not wait until the last day.** Server overload is common in the final hours
- **Double-check all details** before submitting. Corrections may not be possible after submission
- **Keep your registration number safe.** You will need it for admit card download and result checking
- **Use a valid email and phone number.** All communication will be sent to these

**Application Fee Structure:**

| Category | Fee (approx.) |
|---|---|
| General / OBC / EWS | ₹550–650 |
| SC / ST | ₹400–500 |
| Defence | ₹550–650 |

**Previous Year Application Dates:**

| AISSEE 2025 | October 2024 | November 2024 |
| AISSEE 2026 | October 2025 | December 2025 |

### Phase 4: Application Correction Window (November–December 2026)

**What happens:** After the application window closes, NTA typically opens a **correction window** of 3–5 days. During this period, candidates can modify certain fields in their submitted application.

**Fields That Can Usually Be Corrected:**

- Name spelling corrections
- Date of birth corrections
- Category updates
- Address corrections
- School preference changes
- Photograph and signature re-upload

**Fields That Usually Cannot Be Changed:**

- Email ID
- Mobile number
- Exam centre city preference (in most cases)

**What you should do:**

- Log in to your account during the correction window
- Review every field carefully
- Make necessary corrections
- Print the updated application form

### Phase 5: Admit Card Release (December 2026 – January 2027)

**What happens:** NTA releases the AISSEE admit card approximately **2–3 weeks before the exam date**. The admit card is available for download on the NTA AISSEE portal.

**Admit Card Details:**

The admit card contains:

- Candidate's name and photograph
- Roll number
- Exam date and time
- Exam centre name and address
- Reporting time
- Important instructions

**What you should do:**

- Download and print the admit card immediately upon release
- Verify all details on the admit card
- Visit the exam centre location in advance if possible
- Keep multiple copies of the admit card
- Carry the admit card along with a valid photo ID to the exam centre

**Previous Year Admit Card Dates:**

| Year | Admit Card Release | Exam Date |
|---|---|---|
| AISSEE 2024 | December 2023 | January 2024 |
| AISSEE 2025 | December 2024 – January 2025 | January 2025 |
| AISSEE 2026 | December 2025 – January 2026 | January 2026 |
| AISSEE 2027 (Expected) | December 2026 – January 2027 | January 2027 |

### Phase 6: AISSEE 2027 Exam Day (January 2027)

**What happens:** The AISSEE 2027 exam is conducted on a single day across hundreds of exam centres nationwide. The exam is held in **offline (pen-and-paper) mode**.

**Exam Day Schedule:**

| Session | Paper | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Class 6 | 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM (2.5 hours) |
| Afternoon | Class 9 | 2:00 PM – 4:30 PM (2.5 hours) |

**Exam Pattern — Class 6:**

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 50 | 150 |
| General Knowledge | 25 | 50 |
| Language | 25 | 50 |
| Intelligence | 25 | 50 |
| **Total** | **125** | **300** |

**Exam Pattern — Class 9:**

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 50 | 150 |
| English | 25 | 50 |
| Intelligence | 25 | 50 |
| General Science | 25 | 50 |
| Social Studies | 25 | 50 |
| **Total** | **150** | **350** |

**Key Exam Day Instructions:**

1. **No negative marking** — attempt every question
2. **Carry your admit card** and a valid photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, school ID)
3. **Reach the centre 60 minutes before** reporting time
4. **Blue/black ballpoint pen** is required for OMR sheet
5. **Electronic devices** (phones, calculators, smartwatches) are strictly prohibited
6. **No rough paper** will be provided; use the margins of the question paper

**What you should do on exam day:**

- Eat a light, nutritious breakfast
- Carry water in a transparent bottle
- Stay calm and manage time effectively
- Attempt easy questions first to build confidence
- Mark the OMR sheet carefully — no overwriting
- Review answers in the last 15 minutes if time permits

### Phase 7: Answer Key Release (January–February 2027)

**What happens:** Within 1–2 weeks of the exam, NTA releases the **provisional answer key** on the AISSEE portal. Candidates can:

- Download the answer key and question paper
- Compare their answers
- Calculate their expected score
- Challenge any answer they believe is incorrect (for a fee, usually ₹200 per question)

**Challenging the Answer Key:**

If you find an answer that you believe is incorrect:

1. Log in to the NTA portal
2. Select the question number you want to challenge
3. Provide your justification with supporting evidence
4. Pay the challenge fee (typically ₹200 per question)
5. Submit before the challenge window closes (usually 2–3 days)

After reviewing all challenges, NTA releases the **final answer key**, which is used for scoring.

### Phase 8: Result Declaration (February–March 2027)

**What happens:** NTA declares the AISSEE 2027 results on the official portal. Results include:

- Subject-wise marks
- Total marks out of 300 (Class 6) or 350 (Class 9)
- All India Rank (AIR)
- Category-wise rank
- Qualifying status

**How to Check AISSEE 2027 Results:**

1. Visit exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE
2. Click on "AISSEE 2027 Result"
3. Enter your application number and date of birth
4. View and download your scorecard
5. Print the scorecard for counselling

**Cut-off Trends:**

The qualifying cut-off varies each year based on:

- Number of applicants
- Difficulty level of the exam
- Number of available seats
- Category-wise competition

**Approximate Cut-off Trends (Previous Years):**

| Category | Class 6 (approx.) | Class 9 (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| General | 55–65% | 50–60% |
| OBC | 50–60% | 45–55% |
| SC | 40–50% | 35–45% |
| ST | 35–45% | 30–40% |
| Defence | 50–60% | 45–55% |

*Note: These are indicative ranges based on previous years. Actual cut-offs for 2027 may vary.*

### Phase 9: Counselling Process (March–April 2027)

**What happens:** The Sainik School Counselling process is conducted online through the official counselling portal. This is where your AISSEE rank translates into an actual seat allotment.

**Counselling Stages:**

1. **Registration:** Qualified candidates register on the counselling portal
2. **Choice Filling:** Select and prioritise your preferred Sainik Schools (up to 10–15 choices)
3. **Document Upload:** Upload category certificates, domicile proof, and other required documents
4. **Seat Allotment:** Based on rank, category, and preferences, seats are allotted in rounds
5. **Acceptance:** Accept the allotted seat and pay the admission fee
6. **Reporting:** Report to the allotted school for physical document verification

**Counselling Rounds:**

| Round | Purpose | Approximate Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | Primary allotment based on AIR | March 2027 |
| Round 2 | Fill vacancies from Round 1 | March–April 2027 |
| Round 3 | Fill remaining vacancies | April 2027 |
| Spot Round | Fill last remaining seats | April–May 2027 |

For a detailed guide on the counselling process, read our article on [Sainik School Counselling 2027](/blog/sainik-school-counselling-2027/).

### Phase 10: School Reporting and Admission (April–May 2027)

**What happens:** Candidates who accept their seat allotment must report to the allotted school within the specified deadline with all original documents.

**Documents Required for Reporting:**

1. AISSEE 2027 Admit Card
2. AISSEE 2027 Scorecard/Result
3. Counselling Allotment Letter
4. Birth Certificate (original + 2 photocopies)
5. Domicile Certificate (original + 2 photocopies)
6. Category Certificate — SC/ST/OBC (original + 2 photocopies)
7. Defence Certificate (if applicable)
8. Transfer Certificate from previous school
9. Character Certificate from previous school
10. Medical Fitness Certificate
11. Passport-size photographs (8–10 copies)
12. Aadhaar Card
13. Bank Account details (for scholarship, if applicable)

**What happens at the school:**

- Document verification by school administration
- Medical examination by school medical officer
- Physical fitness assessment
- Hostel allocation
- Uniform and book distribution
- Parent orientation programme

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### When is the AISSEE 2027 exam expected to be held?

Based on historical patterns, AISSEE 2027 is expected to be held in **January 2027**. NTA typically conducts the exam on a Sunday in the first or second week of January. The exact date will be confirmed in the official notification, expected in September–October 2026.

### When will the AISSEE 2027 application form be available?

The AISSEE 2027 application form is expected to be available from **October–November 2026** on the NTA portal (exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE). Based on previous years, the application window typically remains open for 4–6 weeks.

### What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?

The last date to submit the AISSEE 2027 application form is expected to be in **November–December 2026**. NTA may extend the deadline by a few days, but candidates should not rely on extensions.

### How can I download the AISSEE 2027 admit card?

The AISSEE 2027 admit card will be available for download on **exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE** approximately 2–3 weeks before the exam. Log in with your application number and date of birth to download and print the admit card.

### When will the AISSEE 2027 results be declared?

AISSEE 2027 results are expected to be declared in **February–March 2027**, approximately 4–6 weeks after the exam. Results are available on the NTA AISSEE portal.

### Is there a correction window for the AISSEE application form?

Yes, NTA typically opens a **correction window of 3–5 days** after the application deadline. During this period, candidates can modify certain fields in their submitted application, including name, date of birth, category, and school preferences.

### When does counselling for Sainik School admission start?

Sainik School counselling for the 2027 session is expected to begin in **March–April 2027**, after the results are declared. The counselling is conducted online through the official portal with multiple rounds.
