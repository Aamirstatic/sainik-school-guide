## The surprise first: Telangana has no central Sainik School — and two of its own

If you are a parent in Hyderabad, Karimnagar or Warangal searching for "Sainik School in Telangana", you will find plenty of confident-sounding answers. Most of them are wrong in an important way.

Here is the honest picture. Telangana has **no Sainik School run by the Ministry of Defence** — none of the 33 established Sainik Schools is in the state, and no new partnership Sainik School approved so far is in Telangana either. But the state government built its own answer: the **Telangana Social Welfare Residential Sainik School at Rukmapur, Karimnagar**, a 26-acre CBSE boys' campus that has run since the 2018-19 academic year, plus a second state-run Sainik School at Ashoknagar, Warangal, run by the tribal welfare society. And Telangana children can still write AISSEE and win seats in Andhra Pradesh's schools.

So a Telangana parent actually has three doors, not one — and they open on different dates, through different exams. This guide puts all three in one place.

## Route 1: TS Sainik School, Rukmapur — the state's own military school

The school at Rukmapur, in Karimnagar district, began as a Telangana Social Welfare Residential school and was converted into a Sainik School from the 2018-19 academic year, on the lines of the Defence Ministry's Sainik Schools. The state government says plainly why it did this: the central Sainik Schools did not give enough representation to children from marginalised communities, so Telangana built one of its own. It is run by the Telangana Social Welfare Residential Educational Institutions Society (TSWREIS), not by the Sainik Schools Society — which is exactly why admission is through its own Common Entrance Test, not AISSEE. Don't let any website tell you otherwise: if someone claims Rukmapur admissions happen through AISSEE, they are wrong.

The campus sits on 26 acres with parade grounds, running tracks and sports fields, and the school is CBSE-affiliated, residential, for boys from Class 6 to Class 12. In 2020, two of its cadets cracked the NDA exam — the first from the school — a serious return for a school that young.

**The exam has three stages.** Stage 1 is a written Common Entrance Test of **100 marks in 150 minutes** (Class 6: Maths 40, English 20, GK 20, Intelligence/Reasoning 20). Stage 2 is screening worth **150 marks** — physical tests (25), a written aptitude test (100) and an oral communication test (25). Stage 3 is a medical fitness test. The registration fee is **₹200**, paid online.

**Seats and who gets them.** Class 6 has **80 seats**; Class 11 has **46**. The Class 6 matrix is heavily weighted to the school's founding purpose: **60 of the 80 seats are for SC candidates (75%)**, with 10 for BC, 5 for ST, 2 for BC-C, 2 for minorities and 1 for OC/EBC. Children from anywhere in Telangana can apply — there is no district quota inside the state.

**Age limits and documents.** For Class 6, the child should be born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017** and be studying in Class 5; for Class 11 entry, the birth window is **1 April 2010 to 31 March 2012**. Documents needed at admission include the caste certificate, income certificate, study and transfer certificates, Aadhaar and five passport photos.

**The 2027 timeline.** The TSWREI Society is expected to release the admission schedule in **February 2027**, with the application window, admit card and written test all falling in February–March 2027 and the result in April. That schedule is reported by Careers360, not yet an official notification — check the official site, tgswreis.telangana.gov.in, when February comes. If your child is in Class 5 now, this is a second chance after AISSEE: the two exams do not clash.

A second state-run option exists alongside Rukmapur: the **Telangana Tribal Welfare Residential Sainik School at Ashoknagar, Warangal**, run by the tribal welfare society, which also admits to Class 6 through its own entrance test. It is smaller and less documented publicly — check the current prospectus before applying.

## Route 2: AISSEE 2027 — the Andhra Pradesh schools

Telangana children write AISSEE like everyone else, but they compete for the **other-state seats — about 33% of seats** at the 33 established Sainik Schools, since 67% are reserved for the home state. That makes the cut-off tougher than for a home-state child, so the choice of school matters.

The nearest schools, and the ones Telangana families most often target, are in Andhra Pradesh. Read our [Andhra Pradesh guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-andhra-pradesh-complete-guide-2027/) for the full detail, but the short version: **Sainik School Korukonda** in Vizianagaram district, founded in January 1962 in a former palace, takes about **80 children in Class 6** — and in 2021 it received about 11,000 applications for those seats. **Sainik School Kalikiri** in Annamayya district, running since 2014, takes about **105 in Class 6**, and its published fee notice for 2025-26 puts the new-admission total at **₹1,95,000** for General and Defence categories.

Two things work in a Telangana child's favour here. First, the Class 6 AISSEE paper is offered in **13 languages, including Telugu** — your child does not have to take the paper in Hindi or English. Second, girls are admitted: **10% of Class 6 vacancies or 10 seats, whichever is more**, are reserved for girls at the 33 schools. That matters because Rukmapur is boys-only — for a Telangana girl, AISSEE is the Sainik School route. The form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM** — nine days from today — with a late window from 3 to 6 December (extra ₹500) and the exam on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**; the fee is ₹850 (₹700 for SC/ST). If the domicile certificate is holding you back, read today's news piece on [the certificates you can't get in a day](/news/aissee-2027-9-days-left-certificates-need-office-visit-2026-11-23/) — this is the last full working week to get one.

## Route 3: Navodaya — 15 schools, and the exam is this Saturday

Don't overlook the third door. Telangana has **15 functional Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas** — confirmed by a Government of India reply in Parliament in February 2026 — covering districts from Adilabad to Warangal. JNVs are fully residential, co-educational and free, and admission to Class 6 is through the JNVST. The exam is on **Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM** — five days away — and admit cards have been out since 28 October. If your child applied, the admit card should already be printed; our [JNVST admit-card guide](/news/jnvst-2027-admit-card-7-details-to-check-15-days-to-exam-2026-11-13/) lists the seven details to check on it.

## What to do this week: a Telangana parent's checklist

1. **If your child is in Class 5 and you want a Sainik School:** submit the AISSEE form before 2 December, and put Korukonda and Kalikiri on the preference list. Read the [Andhra Pradesh guide](/blog/sainik-schools-in-andhra-pradesh-complete-guide-2027/) first — it explains the quota maths that decides your child's real chances.
2. **If your child is a boy in Class 5:** mark February 2027 for the TS Sainik School notification, and keep the Rukmapur CET in the plan as a second attempt after AISSEE. The two exams test similar ground — Maths, English, reasoning and GK — so preparation overlaps.
3. **If your child applied for JNVST:** the exam is Saturday. Admit card printed, centre visited, two pens packed. Our [day-by-day final-week plan](/news/jnvst-2027-5-days-to-exam-28-november-day-by-day-plan-2026-11-23/) covers the last five days.
4. **If your child is a girl:** skip Rukmapur (boys only) and focus on AISSEE's girls' quota and the JNVs, which are co-educational.
5. **Watch for agents.** Admission to every one of these schools is only through the official exam — AISSEE, the TS CET or JNVST. Anyone offering a seat for a payment is running a scam.

Telangana's position is unusual, but it is not a disadvantage once you see it clearly. The state built its own Sainik School because the central ones were out of reach for many of its children — and between Rukmapur, the Andhra schools and the 15 Navodayas, a Telangana child in Class 5 today has three genuine shots at a residential education. Use all three.

## FAQs

**Is there a Sainik School in Telangana?**

There is no Sainik School run by the Ministry of Defence in Telangana. The state does have its own government-run Sainik School — the TSWR Sainik School at Rukmapur, Karimnagar — run by the Telangana Social Welfare Residential Educational Institutions Society, with admission through its own Common Entrance Test, not AISSEE.

**How can a Telangana child get into a Sainik School through AISSEE 2027?**

By writing AISSEE 2027 and competing for the other-state seats — about 33% of seats at the 33 established Sainik Schools. The nearest schools are Korukonda in Vizianagaram and Kalikiri in Annamayya, both in Andhra Pradesh. The AISSEE form closes on 2 December 2026 and the exam is on 31 January 2027.

**When does the TS Sainik School admission 2027-28 start?**

The TSWREI Society is expected to release the admission schedule in February 2027, with the application window, admit card and written test all in February-March 2027. This is the reported schedule from Careers360, not yet an official notification — check tgswreis.telangana.gov.in for the announcement.

**What is the age limit for TS Sainik School Class 6 admission?**

For Class 6, the child should be born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017, and studying in Class 5. For Class 11 entry, the birth window is 1 April 2010 to 31 March 2012. The school admits boys only.

**How many seats does TS Sainik School Rukmapur have?**

80 seats in Class 6 and 46 seats in Class 11, as per the seat matrix reported by Careers360. In Class 6, 60 of the 80 seats are reserved for SC candidates, with the rest spread across BC, ST, BC-C, minority and OC/EBC categories.

**Can Telangana girls join a Sainik School?**

Yes, through AISSEE — girls are admitted to the 33 established Sainik Schools, with 10% of Class 6 vacancies or 10 seats (whichever is more) reserved for them. The state-run TS Sainik School at Rukmapur admits boys only, so for Telangana girls the AISSEE route to schools like Korukonda and Kalikiri is the way in.
