Fourteen days are left to file the AISSEE 2027 form. That deadline is doing exactly what scammers want it to do: it makes parents hurry. And a hurried parent is the easiest person in the world to fool.

Here is the uncomfortable truth about this season. The form is genuinely open, the exam is genuinely on 31 January 2027, and the fee is genuinely ₹850 (₹700 for SC/ST). Every one of those real facts is being used to dress up a fake. The scams below are not new inventions — NTA has warned about each pattern, repeatedly, in public advisories. What is new is the timing: with the 2 December deadline close, the messages are arriving faster, and they sound more urgent. Learn the seven patterns now, and each one takes thirty seconds to kill.

## The seven scams — and the 30-second check for each

| # | The scam | The tell | The 30-second check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A fake "apply" portal** that copies the NTA layout | It arrives as a link — SMS, WhatsApp forward, search ad — and the address is almost right, but not quite | Type **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** into your browser yourself. Never arrive through a forwarded link. If the address is different in any way, close it. |
| 2 | **"Guaranteed seat / direct admission" for money** | A person — often calling himself an agent or an "insider" — promises a seat if you pay now | There is no management quota and no backdoor. Admission is AISSEE merit, then medical fitness, then e-counselling. Anyone selling a seat is selling air. |
| 3 | **"Leaked paper / confirmed questions" on Telegram** | A channel sells "the 2027 paper" or "100 confirmed questions" for a few thousand rupees | NTA has stated categorically that no one has access to the question paper before the exam, and has called leak-sale claims false and fraudulent. There is nothing to buy. |
| 4 | **A call or message from an "NTA official"** asking for an OTP, bank detail or fee | Urgency plus authority: "Your form will be cancelled unless you share the OTP now" | NTA will **never** ask for an OTP, personal banking details or a payment through any message. Its WhatsApp channel is broadcast-only — if a message asks you to reply, share an OTP or click a link, it is not from NTA. Genuine NTA messages carry the blue verified tick next to the name "National Testing Agency". |
| 5 | **"Fee payment failed — pay again" messages** | A message or call says your fee did not go through and sends a payment link or UPI ID | The fee is paid **only inside the official application** — ₹850 for General/OBC-NCL/defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST. If you paid inside the portal, check the payment status on the portal itself. Never pay through a link someone sent you. |
| 6 | **Fake "form correction" links** | A message offers to fix a mistake in your form for a fee, or sends a "correction link" | The correction window is **7–9 December 2026**, on the official portal only. Any correction offer before that window — or any charge for correcting — is fake. |
| 7 | **Fake "merit list / seat allotted" SMS with a payment link** | A message says your child is selected and asks for a "confirmation fee" to hold the seat | Results and allotment are published only on the official portal, and no stage of AISSEE admission asks for a payment by SMS link. A selection message with a payment link is a scam, full stop. |

One rule covers all seven: **if it asks for money, an OTP or a password anywhere except inside the official portal, it is a scam.** That single sentence is worth more than any list, because the scams change their wording every season and the rule does not.

## Why this season is worse than most

Three things are colliding right now. First, the form is genuinely open, so a fake portal has a real thing to imitate. Second, the deadline is genuinely near — 2 December, with a late-fee window of 3–6 December at ₹500 extra — so "pay now or lose the seat" sounds plausible. Third, this is the first cycle in which many parents are applying online for the first time, and the first time is when people trust a forwarded link.

The scammers know all three. That is why the messages are not sloppy. They quote the real fee. They quote the real dates. They use the NTA logo. The only thing wrong with them is the destination — the link, the UPI ID, the phone number — and that is the one thing most parents do not check in a hurry.

## If you already paid

Do not feel ashamed, and do not pay more to "recover" it — that is the second half of the same scam. Do these four things, in this order:

1. **Call your bank immediately.** Ask for the payment to be stopped or reversed, and report the fraud to the bank's fraud desk. Speed matters here more than anywhere else.
2. **Stop all contact** with the person or channel. Do not argue, do not threaten, do not send more money.
3. **Report it** on the national cybercrime portal at **cybercrime.gov.in** or its helpline **1930**, and report the fake website or account to NTA through its official channels. NTA runs a dedicated reporting mechanism for fake claims and works with the PIB Fact Check Unit and law enforcement on takedowns.
4. **Keep everything** — screenshots of the messages, the payment receipt, the phone number, the link. A screenshot costs nothing and is the evidence that makes a report work.

Then check your own form. If you entered your application number or password on a fake portal, change the password on the real portal immediately and watch for any change to your application.

## The two habits that make you nearly scam-proof

**Habit one: type the address yourself.** Bookmark the official portal — exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society — and reach it only by typing or from your own bookmark. Every single scam in the table above dies at this step, because the fake needs you to arrive through its link.

**Habit two: never pay, share or click under urgency.** A real deadline gives you days — the form closes at 11:50 PM on 2 December, and the late window runs to 6 December. A message that gives you minutes is not a deadline; it is a pressure tactic. Close it, open the portal yourself, and check.

Tell your child too. Older siblings in Class 9 are on the same Telegram channels where "leaked papers" circulate, and a teenager who believes a paper is for sale will tell a younger sibling. One honest conversation — "nobody has the paper, nobody can sell you a seat" — is cheaper than a scam.

## FAQs

**What is the only official website for AISSEE 2027 applications?**

exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. Type it into your browser yourself every time. NTA's own advice is to never reach the portal through a link in an SMS, email, WhatsApp message or search ad.

**Can anyone sell a guaranteed Sainik School seat?**

No. Admission is strictly through the AISSEE written exam, medical fitness and e-counselling on merit. There is no management quota, no backdoor and no official who can arrange a seat. Anyone offering one for money is running a scam.

**What is the real AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs. 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and Rs. 700 for SC and ST candidates. The fee is paid only inside the official online application. A late fee of Rs. 500 applies only to applications made from 3 to 6 December 2026, on the portal itself.

**Will NTA ever ask me for an OTP or a payment on WhatsApp?**

Never. NTA has said repeatedly that it will never ask for OTPs, personal banking details or payments through any message, and it never sends exam papers, answer keys or admit cards through links. Its WhatsApp channel is broadcast-only.

**I already paid money to someone offering a Sainik School seat. What do I do?**

Stop all contact, do not pay more, and call your bank immediately to try to stop or reverse the payment. Then report it on the national cybercrime portal at cybercrime.gov.in or its helpline 1930, and report the fake website or account to NTA. Keep screenshots of every message and payment.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 correction window?**

7 to 9 December 2026, on the official NTA portal only. Any message offering a form-correction link before that window, or asking for money to correct your form, is fake.
