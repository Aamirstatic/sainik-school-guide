## One school, one exam — and why that changes your strategy

Parents searching for "Sainik Schools in Gujarat" usually expect a list. The honest core answer is shorter: Gujarat has **one school under the Sainik Schools Society** — **Sainik School Balachadi**, near Jamnagar. Every Gujarat family aiming for a Society school through AISSEE is, in effect, aiming at the same campus. That concentrates the competition, but it also simplifies your planning: one school to research, one campus to visit, one preference to get right.

Around it, newer partnership-mode schools have appeared under the Sainik Schools Society's expansion. This guide separates the two layers so you do not mix them up — the partnership schools follow their own admission arrangements, while Balachadi admits through AISSEE, conducted by NTA.

Balachadi is one of the oldest Sainik Schools in the country — founded in the first wave of the 1960s, when the Sainik Schools system was created to prepare boys for the National Defence Academy. That six-decade history is the school's real strength for parents: a settled campus on the Jamnagar coast, a large alumni base, and a routine refined over generations of cadets.

Two small honesty notes on the campus figure. School profiles list the campus at 375 acres, while an older EducationWorld profile put it at 300 acres — the campus has likely been measured differently over the years, so treat 375 acres as reported rather than surveyed. Similarly, the founding story is sometimes told two ways: the school was established in July 1961, in the period when V. K. Krishna Menon, then Defence Minister, conceived the Sainik Schools system.

## The 67% Gujarat quota — and why it helps you

Sainik Schools reserve **67% of seats for candidates from the home state** and **33% for candidates from other states**. For a Gujarat-domicile child, that is a genuine advantage: most of Balachadi's seats are contested only among Gujarat children, not against the whole country. Within each quota, the standard reservation pattern applies — 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% OBC (non-creamy layer) — with 25% of the remaining seats for children of serving and ex-servicemen, and a 10% girls' quota in Class 6.

The practical consequence is worth stating plainly. A Gujarat child and an out-of-state child with the same marks are not competing for the same seats. Your child's real competition is the Gujarat merit list for their category — which is why the domicile certificate matters so much, and why getting it right before the form opens is worth more than an extra week of revision later.

## Seats and fees: what is reported, what is not

This is the section where most Gujarat guides over-claim, so here is exactly what the sources say — and where they disagree.

**Seats (reported):** one admission portal lists around **100 Class 6 seats** at Balachadi, including about **10 seats for girls**, and around **14 Class 9 seats**. That is a single secondary source, not a seat matrix. Seat counts vary by year, so treat these figures as reported until the AISSEE 2027 seat matrix is published.

**Fees (reported, conflicting):** Careers360's 2026 school-wise fee list puts Balachadi at **Rs 1,65,600** as a lumpsum figure, while another admission portal quotes around **Rs 1,54,900** and notes fees rise by up to 10% a year. The two figures cannot both be the current fee, so budget around the higher figure and confirm with the school's own fee notice before paying anything. Scholarships and fee concessions exist for eligible categories, but the exact concession for your child depends on the school's current notice — do not budget on a concession you have not confirmed.

## The Balachadi checklist: 8 things Gujarat parents should do now

1. **Get the domicile certificate ready** — the 67% Gujarat quota turns on it.
2. **Check the age window** — Class 6: 10–12 years as of 31 March 2027; Class 9: 13–15 years. Confirm the exact date-of-birth window in the AISSEE 2027 bulletin when it appears.
3. **Visit the campus if you can** — Balachadi is a residential school on the coast near Jamnagar; the distance from home and the coastal climate are real factors for a child who will live there.
4. **Put Balachadi first in preferences only if you mean it** — the home-state quota rewards a clear first choice.
5. **Prepare for the AISSEE pattern** — Class 6: 300 marks (Maths, Intelligence, Language, GK); Class 9: 400 marks. No interview for either class.
6. **Keep the medical standard in mind** — AISSEE qualification is followed by a medical examination; a known issue flagged early is easier to manage than one discovered late.
7. **Do not pay any agent** — AISSEE admission runs only through NTA and the official counselling process. Anyone selling a Balachadi seat is running a scam.
8. **Watch the notification, not the rumours** — the AISSEE 2027 bulletin is now out: NTA opened applications on 3 November 2026. Our [notification guide](/news/aissee-2027-notification-out-applications-open-exam-jan-31-2026-11-03/) has the full schedule.

67% of seats are reserved for candidates from the home state, Gujarat, and 33% for candidates from other states, under the standard Sainik School reservation pattern.
