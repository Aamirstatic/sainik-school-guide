## West Bengal's only Sainik School — and one of the oldest in India

If you live in West Bengal and your child dreams of a uniform, there is exactly one door in your own state: Sainik School Purulia. It was established on **29 January 1962**, in only the second year of the Sainik Schools scheme — older than almost every Sainik School your child could apply to, with more than six decades of cadets behind it.

The school began humbly, at Bongabari near Chharra, under its first principal, Lt Col S. Mazumdar. In **1966** it moved to its present campus — about **280 acres on the Purulia–Ranchi road**, roughly 5 km from Purulia bus stand and 8 km from Purulia Junction railway station. That distance matters more than it sounds. A Sainik School is a full residential school: your child lives on campus from Class 6. For a family in Purulia town, the gate is a short drive away. For a family in Kolkata, it is a train journey of several hours — worth planning before the form, not after the allotment. (One discrepancy to note: the school's CBSE mandatory disclosure lists about 100 acres, while the school's own profile describes a 280-acre campus. Either way, it is a large, self-contained residential campus.)

## The seats: 120 in Class 6, and a narrow door in Class 9

For 2027-28, Purulia has **120 Class 6 seats** and **20 Class 9 seats**. Here is the split, exactly as the AISSEE 2027 bulletin lists it:

| Class 6 seats | Boys | Girls | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| West Bengal domicile (67%) | 74 | 7 | 81 |
| Other states (33%) | 34 | 5 | 39 |
| **Total** | **108** | **12** | **120** |

Within the 74 home-state boys' seats, the category split is: SC 11, ST 5, OBC-NCL 20, Defence 9, General 29. The reservation structure follows the Sainik Schools SOP — SC 15%, ST 7.5%, OBC-NCL 27%, and 25% of the remainder for defence wards.

Class 9 is a much smaller door: 20 seats, 14 for West Bengal boys and 6 for boys from other states, with no girls' seats this year. If your child is in Class 8, understand the maths honestly — Class 9 entry at Purulia is one of the tightest in the country, and the form's school-preference list should include other schools too. Our [guide to choosing school preferences](/blog/aissee-2027-form-which-sainik-schools-to-list-rank-order/) explains how to order them.

The home-state advantage is the single biggest fact in this guide. **81 of the 120 Class 6 seats go to West Bengal children first.** A West Bengal child competes against other West Bengal children for those seats — not against the whole country. That is why the domicile certificate matters so much. Our [domicile certificate guide](/blog/domicile-certificate-for-sainik-school-admission/) covers what to obtain and how long it takes.

## The fees: budget on the official sheet, not the old listings

Here is where parents get confused, so let us be plain. The school's official 2026-27 fee document — reproduced by The Speedy Brains after checking the school's site — puts a new Class 6 cadet's first-year total at **Rs 2,07,614** for General category (Rs 2,06,114 for SC/ST):

| Item (2026-27) | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Tuition fees | ₹1,28,614 |
| Sundry charges (washing, barber, medical, telephone, tours, bakery, stationery, incidentals) | ₹26,500 |
| Pocket money | ₹3,000 |
| Diet charges | ₹32,500 |
| Caution money (new admission; ₹1,500 for SC/ST) | ₹3,000 |
| Additional clothing (new students) | ₹14,000 |
| **Total, General** | **₹2,07,614** |

The first instalment is **₹1,00,000**, with the balance in the second instalment. Payment is through the SBI fee portal, a demand draft, or POS at the school — no cash or UPI, according to the same source.

Why flag this? Because older listings — including Wikipedia — quote "about ₹1.6 lakh a year", and our own earlier West Bengal guide quoted roughly ₹1,65,130 from education portals. Those are older figures. Budget on ₹2,07,614, and confirm the 2027-28 figure from the school itself before paying anything. The West Bengal government also awards a few scholarships on a merit-cum-means basis — full fees, clothing and pocket money for those who qualify — but the income criteria are stringent and very few actually qualify, so do not plan on it.

## How admission works: the timeline from form to joining

Admission is only through the AISSEE — there is no management quota, no donation route, no separate Purulia paper. The exam is the same for every school in India.

| Step | Date | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AISSEE 2027 form (online) | 3 November – 2 December 2026 (11:50 PM) | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| Late window (₹500 late fee) | 3–6 December 2026 | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| Correction window | 7–9 December 2026 | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| Exam city announced | 11 January 2027 | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| Admit card download | 25 January 2027 | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| **AISSEE 2027 exam** | **Sunday, 31 January 2027** | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| Answer key, result, e-counselling, medical, admission | February–June 2027 | 🔶 EXPECTED (2026 pattern) |

The exam pattern, confirmed from the 2026 bulletin and expected to repeat: Class 6 is 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes, with Maths alone worth 150 marks. Class 9 is 150 questions, 400 marks, 180 minutes. There is **no negative marking**, and a child must score at least 25% in each section and 40% overall to qualify (not applicable to SC/ST). The application fee is ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and ₹700 for SC/ST.

Two practical warnings. First, the form needs a photo (10–200 KB, JPG, white background), a signature, a left-thumb impression and the certificates — birth, domicile, category and defence papers where applicable. Our [photo and signature guide](/blog/aissee-2027-photo-signature-specs-size-background-rejections/) lists the exact specs and the rejection reasons NTA actually flags. Second, the form is complete only when the Confirmation Page is generated. A fee paid is not a form submitted.

## A day at Purulia: what your child actually signs up for

Purulia does not publish its daily timetable online, so the routine below is a typical Sainik School day, drawn from the routines that Sainik Schools Satara, Nalanda and Rewa publish. Treat the times as a guide — the shape of the day is the point.

| Activity | Usual time |
| --- | --- |
| Reveille (wake-up) | 5:00 – 5:45 am |
| PT / physical training | about 6:00 – 7:00 am |
| Bath, breakfast, assembly | about 7:00 – 8:30 am |
| Classes (7–8 periods, short break) | about 8:30 am – 2:00 pm |
| Lunch and rest / clubs | about 2:00 – 4:00 pm |
| Games (NCC on some days) | about 4:00 – 5:30 pm |
| Evening prep (supervised study) | about 6:00 – 8:00 pm |
| Dinner, night prep, roll call | about 8:00 – 10:00 pm |
| Lights out | 10:00 – 10:30 pm |

That 5 am start is the part parents underestimate. A child who has never woken before 7 am will find the first month hard — not the studies, the mornings. If your child is serious about Purulia, start moving the wake-up time earlier in December, not in January. The school gives 70 days of official vacation a year, which is generous for a residential school, but the term itself is full: PT, classes, games, prep, and house activities every single day.

Houses are the second family. The school's newsletter names Netaji House, Tagore House and Chittranjan House among the houses that compete in lecturettes, sports and cultural events. The girls' hostel is Kangsawati, renovated and inaugurated by the then Governor of West Bengal in January 2021.

## The record: why parents trust a 64-year-old school

Purulia's alumni list is the reason this school carries weight beyond West Bengal. Its former cadets include **Neiphiu Rio**, the three-term Chief Minister of Nagaland, and **Air Marshal Arup Raha**, a former Chief of the Air Staff. By the school's golden jubilee in 2012, about **250 of roughly 2,500 cadets** who had passed out had made it to the National Defence Academy. More recently, the school's January 2024 newsletter reported **20 out of 50 cadets qualifying the UPSC NDA/NA examination** — a strong recent result, though the newsletter does not break the figure down by batch, so treat it as the school's own reported figure rather than an official statistic.

The school also runs the activities parents ask about: NCC, a horse stable inaugurated in 2021, and clubs from astronomy to debating. It is, in short, the full Sainik School package — and the reason West Bengal's single seat in the scheme is one of the most contested in eastern India.

## Questions to ask before you commit

- **Confirm the 2027-28 fee in writing** from the school before admission — the published sheet is 2026-27, and fees are revised every year.
- **Ask about the girls' hostel and the first-term visiting schedule** if your daughter is applying, and about medical facilities and the nearest hospital.
- **Check the domicile certificate now**, not in December — it is the document that unlocks the 67% home-state pool.

## FAQs

**How many seats does Sainik School Purulia have for 2027-28?**

Purulia has 120 Class 6 seats — 108 for boys (74 West Bengal domicile and 34 other states) and 12 for girls (7 West Bengal and 5 other states) — and 20 Class 9 seats, all for boys. The figures are tentative, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin, Appendices VI-A and VI-A1.

**When was Sainik School Purulia founded?**

It was established on 29 January 1962, one of the oldest Sainik Schools in India. Classes began at Bongabari near Chharra, and the school moved to its present 280-acre campus on the Purulia-Ranchi road in 1966.

**How do I get my child admitted to Sainik School Purulia?**

Only through AISSEE, the national entrance exam run by NTA. For 2027-28, apply online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society by 2 December 2026. Class 6 needs age 10 to 12 on 31 March 2027; Class 9 needs age 13 to 15 and a passed Class 8.

**What are the fees at Sainik School Purulia?**

The school's official 2026-27 fee document puts a new Class 6 cadet's first-year total at Rs 2,07,614 for General category (Rs 2,06,114 for SC/ST), including tuition, diet, sundry charges, pocket money, caution money and clothing. Older listings quote around Rs 1.6 lakh, so treat the official sheet as the figure to budget on and confirm the 2027-28 fee from the school before paying.

**Does Sainik School Purulia admit girls?**

Yes — 12 girls' seats in Class 6 for 2027-28, seven for West Bengal girls and five for girls from other states. There are no girls' seats in Class 9 this year. The school has a girls' hostel, Kangsawati, renovated and inaugurated in January 2021.

**What is the home-state advantage for West Bengal children at Purulia?**

67 percent of seats are reserved for West Bengal-domicile children. In Class 6 that is 81 of the 120 seats — 74 boys and 7 girls. A West Bengal child competes first against other West Bengal children for those seats, not against the whole country.
