## Three days, and the clock is already running

Here is the part parents miss. The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**, and the correction window opens on **7 December** and closes on **9 December**. That is three days. Not three days to discover your mistakes — three days to fix the ones you already found.

The difference matters. A parent who opens the confirmation page on 7 December and spots a wrong date of birth has to find the birth certificate, decide what the correct entry is, log in, correct it and re-download the confirmation page — all while thousands of other parents are doing the same thing on a portal that slows down under load. A parent who audits the form tonight does the same work calmly, over the next two weeks, and uses the window for a five-minute edit.

So tonight's job is not the correction. It is the audit.

## The seven-point audit — do it tonight

Open the confirmation page you downloaded after submitting, and check it line by line against the original documents. Not from memory. From the documents.

1. **Name spelling, against the birth certificate.** The name on the form must match the birth certificate and the school record exactly. A single transposed letter is the classic correction-window job — and the classic reason a form gets flagged at document verification.
2. **Date of birth, against the school certificate.** Class 6: born 1 April 2015–31 March 2017. Class 9: born 1 April 2012–31 March 2014. Age is counted as on 31 March 2027, with no relaxation for any category. A wrong date of birth is not a typo — it can mean disqualification.
3. **Category, against the certificate you hold.** General, OBC (NCL), SC, ST, or defence ward — the category on the form must match the certificate you can produce at verification. Claiming a category you cannot prove is worse than claiming none.
4. **School choices, in the right order.** Check that the schools listed are the ones you actually want, in the order you want them. A mis-ordered preference list is quietly expensive: it decides which school your child is offered.
5. **Exam-city choices.** The exam city is announced around 11 January 2027, and it follows the choices in the form. A city you cannot reach on exam morning is a problem you created in November.
6. **Photograph and signature.** Open the confirmation page and look at them. Is the photo actually your child's, clear, recent, and in the format the bulletin asked for? Wrong or unclear uploads are among the most common reasons forms get stuck.
7. **Contact details.** The mobile number and email on the form are where every later message from NTA goes — exam-city intimation, admit card, result. If you typed a number you no longer use, that is the first thing to fix.

Write down every mismatch you find, with the correct value beside it, and keep the note with the confirmation page. When the window opens on 7 December, you will correct from the note, not from memory.

## One honest warning about what the window cannot do

The window is titled "Correction in Particulars", and the Information Bulletin — not this article — is the final word on which fields can be edited. Some fields may be locked even during the window, and the bulletin is where that list lives. Read it on the official portal before 7 December, so you are not surprised on the day.

And one more thing: after 9 December, the published schedule lists no second window. The form stands as submitted, and any error in it travels with the application — to the admit card, to the exam centre, and to document verification. The correction window is a safety net, not a second deadline. The parents who use it well are the ones who never needed it urgently.

## FAQs

**When is the AISSEE 2027 correction window?**

7 to 9 December 2026, as per the NTA schedule published with the AISSEE 2027 notice of 3 November 2026. That is the only window in the published schedule for correcting particulars in a submitted form.

**What should I check on the AISSEE 2027 confirmation page before the correction window?**

Your child's name spelling against the birth certificate, the date of birth, the category, the school choices, the exam-city choices and the photograph and signature. Check them now, while you still have time to gather the right documents, not on 7 December.

**Can every mistake in the AISSEE form be corrected in the correction window?**

No. The window is titled "Correction in Particulars" in the NTA schedule, and the Information Bulletin is the final word on which fields can be edited. Some fields may be locked even during the window, so read the bulletin on the official portal before 7 December.

**What happens if I miss the AISSEE 2027 correction window?**

The published schedule lists no second correction window. After 9 December 2026, the form stands as submitted, and any error in it travels with the application to the admit card and document verification.
