## Five posts, one deadline

Sainik School Rewari, at Village Gothra Ahir in Haryana's Rewari district, has opened recruitment for **five contractual posts** — one vacancy each for Medical Officer, PEM/PTI-cum-Matron (female only), Nursing Assistant (male only), Counsellor, and PGT Physical Education. The appointment is contractual, not regular: an initial **11 months plus a one-month break**, extendable on performance up to three years, with no pay during breaks or vacations.

This is the second Sainik School recruitment story this week here — Chittorgarh's five posts (apply by 7 November) ran on 23 October — and Rewari's deadline falls one day earlier, so do not confuse the two.

## Posts, pay and eligibility at a glance

| Post | Vacancies | Monthly pay (consolidated) | Age as on 17 Oct 2026 | Core qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medical Officer | 1 | Rs 84,960 | 18–50 | MBBS |
| PGT Physical Education | 1 | Rs 76,160 | 21–50 | M.A. Physical Education or M.P.Ed. (50%) + B.P.Ed./D.P.Ed. |
| Counsellor | 1 | Rs 71,840 | 21–50 | Graduation/postgraduation in Psychology, or PG in Child Development, or degree with diploma in career guidance and counselling |
| PEM/PTI-cum-Matron (female) | 1 | Rs 46,720 | 18–50 | Matriculation + fluent English |
| Nursing Assistant (male) | 1 | Rs 40,800 | 18–50 | Nursing diploma/degree + 5 years' experience, or ex-serviceman (Medical Assistant trade) with 5 years' service after training |

Age relaxation applies as notified: **5 years for SC/ST and 3 years for OBC**. All five vacancies are listed as unreserved.

## How to apply — and what not to do

There is **no online form**. Download the prescribed application form and the official notification from the school website, **ssrw.org**, fill it in, and send it with attested copies of mark sheets from Matriculation onwards, qualification and experience certificates, one recent passport-size photograph, and a self-addressed envelope bearing **Rs 56 in postage stamps**. The application fee is **Rs 500 by crossed bank draft in favour of "Principal, Sainik School Rewari", payable at Rewari**; SC/ST candidates are exempt.

Send it by **India Post or by hand** to: The Principal, Sainik School Rewari, Village Gothra Ahir, District Rewari, Haryana – 123102. Write the post clearly on the envelope. **Private courier applications are not accepted**, and late or incomplete applications can be rejected. Selection is by shortlisting, then a written test and/or interview as applicable, then document verification; no TA/DA is paid.

## Why the 6 November date matters

Thirteen days is short for an offline application: the draft must be made, documents attested, and the envelope posted early enough to reach Rewari by 5 PM on the 6th. Treat **3 November** as your personal deadline, and keep the postal receipt — your only proof of timely dispatch.

## FAQs

**How many posts are open in Sainik School Rewari Recruitment 2026?**

Five contractual posts, one each: Medical Officer, PEM/PTI-cum-Matron (female), Nursing Assistant (male), Counsellor, and PGT Physical Education. All five are unreserved vacancies.

**What is the last date to apply for Sainik School Rewari Recruitment 2026?**

The completed application must reach the school by 5 PM on 6 November 2026, by India Post or by hand. Private courier applications are not accepted.

**What salary do the Rewari contractual posts pay?**
