The regular application window for the Rashtriya Military Schools Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026 closed this evening at 5 PM — but the door is not shut. From **tomorrow, 21 October**, NTA opens a five-day late-fee window that runs until **25 October 2026 (5 PM)**, giving families who missed today one final chance to apply for Class 6 and Class 9 admission to the five Rashtriya Military Schools for the 2027-28 session.

> **Last verified:** 20 October 2026 · Late window **21–25 Oct 2026** ✅ · Late fee **Rs. 500** ✅ · Exam **13 Dec 2026** ✅ — all per the official NTA RMS CET 2026 information bulletin (and reported by EdexLive, JagranJosh and The Times of India). Regular-window deadline context from our [day-of playbook](/news/rms-cet-2026-closes-today-5pm-2026-10-20/).

## The late window, exactly

| Detail | What the bulletin says |
|---|---|
| Late application dates | **21 Oct – 25 Oct 2026**, up to 5 PM on 25 Oct ✅ |
| Late fee | **Rs. 500 extra**, on top of the regular fee ✅ |
| Last date for late-fee payment | **25 Oct 2026, up to 11:50 PM** ✅ |
| Total payable (General/OBC/OBC-NCL) | **Rs. 1,500** (Rs. 1,000 + Rs. 500) ✅ |
| Total payable (SC/ST/war widows & KIA wards) | **Rs. 1,250** (Rs. 750 + Rs. 500) ✅ |
| Payment modes | Credit/debit card, net banking, UPI ✅ |
| Official portal | **exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet** ✅ |

Two points matter. First, the late fee is flat — Rs. 500 extra for every category. Second, the 11:50 PM fee-payment deadline means a failed transaction at 5 PM is not fatal; the fee can still go through the same evening. But do not read that as permission to dawdle: NTA warns that the schedule will remain unaltered even if the exam date is declared a public holiday — this machinery does not wait for anyone.

## What happens after 25 October

The bulletin lays out the full Stage-I roadmap, and it is tighter than most families expect:

- **27–28 Oct 2026:** Application correction window — your one shot at fixing a wrong name, photo or category ✅
- **Last week of Nov 2026:** Exam city intimation slip ✅
- **5–7 days before the exam:** Admit card download ✅
- **13 Dec 2026 (Sunday):** Stage-I written exam — pen-and-paper OMR, MCQs, **2 hours 30 minutes**, 63 cities ✅
- **January 2027:** Stage-1 result with final answer keys ✅
- **Feb–Mar 2027:** Medical examinations and interviews ✅
- **1 April 2027:** Joining at school ✅

From tomorrow's late window to the written exam is **53 days**. That is the number to plan around — not the deadline, but the countdown. If you apply this week, the honest move is to start the syllabus audit immediately, because the correction window (27–28 Oct) passes before you have even received a city slip.

## Who the late window is really for

Not everyone who missed today should panic-file. Ask three questions:

1. **Is the child genuinely eligible?** RMS CET has its own age and class bands — confirm them in the information bulletin before paying a rupee.
2. **Is RMS the right fit, or is it a reflex?** The five Rashtriya Military Schools (Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum, Bengaluru) are English-medium residential schools under the Ministry of Defence. They are demanding places, and the route in is the CET — admission is only through this test.
3. **Are documents ready tonight?** Photograph, signature, category certificate, date-of-birth proof. A late-window application done in a rush with wrong uploads is a rejection letter with a Rs. 1,500 price tag.

## What to do tonight

- **Download the information bulletin** from the official NTA RMS CET portal and read the eligibility chapter — not a news summary, the bulletin itself.
- **Keep documents scanned and ready** in the exact file sizes the form demands.
- **Apply mid-window (22–23 Oct)** rather than on the 25th, when server traffic peaks and UPI timeouts spike.
- **Save the confirmation page** after submission — it is your only proof until the city slip arrives.

And remember: this is a second chance, not a second deadline to miss. At 5:01 PM on 25 October, RMS CET 2026 is over — and the next train out is the December exam, not another window.

## FAQs

**When does the RMS CET 2026 late application window open?**
The late-fee window runs from 21 October to 25 October 2026, up to 5 PM on the final day. Late fee payments can be completed until 11:50 PM on 25 October.

**What is the late fee for RMS CET 2026?**
Rs. 500 extra on top of the regular fee: Rs. 1,500 total for General/OBC/OBC-NCL and Rs. 1,250 total for SC/ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**
13 December 2026 (Sunday), pen-and-paper OMR-based, 2 hours 30 minutes, across 63 exam cities. City intimation slips come in the last week of November; admit cards 5-7 days before the exam.

**Can I correct mistakes in my RMS CET form?**
Yes — the correction window is 27-28 October 2026, after both the regular and late windows close. Apply only on the official NTA portal, exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet.
