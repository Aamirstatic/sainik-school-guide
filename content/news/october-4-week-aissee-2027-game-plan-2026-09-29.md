# 124 Days to AISSEE 2027: Your 4-Week October Game Plan (Starts Thursday)

> **Last verified: 29 September 2026.** This is a planning explainer, not a breaking-news report — no new NTA announcement has been made as of today. The exam date is CONFIRMED tentative; all other dates below are EXPECTED based on previous-cycle patterns.

Thursday is 1 October. For most people that is just a date on the calendar. For AISSEE families, it is the month that decides everything — because October is when the AISSEE 2027 notification is **expected** to land (EXPECTED, based on the last three cycles' pattern), and last year parents got exactly **20 days** between notification and the application deadline (CONFIRMED, AISSEE 2026: 10–30 October 2025).

Do the arithmetic that matters: the exam is **31 January 2027** (CONFIRMED tentative, NTA exam calendar 2026-27), which is **124 days** from today. October eats up 31 of those days. How your child spends them is the difference between a calm, prepared application and the annual October panic.

## The 4-week October plan

![Alarm clock beside study materials — October study planning](/images/inbody/october-4-week-aissee-2027-game-plan-2026-09-29.webp)

| Week | Dates | Mission |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | 1–7 Oct | Notification watch + finish the document checklist |
| **Week 2** | 8–14 Oct | Syllabus sprint: close the two weakest chapters |
| **Week 3** | 15–21 Oct | First full mock-test rhythm + error log |
| **Week 4** | 22–31 Oct | Revise, stay application-ready |

**Week 1 — watch and paperwork.** Check the [NTA AISSEE portal](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society) every two or three days. More importantly, finish the document checklist now — birth certificate matching school records, a fresh photograph, category certificates (EXPECTED document list from the 2026 cycle; confirm against the new bulletin). Our [7-documents checklist](/news/aissee-2027-documents-ready-before-notification-2026-09-28/) from Sunday has the full list. If the bulletin drops in Week 1, you apply in days, not scramble in weeks.

**Week 2 — syllabus sprint.** Honestly, this is the week most families waste waiting for "the notification first, then we'll study." Don't. Pick your child's two weakest chapters — for most Class 6 aspirants it is fractions/percentage or mensuration — and finish them this week with concept revision plus 50 practice questions each. New topics in November are far costlier than in October.

**Week 3 — mock rhythm begins.** One full **150-minute, 125-question** paper this week, taken in one sitting with the OMR discipline: bubbling as you go, no phone in the room (CONFIRMED 2026 pattern). Then — and this is the part people skip — maintain an **error log**: every wrong answer gets one line explaining *why*. Three mocks with an error log beat ten mocks without one.

**Week 4 — revise and stay ready.** October's last stretch is revision of everything covered this month plus a second mock. Keep the application documents in one folder, scanned and named, so that if the window opens — and the 20-day 2026 precedent says it could be short — your child loses zero study days to paperwork.
