## 16 days left — the Kunjpura window is closing

Sainik School Kunjpura in Karnal, Haryana, notified 15 teaching and non-teaching vacancies on **25 September 2026**, and the application window closes on **31 October 2026**. With 16 days remaining, this is the last comfortable window to get a paper application together — because this recruitment is **offline only**, and the school counts the date your form *reaches* Kunjpura, not the date you posted it.

If you missed our recent coverage of similar drives, compare notes with the [Sainik School Goalpara recruitment (13 posts, apply by 12 November)](/news/sainik-school-goalpara-recruitment-2026-2026-10-14/) and the [Sainik School Rewari recruitment (5 posts, apply by 6 November)](/news/sainik-school-rewari-recruitment-2026-five-posts-2026-10-14/) — but note that Kunjpura's deadline comes first.

## The 15 posts at a glance

Six TGT posts are **regular appointments** — these are the prize seats. Nine posts are contractual.

| Post | Posts | Nature |
|------|-------|--------|
| TGT (General Science) | 1 | Regular |
| TGT (Hindi / Sanskrit) | 2 | Regular |
| TGT (Maths) | 2 | Regular |
| TGT (English) | 1 | Regular |
| Horse Riding Instructor | 1 | Contractual |
| Band Master | 1 | Contractual |
| Mess Manager | 1 | Contractual |
| Nursing Sister (Female) | 1 | Contractual |
| Ward Boys | 5 | Contractual |

Category-wise reservations are post-specific — per 9curry's report of the notification, the TGT (General Science) seat is OBC-only, TGT (Hindi/Sanskrit) has one SC and one OBC seat, and TGT (Maths) has one SC and one OBC seat. Four of the five Ward Boy seats are UR and one is OBC. Check the official notification for the full reservation table before applying.

## Who can apply

- **TGT posts:** Bachelor's degree in the relevant subject with at least 50% marks, B.Ed (or equivalent), and CTET/STET qualification. Age 21–35 years as on 31 October 2026.
- **Band Master:** 21–50 years as on 31 October 2026; Band Master / Band Major / Drum Major course qualification.
- **Horse Riding Instructor:** Matriculation plus a horse riding instructor course. Age 18–50.
- **Mess Manager:** Matriculation with experience in running a catering organisation. Age 18–50.
- **Nursing Sister (Female):** Nursing diploma / GNM / B.Sc Nursing. Age 18–50. Female candidates only.
- **Ward Boys:** Matriculation. Age 18–50.

Age relaxation applies as per government rules. The selection process runs in stages: shortlisting of applications, written exam and/or practical or skill test, interview, and document verification.

## Pay: Level-7 for TGTs, ₹25,000–₹35,000 for contractual staff

CONFIRMED from multiple portals reporting the notification:

- **TGT (all four subjects):** 7th CPC Level-7 — ₹44,900 to ₹1,42,400, plus allowances. This is a regular government school teaching post, not a short contract.
- **Horse Riding Instructor / Band Master:** ₹35,000 per month (consolidated)
- **Nursing Sister:** ₹30,000 per month (consolidated)
- **Mess Manager:** ₹29,200 per month (consolidated)
- **Ward Boys:** ₹25,000 per month (consolidated)

## How to apply: offline, by post, before 31 October

There is **no online application**:

1. **Download the prescribed form** from the school website (sskunjpura.org), where the notification and form have been available since 25 September 2026.
2. **Fill it in**, affix a recent passport-size photograph, and attach self-attested copies of certificates, testimonials and mark sheets.
3. **Get a demand draft of ₹500** — for all candidates, all categories — drawn in favour of *"Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura"*, payable at Karnal.
4. **Mark the envelope** clearly: *"APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF ……………"* — unmarked packets can sit unopened.
5. **Post or hand-deliver to:** The Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal, Haryana – 132023, so it **reaches by 31 October 2026**.

Post it this week. With a 31 October receipt deadline falling 16 days out, postal delays are the single biggest reason applications fail — the school will not excuse late delivery.

## FAQs

**How many posts are open in the Sainik School Kunjpura Recruitment 2026?**

Fifteen posts in total: 6 regular TGT posts (General Science, Hindi/Sanskrit, Maths and English) and 9 contractual posts including Horse Riding Instructor, Band Master, Mess Manager, Nursing Sister (Female) and Ward Boys.

**What is the last date to apply for Sainik School Kunjpura Recruitment 2026?**

Applications must reach the Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal (Haryana) – 132023, on or before 31 October 2026. That is a receipt deadline, not a postmark deadline — late arrivals are rejected.

**What is the application fee for Kunjpura recruitment?**

₹500 for all candidates, payable only by demand draft drawn in favour of the Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, payable at Karnal. Cash and online payment are not accepted.

**What is the salary for TGT posts at Sainik School Kunjpura?**

TGT posts are regular appointments at 7th CPC Level-7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400) plus allowances. Contractual posts pay ₹25,000–₹35,000 per month consolidated, per the notification as reported by job portals.
