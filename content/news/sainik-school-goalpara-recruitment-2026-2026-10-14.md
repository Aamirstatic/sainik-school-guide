## A school hiring drive, not an entrance exam — but it matters to your family

Before the confusion starts: this is **recruitment**, not admission. Sainik School Goalpara, Assam, has opened applications for 13 teaching and non-teaching posts for 2026 — teachers, administrators, support staff and a horse riding instructor. The announcement (Advt. No. SSG/03/2026, dated 3 October 2026) hit the national news this morning, and applications must reach the school **on or before 12 November 2026**.

Why carry this on a parent-focused admissions site? Because a school that hires well is a school that teaches well. Staffing quality — especially PGT/TGT appointments — is one of the most underrated signals parents can check before choosing a Sainik School during [AISSAC counselling](https://sainikschooleastsiang.in/blog/aissac-choice-filling-mistakes-cost-seats/). And plenty of readers have relatives — uncles, aunts, older siblings — looking for a government school job.

## How to apply: offline, by post, before 12 November

There is **no online form**. This is a paper application, and the school must receive it physically:

1. **Download the prescribed proforma** from the recruitment notification on the school's website.
2. **Fill in the form**, affix a recent passport-size photograph, and attach self-attested copies of mark sheets and testimonials.
3. **Get a demand draft** — ₹300 (General) or ₹200 (SC/ST/OBC) — drawn in favour of *"Principal, Sainik School Goalpara"*, payable at State Bank of India, Mornai (Code 9148).
4. **Write on the envelope:** *"APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF … (REGULAR/CONTRACTUAL)"*.
5. **Post or hand-deliver to:** The Principal, Sainik School Goalpara, PO Rajapara, District Goalpara, Assam – 783133.

Post it at least a week before the deadline — late arrivals are rejected outright, and postal delays are not excused. Keep a photocopy of everything you send.

## How candidates will be selected

The process, per the announcement, may include a **written examination, a skill test, a classroom demonstration and an interview**, depending on the post. Only candidates meeting the post-specific eligibility (age: 18–50 years as on 12 November 2026; 21–50 for PGT/TGT posts, per the notification) will be called for the written test. Shortlisted candidates are informed through the school's website, so keep checking it after you apply.

## What to watch out for

- **One envelope, one post.** Addressed applications to a single post avoid the classic rejection reason.
- **The draft details matter.** A wrong payee name or wrong branch code is an avoidable rejection.
- **Fake notifications exist.** If anyone asks for money beyond the ₹300/₹200 draft — via phone or WhatsApp — it is not the school. Deal only with the official notification.
