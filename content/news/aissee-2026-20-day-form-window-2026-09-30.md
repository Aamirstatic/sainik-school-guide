## What happened

Tomorrow is 1 October. For AISSEE families, October is the month the admission cycle traditionally opens — and last year, NTA did not waste any time. On **Friday, 10 October 2025**, NTA released the AISSEE 2026 notification and information bulletin on the official portal. The application window then ran a mere **20 days**: it closed on **30 October 2025**, with the fee-payment window staying open one extra day, till **31 October 2025**. NTA then opened a short correction window from **2 to 4 November 2025** for candidates who had made mistakes in their forms.

The fees that cycle: **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence-ward candidates, and **₹700** for SC and ST candidates. The exam itself was held in January, and NTA added three new Sainik schools to the counselling list mid-cycle — Srri SPK Public Senior Secondary School (Namakkal, Tamil Nadu), Vadem Nagar Higher Secondary School (Vasco, Goa), and Yogeshwari Sainik School, Ambajogai (Maharashtra) — showing that the school list can grow even after the bulletin is out.

## Why it matters for AISSEE 2027

Twenty days is a short window for a form that demands a birth certificate, domicile proof, category certificate, a photograph in the right size, and school preferences. Every year, families discover on the last weekend that a certificate is misspelled or a scan is the wrong file size — and the correction window only covers limited fields, not everything.

The pattern from the last three cycles, documented by edexlive, shows the notification landing several weeks before the January exam, consistently pointing at **October 2026** for the 2027 bulletin. The exam date for 2027 is already marked in NTA's official examination calendar: **31 January 2027** (tentative, subject to change). So the working assumption is simple — the form window will open within days or weeks, and it will be brief.

## What to do now

- **Gather documents this week**, not when the portal opens: photograph (JPEG, right size), signature, birth certificate, domicile and category certificates.
- **Match names and dates of birth** across every document against the birth certificate. Mismatches are the classic last-minute discovery.
- **Keep ₹850 / ₹700 handy** (expected 2027 fee based on 2026 rates; re-confirm in the bulletin) for online payment.
- **Bookmark the official portal** — [exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/) (NTA migrated it from the old aissee.nta.nic.in domain in October 2026) — and check it directly. Ignore "form is out" claims from unofficial sites until the bulletin is on the NTA site.
