## Four days left — and this is the last weekend

Today is Saturday, 28 November. The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December at 11:50 PM**. That leaves four days — and this weekend is the last stretch of unhurried time you will get.

Most families don't leave the form to the last day because they are lazy. They leave it because one small thing is pending — a certificate that hasn't arrived, a photo that keeps getting rejected, a school-choice list nobody can agree on. And then Wednesday arrives, the portal slows down under the last-day rush, the payment gateway times out, and a form that should have taken twenty minutes becomes a panic.

So use this weekend. Not Monday. Not Tuesday night. This weekend.

## The Saturday checklist — finish the form today if you can

**1. The documents, in one folder.** Birth certificate, domicile certificate, caste certificate if you are claiming reservation, your child's Aadhaar, and the study certificate from the current school. If any certificate has a spelling mismatch with the school records, sort it out now — the December correction window fixes form errors, not certificate errors.

**2. The photo and signature.** This is the single most common reason forms get flagged. Read our [photo and signature requirements guide](/blog/aissee-application-form-photo-signature-requirements/) before you upload — a recent passport photo on a light background, in the size NTA asks for. A photo taken on a phone against a white wall usually works; a cropped family photo usually doesn't.

**3. The school choices, in order.** Put the school you genuinely want first, not the one you think is "safe" — the allotment follows your order. If you haven't decided, read our [guide to ranking your school choices](/blog/aissee-2027-form-which-sainik-schools-to-list-rank-order/) first.

**4. The fee, paid and confirmed.** Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards; Rs 700 for SC/ST. Pay by card, net banking or UPI — and **wait for the confirmation page and save it**. A payment that shows as "pending" is not a submitted form. Download the confirmation page and keep the application number somewhere safe.

**5. One careful read-through.** Before the final submit, read every field aloud. Name spelling, date of birth, category, domicile state, exam city preference. These are the fields that come back to haunt families in December — and some of them cannot be changed even then.

## If you miss Wednesday, you are not finished — but it will cost you

The safety net is real, and it is worth knowing about so you don't panic. If 2 December passes without a submitted form, NTA opens a **late window from 3 to 6 December**, with an extra **Rs 500 late fee** on top of the regular fee. The last date for the late fee payment is 6 December at 11:50 PM.

After that, a **correction window runs from 7 to 9 December** for permitted changes to forms already submitted. But note the limit: the correction window fixes mistakes in a submitted form. It does not let you submit a form you never filled. Treat it as a safety net for errors, not as a second deadline.

## What comes after the form

Once the form is in, the wait is short by entrance-exam standards. The advance city intimation slip is expected around 11 January 2027, the admit card around 25 January, and the exam itself is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027** — Class 6 from 2:00 to 4:30 PM, Class 9 from 2:00 to 5:00 PM, pen-and-paper OMR, no negative marking, with 25% qualifying marks in each section and 40% in aggregate.

That gives your child 64 days from today to the exam. The form is the easy part. But it is also the part that decides whether the other 64 days count for anything at all.

Submit it this weekend. Then the preparation can begin in earnest.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**
Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM. Both the form and the fee payment close at the same time, on the NTA portal exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society.

**Can I still apply after 2 December 2026?**
Yes, but only in the late window from 3 to 6 December 2026, and only by paying an extra late fee of Rs 500 on top of the regular application fee. The late window closes on 6 December at 11:50 PM.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**
Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence-ward candidates, and Rs 700 for SC/ST candidates. The fee is paid online by credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI.

**Can I correct mistakes in my AISSEE 2027 form?**
Yes. NTA will open a correction window from 7 to 9 December 2026 for permitted changes to submitted forms. But the correction window is for fixing mistakes, not for filling a form you never submitted — the form itself must be in by 6 December at the latest.
