## The meet opened on Thursday, with a general and two Olympians in attendance

The 63rd annual athletic meet of Sainik School Kapurthala commenced on Thursday, 19 November 2026. The chief guest was **Major General P K Singh, SM, Chief of Staff at Headquarters 11 Corps and Chairman of the Local Board of Administration**. His wife, Shivani Singh, accompanied him. The special guests of honour were **Olympians Deepika and Reena Khokhar** — two athletes who have worn India colours, standing in front of boys who dream of wearing a uniform one day.

Congratulating the principal, **Group Captain I Manoj Menon**, and the school fraternity, General Singh praised their sustained efforts in maintaining the standards and traditions of the institution. His point was simple and worth repeating to any parent: institutions are built not merely through infrastructure and facilities, but through commitment, dedication, resilience and collective effort.

## "An athletic field is where character is tested"

The heart of the chief guest's address was about why a school like this treats sport as seriously as it treats the classroom. Physical endurance, mental robustness and the ability to perform under pressure, he said, are essential qualities for a soldier and an officer. An athletic field, in his words, is much more than a place where races are won and medals are awarded — it is where character is tested, leadership is developed and camaraderie is strengthened.

He encouraged the cadets to compete with determination, respect their opponents, accept setbacks gracefully and keep striving to improve. Excellence, he reminded them, is not achieved overnight — it is the outcome of consistent effort, hard work, perseverance and self-discipline. And he asked them to dream big, whether in the Armed Forces, in international sport, or in any other profession — while remaining humble, upholding integrity and respecting others. The true worth of a person, he said, is reflected not merely in achievements but in character and values.

It is a speech worth reading twice if your child is preparing for AISSEE 2027. The exam tests maths, language, intelligence and general knowledge. The school tests everything else — and it starts on the field.

## Why this matters to parents weighing a Sainik School

Look, nobody picks a school because of one athletic meet. But a meet like this tells you three honest things about what your child is signing up for.

**First, physical training is not an extra here — it is the timetable.** Sainik Schools run on early mornings, PT, games and drill alongside classes. A child who hates every minute of running will find the first term hard, whatever their maths score. A child who loves it will find the school fits them like a glove.

**Second, the schools measure character in public.** Cadets compete in front of their housemates, their teachers and visiting officers. Winning graciously and losing gracefully are both on the syllabus, even if no marks are attached. That is the part of Sainik School life no guidebook can teach — and it is exactly what the chief guest was describing.

**Third, the alumni path is real.** Kapurthala is one of the 33 established Sainik Schools, and its former cadets have gone on to the NDA, the IMA and international sport — the same week, The Tribune reported a Kapurthala alumnus winning silver at the IMA after a gold at the NDA. The meet's guests were not there by accident. They are the picture of where this road leads.
