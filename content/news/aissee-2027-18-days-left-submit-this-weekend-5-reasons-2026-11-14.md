Eighteen days sounds like plenty. It isn't — not when the form asks for a scanned photo in the right size, a signature in the right format, a category certificate, three exam-city choices and a fee payment that has to succeed. The form closes at **11:50 PM on Wednesday, 2 December 2026**, and this weekend — 14 and 15 November — is the calmest window you will get before the rush.

## 1. A failed payment needs a buffer

Your application is not complete until the fee goes through — Rs. 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and defence wards, Rs. 700 for SC/ST. UPI and net-banking payments do fail, especially late at night when thousands of parents are paying at once. If a payment fails on 2 December night, you may have no working hours left to retry it. Submit this weekend and a failed payment is a 10-minute annoyance, not a lost year.

## 2. The late window costs Rs. 500 — and it is short

Miss 2 December and NTA does allow late applications from **3 to 6 December**, but with an extra Rs. 500 late fee, and the fee itself must be paid by 11:50 PM on 6 December. That is a real option, not a disaster — but it is a needless Rs. 500 for a four-day window. Our earlier explainer on [whether the late fee is worth paying](/news/aissee-2027-late-fee-window-dec-3-6-worth-paying-2026-11-08/) walks through the trade-off.

## 3. The correction window is not a redo button

NTA opens a correction window from **7 to 9 December 2026** for corrections in particulars. Three days, and it covers particulars — it is not a second chance to rebuild the whole form. If your child's photo, signature or school list is wrong, you want to catch it yourself this weekend, calmly, not in a three-day scramble in December.

## 4. Photo and signature rejections are the silent killer

The most common reason forms get stuck is not eligibility — it is the photo or signature not matching NTA's size and background rules. Our guide to [AISSEE 2027 photo and signature specs](/blog/aissee-2027-photo-signature-specs-size-background-rejections/) covers the exact requirements. Check yours against it today, while you still have a weekend to retake a photo in daylight.

## 5. The last days are always the busiest

🔶 **EXPECTED:** in every recent NTA cycle, the final 48 hours see the heaviest traffic, the slowest pages and the most timeouts. NTA has not announced anything about server capacity for this cycle, so treat this as a pattern, not a guarantee. A form submitted on a quiet Saturday morning goes through in minutes; the same form at 10 PM on 2 December is a coin toss.

## What to finish this weekend

- Fill the form on the official portal only: **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**. Only one application per candidate is allowed.
- Keep your school preference list ready before you start — our guide on [which Sainik Schools to list, in what order](/blog/aissee-2027-form-which-sainik-schools-to-list-rank-order/) helps you rank them.
- Pay the fee, save the confirmation page and the payment receipt, and note your application number somewhere safe.
- Then turn back to preparation. The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027** — Class 6 from 2:00 to 4:30 PM, Class 9 from 2:00 to 5:00 PM — and the admit card comes on **25 January 2027**, with the exam city announced on **11 January 2027**. Our [city slip and admit card explainer](/news/aissee-2027-exam-city-slip-jan-11-admit-card-jan-25-what-to-check-2026-11-13/) covers what to check when those arrive.

Last year, a parent called us in a panic because the portal kept logging him out on the last evening. He got through — barely, at 11:40 PM. Don't be that parent. The form takes an hour on a calm Saturday. The same hour on 2 December can feel like a whole year.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. Both the application and the fee payment must be completed by then. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December 2026 with an extra fee of Rs. 500.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs. 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and Rs. 700 for SC and ST candidates. The late fee for applications submitted from 3 to 6 December 2026 is Rs. 500 extra.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**

Sunday, 31 January 2027. Class 6 runs from 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM (150 minutes) and Class 9 from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM (180 minutes), in offline pen-and-paper OMR mode with no negative marking.

**When can I correct mistakes in my AISSEE 2027 form?**

The correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026. It covers corrections in particulars — it is not a second chance to redo the whole form, so check every field before you submit.
