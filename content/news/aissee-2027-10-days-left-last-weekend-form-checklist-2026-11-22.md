## Ten days. One calm Sunday. Use it.

The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM** — ten days from today. That is not a lot of time, but it is enough, if you do it on the right day. And the right day is today, this Sunday evening, when the house is quiet and nobody is rushing.

Here is the honest reason to move now rather than later. The last weekend before the deadline — 28 and 29 November — is not free for many families: **Saturday, 28 November is the JNVST Class 6 exam**, and plenty of homes have one child sitting that and another applying for AISSEE. And 2 December itself is a Wednesday, a working day, when the portal is always at its slowest and the helpline at its busiest. A form filled calmly tonight is a form filled correctly. A form filled at 11 PM on the last day is where the spelling mistakes happen.

## The weekend checklist — do it in this order

| Step | What to do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Check the date of birth** | Match it digit by digit against the birth certificate: Class 6, born **1 April 2015–31 March 2017**; Class 9, born **1 April 2012–31 March 2014** | A wrong DOB is the commonest reason a form gets rejected — and it cannot be fixed later |
| **2. Keep the documents ready** | Recent passport photo and signature in the format the portal asks for, plus the domicile and category certificates if you are claiming a quota | A rejected photo or a missing certificate stalls the whole form |
| **3. Fill the form** | At **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**, with a working email ID and mobile number | This is the only official portal — do not pay anyone who offers to fill it for you |
| **4. Pay the fee** | **Rs 850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards; **Rs 700** for SC/ST, by card, net banking or UPI, before 2 December, 11:50 PM | An unpaid form is an unsubmitted form |
| **5. Save the proof** | Download the confirmation page, save the fee receipt and note the application number | You will need the application number for the admit card in January |

## What the safety nets do — and do not — cover

Miss 2 December and there is still a way in: the **late window runs from 3 to 6 December** with an additional **Rs 500** late fee, and the fee must be paid by 6 December, 11:50 PM. That is new this year — last year there was no late window at all. But treat it as an emergency exit, not a plan. It costs ₹500 for the same form, and after 6 December there is no way to apply for this cycle at all.

Then comes the **correction window, 7 to 9 December** — and here is the part parents keep getting wrong. It is **one-time-only**, and in the 2026 cycle the two fields that mattered most — the candidate's **name and date of birth** — could not be changed in it. The 2027 bulletin keeps the one-time rule. So check the name spelling against the school record and the date of birth against the birth certificate *before* you press submit, not in December.

After that, the calendar runs on its own: the **exam city is announced on 11 January 2027**, **admit cards come on 25 January**, and the exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027** — Class 6 from **2:00 to 4:30 PM**, Class 9 from **2:00 to 5:00 PM**. Class 6 is a 150-minute, 125-question, 300-mark paper in 13 languages; Class 9 is 180 minutes, 150 questions, 400 marks, in English only. There is **no negative marking**, and the qualifying rule is **25% in every section and 40% in aggregate**.

One last thing. If you are stuck, NTA's helpline is **1800-180-4747** and the email is **aissee@nta.ac.in**. Use them. Do not pay an agent, a coaching centre or a WhatsApp contact who promises a seat — admission is only through this exam, and the form costs ₹850, not a lakh.

## FAQs

**When does the AISSEE 2027 form close?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM, for both Class 6 and Class 9. The fee must also be paid by the same time. Apply at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society.

**What happens if I miss the 2 December deadline for AISSEE 2027?**

You can still apply from 3 to 6 December 2026 by paying an additional late fee of Rs 500 over the regular fee. After 6 December at 11:50 PM, the window closes — no second extension has been announced.

**Can I correct my AISSEE 2027 form after submitting it?**

Yes, once, in the correction window from 7 to 9 December 2026. The window is one-time-only, and in the 2026 cycle the candidate's name and date of birth could not be changed — check those two fields digit by digit before you submit, not after.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam and what time does it start?**

Sunday, 31 January 2027. Class 6 runs from 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM and Class 9 from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM. The exam city is announced on 11 January 2027 and admit cards come on 25 January 2027.
