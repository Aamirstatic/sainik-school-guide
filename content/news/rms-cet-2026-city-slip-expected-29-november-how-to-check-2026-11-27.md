## The slip lands this weekend — probably

Here is the thing parents forget in the last fortnight before a defence-school exam: the exam is not the next deadline. The city slip is.

For RMS CET 2026, the National Testing Agency issues the **exam city intimation slip in the last week of November** — and the NTA's own FAQ puts that date at **tentatively 29 November 2026, this Sunday**. The word "tentatively" matters. NTA has not announced a fixed day for the slip, so it could appear on Saturday, on Sunday, or a day or two later. If it has not appeared by Monday 30 November, that is not, by itself, a problem. It is a reason to keep checking the portal, not a reason to panic.

What you should do this weekend is simple: bookmark **exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet** today, and check it once on Saturday morning and once on Sunday evening. That is the whole job. The slip will not arrive by email, and it will not arrive by post. It appears on the portal — and only on the portal.

## What the slip tells you — and what it does not

The city slip answers exactly one question: **which city your child will sit the exam in**. That is all it is for. It exists so a family in, say, Jaipur can work out whether the exam is in Jaipur or in Ajmer, and plan travel and a night's stay if needed.

It does **not** tell you the centre. The exact school or centre name and address come only on the **admit card, expected on 8 December 2026** — five days before the exam. Do not book a hotel against the slip alone, and do not treat the slip as an admit card. You cannot carry it into the exam hall.

To download the slip you need two things: the **application number** and your child's **date of birth** — the same credentials the form used. Keep them on a piece of paper next to the computer, the way you would for any NTA download. And the warning that applies to every NTA document this season: if a WhatsApp forward, an SMS or a phone call offers you a download link, it is not from NTA.

| 13 December 2026 (Sunday) | RMS CET written exam, 2 hours 30 minutes | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| January 2027 | Stage-I result | ✅ Published schedule |
| February–March 2027 | Medical exam and interview | ✅ Published schedule |
| March 2027 | Final merit list; joining 1 April 2027 | ✅ Published schedule |

The written exam is a pen-and-paper OMR paper. Class 6 is 200 multiple-choice questions — English (50, qualifying only), Intelligence (50), Mathematics (50) and General Knowledge (50) — plus a 20-mark interview for those who clear the written stage. Class 9 is two papers — English, Hindi and Social Science, then Mathematics and Science — plus a 50-mark interview. There is no negative marking. To qualify, Class 6 needs 35% in English and 40% in each of the other three sections; Class 9 needs 50% in each paper.

The five schools in play are **Chail (Himachal Pradesh), Ajmer and Dholpur (Rajasthan), and Belgaum and Bengaluru (Karnataka)**. Seats are vacancy-based, so there is no fixed seat count to quote — the merit list, not a number, decides who gets in. Reservation runs 70% for wards of JCOs and other ranks, 20% for wards of officers and 10% for civilians, with 10% of Class 6 seats for girls.

## FAQs

**When will the RMS CET 2026 city slip be released?**

It is expected around Sunday, 29 November 2026, according to the NTA's own RMS CET FAQ. That date is tentative — NTA has not published a separate release notice — so check the portal exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet from the weekend onward rather than waiting for a message.

**What does the RMS CET city slip tell me?**

Only the city where your child will sit the exam. The exact centre name and address come later, on the admit card, which is expected on 8 December 2026. The slip exists so families can plan travel; it is not the admit card and cannot be carried into the exam hall.

**Where do I download the RMS CET 2026 city slip?**

Only from the official NTA RMS CET portal, exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet, using the application number and date of birth. NTA does not email the slip or send it by post, and no WhatsApp forward offering a download link is genuine.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**

Sunday, 13 December 2026 — a pen-and-paper OMR test of 2 hours 30 minutes for Class 6 and Class 9 admission to the five Rashtriya Military Schools (Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum and Bengaluru). The result is scheduled for January 2027, followed by medical exams and interviews in February to March 2027.
