> **Last verified: 2 October 2026.** Readiness update, not a notification alert — the AISSEE 2027 bulletin is **not released yet** (CONFIRMED: the NTA portal was still showing 2026 items on 1 October 2026). Everything dated for 2027 below is EXPECTED; 2026-cycle facts are CONFIRMED from cited sources.

October is here, and with it — if recent cycles are any guide — the AISSEE 2027 application form. Buddy4Study (updated two days ago), Collegedunia and AglaSem independently converge on the same read: **NTA is expected to open AISSEE 2027 registration in October 2026**. Last year the form opened on **10 October 2025** and the deadline was extended to **9 November 2025** (CONFIRMED — Buddy4Study) — a window of roughly three to four weeks.

![Parent reviewing an admission checklist before filling the form](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-form-expected-october-readiness-checklist-2026-10-02.webp)

The families who sail through aren't the fastest fillers — they're the ones ready before the link goes live.

## 1. Settle the class and the age math

First decision on the form: Class 6 or Class 9. The rule is strict — **10–12 years as on 31 March for Class 6; 13–15 for Class 9** (EXPECTED; the 2027 bulletin confirms the cut-off). Check your child's age against it *today*. Age mismatch is the most common heartbreak at the form stage, and it's not fixable later.

## 2. Get the scanned files exactly right

Keep ready: **photograph (10–200 KB, JPG), signature (10–50 KB, JPG), left-hand thumb impression (10–50 KB)**. Wrong-size uploads are the biggest time-waster on form day. Our [photo and signature guide](/blog/aissee-application-form-photo-signature-requirements) covers the exact specs and the rejections to avoid.

## 3. Chase the slow certificates now

The form asks for **date-of-birth and domicile certificates**, plus a **caste certificate** (SC/ST/OBC-NCL claims) or **serving certificate/PPO** (defence-ward claims). These come from government authorities and can take a week to a month — you can't start this on form-opening day. If anything's missing, apply this week. See our [domicile certificate guide](/blog/domicile-certificate-sainik-school-guide).

## 4. Keep the fee payment ready

**₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence-ward candidates; ₹700 for SC and ST** — EXPECTED to continue (CONFIRMED 2026 rates — Times of India citing the NTA bulletin; Collegedunia). Online only (card, net banking, UPI), non-refundable. Make sure a working debit card or UPI handle is ready and daily limits won't block the payment. Details and traps in our [fee explainer](/news/aissee-2027-application-fee-explained-2026-10-02).

## 5. Draft school preferences early

The form asks for school and exam-city choices, and those locked preferences feed directly into counselling later. Read our [choice-filling mistakes guide](/blog/aissac-choice-filling-mistakes-cost-seats) before ranking schools, and shortlist exam cities with our [exam-city guide](/news/aissee-2027-exam-city-choices-guide-2026-10-01).

## 6. Know the correction window's limits

NTA opens a short **correction window** (a few days, usually November) after the form closes — but only for limited fields. Name, parents' names, date of birth and category typically *cannot* be changed. Our [correction-window guide](/blog/aissee-correction-window-what-you-can-cannot-edit) lists what's editable. Treat the first submission as final.

## 7. Bookmark the only official page

The form will appear at **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** — nowhere else. Every year, lookalike sites and "agents" charge parents for a free form. There is no offline application and no authorised agent.

## What happens next

When the notification drops, we'll publish a same-day breakdown of the confirmed dates, fee and eligibility. Until then, use this October window: documents, scans, certificates, preferences. **EXPECTED timeline:** form October 2026 → correction window November → admit card January 2027 → **exam 31 January 2027** (per the NTA exam calendar — tentative) → results February–March.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 application form been released?**
No. As of 2 October 2026, NTA hasn't released the notification. Three independent portals expect the form in October 2026 — last year it opened on 10 October 2025.

**What is the expected last date for the form?**
Not announced. The 2026 window ran 10 October–9 November 2025 (after extension), so expect roughly three to four weeks.

**What should I keep ready before the form opens?**
Scanned photo, signature and thumb impression to spec; date-of-birth and domicile certificates; caste or defence certificates if claiming reservation. Start the certificate paperwork now — it can take weeks.

**What will the application fee be?**
Expected to stay at ₹850 (General/OBC-NCL/defence) and ₹700 (SC/ST) — the confirmed 2026 rates. Online payment only, non-refundable.
