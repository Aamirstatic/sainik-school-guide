## Five days. The syllabus is closed — now it's about the week itself

The Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test for Class 6 is on **Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**. Five days from today. One phase, every Navodaya Vidyalaya in the country, summer-bound and winter-bound alike.

At five days out, the exam is no longer won by studying harder. It is won by sleeping properly and removing every avoidable surprise from Saturday morning. A calm child who knows the route, the rules and the paper starts ahead — no last-minute chapter can give that.

## The facts, in one place

**The paper:** 80 objective questions, 100 marks, two hours. No negative marking — 1.25 marks per correct answer. Mental Ability and EVS carry 40 questions (50 marks); Arithmetic 20 (25 marks); Language 20 (25 marks). Qualifying needs at least **14 in Mental Ability and EVS, 7 in Arithmetic and 7 in Language** — no section can be left blank.

**The admit card:** released on **28 October 2026** on the NVS admission portal, linked from navodaya.gov.in. Download it with the registration number and the child's date of birth — it is free, and none is sent by post or to the school. If you still haven't printed it, do it today: two printouts on A4 paper, one in the bag, one at home.

**Check three things on the card:** the child's name, photo and date of birth — the invigilator will match the photo with the child. The exam centre name and address — **the centre cannot be changed**. And the medium/language of the test — the question booklet must be in the language you chose on the form. If anything is wrong, contact your district JNV's principal right away — not on Friday.

## The five-day plan

| Day | Do this | Stop doing this |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon 23 Nov** | One timed mock or previous paper, 11:30 AM–1:30 PM — the real exam slot. Review only the mistakes. | No new chapters, no new books, no new teacher. |
| **Tue 24 Nov** | Revise Mental Ability and EVS — the 50-mark section. Short sets of 20 questions. | No comparing your child with anyone else's. |
| **Wed 25 Nov** | Arithmetic formulas and Language practice. Visit the exam centre — check the route, the gate, the parking. | No studying after 8 PM from tonight. |
| **Thu 26 Nov** | Light revision: the error notebook, the formulas, one easy set. Pack the bag — admit card, ID, two pens. | No full mock today — a tired child is worse than an unrevised one. |
| **Fri 27 Nov** | A 30-minute glance at the error notebook, then stop. Early dinner, normal bedtime, two alarms set. | No paper discussion, no "last tips" lecture, no quizzing relatives. |

## Exam day, hour by hour

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| Before 11:00 AM | Reach the centre; get your child seated. Latecomers are not allowed in. |
| 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM | The test runs two hours, no break. Divyang candidates get 40 extra minutes. |
| After 1:30 PM | Collect your child and go home. Do not dissect the paper question by question. |

**What to carry:** the printed admit card, the child's Aadhaar card or the parent's residence certificate uploaded with the form, and a blue or black ballpoint pen. Pencils are not allowed on the OMR sheet, and a marked answer cannot be changed — decide before bubbling, not after. And because there is no negative marking, every question deserves an answer: a guess in the last two minutes costs nothing and can add 1.25 marks.

The result is 🔶 **EXPECTED** by the end of March 2027 on the same portal — NVS has not announced a date. For now, the only date that matters is Saturday, 28 November, 11:30 AM. And if your child is also applying for AISSEE 2027 — form closes 2 December, exam 31 January — treat Saturday as a rehearsal for January.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam?**

Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, in a single phase for both summer-bound and winter-bound Navodaya Vidyalayas.

**What time must my child reach the exam centre?**

Children must be seated in their exam room by 11:00 AM. Latecomers will not be allowed in, so reach the centre well before that — plan for traffic and a wrong turn.

**What should my child carry to the JNVST exam?**

A printed copy of the admit card, the child's Aadhaar card or the parent's residence certificate that was uploaded with the form, and a blue or black ballpoint pen. Pencils are not allowed on the OMR sheet, and answers cannot be changed or erased once marked.

**Can my child still download the JNVST 2027 admit card?**

Yes. Admit cards were released on 28 October 2026 on the NVS admission portal, linked from navodaya.gov.in. Download with the registration number and the child's date of birth, and print two copies on A4 paper. No admit card is sent by post.
