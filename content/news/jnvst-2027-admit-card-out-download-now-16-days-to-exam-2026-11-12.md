## If you have not downloaded it yet, tonight is the night

The JNVST 2027 Class 6 admit card has been sitting on the NVS portal since **28 October 2026** — two weeks now. And yet, every year, a handful of parents discover on the morning of the exam that they never actually downloaded it, or that the printout at home has the wrong photograph, or the wrong centre.

Do not be that family. The exam is on **Saturday, 28 November 2026 — 16 days from today** — from **11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**. Download the card tonight, print two copies, and check every line on it while there is still time to fix a mistake.

## How to download the admit card

1. Go to **navodaya.gov.in** and click the **JNVST 2027 Class VI admit card** link. It opens the same admission portal you used to apply.
2. Enter the **registration number** you received by SMS when the form was submitted, and the **child's date of birth**.
3. Solve the captcha and log in.
4. Download the admit card PDF and take **two clear printouts on A4 paper**.

The download is **free** — NVS does not charge for it, and nobody needs to pay an agent or a cyber-cafe "service charge" beyond the printing cost. If you have forgotten the registration number, search your phone's SMS and email for the message sent when the form was submitted. The portal also has a "find your registration number" option, and the Principal of your district's JNV can help — the district JNV runs the selection test locally.

## Check these 7 things on the card — tonight, not on 28 November

- **Child's name, father's and mother's names, and date of birth** — exactly as in the application.
- **Photograph and signature** — the invigilator matches the photo with the child at the centre.
- **Category, rural or urban status, and Divyang status** — as declared in the form.
- **Medium or language of the test** — the question booklet comes only in the language chosen in the form. It cannot be changed later.
- **Exam centre name and address, roll number, exam date and time.**

Found a mistake? **Contact the Principal of your district's JNV immediately**, with the child's documents. Do not wait until exam day — the centre and the language cannot be changed at the gate, and a wrong centre on the card means the child sits the exam at the centre printed on it, nowhere else.

## The exam-day rules, in one place

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| Exam date and time | Saturday, 28 November 2026, 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM (2 hours, no break) |
| Reach the centre | By 10:15–10:30 AM |
| Be seated by | 11:00 AM — latecomers are not allowed in |
| Carry | Admit card printout + child's Aadhaar card, or the parent's residence certificate uploaded in the application |
| Pens | Two or three blue or black ballpoint pens — pencils are strictly prohibited on the OMR sheet |
| Leave at home | Phones, calculators, smart watches |
| Leaving the hall | Children cannot leave until 1:30 PM; a bell rings every 30 minutes |
| Divyang candidates | 40 minutes extra time |

One detail worth repeating to your child: on the OMR sheet, darken **one circle per question fully**. Erasing, striking out or using whitener means that answer is not evaluated.

## The paper your child will face

This year the Class 6 paper has a new section — **Environmental Studies (EVS)** — sitting alongside Mental Ability. The pattern, as per the NVS prospectus:

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mental Ability + EVS | 20 + 20 | 25 + 25 | 60 min |
| Arithmetic | 20 | 25 | 30 min |
| Language | 20 | 25 | 30 min |
| **Total** | **80** | **100** | **2 hours** |

Each correct answer carries **1.25 marks** and there is **no negative marking** — so your child should attempt every single question. The prospectus also sets section-wise minimum qualifying marks (14 in Mental Ability + EVS, 7 each in Arithmetic and Language), so a child cannot clear the paper on one strong section alone.

## What to do with the 16 days left

The admit card is the admin. The preparation is the rest. In the final stretch, three full 2-hour mock papers on an OMR sheet — timed from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, the real slot — are worth more than a dozen untimed worksheets. Visit the exam centre once if it is new to you, so the morning of the 28th holds no surprises. And in the final week, revise mistakes only. The result is expected by the **end of March 2027**, after which shortlisted children go through document verification at the JNV.

Tonight's job takes ten minutes: download, print, check. Do it before the exam week swallows your evenings.

## FAQs

**Is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 admit card out?**

Yes. Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti released the JNVST 2027 Class 6 admit card on 28 October 2026 on the NVS admission portal linked from navodaya.gov.in. It is free to download using the registration number and the child's date of birth.

**How do I download the JNVST 2027 admit card?**

Go to navodaya.gov.in, open the JNVST 2027 Class VI admit card link, enter the registration number received by SMS at the time of applying and the child's date of birth, solve the captcha, then download the PDF and take two clear printouts on A4 paper.

**What should I check on the admit card before exam day?**

Check the child's name, parents' names, date of birth, photograph, category, rural or urban status, the medium or language of the test, the exam centre name and address, roll number and the exam date and time. If anything is wrong, contact the Principal of your district's JNV immediately with the child's documents — do not wait until exam day.

**What must my child carry to the JNVST exam centre?**

The admit card printout and the child's Aadhaar card, or the parent's government residence certificate that was uploaded in the application. Carry two or three blue or black ballpoint pens — pencils are not allowed on the OMR sheet. Reach the centre by 10:15 to 10:30 AM; the child must be seated by 11:00 AM.
