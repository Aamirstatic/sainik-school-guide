Three days. That's all that stands between your child's UP Sainik School application and a ₹2,000 bill instead of ₹1,000.

The regular window closes Sunday, 25 October 2026, at 5 PM. From 26 to 31 October, applications cost ₹2,000 in total. After 31 October at 5 PM, the window shuts completely — the correction window (3–5 November) is your only chance to fix details.

## The key dates, one more time

| Event | Date | Fee / note |
|---|---|---|
| Regular deadline | 25 Oct 2026, 5 PM | ₹1,000 |
| Late-fee window | 26–31 Oct 2026, 5 PM | ₹2,000 in total |
| Correction window | 3–5 Nov 2026, 5 PM | One-time; edit only editable fields |
| Tentative exam date | 20 Dec 2026 | Sunday; 200 marks, 2 h 30 min (prospectus) |

## Why you should apply this weekend, not on Sunday

Here's the honest bit nobody tells you. Sunday is the worst day to apply. Admission portals slow down on deadline day — and this one falls on a weekend, when cyber-cafe support is thin and payment gateways occasionally hiccup. **Apply by Saturday, 24 October.** If an OTP delays or a payment sticks, you have Sunday to recover instead of watching the 5 PM cutoff tick by.

## Your 48-hour action plan

1. **Eligibility check (15 minutes).** Only UP domicile children are eligible — both the applicant *and* the father must be UP domiciles, with an affidavit of permanent residence at admission. Age windows are strict: Class 6, born 2 July 2015 to 1 January 2018; Class 9, born 2 July 2012 to 1 January 2015. The child must have passed Class 5 or Class 8 by 20 May 2027 — current Class 5/8 students can apply.
2. **Documents ready (30 minutes).** Domicile certificate, birth certificate, photograph, signature, and category certificate if applicable. Bad uploads are the most common reason forms get flagged — our [form rejection checks](/blog/up-sainik-school-form-rejection-checks-2026-10-10/) list exactly what trips people up.
3. **One mobile, one registration.** The portal allows only one registration per mobile number — you cannot create two and pick the better one ([explainer](/blog/up-sainik-school-one-mobile-one-registration-2026-10-16/)).
4. **Apply on upsainikschool.org.** Register via the "Open Application" section, fill the form, upload documents, pay ₹1,000. Save the confirmation page — you'll need the application number for the admit card and the correction window.
5. **Mark the calendar.** The correction window (3–5 November) only fixes editable fields, so correctness at submission matters. The tentative exam is 20 December — the full post-application roadmap is in our [timeline guide](/blog/up-sainik-school-full-timeline-after-apply-2026-10-18/).

The UP Sainik Schools — Lucknow and Gorakhpur — run their own entrance exam, separate from the national AISSEE.

Three days, then the price doubles. Don't pay for Sunday.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for the UP Sainik School entrance exam 2027-28?**

Sunday, 25 October 2026, 5 PM — applications close on the official portal upsainikschool.org. Applying from 26 to 31 October is possible only with the late fee, making the total ₹2,000.

**What is the application fee with the late fee for UP Sainik Schools?**

The application fee is ₹1,000 until 25 October 2026, 5 PM. From 26 to 31 October 2026, the total payable is ₹2,000 including the late fee.

**Who can apply for the UP Sainik Schools entrance exam 2027-28?**

Only Uttar Pradesh domicile children: both the applicant and the father must be UP domiciles. Class 6 applicants must be born between 2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018, and Class 9 applicants between 2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam 2027-28?**

The exam is tentatively scheduled for Sunday, 20 December 2026, for admission to Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow, and Sainik School, Gorakhpur.
