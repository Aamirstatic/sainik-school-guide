## Six races, one cold morning in Peren

Sainik School Punglwa held its annual Inter-House Cross Country Championship on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, and it was the kind of morning these schools are built for — cadets from Classes VI to XI running for their houses, teachers and officers cheering from the side, and medals waiting at the finish line.

The championship, held for the academic session 2026–27, tested the cadets across six age and gender categories. The school's press release, reported by the Morung Express, described it as a test of "endurance, determination and athletic abilities" — and honestly, that is exactly what cross country is. There is no crowd noise to carry you, no shortcut, just your own legs and your own breathing.

## Who won what

In the boys' division, the youngest group went to Cadet Gaurav Kumar of Dzukou House, who won gold in Group A (Class VI). Cadet Rokovito Kuatsu of Shilloi House finished first in Group B (Classes VII, VIII and IX), and Cadet Ampungsangle of Tizu House took the gold in Group C (Classes X and XI).

The girls' division was just as competitive. Cadet Koklila Sangtam of Dhansiri House won Group D (Class VI), Cadet Alumi Kikon of Melak House finished first in Group E (Classes VII and VIII), and Cadet Laishram Daina Devi of Shilloi House won the gold in Group F.

When the points were added up, Patkai House emerged as the champion in the junior category, and Saramati House claimed the championship trophy in the senior category. Two houses, two trophies — and every other house already planning next year's revenge, if school tradition is anything to go by.

Officiating Principal Lt Col Amit Chaudhary attended as the chief guest and felicitated the winners with medals, certificates and trophies. He praised the cadets for their enthusiasm, discipline and exemplary sportsmanship, the release said. Officers, staff members and cadets attended the championship and cheered the runners through the course.

## Why this matters to parents eyeing AISSEE 2027

If your child is preparing for AISSEE 2027 — applications close on **2 December 2026**, and the exam is on **31 January 2027** — a story like this is worth more than a brochure. It shows what the school actually does with a child once they get in.

Cross country is not a one-day event at a Sainik School. It is the visible tip of daily physical training: morning PT, games every evening, inter-house competitions through the year. That is the life your child is applying for, and it is also the life that builds the stamina and discipline the NDA expects later.

A word of honesty, though. Do not read a sports result as a sign that your child must already be an athlete to get in. AISSEE selection is a written exam — Maths, Language, Intelligence and GK — followed by a medical test. What the running does is show you the culture: a child who likes moving, competing and being part of a team will fit in faster.

If Punglwa is on your list of schools, this is also a small, useful signal: the school is active, its houses compete properly, and girls are running in their own categories with their own medals. Punglwa admits girl cadets, and their races get the same billing as the boys'.

## FAQs

**When was the Sainik School Punglwa cross country championship held?**

On Wednesday, 11 November 2026, at the school campus in Punglwa, Peren district, Nagaland. It was the school's annual Inter-House Cross Country Championship for the academic session 2026–27.

**Who won the gold medals at the Punglwa cross country championship?**

In the boys' division, Gaurav Kumar (Group A, Class VI), Rokovito Kuatsu (Group B, Classes VII–IX) and Ampungsangle (Group C, Classes X–XI). In the girls' division, Koklila Sangtam (Group D, Class VI), Alumi Kikon (Group E, Classes VII–VIII) and Laishram Daina Devi (Group F).

**Which houses won the team championships at Punglwa?**

Patkai House won the junior category team championship, and Saramati House claimed the senior category championship trophy.

**Why does cross country matter in a Sainik School?**

Running is part of the daily physical training that prepares cadets for the NDA and the armed forces. Inter-house championships build endurance, house spirit and the habit of competing — the same qualities the AISSEE selection and later SSB interviews look for.
