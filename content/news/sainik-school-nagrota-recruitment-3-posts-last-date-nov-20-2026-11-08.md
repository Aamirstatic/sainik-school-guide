## Three posts, one of them regular — and the clock is ticking

Sainik School Nagrota, Jammu, is hiring three staff under advertisement number EN 31/61: one **TGT (Computer Science)** on a regular post, and two contractual posts — **Art Master** and **Nursing Sister (Female)**. The application must **reach the Principal by Speed Post on or before 20 November 2026**. That is twelve days from today, and the last date counts the day the envelope arrives, not the day you post it.

The regular post is the one to notice. A TGT (Computer Science) on Pay Matrix Level-7 — **Rs 44,900 to Rs 1,42,400** plus DA and allowances, with rent-free accommodation and free messing with cadets — is a proper government-school teaching job, not a short contract. Regular Sainik School teaching posts do not come up every month. If you hold the qualification, this is worth a serious application.

## The three posts at a glance

| Post | Vacancies | Nature | Pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| TGT (Computer Science) | 1 | Regular | Level-7: Rs 44,900–1,42,400 + DA and allowances |
| Art Master | 1 | Contractual | Rs 35,000 per month (excluding vacation/break periods) |
| Nursing Sister (Female) | 1 | Contractual | Rs 25,500 per month (excluding break period) |

All three posts are in the unreserved (UR) category. The school is at Nagrota, NH1A, Jammu, Jammu and Kashmir – 181221, one of the 33 Sainik Schools under the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence.

## Who can apply

**TGT (Computer Science)** — age **21 to 35 years** as on 20 November 2026. You need one of these: a BCA with at least 50% marks; or graduation in Computer Science/IT with at least 50% marks in the subject and in aggregate, with Computer Science studied as a main subject in all years; or a B.E./B.Tech. in Computer Science or IT with at least 50% marks. On top of that, a B.Ed. with at least 50% marks from an NCTE-recognised institution (or a 3-year integrated B.Ed.-M.Ed., or a 4-year integrated degree with a B.Ed. component) and qualification in **CTET Paper-II or STET** are essential.

**Art Master** — age **21 to 50 years** as on 20 November 2026. A Master's degree in Fine Arts with specialisation in Painting is required.

**Nursing Sister (Female)** — age **18 to 50 years** as on 20 November 2026. A B.Sc. Nursing or a nursing diploma from a recognised institute, registration with the Indian/State Nursing Council, and at least **two years' experience** working in a hospital or clinic are required.

Age relaxation applies as per government rules for reserved categories.

## Fee and how to apply

The application fee is **Rs 500, non-refundable, for all candidates**. Pay by account-payee demand draft from any bank, payable at Nagrota and drawn in favour of the **Principal, Sainik School Nagrota**, or deposit the fee in the school's SBI account and attach the payment proof. (One portal notes the notification does not mention a fee — the other three reports of the same advertisement all list Rs 500, so treat Rs 500 as the figure and confirm it in the official notice.)

1. Download the prescribed application form and the official advertisement from **sainikschoolnagrota.org** (Notices section).
2. Fill in the form and attach self-attested copies of your certificates, experience proof and the fee payment proof.
3. Send the complete packet **by Speed Post only** so that it reaches **The Principal, Sainik School Nagrota, Nagrota, Jammu, J&K – 181221** on or before **20 November 2026**. Hand delivery is also accepted at the school.
4. Keep a photocopy of everything you send, and the Speed Post tracking receipt.

Do not wait until the last week. Speed Post from outside Jammu takes days, and a form that arrives on 21 November is a form that was never received. Selection is 🔶 **EXPECTED** around December 2026 to January 2027, based on one portal's report of the notice — the official advertisement will confirm the schedule. For a computer science graduate with a B.Ed. and CTET, the regular TGT post is one of the better teaching openings of the season.
