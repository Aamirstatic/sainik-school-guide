> **Last verified: 11 October 2026 (Sunday morning).** The deadline and editable-fields list below are **CONFIRMED** from NTA's public notice of 7 October 2026, as reported by NDTV and the Free Press Journal. This is a live-window update — the window shuts tonight.

![Rows of desks in an examination hall where RIMCEE candidates will sit on 6 December](/images/inbody/rimcee-correction-window-closes-tonight-2026-10-11.webp)

If you filled the RIMCEE 2026 form and something in it is wrong, today is your last chance to fix it. NTA's one-time application correction window — open since 9 October — closes **tonight, 11 October 2026, at 11:50 pm**. After that, the form is frozen as-is.

## What you can still change (CONFIRMED)

Per NTA's correction guidelines, you get one shot at editing. The fields split into three groups:

**Change any ONE of:** the candidate's name, the father's name, or the mother's name. Pick carefully — only one of these three can be corrected.

**Change freely:** date of birth, gender, category, signature, Aadhaar details (only if you originally registered with a different ID document), Class VII equivalent details, and the exam city.

**Cannot change:** mobile number, email address, present or permanent address, emergency contact details, and photograph. If any of these is wrong, the correction window will not help — which is why the photo and contact details needed to be right at registration.

Two cost notes from the notice: if your correction raises the applicable fee (for example, a category change), you pay the difference now — and it is **not refundable**. Once you submit the corrected form, it is final; review every field before hitting submit.

## How to do it today

1. Go to the official RIMCEE portal — exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE/ — and log in.
2. Open the submitted application and find the correction link.
3. Make every needed change in one pass; don't submit, discover a second error, and reopen.
4. Pay any additional fee if applicable, submit, and **save the updated confirmation page** — it's your proof of the corrected form.

Do it in daylight hours. NTA portals slow down badly in the last few hours before a deadline, and an 11:50 pm cutoff leaves no margin for a stuck payment gateway.

## The exam is 56 days away

The written examination is on **6 December 2026 (Sunday)** — 56 days from today — for admission to Class 8 at Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun. Key facts about this exam, confirmed from the official schedule: it is the first stage (a viva voce follows for written qualifiers), candidates need at least 50% in each subject, and the medium is English or Hindi except the English paper. City slips arrive roughly two weeks before the exam; admit cards about a week before.

Our [RIMCEE correction explainer from two days ago](/news/rimcee-2026-correction-window-live-closes-october-11-2026-10-09/) covered the window's opening — this piece is the closing-day call. If the form is already correct, you're done; close the tab and go study. If it isn't, today is the day.

## FAQs

**When does the RIMCEE 2026 correction window close?**
Tonight — 11 October 2026 at 11:50 pm. The window ran from 9 to 11 October, and NTA has said this is a one-time facility with no further chance to edit.

**What can be corrected in the RIMCEE 2026 form?**
You can change any one of the candidate's name, father's name, or mother's name; plus date of birth, gender, category, signature, Aadhaar details (if you registered with another ID), Class VII details and exam city. You cannot change mobile number, email, address, emergency contacts or photograph.

**Where is the RIMCEE correction facility?**
On the official NTA RIMCEE portal at exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE/ — log in with your application credentials and open the correction link.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 written exam?**
6 December 2026 — 56 days from today. The exam is the first stage for Class 8 admission to RIMC Dehradun; a viva voce follows for written-test qualifiers.
