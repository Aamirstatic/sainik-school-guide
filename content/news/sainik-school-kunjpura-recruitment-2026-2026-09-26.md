## What's happened — Sainik School Kunjpura hiring for 2026

Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal (Haryana) — run by the Sainik Schools Society under the Ministry of Defence — released its 2026 staff recruitment notification on **25 September 2026** (CONFIRMED). There are **15 teaching and non-teaching vacancies** (CONFIRMED), and this round is interesting because it isn't just classroom posts: alongside TGT teachers, the school is hiring a Horse Riding Instructor, a Band Master, a Nursing Sister, and five Ward Boys.

## Post-wise vacancies

All figures above are CONFIRMED from both reporting sources.

## Who can apply — eligibility

- **TGT posts:** Graduate with 50% marks in the relevant subject + B.Ed + CTET/STET passed (CONFIRMED).
- **Horse Riding Instructor:** Matriculation + instructor course (CONFIRMED).
- **Band Master:** Band Master / Band Major / Drum Major course (CONFIRMED).
- **Mess Manager:** Matriculation + 5 years of catering experience (CONFIRMED).
- **Nursing Sister (Female):** Nursing diploma / GNM / B.Sc Nursing + 5 years experience (CONFIRMED).
- **Ward Boys:** Matriculation (CONFIRMED).

**Age limit:** 18–50 years as of **31 October 2026** (CONFIRMED). ⚠️ **One source states 21–35 years for TGT posts, while another states 21–50.** Please check the age-relaxation clause in the official notification PDF on sskunjpura.org before you apply — this discrepancy matters for TGT applicants.

## How to apply — offline only (step by step)

This is an **offline application** (CONFIRMED). There is no online form:

1. Download the prescribed application form from **sskunjpura.org**.
2. Fill it carefully and attach self-attested copies of all documents.
3. Prepare a Demand Draft of **Rs 500**, drawn in favour of **"Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura"**, payable at Karnal (CONFIRMED).
4. Send the complete application to **The Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal (Haryana) – 132023**, so it reaches **on or before 31 October 2026** (CONFIRMED).

## Selection process

Shortlisting of applications → written exam → practical/skill test (where applicable) → interview → document verification (CONFIRMED).

## What to do next

Don't wait until the last week — postal delays have derailed many such applications in the past. Get your Demand Draft made early, keep self-attested photocopies of every certificate ready, and cross-check the official notification PDF for the exact TGT age band before posting your form.
