## It closes today — here is what to do before 5 PM

The NTA window for the Rashtriya Military Schools Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026 — the gateway to Classes 6 and 9 at India's five Rashtriya Military Schools (Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum and Bengaluru) — shuts at **5:00 PM today, 20 October 2026**.

If you have not applied yet, this is genuinely the last day for a regular-fee application. One crucial detail the reports flag: the 5 PM cutoff is for the application form, but the **fee payment link stays open until 11:50 PM tonight**. So even if you finish the form in the last hours, complete the payment the same night — do not sleep on it.

## The last-hour checklist

- **Register first, polish later.** If you are short on time, get the registration and form submitted within the window; the correction window (27–28 October) exists for fixes.
- **Watch the confirmation page, not the bank balance.** NTA is explicit: money deducted from your account does not by itself mean you are registered. Only a generated confirmation page completes the application. If the page does not appear, do not assume it went through — retry the payment flow rather than walking away.
- **Keep the fee chart right:** Rs 1,000 for General/OBC/OBC-NCL; Rs 750 for SC/ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action. Payment gateway charges are extra, and wards of KIA personnel also get a six-month relaxation in the upper age limit.
- **Check the age math once.** Class 6: 10–13 years on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2014 – 31 March 2017). Class 9: 13–16 years on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2011 – 31 March 2014). Class 6 needs Class 5 passed or in progress; Class 9 needs Class 8 from a recognised school before admission.

## Miss the 5 PM cutoff? Tomorrow is not the end

A **late-fee window opens tomorrow, 21 October**, running to **25 October (5 PM; fee by 11:50 PM)** with a flat **Rs 500** late fee over the category fee. That is the final chance for the 2027-28 session — see [our late-fee window piece](/news/rms-cet-2026-late-fee-window-opens-oct-21-2026-10-19/) for the full fee table.

The exam itself is on **13 December 2026** — a pen-and-paper OMR test of 2 hours 30 minutes, with city-intimation slips expected in the last week of November and admit cards 5–7 days before the test.

## FAQs

**What time does the RMS CET 2026 application window close today?**
The regular window closes at 5:00 PM on 20 October 2026. Fee payment is accepted until 11:50 PM the same night.

**What if I miss today's deadline?**
The late-fee window runs 21–25 October 2026 with a flat Rs 500 late fee. That is the final chance for this session.

**Is fee payment alone enough to register?**
No — NTA states clearly that deduction from your bank account does not establish registration. The confirmation page must be generated. Save and print it.

**When are the exam and correction window?**
The correction window is 27–28 October 2026. The written exam is 13 December 2026.
