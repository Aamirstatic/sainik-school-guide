If you applied for a teaching or administrative post at Sainik School Chittorgarh in October, check the school website today — the shortlist is out, and the dates are close.

The school issued its call letter on **13 November 2026** for three posts on a regular basis: **TGT (Mathematics)**, **TGT (Science)** and **Mess Manager**. In total **104 candidates** have been shortlisted, and each group reports at **7:00 AM** on its own day. This is the next step after the advertisement published in Employment News for 17–23 October 2026 and The Times of India on 18 October 2026.

## The schedule — three days, three posts

| Post | Date | Report time | Shortlisted |
|---|---|---|---|
| TGT (Mathematics) | **30 November 2026** | 7:00 AM | 68 candidates (M-2701 to M-2768) |
| TGT (Science) | **2 December 2026** | 7:00 AM | 16 candidates (S-2801 to S-2816) |
| Mess Manager | **3 December 2026** | 7:00 AM | 20 candidates (MM-2901 to MM-2920) |

The venue is Sainik School Chittorgarh, Bhilwara Road, Chittorgarh, Rajasthan — 312021. The school notes the town is well connected by train and road, and about 90 km from Dabok Airport, Udaipur.

## What happens on the day

Everyone sits a **60-minute written test** first. The paper is different for the two streams:

- **TGT (100 marks):** General Knowledge 15, General English 15, Basic Mathematics 10, and a subject test of 60 marks.
- **Mess Manager (50 marks):** General Knowledge 5, General English 10, Basic Mathematics 10, and a subject test of 25 marks.

Qualify in the written test and the day continues immediately — there is no second call. **TGT candidates** go on to a **class demonstration (100 marks)** and an **interview (100 marks)** the same day. For the demonstration, prepare a topic from the Class IX and X syllabus of your subject; the interview tests general knowledge and current affairs (25), subject knowledge (50) and knowledge of CBSE examination bye-laws (25). **Mess Manager candidates** face a **skill and proficiency test (50 marks)** — drafting official letters, mess management and stock records, a statement of case for procurement, practical knowledge of menu planning, ration calculation, kitchen hygiene and food safety — plus an interview.

## The two rules that end candidatures

The notice is unusually blunt, and worth reading twice:

1. **Report late, or without original certificates, and you are disqualified** — "no representation will be entertained." Bring every original certificate and testimonial submitted with your application, including proof of experience and caste, plus attested copies.
2. **The process may spill over to a second day.** Make your own arrangements for lodging and boarding — no TA or DA is paid for the journey.

One more caution from the notice itself: this is only a call letter for the selection process. It is **not a guarantee of selection**.

## If your name is on the rejected list

The same notice publishes rejected candidates — one for TGT Mathematics (overage), two for TGT Science and 30 for Mess Manager, mostly for missing demand drafts, missing documents or no relevant experience. The window for rejected candidates to send an observation by email **closed on 16 November 2026**; the notice says no representation will be entertained after that date.

If your roll number is on the shortlist, the practical checklist is short: confirm your date from the table above, prepare the class-demonstration topic (TGT) or the mess-management practicals (Mess Manager), keep your originals in one folder, and reach Chittorgarh the previous evening — a 7 AM report leaves no room for a delayed train. For queries, the school can be reached at **01472-295322** between 9 AM and 5 PM.

## FAQs

**When is the Sainik School Chittorgarh recruitment process?**

TGT (Mathematics) on 30 November 2026, TGT (Science) on 2 December 2026, and Mess Manager on 3 December 2026. All three report at 7:00 AM at Sainik School Chittorgarh, Bhilwara Road, Chittorgarh, Rajasthan.

**How many candidates are shortlisted for Sainik School Chittorgarh?**

104 in total: 68 for TGT (Mathematics), 16 for TGT (Science) and 20 for Mess Manager. Roll numbers run from M-2701 to M-2768 for Maths, S-2801 to S-2816 for Science, and MM-2901 to MM-2920 for Mess Manager.

**What is the selection process at Sainik School Chittorgarh?**

A 60-minute written test first. TGT candidates who qualify then appear for a class demonstration and an interview on the same day. Mess Manager candidates who qualify appear for a skill and proficiency test and an interview. Original certificates are verified on the spot.

**What happens if I report late or forget my original certificates?**

The notice is blunt: candidates who report late or do not carry the requisite original documents will be disqualified, and no representation will be entertained. No TA or DA is paid, and the process may spill over to a second day, so arrange lodging in advance.
