## The Situation This Morning

If you have been refreshing the NTA AISSEE portal every day, here is the honest update: there is still nothing new to click. Mid-October is the traditional window — last year the form opened on **10 October 2025** — but this year the notification has not dropped yet, and NTA has not said why or when.

That silence is uncomfortable, but it is not unusual. The notification date has never followed a fixed calendar; what matters is the pattern that follows it, and that pattern is remarkably consistent.

## What the Pattern Tells Us

Looking at the last three cycles, the sequence after the notification is predictable — which is exactly why the wait should not waste your time:

| Stage | AISSEE 2026 (actual) | AISSEE 2027 (expected) |
|---|---|---|
| Application opens | 10 October 2025 | 🔶 October 2026 |
| Application deadline | 30 October, extended to 9 November 2025 | 🔶 November 2026 |
| Correction window | 2–4 November 2025 | 🔶 November 2026 |
| Admit card | January 2026 | 🔶 January 2027 |
| Exam | 18 January 2026 | ✅ 31 January 2027 (tentative) |
| Result | 27 February 2026 | 🔶 February 2027 |

The uncomfortable part of this table: last year parents got barely **20 days** between the form opening and the original deadline. When the window finally opens this year, there will be no time to start scanning documents.

## 115 Days to the Exam — What to Finish This Week

Forget the notification. Treat this week as the final quiet stretch and get these five things done:

1. **Birth certificate, domicile certificate and category certificate** — originals in hand, and scanned copies saved. These are the three documents that delay most applications.
2. **Photograph and signature** in the right format — .jpg, under 1 MB each, per last year's specs (the bulletin may tweak these, but the ballpark will not change).
3. **School study certificate** — if your child is in Class 5 or Class 8, ask the school office now for a bonafide/study certificate. School offices are slow; AISSEE windows are not.
4. **Check the age math once** — Class 6 needs 10–12 years as of 31 March 2027 (🔶 expected, per the previous cycle). Do not trust coaching-institute age charts; wait for the bulletin for the final word.
5. **Keep ₹850 ready in an online payment mode** — ₹700 for SC/ST candidates (🔶 expected). A failed payment on deadline day is a surprisingly common tragedy.

## One Warning About Rumours

Every year, fake "AISSEE 2027 form out" screenshots circulate on WhatsApp and YouTube within days of a delay like this. The rule is simple: **if it is not on exams.nta.nic.in, it is not real.** The application link, the fee, the exam date — all of them come from the bulletin, and the bulletin comes only from NTA. Do not pay anyone a "registration fee" through a third-party link, ever.

## FAQs

**Has NTA released the AISSEE 2027 application form?**

No. As of 8 October 2026, NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or application form. Multiple education portals report the notification is expected in October 2026, but no date has been announced.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**

NTA's published 2026-27 examination calendar lists AISSEE on 31 January 2027. This is a tentative date and will be confirmed in the official notification.

**What was last year's AISSEE application timeline?**

For AISSEE 2026, applications opened on 10 October 2025, the deadline was extended to 9 November 2025, the correction window ran 2–4 November, the exam was held on 18 January 2026, and results came on 27 February 2026.

**What is the expected AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Expected to stay at Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL, defence and ex-servicemen candidates, and Rs 700 for SC/ST candidates — the same as last year. The final fee will be confirmed in the official bulletin.
