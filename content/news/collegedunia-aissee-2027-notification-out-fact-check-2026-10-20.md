> **Last verified: 20 October 2026 (Tuesday).** The claim being checked, the publisher's own contradictory page, the second portal's update, and our own check of NTA's portal are all **CONFIRMED** from pages we actually opened this morning. The AISSEE 2027 notification status remains: **not released**. This is an **update/fact-check**, not breaking news.

## The claim

A collegedunia page titled "AISSEE 2027 Notification: Dates, Eligibility, Apply" — published 14 October 2026, updated 16 October — states plainly: *"The dates below are drawn from the official AISSEE 2027 notification released on October 13, 2026."* Its FAQ section doubles down: asked when the notification was released, it answers, "The AISSEE 2027 notification was released on October 13, 2026, by NTA on behalf of the Ministry of Defence."

Collegedunia is a large, generally trusted education portal. When a site like that dates a government notification, parents listen. So we checked.

## Why the claim does not hold up

**Contradiction one — the publisher's own other page.** Another collegedunia page, updated on 16 October 2026, says the opposite: *"As of October 16, 2026, no AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or registration link is listed on the official NTA portal."* It also notes the AISSEE 2026 notification came out on 10 October 2025, and the 2027 bulletin had not appeared six days past that date. The same publisher is, at the same time, both dating an October 13 release and reporting no bulletin exists. Only one of those can be true.

**Contradiction two — an independent portal.** Aglasem's AISSEE 2027 registration guide, updated on 19 October 2026, is unambiguous: *"As of October 18, 2026, no AISSEE 2027 notice has been posted on the official website."*

**Contradiction three — NTA's own portal.** We checked NTA's AISSEE portal ourselves this morning, 20 October. The URL served NTA's own 404 error page — no information bulletin, no registration link, no notices section visible at all. A notification cannot be "out" on a portal that has nothing to show.

There is also a telling detail inside the collegedunia page itself. Its fee table lists **₹800 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹650 for SC/ST**. But the AISSEE 2026 bulletin charged ₹850 for General/OBC/Defence wards and ₹700 for SC/ST. If the page had truly been drawn from an official 2027 notification, its fee figures would reflect the current cycle — they don't. (EXPECTED: fees may change for 2027; the official 2027 figures simply aren't published yet.)

## Why this matters for parents

Yesterday we [flagged claims spreading online that the AISSEE 2027 form is already out](/news/aissee-2027-form-out-claims-warning-2026-10-19/). Every year, fake "apply now" pages appear within days of such claims, collecting fees and personal data from families.

## What to do

- **Trust only NTA's official portal** for the release. If the bulletin isn't there, it isn't out — no matter what any education site says.
- **Never pay through third-party "apply now" buttons.** NTA never collects application fees through education portals or agents.
- **Use the wait well.** Keep the birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), and a recent passport-size photograph ready, and read our [first-timer's registration walkthrough](/blog/aissee-2027-registration-step-by-step-walkthrough/) so the form takes twenty calm minutes when it finally opens.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No. As of 20 October 2026 there is no AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or registration link on NTA's official portal. Any page claiming it is out should be cross-checked against the official site.

**Which site shows the official AISSEE 2027 registration?**
Only NTA's official exam portal. When the notification releases, it will appear on the NTA website first — not on education news sites, and not through third-party apply links.

**Can a trusted education portal confirm the notification is out?**
No portal can substitute the official source. In this case the claim collapses on the publisher's own other page, which said on October 16 that no bulletin was listed on the NTA portal.

**What should parents do while waiting for the AISSEE 2027 notification?**
Keep documents ready — birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate if applicable, and a recent photograph — and check only the official NTA portal for the release.
