## The headline: November is not a waiting month

Parents waiting for the AISSEE 2027 notification have now waited nearly three weeks past the date the last cycle dropped — 10 October 2025. That wait is real, but treating November as dead time would be a mistake: a UP Sainik School deadline, a correction window, Diwali, and the start of the December exam season all fall in it, while the AISSEE countdown drops below 100 days.

As of today, 29 October, there are **94 days** to the expected AISSEE exam date of 31 January 2027. November is the last full month in which a child can still fix weak chapters without panic, because December belongs to the other entrance exams and January belongs to revision.

## The one thing to check first: the portal, not the rumours

A Collegedunia page still claims the AISSEE 2027 notification was released on 13 October 2026, complete with a fee figure and a link that does not resolve on the NTA site. We debunked that claim on 18 October, and the evidence has only hardened since: the NTA portal's Latest News section, captured on 28 October, still shows only AISSEE 2026 items — score cards, qualified lists, answer keys and the 2026 counselling notice. No 2027 bulletin, no registration link.

An independent coaching guide updated on 24 October 2026 reaches the same conclusion in plain words: the NTA has not yet released the AISSEE 2027 notification. Two different checks, same answer. Until the portal itself changes, every "form out" message on social media should be treated as unverified.

## November, week by week

| Week | What happens | Your job |
|---|---|---|
| 29–31 Oct | UP Sainik late window closes 31 Oct, 5 PM (Rs 2,000 total) | UP applicants: submit and pay before 5 PM on 31 Oct |
| 3–5 Nov | UP Sainik correction window (till 5 PM) | UP applicants: fix errors — name, class, mobile, email and date of birth cannot be changed |
| 8 Nov | Diwali | Light revision only: one short set a day, no new chapters |
| 10–16 Nov | Post-Diwali reset week | One full mock test, error-log review, weakest chapter fixed first |
| 17–30 Nov | Final November push | Second mock, OMR practice under time, documents folder ready for the AISSEE form |

For UP Sainik School families, the stakes this weekend are concrete. The late window closes on **31 October at 5 PM** with a total fee of **Rs 2,000**, and the entrance exam follows on **20 December 2026** — a 200-mark OMR paper in 2 hours 30 minutes, with admit cards available after 25 November. The correction window on **3–5 November** is the last chance to fix the form, but it has hard limits: registration-stage details cannot be edited after successful registration, so check those before submitting, not after.

Just beyond November sit three more dates worth knowing now: the RIMC entrance (RIMCEE) on **6 December**, the Rashtriya Military School CET on **13 December**, and the UP Sainik School exam on **20 December**. Children appearing for any of these should treat November as their final preparation month for that exam, since the syllabi overlap heavily and the work counts twice.

## What to do today

1. **Check the NTA portal yourself** at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society — if a 2027 bulletin appears, it will appear there first.
2. **UP families:** finish the form and payment before 31 October, 5 PM, and keep the receipt printout.
3. **Everyone:** keep the documents folder ready — birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate if applicable, photo and signature in the specified sizes — so a short AISSEE window cannot catch you unprepared. Last year's window ran only from 10 to 30 October before extension.
4. **Book the Diwali plan now:** light revision through the festival, one mock in the week after. Do not let a festival week become a lost fortnight.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**

No. The official NTA AISSEE portal still lists only 2026 items, and an independent coaching guide updated on 24 October 2026 also records the notification as not yet released. Ignore pages claiming a 13 October release; that claim was already debunked against the portal.

**When does the UP Sainik School late-fee window close?**

On 31 October 2026 at 5 PM, with a total fee of Rs 2,000. The correction window for UP Sainik School applicants follows on 3 to 5 November 2026.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam expected?**

31 January 2027 is the expected date as per the NTA calendar, reported by Collegedunia. It is labelled EXPECTED until the official bulletin confirms it.

**What should my child focus on during Diwali week?**

Keep the routine light but unbroken: one short revision set a day, no new chapters, and a full mock test in the week after Diwali. A complete break in festival week is the most common reason November preparation stalls.
