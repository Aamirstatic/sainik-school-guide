## Thirteen days left changes the maths on a failed payment

There are **13 days** left to apply for AISSEE 2027. The regular window closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**, and the exam is on **31 January 2027**.

Here's why the calendar matters for this one problem. A failed fee payment in October was an annoyance — you waited two days, it sorted itself out, and the form was still weeks from closing. A failed payment now is different. Pay twice in a panic and you have two debits to chase. Wait too long without checking and the window closes under you. The good news: the fix is boring and it works, as long as you do the steps in the right order.

First, the fact that decides everything. Your form is **not** submitted until the **confirmation page** is generated on your candidate login at [exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society). A bank SMS saying the money left your account is not proof. A payment receipt alone is not proof. Only the confirmation page counts — download it and save it the moment it appears.

## Which situation are you in?

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Money deducted, no confirmation page | Payment is stuck between the bank and NTA | Wait 24–48 hours, then check your login again |
| Payment failed, no money deducted | Nothing went through | Retry the payment while the window is open |
| Confirmation page generated | Your application is complete | Download and save it — you are done |
| Paid twice by mistake | Duplicate payment | Do not pay a third time; raise it with the bank/gateway and the NTA helpdesk with both transaction references |

The most common case is the first row, and the most common mistake in it is paying again within the hour. Don't. Payments regularly take a day or two to reflect on the portal, and a second payment made too soon is how families end up chasing a refund instead of a form.

## The 48-hour rule — and the one exception

The rule is simple: **wait 24 to 48 hours** after a deducted-but-unreflected payment, checking your login once or twice a day. If the confirmation page appears, the application is complete and the wait cost you nothing.

The exception is the calendar. If you are paying on **1 or 2 December** and the payment stalls, you cannot wait out the full 48 hours inside the regular window — and that is exactly what the late window is for. Forms can still be submitted from **3 to 6 December** with a **₹500 late fee** on top of the regular fee (₹850 General/OBC/Defence, ₹700 SC/ST). So a payment that fails on the last day is not the end of the road; it is a ₹500 problem, not a lost year. And if a detail on the form itself is wrong, the **correction window runs 7–9 December**.

One more thing to check while you wait: log in and confirm the form is otherwise complete. A payment can be perfect and the form still incomplete — the confirmation page only appears when every step, including the fee, is done.

## Who to contact, in what order

1. **Your bank or payment gateway first** — NTA's own guidance says exactly this: if the fee status is not 'OK' or the confirmation page is not generated after payment, contact the helpline or email of the bank or payment gateway you paid through. Keep the transaction reference ready.
2. **The NTA AISSEE helpdesk** for the application itself: **011-40759000** or **011-69227700**, or email **aissee@nta.ac.in**. Quote your application number and the transaction reference. Never share your password or any OTP with anyone who calls you — NTA's helplines will never ask for them.
3. **Do not create a fresh registration** to retry the payment. One application number, one trail of evidence, one complaint if needed.

If a duplicate payment did go through, past NTA cycles have typically seen the extra amount refunded to the original payment source after reconciliation, usually within 5 to 7 working days — treat that as the pattern, not a promise, and keep both transaction references until the refund lands.

## FAQs

**My AISSEE 2027 fee was deducted but no confirmation page appeared. What should I do first?**

Wait 24 to 48 hours and check your candidate login on exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. The payment often reflects late. Do not pay again during that wait. If the confirmation page appears, your application is complete.

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM, for the regular window. Forms can still be submitted from 3 to 6 December 2026 with a late fee of Rs 500. The correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026.

**How much is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence-wards candidates, and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates, as confirmed in the NTA information bulletin released on 3 November 2026. The fee is non-refundable.

**Who do I contact if my AISSEE fee payment is stuck?**

First, the helpline of the bank or payment gateway you paid through — NTA's own guidance says to contact them when the fee status is not OK or the confirmation page is not generated. For the application itself, call the NTA AISSEE helpdesk on 011-40759000 or 011-69227700, or email aissee@nta.ac.in, quoting your application number and transaction reference.
