## Saturday is the exam. Sunday is the wait.

Your child has spent months on mental ability, arithmetic and language. On **Saturday, 28 November 2026**, the Class 6 Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test runs from **11:30 AM to 1:30 PM** — two hours, one paper, no negative marking. Our [final-checks guide](/news/jnvst-2027-3-days-to-exam-28-november-final-checks-2026-11-25/) covers what to carry and what to check before Saturday morning.

But here is the part nobody tells parents on exam week: the test ends on Saturday, and then — nothing happens for weeks. No score on the screen, no answer key the next morning, no result before the winter holidays. Knowing what comes next is the difference between a calm household and three months of refreshing the NVS website every evening.

## The answer key: unofficial on Saturday, official much later

By Saturday evening, YouTube and coaching websites will be full of "JNVST 2027 answer key" videos and PDFs. Treat them for what they are — **unofficial keys**, put together by teachers solving the paper from memory. They are useful for one thing only: a rough idea of how your child did. A mismatch with them means nothing.

NVS has announced **no official answer-key date** for 2027. In the 2026 cycle, the official answer key arrived with the result — on **17 March 2026**, for an exam held on 13 December 2025. Some portals expect an official key in December 2026 this time; others say March 2027. Both are guesses. We will update this article the day NVS actually publishes one.

One practical tip: on Saturday evening, ask your child to write down the answers they remember, question by question, while the paper is still fresh. That single sheet is worth more than any coaching video, because it is the only record of *their* paper.

## The result: expect March, plan for April

Here is the timeline from the last cycle, the only honest guide we have:

| Stage | 2026 cycle (what actually happened) | 2027 cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Class 6 exam | 13 December 2025 | **28 November 2026** ✅ CONFIRMED |
| Phase 1 result (summer-bound JNVs) | **17 March 2026** | 🔶 **EXPECTED March–April 2027** |
| Phase 2 result (winter-bound JNVs) | 22 May 2026 | 🔶 **EXPECTED May–June 2027** |

The 2026 result took almost exactly three months from exam to declaration. If the same rhythm holds, the 2027 result lands in **March 2027** — but NVS has announced nothing, and a delay of a few weeks either way would be entirely normal. Do not believe any WhatsApp forward carrying a "result date" until navodaya.gov.in says it.

When the result does come, check it on **navodaya.gov.in** with your child's **roll number and date of birth**. Selected candidates are also informed by **SMS and Speed Post**, and the selection lists go up at the JNV itself, the District Magistrate's office and the District Education Officer's office. Keep the admit card safe until then — you will need the roll number on it.

## If your child is selected: the documents decide everything

Selection is not admission. Every selected child goes through **document verification** at the allotted JNV, and a missing or mismatched document can cost the seat even after clearing the exam. Get these ready now, while there is time to fix them:

- **Birth certificate** (school-issued or municipal)
- **Residence certificate** issued by the district authority
- **Rural area certificate**, if claiming the rural quota
- **Caste certificate** for SC/ST/OBC candidates
- **Disability certificate**, if applicable
- **Proof of studying in the same district for the past two years**
- **Transfer certificate** from the previous school
- **Passport-size photographs**, plus the **result printout and admit card**

Keep originals plus self-attested photocopies. And check one thing tonight: does the name and date of birth on the birth certificate match the school records exactly? That single mismatch is the most common reason verification stalls.

## What about Class 9?

The **Class 9 JNVST** follows in **February 2027**, with its result expected around **May 2027** — both 🔶 **EXPECTED** on the usual cycle, not announced. If you have an older child aiming for Class 9, the same advice applies: keep the documents ready, and do not trust result dates until NVS publishes them.

Saturday's paper is two hours. The wait after it is three months. Use the wait well — keep the documents ready, keep the admit card safe, and let your child get back to being a child for a while.

## FAQs

**When will the JNVST 2027 Class 6 result be declared?**

No date has been announced. In the 2026 cycle the exam was held on 13 December 2025 and the Phase 1 result came on 17 March 2026, about three months later. On that pattern the 2027 result is expected in March or April 2027, but treat that as an expectation, not an announced date.

**Will NVS release an official JNVST 2027 answer key?**

Nothing has been announced. In the 2026 cycle the official answer key arrived with the result in March 2026, not in the days after the exam. Coaching institutes publish unofficial answer keys on the evening of the exam, but those are not official and should only be used as a rough self-check.

**How do I check the JNVST 2027 result?**

On navodaya.gov.in, using your child's roll number and date of birth. Selected candidates are also informed by SMS and Speed Post, and selection lists are displayed at the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, the District Magistrate's office and the District Education Officer's office.

**What documents are needed after selection in JNVST?**

Birth certificate, residence certificate, rural area certificate if applicable, caste certificate for reserved categories, disability certificate if applicable, proof of studying in the same district for the past two years, passport-size photographs and the transfer certificate from the previous school. Keep originals and self-attested photocopies ready.
