Thirteen days. That's all that's left between your child and the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test for Class 6 — **Saturday, 28 November 2026, 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**. The admit card has been out since 28 October, the preparation is mostly done, and now the thing that decides the day is not another mock test. It's logistics. A child who arrives late, forgets one document, or brings the wrong pen loses the exam before writing a single answer.

Here's the thing — the rules for this exam are stricter than most parents expect. Read them once, check them off, and the 28th becomes a normal morning.

## The exam-day facts, in one place

| What | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Exam date | **Saturday, 28 November 2026** |
| Timing | **11:30 AM to 1:30 PM** (2 hours, no break) |
| Reach the centre by | **10:15–10:30 AM** |
| Seated by | **11:00 AM — latecomers are not allowed** |
| Admit card | Released **28 October 2026**; download free from the NVS portal |
| Paper | 80 questions, 100 marks, OMR sheet, **no negative marking** |
| Result | Expected by **end of March 2027** |

The paper itself is 80 questions for 100 marks — Mental Ability and the new EVS section together carry half the marks (20 + 20 questions, 50 marks, 60 minutes), Arithmetic is 20 questions for 25 marks in 30 minutes, and Language is 20 questions for 25 marks in 30 minutes. Each correct answer is worth 1.25 marks, and there is no negative marking, so your child should attempt every single question. But — and this matters — each section has its own minimum qualifying mark, so a strong score in one section cannot rescue a weak one.

## What to carry (and what to leave behind)

**Carry these two documents, no exceptions:**

1. **The admit card printout.** No child is allowed in without it. Download it free from the NVS admission portal linked on navodaya.gov.in, using the registration number and your child's date of birth. Take two clear printouts on A4 paper, and check the name, photo, category, medium of the test and exam centre on it today — not on the 27th.
2. **The child's Aadhaar card** — or, if your child doesn't have one, the parent's government residence certificate that was uploaded in the application form. Either one works at the gate.

**Also carry:** two or three blue or black ballpoint pens. Answers are marked on an OMR sheet, and **pencils are strictly prohibited**. A water bottle and a light snack for after the exam are fine. Leave phones, calculators and smart watches at home or with the parent.

One more detail worth knowing: a bell rings every 30 minutes during the paper, and children cannot leave the hall until 1:30 PM. Divyang candidates get 40 minutes of extra time.

## The 3 mistakes that cost a seat

**1. Arriving after 11 AM.** This is the one that hurts most, because it's the easiest to avoid. The gate rule is simple: seated by 11:00 AM, latecomers not allowed. Visit the centre once this week if it's new to you, and plan to reach by 10:15 AM. Traffic on a Saturday morning in a district town is nobody's idea of predictable.

**2. Not checking the admit card.** The question booklet comes only in the language chosen in the form — it cannot be changed later. The centre on the admit card is the only centre your child can write at; no change of centre is entertained. If you spot a mistake in the name, date of birth, category or medium, contact the Principal of your district's JNV immediately, with your child's documents. Do not wait until exam day.

**3. Forgetting the second document.** Parents remember the admit card and forget the Aadhaar card (or the residence certificate). Both are checked. Put both in the same folder tonight, with the pens, so exam morning has nothing left to hunt for.

## What happens after 28 November

The result is expected by the end of March 2027, on the same NVS portal. Shortlisted children then go through document verification at the JNV. Between now and then, nothing is required from you — but do keep the admit card and the registration number safe, because the result is checked with the same login.

Thirteen days is enough to fix every one of these details. It is not enough to fix them on the 28th.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam?**
Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM. It is a single-phase, pen-and-paper test of 2 hours with no break.

**What time must my child reach the JNVST exam centre?**
Aim for 10:15 to 10:30 AM. Your child must be seated in the allotted room by 11:00 AM — latecomers are not allowed in, and no one can leave the hall before 1:30 PM.

**What documents must my child carry to the JNVST 2027 exam?**
Two things: a printout of the admit card, and the child's Aadhaar card — or the parent's government residence certificate that was uploaded in the application. Also carry two or three blue or black ballpoint pens. Pencils are strictly prohibited on the OMR sheet.

**Can I change my child's JNVST exam centre or test language now?**
No. NVS does not allow a change of exam centre, and the medium of the test chosen in the application form cannot be changed after the admit card is issued. If any detail on the admit card is wrong, contact the Principal of your district's JNV immediately.
