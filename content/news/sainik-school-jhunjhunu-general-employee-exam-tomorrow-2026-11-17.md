## Tomorrow, 7 AM — and 7 AM means 7 AM

If you are one of the shortlisted candidates for the General Employee post at Sainik School Jhunjhunu, your written test is tomorrow, **18 November 2026, from 7:00 to 8:00 AM**. That is a one-hour paper, and it is early. The school's notice is blunt about it: report at **7:00 AM**, the gates close at **8:00 AM**, and late entry is refused. There is no second chance for late reporting, and no separate opportunity for document verification later.

This is not a morning to test the route. The school is at Village Dorasar, about **16 km from Jhunjhunu city** — plan the journey tonight, and aim to be at the gate well before 7.

## What the two stages look like

| Stage | What it is | Date | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Written test | General Knowledge and Mathematics of the appropriate level | 18 November 2026 | 7:00–8:00 AM |
| II — Physical / Skill Proficiency Test | Running, shuttle run, specialised skill test | 19–23 November 2026 | 7:00–8:00 AM |

One thing to understand clearly: the written test is a gate, not a formality. Candidates who fail to obtain the minimum marks in it are **automatically disqualified** from the physical and skill test. So tomorrow morning decides whether the rest of the week happens for you at all.

## What to carry — and what to leave behind

Carry three things, and check them tonight:

1. **The admit card** — download it from the school's website, ssjhunjhunu.com, where it is listed as "Admit Cards: General Employee (Regular)". It is provisional, which means it gets you into the process but does not by itself prove eligibility — that is decided on documents.
2. **All original documents and testimonials** submitted with your application, or claimed in it.
3. **A valid government-issued photo ID** — PAN card, driving licence, voter ID, passport or Aadhaar card. No admit card and no valid ID means no entry.

Leave the phone at home, or at least out of the hall: **no electronic gadgets, smart watches, earphones or mobile phones** are allowed, and no personal belongings at all during the exam. The notice says plainly that examination officials will not be responsible for the safe custody of personal belongings. Bring a transparent filled water bottle — water will be available for refilling — and adequate writing material, including a clipboard.

Two practical warnings from the notice itself: there is **no arrangement for lunch** at the school premises or nearby, so carry food and drink as needed; and candidates must arrange their own boarding and lodging, with **no travel or daily allowance** admissible.

## A word on the rules inside the hall

The exam centres are under **CCTV surveillance**, and anyone found using unfair means is disqualified immediately, with the candidature cancelled. No candidate may leave the examination room or hall before the end of the examination. If your religion or customs require specific attire, reach the centre early for thorough checking.

If the date changes for administrative reasons, the school says all further information will appear on **www.ssjhunjhunu.com** — check it before you travel. For clarifications, the school's recruitment helpline is **+91 9509941985**, on working days from 10:00 AM to 12:00 noon, and the recruitment email is ssjr.recruitment@ssjhunjhunu.com.

Tonight's job is simple: admit card printed, documents in a folder, ID in your pocket, alarm set for well before dawn. The paper is one hour. The preparation for it is tonight.

## FAQs

**When is the Sainik School Jhunjhunu General Employee written test?**

18 November 2026, from 7:00 AM to 8:00 AM. Candidates must report at 7:00 AM; the gates close at 8:00 AM and late entry is refused.

**What is the Sainik School Jhunjhunu General Employee written test about?**

General Knowledge and Mathematics of the appropriate level. Candidates who fail to obtain the minimum marks are disqualified from the next stage, the physical and skill proficiency test.

**What documents must candidates carry to the Jhunjhunu General Employee exam?**

The admit card, all original documents and testimonials submitted with the application, and a valid government-issued photo ID — PAN card, driving licence, voter ID, passport or Aadhaar card.

**When is the physical and skill test for Sainik School Jhunjhunu General Employee?**

19 to 23 November 2026, from 7:00 AM to 8:00 AM each day. It covers running, the shuttle run and a specialised skill test. Only candidates who clear the written test take part.
