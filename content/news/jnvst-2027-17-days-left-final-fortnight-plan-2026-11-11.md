## 16 days left — and the admit card is already in your hands

The Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) 2027 for Class 6 is on **Saturday, 28 November 2026**, from **11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**. Today, 12 November, that leaves 16 days. Unlike AISSEE, where the admit card is still months away, the JNVST admit card has been live since **28 October 2026** — if you have not downloaded it yet, that is tonight's first job, not tomorrow's.

Download it free from the NVS admission portal linked on [navodaya.gov.in](https://navodaya.gov.in), using the registration number from the application SMS and the child's date of birth. Take two printouts. Then check four things on it before anything else: the child's name and date of birth, the photograph, the exam centre address, and the **language of the question booklet** — the booklet comes only in the language chosen in the form, and it cannot be changed later. If anything is wrong, contact the Principal of your district's JNV immediately. Do not wait until exam week.

## The paper your child will actually sit

This year's paper has one change parents must not miss: the first section now combines **Mental Ability (20 questions) with EVS (20 questions)** — 50 marks in 60 minutes, half the paper's marks. Arithmetic is 20 questions for 25 marks in 30 minutes, and Language is 20 questions for 25 marks in 30 minutes. In total: **80 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours**, with 1.25 marks per correct answer and **no negative marking** — so every question should be attempted.

There is a catch that catches families out: each section has a **minimum qualifying mark** — 14 in the combined Mental Ability plus EVS section, and 7 each in Arithmetic and Language. A child who aces Maths but blanks the EVS half does not qualify. That is why the final fortnight should not be spent only on the child's strongest subject.

## The final-fortnight plan

**Days 17 to 11 (12–18 November): one section test a day.** Alternate Mental Ability, EVS, Arithmetic and Language, and spend the longest sessions on Mental Ability plus EVS — together they carry half the marks. Every wrong answer goes into a notebook with the reason, not just the correct answer.

**Days 10 to 4 (19–25 November): three full two-hour papers.** Sit them on an OMR sheet, timed **11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**, the exact exam slot, so the child's body clock learns the paper. Review each paper the same evening; a mock without review is just a confidence test.

**Days 3 to 1 (26–28 November): revise mistakes only.** No new topics, no new books. Re-read the error notebook, glance at the admit card and the centre's route, and sleep early. Visit the exam centre once if it is new to you.

## Exam-day rules to tell your child tonight

Reach the centre by **10:15–10:30 AM**; the child must be seated by **11:00 AM**, and latecomers are not allowed in. Carry the admit-card printout and the child's **Aadhaar card** (or the parent's residence certificate uploaded in the application). Answers go on an OMR sheet with **blue or black ballpoint pens only — pencils are strictly prohibited**, and erasing, striking out or using whitener means that answer is not evaluated. A bell rings every 30 minutes, and no child can leave the hall before 1:30 PM. Phones, calculators and smart watches stay at home.

The result is 🔶 **EXPECTED by the end of March 2027**, after which shortlisted children go through document verification at the JNV. For now, the only date that matters is 28 November — and the only work that matters is the plan above.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam?**

Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM — a two-hour offline OMR paper in a single phase for all JNVs. Children must be seated by 11:00 AM; latecomers are not allowed in.

**Has the JNVST 2027 admit card been released?**

Yes. NVS released the Class 6 admit cards on 28 October 2026 on the NVS admission portal linked from navodaya.gov.in. Download it free with the registration number and the child's date of birth, and take two printouts.

**Is there negative marking in JNVST 2027?**

No. Each correct answer carries 1.25 marks and wrong answers score zero, so the child should attempt every question. Each section also has a minimum qualifying mark — 14 in Mental Ability plus EVS, and 7 each in Arithmetic and Language.

**What is the new EVS section in JNVST 2027?**

This year the first section combines Mental Ability (20 questions) with EVS (20 questions) — 50 marks in 60 minutes. It is the biggest section of the paper, so it deserves the most revision time in the final fortnight.
