## The games close today — and this is the part of Sainik School most parents never see

If your child is preparing for AISSEE 2027, your whole world right now is the form, the fee and the syllabus. Fair enough — the form closes on 2 December and the exam is on 31 January. But today, 7 November, something is happening in Karnataka that is worth five minutes of your attention, because it shows what you are actually applying for.

The fourth All India Sainik Schools National Games (AISSNG) 2026 close today at Sainik School Bijapur in Vijayapura, Karnataka. Five days, 544 cadets, 43 schools — and a closing ceremony attended by the Raksha Rajya Mantri himself. This is not a side event. It is the system working exactly as it was designed to.

## What happened over the five days

The games ran from 3 to 7 November 2026 at Sainik School Bijapur, organised under the aegis of the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence. The numbers tell the story:

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cadets | 544 — 296 boys and 248 girls |
| Schools | 43 — 30 established Sainik Schools and 13 New Sainik Schools |
| Zones | 8 — contingents are the winners of the zonal championships |
| Host | Sainik School Bijapur, Vijayapura, Karnataka — 63 years of service |
| Events | Basketball, volleyball, track and field, debate, quiz |

The competitions covered basketball for junior boys and girls, volleyball for junior boys, and track and field for sub-junior and junior boys and girls — 100-metre, 200-metre and 400-metre sprints, the 1,500-metre race, the 4×100-metre relay and long jump. Debate and quiz contests ran alongside the sport, which is the point: the championship is explicitly built around physical fitness *and* mental agility, teamwork and leadership.

## Who is at the closing ceremony today

Raksha Rajya Mantri Sanjay Seth attends as the chief guest and will interact with the cadets. Also present: Chief of the Naval Staff Admiral Krishna Swaminathan — himself an alumnus of Sainik School Bijapur — and M B Patil, Karnataka's Minister for Large and Medium Industries and Infrastructure Development, also a Bijapur alumnus. The host school, which has completed 63 years of service, carries the motto "Ajeet Hai Abheet Hai" — unconquered and fearless.

## Why this matters to a parent filling the AISSEE form

Here's the thing most admission guides skip. The entrance exam is the gate, not the destination. A Sainik School education is deliberately built on three legs — academics, physical training and character — and the National Games are where all three are tested in public. Your child will not just sit in a classroom for six years. They will run, play, debate, march and compete against the best cadets in the country, and that is where the confidence that later shows up in an SSB interview is actually built.

The proof turned up the same week. Two cadets of Sainik School Punglwa — Cadet Dev Dagar (All India Rank 61) and Cadet Anshik Yadav (All India Rank 125) — have secured selection for the 157th course of the National Defence Academy and Naval Academy. That is the pipeline working end to end: entrance exam at 11, national-level sport and academics through school, and an NDA rank at 18. If you want to understand what the [NDA path after Sainik School](/blog/nda-after-sainik-school-career-path/) looks like in practice, this week gave you two live examples.

None of this changes your immediate job. The [AISSEE 2027 form is open until 2 December 2026](/news/aissee-2027-notification-out-applications-open-exam-jan-31-2026-11-03/), with a late-fee window from 3 to 6 December and a correction window from 7 to 9 December, and the exam is on 31 January 2027 at 2 PM. But when your child asks what a Sainik School is *really* like — beyond the books and the mock tests — today is a good day to show them. A school where 544 teenagers from 43 schools spend five days competing, and a minister and a Navy chief turn up to watch them finish, is a school that takes the whole child seriously.

## FAQs

**When and where are the All India Sainik Schools National Games 2026 held?**

From 3 to 7 November 2026 at Sainik School Bijapur in Vijayapura, Karnataka. The closing ceremony is on 7 November 2026.

**How many cadets took part in AISSNG 2026?**

544 cadets — 296 boys and 248 girls — representing 30 established Sainik Schools and 13 New Sainik Schools from eight zones across India.

**Who is the chief guest at the AISSNG 2026 closing ceremony?**

Raksha Rajya Mantri Sanjay Seth. Chief of the Naval Staff Admiral Krishna Swaminathan and Karnataka minister M B Patil, both alumni of Sainik School Bijapur, are also present.

**Which sports and events are part of the Sainik Schools National Games?**

Basketball for junior boys and girls, volleyball for junior boys, track and field for sub-junior and junior boys and girls, plus debate and quiz contests.
