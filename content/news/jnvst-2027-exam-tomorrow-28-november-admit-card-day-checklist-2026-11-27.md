## Tomorrow is the day

If your child is registered for the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) Class 6, the wait is over. The Phase I exam for Summer Bound Schools is **tomorrow, Saturday 28 November 2026**, held offline at centres across the country. Two hours, one OMR sheet, three sections — Mental Ability, Arithmetic and Language.

This is the last evening before the exam, so today's job is not revision. It is paperwork. The one thing that can still go wrong tonight is the admit card, and it is also the one thing you can still fix.

## Download and print the admit card today

The Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti has released the admit cards for the Class 6 Phase I test. Download it from **navodaya.gov.in** or the NVS admission portal at **cbseitms.rcil.gov.in/nvs**. The steps are simple: open the candidate corner or latest announcements section, click the link for the Class VI JNVST admit card, enter the registration number and the child's date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format, fill in the security code, and sign in.

Then print it — twice. The Times of India advises taking at least two clear printouts, and that is good advice: a spare copy in a separate bag costs nothing and saves the morning if one gets wet, torn or left behind. NVS does **not** send admit cards by post, so the printout you carry is the only ticket in.

## Check every line before you print

Do not just download and file it away. Read the hall ticket line by line tonight. It carries the child's name, roll number, registration number, photograph and signature, gender and category, rural or urban status, the examination medium, the exam date, the centre's name and address, the reporting time and the exam-day instructions.

If anything is wrong — a misspelt name, the wrong photograph, the wrong medium — report it **immediately to the principal of the district Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya concerned**, before the exam. That is the route Jagran Josh confirms. Do not leave it for the exam morning, when the centre staff will have no time to fix it.

## Tonight's checklist

- **Admit card:** two printouts, in a clear folder, in the bag already.
- **Centre:** read the address on the admit card, not from memory. If the centre is in another town, decide tonight who is going, how you are getting there, and when you must leave. The reporting time is printed on the admit card — plan to arrive comfortably before it.
- **Sleep:** this matters more than one more practice paper. A child who slept well answers better than a child who revised until midnight.
- **Morning plan:** a light breakfast, water, and nothing new — no new pen, no new route, no new anything.

One honest note: the admit-card reports in the press this week disagree slightly on when exactly the cards were released, with one portal dating the release to late October. It does not matter now. What matters is that the cards are live on the NVS website today, and your child's card is either in your hand or one download away.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST Class 6 exam 2027?**

Tomorrow, Saturday 28 November 2026, for Phase I (Summer Bound Schools). It is an offline pen-and-paper test on an OMR sheet, lasting two hours.

**Where do I download the JNVST 2027 admit card?**

From navodaya.gov.in or the NVS admission portal cbseitms.rcil.gov.in/nvs. Log in with the registration number and the child's date of birth, then download and print the hall ticket.

**Is a printed admit card compulsory for the JNVST exam?**

Yes. NVS does not send admit cards by post, and a printed copy is mandatory for entry into the exam hall. Take two clear printouts, in case one gets damaged.

**What should I do if there is a mistake on the JNVST admit card?**

Report it immediately to the principal of the district Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya concerned, before the exam. Do not wait until the morning of 28 November.
