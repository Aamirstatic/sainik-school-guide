## What's happening

The last door to RIMC Dehradun's Class 8 admission for the July 2027 term closes in **4 days**. The regular RIMCEE 2026 application window shut on 30 September, but the National Testing Agency's late-fee window is still open — **applications are accepted until 7 October 2026**, with the fee transaction permitted until **11:50 PM that night** ✅ (CONFIRMED — EdexLive, 2 September 2026; Telegraph India, 30 September 2026).

This is not a deadline you can negotiate with. After 7 October, there is no second late window — the portal simply moves to the **correction window (9–11 October)** and then to admit cards.

## What you pay now (CONFIRMED)

| Candidate category | Regular fee | With Rs 500 late fee (until 7 Oct) |
|---|---|---|
| General, wards of defence personnel, OBC-NCL | Rs 1,000 | **Rs 1,500** |
| SC / ST | Rs 750 | **Rs 1,250** |

The late fee applies on top of the base fee — it is not a substitute for it. Apply on the NTA's RIMCEE portal at exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee/ (CONFIRMED — Telegraph India).

## Who can still apply (CONFIRMED)

- **Age:** 12½ to 14 years as on **1 July 2027** — date of birth between **2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015**, both dates inclusive.
- **Education:** passed Class 7, or currently studying in Class 7 in a recognised school.
- **Process:** completely online. Verify every detail and **save the Confirmation Page** after the fee payment succeeds — it is your proof of application.

## What happens next

| Milestone | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Late-fee window closes | **7 Oct 2026, 11:50 PM** ✅ | Correction window opens 9–11 Oct ✅ |
| Stage-I written exam | **6 Dec 2026** ✅ | 4 hours 30 minutes, pen-and-paper |
| Stage-II viva voce | April 2027 (tentative) 🔶 | Must qualify separately, with the medical |

Note the correction window is only **three days (9–11 October)** — if your form has a wrong date of birth or a blurred photograph, that is your one chance to fix it.

## What to do this weekend

1. **Submit by 6 October, not 7.** The last evening is when portals crash and payments hang. File one day early and sleep well.
2. **Check the DOB math yourself.** The July 2013–January 2015 window is strict; an ineligible candidate is rejected at verification even with a valid admit card.
3. **Keep documents ready:** birth certificate, Class 7 study/pass certificate, caste certificate (if claiming), photograph and signature in the specified format.
4. **Start the 6 December countdown now.** Nine weeks of structured English, Maths, Science and GK preparation is very winnable — the [AISSEE preparation roadmap](/blog/aissee-preparation-without-coaching/) applies equally to RIMCEE's written stage.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RIMCEE 2026?**
7 October 2026 — the late-fee window runs 1–7 October 2026, with the fee transaction accepted until 11:50 PM on 7 October. No applications are accepted after this.

**What is the late fee for RIMCEE 2026?**
Rs 500 extra on top of the regular fee: Rs 1,500 total for General, defence wards and OBC-NCL candidates, and Rs 1,250 total for SC/ST candidates.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 exam?**
The Stage-I written examination is on 6 December 2026, pen-and-paper mode with a 4 hour 30 minute duration. The Stage-II viva voce is tentatively scheduled for April 2027.

**Is there a correction window for RIMCEE 2026?**
Yes — the application correction window is open from 9 to 11 October 2026, just two days after the late-fee window closes.
