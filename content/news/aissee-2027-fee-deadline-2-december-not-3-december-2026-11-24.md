## 8 days left — and one wrong date is doing the rounds

Here is the thing. With eight days to go before the AISSEE 2027 form closes, the most dangerous page on the internet for a parent right now is not a fake website. It is a familiar one, with the right form link and the right exam date — and one wrong date buried in its table.

SarkariResult.app, in a post published today, 24 November, lists the AISSEE 2027 dates as: last date to apply online, **2 December 2026** — correct — and then, directly underneath it, "Last Date for Online Fee Payment: **03 December 2026**". That second line is wrong. Two other result sites we checked this evening, sarkaritoday.com and sarkariresulttrack.com, print the same 3 December fee date.

The NTA information bulletin — the only document that decides anything — says something else entirely. Under "Fee Details and Important Dates" it states, in plain words: "Last Date of Successful Transaction of Fee through Credit / Debit Cards / Net-Banking / UPI — **02nd December 2026 (Upto 11:50 PM)**."

3 December is not the fee deadline. 3 December is the day the **late window opens** — late submission with an extra ₹500, running to 6 December. Whoever copied that date into the fee row has confused the two. And if you plan your week around the wrong one, you will find out the difference the expensive way.

## Why this mistake costs real money — or the whole form

This is not a typo you can shrug off, because the bulletin ties the fee to the validity of the form itself. Read it slowly:

> "The final submission of Online Application Form will remain incomplete, if Step-3 is not complete. Such forms will stand rejected and no correspondence on this account will be entertained."

Step 3 is the fee payment. A form filled perfectly, with every certificate uploaded, but with the fee unpaid at 11:50 PM on 2 December, is not a form waiting in a queue. It is a rejected form.

Now, to be fair to parents: the late window does give you a second chance. From Thursday 3 December to Sunday 6 December you can submit late, paying ₹500 on top of the normal fee — ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST, so ₹1,350 or ₹1,200 in total. The last late-fee payment must succeed by 6 December, 11:50 PM.

But that second chance only helps if you actually submit in the late window. A parent who filled the form on 2 December, believed the fee could wait until 3 December, and then simply paid on the 3rd without re-submitting, is relying on a process the bulletin does not describe. Do not test it. Pay by 2 December, and treat the late window as what it is — a rescue lane, not a plan.

## The one proof that matters: the confirmation page

There is a second trap hiding inside the first one, and the bulletin spells it out too. Paying is not the same as having paid. The payment only counts when the **confirmation page is generated** after it:

> "In case the Confirmation Page is not generated after payment of fee, then the transaction is cancelled and amount will be refunded to the candidate's account. However, the candidate has to make another transaction."

So tonight's job, if you have already applied, is a two-minute check, not a form-filling session. Open the portal at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society, log in with your application number, and confirm two things: the fee shows as paid, and you hold a downloaded confirmation page. If the page never appeared, the money is coming back to your account — and the form is not done. Pay again, and do it well before the last evening, when the payment gateway is at its slowest and the helpdesk is at its busiest.

If you do get stuck, NTA's helpdesk is 011 40759000 or 011 69227700 (10 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Saturday), and aissee@nta.ac.in. Call before the last day, not on it. ## What to do tonight

One honest piece of advice, parent to parent: do not trust any date you read on a result site, including this one, without checking it against the bulletin. The bulletin is a PDF on the NTA site. It takes two minutes to open, and it is the only page that can cost you ₹500 — or your child's form — if you get it wrong.

If your fee is paid and your confirmation page is saved, you are done with the form. The exam is on Sunday, 31 January 2027 — Class 6 from 2:00 to 4:30 PM, Class 9 from 2:00 to 5:00 PM — and the next milestone is the correction window, 7 to 9 December, which is your one chance to fix mistakes. ## FAQs

**What is the last date to pay the AISSEE 2027 fee?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM — the same as the last date to submit the form. That is what the NTA information bulletin says. Several result-aggregator sites print 3 December, which is wrong.

**What happens if I fill the form but do not pay the fee by 2 December?**

The bulletin is blunt: if Step 3, the fee payment, is not complete, the final submission remains incomplete and the form stands rejected, with no correspondence entertained. You would have to submit afresh in the late window from 3 to 6 December, paying an extra Rs 500.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 late fee window?**

From Thursday, 3 December to Sunday, 6 December 2026, with an additional late fee of Rs 500 over the normal fee. The last date for a successful late-fee payment is 6 December, 11:50 PM.

**How do I know my AISSEE 2027 payment went through?**

Only when the confirmation page is generated after payment. Download, save and print it — the bulletin says to keep copies safely. If no confirmation page appears, the transaction is cancelled, the money is refunded, and you must pay again.
