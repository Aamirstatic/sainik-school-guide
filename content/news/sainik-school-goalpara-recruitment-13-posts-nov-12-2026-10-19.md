## What happened

Sainik School Goalpara, Assam, has opened recruitment for teaching and non-teaching staff under Advertisement No. SSG/03/2026, dated 3 October 2026. The drive covers roughly a dozen post categories — from PGT Physics and TGT teachers to the Quarter Master, Band Master, clerks, and support roles — and the application process is **offline only**, by post or by hand.

This is the rare government-school recruitment that matters to two audiences at once: job seekers, obviously, but also AISSEE parents — this year's intake of teachers will be teaching the batches entering through AISSEE 2027.

## How to apply (offline — don't wait till November)

There is no online form. The reported steps:

1. Download the prescribed application form from the official school website.
2. Fill the proforma with your full postal address, email, and contact number.
3. Attach a recent passport-size photograph, resume, and attested copies of mark sheets and testimonials.
4. Enclose the fee **Demand Draft** — Rs 300 (General) or Rs 200 (SC/ST/OBC), non-refundable, drawn in favour of **"Principal, Sainik School, Goalpara"**, payable at State Bank of India, Mornai (Code No. 9148). (CONFIRMED)
5. Write **"APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF … (REGULAR/CONTRACTUAL)"** on the envelope.
6. Post or hand-deliver it to: The Principal, Sainik School Goalpara, PO Rajapara, District Goalpara, Assam – **783133**. (CONFIRMED)

Selection is reported to include a written test (GK, General English, Basic Maths, plus a subject test), a class demonstration or skill test where applicable, and a personal interview — with dates communicated by email. TA/DA is reported as not admissible. Treat these process details as EXPECTED until the official notice confirms them.

## Why this deadline is different

This is a **receipt** deadline, not a postmark deadline — your application must be *in the school's hands* by 12 November 2026. With the Diwali week in early November slowing courier and postal movement, send it in the last week of October at the latest. That is the single most actionable line in this article.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for Sainik School Goalpara recruitment 2026?**
12 November 2026 is the last date for receipt of the application at the school. Because this recruitment is offline, post your form early enough that it reaches the school on or before that date — posting on the deadline day is not enough.

**How do I apply for the Sainik School Goalpara jobs?**
The process is offline only. Download the prescribed form from the official notice on sainikschoolgoalpara.org, fill it in, attach documents and the fee Demand Draft, write the post name and Regular/Contractual on the envelope, and send it to The Principal, Sainik School Goalpara, PO Rajapara, District Goalpara, Assam – 783133.

**What is the salary for Sainik School Goalpara posts?**
Reported monthly pay ranges from Rs 21,600 for the Ward Boy post to Rs 57,120 for PGT Physics, with TGT Mathematics and TGT Social Science at Rs 53,880. Only the Quarter Master post is regular; the rest are reported as contractual.

**What is the application fee for Sainik School Goalpara recruitment?**
Rs 300 for General candidates and Rs 200 for SC/ST/OBC candidates, payable by a non-refundable Demand Draft in favour of Principal, Sainik School, Goalpara. Applications without the required DD are not accepted.
