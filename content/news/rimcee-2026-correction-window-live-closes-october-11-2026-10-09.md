## The window is live — and it is short

The National Testing Agency has opened the application correction window for the **RIMCEE 2026** (Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination) **today, 9 October 2026**. It closes at **11:50 PM on 11 October** — barely three days.

Two words in the NTA notice matter most: **one-time facility**. Once you submit a correction, the form freezes. And there will be no second correction window after the 11th. If your form has even one mistake in a name, a date of birth, or the exam city, this weekend is the time to fix it.

The correction facility is available on the official NTA RIMCEE portal (exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE/) — the same place you registered.

## What you can actually change

This is where families trip up, because "correction window" sounds like you can fix anything. You cannot. NTA has split the fields into three groups:

**You can change any ONE of these three:**
- Candidate's name
- Father's name
- Mother's name

**You can also change all of these:**
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Category
- Signature
- Class 7 equivalent details
- Exam city selection (subject to NTA's conditions)
- Aadhaar details — but **only** if you originally registered using an identity document other than Aadhaar

**You CANNOT change:**
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Present or permanent address
- Emergency contact details
- Photograph

The name rule is the most commonly misunderstood one. It is not three edits — you get to change **any one** of the three names. If both the candidate's name and the father's name are wrong, you can fix only one. Choose carefully and re-read the form before you touch it.

## The fee trap to avoid

If your correction changes the fee category — say, a change from General to SC — NTA will ask you to pay the difference. That additional fee is **non-refundable** once paid. Corrections that do not affect the fee carry no charge.

Practical advice: submit the correction, then immediately download and save the updated confirmation page. After submission the form is frozen — if you made a second mistake while fixing the first, there is no undo.

## Why this correction window exists

This is the first year NTA — rather than the old state-level commissions — is conducting the RIMC entrance exam, so the process is new for most families. Registration for the regular window closed on 30 September at 5 PM, with a late-fee window from 1 to 7 October, and the written examination is on **6 December 2026**. The written test is followed by a viva voce round; both must be cleared to secure a Class 8 seat at RIMC, Dehradun for the July 2027 term.
