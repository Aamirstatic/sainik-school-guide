## The next document on the RMS calendar is ten days away

If your child applied for the Rashtriya Military Schools Common Entrance Test, the next ten days bring the first document of the final stretch. The **RMS CET 2026 exam city intimation slip is expected around 29 November 2026** — roughly twelve to fifteen days before the exam — on the official NTA portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet**. The exam itself is on **Sunday, 13 December 2026**, for Class 6 and Class 9 admission to the five Rashtriya Military Schools — Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belagavi and Bengaluru — for the 2027–28 session.

One honest caveat before anything else: NTA has **not announced** the 29 November date. It is the expected date under the pattern NTA follows for its entrance exams — city slip twelve to fifteen days out, admit card five to seven days out — and both AglaSem and Enunciate Academy report it on that basis. Treat it as a date to watch for, not a date to plan travel around.

## What the city slip is — and what it is not

Here is the distinction that trips up parents every year, because the two documents look alike and arrive close together. The city slip tells you **which city** your child's exam centre is in. That is all it does. It carries no centre name, no address, no reporting time, and it will not get your child through the exam-hall gate.

Its whole purpose is logistics. If the centre turns out to be in another city, you get ten days to book trains and a place to stay, instead of discovering it five days before the exam.

The admit card is the document that matters at the gate. When it arrives — in the final week, going by the pattern — log in with the **application number and password**, download the PDF, and check the centre name and address, the reporting time, and your child's name, photograph and class against the application. The admit card carries a self-declaration page: print it, paste the same passport-size photograph used in the form, and put the thumb impression where marked. NTA does not send admit cards by post, and no duplicate is issued at the centre. If a detail is wrong, contact NTA at once — the helpline is **011-40759000 / 011-69227700**, or email **rmscet@nta.gov.in**.

## A quick reminder of the paper

The Class 6 paper is four sections of 50 marks each: English, which is qualifying only and needs 35%; Intelligence, Mathematics and General Knowledge, each needing 40% to qualify. Children who clear the written paper face a 20-mark interview. The Class 9 paper is two 100-mark papers — Paper 1 (English 50, Hindi 20, Social Science 30) and Paper 2 (Mathematics 50, Science 50) — each needing 50% to qualify, followed by a 50-mark interview. The Class 6 paper is bilingual (English and Hindi); the Class 9 paper is in English only. The exam runs 2 hours 30 minutes.

## FAQs

**When will the RMS CET 2026 city slip be released?**
No date has been announced by NTA yet. Based on the standard pattern of 12 to 15 days before the exam, the city intimation slip is expected around 29 November 2026, in the last week of November. Treat that as an expected date, not an announced one, and keep checking exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet.

**Is the RMS CET city slip the same as the admit card?**
No. The city slip only tells you which city your exam centre is in, so you can plan travel. It carries no centre address and no reporting time, and it will not get your child into the exam hall. The admit card comes separately, about 5 to 7 days before the exam.

**When will the RMS CET 2026 admit card be released?**
The admit card is expected around 6 to 8 December 2026, about 5 to 7 days before the 13 December exam. NTA has not announced a fixed date yet. Download it from exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet using the application number and password — it is not sent by post.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam and what is the pattern?**
Sunday, 13 December 2026, pen-and-paper OMR, 2 hours 30 minutes. The Class 6 paper has four sections of 50 marks each — English (qualifying only), Intelligence, Mathematics and General Knowledge — followed by a 20-mark interview. The Class 9 paper has two 100-mark papers followed by a 50-mark interview.
