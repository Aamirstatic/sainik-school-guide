## It is out — stop waiting for a message

If your child applied to the UP Sainik Schools — Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School in Lucknow or Sainik School in Gorakhpur — the wait is over. The admit card for the 2027-28 entrance exam is **out now**, and you can download it today from the candidate login at **upsainikschool.org**.

We checked the official site ourselves this morning. The homepage's What's New section carries the notice, marked "New": admit card released, download and print using your registered credentials. The entrance-exam portal repeats it in a red banner at the top. JagranJosh reported the same release this morning. Three days ago, in our [admit-card preview](/news/up-sainik-school-admit-card-from-25-november-download-tomorrow-2026-11-23/), we told you to check the dashboard from 25 November onwards — today is the day that advice pays off.

One small thing to watch: the portal link sits under a web address that says "entranceexam2026", but the page itself is headed "Entrance Exam 2027-28". That is the right page. Do not let the folder name put you off.

## How to download it in two minutes

1. Go to **upsainikschool.org** and open the entrance-exam portal from the What's New link.
2. Click **Candidate Login** and enter the **username and password you created during registration**.
3. Download the admit card from your dashboard and **print both copies**.

That last step is the one parents keep missing, so here it is again: this admit card has **two sections** — your child's copy and the school's office copy. The school copy must be handed to the invigilator during the exam. Print both, and put both in the same folder.

If the login fails, check the password you set during registration first — lost credentials are your responsibility. The helpline is **7052777795, 9 AM to 2 PM on working days**, and it will ask for the application ID, registered mobile number and the child's name. Keep those ready before you call.

## The 5 checks to run the evening you download it

Our [detailed 5-checks guide](/news/up-sainik-school-admit-card-after-25-nov-5-checks-20-dec-exam-2026-11-14/) covers this in full. The short version, for tonight:

1. **Name, photograph and date of birth** — against the original documents. The prospectus is unusually specific here: the admit card must carry the **same recent photograph you uploaded while filling the form**.
2. **Exam centre and reporting time.** The exam is held at centres in **Agra, Ayodhya, Bareilly, Ghaziabad, Gorakhpur, Jhansi, Meerut, Prayagraj, Varanasi and Lucknow**. Know which one is yours before exam week.
3. **The school's office copy** — printed, in the same folder as your child's copy.
4. **The identity document.** Along with the admit card, your child must bring the **student identity card or bonafide certificate with photograph, attested by the head of the institution** of the school they currently attend.
5. **Login credentials.** If the login fails, check the password you set during registration first — lost credentials are your responsibility, and the helpline (7052777795, 9 AM to 2 PM on working days) will ask for the application ID, registered mobile number and the child's name.

One deadline has already passed and cannot be recovered: the correction window closed in early November, so what the admit card says now is, for most fields, final. That is exactly why the checks above matter — the 22 days between today and the exam are your only window to spot a mistake and get it fixed.

## 22 days to the exam — and the road after it

The exam itself is on **Sunday, 20 December 2026** — 22 days from today. It is a single OMR-based objective paper: 🔶 **expected 200 questions for 200 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes**. Mathematics carries 75 of those marks and English 50, with Intelligence and General Knowledge making up the rest. Lucknow admits boys only in Class 6 and boys and girls in Class 9; Gorakhpur admits boys and girls in both classes.

## FAQs

**Is the UP Sainik School admit card out now?**

Yes. The UP Sainik Schools Society has released the admit cards for the 2027-28 entrance exam, and the official website upsainikschool.org shows "ADMIT CARD RELEASED" in its What's New section and on the entrance-exam portal. Download it from the candidate login.

**How do I download the UP Sainik School admit card?**

Go to upsainikschool.org, open the entrance-exam portal, and log in with the username and password you created during registration. The admit card appears on your candidate dashboard. Download it and print both copies — your child's copy and the school's office copy.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam?**

Sunday, 20 December 2026. It is a single OMR-based paper, expected to be 200 questions for 200 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes, for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 at Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow and Sainik School, Gorakhpur.

**What should I check on the admit card the moment I download it?**
