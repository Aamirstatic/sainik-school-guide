> **Last verified: 21 October 2026.** The claim that the AISSEE 2027 notification was "released on 13 October 2026" comes from a third-party portal. The official NTA AISSEE portal carries no AISSEE 2027 bulletin, notice or registration link — CONFIRMED by a live check of the portal today, backed by two independent education sites. Treat any "apply now" button on a non-NTA website as unofficial.

You may have seen a confident headline this week: AISSEE 2027 notification released 13 October 2026, applications open. It is premature. We checked the official source directly today, and the story does not hold up.

## The claim

On 14 October 2026, an education portal published a page stating: *"The AISSEE 2027 notification was released on October 13, 2026 by the National Testing Agency (NTA), officially opening admissions."* Its quick-facts box lists the notification as **"Released: October 13, 2026"** and **application status "Open"**.

That is a strong claim. It deserves a check against the one source that decides the matter: NTA itself.

## What the official portal actually shows

We opened the official NTA AISSEE page (exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society) today, 21 October 2026. What we found:

- **No AISSEE 2027 bulletin, notice, or registration link anywhere on the page.** A site search for **"AISSEE 2027"** on the portal returned **"No post found."**
- The **Public Notices** section lists 10 items — every one concerns **AISSEE–2026**. The **Latest News** ticker shows only "Score Card for AISSEE – 2026"; News & Events shows "No post to display."
- The footer reads **"Last Updated: 16.10.2026"** — the site was updated *after* the alleged 13 October release date, and still carries nothing about AISSEE 2027.

This is not just our finding. News-aglasem (19 October 2026) wrote that *"as of October 18, 2026, no AISSEE 2027 notice has been posted on the official website"* — and noted that *"some education websites have reported that the AISSEE 2027 form is already out. This could not be confirmed from NTA."* Our own [notification watch](/news/aissee-2027-notification-still-not-out-oct-21-2026-10-21/) has reached the same conclusion every day this week.

## The claiming page has other problems

The same portal's AISSEE 2027 page contradicts verifiable facts:

- Its fee table lists **Rs. 800 / Rs. 650** "based on the 2026 cycle." The actual AISSEE 2026 bulletin set **Rs. 850** for General, OBC (NCL) and Defence wards and **Rs. 700** for SC/ST candidates.
- Its Class 6 age rule says "born between April 1, 2014 and March 31, 2017" — but 10 to 12 years as on 31 March 2027 works out to **April 1, 2015 to March 31, 2017**, exactly as the AISSEE 2026 bulletin structured it.

None of this proves bad faith — education sites routinely publish "expected" pages to rank early. But parents cannot tell an expected-dates page from an official release, and the "Application status: Open" line is where that confusion turns dangerous.

## Why this matters

The real notification will eventually arrive — last year's came on 10 October 2025. In that vacuum, two risks grow: parents may hunt for an apply link that doesn't exist and land on copycat sites, or they may relax — assuming the window is already running — and get caught short when the genuine window opens. The rule is simple: **if it is not on the NTA AISSEE portal, it is not the notification.**

## What to do next

1. Bookmark the official AISSEE page — the bulletin and the apply link will appear under **Public Notices** and **Latest News** first.
2. Keep documents scanned and ready: birth certificate, domicile certificate, category or service certificate, photo and signature.
3. **Do not pay any fee on a third-party website.** When the bulletin is out, fees go only through NTA's own portal.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No. The official NTA AISSEE portal shows no AISSEE 2027 bulletin as of 21 October 2026. A live check found only AISSEE-2026 notices.

**Why do some websites say it was released on 13 October 2026?**
One portal published that claim on 14 October and shows applications as open. Unverified on the official portal.

**Where will the real notification appear first?**
On the official NTA AISSEE page, under Public Notices and Latest News. That is the only source to trust for the release and the apply link.

**What should parents do while waiting?**
Keep the birth certificate, domicile certificate and category or service certificate ready and scanned, and watch the official portal. Never pay a fee on a third-party website.
