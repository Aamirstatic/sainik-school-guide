> **Last verified: 16 November 2026.** The recruitment itself is ✅ **CONFIRMED** — the application form for Nursing Assistant and General Employee is live on the school's official website. But the fee, the last date and the payment mode are 🔶 **UNVERIFIED** from any official source: the notification PDF is not on the school website, and the portals that quote figures disagree with each other. This article tells you exactly what is confirmed, what conflicts, and what to do about it.

Yesterday we reported Sainik School Ambikapur's recruitment for four posts — one Nursing Assistant and three General Employees — and flagged a problem: two sources gave different fees and different last dates. We said to check the official notification on the school website. This morning we went and looked for it properly. It is not there.

That is the news. The school's own website links only to the application portal. The portal asks you to confirm you have read the recruitment notification — but links to no notification. And the form itself, which is live and accepting applications, states no fee amount, no payment mode, no last date and no advertisement number. You are being asked to pay a fee that the official channels do not state.

## The three versions that cannot all be right

Here is what the different sources say, side by side:

| Source | Fee | Last date |
|--------|-----|-----------|
| Odisha TV, reporting the advertisement (14 Nov 2026) | **₹1,500**, non-refundable | 21 days from publication |
| Sarkari Job Alert 24 (advertisement no. SSAMB/Rectt/2026/EN33) | **₹500** Gen/OBC/EWS; **₹250** SC/ST/PwD, by demand draft | **4 December 2026** |
| MySarkariNaukri | Not stated | **6 December 2026** |

Twenty-one days from 14 November works out to **5 December** — a third date again. So depending on which page you believe, the deadline is 4, 5 or 6 December, and the fee is ₹1,500 or ₹500. A ₹1,000 difference and a two-day spread is not a rounding error. It is two different stories.

One detail is worth knowing. The school's previous recruitment notice, from June 2026, charged ₹500 for regular posts. The ₹500 figure on the job portals matches that older notice exactly — so it may simply have been carried over from the old advertisement. That is a guess, not a fact, but it is one more reason not to trust any portal's figure blindly.

## What the school's own site actually shows

We checked the school website, sainikschoolambikapur.org.in, on the morning of 16 November 2026:

- The homepage alert ticker carries an item, "Application for the post of Nursing Assistant and General Employee" — but it links only to the application portal, not to a notification PDF.
- The career section links to the same portal. The downloads section has no recruitment item.
- The portal's declaration page asks applicants to confirm they have read and understood the recruitment notification. It links to no notification.
- The application form lists exactly the two posts, "General Employee" and "Nursing Assistant", and asks for an upload under "Details of recruitment fees paid" — without stating any fee, mode, last date or advertisement number anywhere on the form.
- A search of the school's own domain turns up only one recruitment PDF, and it is the older June 2026 notice for different posts. It is not the EN33 notification.

None of this means the recruitment is fake — the form is genuinely live on the school's own domain, and the posts, pay scales and eligibility we reported yesterday come from the advertisement as reported by Odisha TV. It means the one document that settles the fee and the deadline is not where applicants can read it.

## What to do before you pay anything

1. **Call the school.** The number on the school homepage is **07774-261609**. Ask for the fee, the payment mode and the exact last date for advertisement SSAMB/Rectt/2026/EN33, and note down who you spoke to.
2. **Do not pay ₹1,500 or ₹500 on a portal's word.** The fee is non-refundable in both versions. Money paid on the wrong figure is money lost.
3. **Do not trust any single last date.** If you are eligible, get your documents ready now — nursing diploma or Class 10 certificate, Aadhaar, photograph and signature — so you can apply the moment the school confirms the date.
4. **Apply only through the school website.** The application link is on sainikschoolambikapur.org.in. No agent, no third-party site, no payment link forwarded on WhatsApp.

We will update this story the moment the school publishes the official notification or confirms the figures. Until then, the honest position is the one we have to give you: the job is real, the form is live, and the two numbers that matter most are still not officially confirmed anywhere.

## FAQs

**What is the application fee for Sainik School Ambikapur recruitment 2026?**
It is not settled. Odisha TV, reporting the advertisement, says the registration fee is Rs. 1,500 and non-refundable. One job portal lists Rs. 500 for General, OBC and EWS candidates and Rs. 250 for SC, ST and PwD candidates. The school's own website does not state any fee, so confirm it with the school before paying.

**What is the last date to apply for Sainik School Ambikapur recruitment 2026?**
It is not settled either. The advertisement says applications stay open for 21 days from publication, which works out to 5 December 2026 if the notice appeared on 14 November. Job portals list 4 December and 6 December. Until the school's official notification is available, treat every date as unconfirmed.

**Where is the official notification for Sainik School Ambikapur recruitment?**
As of 16 November 2026, the official notification PDF for advertisement SSAMB/Rectt/2026/EN33 is not linked from the school website's homepage, career section or downloads section, and the application portal links to no notification. The application form itself is live on the school site and lists the two posts.

**How do I apply for the Ambikapur Nursing Assistant and General Employee posts?**
Apply online only, through the application link on the school's official website, sainikschoolambikapur.org.in. But first call the school on the number on its homepage (07774-261609) and confirm the fee, the payment mode and the exact last date. Do not pay any agent or third-party site.
