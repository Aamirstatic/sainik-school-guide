> **Last verified: 2 October 2026, evening.** CONFIRMED by a live check of NTA's official AISSEE portal this evening: no AISSEE 2027 notification, public notice, or application link exists yet.

![Students writing an exam on OMR sheets in an exam hall](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-notification-not-out-portal-check-2026-10-02.webp)

## What happened

Today, several social media reels and WhatsApp forwards told parents that the AISSEE 2027 notification — or even the application form — had dropped on 2 October. It hasn't. We checked NTA's official AISSEE section at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/ this evening, and the portal is still running entirely on the AISSEE 2026 cycle.

The Public Notices tab lists eight items — every single one is for AISSEE 2026. The latest entry is the AISSEE 2026 result declaration (dated 28 May 2026), followed by the final answer keys, counselling notices, OMR sheets, score cards, admit cards, and — right at the bottom — the "Inviting Online Applications for AISSEE 2026" notice from 30 October 2025. The News & Events tab reads "No post to display". The page footer says it was last updated on 23 September 2026.

In short: **nothing for 2027 exists on the official portal yet.** (One note: the older standalone portal aissee.nta.nic.in appears to be unreachable right now — the exams.nta.nic.in AISSEE section above is the working official source.)

## Why it matters

False "form out" claims do real damage. Parents rush to sketchy links, some pay fees to fake websites that impersonate the NTA portal, and families who believe the window has opened start panicking about a deadline that doesn't exist yet. The rumours also create the opposite danger: if a parent believes the form is already out and finds it isn't, they may stop checking — and miss the real announcement when it comes.

## What to do next

The only check that matters takes thirty seconds:

1. Open the official AISSEE section: **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/**
2. Look at the **Public Notices** tab.
3. If you don't see a notice titled something like "Inviting Online Applications for AISSEE 2027", the form isn't out — whatever a reel, a forward, or a neighbour says.

Bookmark that page (not a YouTube channel, not a WhatsApp group). And keep your documents ready — when the form does open, last year it stayed open for only about three to four weeks (10 October to 9 November 2025, extended), so the prepared families will have the advantage.

## FAQs

**Is the AISSEE 2027 application form out?**

No. As of the evening of 2 October 2026, NTA has released no notification or application link for AISSEE 2027. The official portal still shows only AISSEE 2026 content.

**Where do I check the official AISSEE 2027 status myself?**

Check the official AISSEE section at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/ — look at the Public Notices tab. An AISSEE 2027 notification would appear there before any YouTube or WhatsApp forward.

**When will the AISSEE 2027 form release?**

NTA has not announced a date. Based on the previous cycle, when the form opened on 10 October 2025, an October 2026 release is the pattern-based expectation — but it is EXPECTED, not confirmed.

**A reel or forward said the form is out today. Should I believe it?**

No — verify on the official NTA portal first. Every season sees fake AISSEE form links and false release-date claims circulating on social media. The official portal's Public Notices tab is the only confirmation that counts.
