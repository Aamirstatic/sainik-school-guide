> **Last verified: 9 October 2026 (evening).** We opened the official NTA AISSEE portal ourselves this evening. The page footer reads "Last Updated: Oct 09, 2026" — and the page still shows **no AISSEE 2027 notification, no application link, and no 2027 dates**. Everything below is what the portal actually displays right now, plus pattern-based expectations clearly marked **EXPECTED**.

![Study planner and calendar marking the AISSEE 2027 application window](/images/inbody/nta-portal-no-aissee-2027-link-evening-check-2026-10-09.webp)

## What the portal shows tonight

If you've been refreshing the NTA AISSEE page all week hoping the 2027 link quietly appeared, here is the honest answer: it hasn't. This evening's live check of exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society found:

- **Latest News carousel:** a single item — "Score Card for AISSEE – 2026". Nothing for 2027.
- **Public Notices tab:** every entry is from the 2026 cycle — the AISSEE 2026 result declaration, final answer keys for Class VI and IX, the AISSAC 2026 counselling notice, the admit-card and exam-city intimation notices from last January.
- **Candidate Activity section:** score card, admit card, city intimation, and the OMR instructions — all labelled AISSEE–2026.
- **News & Events tab:** "No post to display."
- **Dates visible anywhere:** only 18.01.2026, last year's exam date.

So the notification genuinely isn't out — and it's not hiding in some corner of the site either. **CONFIRMED** by direct observation today.

## Why this actually matters tonight

Last year, the AISSEE 2026 form opened on **10 October 2025** — tomorrow's date, exactly one year ago. That's why this week feels so tense: the 2026-cycle window ran 10–30 October 2025, and every quiet day now pushes the probable exam calendar slightly forward. (We tracked this countdown in detail earlier this week — see [how many days are left and what the delay means](/news/aissee-2027-notification-not-out-115-days-2026-10-08/).) None of this is an NTA announcement; it's **EXPECTED** from the pattern.

But here's the thing — a quiet portal is not bad news. NTA has not announced any delay, and there's no official statement of any kind about AISSEE 2027 yet. Panic-reading every education blog's "expected date" table won't move the process along. What will help is using this weekend properly.

## Where the link will appear first

When NTA does move, the 2027 link will almost certainly surface in this order:

1. **The AISSEE portal homepage** — the "Latest News" carousel and the "Candidate Activity" section, where last year's application and admit-card links sat.
2. **nta.ac.in** — the main NTA site's "Latest" notices feed.
3. Reputable education desks (Times of India, edexlive, Careers360) within hours.

Bookmark the portal, check it once a day — morning is enough — and stop refreshing it every hour. The evening check is on us now; we'll keep watching.

## Your weekend prep list (do this before the link drops)

The 2026 window was only 20 days long. When the 2027 form opens, you don't want to be the parent hunting for a thumb impression scan at midnight. Get these ready this weekend:

- [ ] **Photo, signature, left-thumb impression** — scanned in JPG, within the size limits NTA specified last year (photo 10–200 KB, signature and thumb 10–50 KB). **EXPECTED** to repeat. Full specs in our [AISSEE 2027 documents checklist](/news/aissee-2027-application-documents-checklist-2026-10-06/).
- [ ] **Birth certificate** — confirms the age window (born 1 Apr 2015–31 Mar 2017 for Class 6; 1 Apr 2012–31 Mar 2014 for Class 9 — **PROJECTED** from the 2026 bulletin).
- [ ] **Domicile certificate** — home-state quota is 67% of seats; you'll need proof.
- [ ] **Category certificate** (SC/ST/OBC-NCL), if applicable — must be in the prescribed format and valid on the application date.
- [ ] **Fee ready for online payment** — ₹850 (Gen/OBC/Defence wards) or ₹700 (SC/ST) **EXPECTED** at 2026 rates; keep a working debit card/UPI handy.
- [ ] **Decide your exam-city preference now**, not on form day. Last year there were around 190 cities — pick the one you'd genuinely travel to.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No. As of the evening of 9 October 2026, the NTA AISSEE portal shows no 2027 notification, application link, or dates. Everything visible on the portal still belongs to the completed AISSEE 2026 cycle.

**Where will the AISSEE 2027 application link appear first?**
On the NTA AISSEE portal itself (exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society) — the "Latest News" carousel and the "Candidate Activity" section are where last year's link appeared. nta.ac.in carries it next.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 form expected?**
October 2026 is the expected window based on the 2026 cycle, when the form opened on 10 October 2025. That is pattern-based projection, not an NTA announcement.

**What should I keep ready before the AISSEE 2027 form opens?**
Scanned photo, signature, left-thumb impression in the specified sizes, your child's birth certificate, domicile certificate, and category certificate if applicable, plus the application fee ready for online payment.
