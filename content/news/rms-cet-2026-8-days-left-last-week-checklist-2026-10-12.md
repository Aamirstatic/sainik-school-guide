## Eight days left — and the clock has a hard stop

The National Testing Agency opened the Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026 on **23 September**, and the window closes on **20 October 2026** — applications up to 5:00 pm, fee payment up to 11:50 pm the same day. From today, you have eight days.

Yes, there is a late-fee window — **21 to 25 October** with an extra **Rs 500** — but here's my honest advice after years of watching these NTA cycles: do not plan for it. The late window is a rescue rope, not a strategy. It exists for the family that had a genuine emergency, not for the one who felt they had plenty of time. Treat 20 October as the real deadline and finish this week.

## Your last-week checklist

**1. Check eligibility before you log in.** Class 6 candidates must have passed or be studying in Class 5; Class 9 candidates must have passed Class 8 from a government or recognised school. Age matters too: Class 6 must be **10 to 13 years** as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2014 to 31 March 2017); Class 9 must be **13 to 16 years** (born 1 April 2011 to 31 March 2014). Note the wider window compared to AISSEE — a 13-year-old too old for AISSEE Class 6 can still apply here.

**2. Ready the documents first.** Birth certificate, a government photo ID, a recent photograph and signature (per the specifications in the official information bulletin), and the previous class marksheet or study certificate. Fill the form only when the folder is complete — half-filled forms saved as drafts are how details get entered wrong.

**3. Pay the right fee the same day.** **Rs 1,000** for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates; **Rs 750** for SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action (KIA). Payment goes through the online gateway on the NTA RMS CET portal — complete it the same day you fill the form, and **save the confirmation page and the final submitted application** as PDFs.

**4. Don't rely on the correction window.** NTA's schedule includes a brief correction window EXPECTED around **27–28 October** — but it is meant for small fixes, not for completing a half-filled form or paying a missed fee. Assume it won't save you.

**5. Finish on a working day, not the weekend.** NTA portals historically slow down on the last two days as thousands of families log in together. Aim to submit by **18 October** (Sunday) at the latest.

## What you're applying for

RMS CET 2026 decides admission to **Class 6 and Class 9** in the five Rashtriya Military Schools — Chail (Himachal Pradesh), Ajmer and Dholpur (Rajasthan), Belgaum and Bengaluru (Karnataka).

The written test is on **13 December 2026 (Sunday)** — a pen-and-paper OMR test with multiple-choice questions, conducted across **63 cities**. For many families this is the parallel track to AISSEE 2027: the same December exam season (RIMC on 6 December, RMS on 13 December, UP Sainik School on 20 December), and preparing for both is not double work. See our [December exam-season guide](/news/december-exam-gauntlet-rimcee-rms-up-sainik-2026-10-06/) for the combined plan.

20 October 2026 — applications up to 5:00 pm, fee payment up to 11:50 pm. The late-fee window (extra Rs 500) runs 21 to 25 October 2026, applications up to 5:00 pm.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**

13 December 2026 (Sunday). It is a pen-and-paper OMR test with multiple-choice questions, held across 63 exam cities.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 application fee?**

Rs 1,000 for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates; Rs 750 for SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action (KIA). Late-window applicants pay an additional Rs 500.
