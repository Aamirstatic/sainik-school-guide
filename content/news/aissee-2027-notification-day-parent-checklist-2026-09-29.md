> **Last verified: 29 September 2026, 9:15 PM IST.** This is a **parent explainer**, not breaking news — NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 notification as of today. Everything below is what to verify *on notification day*, drawn from the 2026 cycle's bulletin. Treat all figures as **EXPECTED until the official notice confirms them**.

October begins on Thursday, and if the last three cycles are any guide, this is the month the AISSEE 2027 form opens. Last year the entire window lasted **20 days** (10 October to 30 October 2025, later extended to 9 November) — and the families who suffered were the ones who filled the form in a hurry and found a wrong date of birth or an expired category certificate too late to fix cleanly.

Here are the ten things to check **before** you submit, not after.

![A parent planning on a study calendar before an exam application](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-notification-day-parent-checklist-2026-09-29.webp)

## 1. Your child's age on the cut-off date

**EXPECTED (2026 cycle):** 10–12 years for Class VI and 13–15 years for Class IX as of 31 March. Even a one-day miss disqualifies. Count from the official date of birth certificate — not the school diary, not memory.

## 2. The qualifying class

**CONFIRMED (pattern):** Class VI applicants must have passed Class V; Class IX applicants must have passed Class VIII. Check the exact wording in the bulletin — "passed" versus "appearing" matters.

## 3. Category certificate validity

An OBC-NCL certificate older than the bulletin's specified cut-off is the most common rejection trigger. **EXPECTED:** the bulletin will name a date by which the certificate must be valid. If yours predates it, get it renewed *this week* — renewals take longer than you think.

## 4. Domicile and the home-state quota

**CONFIRMED (established rule):** 67% of seats go to candidates from the state where the school is located, 33% to other states. Your domicile decides which quota your child competes in — and it also shapes school choice strategy.

## 5. The full school list

The 2026 cycle covered existing Sainik Schools **and** approved New Sainik Schools, including the 40%/60% admission route ([afternoon update](https://sainikschooleastsiang.in/news/new-sainik-schools-40-60-route-explained-2026-09-29/)). Read the participating-schools list carefully — new schools get added every cycle.

## 6. Photo, signature and thumb impression specs

The portal rejects uploads that don't match the specified size, format and background. **Scan before the form opens:** photo, signature, thumb impression, DOB, domicile and category certificates ([full documents guide](https://sainikschooleastsiang.in/news/aissee-2027-documents-ready-before-notification-2026-09-28/)).

## 7. The fee and payment mode

**EXPECTED (2026 cycle):** Rs 850 (General) and Rs 700 (SC/ST), paid online. Save the payment receipt — a deducted-but-unconfirmed payment is the classic last-day nightmare.

## 8. Exam city preferences

You will pick exam cities in order of preference. Choose cities you can realistically reach on **31 January 2027** — the **CONFIRMED tentative** exam date per NTA's 16 September 2026 examination calendar — not aspirational ones.

## 9. The correction window dates

NTA typically opens a short correction window after applications close (December in the 2026 cycle). Note the dates, but do not rely on it: date of birth, category and school choices are often only partially editable or not editable at all.

## 10. The confirmation page

After submission, download and **print** the confirmation page — it is the only proof your application exists. If the site shows a pending status, resolve it the same day.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**

No. As of 29 September 2026, NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 notification or information bulletin on aissee.nta.nic.in. The checks in this article are for the day it drops.

**What was the AISSEE application fee in the last cycle?**

In the 2026 cycle, the fee was Rs 850 for General candidates and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates. The 2027 fee must be re-confirmed in the new information bulletin.

**What happens if I make a mistake in the AISSEE form?**

NTA usually opens a correction window after the application closes — in the 2026 cycle it ran in December 2025. But not every field is editable, so date of birth and category should be right the first time.

**How long is the AISSEE application window usually?**

Very short. In the 2026 cycle it ran from 10 October to 30 October 2025 — just 20 days — with a later extension to 9 November. Do not plan to apply in the last week.
