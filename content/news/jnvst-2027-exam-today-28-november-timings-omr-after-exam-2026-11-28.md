Today is the day. After months of preparation, the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) for Class 6 is being held this morning across the country, for admission to Class 6 in the 2027–28 session. If your child is sitting it, the work is done — this morning is about getting to the right room, on time, with the right things in hand. Here's everything that matters, in the order you'll need it.

## This morning, minute by minute

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Before you leave | Check the centre address on the printed admit card — not from memory, not from a neighbour's child. Agree on a meeting point for after the exam. |
| By 11:00 AM | Your child must be **seated in the allotted classroom**. This is the hard deadline, and latecomers cannot take the test. |
| 11:30 AM | The paper begins. A bell rings every 30 minutes. |
| 1:30 PM | The paper ends. Pupils **cannot leave early** — they stay in the room until their allotted time is over. |
| ~1:45 PM | Pickup. Allow a few minutes for your child to come out and reach the agreed meeting place. |

One practical point: 11:00 AM is the seating time, not the time to reach the school gate. Count backwards — the journey, the walk from the gate, finding the room. If the admit card prints an earlier reporting time, follow that instead.

## What to carry — and what to leave behind

The list is short, and that's the point. A printed admit card and a working blue or black ballpoint pen. Take a spare pen too — a pen that runs out halfway through a two-hour paper is a problem you can prevent for the price of one extra pen.

What not to carry matters just as much. Pencil marks are not allowed on the OMR sheet, even if your child plans to go over them in pen later. Gel pens and sketch pens are out too. And leave correction fluid and whiteners at home — once a circle is darkened, it cannot be erased, crossed out or covered up to pick another answer. A response changed that way will not be evaluated. For bags, watches and phones, follow whatever the admit card itself says.

If you still need the admit card this morning, download it now from [navodaya.gov.in](https://navodaya.gov.in) or the NVS admission portal at cbseitms.rcil.gov.in/nvs, using the registration number and your child's date of birth. NVS does not send admit cards by post. Our [admit-card checklist from yesterday](/news/jnvst-2027-exam-tomorrow-28-november-admit-card-day-checklist-2026-11-27/) has the full download steps and the details to check on the card.

## The paper itself: 80 questions, 100 marks, no negative marking

| Section | Questions | Marks | Suggested time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mental Ability Test + Environmental Studies | 40 | 50 | 60 minutes |
| Arithmetic Test | 20 | 25 | 30 minutes |
| Language Test | 20 | 25 | 30 minutes |
| **Total** | **80** | **100** | **2 hours** |

Every correct answer earns 1.25 marks, and there is no negative marking — a wrong answer costs nothing. That changes the strategy: a question your child cannot solve is still worth a guess, but only after the questions they know are safely marked. The suggested split above comes from NVS itself; your child can adjust it, but the discipline is the same — if one arithmetic problem is eating minutes, move on and come back.

Two checks in the first minute, before answering anything. First, is the question booklet in the language your child chose while applying? If not, raise a hand and ask for the correct booklet before the test begins. Second, does the number on the booklet match the number on the OMR sheet? Report any mismatch to the invigilator straight away.

And the OMR habit worth one minute of practice at the breakfast table: darken the circle fully, in the row that matches the question number. If your child skips question 19 and answers question 20, the mark goes in row 20 — row 19 stays blank, and they come back to it later. Misaligned rows are the silliest way to lose marks in this exam, and they happen every year to children who knew the answers.

## After 1:30 PM: what happens next

When the paper is over, the waiting begins — and it is a long one. The result is expected in **March 2027**, and will be published on navodaya.gov.in and displayed at the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, the District Education Officer's office and the District Magistrate's office. Selected candidates are also informed by SMS and speed post. Keep the admit card safe until admission is complete; you will need the details on it again.

One thing to settle today, while it's fresh: how did the paper go, section by section? Not to relitigate answers — to know, honestly, whether the Arithmetic section or the Language section needs work if a second attempt at a residential-school entrance is on the cards. Many of today's children are also preparing for AISSEE 2027, whose exam is on Sunday, 31 January 2027 — and the form for that closes on Wednesday, 2 December. Our guide to [what happens after the JNVST exam](/news/jnvst-2027-after-28-november-exam-answer-key-result-documents-2026-11-25/) covers answer keys, the result and the documents to keep ready, and our [Navodaya vs Sainik School comparison](/blog/navodaya-vs-sainik-school-which-to-choose-after-clearing-both/) helps if your child ends up with a choice to make.

Good luck to every child sitting the paper this morning. The preparation is done. Now it's just two hours, one circle at a time.

## FAQs

**What time does the JNVST 2027 exam start today?**
Pupils must be seated in their allotted classrooms by 11:00 AM today, Saturday 28 November 2026. The paper itself runs from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, with no break. Arrive early enough to find the room — the admit card's reporting time takes priority if it says earlier.

**What should my child carry to the JNVST exam centre today?**
A printed admit card and a blue or black ballpoint pen, plus a spare pen. NVS does not send admit cards by post, so the printout is mandatory for entry. Check the admit card for any other item it lists.

**Is there negative marking in the JNVST Class 6 exam?**
No. Each correct answer earns 1.25 marks and a wrong answer costs nothing, so it is worth attempting every question. The paper has 80 questions for 100 marks in total.

**When will the JNVST 2027 result come?**
The result is expected in March 2027, and will be published on navodaya.gov.in and displayed at the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, the District Education Officer's office and the District Magistrate's office. Selected candidates are also informed by SMS and speed post.
