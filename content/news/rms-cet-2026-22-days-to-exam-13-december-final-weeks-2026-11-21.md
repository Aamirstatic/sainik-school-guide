## Three Sundays left. That is the whole plan

The RMS CET 2026 written exam is on **Sunday, 13 December 2026**. Today is Saturday, 21 November. Count the Sundays between now and the exam: 22 November, 29 November, 6 December — three full Sundays of preparation, then the exam itself on the fourth.

That is actually a comfortable amount of time, if you use it properly. Three weeks is enough to finish the syllabus, take two full-length mocks under exam timing, and fix the two or three weak spots that keep costing marks. It is not enough to start a new book or panic-buy a crash course. At this stage, the children who improve most are the ones who stop adding material and start finishing what they have.

## The paper, in one place

Both papers are pen-and-paper OMR tests of **2 hours 30 minutes**, with no negative marking. The Class 6 paper is bilingual (English and Hindi); the Class 9 paper is in English only.

| | Class 6 | Class 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper** | 200 MCQs, 200 marks | Two papers, 200 marks total |
| **Sections** | English 50, Intelligence 50, Maths 50, GK & Current Affairs 50 | Paper 1: English 50, Hindi 20, Social Science 30; Paper 2: Maths 50, Science 50 |
| **Qualifying marks** | English 35%; Intelligence, Maths, GK 40% each | 50% in each paper |
| **English counts in merit?** | No — qualifying only | Yes, as part of Paper 1 |
| **Interview after written test** | 20 marks | 50 marks |
| **Standard** | Class 5 level | Class 8 (NCERT) level |

One detail parents keep missing: in Class 6, the English section is qualifying only. A child can score full marks in English and it will not add a single mark to the merit list — but scoring below 35% in it ends the run entirely. So English deserves steady practice, not obsession. The merit list is decided by Intelligence, Maths and GK — 150 marks between them.

## The three weeks

**Week 1 — 23 to 29 November: finish, don't start.** Close the syllabus. Every chapter your child has "almost finished" should be finished this week. One full mock on Sunday 29 November, in the exact 2.5-hour sitting. And watch for the city intimation slip: the bulletin says it comes 10 to 15 days before the exam, so the window opens around **28 November**. The slip only tells you the exam city — no centre address, no reporting time — but it settles the travel question.

**Week 2 — 30 November to 6 December: mocks and the admit card.** Two full mocks this week, with at least one under the real morning timing. Analyse each mock the same evening: which section leaked marks, and was it knowledge or time? The admit card is expected around **6 to 8 December**, 5 to 7 days before the exam, on exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet. The moment it lands, check the name, date of birth, photo, centre address and reporting time — and print two copies.

**Week 3 — 7 to 13 December: taper, don't cram.** No new topics. Short revision of formulas, GK notes and the mistakes diary from the mocks. One light mock early in the week, then nothing heavy after Wednesday. Sleep properly on Saturday night — a tired child loses more marks than an unprepared one.

## What happens after the exam

The Stage-I result, with answer keys, is expected in **January 2027**. Qualified children then go through medical examinations and interviews in **February–March 2027**, with joining on **1 April 2027**. All of those dates are expected, not announced — NTA will confirm each stage on the RMS CET portal. Keep the application number and password safe until the whole process is over; every stage uses them.

If your child is appearing for RMS CET, do three small things this weekend: mark Sunday, 13 December on the family calendar, check that the NTA RMS CET portal login works, and agree the three Sundays — one mock each Sunday, no negotiation. The rest of the plan follows from that.

## FAQs

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**

Sunday, 13 December 2026. It is a pen-and-paper OMR test of 2 hours 30 minutes, held for Class 6 and Class 9 entry to the five Rashtriya Military Schools — Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belagavi and Bengaluru.

**When will the RMS CET 2026 city slip and admit card be released?**

NTA has not announced fixed dates. The information bulletin says the city intimation slip comes 10 to 15 days before the exam, which points to around 28 November to 3 December, and the admit card 5 to 7 days before, around 6 to 8 December. Both are expected dates, not announced ones — check exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet.

**What is the RMS CET Class 6 paper pattern?**

Four sections of 50 marks each: English, Intelligence Test, Mathematics, and General Knowledge and Current Affairs — 200 questions, 200 marks, in 2 hours 30 minutes. English is qualifying only (35 percent needed) and does not count in the merit list; the other three sections need 40 percent each. There is no negative marking, and a 20-mark interview follows the written test.

**What is the RMS CET Class 9 paper pattern?**

Two papers of 100 marks each: Paper 1 is English (50), Hindi (20) and Social Science (30); Paper 2 is Mathematics (50) and Science (50). Each paper needs 50 percent to qualify. A 50-mark interview follows the written test, and the Class 9 paper is in English only.
