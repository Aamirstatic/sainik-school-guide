## Nine days to the normal-fee deadline

The Uttar Pradesh Sainik Schools Society is taking online applications for the **UP Sainik Schools Entrance Examination 2027-28** for Classes 6 and 9 at **upsainikschool.org** — the portal the Society itself runs. If you have not started yet, here is the arithmetic: **9 days remain** before the no-late-fee deadline of **25 October 2026, 5 pm**.

Miss that date and you can still apply until **31 October 2026, 5 pm**, but every form filed after the 25th carries a **late fee of Rs 2,000**. A correction window for eligible details runs **3 to 5 November 2026, until 5 pm**. The entrance exam itself is **tentatively scheduled for 20 December 2026**.

## The rule most parents will read too late

Buried in the reporting on the portal's instructions today is the detail that actually matters more than the deadline: **the information you enter during registration cannot be edited after successful registration, and only one registration is permitted per mobile number.**

Read that twice, because it changes how you should approach the form:

1. **Do a dry run on paper first.** Write down the child's name exactly as on the birth certificate, the date of birth, category, and the school choice before touching the portal.
2. **Use a number you control.** The mobile number receives the login credentials. A relative's number that changes in six months is a future problem — use the parent's own active number.
3. **Save the credentials immediately.** With no second registration allowed on the same number, a lost password is a mess you cannot paper over by starting fresh.

The correction window (3–5 November) covers *eligible details* in the application form per the Society's instructions — it is a safety net, not a plan. Get it right the first time.

## What the exam leads to

The entrance examination is for admission to Classes 6 and 9 at the UP Sainik Schools — Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow, and Sainik School, Gorakhpur. The official website also hosts the school prospectus, general instructions in English and Hindi, a sample OMR answer sheet and a sample question paper — download the sample paper; it is the closest thing to an official blueprint you will get.

## What to do tonight

If the UP Sainik route is on your list, do not wait for the weekend. Photograph and signature scans, the birth certificate, and a working email ID take an evening to assemble — and the portal gets slower as the 25th approaches, as it does every year.

## FAQs

**What is the last date for the UP Sainik School 2027-28 application without late fee?**

25 October 2026, by 5 pm. Forms submitted until 31 October 2026, 5 pm, are accepted with a late fee of Rs 2,000.

**Can I register twice on the UP Sainik School portal with the same mobile number?**

No. The official portal permits only one registration per mobile number, and details entered during registration cannot be edited after it is completed. Keep the login credentials safe.

**Is there a correction window for the UP Sainik School 2027-28 form?**

Yes. The application correction window runs from 3 November to 5 November 2026, until 5 pm, for eligible details in the application form.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam for 2027-28?**

The examination is tentatively scheduled for 20 December 2026. The date is tentative — the official portal is the final authority for any change.
