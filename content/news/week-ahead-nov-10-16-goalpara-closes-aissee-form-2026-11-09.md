## One deadline this week — and it is a postal one

The week of 10–16 November has a single hard deadline, and it belongs to job-seekers, not students. Applications for the 13 teaching and non-teaching posts at **Sainik School Goalpara (Assam) must reach the school by Thursday, 12 November 2026**. This is an offline recruitment: the form goes by registered or speed post, or by hand delivery, to the Principal at PO Rajapara, Goalpara, Assam – 783133. The notification is dated 3 October 2026, and the school has been clear that the application must be *received* by 12 November — posting it on the 12th is not enough.

If you are applying, do not trust the post this week. Send it today, or better, deliver it by hand if you are anywhere near Goalpara. The fee is Rs. 300 for General candidates and Rs. 200 for others, paid by demand draft. Full post details are in our [Goalpara recruitment — 13 posts, now closed](/news/sainik-school-goalpara-recruitment-closed-what-happens-next-2026-11-12/).

## AISSEE 2027: the form window keeps running

For parents, the week's real work is the AISSEE 2027 form. Applications opened on 3 November and stay open until **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**. That is 23 days from today — comfortable, but the families who leave it to the last week are the ones who hit payment failures and photo rejections at midnight.

If the form is already submitted, there is nothing to do this week except save the confirmation page and check the details once against the birth certificate. The late-fee window (Rs. 500 extra) runs 3–6 December, the correction window 7–9 December, the exam city is announced on 11 January 2027, the admit card comes on 25 January 2027, and the exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. Before you submit, read our [7 mistakes that get forms rejected](/news/aissee-2027-form-mistakes-to-avoid-before-you-submit-2026-11-04/) and the [full post-submission timeline](/news/aissee-2027-after-you-submit-correction-admit-card-timeline-2026-11-04/).

## The December exam cluster is now under seven weeks away

No defence-school exam falls this week, but the calendar tightens fast after it. JNVST Class 6 is on **28 November**, RIMCEE on **6 December**, RMS CET on **13 December**, and the UP Sainik School entrance exam on **20 December**. Children appearing for more than one of these — and many do — should treat this week as the last calm one: finish the syllabus gaps now, because December leaves no room for new topics.

Two more dates sit just beyond this week: Sainik School Nagrota's recruitment closes on 20 November, and Sainik School Purulia holds its walk-in interviews on 23 November.

## FAQs

**What is the last date for Sainik School Goalpara recruitment?**
12 November 2026. Offline applications must reach the Principal, Sainik School Goalpara, by that date — postal delay is the applicant's own risk, so send it today or deliver it by hand.

**When does the AISSEE 2027 application window close?**
2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM. A late-fee window of Rs. 500 runs from 3 to 6 December, and the correction window is 7 to 9 December 2026.

**Which defence-school exams are in December 2026?**
RIMCEE on 6 December, RMS CET on 13 December, and the UP Sainik School entrance exam on 20 December. JNVST Class 6 comes first, on 28 November 2026.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**
Sunday, 31 January 2027. It is a pen-and-paper OMR test — 150 minutes for Class 6 and 180 minutes for Class 9 — with no negative marking.
