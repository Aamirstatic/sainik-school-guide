## Friday 20 November: Nagrota closes — and "posted" is not "received"

Tomorrow is the last day for the Sainik School Nagrota recruitment — three posts (one regular TGT Computer Science, one contractual Art Master, one contractual Nursing Sister), and the application must *reach* the Principal by Speed Post on or before 20 November. Posting it tomorrow is not enough; the school has said late applications will be rejected and it will not be responsible for postal delays. If the envelope is not already on its way, send it today. We covered this recruitment in detail on [8 November](/news/sainik-school-nagrota-recruitment-3-posts-last-date-nov-20-2026-11-08/) and [11 November](/news/sainik-school-nagrota-recruitment-3-posts-apply-by-nov-20-2026-11-11/) — the fee is ₹500, and the selection process is tentatively set for December 2026 or January 2027.

## Wednesday 25 November: two things land the same day

First, the **Purulia PRT walk-in interview** — four contractual Primary Teacher posts at Sainik School Purulia, reporting at 8:00 AM, on a consolidated pay of ₹16,000 a month. It is a walk-in, so there is no form to submit in advance, but carry the documents the advertisement asks for and reach the campus early. Our [18 November piece](/news/sainik-school-purulia-prt-walk-in-25-november-2026-11-18/) has the full details.

Second, the **UP Sainik School admit card** is released after 25 November on upsainikschool.org, through the candidate login. The exam itself is on Sunday, 20 December 2026 — a single 200-mark OMR paper in two and a half hours, for Class 6 and Class 9 at Lucknow and Gorakhpur. Download the card the day it appears, print two copies, and check the name, photograph, date of birth, exam centre and reporting time against the original documents. If anything is wrong, contact the UP Sainik Schools Society at once — do not wait for exam week. The full timeline, including the tentative answer key around 24 December and the written result around 30 December, is in our [18 November piece](/news/up-sainik-school-admit-card-after-25-november-32-days-to-exam-2026-11-18/).

## Saturday 28 November: JNVST exam day

Nine days from today, the JNVST Class 6 exam runs from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM across the country. The admit card has been available since 28 October on the NVS portal — if you have not downloaded it yet, do it tonight, not on the 27th. Check the centre address and the language of the paper, and confirm the child knows the route. Our [9-day final-week plan](/blog/jnvst-2027-9-days-left-final-week-plan-class-6/) covers what to revise and what to stop doing in these last days, and our [13 November piece](/news/jnvst-2027-admit-card-7-details-to-check-15-days-to-exam-2026-11-13/) lists the seven details to check on the card.

## Just beyond the week: RIMCEE, RMS CET, and the AISSEE deadline

The **RIMCEE city slip is already out** — NTA released it on 17 November — and the admit card is expected around 29 November to 1 December, five to seven days before the 6 December exam. The **RMS CET city slip** is expected around 29 November, with the admit card around 6–8 December, before the 13 December exam. Both dates are expected, not announced, so keep checking exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE and exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet rather than trusting any forwarded message.

And the date that frames everything else: the **AISSEE 2027 form closes on Wednesday, 2 December at 11:50 PM** — thirteen days from today. After that, the late window (3–6 December, ₹500 extra) and the correction window (7–9 December) are the only safety nets, and neither fixes everything. If the form is still unsubmitted, this week is the week to finish it — our [14-days-left countdown](/news/aissee-2027-14-days-left-apply-2-december-deadline-2026-11-18/) and our [correction-window audit guide](/news/aissee-2027-correction-window-7-9-december-audit-confirmation-page-2026-11-18/) tell you exactly what to check before you hit submit.

## FAQs

**What are the big dates in the week of 20–26 November 2026?**

The Sainik School Nagrota recruitment closes on 20 November, the Purulia PRT walk-in interview is on 25 November, and the UP Sainik School admit card is released after 25 November. The JNVST Class 6 exam follows just after the week, on Saturday, 28 November 2026.

**When does the AISSEE 2027 form close?**

2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM, without a late fee. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra fee of Rs. 500, and the correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026. The exam is on Sunday, 31 January 2027.

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam, and is the admit card out?**

Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM. Yes — the admit card has been available since 28 October 2026 on the NVS admission portal linked from navodaya.gov.in. Download it with the registration number and the child's date of birth, and check the centre details the same day.

**When do the RIMCEE and RMS CET admit cards come?**

No dates have been announced. The RIMCEE admit card is expected around 29 November to 1 December, about 5 to 7 days before the 6 December exam, and the RMS CET admit card around 6 to 8 December, before the 13 December exam. Treat both as expected dates, not announced ones, and keep checking the NTA portals.
