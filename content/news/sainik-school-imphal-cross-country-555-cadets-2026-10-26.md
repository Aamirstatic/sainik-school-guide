## 555 cadets, three distances, one house title

Sainik School Imphal held its Inter-House Cross Country Competition for the academic session 2026-27 on Saturday, 25 October 2026. A total of 555 cadets took part — 491 boys and 64 girls — racing in three categories: 3 km for Sub-Junior cadets, 6 km for Junior cadets and 11 km for Senior cadets. Tagore House emerged as the overall champions.

Cross-country is one of those Sainik School traditions that tells parents more about the place than any prospectus paragraph. It is not a showcase event for a handful of athletes. When 555 cadets line up, the race is the whole school — every house, every age group, boys and girls together — and the house trophy is decided by depth, not by one star runner.

## Who won each race

The Sangai Express reports the following category winners:

| Category | Distance | Winner |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-Junior boys | 3 km | Cadet Khaidem Motilal |
| Junior boys | 6 km | Cadet Boyesh Mayanglambam |
| Junior girls | 6 km | Cadet Cham Cham Oinam |
| Senior boys | 11 km | Cadet Seitamjit Thongram |
| Senior girls | 11 km | Cadet Linthoinganbi Sharungbam |
| Overall house champions | — | Tagore House |

Note what the results show: 64 girl cadets ran, and girls' winners are named in both the Junior and Senior categories — a concrete answer to what life is like for girl cadets.

## Why this matters to an AISSEE parent

If your child is preparing for AISSEE 2027, read this as a preview, not just as news. A Sainik School day is built around physical training alongside academics. Our guide to the [house system](/blog/sainik-school-house-system-what-it-means-2026-10-25/) explains how house rivalries shape daily life, and the [PT and games routine](/blog/pt-drill-games-physical-routine-sainik-school-2026-10-21/) shows what a typical week of physical activity looks like.

Three practical takeaways for aspirants:

- **Stamina is built, not assumed.** An 11 km Senior race is the end of a long build-up. Children with a basic running habit settle into the PT routine faster.
- **House life rewards participation.** Tagore House won as a house, so points came from across the categories. Turning up and finishing counts for your house.
- **Girls compete on the same stage.** The named girls' winners at Junior and Senior level are a useful, concrete answer to the question many parents ask about what life is actually like for girl cadets.
