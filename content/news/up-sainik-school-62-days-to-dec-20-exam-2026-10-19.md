## The countdown, in numbers

62 days sounds generous. It is not — December arrives fast, and the last two weeks will be swallowed by revision and admit cards. Here is what the stretch actually looks like:

| Stretch | What it is | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| This week | Normal-fee application closes (25 Oct, 5 pm); late window 26–31 Oct | 31 Oct |
| Weeks 2–3 | Correction window (3–5 Nov); study at full intensity | mid-Nov |
| Weeks 4–7 | The real preparation block: 4 full weeks of timed practice | mid-Dec |
| Weeks 8–9 | Admit card (after 25 Nov); revision + one mock per week | 20 Dec exam |

**Nine weeks is roughly two months of usable study** once the form-filling and correction chaos is subtracted. If your child has not applied yet, the form comes first — everything else waits behind it.

## What the paper looks like

The UP Sainik entrance exam is a written paper followed, for shortlisted candidates, by a medical examination and interview. The written paper covers **English, General Knowledge, Mathematics, and Intelligence** — the same four families of sections every Sainik School aspirant already studies. Press reports describe an OMR-based objective paper of 200 marks running about two and a half hours, with section-wise qualifying marks, but coaching-site numbers vary, so **treat the exact marks split as something to confirm from the official prospectus and the sample question paper**, both of which the UP Sainik Schools portal publishes.

One detail worth knowing early: the medical examination and interview stage comes *after* the written paper, not alongside it (tentatively February 2027). Strong written scores get you through the door; fitness and temperament get you the seat.

## What to finish this week

Pick five, not fifteen. The goal for this week is momentum, not syllabus completion:

1. **Submit the application — or schedule the exact hour you will.** Normal fee till 25 October, 5 pm. The late window costs Rs 1,000 extra and buys you nothing but delay.
2. **Download the official prospectus and the sample paper** from upsainikschool.org. Read the instructions the way you would read a board exam paper — once, carefully.
3. **Fix the study clock to the exam's shape.** The paper is a timed objective test; start solving everything with a timer from now on, not from December.
4. **Give Mathematics the biggest slice.** Maths plus Intelligence typically decide the merit list in these papers — daily timed sums beat weekend marathon sessions.
5. **Start a one-page GK notebook.** Defence facts, national symbols, states and capitals, recent awards — small daily doses, not bulk reading.

## FAQs

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam for 2027-28 admission?**
20 December 2026, at centres across Uttar Pradesh. The date is confirmed across multiple education portals quoting the UP Sainik Schools Society admission schedule.

**Can I still apply for the UP Sainik School 2027-28 exam?**
Yes. The normal-fee window (Rs 1,000) runs till 25 October 2026, 5 pm, and a late-fee window (Rs 2,000) runs from 26 to 31 October 2026, 5 pm. Apply only at upsainikschool.org.

**Which schools does the UP Sainik entrance exam cover?**
Two state-run schools: Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School in Lucknow and UP Sainik School in Gorakhpur, for Class 6 and Class 9 admission.

**Is the UP Sainik exam the same as AISSEE?**
No. It is run by the UP Sainik Schools Society, separate from the NTA-run AISSEE used by other Sainik Schools. The exam comes first; a medical examination and interview follow for shortlisted candidates.
