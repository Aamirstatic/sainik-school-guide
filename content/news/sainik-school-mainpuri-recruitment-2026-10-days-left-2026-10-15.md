## 10 days left — Mainpuri's small but real window

Sainik School Mainpuri (Uttar Pradesh) has notified two contractual vacancies — **1 TGT English and 1 Counsellor** — and the application window closes on **25 October 2026**. With 10 days remaining, the timeline is tighter than it looks: this recruitment is **offline only**, the school counts the date your form *reaches* Mainpuri (not the date you posted it), and speed post from distant districts can easily take 4–5 working days.

This follows a busy October for Sainik School hiring — compare with the [Sainik School Kunjpura recruitment (15 posts, apply by 31 October)](/news/sainik-school-kunjpura-recruitment-2026-16-days-left-2026-10-15/) and the [Sainik School Goalpara recruitment (13 posts, apply by 12 November)](/news/sainik-school-goalpara-recruitment-2026-2026-10-14/). Mainpuri's window is the shortest of the three, which is precisely why it gets its own reminder.

## The 2 posts at a glance

| Post | Posts | Monthly remuneration (fixed) | Nature |
|------|-------|------------------------------|--------|
| TGT English | 1 | ₹35,000–₹60,000 | Contractual |
| Counsellor | 1 | ₹25,000–₹45,000 | Contractual |

The engagement is contractual — reported as initially for one year, extendable — not a regular appointment. If you are weighing this against regular TGT posts elsewhere, go in with open eyes: the pay range is respectable, but there is no permanent-service claim.

## Eligibility, age, fee — the short version

- **TGT English:** Bachelor's/Honours degree (or integrated degree) with at least 50% marks in English, plus B.Ed, and a valid CTET Paper-II qualification. The notification also asks for competence to teach through both English and Hindi.
- **Counsellor:** Graduate or postgraduate in Psychology, postgraduate in Child Development, or a graduation/postgraduation with a diploma in career guidance and counselling. Candidates scoring below 50% marks up to graduation are not eligible.
- **Age limit:** 21 to 50 years, with age relaxation as per government norms.
- **Application fee:** ₹500 (General/OBC), ₹250 (SC/ST), payable by demand draft.
- **Selection:** written test, followed by a class demonstration or skill test, then a personal interview.

## How to apply (offline only)

Download the application format from the school's official website, fill it carefully, attach self-attested copies of your documents and the demand draft, and send the completed packet by **ordinary, registered or speed post** to: **The Principal, Sainik School Mainpuri, Village – Nouner Kharra, Agra Road, Mainpuri, Uttar Pradesh – 205119.** By-hand and email applications are not accepted. Keep a photocopy of everything you send.
