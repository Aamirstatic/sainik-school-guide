## Three teaching posts, one morning, no online form

Sainik School Purulia in West Bengal has announced a walk-in interview for three teaching posts on an ad hoc basis. The interview is on **Monday, 23 November 2026**, and candidates report at **8:30 AM** at the school campus — Sainik School Purulia, PO Sainik School-723104, District Purulia (WB).

There is no online application and no last date to post a form. You simply turn up on the day with your papers. That is the whole process, which is exactly why it is easy to get wrong: if a certificate is missing or the eligibility does not match the notice line by line, there is no second chance to fix it later.

The three posts are one **TGT (Social Science)**, one **TGT (Hindi)** and one **Art Master (UR)** — one vacancy each. The TGT posts carry a consolidated salary of **Rs. 25,000 per month**; the Art Master post carries **Rs. 21,000 per month**. The notice mentions no allowances above the consolidated pay, and no TA or DA is paid for attending.

## Who can attend — post by post

| Post | Vacancies | Age (as on 1 Nov 2026) | Consolidated pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| TGT (Social Science) — ad hoc | 1 | 21–35 years | Rs. 25,000/month |
| TGT (Hindi) — ad hoc | 1 | 21–35 years | Rs. 25,000/month |
| Art Master (UR) — ad hoc | 1 | 21–35 years | Rs. 21,000/month |

**TGT (Social Science):** Graduate with History and Political Science as two subjects, plus B.Ed from a recognised university or institute. Or a B.A. B.Ed with Social Science (History and Political Science) from a Regional College of Education. Desirable: teaching experience in an English-medium residential school, knowledge of computer applications, and at least 50% marks in graduation and B.Ed.

**TGT (Hindi):** A bachelor's degree with Hindi studied as an elective or main subject for at least three years (six semesters), plus B.Ed from a recognised university or institute. Or a four-year integrated degree from an NCTE-recognised institution with the required subject combination. The same desirables apply — residential-school teaching experience, computer knowledge, and 50% in graduation and B.Ed.

**Art Master:** B. Fine Arts, or a five-year recognised diploma in Drawing and Painting, Sculpture or Graphic Art — or an equivalent degree from a recognised university. Desirable: past teaching experience in an English-medium residential school, and proficiency in games, sports, NCC and co-curricular activities such as music, dance or drama.

The age limit is **21 to 35 years as on 1 November 2026** for all three posts, and the notice does not mention any age relaxation. Only the Art Master post carries a printed category — UR. No category is printed for the two TGT posts.

## What to carry on 23 November

Bring your application and bio-data, your **original certificates**, and **attested copies of certificates and mark sheets**. The notice does not include an application form or prescribe a format, so candidates bring their own application on the day. Keep the originals and the attested copies in separate folders — the verification desk will want the originals first, and hunting through one mixed stack wastes your own time.

Two honest cautions. First, these are **ad hoc appointments, not regular posts** — the notice says so plainly, and the school administration reserves the right to cancel a vacancy for administrative or policy reasons. Go in with that understood. Second, the notice does not specify a selection process beyond the walk-in interview itself, so expect an interview on the day and be ready to demonstrate your subject, not just your certificates.
