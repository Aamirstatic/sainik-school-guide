## Seven days. The learning is done — now it's about finishing well

The Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test for Class 6 is on **Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**. Seven days from today. One phase, every Navodaya Vidyalaya in the country, summer-bound and winter-bound alike.

Here's the thing parents get wrong in this last week: they try to teach new things. Don't. At this point your child knows what they know. The next seven days are for checking the admit card, fixing anything wrong on it, rehearsing the exam morning once, and keeping the child calm and rested. That is the whole job — and it matters more than one more chapter.

## The facts, in one place

**The paper:** 80 objective questions, 100 marks, two hours. No negative marking — 1.25 marks per correct answer. Mental Ability and EVS carry 40 questions (50 marks); Arithmetic 20 questions (25 marks); Language 20 questions (25 marks). To qualify, a child must score at least **14 in Mental Ability and EVS, 7 in Arithmetic and 7 in Language**.

**The admit card:** released on **28 October 2026** on the NVS admission portal, linked from navodaya.gov.in. Download it with the registration number and the child's date of birth. It is free, and no admit card is sent by post or to the school — you have to download it yourself. If you haven't printed it yet, do it today, not on the 27th. Take two printouts on A4 paper.

**The three things to check on the card:** the child's name, photo and date of birth — the invigilator will match the photo with the child. The exam centre name and address — **the centre cannot be changed**, so plan the journey now. And the medium/language of the test — the question booklet must be in the language you chose on the form. If anything is wrong, contact the principal of your district's Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya right away.

## Exam day, hour by hour

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| Before 11:00 AM | Reach the centre and get your child seated in the allotted room. Latecomers are not allowed in. |
| 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM | The test runs for two hours, no break. Divyang candidates get 40 extra minutes. |
| After 1:30 PM | Collect your child, go home, and do not dissect the paper question by question. |

**What to carry:** the printed admit card, the child's Aadhaar card or the parent's residence certificate uploaded with the form, and a blue or black ballpoint pen. Pencils are not allowed on the OMR sheet, and once an answer is marked it cannot be changed or erased — so teach your child to decide before bubbling, not after.

## The last seven days

Our [9-day final-week plan](/blog/jnvst-2027-9-days-left-final-week-plan-class-6/) laid out the day-by-day revision. For this final week, keep it simple: one short mock or previous paper a day, timed, in the morning; revise only what your child already knows; no new topics after Wednesday. Sleep on time every night — a tired 11-year-old loses more marks to silly mistakes than to anything they didn't study. And if your child is also preparing for AISSEE 2027, remember the two exams reward the same skills: the JNVST paper is good AISSEE practice, not a distraction from it.

The result is 🔶 **EXPECTED** by the end of March 2027 on the same portal — NVS has not announced a date, so anyone quoting one is guessing.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam?**
Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, in a single phase for both summer-bound and winter-bound Navodaya Vidyalayas.

**How do I download the JNVST 2027 admit card?**
Visit navodaya.gov.in and open the JNVST 2027 Class 6 admit card link, which takes you to the NVS admission portal. Enter the registration number and the child's date of birth, then download and print the card. Admit cards were released on 28 October 2026 and are free.

**What time must my child reach the exam centre?**
Children must be seated in their exam room by 11:00 AM. Latecomers will not be allowed in, so plan to reach the centre well before that.

**What should my child carry to the JNVST exam?**
A printed copy of the admit card, the child's Aadhaar card or the parent's residence certificate that was uploaded with the form, and a blue or black ballpoint pen. Pencils are not allowed on the OMR sheet, and answers cannot be changed or erased once marked.
