## Day 15, and still no bulletin

Today, 25 October 2026, is the fifteenth day past the date on which last year's AISSEE notification appeared. The AISSEE 2026 notification was released on 10 October 2025, and the form stayed open until 9 November 2025 after an extension from 30 October. This year, that calendar has simply not repeated itself yet: no AISSEE 2027 information bulletin, no registration link, and no official schedule has appeared on the NTA's Sainik School portal.

This is not a one-source claim. Collegedunia's AISSEE page, updated on 21 October, states plainly that no 2027 information bulletin was listed on the official portal as of 16 October. AglaSem, reporting on 19 October, likewise found no AISSEE 2027 notice on exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society as of 18 October. And the last portal check recorded on this site, on the evening of 24 October, still showed only 2026 items. Three checks, a week apart, all pointing the same way.

## Why today makes the delay feel sharper

Today is also the day the rest of the defence-school form season closes. The UP Sainik School normal-fee window and the RMS CET late-fee window both end at 5 PM today. For families juggling more than one application, AISSEE is now the only major window that has not even opened — the others are closing while this one has not started.

## The fake "form out" problem, again

The delay has a side effect parents should watch for: websites claiming the form is already out. One education portal currently carries a page claiming the notification was released on 13 October — while the same portal's updated AISSEE page says the bulletin was still awaited on 16 October. Both cannot be true, and the official portal settles it: nothing is listed there. Before believing any "form out" post, open exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society yourself and look for a 2027 bulletin — and never pay any fee on a third-party site.

## What to do during the wait

The waiting period is best used on things that do not depend on the bulletin:

- **Documents first.** Birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate where applicable, and the candidate's photograph and signature in the usual NTA format.
- **Age check.** Under last year's rules, Class 6 applicants had to be 10 to 12 years old as on 31 March of the admission year (born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017), and Class 9 applicants 13 to 15 (born between 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014). Confirm the 2027 ranges when the bulletin lands.
- **Fee readiness.** Last year's fee was Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and Defence wards and Rs 700 for SC/ST candidates. The 2027 fee will be confirmed in the bulletin.
- **One mock a week.** With 98 days to the expected exam date, a weekly mock plus error review beats any last-month cramming plan.

## FAQs

**Is the AISSEE 2027 notification out yet?**

No. As of the latest checks reported on 16 and 18 October, and our own last portal check on the evening of 24 October, no AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or registration link was listed on the official NTA portal.

**How long is the delay now?**

Fifteen days. Last year's notification was released on 10 October 2025, so 25 October 2026 marks 15 days past that date with no 2027 bulletin.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam expected?**

31 January 2027, as per the NTA calendar. That date is expected, not yet confirmed by a 2027 bulletin, which leaves 98 days from 25 October.

**Should I trust websites that say the AISSEE 2027 form is already out?**

No, not without checking the official portal yourself. One education site claims an October 13 release while its own updated page says the bulletin was still awaited on October 16. Only exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society counts as proof.
