## The number that should worry you is not 1,25,332

On Wednesday, 25 November, the National Testing Agency posted its first registration update for AISSEE 2027 — and the headline number travelled fast. **1,25,332 registration forms** have been submitted for the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination. It sounds like a crowd. It is a crowd. But it is not the number that matters tonight.

Here is the second number from the same update: **73,669 candidates have paid the examination fee.** Do the subtraction and you get the number that actually matters: **51,663 families have started a form and not finished it.** That is more than four in ten of everyone who has registered.

If your family is one of the 51,663, read this carefully: **a registration is not an application.** Until the fee is paid and the Confirmation Page is generated, your child is not in the exam. There is no admit card coming for an unpaid form, and no list your child will appear on. The form you started is a draft until the money moves.

## Four days left — and the last evening is the worst time to pay

The regular window closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM**. Both the form and the fee must be done by then. After that, the late window runs from **3 to 6 December** with an extra **Rs 500** on top of the normal fee — Rs 1,350 instead of Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and Rs 1,200 instead of Rs 700 for SC/ST candidates. The correction window follows from **7 to 9 December**.

Now, the honest part. The 2 December evening is when tens of thousands of families will all try to pay at once — including many of the 51,663 who are still unpaid. Portals slow down. Payments fail halfway. A failed payment on the last evening is a different problem from a failed payment tonight, because tonight you have four days to try again. NTA has never extended an AISSEE deadline because the website was slow, and there is no reason to expect it to start now.

Our [last-weekend checklist](/news/aissee-2027-4-days-left-last-weekend-submit-form-2026-11-28/) walks through the form, the documents and the photo and signature specs. If the form is already filled and only the payment is pending, the job tonight takes ten minutes: log in, pay, download the Confirmation Page, and save it where you can find it in January.

## How to finish tonight — and how to know it worked

Open only the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**, and log in with the application number and password created during registration. Pay the fee — Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, Rs 700 for SC/ST — through the portal's own payment options. Then download the **Confirmation Page**. That page is the proof. If you do not have it, the application is not complete, whatever the portal showed you before you logged out.

Three checks before you close the tab. First, the name and date of birth on the Confirmation Page must match the birth certificate exactly — a mismatch now is a correction-window job in December, and a much bigger job after that. Second, the photo and signature must be the ones you uploaded, not placeholders. Third, save the application number somewhere outside the phone — a notebook, a diary, anywhere a dead battery cannot reach. You will need it for the city slip on 11 January and the admit card on 25 January, ahead of the exam on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**.

If the payment fails, do not pay twice in a panic. Check the bank statement first, then the portal's payment status, and only then retry. A double debit is recoverable — NTA's own guidance routes it through the helpline — but it is a week of phone calls you do not need in the last four days. The helpline is **011-40759000** and the email is **aissee@nta.ac.in**, both published in NTA's own update.

## What the 1.25 lakh figure actually means for your child

One thing the numbers do tell you: this is a big year. The 33 Sainik Schools have **3,160 Class 6 seats** and **731 Class 9 seats** for 2027-28, as listed in the bulletin — our [seat matrix](/news/aissee-2027-3160-class-6-seats-731-class-9-seats-2026-11-24/) breaks them down school by school. Against 73,669 paid applications and counting, the competition is real. But it is also the same competition every year, and the children who clear it are not the ones who registered earliest. They are the ones whose forms were complete, whose documents were right, and who spent December and January preparing instead of chasing paperwork.

That is the whole point of tonight. The 51,663 unpaid forms are not 51,663 children who will miss the exam. Most of them will pay in the next four days. The question is only whether yours is finished tonight, calmly, or on the evening of 2 December, with the portal groaning under the load. Finish it tonight.

## FAQs

**How many candidates have registered for AISSEE 2027?**
As of NTA's update on 25 November 2026, 1,25,332 registration forms have been submitted and 73,669 candidates have paid the examination fee. The remaining 51,663 have registered but not yet completed payment.

**I registered but haven't paid the fee — is my AISSEE 2027 application complete?**
No. An application is complete only after the fee is paid and the Confirmation Page is generated. A registration without payment does not count as an application, and it will not get you an admit card.

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**
Wednesday, 2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM — for both the form and the fee payment. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra Rs 500, and the correction window follows from 7 to 9 December.

**Where do I pay the fee and check my AISSEE 2027 application status?**
On the official NTA portal, exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society, by logging in with your application number and password. If the portal does not respond, NTA's helpline is 011-40759000 and the email is aissee@nta.ac.in.
