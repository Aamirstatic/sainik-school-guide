> **Last verified: 2 October 2026.** This is a countdown explainer, not a news flash — every date below is marked CONFIRMED or EXPECTED.

121 days. That is all that stands between today and **31 January 2027** (CONFIRMED, tentative — NTA's exam calendar notice of 16 September 2026, reported by the Times of India) — a Sunday, the proposed date for the AISSEE 2027 written exam. It sounds like plenty of time. It is not. The next 17 weeks will be eaten by school exams, Diwali, winter breaks, and three other admission deadlines that close *this month*. Here is the countdown, in order of urgency.

![Students studying together for an exam](/images/inbody/121-days-to-aissee-2027-countdown-checklist-2026-10-02.webp)

## This week: don't miss the live deadlines

Three forms are genuinely open right now, and one of them closes in five days:

- **RIMCEE late-fee window closes 7 October 2026** (CONFIRMED) — missed the 30 September deadline? NTA's late-fee window is your last shot; the written exam is 6 December 2026. Details in our [RIMCEE late-fee piece](/news/rimcee-2026-late-fee-window-open-october-1-7-2026-10-01/).
- **RMS CET applications close 20 October 2026** (CONFIRMED) — last date for Class 6 and 9 admission to the five Rashtriya Military Schools; exam on 13 December 2026 ([full details](/news/rms-cet-2026-applications-open-nta-2026-09-27/)).
- **UP Sainik School applications close 25 October 2026 at 5 PM** (CONFIRMED) — 23 days left, with a late-fee window till 31 October and the exam on 20 December 2026 ([countdown piece](/news/up-sainik-school-deadline-24-days-left-2026-10-01/)).

If your child is eligible for any of these, treat this weekend as the deadline, not the date on the notice.

## The notification watch (EXPECTED)

The AISSEE 2027 notification itself is **still not out** — we [checked the NTA portal on 2 October](/news/aissee-2027-notification-still-not-out-2026-10-01/): the Latest News section lists only AISSEE-2026 items. Based on the 2026 cycle (applications 10–30 October 2025), the notification is EXPECTED this month. The bulletin is the single most important document of the cycle — application dates, fee, age limits, exam pattern, school list, reservation rules. Until it appears, everything is pattern-based reasoning, honestly labelled.

## How to split the 121 days

| Phase | Weeks | Dates (approx.) | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 10 | 2 Oct – 11 Dec | Finish the full syllabus, one subject a day |
| Mock season | 4 | 12 Dec – 8 Jan | Full-length papers, timed, with error logs |
| Final revision | 3 | 9 Jan – 30 Jan | Weak areas only + previous years' papers |

Rough, but realistic: Mathematics carries half the marks in both Class 6 (150 of 300) and Class 9 (200 of 400) papers, so give it roughly half your study time (CONFIRMED — 2026 exam pattern).

## Three things to do this week

1. **Close the open forms first.** Paperwork beats preparation in October — a missed form is unfixable, a weak chapter is fixable.
2. **Book the notification check.** Check the [NTA AISSEE portal](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/) yourself every few days; do not rely on WhatsApp forwards.
3. **Run a 10-week syllabus plan.** Count chapters per subject, divide by 10, and write the week's target on paper where your child can see it. Vague plans produce vague results.

**How many days are left for AISSEE 2027?**

121 days, as of 2 October 2026. The exam is proposed for 31 January 2027 (Sunday) in NTA's 2026-27 exam calendar notice — a tentative date.

**Is the AISSEE 2027 notification out?**

No. As of 2 October 2026, the NTA portal's Latest News lists only AISSEE-2026 items. It is expected in October based on past cycles.

**Which admission forms are open right now?**

RIMCEE late-fee window till 7 October 2026, RMS CET till 20 October 2026, and UP Sainik School till 25 October 2026 (late fee till 31 October).

**How should I split 121 days of AISSEE preparation?**

About 10 weeks to finish the syllabus, 4 weeks of timed mocks with error analysis, and 3 weeks of light revision and previous papers.
