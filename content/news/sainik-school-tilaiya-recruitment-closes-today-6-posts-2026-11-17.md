## Today is the last day — and "posted today" is not enough

This is the part that catches people out every year, so let us say it plainly: the last date for Sainik School Tilaiya is **today, 17 November 2026**, and the rule is about *receipt*, not posting. Your completed application must **reach the Principal, Sainik School Tilaiya, by today**. The notice says clearly that the school will not be responsible for postal delays, and applications received after the last date will not be considered.

If your application is already in transit, keep your proof of dispatch safe and hope the post was quick. If it is still sitting on your desk, the honest truth is that ordinary post will not get it to Koderma district in Jharkhand today. [We flagged this deadline yesterday](/news/sainik-school-tilaiya-recruitment-closes-tomorrow-6-posts-2026-11-16/) — today is the day it closes.

## The 6 posts at a glance

| Post | Category | Type | Monthly pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| TGT (Social Science) | SC | Contractual | ₹44,900 fixed |
| TGT (Maths) | OBC | Contractual | ₹44,900 fixed |
| Music Teacher | OBC | Contractual | ₹29,200 fixed |
| Craft & Workshop Instructor | SC | Contractual | ₹29,200 fixed |
| Medical Officer | UR | Contractual | ₹70,000 fixed |
| Quartermaster | UR | Regular | Pay Level-5 (7th CPC) + allowances |

The five contractual posts are initially for **11 months**, with extension possible based on the school's requirements. The Quartermaster post is the only regular appointment of the six. Age limits are calculated as on **1 October 2026**: 21–35 years for the TGT and Medical Officer posts, 18–50 for Music Teacher, and 18–44 for the Craft & Workshop Instructor. The Quartermaster's age is as per the official notification.

The eligibility bar is specific, so check it against your own certificates before you send anything. TGT (Social Science) needs a graduation with any two of Geography, History, Economics or Political Science — one must be Geography or History — with at least 50% marks, plus B.Ed. TGT (Maths) needs a bachelor's degree in Mathematics with two of Physics, Chemistry, Electronics, Computer Science or Statistics, plus B.Ed. and CTET Paper-II. The Medical Officer post needs an MBBS. The Quartermaster post needs a B.A. or B.Com. with at least five years of experience as UDC (Stores), Quartermaster, or as an ex-serviceman, preferably a JCO with 10 years of stores experience.

## What your envelope must contain

This is an **offline-only** application — there is no online form. Download the prescribed form from the school's official website, **sainikschooltilaiya.org**, fill it in, and send it with:

- **Self-attested copies** of your educational certificates, professional certificates, experience certificates, date-of-birth proof and category certificate (if applicable).
- **A Demand Draft** for the fee — ₹400 for General/OBC, ₹250 for SC/ST — drawn in favour of **Principal, Sainik School Tilaiya**, payable at **State Bank of India, Sainik School Tilaiya Branch, Branch Code 3502**. The fee is non-refundable.
- **A self-addressed envelope bearing a ₹55 postage stamp.**
- **The post applied for written clearly on the envelope**, addressed to the Principal, Sainik School Tilaiya, PO: Tilaiya Dam, Dist: Koderma, Jharkhand.

Incomplete applications will not be considered. Keep a copy of everything you send, along with the proof of dispatch.

## What happens after today

The school will shortlist candidates based on eligibility, and the **test and interview dates will be notified later on the official website**. There is no fixed date in the notice, so the only reliable move after today is to keep checking sainikschooltilaiya.org for the schedule — the written test, practical test, interview and document verification dates will all appear there. Final selection will be based on performance in those tests and the interview.

One more deadline in the same week, if you missed this one: [Sainik School Nagrota's 3 posts close on 20 November](/news/sainik-school-jobs-3-deadlines-week-tilaiya-nov-17-nagrota-nov-20-2026-11-15/) — TGT (Computer Science), Art Master and Nursing Sister — also by Speed Post only. That is three days away, and the same "must reach by the date" rule applies.
