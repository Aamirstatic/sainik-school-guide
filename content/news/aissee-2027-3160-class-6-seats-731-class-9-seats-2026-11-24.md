## The numbers parents have been waiting for

Eight days before the form closes, the AISSEE 2027 bulletin finally puts real numbers on the table. Across the **33 established Sainik Schools**, there are **3,160 seats in Class 6** — **2,809 for boys and 351 for girls** — and **731 seats in Class 9**.

That is the first time since the bulletin came out on 3 November that parents can see exactly how many chairs are actually available, school by school, instead of guessing from last year's figures. And the picture is uneven in ways that matter for your choice-filling later.

Here's the thing: Class 6 is where the real opportunity sits. With 3,160 seats, it is more than four times the size of the Class 9 intake. If your child is in Class 5 now, this is the entry point the system is built around. Class 9, with 731 seats spread across 33 schools, is a much narrower door — and in four schools, there is no door at all this year.

## Where the seats are — and where they aren't

The biggest Class 6 intakes this year:

| School | Class 6 seats |
|---|---|
| Tilaiya (Jharkhand) | 140 |
| Amaravathinagar (Tamil Nadu) | 122 |
| Balachadi, Chhingchhip, Goalpara, Purulia, Kunjpura | 120 each |
| Gopalganj (Bihar) | 110 |

At the other end, Jhansi has just 50 Class 6 seats, Sujanpur Tira 59, and Imphal 70. A smaller intake doesn't mean a worse school — Jhansi is one of the newer schools, and its small numbers reflect its size, not its quality. But it does mean the competition for those 50 chairs will be fierce, and your child needs a strong score, not just a pass.

Class 9 is where the surprises are. Four schools — **Bhubaneswar, Bijapur, Gopalganj and Jhunjhunu** — have **zero Class 9 vacancies** listed for 2027-28. If your child is in Class 8 and you had your heart set on one of those four, the bulletin is telling you plainly: pick another school in counselling. The largest Class 9 intakes are Goalpara (49), Nalanda (48) and Rewari (45).

One more detail worth knowing: the bulletin says candidates should list their choice of school **even if no vacancy is shown for their category**. Vacancies get redistributed when cadets withdraw, so a zero in your category today can become a seat later. Don't self-reject before the system has had its say.

## What this means for the 8 days left

The form closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. The fee is **Rs 850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and **Rs 700** for SC and ST candidates. Miss the date and you can still apply from **3 to 6 December** — but it will cost you an extra **Rs 500** as late fee, and the correction window (7 to 9 December) is your only chance to fix mistakes.

Two things to do with these numbers before you submit:

1. **Check the seats at your target school, not just the total.** Our [full seat matrix guide](/blog/sainik-school-seats-2027-all-schools-matrix/) explains how the 67% home-state quota and category reservations split each school's seats — a school with 120 seats may have only a handful in your child's category and state.
2. **If Tilaiya is on your list, read our [complete parents' guide to Sainik School Tilaiya](/blog/sainik-school-tilaiya-complete-parents-guide-2027-2026-11-24/)** — with 140 Class 6 seats, it has the largest intake in the country this year, and it is also one of the oldest schools, founded in 1963.

And remember the one number that matters more than any seat count: the exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. Eight days to apply, then sixty-eight days to prepare. The form is the easy part. Use the time after it well.

## FAQs

**How many seats are there in Sainik Schools for AISSEE 2027?**
In the 33 established Sainik Schools, there are 3,160 Class 6 seats (2,809 boys and 351 girls) and 731 Class 9 seats, as listed in the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin. Approved New Sainik Schools have a separate vacancy list in the same bulletin.

**Which Sainik School has the most Class 6 seats in 2027?**
Sainik School Tilaiya in Jharkhand, with 140 Class 6 seats — 126 boys and 14 girls. Amaravathinagar in Tamil Nadu is next with 122 seats.

**Does every Sainik School admit students in Class 9?**
No. Four of the 33 schools — Bhubaneswar, Bijapur, Gopalganj and Jhunjhunu — have no Class 9 vacancies listed for 2027-28. Class 9 entry is also much smaller overall, with only 731 seats against 3,160 in Class 6.

**Are the AISSEE 2027 seat numbers final?**
No — the bulletin calls them tentative. The numbers are based on the current strength of Class 12 and existing gaps in other classes, and they can change if cadets withdraw. The bulletin advises candidates to list their choice of school regardless of the vacancies shown for a particular category.

**Can I still apply for AISSEE 2027?**
Yes. The online form is open until 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM, at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. The fee is Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards and Rs 700 for SC and ST. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra Rs 500.
