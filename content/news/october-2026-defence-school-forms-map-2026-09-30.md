## RIMCEE: missed today's 5 PM deadline? You get a second chance

NTA closed the regular RIMCEE 2026 window at 5 PM today — but a **late-fee window runs 1–7 October 2026**, with an extra ₹500 over the regular fee (₹1,000 General/OBC-NCL/defence wards; ₹750 SC/ST). If the form is ready but the payment slipped, apply tomorrow morning — NTA portals crawl on closing days, and this window also shuts at 5 PM. Stage-I written exam: **6 December 2026**. Full timeline: [RIMCEE 2026: After the Deadline]({{< relref "rimcee-2026-after-deadline-what-happens-next-2026-09-28.md" >}}).

## RMS CET: three weeks left, and most parents haven't noticed

The Rashtriya Military Schools CET — Class 6 and 9 at the five Rashtriya Military Schools — has been open since 23 September and closes **20 October 2026**; the written exam is **13 December 2026**. It flies under the radar every year because parents fixate on AISSEE, and the Class 6 paper is bilingual (Hindi and English). Verified details: [NTA Opens RMS CET 2026 Applications]({{< relref "rms-cet-2026-applications-open-nta-2026-09-27.md" >}}).

## UP Sainik School: Lucknow and Gorakhpur close 25 October

The UP Sainik Schools Society's own entrance — separate from NTA's AISSEE — closes **25 October 2026** (₹1,000), with a late window (₹2,000 total) from 26 to 31 October. Written exam: **20 December 2026**. Applying here doesn't affect your AISSEE application. Details: [UP Sainik School Admission 2027]({{< relref "up-sainik-school-admission-2027-2026-09-27.md" >}}).

## FAQs

**Which defence-school entrance forms are open in October 2026?**
Four matter for parents: RIMCEE 2026 (late-fee window 1–7 October), RMS CET 2026 (open till 20 October), UP Sainik School Lucknow and Gorakhpur (open till 25 October), and AISSEE 2027 — notification not released yet but expected in October.

**I missed the RIMCEE 2026 deadline today. Can I still apply?**
Yes. NTA opens a late-fee window from 1 to 7 October 2026 with an extra ₹500 over the regular fee. The Stage-I written exam is on 6 December 2026.

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No. As of 30 September 2026 the NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin. It is expected in October 2026, and the exam is tentatively dated 31 January 2027 in NTA's exam calendar.

**Can my child apply for both RMS CET and AISSEE 2027?**
Yes. They are separate exams run by different bodies with months between the written tests (RMS CET on 13 December 2026, AISSEE tentatively 31 January 2027), so one application does not block the other.
