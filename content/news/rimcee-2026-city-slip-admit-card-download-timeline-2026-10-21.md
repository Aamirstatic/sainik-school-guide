## The two documents, in order

NTA releases these in a fixed sequence. Do not mix them up:

| Document | When (expected) | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **City intimation slip** | 12–15 days before the exam → 🔶 **21–24 November 2026** | The exam **city** allotted to you — not the centre |
| **Admit card** | 5–7 days before the exam → 🔶 **29 November–1 December 2026** | The exact **centre, reporting time, paper timing**, and your roll number |

The city slip exists so that families who need to travel can book tickets and rooms in advance. The admit card is the document you must carry into the exam hall. Neither arrives by email or post — both are downloads from the official NTA RIMCEE portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee**, via your application number and date of birth.

## Six things to verify the day each document arrives

**On the city slip:** confirm the city matches the preference you filled in the form (or the nearest alternative NTA allotted). If the city looks completely wrong, raise it with NTA immediately rather than waiting for the admit card — logistics get harder to fix later.

**On the admit card, check these six:**

1. **Name spelling** — exactly as on the application, matching the birth certificate.
2. **Date of birth** — RIMCEE eligibility hinges on the 12½–14 years window as of 1 July 2027; a wrong DOB can become an eligibility dispute.
3. **Photograph and signature** — if the uploaded photo failed to render, download and print may still work, but carry the same ID proof used at registration.
4. **Exam centre address and reporting time** — plan the route now; on 6 December morning there is no margin for a wrong turn.
5. **Paper timing and instructions** — the written exam covers English, Mathematics and General Knowledge in pen-and-paper mode; note what you may and may not carry.
6. **Barcode/QR on the printout** — take a clear printout on A4, not a phone screenshot. Centres verify from paper.

## What remains on the RIMCEE calendar

After the written exam, the stages continue: the Stage-I result is declared by **20 February 2027**, and the Stage-II viva voce is tentatively scheduled for **April 2027** — 4 to 6 weeks after the result — with the exact date and venue to be announced on the official NTA/RIMC websites.

## Why this matters now

The biggest exam-day disasters in every NTA cycle are not about preparation — they are about a centre 40 kilometres away that a parent discovered at midnight, or a name mismatch nobody reported. Both are free to prevent in November. Mark the two expected windows in your calendar today: **21–24 November** for the city slip, **29 November–1 December** for the admit card.

## FAQ

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 admit card be released?**
Five to seven days before the written exam — which puts it around 29 November to 1 December 2026, for the 6 December exam. The city intimation slip arrives earlier, 12 to 15 days before the exam.

**Where do I download the RIMCEE 2026 admit card?**
Only from NTA's official RIMCEE portal, exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee, using the application number and date of birth. NTA does not email admit cards.

**What is the RIMCEE 2026 exam date and mode?**
The Stage-I written examination is on 6 December 2026, held in pen-and-paper mode across 39 cities, per the official information bulletin.

**What should I check on the admit card the day it arrives?**
Your name, date of birth, photograph, exam city and centre, paper timing, and the reporting-time instructions. Any mismatch must be reported to NTA immediately.
