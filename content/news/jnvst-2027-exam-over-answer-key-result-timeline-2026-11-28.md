## The paper is over — and now the long wait begins

At 1:30 PM today, Saturday 28 November 2026, the JNVST 2027 Class 6 paper ended. Eighty questions, 100 marks, two hours on an OMR sheet — and for the first time, the summer-bound and winter-bound Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas held the test in a single phase, for the 2027-28 session.

If your child sat the paper today, here is the honest picture of what comes next: nothing quick. The result is expected by the end of March 2027, and the official answer key will most likely arrive with it — or after it. That is not a delay or a failure. It is how NVS has run this exam for years.

## What NVS has actually said — and what it hasn't

Two things are confirmed, and both come from the official admission prospectus:

**The result is expected by the end of March 2027.** That is the prospectus's own timeline. NVS has announced no specific date, and no date has leaked through any official channel. Anyone giving you an exact date right now is guessing.

**There is no official answer-key date.** The prospectus simply does not specify one. That matters, because the keys already circulating on YouTube and WhatsApp this evening are not from NVS. They are memory-based reconstructions put together by coaching centres within hours of the paper — useful for a rough sense of how the paper went, and nothing more.

## What the last three years actually show

The pattern is consistent enough to plan around:

| Cycle | Exam date | Result date | Gap | Answer key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JNVST 2024 | 20 January 2024 | 31 March 2024 | 71 days | Not released before result |
| JNVST 2025 | 18 January 2025 | 25 March 2025 | 66 days | 9 April 2025 — after the result |
| JNVST 2026 | 13 December 2025 | 17 March 2026 | 94 days | 17 March 2026 — same day as result |

Three things stand out. First, every summer-bound result in the last three years landed in March. Second, the gap between exam and result ran from 66 to 94 days. Third — and this is the one parents keep missing — the official answer key has never come first. In 2025 it arrived two weeks *after* the result. In 2026 it arrived *with* the result.

This year the exam was held earlier than usual, on 28 November, and the prospectus still points to the end of March. That means a wait of around four months. It also means the exam-day answer keys doing the rounds tonight will be four months stale by the time the official key appears — another reason not to treat them as a verdict on your child.

## How the marks work, for the score-checkers

If you do sit down with an unofficial key tonight, here is the arithmetic, confirmed from the official pattern: each correct answer is worth 1.25 marks, wrong answers cost nothing, and unattempted questions score zero. So 60 correct answers is 75 marks; 72 correct is 90. There is no negative marking, so a guessed answer can only help.

But read that score the way it deserves to be read: as an estimate with a margin of error. A memory-based key gets a handful of answers wrong every year, and at 1.25 marks a question, five wrong key entries move the total by more than six marks. That is enough to turn a hopeful evening into a miserable one — or the other way round. Neither is real until NVS speaks.

## What to do this weekend — and what not to

**Do keep the admit card and registration number safe.** The result is checked on navodaya.gov.in with the roll number and date of birth, and the document verification that follows a selection needs the same details. Lose them now and March becomes a scramble.

**Do not start a four-month post-mortem.** There is no re-evaluation in JNVST, and no answer-key challenge process like the one NTA runs for AISSEE. The OMR sheet is submitted; the marks are what they are.

**Do use the time for the exam that is still open.** The AISSEE 2027 form — the Sainik School entrance — closes on Wednesday, 2 December at 11:50 PM, and the exam is on 31 January 2027. If your child sat JNVST today and has not applied for AISSEE, this weekend is the moment to decide. Our [AISSEE 2027 last-weekend checklist](/news/aissee-2027-4-days-left-last-weekend-submit-form-2026-11-28/) walks through the form, the fee and the documents.

**Do not pay anyone for a "result" or a "confirmed selection list."** NVS publishes results only on its own website and at the schools and district offices. Any person selling an early result is selling nothing.

## FAQs

**When will the JNVST 2027 Class 6 result be declared?**
No specific date has been announced. The NVS admission prospectus says results are expected by the end of March 2027. In the last three cycles, results came 66 to 94 days after the exam — 31 March 2024, 25 March 2025 and 17 March 2026. Check navodaya.gov.in with your child's roll number and date of birth.

**When will the official JNVST 2027 answer key be released?**
NVS has not specified an answer-key date in the admission prospectus. In the last two cycles the official key came with or after the result — 9 April 2025 (after the 25 March result) and 17 March 2026 (the same day as the result). Do not expect a key in December.

**Can I check my child's answers against the answer keys on YouTube and WhatsApp?**
You can, but treat the score as a rough estimate only. Those keys are memory-based reconstructions by coaching centres, not NVS. The official key is the only one that counts, and in past cycles it arrived with or after the result.

**Where do I check the JNVST 2027 result?**
On the official NVS website navodaya.gov.in and the admission portal, using the roll number and date of birth from the admit card. Results are also displayed at the respective Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas and district and regional authorities. Keep the admit card safe until admission is complete.
