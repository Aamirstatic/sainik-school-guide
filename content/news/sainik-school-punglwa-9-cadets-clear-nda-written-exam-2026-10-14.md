## Nine written-stage clears from Nagaland

Nine cadets of Sainik School Punglwa, Nagaland, have cleared the written stage of the UPSC National Defence Academy examination — seven from the school's current batch and two senior cadets. The school's announcement came from Peren on 2 October and was reported on 13 October.

The current-batch qualifiers are **Machitube Ndang, Aditya Kumar, Abhishek Kr. Singh, Adarsh Sen, Pranav Kumar, Rishab Mishra, and Roshan Kumar**. The two senior cadets are **Dev Dagar and Rishabh Raj**. Officiating Principal **Lt Col Amit Chaudhury** congratulated the cadets alongside the staff, calling the result "highly encouraging and a source of inspiration for the school fraternity and the state." The school also thanked its Local Board of Administration for steady guidance and acknowledged the support of parents.

## Part of this year's NDA written wave

This result belongs to the same examination cycle as the other Sainik School successes reported this month: the written stage of the **NDA & NA Examination (II) 2026**, conducted by the UPSC on 13 September 2026 (EXPECTED — inferred from the timing of the school's announcement and the parallel results reported for other schools; the UPSC has not been separately verified for this school).

Consider the company Punglwa is in this month alone: **50 cadets from Sainik School Kalikiri** (including three girls from its first-ever girls' batch), **23 from Sainik School Nalanda**, **44 from Sainik School Ghorakhal**, and **17 girls from Punjab's Mai Bhago Armed Forces Preparatory Institute** all cleared the same written stage. Nine more from a smaller Northeastern school fits the pattern — and the pattern is the story.

## What comes next for the nine

Clearing the written paper is only the first filter. The qualifiers now face the **Services Selection Board (SSB) interview**, run under the Ministry of Defence — five days of psychology tests, group tasks and the personal interview, where most written-stage qualifiers fall out. After the SSB come medical tests and the final merit list for the NDA's 158th Course (CONFIRMED for the other schools' qualifiers per earlier reports; EXPECTED to apply here).

Punglwa has been here before. The school previously recorded 11 written-stage clears (its highest at the time), and four of its cadets went on to secure SSB recommendations in an earlier cycle. Nine clears with SSB still to come keeps the school firmly on its upward curve.
