## The form closes on 2 December — and one field matters more than the rest

Eight days remain. The AISSEE 2027 application window closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**, and the fee — **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, **₹700** for SC/ST — must be paid by the same hour. The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027, at 2:00 PM**.

Most parents filling the form this week are focused on documents and photographs. Fair enough. But one field quietly decides how hard your child's competition will be: **the Sainik School you opt for**. That single choice decides whether your child counts as a home-state candidate or an outside-state candidate — and the two pools are very different sizes.

## Home state is decided by the school you pick, not by where you live alone

The bulletin puts it plainly. You are a home-state candidate for a school **only if your state of domicile is the same as the state where that school stands**. Pick the school in your own state and you compete for roughly two-thirds of its seats. Pick a school in another state and you compete for the remaining third.

Take a Kerala-domicile boy applying for Class 6 at **Sainik School Kazhakootam**. Of its 75 seats, **44 are home-state boys' seats** and 21 are outside-state boys' seats. The same boy applying to **Sainik School Kunjpura** in Haryana would be an outside-state candidate there — chasing 35 of its 120 Class 6 seats instead of 73 home-state ones. Same child, same marks, very different odds.

## The zero-seat rule: list the school anyway

Here is the part most parents get wrong. Open the bulletin's seat tables and you will find zeros everywhere. For Class 9, four of the 33 schools — **Bhubaneswar, Bijapur, Gopalganj and Jhunjhunu** — show **zero seats at all**. And in many schools, particular categories show zero: Kazhakootam, for instance, has **no home-state girls' seats in Class 9** — its two girls' seats are both in the outside-state pool.

The instinct is to skip a school with a zero in your column. The bulletin says the opposite. In a note printed under both seat tables, it tells candidates to **specify their choice of Sainik School irrespective of availability of vacancies in a specified category**. The reason is simple: the vacancy numbers are tentative. When cadets withdraw, seats open up and are redistributed across categories — and a zero today can become a seat by counselling time.

So: list the school your child actually wants, even at zero. But make sure at least one school on your list has real, visible seats in your child's class, gender and category. The bulletin warns that NTA and the Sainik Schools Society will not be responsible for errors in school selection. Dream big on one choice; keep one choice grounded.

## After the result: the choices move to the AISSAC portal

The form is only the first half of the choice. After the result, qualified candidates must **re-register on the AISSAC e-counselling portal** using their NTA application number. Seats are then allotted on the school-wise, gender-wise and category-wise merit list, plus medical fitness and document verification.

One warning from the bulletin deserves underlining: you are allotted a school **based on the choices you select on the AISSAC portal**, and **if you do not accept an allotted school, you will not be considered for that school in future counselling rounds**. So the choices you enter after the result deserve the same care as the ones you enter this week.

## What to do before 2 December

Sit with your child and the bulletin's seat tables tonight. Put the home-state school first if it is a genuine fit. List the dream school even if your category shows zero. And keep one choice where the seats are real and visible. The form closes at 11:50 PM on 2 December — the late window (3–6 December, extra ₹500) exists, but it is a safety net, not a plan.

## FAQs

**When does the AISSEE 2027 form close?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM, for both Class 6 and Class 9. The fee — Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, Rs 700 for SC/ST — must also be paid by the same time. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra Rs 500.

**How does my school choice affect my home-state quota?**

You count as home-state for a school only if your state of domicile is the same as the state where that school is located. Pick a school in another state and you compete for the smaller outside-state share of its seats — about a third of them.

**Should I list a school if my category shows zero seats there?**

Yes — the AISSEE 2027 bulletin says so explicitly. Vacancies are tentative and get redistributed when cadets withdraw, so a zero in your category today can become a seat later. List the school you actually want, but make sure at least one of your choices has real seats in your category.

**What happens after the AISSEE 2027 result?**

Qualified candidates re-register on the AISSAC e-counselling portal using their NTA application number. Seats are allotted on merit, your choices on the portal, medical fitness and document verification. If you do not accept an allotted school, you will not be considered for that school in later rounds.
