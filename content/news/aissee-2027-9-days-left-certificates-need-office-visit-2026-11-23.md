## Nine days. The form takes an evening — the certificates don't

The AISSEE 2027 application window closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM**. Nine days from today. If you have been telling yourself you will do the form "this weekend", here is the problem with that plan: the form itself takes an evening, but the documents behind it can take a working week.

Today is Monday. That matters more than the countdown does. Domicile certificates, caste certificates and birth-certificate corrections are not downloads — they are visits to a government office, an application, and a wait of several working days. Start on Monday and you have a full working week. Start on Saturday the 28th and you are asking an office to move in three working days, with the deadline on Wednesday night.

## The three certificates to check today

**1. The domicile certificate.** This is the one that decides which quota your child competes in. At the 33 established Sainik Schools, **67% of seats are reserved for children domiciled in the home state** and 33% for other states. Without a domicile certificate uploaded with the form, your child falls into the smaller other-state pool — at popular schools, that pool is brutally competitive. Our [domicile certificate guide](/blog/domicile-certificate-online-apply-state-wise-process-2026-10-11/) explains the state-wise process; the point today is simply: if you don't have it in hand, apply for it this morning.

**2. The caste or category certificate.** If your child claims SC, ST or OBC-NCL reservation, the certificate must be valid and in the right format. OBC-NCL certificates in particular have a validity rule parents keep missing — our [OBC-NCL validity guide](/blog/obc-ncl-certificate-validity-rule-aissee-2027/) covers it. An expired or wrongly-formatted certificate is the same as no certificate at the verification stage.

**3. The birth certificate — and the name on it.** Check that the name and date of birth on the birth certificate match the school records and Aadhaar exactly. Class 6 applicants must be born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017**; Class 9 between **1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014**. A spelling mismatch — one letter different between the birth certificate and the school register — is one of the commonest reasons a form gets stuck. Fixing it means a visit to the municipal office or the school, and that takes working days. Do it now, while there are still working days left.

## What the safety nets do — and do not — cover

There are two safety nets after 2 December, and both are narrower than parents assume.

The **late-fee window (3–6 December)** lets you submit the form with an extra **₹500** on top of the regular fee — ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST. It buys you four more days. It does not buy you a domicile certificate.

The **correction window (7–9 December)** lets you fix some details on a submitted form. Our [correction-window guide](/news/aissee-2027-correction-window-7-9-december-audit-confirmation-page-2026-11-18/) explains what it does and doesn't cover. The honest summary: it fixes mistakes, it does not create documents. A certificate that doesn't exist yet cannot be corrected into existence.

## Your Monday checklist

- Pull out the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records. Check the name spelling and date of birth on all three, letter by letter.
- If the domicile certificate is not in your hand, apply for it today — not "this week", today.
- If you claim a category, check the certificate's format and validity now.
- Then, and only then, sit down with the form. Our [10-days-left weekend checklist](/news/aissee-2027-10-days-left-last-weekend-form-checklist-2026-11-22/) walks through the submission itself, and our [upload-mistakes guide](/news/aissee-2027-15-days-left-5-upload-mistakes-that-reject-forms-2026-11-17/) covers the photo, signature and thumb-impression errors that reject forms.

The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. The exam city is announced on **11 January** and admit cards come on **25 January**. None of that matters if the form never gets in — and the form never gets in if the certificates aren't ready. Nine days is plenty, if the office visits start today.

## FAQs

**When does the AISSEE 2027 application form close?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM. The fee must also be paid by then. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra ₹500, and the correction window is 7 to 9 December.

**Can I apply for AISSEE 2027 without a domicile certificate?**

You can submit the form, but the 67% home-state quota at the 33 established Sainik Schools is decided by the domicile certificate you upload. Without it, your child competes for the much smaller other-state pool. Get the certificate first if you possibly can.

**What if my child's name is spelt differently on the birth certificate and the school records?**

Fix it before you submit the form. Name mismatches between the birth certificate, school records and Aadhaar are one of the commonest reasons forms get stuck at verification. A correction at the municipal office or school takes working days, so start on Monday, not on 1 December.

**Can the correction window fix a wrong certificate after I submit?**

Only partly. The correction window from 7 to 9 December lets you fix some details, but not everything — and a certificate that does not exist yet cannot be corrected into existence. Treat 2 December as the real deadline and the correction window as a safety net, not a plan.
