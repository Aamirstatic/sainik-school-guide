## Thirty days. That is the whole runway now.

The RMS CET 2026 written exam is on **Sunday, 13 December 2026** — exactly 30 days from today. The application window closed on 20 October, the late window on 25 October, and the correction window on 28 October. Nothing is left to file, fix or pay. From here on, it is only preparation — and two downloads.

Here is the thing parents forget in this last month: the exam is not the next deadline. The city slip is. NTA issues the **city intimation slip in the last week of November** (tentatively around 29 November), and the **admit card around 8 December**, five to seven days before the exam. Both come only on the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet**. No post, no SMS with a link — if a message offers you a download link, it is not from NTA.

## What the final month should look like

Thirty days is not the time for new chapters. It is the time for papers. One full-length mock a week, in the exact 2-hour-30-minute window, with the OMR sheet filled in by hand. Between mocks, only the weak section gets new study — everything else gets revision.

For Class 6, remember the qualifying trap: English needs **35%** to qualify but does not count in the merit list, while Intelligence, Maths and GK each need **40%**. A child who aces Maths and ignores English can still fail the paper. For Class 9, each paper needs **50%** — there is no carrying a weak paper with a strong one. And there is **no negative marking** in either class, so every question gets an answer on exam day.

## After the exam: what happens next

The written test is only Stage I. The published schedule puts the **result in January 2027**, followed by the **medical examination and interview in February–March 2027**, and the final merit list in March 2027, with joining on 1 April 2027. Selection runs through four stages — written test, medical, interview, then a final category-wise merit list — and the interview carries real weight: 20 marks for Class 6, 50 for Class 9. So the child who clears the written test in December should start preparing for the interview, not relax.

Five schools are in play — Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belagavi and Bengaluru — and seats are vacancy-based, so the merit list, not a fixed number, decides who gets in.

## What to do this week

1. **Bookmark the portal today** — exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet — and check it every Monday and Thursday for the city slip.
2. **Keep the application number and date of birth handy** — that is all the admit-card download needs.
3. **Book one full mock this Sunday, 15 November**, in real exam conditions: same time of day, same 150 minutes, phone in another room.
4. **Do not start a new book.** Finish the ones already open.

Thirty days sounds short. It is also exactly enough — if the next four Sundays are used properly.
