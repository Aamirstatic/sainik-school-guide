The RMS CET 2026 regular application window closes **Tuesday, 20 October at 5 PM**. Miss it, and you don't lose your chance — you lose **Rs. 500** and five extra days.

If you have already submitted, this article is still for you: what happens *after* you hit submit matters just as much. Read to the end.

## The deadline mechanics, exactly as they are

The NTA RMS CET portal has two deadlines that people constantly confuse — the **form submission** deadline and the **fee transaction** deadline. They are six hours apart, and both are hard stops:

| Event | Date and time |
|---|---|
| Regular application window closes | **20 Oct 2026, 5:00 PM** |
| Last successful fee transaction | **20 Oct 2026, 11:50 PM** |
| Late application window | 21–25 Oct 2026, up to 5:00 PM |
| Late fee payment closes | 25 Oct 2026, up to 11:50 PM |
| Written exam (pen-paper, OMR) | **13 Dec 2026, Sunday** |

The dangerous scenario is this: you submit the form at 5 PM on 20 October, tell yourself you'll pay tomorrow, and wake up to discover the payment link for the regular window closed at 11:50 PM. **Finish the payment the same day you submit.** A form without a successful fee transaction is an incomplete application.

## What the last two days actually cost you

If you apply between 21 and 25 October, every part of the process is identical — except the fee. Here is the honest price of waiting:

| Category | Regular fee | Late-window fee |
|---|---|---|
| General / OBC / OBC-NCL | Rs. 1,000 | **Rs. 1,500** |
| SC / ST / wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action | Rs. 750 | **Rs. 1,250** |

Fees are paid online through credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI. The Rs. 500 late fee is not refundable — it is simply the price of the extra five days. If your documents are ready this weekend, there is no reason to pay it.

## Who can apply — age check

Rashtriya Military Schools are English-medium residential schools under the Ministry of Defence, and the eligibility is narrow. Check the birth-date window before you start the form:

- **Class 6:** born between **1 April 2014 and 31 March 2017** (10–13 years as on 31 March 2027)
- **Class 9:** born between **1 April 2011 and 31 March 2014** (13–16 years as on 31 March 2027)

Wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action get a six-month relaxation in the upper age limit. Class 6 candidates must have passed Class 5 (or be studying in it and pass before admission); Class 9 needs Class 8 from a recognised school.

## The five schools you're applying to

One form, five schools: **Chail** (Himachal Pradesh), **Ajmer** and **Dholpur** (Rajasthan), **Belgaum** and **Bengaluru** (Karnataka). One thing worth knowing about how the process works: NTA handles the application process, the written examination, evaluation and the Stage-1 result. Everything after Stage-1 — the subsequent selection stages — is handled by the Rashtriya Military Schools themselves.

## After you submit: the roadmap

Submitting the form is only step one. Here is what the calendar looks like from here — **CONFIRMED** items are per the notification; the rest is EXPECTED from the pattern:

- ✅ **Regular window:** closes 20 Oct, 5 PM (this Tuesday)
- ✅ **Late window:** 21–25 Oct, +Rs. 500
- 🔶 **Correction window:** EXPECTED around 27–28 October — not officially announced yet. Keep your photo, signature and documents correct *now* rather than relying on it.
- 🔶 **City intimation and admit card:** EXPECTED in late November / early December, per the usual NTA rhythm — watch the portal, not WhatsApp forwards.
- ✅ **Exam:** 13 December 2026, Sunday — pen-and-paper OMR, multiple-choice questions, 63 cities across India.
- 🔶 **Results and later stages:** handled by the schools after NTA's Stage-1 result.

Parents filling both RMS CET and AISSEE forms should remember: RMS CET (13 Dec) and AISSEE (31 Jan 2027, tentative) are separate exams with separate portals. One does not substitute for the other — [yesterday's final-weekend checklist](/news/rms-cet-2026-3-days-left-final-weekend-checklist-2026-10-17/) covered the document side; this piece covers the mechanics.

## Why the last weekend is the danger zone

Every year, the heaviest server load hits between 5 PM and 11:50 PM on the closing day. Uploads fail, payments time out, and OTPs arrive late. If you are reading this on Sunday: **do not plan to fill the form on Tuesday.** Do it today or Monday morning, pay immediately, and download the confirmation page. A screenshot is not a confirmation — the downloaded PDF from the portal is.

## FAQs

**When does the RMS CET 2026 regular application window close?**
20 October 2026 at 5 PM. The application fee can be paid until 11:50 PM the same night on the NTA RMS CET portal.

**What happens if I apply after 20 October 2026?**
You enter the late window (21 to 25 October, 5 PM) and pay an extra Rs. 500: Rs. 1,500 for General/OBC or Rs. 1,250 for SC/ST and KIA wards.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 exam date?**
13 December 2026 (Sunday). It is a pen-and-paper OMR exam with multiple-choice questions, held in 63 cities across India.

**Which Rashtriya Military Schools can I get into through RMS CET?**
Five schools: Chail (Himachal Pradesh), Ajmer and Dholpur (Rajasthan), and Belgaum and Bengaluru (Karnataka). NTA handles the application, exam, evaluation and Stage-1 result; the schools handle the later selection stages.
