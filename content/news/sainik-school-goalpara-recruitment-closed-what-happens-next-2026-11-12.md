## What happens next

This is the part nobody can rush, and the notice is honest about it. The school will first scrutinise the applications it received — checking eligibility, certificates and the demand draft — and then shortlist candidates. The **written test and interview dates will be intimated by email**. No date has been announced, and no list has been published yet.

So if you applied, here is the honest picture: do not expect a call tomorrow. Shortlisting takes days, sometimes weeks. What you can do is make sure the school can actually reach you.

## Your "while you wait" checklist

1. **Guard the email address you put on the form.** The test and interview call will come by email. Check the inbox and spam folder daily — school emails from a new sender often land in spam.
2. **Keep your phone number live.** If the school needs a document clarified, it will call the number on your form.
3. **Keep the proof safe.** Hold on to your speed-post receipt (or the acknowledgement if you hand-delivered), a photocopy of the full application, and the demand draft details. If there is ever a question about whether your form arrived in time, that receipt is your answer.
4. **Keep your certificates in one folder.** Mark sheets, degree and B.Ed. certificates, CTET/STET certificate for TGT posts, caste certificate and experience letters — the same set you attached to the form. Shortlisted candidates are usually asked to bring originals.
5. **Do not pay anyone who promises selection.** No agent or "school contact" can move your name up the list. The school communicates by email, and the process is a written test followed by an interview. Anyone asking for money for a confirmed seat is running a scam — report it, don't pay it.
