Forty-eight hours. That is all that separates you from the regular-fee deadline for the UP Sainik Schools Entrance Examination 2027-28 — 25 October 2026, 5 PM. And if you are reading this with the sinking feeling that you may have mistyped something on the form — a letter in the name, the wrong category, a blurry photo — here is the good news: the UP Sainik Schools Society gives every applicant one edit round.

The correction window runs from **3 to 5 November 2026, closing at 5 PM on 5 November**. Three days, one hard cutoff. Here is how to use them properly — starting now, before the deadline rush.

The regular fee is ₹1,000; missing the 25 October deadline doubles it to ₹2,000 — we broke down that decision [here](/news/up-sainik-school-late-fee-window-oct-26-31-decision-guide-2026-10-22/). Applications go only through [upsainikschool.org](https://upsainikschool.org) for the two UP-run schools: Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow, and Sainik School, Gorakhpur.

## Before 25 October: submit clean, not fast

The correction window is a safety net, not a strategy. Fields the society locks — if it locks them — cannot be changed at any price. So submit your form this weekend with this checklist, not after:

1. **Name spelling against the birth certificate.** Letter-for-letter. Name mismatches between the form, the birth certificate and the school records are the single most common reason documents get questioned at the interview stage.
2. **Date of birth against the official certificate.** Class 6 eligibility requires birth between 2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018; Class 9 between 2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015 — both dates inclusive. One digit off here ends the application.
3. **Category selection.** Pick only what your certificate supports. A wrong category can be worse than no category — it invites disqualification at document verification.
4. **Gender and class.** Lucknow admits boys only to Class 6; Class 9 there is open to boys and girls, while Gorakhpur takes both genders in both classes.
5. **Father's domicile details.** Both the applicant and the father must be domiciled in Uttar Pradesh — this is checked.
6. **Mobile number and email.** The OTP, the correction-window access and the admit-card alerts all flow here. Use a number you will keep till March 2027.
7. **Photo and signature clarity.** A dark, cropped, in-focus photo and a signature that fits the box. Blurry uploads are the most common *fixable* mistake.
8. **Print the confirmation page.** Save the application number, password and payment receipt. You will need the login credentials for the correction window, the admit card and the interview letter.

## On 3 November: how the window actually works

When the window opens, log in at upsainikschool.org and look for the correction link on your dashboard:

1. **Read the editable-field list first.** The society publishes which fields can be changed when the window opens. In NTA-style correction windows, contact details, category, address and uploaded documents are typically editable, while identity-critical fields such as the candidate's name and date of birth are often locked. That is a general pattern, not a promise for this portal — the list on the dashboard is the final word.
2. **Fix everything in one sitting.** The window closes at 5 PM on 5 November. Do not plan to "do it tomorrow" — portal traffic spikes on the last evening and sessions time out.
3. **Re-upload documents in the portal's stated format.** If the photo or signature was the problem, prepare fresh scans that meet the size and format requirements printed on the upload page, then replace them.
4. **Submit and print again.** A correction that is edited but not re-submitted does not count. Print the updated confirmation page and keep it with the original.
5. **No more edits after 5 November.** There is no second round. After the window closes, the data you have is the data you sit the exam with — admit cards come out after 25 November with those exact details.

## Why this matters more than parents think

A form error that survives the correction window does not disappear — it travels with you. At the interview and medical stage (February 2027), and again at final admission, original documents are verified against the application data. A category you cannot prove, a DOB that does not match the birth certificate, a name that differs across documents — any of these can convert a qualified candidate into a rejected file. Three days in November is a cheap price for avoiding that.

File before Sunday 5 PM. Review everything this week. Then use 3–5 November as your final quality check — not your first draft.

## FAQs

**When is the UP Sainik School correction window for the 2027-28 session?**
The correction window runs from 3 to 5 November 2026, closing at 5 PM on 5 November, as reported independently by the Times of India, Jagran Josh and Careers360.

**Can I still apply for UP Sainik School 2027-28 after 25 October 2026?**
Yes — with a late fee. Applications close at the regular fee of ₹1,000 on 25 October 2026 at 5 PM; from 26 to 31 October you can still apply by paying ₹2,000 total, until 5 PM on 31 October.

**Which fields can I edit in the UP Sainik School correction window?**
The society has not published a detailed editable-field list in advance. The portal will display which fields are editable when the window opens on 3 November. As a general guide from NTA-style correction windows, contact details, category and uploaded documents are usually editable while identity-critical fields may be locked — confirm on upsainikschool.org.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam 2027-28?**
The entrance examination is tentatively scheduled for 20 December 2026, with admit cards expected after 25 November 2026.
