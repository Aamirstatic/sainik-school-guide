If your child is in Class 5 or 6 and you have been thinking only about Sainik Schools, here is the second big date on the calendar: the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) for Class 6 admission is confirmed for **28 November 2026** (CONFIRMED, via NVS schedule as reported by The Telegraph, NDTV, Times Now and Jagran Josh) — 54 days from today.

Navodaya Vidyalayas matter to AISSEE families for a simple reason: they are the country's other major network of free, residential, merit-based schools. Admission to Class 6 is through this single selection test, and the application costs nothing. Unlike Sainik Schools, they are not defence-oriented — but for a bright Class 5 student, they are the same kind of ticket out: free quality education with boarding and lodging.

## How it overlaps with AISSEE 2027 preparation

![Students sitting at desks writing an exam in a hall](/images/inbody/jnvst-class-6-exam-november-28-54-days-to-go-2026-10-05.webp)

The overlap is real. JNVST's mental-ability and arithmetic sections build the exact same skills AISSEE tests under its Intelligence and Mathematics sections. And the timing works in your favour: the JNVST on 28 November becomes a full, real exam-hall rehearsal for AISSEE 2027, tentatively scheduled for **31 January 2027** (EXPECTED, per NTA's 2026-27 exam calendar) — about two months later.

That said, the two exams are not the same. AISSEE's Class 6 paper runs 150 minutes with 125 questions across four sections including GK, and qualifying requires 25% in each section plus 40% aggregate. JNVST is shorter, has only three sections, and is free. Do not treat one as a perfect mock of the other — treat the JNVST as warm-up pressure, not a scorecard.

## What to do in the next 54 days

1. **Download the admit card on time.** NVS issues admit cards on navodaya.gov.in in November (EXPECTED, per the official schedule cycle). Check the portal in the second week of November — do not wait for SMS forwards.
2. **Prioritise mental ability.** It is the biggest section of the JNVST and the one most Class 5 students have never seen in school exams. Fifteen focused minutes a day beats weekend cramming.
3. **Keep AISSEE prep running.** If your child is also targeting AISSEE 2027, remember the NTA form is expected in October — two application timelines can overlap. Keep documents ready: photograph, signature, birth certificate, domicile.
4. **Do not compare the two exams in front of your child.** Different papers, different competition pools, different dates. One calm exam at a time.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST Class 6 exam for admission to the 2027-28 session?**
The selection test will be conducted on 28 November 2026, as confirmed by NVS and reported by The Telegraph, NDTV, Times Now and Jagran Josh in August 2026. It is a single-phase test for both summer-bound and winter-bound schools.

**What is the JNVST Class 6 exam pattern?**
The paper carries 100 marks with 80 questions, to be solved in 2 hours. It tests mental ability, arithmetic, and language skills.

**Is the JNVST free?**
Yes. The application was free, and Navodaya Vidyalayas provide free education along with free boarding and lodging for admitted students.

**Can a child prepare for both JNVST and AISSEE 2027?**
Yes. Mental-ability and arithmetic preparation overlaps heavily, and the AISSEE 2027 exam is tentatively scheduled for 31 January 2027 — about two months after the JNVST. Note that JNVST registration for this cycle closed on 10 August 2026, so this year's route is only for families who already applied.
