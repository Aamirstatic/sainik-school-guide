Almost every RIMC aspirant's calendar stops at 6 December 2026 — the written exam. That is a mistake. The written paper is stage one of three, and the second stage, the **viva-voce**, is the one most families start preparing for far too late. Consider this your early warning, 49 days out.

## The three stages, in order

The Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun, selects its Class 8 cadets through a written exam, a viva-voce, and a medical examination — in that order:

| Stage | What it is | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Written exam | English (125), Mathematics (200), General Knowledge (75) | 400 |
| 2. Viva-voce | Oral test of intelligence, personality, communication | 50 |
| 3. Medical | Fitness exam at a military hospital | Qualifying |
| **Total for merit** | Written + viva-voce | **450** |

Only candidates who score **at least 50% in each written subject** are called for the viva. That bar is per subject, not aggregate — a brilliant maths score cannot rescue a GK paper below 37.5 marks. This is where many strong students quietly fall: they aim for a good total instead of protecting every single paper.

## What the viva actually tests

The viva-voce is not a second written exam conducted out loud. Multiple education sources describe it as an assessment of **intelligence, personality, confidence, and communication skills**, with general awareness thrown in. In practice, that means a panel conversation — questions about the child, their interests, school life, current affairs they can discuss with opinions, and basic reasoning.

Two things parents should understand:

1. **It is a conversation, not a quiz.** A child who can chat naturally about what they read beats a child who memorised facts but freezes when asked "why do you think so?"
2. **English matters here more than anywhere else.** The written English paper is already the section RIMC aspirants underestimate; in the viva, fluency and the ability to express a thought clearly carry the marks.

One source also cites a **50% qualifying bar in the viva itself** — treat that as worth preparing for but verify it against the official December-cycle notice when it is published.

## What to do this week (49 days to the written)

You cannot prepare for a personality test in a weekend, but you can start the habits now:

- **Read aloud, daily.** Fifteen minutes of reading a newspaper or a story aloud builds the fluency and pace the viva rewards.
- **One "opinion" question at dinner.** Ask your child what they think about something in the news and why. Defend-able opinions are exactly what panels probe.
- **Protect the 50% floor in GK.** It is the shortest paper (75 marks, one hour) and the easiest one to let slip below the qualifying line. Current affairs, general science, history, and geography — steady, daily, small doses.
- **Watch rimc.edu.in after the written.** Results come as state-wise and All-India merit lists, separately for boys and girls, and viva intimation follows for qualifiers.

## Why this stage decides seats

RIMC takes a small number of cadets each cycle — roughly three dozen across all states and union territories, with most states sending a single candidate. When the intake is that small, the written exam filters; the viva **ranks**. Two children with similar written totals can end several places apart on the final 450-mark merit list because of 20–30 viva marks. Start treating the viva as a real paper — because it is one.

## FAQs

**What is the RIMCEE 2026 viva-voce?**
It is a 50-mark oral stage held only for candidates who score at least 50 percent in each written subject. It tests intelligence, personality, and communication, and its marks feed into the final merit list.

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 viva-voce be held?**
The exact date is not announced yet. Written results are published as state-wise and All-India merit lists on rimc.edu.in, and viva-voce intimation follows for qualified candidates.

**Do you need 50 percent in the viva too?**
At least one education source cites a 50 percent qualifying bar for the viva as well, but this should be confirmed against the official notice for the December 2026 cycle when it is released.

**What comes after the viva-voce in RIMC selection?**
A medical examination at a military hospital. Only medically fit candidates are finally selected, and the final merit combines written and viva-voce scores.
