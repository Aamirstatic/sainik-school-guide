A rejection costs you more than the Rs 1,000 fee; it costs the seat.

## 7 checks before you hit pay

**1. Domicile of both child AND father.** This is the strictest UP Sainik School rule and the one most out-of-state families miss: the child's own domicile certificate is not enough — the father must also be a UP domicile holder. If that is not your case, do not fill this form; [AISSEE covers the other three UP schools (Mainpuri, Jhansi, Amethi) for everyone](/news/up-sainik-school-vs-aissee-which-form-to-fill-2026-10-07/).

**2. Name spelling matched to the birth certificate.** Write the child's name and date of birth exactly as they appear on the birth certificate issued by the Nagar Nigam, Nagar Palika or Zila Panchayat — not as the school diary spells it. Mismatches are the most common reason forms come back.

**3. School + class choice that respects the girls' rule.** For a daughter seeking Class 6, only UP Sainik School Gorakhpur admits girls — Lucknow Class 6 is boys only. Both schools admit girls in Class 9. Picking Lucknow Class 6 for a daughter is an instant rejection.

**4. Age band, not guesswork.** The prospectus gives Class 6 applicants a birth window of roughly mid-2015 to early-2018 and Class 9 of mid-2012 to early-2015 — read the official prospectus on upsainikschool.org before assuming your child qualifies. Do not fill the form first and check later.

**5. Photo and signature scanned to spec.** Keep a recent passport-size photo and the child's signature ready before you start. Uploads that are blurred, old, or the wrong size are the leading cause of correction-window stress — and only 3 to 5 November is available to fix them.

**6. Caste certificate ready, if claiming reservation.** OBC (27%), SC (21%), ST (2%) and EWS (10%) reservations exist, but the school cancels candidature if a caste certificate is later found false. Carry the real certificate; do not claim a category you cannot document.

**7. Pay before 25 October, 5 pm — and keep the receipt.** After the deadline the fee doubles to Rs 2,000 and there is no refund if you made an avoidable error. Save the submitted form's PDF and the payment receipt in two places; you will need them at counselling.

## Why it matters

The exam is a 200-mark, 2.5-hour OMR test (Maths, English, GK, Intelligence) across ten centres — Agra, Ayodhya, Bareilly, Ghaziabad, Gorakhpur, Jhansi, Meerut, Prayagraj, Varanasi and Lucknow. Roughly three times the number of seats are called for the medical + interview round in February 2027 at Lucknow, so every shortlisted child matters. The form is the cheapest part of this journey. Getting it right takes one careful evening — getting it wrong costs the season.

## FAQs

**What is the last date for the UP Sainik School 2027-28 form?**
25 October 2026 at 5 pm with the normal fee of Rs 1,000. Applications stay open till 31 October 2026 at 5 pm with a late fee of Rs 2,000.

**Where do I fill the UP Sainik School application form?**
On the official UP Sainik Schools Society portal, upsainikschool.org, through the Entrance Exam (Class 6 and 9) 2027-28 link. The form is online only.

**Is there a correction window for the UP Sainik School form?**
Yes — 3 to 5 November 2026. Only limited fields can be corrected, so it is better to get the form right the first time.

**Can children from other states apply to UP Sainik School?**
No. Both the child and the father must hold a valid Uttar Pradesh domicile certificate. Applications without this are rejected.
