## Eleven days left — and this is not last year's form

If your child is applying for AISSEE 2027, the form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM** — eleven days from today. The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**, seventy-one days away.

Here's the thing most parents miss: the form looks almost identical to last year's, but five things about this cycle are genuinely different. Two of them can cost you money — and one can cost you the form itself.

## What changed

**1. There is a late-fee window — and it is brand new.** Miss 2 December and you can still apply from **3 to 6 December** by paying **Rs 500 extra** over the regular fee. Last year there was no such window at all: when the 2026 deadline passed, NTA simply extended it to 11 November. This year the late window *is* the safety net. Do not plan on another extension; none has been announced.

**2. The whole calendar moved a month later.** The 2027 form opened on 3 November and closes on 2 December. Last year's form opened on 10 October and closed on 30 October. Everything downstream moved with it: the correction window is **7–9 December** this year, against 2–4 November last year.

**3. The exam is thirteen days later.** AISSEE 2027 is on **31 January 2027**; AISSEE 2026 was on 18 January 2026. Thirteen extra days of preparation.

**4. More new Sainik Schools are taking admissions.** The 2027 bulletin lists **80 new Sainik Schools for Class 6 and 40 for Class 9**, up from 69 and 19 in the 2026 cycle. That is more choice on the form — and more reason to read the school list in the bulletin instead of picking names you recognise. [We explained the 80/40 split here.](/news/aissee-2027-new-sainik-schools-80-class-6-40-class-9-explained-2026-11-06/)

**5. The correction window is one-time-only — and two fields stay locked.** Last year's extended correction window let candidates change the mobile number, email, address, parents' names, class, medium, photograph, signature and certificates, and even the exam city within the same state. But the candidate's **name and date of birth could not be changed** — and the 2027 bulletin carries the same one-time-only rule. Check the spelling of your child's name against the birth certificate before you submit, because December's correction window will not save it. [Our correction-window audit walks through every field.](/news/aissee-2027-correction-window-7-9-december-audit-confirmation-page-2026-11-18/)

## What did not change

**The fee is identical.** Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards; Rs 700 for SC/ST. Same as 2026, to the rupee.

**The paper is identical.** Class 6: 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes. Class 9: 150 questions, 400 marks, 180 minutes. No negative marking. The qualifying rule is unchanged too — at least **25% in every section and 40% overall**.

**The age windows are identical.** Class 6: born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017**. Class 9: born between **1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014**. Both dates inclusive, as on 31 March 2027.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. The fee must also be paid by then. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December 2026 with an extra fee of Rs 500.

**Is there a late fee window in AISSEE 2027?**

Yes, and it is new this year. Candidates who miss 2 December can still apply from 3 to 6 December 2026 by paying Rs 500 extra over the regular fee. Last year there was no late window — NTA extended the deadline instead.

**Has the AISSEE exam pattern changed for 2027?**

No. Class 6 is still 125 questions for 300 marks in 150 minutes, and Class 9 is still 150 questions for 400 marks in 180 minutes. There is no negative marking, and a candidate must score at least 25 percent in each section and 40 percent overall.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**

Sunday, 31 January 2027. Last year the exam was on 18 January 2026, so this year's exam is 13 days later in the calendar.
