## The week in one paragraph

Three different milestones land in the same seven days, and they belong to three different exams — so don't mix them up. **Saturday 28 November** is the JNVST Class 6 exam. **Tuesday 25 November** is the day the UP Sainik School admit card is due to appear. And **around Saturday 29 November**, the RMS CET city slip is expected. Running underneath all of it, the AISSEE 2027 form is in its last nine days, closing on **Wednesday 2 December at 11:50 PM**.

## Saturday 28 November: JNVST Class 6 exam

This is the week's main event, and for most families reading this, it is the only one that needs a child to leave the house. The paper runs **11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**, in a single session for summer-bound and winter-bound Navodaya Vidyalayas alike. Children must be **seated by 11:00 AM** — latecomers are not allowed in, so treat 10:30 AM as your arrival target, not 11:00.

The admit card has been downloadable since **28 October** from navodaya.gov.in, using the registration number and the child's date of birth. If you have not printed it yet, do it today, not on Friday — and check the three things that actually cause trouble on exam morning: the child's name and photo, the centre's name and address, and the medium of the question paper. Centres cannot be changed.

What to carry: the printed admit card, the child's Aadhaar card or the parent's residence certificate uploaded with the form, and a blue or black ballpoint pen. The paper is 80 objective questions for 100 marks — Mental Ability and EVS 40 questions, Arithmetic 20, Language 20 — with no negative marking and 1.25 marks per correct answer. To qualify, a child needs at least 14 in Mental Ability and EVS, 7 in Arithmetic and 7 in Language. Our [day-by-day final-week plan](/news/jnvst-2027-5-days-to-exam-28-november-day-by-day-plan-2026-11-23/) covers what to revise each day this week.

## After Tuesday 25 November: UP Sainik School admit card

The UP Sainik School entrance exam is on **Sunday, 20 December 2026**, and the admit card is due **after 25 November** on the UP Sainik Schools Society portal, upsainikschool.org. Check the portal from Tuesday morning rather than waiting for an SMS — admit cards are never sent by post. When it appears, verify the centre address and the child's details the same day; the exam is an OMR paper of 200 marks over 2 hours 30 minutes, and a wrong centre on the card is the one error you cannot fix on exam morning.

## Around Saturday 29 November: RMS CET city slip

The RMS CET is on **Sunday, 13 December 2026**, and the city intimation slip is expected in the last week of November — around **29 November** by the schedule pattern. The city slip only tells you the exam city; the admit card follows about five to seven days before the exam. Both come from the NTA RMS CET portal, and both are free to download. If the slip has not appeared by Monday 30 November, that is not, by itself, a problem — the date was never officially announced — but check the portal daily from the weekend onward.

## Underneath it all: AISSEE 2027, nine days left

The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December at 11:50 PM**, and the fee must be paid by the same time — ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST. This is the last full working week before the deadline, and it is also the last week to fix the certificates that need a government office: domicile, caste and birth-certificate corrections cannot be done in a day. Our [9-days-left certificates guide](/news/aissee-2027-9-days-left-certificates-need-office-visit-2026-11-23/) lists exactly which ones to chase first.

If you do miss 2 December, there is one safety net: the late-fee window from **3 to 6 December** with an extra ₹500, and then the correction window from **7 to 9 December**. After 6 December, there is no second chance to apply.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam?**
Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM. Children must be seated by 11:00 AM, and the admit card has been available to download since 28 October 2026 from navodaya.gov.in.

**When will the RMS CET 2026 city slip come?**

**When does the UP Sainik School admit card come?**
After 25 November 2026, as per the UP Sainik Schools Society's instructions. The exam is on Sunday, 20 December 2026. Download it from upsainikschool.org as soon as it appears.
