If you have been watching Sainik School job notices this week, here is one more — and this one is for non-teaching staff. Sainik School Ambikapur in Chhattisgarh has invited online applications for four posts: one Nursing Assistant and three General Employees, both on a regular pay scale. The application link is already live on the school's own website, so eligible candidates can apply right away.

## The four posts at a glance

| Post | Vacancies | Category | Pay scale |
|------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **Nursing Assistant** | 1 | Unreserved | Level 4, 7th Central Pay Commission |
| **General Employee** | 3 | 1 SC, 2 Unreserved | Level 1, 7th Central Pay Commission |

## Who can apply

**Nursing Assistant (1 post).** You need a diploma or degree in Nursing with five years of experience. Ex-servicemen from the Medical Assistant trade with at least five years of service after training are also eligible. The age limit is 18 to 50 years as on 1 February 2027. Higher qualifications, knowledge of computer applications, and the ability to understand, read, write and communicate in Hindi and English are listed as desirable.

**General Employee (3 posts).** You need to have passed Matriculation (Class 10) or an equivalent examination from a recognised State or Central Board. The age limit is 18 to 50 years as on 1 January 2027. Higher qualifications and skills in computers, gardening, plumbing, electrical work and other relevant areas are desirable, and candidates should be able to understand, read and communicate in Hindi and English.

## Fee and last date — read this carefully

The advertisement, as reported by Odisha TV, sets a **non-refundable registration fee of ₹1,500** and says applications stay open for **21 days from the date of the advertisement's publication**. The notice appeared on 14 November 2026.

Here is the honest part: one job portal lists different figures — a fee of ₹500 for General, OBC and EWS candidates and ₹250 for SC, ST and PwD candidates, with a last date of 4 December 2026. Those two versions cannot both be right. Do not pay anything until you have read the official notification on the school website and confirmed the fee and the exact last date there. If the figures still look unclear, call the school on the number given in the advertisement before you submit.

## How to apply

Apply online only, through the application link on the school's official website — **sainikschoolambikapur.org.in**. The form asks for personal and academic details, work experience, a photograph, signature, Aadhaar card and certificates, and details of the recruitment fee paid. Keep scanned copies ready within the file-size limits the portal states.

Candidates must also download the duly completed application form, along with the supporting documents, and submit them on the day of the selection test. The school has reserved the right to cancel the recruitment process for any post at any stage, or to reject incomplete applications without assigning a reason.

## What to do next

1. Open **sainikschoolambikapur.org.in** and read the official notification in full.
2. Confirm the fee and the exact last date from the notification — not from any job portal.
3. Keep your nursing diploma or Class 10 certificate, Aadhaar, photograph and signature scans ready before you start the form.
4. Apply well before the last day. The portal, not a deadline extension, is what you are racing.

This is the fourth Sainik School recruitment with a deadline this month, after Tilaiya (17 November), Nagrota (20 November) and Purulia's walk-in on 23 November. If Ambikapur fits your qualification, do not let the fee confusion slow you down — the official notice settles it in two minutes.
