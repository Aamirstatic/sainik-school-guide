December is not just a month anymore for defence-school aspirants — it is a gauntlet. Three separate entrance exams land within fifteen days of each other: **RIMCEE on 6 December**, **RMS CET on 13 December**, and the **UP Sainik School exam on 20 December**. And just to keep the year-end interesting, the JNVST Class 6 paper sits a week before all of them, on 28 November. If your child is appearing in more than one, this is the month that decides their year.

## Why these three get mixed up — and why they shouldn't

Parents often treat these as interchangeable "military school exams." They are not. RIMCEE is the gateway to a single institution — the Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun — and it is a two-stage process: a written exam in English, Mathematics and General Knowledge, then a viva-voce for those who clear it (tentatively April 2027). RMS CET is a single pen-and-paper MCQ test for five schools across four states — Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum and Bengaluru. The UP Sainik School exam is the outlier: one 200-mark OMR paper in 2 hours 30 minutes, run by the UP Sainik Schools Society, with strict domicile rules that quietly disqualify families every year.

The preparation overlap is real — mathematics, English and general knowledge are common ground — but the pace of the papers is very different. A child sitting the UP paper on 20 December a week after RMS CET needs to mentally switch gears, not just subjects.

## What to do this week

1. **RMS CET is the last open door.** If your child hasn't applied, file before 20 October (5 pm) at exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet. After that, only the 21–25 October late-fee route remains.
2. **RIMCEE candidates: use the correction window.** It runs 9–11 October — three days to fix a wrong date of birth, blurred photograph, or a swapped category. After that, no edits.
3. **UP Sainik School forms: don't wait for 25 October.** The father-child domicile rule is the silent killer of applications — both must hold a UP domicile certificate.
4. **Map the gaps between papers.** There are 7 days between RIMCEE and RMS CET, and 7 more to the UP exam. Plan full-length mock papers in each exam's exact format, not generic practice.

## FAQs

**Which defence-school entrance exams are held in December 2026?**
Three: the RIMCEE Stage-1 written exam for RIMC Dehradun on 6 December, the RMS CET for Rashtriya Military Schools on 13 December, and the UP Sainik School entrance exam on 20 December (tentative). AISSEE 2027 for Sainik Schools follows on 31 January 2027 (tentative).

**Is the RMS CET 2026 application still open?**
Yes — the NTA application window runs till 20 October 2026 (5 pm), with a late-fee window from 21 to 25 October. The fee is Rs 1000 for General/OBC and Rs 750 for SC/ST and wards of personnel killed in action.

**Are the RIMCEE and RMS CET papers the same?**
No. RIMCEE Stage-1 covers English, Mathematics and General Knowledge in a written exam plus a viva-voce stage for shortlisted candidates. RMS CET 2026 is a pen-and-paper MCQ test for Class 6 and 9 admission to the five Rashtriya Military Schools.
