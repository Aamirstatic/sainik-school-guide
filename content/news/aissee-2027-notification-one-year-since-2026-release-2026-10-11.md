![A study planner and calendar marking the one-year anniversary of last year's AISSEE notification release](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-notification-one-year-since-2026-release-2026-10-11.webp)

There is a date on the calendar that should matter to every AISSEE 2027 parent. Yesterday — 10 October — was the exact one-year anniversary of the AISSEE 2026 notification release. NTA dropped it on Friday, 10 October 2025, opened applications the same day, and kept the window open till 30 October 2025.

So what did the portal look like this morning, one year later?

## What the portal shows today (CONFIRMED)

A live check of [exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/) on 11 October 2026 shows the "Latest News" column carrying only AISSEE-2026 items: the 2026 score card, the qualified/not-qualified lists for the 18 January 2026 exam, result declarations, final answer keys for Class VI and IX, AISSAC-2026 counselling, and the 2026 admit-card and city-intimation notices. **There is no AISSEE 2027 notification, no information bulletin, and no "apply online" link.**

That is not unusual, but it is worth saying plainly: the 2027 cycle has not officially begun.

## The pattern from last year (CONFIRMED)

The 2026 cycle ran like this, all dates confirmed from contemporary reporting:

- **10 October 2025** — notification released; applications opened the same day
- **30 October 2025 (5 pm)** — last date to apply
- **31 October 2025** — last date for fee payment
- **2–4 November 2025** — correction window
- **18 January 2026** — examination
- **27 February 2026** — result declaration

Application fee was Rs 850 for General/OBC/Defence candidates and Rs 700 for SC/ST candidates, and the exam ran offline on OMR sheets — 150 minutes and 300 marks for Class VI, 180 minutes and 400 marks for Class IX.

The 2027 exam date — **31 January 2027**, from NTA's official exam calendar — is two weeks later in the season than 2026's 18 January. That alone could explain a notification that lands slightly later than 10 October. NTA is not "late"; it is simply not on last year's exact schedule.

## What to finish while you wait

**112 days** remain to the exam. The notification, when it lands, will give you roughly three weeks to apply. Spend this week on things the notification cannot wait for:

1. **Confirm the age bracket.** For Class VI, the child must be 10–12 years old on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017, EXPECTED per last year's pattern). For Class IX, 13–15 on the same date. Our [AISSEE 2027 application guide](/blog/aissee-application-form-2027-guide/) walks through eligibility in full.
2. **Get the documents in hand.** Birth certificate, domicile certificate, a recent photograph, and a signature file — in the exact NTA format. The [photo and signature requirements](/blog/aissee-application-form-photo-signature-requirements/) cover the specs.
3. **Ignore "leaked dates".** Every October, social media fills with fake notification screenshots and coaching-centre "confirmed dates". The only date that exists is 31 January 2027. Everything else is expected, not confirmed — and we label it that way too.
4. **Bookmark the portal, not rumour pages.** Check the NTA portal's "Latest News" column once a day. The day the 2027 bulletin appears, this site will publish a same-day breakdown of every date, fee, and eligibility rule in it.

No. As of 11 October 2026, NTA's official AISSEE portal still lists only AISSEE-2026 items. The notification has not been released yet.

It was released on Friday, 10 October 2025 — exactly one year before today's date. The application window ran from 10 to 30 October 2025.
