> **Last verified: 29 September 2026, 6:20 PM IST.** This is an **explainer**, not breaking news — no AISSEE 2027 notification has been released as of today. Here is what the last three NTA cycles reveal, so your family enters October fully prepared.

October begins tomorrow, and for Sainik School aspirant families this is the month that decides whether the admission process starts calmly or in a last-week panic. **CONFIRMED:** NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 notification or information bulletin as of today. The only portal that matters is [aissee.nta.nic.in](https://aissee.nta.nic.in) — anything appearing anywhere else first is a rumour, not a release.

![Students writing a written examination in a classroom](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-notification-october-watch-2026-09-29.webp)

## What the calendar confirms

The one thing NTA has put in writing is the exam itself. The NTA Examination Calendar 2026-27 (public notice dated 16 September 2026) lists **31 January 2027** as the tentative date — that is **124 days** from today. "Tentative" means it can shift, but every serious plan should treat it as the working date. [Families are already using the countdown](https://sainikschooleastsiang.in/news/aissee-2027-notification-watch-125-days-to-go-2026-09-28/).

## What the last three cycles say

The past three cycles show a consistent rhythm: the notification lands **several weeks before the January exam**, which puts October–December firmly in the expected window (reported by [edexlive, 8 September 2026](https://www.edexlive.com/exams/saink-school-exam-aissee-2027-no-dates-yet-but-the-previous-three-cycles-reveal-a-pattern)).

Read that carefully: October–December is a *trend*, **not** an official NTA prediction — NTA has said nothing about the 2027 form. But the pattern matters because of last cycle: AISSEE 2026 applications opened on **10 October 2025 and closed on 30 October 2025** — a window of just **20 days**. Families that waited to "prepare documents after the form opens" ran out of calendar.

## What the notification will contain

When the bulletin drops, it will specify: application open and close dates, exam date and city intimation, eligibility and age criteria, the application fee, syllabus and exam pattern, the list of participating schools, reservation provisions, the correction window, the admit-card schedule, and result and counselling details.

One caution: the previous cycle's age eligibility was **10–12 years for Class VI and 13–15 years for Class IX**. That **must be re-confirmed** in the 2027 bulletin — do not treat it as final until NTA says so. Our [detailed age-limit guide]({{< relref "../blog/sainik-school-age-limit-2027-detailed-guide.md" >}}) breaks down the exact cut-off math for both classes.

## The document checklist, ready before October

Because the window was only 20 days last time, get these scanned **before** the form opens: recent passport-size photo, the candidate's signature, the left-hand thumb impression, the date of birth certificate, the domicile certificate, the category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL), and the service certificate for defence wards. [Our full documents-ready guide](https://sainikschooleastsiang.in/news/aissee-2027-documents-ready-before-notification-2026-09-28/) walks through each one.

## The exam itself, in one paragraph

AISSEE 2027 is the single entrance test for **Class VI and Class IX** admission to Sainik Schools and eligible New Sainik Schools, held in **pen-and-paper OMR mode**: Class VI runs **150 minutes for 300 marks**, Class IX **180 minutes for 400 marks** (details via [EntranceZone](https://www.entrancezone.com/admissions/sainik-school-admission/)). For Class VI aspirants, [this four-month study plan](https://sainikschooleastsiang.in/blog/sainik-school-study-plan-class-6/) maps the preparation ahead.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**

No. As of 29 September 2026, NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 notification or information bulletin on aissee.nta.nic.in. Keep checking the official portal and get your documents ready now.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 notification expected?**

Expected in October 2026 based on the pattern of the last three cycles, when notifications arrived several weeks before the January exam. This is a trend, not an official NTA announcement.

**Is 31 January 2027 the confirmed AISSEE 2027 exam date?**

It is confirmed as tentative. The date appears in NTA's official Examination Calendar 2026-27 (public notice dated 16 September 2026), marked subject to change. From today, that is 124 days away.

**How long does NTA keep the AISSEE application window open?**

Very short. In the 2026 cycle, applications opened on 10 October and closed on 30 October 2025 — just 20 days. Have the photo, signature, thumb impression, DOB and domicile certificates scanned before the form opens.
