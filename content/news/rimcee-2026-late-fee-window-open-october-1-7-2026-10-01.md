> **Last verified: 1 October 2026.** The dates marked CONFIRMED below are reported today by two independent education portals (Education Times and Telegraph India / Edugraph) covering NTA's RIMCEE schedule. The late-fee amount and the correction window come from a single portal each and are marked EXPECTED — verify them in the official information bulletin.

Missed the 30 September deadline for the Rashtriya Indian Military College entrance exam? You still have a second chance — and it closes in six days. NTA's regular application window for RIMCEE 2026 shut yesterday at 5 PM, but the late-fee window is now open and runs **till 7 October 2026** on the official NTA RIMCEE portal.

![Students writing an exam paper in an exam hall](/images/inbody/rimcee-2026-late-fee-window-open-october-1-7-2026-10-01.webp)

## What happened (CONFIRMED)

The National Testing Agency ran the regular application window for the RIMC Entrance Examination 2026 (for the July 2027 term, admission to Class 8 at RIMC Dehradun) till **30 September 2026, 5:00 PM**. This is the first cycle in which RIMC applications are handled completely online through NTA — no hard copy needs to be submitted.

Parents who missed the deadline can still apply with a late fee from **1 October to 7 October 2026**. Applications go through the official NTA examination portal only.

## Key dates at a glance

| Milestone | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Regular application window | 1 Sep – 30 Sep 2026 (5 PM) | CONFIRMED, closed |
| Late-fee application window | **1 – 7 Oct 2026** | CONFIRMED, open now |
| Correction window | 9 – 11 Oct 2026 | EXPECTED (single source) |
| Written exam (Stage 1) | **6 Dec 2026 (Sunday)** | CONFIRMED |
| Viva voce (Stage 2) | April 2027 (tentative) | EXPECTED (single source) |

## Who can apply (CONFIRMED)

- **Education:** studying in Class 7 or passed Class 7 from a recognised school.
- **Age:** born between **2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015**, both dates inclusive.
- **Admission to:** Class 8 at Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC), Dehradun, for the **July 2027 term**.

## The paper (CONFIRMED)

The written examination is pen-and-paper: **400 marks over 4 hours and 30 minutes**. English and Mathematics papers are descriptive; the General Knowledge paper is objective-type. Candidates who clear the written stage face a viva voce round, after which final admission is subject to both stages plus medical and eligibility checks.

## What to do right now

1. Keep scanned copies ready before you start: photograph, signature, birth certificate, domicile certificate, school studying certificate, category certificate if applicable.
2. Apply only on the official NTA RIMCEE portal — avoid third-party sites.
3. Save your application number, password and payment receipt; you will need them for the admit card.
4. The application fee is category-based — one portal reports Rs. 1,000 for General/OBC-NCL/Defence/Ex-Servicemen and Rs. 750 for SC/ST, with a reported Rs. 500 late fee on top. Both figures are EXPECTED until the official bulletin confirms them.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RIMCEE 2026 with a late fee?**
7 October 2026. The regular window closed on 30 September 2026 at 5 PM; the late-fee window runs 1–7 October 2026.

**How much is the RIMCEE late fee?**
An education portal reports it as Rs. 500 extra. Treat this as EXPECTED and confirm it in the official information bulletin before paying.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 written exam?**
6 December 2026 (Sunday), pen-and-paper. Written qualifiers move to the viva voce stage, tentatively in April 2027 (EXPECTED).

**Who can apply for RIMCEE 2026?**
Students studying in or having passed Class 7 from a recognised school, born between 2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015, for Class 8 admission at RIMC Dehradun for the July 2027 term.
