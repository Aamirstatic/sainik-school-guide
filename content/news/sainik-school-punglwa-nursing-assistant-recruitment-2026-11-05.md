The application is offline. Download the application form from the school's official website, fill it, and send it by post with self-attested copies of your testimonials, a passport-size photograph, and a demand draft if the notification asks for one. The school's address, as given in the listing, is: Principal, Sainik School Punglwa, PO Punglwa B.O, Dist Peren, Nagaland – 797114.

Two practical points. First, postal applications take days, and the 21-day window includes that transit time — post early, and keep the speed-post receipt. Second, Sainik School recruitment notices routinely say no TA or DA is paid for appearing in tests or interviews, and that the school can cancel a vacancy; check whether this notification says the same before you travel.
