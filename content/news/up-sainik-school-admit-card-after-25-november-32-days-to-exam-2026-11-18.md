## The admit card is the next thing on the calendar

If your child applied for UP Sainik School — Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School in Lucknow or UP Sainik School in Gorakhpur — the waiting now has a date. The admit card for the 2027-28 entrance exam will be available to download **after 25 November 2026**, from the candidate login on the official portal, **upsainikschool.org**. That is seven days from today.

The exam itself is on **Sunday, 20 December 2026** — 32 days away. It is a single OMR paper of **200 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes**, covering English, General Knowledge, Mathematics and Intelligence, for admission to Class 6 and Class 9.

Here is the thing parents get wrong every year: they treat the admit card as a formality. It is not. It is the first document that tells you where your child is actually sitting on exam day — the centre, the reporting time, the room instructions. Download it the day it lands, and check it the same evening. A wrong centre discovered on 19 December is a crisis. A wrong centre discovered on 26 November is a phone call.

## How to download it — and what to check

1. Go to **upsainikschool.org** and open the entrance-exam login. Do not use a link from a message or a search ad — type the address yourself.
2. Log in with the **application number and password** you created while applying. Keep them safe; you will need the same login for the answer key, the result and the interview letter.
3. Download the admit card and **print two copies** — one for the exam day, one kept at home.
4. Check these seven details immediately: your child's **name** (spelling, exactly as on the birth certificate), **photograph**, **date of birth**, **exam date (20 December 2026)**, **reporting time**, **exam centre name and full address**, and the **instructions** printed on the card.

One honest warning: some older pages on the society's own site still show a 5 December admit-card date. That is last cycle's date — the 2026-27 admit card did come out on 5 December 2025. The 2027-28 prospectus schedule, confirmed by Careers360 and JagranJosh, says **after 25 November 2026**. If the portal does not show your admit card on 25 November itself, check again the next day — and if it is still missing after two days, contact the society rather than assuming the form was rejected.

## The road after 20 December

The written test is only the first gate. The prospectus schedule after it runs like this:

| Stage | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Admit card download | After 25 November 2026 | ✅ Confirmed |
| Written exam (OMR, 200 marks) | Sunday, 20 December 2026 | ✅ Confirmed |
| Tentative answer key | 24 December 2026 | 🔶 Tentative |
| Written exam result | 30 December 2026 | 🔶 Tentative |
| Interview letter download | 7 January 2027 | 🔶 Tentative |
| Interview + medical examination | February 2027 | 🔶 Tentative |
| Final result | 15–20 March 2027 | 🔶 Tentative |

Candidates who clear the written test are called for an interview and a medical examination, on merit. Final selection rests on the written score, the interview, medical fitness and the reservation rules. And remember the rule that quietly disqualifies families every year: **both the candidate and the father must hold a Uttar Pradesh domicile certificate.** If that paperwork is not in order, no score will save the admission — so if you have not already checked it, check it this week, while there is still time to fix it.

## What to do in the next 32 days

Thirty-two days is not the time for new chapters. It is the time for papers. One full 200-mark paper a week, in the exact 2-hour-30-minute window, with the OMR sheet filled in by hand. Between papers, only the weakest section gets new study — everything else gets revision. And keep the admit card, the application number and the password in one place, on paper as well as on the phone. The families who panic in December are almost never the ones whose children are unprepared. They are the ones who cannot find a login.

## FAQs

**When will the UP Sainik School 2027-28 admit card be released?**

After 25 November 2026, on the official portal upsainikschool.org, through the candidate login. Download it with your application number and password, print two copies, and check every detail the same day.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam 2027-28?**
