> **Last verified: 6 October 2026.** Deadlines, the late-fee rule, the correction window and the tentative exam date are ✅ **CONFIRMED** from the official UP Sainik Schools Society site (upsainikschool.org), checked today. The 200-mark paper pattern is from the 2026 admission cycle (reported by TOI) — 🔶 **EXPECTED** to repeat; treat the school's prospectus as final.

If your child is aiming for one of Uttar Pradesh's two Sainik Schools — Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School in Lucknow or UP Sainik School in Gorakhpur — the clock is properly ticking now. Forms have been live since 10 September, and the regular-fee window closes in **19 days**. But the deadline is only half the story. The other half is the exam itself, which looks quite different from AISSEE — and a surprising number of parents mix the two up.

## Where things stand right now

The UP Sainik Schools Society runs its own entrance exam, separate from NTA's AISSEE. Here is the schedule, straight from the society's official homepage:

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Applications opened | 10 September 2026 |
| **Last date (regular fee, Rs 1000)** | **25 October 2026, till 5 pm** |
| Last date with late fee (Rs 2000) | 31 October 2026, till 5 pm |
| Application correction window | 3–5 November 2026, till 5 pm |
| Admit card | After 5 December 2026 |
| **Entrance exam (tentative)** | **Sunday, 20 December 2026** |
| Written result (tentative) | 30 December 2026 |

One detail worth noting on the official site: there is a visible **advisory regarding fraudulent persons** on the homepage. Apply only through upsainikschool.org. Anyone "guaranteeing" admission for a fee is running the exact scam the society is warning you about.

## The 200-mark paper, explained

Unlike AISSEE's two-section format, the UP Sainik School entrance test is a **single OMR paper** — one sitting, one answer sheet:

- **Mathematics & Intelligence**
- **English**
- **General Knowledge**
- **Total: 200 marks · Duration: 2 hours 30 minutes**

That 200-mark, 2.5-hour structure is the pattern from the 2026 cycle, reported by the Times of India, and the society's own process page describes the same scheme for the current cycle — so treat it as EXPECTED unless the prospectus says otherwise. A single paper sounds simpler than AISSEE, but don't let that fool you: 200 marks in 150 minutes means roughly **45 seconds a question**. Speed and clean OMR bubbling matter more here than in almost any other defence-school entrance. Students who qualify in the written test are called for interview and medical examination on merit — admission only clears after all stages.

## Who can actually apply — the domicile rule

This is the rule that quietly disqualifies families every year: **both the candidate and the father must hold a Uttar Pradesh domicile certificate.** If the father's domicile is from another state, the child stays ineligible — no matter how long the family has lived in UP. Age-wise, per the admission portals reproducing the prospectus: Class 6 candidates must be born between **2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018**, and Class 9 candidates between **2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015**. Lucknow's school takes boys only in Class 6 (boys and girls in Class 9); Gorakhpur takes boys and girls in both classes — so if you are applying for your daughter in Class 6, Gorakhpur is your only UP option.

## What to do this week

1. **If you haven't applied, do it now — not on 25 October.** Server congestion on deadline day is a ritual, not a risk worth taking.
2. **Get the domicile certificates in order.** This is the single most common reason UP applications get rejected, and it cannot be fixed during the correction window.
3. **Double-check the photograph rules** before you pay — after payment, the form locks until the correction window (3–5 November), and even then, name, class, mobile number and email stay uneditable.
4. **Plan prep around 20 December.** Roughly 75 days of runway. One full 150-minute OMR paper every week from now is the most honest preparation plan there is.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for UP Sainik School 2027-28?**
25 October 2026 till 5 pm, without a late fee, as per the official UP Sainik Schools Society website. You can still apply till 31 October 2026 (5 pm) with a late fee of Rs 2000.

**What is the UP Sainik School entrance exam pattern?**
It is an OMR-based single paper of 200 marks to be completed in 2 hours 30 minutes, covering Mathematics and Intelligence, English, and General Knowledge. This is the 2026-cycle pattern — confirm it in this year's prospectus before treating it as final.

**Who can apply — is there a domicile rule?**
Yes, and it is strict: both the candidate and the father must hold a Uttar Pradesh domicile certificate. If the father's domicile is from another state, the child is ineligible even if the family currently lives in UP. Class 6 is boys only at Lucknow and boys and girls at Gorakhpur; Class 9 takes both at both schools.

**When is the UP Sainik School 2027-28 entrance exam?**
The official society website lists a tentative exam date of Sunday, 20 December 2026. Admit cards are expected after 5 December 2026, and the correction window runs 3 to 5 November 2026.
