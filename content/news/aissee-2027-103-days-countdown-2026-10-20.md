One hundred and three days. That is where AISSEE 2027 stands today — and it is the last milestone that still feels comfortable. Three days from now, on 23 October, the count drops below 100, and the psychology of the final stretch kicks in for every serious aspirant household.

The notification has still not been released, which is unsettling for parents who are watching the portal daily. But here is the honest position: the exam pattern, the syllabus structure and the qualifying marks (25% per section, 40% aggregate) are stable year to year. Nothing you need for the next fortnight depends on the notification.

## The final stretch in four phases

With 103 days to go — roughly 15 weeks — the preparation has a clear shape:

| Phase | Days left | The job |
|---|---|---|
| Lock-in | 103–90 | Finish every unstarted chapter; first full-length mock under timed conditions |
| Weakness surgery | 89–60 | Revise by error log, not by chapter; fix the topics your mock keeps bleeding marks on |
| Mock season | 59–30 | One mock a week moving to two; OMR bubbling practise starts here |
| Calm run-in | 29–0 | Admit card (🔶 expected mid-January), centres, sleep routine, light revision |

Note the first phase ends exactly where the sub-100-days mark begins. The fortnight you are in right now is the last "big work" window before the grind phase.

## 7 things to finish this week

1. **Print the Class 6 or Class 9 pattern sheet.** 125 questions / 300 marks for Class 6 (150 minutes), 150 questions / 400 marks for Class 9 (180 minutes), no negative marking — pin it where your child studies.
2. **One full mock, timed, OMR-style.** Not to judge — to diagnose. Score by section.
3. **Start the error log.** Every wrong or skipped mock question goes in one notebook with the reason: concept gap, silly slip, or time panic.
4. **Maths first.** It carries half the Class 6 paper (150 of 300 marks) and half of Class 9 (200 of 400) — the highest-ROI section in the exam.
5. **Lock the documents folder.** Birth, domicile, category certificates scanned — the form can open any day and the first 48 hours always see the worst server congestion.
6. **Stop buying new books.** The countdown phase rewards depth, not width. One book per subject, finished, beats four started.
7. **Protect sleep.** The families that peak in January are the ones whose child was sleeping eight hours in October.

## FAQs

**How many days are left for AISSEE 2027?**
103 days as of 20 October 2026. The exam is expected on 31 January 2027, per the NTA exam calendar 2026-27 — the sub-100-days mark begins on 23 October.

**Is the AISSEE 2027 notification out yet?**
No. As of 20 October 2026, NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin. Claims on some websites that the form is out could not be confirmed from any official source.

**Should students wait for the notification before starting preparation?**
No. The exam pattern, syllabus and qualifying marks are stable year to year — Class 6 (300 marks) and Class 9 (400 marks), no negative marking — so the last-100-days plan does not change with the notification.

**When is AISSEE 2027 likely to be held?**
31 January 2027 is the expected date per the NTA exam calendar 2026-27. It is a calendar entry, not a bulletin announcement, so treat it as expected, not final.
