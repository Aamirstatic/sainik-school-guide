## The form ends on Wednesday. The preparation starts on Thursday

For three weeks, every AISSEE 2027 article — including ours — has been about the form: the deadline, the late fee, the photo, the correction window. That conversation ends on **Wednesday, 2 December, at 11:50 PM**, when the regular application window closes.

Then something useful happens: the noise stops. From 3 December to the exam on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**, there is nothing left to file, upload or pay. 59 days of pure preparation. And the families who use December well are the ones whose children walk into the hall calm — we said the same thing in our [post-2 December timeline](/news/aissee-2027-after-2-december-what-happens-next-timeline-2026-11-24/), and it bears repeating, because December is where this exam is actually won.

Here is the thing most parents get wrong at this stage: they keep teaching new topics right up to January. Don't. The plan below is built on one rule — **finish learning by 20 December, then practise and revise**.

## The December plan, week by week

| Week | Dates | The work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3–9 December | Finish the weakest chapters first. Maths first — it carries 150 of 300 marks in Class 6 and 200 of 400 in Class 9. The correction window (7–9 December) is a ten-minute admin job inside this week, not the week's work. |
| 2 | 10–16 December | Complete the syllabus, at least once, every section. First full-length mock paper on Sunday 13 December, in exam conditions: 150 minutes, phone in another room. |
| 3 | 17–23 December | No new topics. Second mock on Sunday 20 December, then two days of error-log review — every wrong answer traced back to the chapter it came from. |
| 4 | 24–31 December | Light revision only on festival days, then a third mock on Sunday 27 December. Revise formulas and GK notes; do not start anything new. |

January then runs on the same rhythm: one mock every Sunday, error-log review the same day, and a taper in the final week. The exam city is announced on **11 January** and admit cards come on **25 January** — check them, print two copies, and keep studying.

## Three rules that make the plan work

**First, protect the Sunday mock.** One full 150-minute paper (Class 6) or 180-minute paper (Class 9) every Sunday morning, in one sitting, with OMR-style bubbling if you have the sheets. A mock taken in pieces is not a mock — it is homework with a timer.

**Second, the error log matters more than the score.** After every mock, write down each wrong answer and the chapter it belongs to. Within three Sundays you will see the pattern: it is almost always one section, not everything. Fix that section and the score moves. Skip the log and you will take the same mock eight times and learn nothing from it.

**Third, maths decides this exam.** Half the paper in both classes is maths. If your child is weak there, December's first two weeks belong to maths — one chapter a day, with the previous day's mistakes redone before moving on. English, intelligence and GK can be revised in shorter daily slots; maths needs the long, quiet hours.

## What not to do in December

Do not buy a new guidebook now. Do not start a new coaching batch in December. Do not let the correction window, the late-fee window or the JNVST and RIMCEE results of other children eat your child's study week — those are admin and other people's exams. And do not, in the last week of December, decide the syllabus is "almost done" when one chapter is still untouched. An untouched chapter in December becomes a blank section in January.

If the form itself is still not submitted, stop reading this and read our [last-weekend plan](/news/aissee-2027-6-days-left-last-weekend-finish-form-sunday-2026-11-26/) instead — finish the form by Sunday night. The study plan starts on Thursday, but only for families whose form is already in.

## FAQs

**How many days are left for AISSEE 2027?**
66 days from 26 November 2026 to the exam on Sunday, 31 January 2027. Once the form closes on 2 December, 59 days of pure preparation remain before the exam.

**What should my child finish by 20 December 2026?**
The full syllabus, at least once. December 3 to 20 is for finishing weak chapters and first full-length mock papers. After 20 December, the work shifts to revision and mock analysis, not new topics.

**How many mock tests should my child take before AISSEE 2027?**
One full-length paper every Sunday from December onwards gives about eight mocks before 31 January. Each mock must be followed by an error-log review the same day, or the mock teaches nothing.

**Does the correction window week affect the study plan?**
No. The correction window runs 7 to 9 December, but it is a ten-minute admin job: check the confirmation page, fix what NTA allows, and get back to studying. Do not let form admin eat a study week.
