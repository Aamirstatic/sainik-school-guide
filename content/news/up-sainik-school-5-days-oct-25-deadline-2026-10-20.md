Five days. That is all that stands between a Rs 1,000 application and a costlier one for UP's two government Sainik Schools — Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School in Lucknow and Sainik School Gorakhpur.

The UP Sainik Schools Society closes the regular application window for the 2027-28 entrance exam (Class 6 and Class 9) on **25 October 2026 at 5 PM**. Apply by then and you pay the regular fee. Miss it and you can still apply until **31 October (5 PM)** — but Jagran Josh reports the fee doubles, from Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,000 total. A few hundred rupees is not what will decide your child's future, but there is no reason to pay them when the reminder is free.

## Why the correction window is your safety net

Every year, families get forms rejected or delayed over the same small errors — a name spelled differently from the birth certificate, a date-of-birth mismatch, a blurry photograph. This year the Society has given applicants a **3-day correction window (3–5 November)** to fix allowed details after submission.

The smart sequence: submit by 25 October without the late fee, then double-check every field during the correction window with a printed confirmation page beside you. The fee is already paid; the correction window is free.

## Who can apply — the quick eligibility check

Jagran Josh, citing the official portal, reports these conditions (verify against the prospectus):

- **Class 6:** studying in or passed Class 5; born between **2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018**
- **Class 9:** studying in or passed Class 8; born between **2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015**
- **Domicile:** both the applicant and the father need valid **Uttar Pradesh domicile** proof
- The exam is for the two UP-government Sainik Schools only — the 33 NTA-run Sainik Schools use AISSEE, a separate form

If the domicile rule is a concern in your family, sort that certificate out this week — it is the one document that cannot be fixed in the correction window.

## What to do tonight or tomorrow

1. **Register on upsainikschool.org tonight** — evenings see lighter portal traffic than deadline afternoons.
2. **Scan documents in advance:** photograph, signature, birth certificate, domicile, category certificate (if any).
3. **Pay online only** (UPI, net banking, debit card) and save the payment receipt — not just the application PDF.
4. **Do not share OTPs or "agent" fees** with anyone promising admission help — there is no backdoor in this process.

If you applied earlier in the window, you are done until November — our [correction-window guide](/news/up-sainik-school-correction-window-nov-3-5-2026-10-16/) covers what to fix when it opens. ## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for UP Sainik School 2027-28 without a late fee?**
25 October 2026, 5 PM. After that, a late-fee window stays open until 31 October 2026, 5 PM, at a higher total fee.

**What is the application fee for UP Sainik School 2027-28?**
Reports put the regular fee at Rs 1,000 and the total in the late window at Rs 2,000. Confirm the exact figure on the official portal before paying.

**Is there a correction window for UP Sainik School applications?**
Yes — 3 to 5 November 2026. Use it to fix names, dates of birth, or uploaded documents before the admit card stage.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam 2027-28?**
20 December 2026 is the tentative exam date, announced by the UP Sainik Schools Society. Admit cards are expected after 25 November 2026.
