# AISSEE 2027 Notification Expected in October: 7 Documents to Keep Ready This Week

> **Last verified: 28 September 2026.** The AISSEE 2027 notification has **not been released** by the NTA as of today. Dates below marked EXPECTED are based on previous-cycle patterns, not official announcements.

October begins this week — and for thousands of parents, that is the month that matters. The National Testing Agency's AISSEE 2027 notification is **expected in October 2026** (EXPECTED), following the pattern of previous cycles, most notably AISSEE 2026, when applications ran from 10 October to 30 October 2025.

Here is the part that catches families off guard every year: in the last cycle, parents got just **20 days** between the notification and the application deadline (CONFIRMED, per the AISSEE 2026 schedule reported by EdexLive). Twenty days to collect documents, scan photos, get certificates attested and fill the form — while school exams and festivals compete for the same evenings. The notification itself has **not yet been released** (CONFIRMED), but the window to prepare for it is right now.

## What happened

Nothing — officially. The NTA's AISSEE portal is still carrying updates related to AISSEE 2026, and no 2027 application schedule has appeared (CONFIRMED, as reported by EdexLive on 8 September 2026). The one fixed point on the calendar is the exam date: **31 January 2027**, listed in the NTA examination calendar 2026-27 released on 16 September 2026 (CONFIRMED, tentative until the bulletin confirms it).

Everything else — application window, correction window, admit-card dates, application fee — will be announced in the information bulletin whenever it drops. And if the last cycle is any guide, the bulletin gives you no breathing room.

## Why this matters for your family

The compressed 2026 window created exactly the problems you would expect: parents discovering a spelling mismatch between the birth certificate and the school records on 28 October, with two days left. A caste certificate that needs the tehsil office's signature — which closes for holidays. A photograph that the portal rejects because it is six months old.

None of these are academic problems. They are paperwork problems, and they are the single most avoidable reason children miss the AISSEE each year. October will not wait for your documents to be in order.

## The 7-document checklist: finish it this week

This list is based on the documents the AISSEE 2026 application required (EXPECTED to repeat for 2027 — confirm against the new bulletin once published):

![Mother helping her child organise admission documents](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-documents-ready-before-notification-2026-09-28.webp)

## Get your paperwork in order before the rush

1. **Birth certificate** — the date of birth on this document must match what you will enter. If there is any discrepancy with school records, start correcting it now, not in October.
2. **Recent passport-size photograph** — fresh, not the one from last year's school ID. NTA portals have historically rejected older or unclear photos.
3. **Scanned signature** — for Class 6 applicants, this is typically the parent's signature. Have a clean scanned copy ready.
4. **Previous class report card** — the marksheet of the last class passed. If the school has not issued it yet, request it this week.
5. **Domicile / residence certificate** — some schools' home-state quotas are verified at the counselling stage (AISSAC), so this matters beyond the application.
6. **Category certificate (if applicable)** — SC/ST/OBC-NCL certificates issued by the competent authority. These take the longest to obtain; if yours is expired or missing, begin immediately.
7. **Disability certificate (if applicable)** — issued by the authorised medical board.

Also keep ready: a valid mobile number and email ID that you actually check (NTA communicates through these), and the online fee payment method — the application fee was **₹850** for General/OBC/Defence wards and **₹700** for SC/ST in the last cycle (EXPECTED to remain similar).
