## What happened

Sainik School Kunjpura (Karnal, Haryana) — one of the oldest Sainik Schools, which made the news this morning for its 45 cadets clearing the NDA written exam — has announced recruitment for **15 teaching and non-teaching posts** spanning regular and contractual engagement. Applications opened on **25 September 2026** and the **last date is 31 October 2026**, leaving just **seven days**.

The post break-up:

| Post | Vacancies | Category |
|------|-----------|----------|
| TGT (General Science) | 1 | OBC |
| TGT (Hindi/Sanskrit) | 2 | SC–1, OBC–1 |
| TGT (Mathematics) | 2 | SC–1, OBC–1 |
| TGT (English) | 1 | UR |
| Horse Riding Instructor | 1 | UR |
| Band Master | 1 | UR |
| Mess Manager | 1 | UR |
| Nursing Sister (Female) | 1 | UR |
| Ward Boys | 5 | UR–4, OBC–1 |

## Why it matters

This is the biggest Sainik School staff round open right now. For comparison, Sainik School [Chittorgarh's 5-post regular-basis round](/news/sainik-school-chittorgarh-recruitment-5-posts-nov-7-2026-10-23/) closes on **7 November 2026**, and [Goalpara's 13-post round](/news/sainik-school-goalpara-recruitment-13-posts-nov-12-2026-10-19/) closes on **12 November 2026**. Kunjpura's deadline is the earliest of the three — and because the application travels by post, waiting until the last day means it arrives after the deadline. ## Who can apply

**Age limits (as on 31 October 2026):**

- **TGT posts:** 21–35 years
- **Band Master:** 21–50 years
- **Horse Riding Instructor, Mess Manager, Nursing Sister, Ward Boys:** 18–50 years

**Qualifications (per the official notice, as reported):**

- **TGTs:** graduation in the relevant subject + B.Ed. + CTET/STET.
- **Horse Riding Instructor:** Class 10 pass + Horse Riding Instructor course.
- **Band Master:** Band Master / Band Major / Drum Major or an equivalent course.
- **Mess Manager:** Class 10 pass + 5 years of experience.
- **Nursing Sister (female):** Nursing Diploma / GNM / B.Sc Nursing + experience.
- **Ward Boys:** Class 10 pass.

**Pay (as reported):** TGT posts are in **Pay Matrix Level 7**; the support posts carry consolidated monthly pay — Horse Riding Instructor and Band Master ₹35,000/month, Mess Manager ₹29,200/month, Nursing Sister ₹30,000/month, and Ward Boys ₹25,000/month. Confirm regular vs. contractual terms for your post in the official notification.

## How to apply — offline, by post

There is no online application. The steps:

1. Download the prescribed application format from **www.sskunjpura.org**.
2. Fill it carefully on A4-size paper.
3. Attach self-attested copies of certificates (birth certificate, marksheets from Matric onwards, degrees, CTET certificate for TGTs, experience testimonials), a passport-size photograph, and the fee.
4. The application fee is **₹500 for all candidates**, non-refundable, paid only through a **demand draft drawn in favour of "Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal", payable at Karnal**.
5. Send the packet to: **The Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal, Haryana — 132023**.

The packet must **reach the school on or before 31 October 2026**. Post it by the middle of next week at the latest — rural postal delivery times mean a parcel sent on the 30th may not arrive in time.

## Selection process

Shortlisted candidates will be called for a **written exam**, a **practical/skill test** (as applicable to the post), and an **interview**, followed by **document verification**. The shortlisted list will be put up on the school website, and shortlisted candidates will be informed by email and post. **No TA/DA** is admissible for attending the selection stages.

## FAQs

**How many posts are open in Sainik School Kunjpura Recruitment 2026?**

Fifteen: 6 TGT posts (General Science 1, Hindi/Sanskrit 2, Maths 2, English 1), plus one Horse Riding Instructor, one Band Master, one Mess Manager, one Nursing Sister and five Ward Boys — a mix of regular and contractual posts.

**What is the last date to apply for Sainik School Kunjpura Recruitment 2026?**

The application must reach the school by post on or before **31 October 2026** — only seven days from today. This is a receipt deadline, so allow postal travel time.

**What is the application fee and how is it paid?**

A non-refundable **₹500 for all candidates**, paid only through a demand draft drawn in favour of "Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal", payable at Karnal.

**Can I apply online for Sainik School Kunjpura Recruitment 2026?**

No. Applications are offline only — download the form from sskunjpura.org and send the filled form with the demand draft and attested documents by post to The Principal, Sainik School Kunjpura, Karnal, Haryana — 132023.
