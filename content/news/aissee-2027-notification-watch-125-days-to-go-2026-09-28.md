# 125 Days to AISSEE 2027: Notification Still Awaited — What to Finish This Week

> **🔄 Last verified: 28 September 2026** — ✅ **CONFIRMED**: AISSEE 2027 notification NOT yet released (NTA portal still shows AISSEE 2026 updates, per EdexLive's 8 Sept check and EntranceZone's 4 Sept check — two independent sources). Exam date **31 January 2027** is ✅ in NTA's official Examination Calendar (notice 16 Sept 2026) but explicitly **tentative**.

The countdown has begun, but the starting gun hasn't fired. Today marks exactly **125 days** to the proposed AISSEE 2027 exam date of 31 January 2027 — and the notification that officially opens the admission cycle still hasn't been released.

This is not unusual. The AISSEE 2026 notification appeared only about a month before its application window, and in that cycle families got just 20 days between the notification and the form deadline. The quiet period is precisely when the best-prepared families pull ahead. Here is where things stand, and what this week is for.

## Why This Week Matters

The pattern of recent cycles is a short fuse. AISSEE 2026's applications ran roughly **10–30 October 2025** — about 20 days — with a correction window in early November. Families who scrambled for documents in October lost prep days in November and December. The families who already had everything scanned and ready treated the form window as a 20-minute administrative task and kept studying.

The other reason this week matters: **the OMR format punishes unpractised students.** AISSEE 2026 was pen-and-paper OMR with multiple-choice questions — Class VI a 150-minute paper, Class IX a 180-minute paper. If your child has only ever solved in a notebook, the first OMR mock usually exposes time-management problems. Now is the time to discover them.

## Your 7-Day Checklist

1. **Gather documents now** — birth certificate, domicile/state residence proof, category certificate (if applicable). Scan them; NTA forms typically need a recent photo and signature in specified formats.
2. **Finish the syllabus first pass** — every chapter touched once, so the notification period can be pure revision and mocks.
3. **Do one full OMR mock this week** — time it strictly: 150 minutes for Class VI, 180 for Class IX. Then [analyse it with the error-log method]({{< relref "../blog/aissee-mock-test-analysis-error-log-method.md" >}}) the same evening — the marks come from the analysis, not the attempt.
4. **Fix the weakest section** — for most students it is Intelligence (Class VI) or Social Science (Class IX). Twenty minutes a day beats a weekend cram.
5. **Bookmark the official portals** — the AISSEE page on the NTA examination portal and nta.ac.in. Ignore WhatsApp forwards about dates.
6. **Check the girls' quota reality** — 10% of seats (or 10 seats, whichever is higher) are reserved for girls in Class VI; plan school choices accordingly when the bulletin lists participating schools. See our [Sainik Schools for girls guide]({{< relref "../blog/sainik-school-for-girls-2027.md" >}}) for which schools admit girls.
7. **Set a weekly routine** — one mock per week now, two per week from December. Consistency over these 125 days is the whole game.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
Not yet, as of 28 September 2026. The NTA's official AISSEE portal still carries AISSEE 2026 updates. The notification with the application window, fee and eligibility is expected in October–November 2026 based on previous-year trends.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 exam date?**
31 January 2027, listed in NTA's official Examination Calendar (notice of 16 September 2026). The date is tentative and may change, but it is the official plan as of now.

**How many days are left for AISSEE 2027?**
From 28 September 2026, exactly 125 days remain to the proposed exam date of 31 January 2027. Roughly the first 30–40 of those days are likely to fall before the application window closes.

**What should parents do before the AISSEE 2027 notification releases?**
Keep documents ready (birth certificate, domicile, category certificate, photo and signature in NTA format), finish the syllabus once, and start one full-length OMR mock every week. In AISSEE 2026, applicants got only about 20 days between the notification and the form deadline.
