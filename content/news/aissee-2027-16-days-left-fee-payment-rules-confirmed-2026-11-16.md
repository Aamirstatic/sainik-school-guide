## 16 days left — and the fee is the step that decides everything

Sixteen days. That is all that stands between your child and the closing of the AISSEE 2027 application form — **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**. The exam itself is still 76 days away, on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**, so it is easy to treat the form as something for later. Don't. Every year, parents discover a failed payment on the last evening, when there is no time left to fix it.

Here is the good news: the payment rules are no longer guesswork. Our [earlier guide to failed payments](/blog/aissee-fee-payment-failed-what-to-do/) was written in October, before the bulletin came out, and it had to say the 2027 fee was "not confirmed yet". The bulletin has now confirmed all of it.

The fee is **Rs. 850** for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of Defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and **Rs. 700** for SC/ST candidates. It is **non-refundable** — once paid, it will not come back under any circumstances — and it is exclusive of any bank or payment-gateway charges. You pay online through the SBI or UBI payment gateway, by debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI, and the last date for a successful fee transaction is the same as the form deadline: **2 December, 11:50 PM**.

## If the confirmation page never appears, your form is not done

This is the rule parents most need to know, and it comes straight from the bulletin:

> If the confirmation page is not generated after payment of fee, the transaction is cancelled and the amount will be refunded to the candidate's account. However, the candidate has to make another transaction.

Read that twice. Money leaving your bank account is **not** proof that your form is complete. The form is complete only when the fee is paid **and the confirmation page is generated**. If the page never loads — a dropped connection, a timed-out gateway, a frozen screen — the transaction stands cancelled, the money comes back, and you have to pay again. So check before you panic and check before you pay twice: log in to the candidate portal with your application number and password and look for the confirmation page. If it is there, your application is complete. If it is not there, pay again — and this time, stay on the page until the confirmation page downloads. Print it, save a PDF, and keep it safe. You will need the application number and password again for the admit card, the answer key and the result.

Two more bulletin rules worth knowing before you submit. First, **only one application per candidate** — the form is common for Sainik Schools and approved New Sainik Schools, and the fee is the same either way. If you do submit more than one form for the same class, NTA will retain the **latest successfully paid form**. Second, a form whose fee step is incomplete **stands rejected, and no correspondence on this account will be entertained**. That is the bulletin's own language. There is no appeal for an unpaid form.

## Stuck? Call the helpdesk before the last day — not on it

If a payment fails, or the portal behaves strangely, the bulletin gives you a direct line: call the NTA helpdesk on **011-40759000 or 011-69227700**, open **10 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Saturday**, or email **aissee@nta.ac.in**. Quote your application number, email ID and mobile number in your message — the helpdesk asks for all three.

Do it this week, while there are still 16 days to recover. The helpdesk cannot fix a form that was never completed, and the correction window from **7 to 9 December** cannot fix a missing payment either. And if you do miss 2 December, there is still the late window from **3 to 6 December** with an extra fee of **Rs. 500** — [we explained last week whether that safety net is worth planning around](/news/aissee-2027-late-fee-window-dec-3-6-worth-paying-2026-11-08/) — but a safety net is not a plan. Pay the fee this week, get the confirmation page, and the form is done.

## What to do this week

- **Pay the fee now, not on 2 December** — Rs. 850 or Rs. 700, through the SBI/UBI gateway by card, net banking or UPI.
- **Wait for the confirmation page, then download and print it.** No confirmation page means the transaction was cancelled — pay again.
- **Save your application number and password** the moment they are generated, and use a mobile number and email that will stay active for months — they cannot be changed after payment.
- **Run the other pre-submit checks** — names and dates, photo and signature sizes, four exam cities, paper medium — in [our 4-check guide from last week](/news/aissee-2027-19-days-left-4-checks-before-you-submit-2026-11-13/).

## FAQs

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs. 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of Defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and Rs. 700 for SC/ST candidates. The fee is non-refundable and is exclusive of any bank or payment-gateway charges.

**My AISSEE fee was deducted but no confirmation page appeared. What should I do?**

According to the NTA bulletin, if the confirmation page is not generated after payment, the transaction is cancelled and the amount will be refunded to your account — but you must make another transaction. A deducted amount on its own does not mean your form is complete.

**I submitted the AISSEE form twice by mistake. Which one counts?**

The NTA bulletin says that if a candidate submits more than one application form for the same class, NTA will retain the latest successfully paid form. Submit only one form per candidate.

**Who do I contact for AISSEE 2027 payment problems?**

Call the NTA helpdesk on 011-40759000 or 011-69227700 between 10 AM and 5 PM, Monday to Saturday, or email aissee@nta.ac.in. Quote your application number, email ID and mobile number in your message.
