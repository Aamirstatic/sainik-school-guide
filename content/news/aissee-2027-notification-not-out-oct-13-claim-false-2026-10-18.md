A widely-shared education page now claims the AISSEE 2027 notification was "released on October 13, 2026" — and quotes a full fee table and application steps as if the window were open. Parents are forwarding it in study groups this weekend.

**It is not true.** We checked the official NTA portal this morning, 18 October 2026: there is no AISSEE 2027 notice, no information bulletin, and no registration link. The portal carries only AISSEE 2026 material.

> **Last verified:** 18 October 2026 · AISSEE 2027 notification: **not yet released** ✅ (official portal checked today) · Exam date: **31 Jan 2027** ✅ (NTA exam calendar, 16 Sept 2026, tentative) · Fee figures: **CONFIRMED** from AISSEE 2026 bulletin via TOI.

## The claim vs the facts

The page (published 14 Oct, updated 16 Oct) contradicts itself in three places:

| What the page claims | What the evidence says |
|---|---|
| FAQ: notification "released on October 13, 2026, by NTA" | Same article's opening line: notification "will be released **very soon**" — it can't be both |
| Fee table: Rs. 800 (Gen/OBC/EWS), Rs. 650 (SC/ST) | AISSEE 2026 bulletin fee, CONFIRMED via TOI citing NTA: **Rs. 850** (Gen/OBC/defence wards), **Rs. 700** (SC/ST). The 2027 fee is not announced yet |
| "Apply online only at exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE" | That URL returns a 404. The working official portal is **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/**, checked today — 2026 material only |

Two invented specifics, one dead link, and a date the page's own intro disagrees with. That is the full anatomy of a fabricated "notification released" story.

## Why this one is dangerous

A wrong exam date is annoying. A wrong fee table and a wrong portal URL are worse — a parent could pay the wrong amount on a lookalike site, or waste days refreshing a dead page while the real window (last year: just 20 days) opens and closes. Keep your [documents ready](/news/aissee-2027-application-window-7-documents-ready-2026-10-18/) so you can file on day one instead of scrambling. The AISSEE 2027 exam itself is **31 January 2027** ✅ (NTA exam calendar, notice dated 16 Sept 2026) — the one hard date we genuinely have.

## The 30-second check that kills every fake notification

1. Open **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/** directly — not through a forwarded link.
2. Look for a 2027 information bulletin or a "New Registration" button. If everything says 2026, nothing has released.
3. Search the claim's exact date ("October 13, 2026") — if only that one page repeats it and no newspaper or the NTA site carries it, it is invented.
4. Check the fee. If a page quotes 2027 fees before any bulletin exists, it is guessing.

The notification will come — expected in October–November, based on the 2026 pattern. When it does, this site will publish the verified bulletin link within the day. Until then, trust the portal, not the forwards.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No. As of 18 October 2026, NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 notification. The official portal carries only AISSEE 2026 material — we verified this today.

**Did the AISSEE 2027 notification release on October 13, 2026?**
No. That date appears on one education portal's article, contradicted by the same article's own "will be released very soon" line. NTA has published nothing on that date.

**What is the real AISSEE 2027 application fee?**
Unannounced — anyone quoting 2027 fees now is guessing. The 2026 figures (Rs. 850 General/OBC/defence wards; Rs. 700 SC/ST) are the best guide until the bulletin lands.

**Where is the official AISSEE portal?**
https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/ — check it directly rather than trusting third-party pages.
