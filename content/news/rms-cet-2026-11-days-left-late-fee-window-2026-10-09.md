## The deadline picture this morning

If your child is targeting one of the five Rashtriya Military Schools for the 2027–28 session, the clock is now officially loud. The regular application window for **RMS CET 2026 closes on 20 October 2026 at 5 PM** — that is 11 days from today. The fee-payment link stays alive until **11:50 PM the same day**.

Here is the calendar in one glance:

| Milestone | Date (CONFIRMED) |
|---|---|
| Regular application window closes | 20 October 2026, 5 PM |
| Last date for fee payment (regular) | 20 October 2026, 11:50 PM |
| Late-fee application window | 21–25 October 2026, till 5 PM |
| Extra late fee | Rs 500 over the regular fee |
| RMS CET 2026 exam | 13 December 2026 (Sunday) |
| Exam mode | Pen-and-paper, OMR, MCQs |

The entrance test is conducted by the National Testing Agency for admission to **Class 6 and Class 9** in the five Rashtriya Military Schools — Chail (Himachal Pradesh), Ajmer and Dholpur (Rajasthan), and Belgaum and Bengaluru (Karnataka) — English-medium residential schools run by the Ministry of Defence.

## The Rs 500 safety net — how it actually works

This is the part parents miss. If 20 October passes you by, the door does not slam shut. NTA keeps a **late-fee window from 21 to 25 October 2026** (up to 5 PM on the 25th). You fill the same form, pay the same category-wise examination fee, and add **Rs 500 as late fee**.

The regular fees are: **Rs 1,000** for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates, and **Rs 750** for SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action. So a General-category parent applying on 23 October pays Rs 1,500 in total.

A blunt piece of advice: treat the late window as insurance, not a plan. Late-window applicants have fewer days to arrange documents, and every year a handful of families discover an eligibility problem (usually the age calculation, below) only after paying. Apply before 20 October.

## Check eligibility before you pay

- **Class 6:** 10 to 13 years old as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2014 and 31 March 2017, both dates inclusive). Must have passed Class 5, or be studying in Class 5 and pass before admission.
- **Class 9:** 13 to 16 years old as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2011 and 31 March 2014, both dates inclusive). Must have passed Class 8 from a government or recognised school. English is the medium of instruction, so working English knowledge is preferred.
- A six-month relaxation in the upper age limit is permissible for wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action.

One practical note: the birth-date arithmetic is unforgiving. If your child's date of birth falls within a few days of either boundary, re-check it against the exact inclusive ranges above before paying the fee — a rejected form costs you the money and the year.

## What to do in the next 11 days

1. **Verify the age window** using the birth certificate — not memory, the certificate.
2. **Gather documents:** recent photograph, signature, birth certificate, domicile, caste certificate if applicable, and school studying certificate.
3. **Register on the official NTA RMS CET portal** (exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet) and fill the form in one careful sitting — late-window corrections are not guaranteed.
4. **Pay the fee the same day** you submit; the 11:50 PM same-day fee cutoff on 20 October catches people out.
5. **Save the confirmation page** and keep a printed copy — you will need the application number for admit-card download.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**
The regular application window closes on 20 October 2026 at 5 PM, with fee payment allowed until 11:50 PM the same day. A late-fee window runs from 21 to 25 October 2026 (till 5 PM) with an additional Rs 500.

**What is the late fee for RMS CET 2026?**
An additional Rs 500 is charged for applications submitted between 21 and 25 October 2026, over and above the regular category-wise examination fee.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 exam date?**
The Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test 2026 will be held on 13 December 2026 (Sunday) in pen-and-paper OMR mode with multiple-choice questions.

**Who can apply for RMS CET 2026 Class 6 and Class 9?**
Class 6 applicants must be 10 to 13 years old as on 31 March 2027, and Class 9 applicants 13 to 16 years old. Class 6 requires passing Class 5 and Class 9 requires passing Class 8 from a recognised school.
