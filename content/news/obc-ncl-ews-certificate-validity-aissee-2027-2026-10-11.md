Of all the documents that sink a Sainik School admission, category certificates are the quietest killers. Nobody panics about them in October — and then, at counselling in spring, a certificate issued two years ago gets rejected and the seat goes to someone else. If your child is applying under OBC-NCL or EWS for AISSEE 2027, tonight is the night to check the date on that certificate.

## The one-year rule (CONFIRMED)

Here is what exam authorities actually do:

- **OBC-NCL:** A Central OBC-NCL certificate is treated as valid for about **one year**. Even when no expiry date is printed on it, national-exam authorities usually ask for one issued **within the last year**. As a student, assume it needs annual renewal.
- **EWS:** The EWS income-and-asset certificate is typically valid for **one financial year**. For recent national exams, guidance has been explicit — for example, for NEET UG 2026, the certificate should ideally have been issued on or after **1 April 2025**, the start of that cycle's financial year.

Apply the same logic to AISSEE 2027: **hold a certificate issued on or after 1 April 2026** (EXPECTED — the 2027 bulletin will confirm, but every national exam follows this pattern). If your certificate predates that, get a fresh one issued now, while tehsil offices are still operating at normal speed.

Two related facts worth knowing: **SC/ST certificates** usually carry lifetime validity — no renewal worry there. **PwD certificates** are often valid for around five years, but check yours against the disability percentage and reissue rules.

## Central vs state — this one is decisive (CONFIRMED)

AISSEE is a national examination. For it you need a **Central OBC-NCL certificate** — one that cites the Government of India's Gazette resolution recognising your caste in the Central OBC list. A **state OBC certificate** is valid only within that state, for state-level admissions and jobs. Showing up at AISSAC counselling with a state-only certificate under a central quota is one of the most common — and most avoidable — document rejections in the country.

## The 4-point certificate check (do it tonight)

1. **Date of issue.** On or after 1 April 2026 for OBC-NCL/EWS. Older? Apply for renewal tomorrow.
2. **Issuing authority.** OBC-NCL from the **SDM or District Magistrate**; EWS from the **Tehsildar or SDM**. It must carry the official seal, the authority's signature and a legible date.
3. **Name match.** The name on the certificate must match the child's name on the birth certificate and the AISSEE form **exactly** — no nicknames, no spelling drift. Mismatches are the number-two cause of counselling-day disputes after expired certificates.
4. **Scan quality.** When the form opens, you will upload a scan. It must be clear, legible and in the format the bulletin asks for. A blurred upload can be rejected outright.

## Why this week, not next month

The [AISSEE 2027 notification is expected in October 2026](/news/aissee-2027-112-day-countdown-plan-2026-10-11/) — last year's dropped on 10 October. Tehsil and SDM offices slow down as festival season deepens (Diwali is 8 November), and a fresh OBC-NCL certificate typically takes **10–30 days** from application to issue. If you apply this week, the new certificate lands comfortably before the form's closing date. Wait until the form is live, and you will be queuing behind everyone else.

One caution from real counselling floors: some portals and offices resist issuing a fresh certificate if the old one "looks valid" — national exam authorities have still rejected such certificates when they predated the financial-year cutoff. For AISSEE, follow the exam's rule, not the clerk's.

## FAQs

**How long is an OBC-NCL certificate valid for AISSEE 2027?**
Treat it as valid for one year. Exam authorities commonly ask for a certificate issued within the last year, and for counselling it is safest to hold one issued on or after 1 April 2026, covering the current financial year.

**How long is an EWS certificate valid?**
EWS certificates are typically valid for one financial year. Like OBC-NCL, the safe move is a fresh certificate issued on or after 1 April 2026 for the AISSEE 2027 cycle.

**Who issues the OBC-NCL and EWS certificates?**
OBC-NCL certificates are issued by the SDM or District Magistrate; EWS income and asset certificates by the Tehsildar or SDM. They must carry the official seal, the issuing authority's signature and a clear date of issue.

**Is a state OBC certificate enough for AISSEE?**
AISSEE is a national-level exam, so get a Central OBC-NCL certificate citing the Government of India's central OBC list. A state OBC certificate is valid only for state-level admissions and jobs.
