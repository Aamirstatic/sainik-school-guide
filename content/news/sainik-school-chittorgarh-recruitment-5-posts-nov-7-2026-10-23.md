## What happened

Sainik School Chittorgarh (Rajasthan) has announced recruitment for **5 regular posts** — four teaching posts and one non-teaching post — under advertisement number SSC/201/M dated 17 October 2026. This is a regular-basis appointment, not a contractual one, which makes it a notably strong opening in the Sainik School job market.

The posts and their reservation break-up:

| Post | Vacancies | Category | Pay Matrix |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| TGT (Mathematics) | 1 | OBC | Level 7 (₹44,900–1,42,400) |
| TGT (Science) | 1 | SC | Level 7 (₹44,900–1,42,400) |
| TGT (English) | 1 | OBC | Level 7 (₹44,900–1,42,400) |
| TGT (Computer Science) | 1 | ST | Level 7 (₹44,900–1,42,400) |
| Mess Manager | 1 | UR | Level 5 (₹29,200–92,400) |

## Why it matters

Sainik Schools recruit rarely and in small numbers, so a 5-post regular-basis round at one school gets filled fast. For comparison, Sainik School [Goalpara's 13-post round](/news/sainik-school-goalpara-recruitment-13-posts-nov-12-2026-10-19/) closes on **12 November 2026**, and Sainik School [Rewari's 5-post round](/news/sainik-school-rewari-recruitment-2026-5-posts-2026-10-15/) has already run its course — Chittorgarh is currently the freshest regular-basis opening on the board. Teaching at a Sainik School means a residential CBSE school run by the Sainik Schools Society under the Ministry of Defence, with structured pay and a disciplined campus environment. Read our [Sainik School Chittorgarh profile](/schools/sainik-school-chittorgarh/) for the school's background.

## Who can apply

**TGT posts (age 21–35 years as on 31 October 2026):**
- A four-year integrated degree from an NCTE-recognised university with at least 50% marks in the concerned subject and in aggregate, including the B.Ed. component — **or** a Bachelor's/Honours degree with at least 50% marks in the concerned subject plus a B.Ed. from an NCTE-recognised institution with at least 50% marks.
- CTET (Paper-II), conducted by CBSE, must be qualified.
- Proficiency in teaching in the English medium is required.
- TGT (Computer Science) has a separate track: BCA / graduation in Computer Science or IT with at least 50% marks, or B.E./B.Tech. in CS/IT with at least 50% marks, plus a B.Ed. and CTET Paper-II.

**Mess Manager (age 18–50 years as on 31 October 2026):**
- Matriculation or equivalent, plus at least five years of experience running a catering organisation independently, and the ability to maintain mess accounts.

## How to apply — offline, by post

There is no online application. The application fee is **₹500 for UR/OBC** and **₹250 for SC/ST** candidates, payable only through a **demand draft drawn in favour of "Sainik School Chittorgarh", payable at Chittorgarh** — the fee is non-refundable. Send the duly filled application in the prescribed format on A4-size paper, along with the demand draft and self-attested copies of all documents (birth certificate, Aadhaar, marksheets, degrees, CTET certificate for TGTs, experience certificates, caste certificate where applicable), to:

**The Principal, Sainik School Chittorgarh, Bhilwara Road, Rajasthan — 312021**

Write **"APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF _____________"** on the envelope. The packet must **reach the school on or before 7 November 2026** — this is a receipt deadline, not a posting deadline, so allow postal time.

## Selection process

Eligible candidates are shortlisted for a **written test**, then **class demonstration**, **proficiency/skill test**, and **interview**. The written exam pattern:

- **Academic posts:** General Knowledge (15) + General English (15) + Basic Maths (10) + subject test (60) = **100 marks**
- **Mess Manager:** General Knowledge (5) + General English (10) + Basic Maths (10) + subject test (25) = **50 marks**

Call letters are issued by email and through the school website. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the selection test.

## FAQs

**How many posts are open in Sainik School Chittorgarh Recruitment 2026?**

Five posts on a regular basis: TGT (Mathematics), TGT (Science), TGT (English), TGT (Computer Science) — one each — and one Mess Manager post, under advertisement number SSC/201/M.

**What is the last date to apply for Sainik School Chittorgarh Recruitment 2026?**

The application form must reach the school by post on or before **7 November 2026**. The advertisement is dated 17 October 2026.

**What is the salary for TGT and Mess Manager at Sainik School Chittorgarh?**

TGT posts are in Pay Matrix Level 7 (₹44,900 to ₹1,42,400) and the Mess Manager post in Level 5 (₹29,200 to ₹92,400) under the 7th CPC, plus DA and allowances.

**Can I apply online for Sainik School Chittorgarh Recruitment 2026?**

No. Applications are offline only — send the filled form on A4 paper with the demand draft and documents by post to The Principal, Sainik School Chittorgarh, Bhilwara Road, Rajasthan 312021.
