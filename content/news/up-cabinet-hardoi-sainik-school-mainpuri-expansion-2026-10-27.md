## What happened on 27 October

The Uttar Pradesh cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, met in Lucknow on Tuesday, 27 October 2026 and cleared all 22 proposals placed before it, according to Finance and Parliamentary Affairs Minister Suresh Kumar Khanna's briefing. Two decisions concern Sainik Schools — and they are very different kinds of news, so do not mix them up. The cabinet approved a Sainik School in Hardoi, and it approved the free transfer of 9.524 hectares of land for the expansion of the Sainik School in Mainpuri, to be placed at the disposal of the Secondary Education Department.

## Hardoi: a cabinet approval, not an admission notice

Here is the honest position, and it matters more than the headline. The reports mention "a Sainik School in Hardoi" in a list of approvals. They do not say when it will open, which classes it will start with, how many seats it will have, who will run it, or which entrance exam will feed it.

That last point is the one to be careful about. UP has two Sainik School worlds: Society schools admit through AISSEE (NTA), while the state's own schools — Lucknow, Mainpuri, Jhansi, Amethi, Gorakhpur — run a separate UP process. A cabinet approval does not say which world Hardoi belongs to.

So for a Hardoi parent tonight: note it, do not act on it. Nobody can legitimately sell you a Hardoi seat or form today, because no such form exists. A real notice will name the school, classes, exam and dates; anything before that is noise.

## Mainpuri: extra land for an existing school

The Mainpuri decision is much more concrete. The school there has been operational since 22 July 2019, Minister Khanna said, and it now needs more space for additional buildings and educational facilities. The reason is unusually specific: high-tension electricity lines pass over part of the existing campus, restricting how far the school can expand upwards. Extra land, transferred free of cost, solves that problem sideways instead of upwards.

Note what this decision is not: not a new school, not a new admission window, and not a change to the UP Sainik School exam. It is infrastructure for a school that already exists. For Mainpuri families, the practical effect will come later, when new buildings actually open — not this admission season.

## What this does not change, and what to watch

Nothing here changes AISSEE 2027: the NTA bulletin is still awaited, and 31 January 2027 remains the tentative exam date. If Hardoi ever joins AISSEE, it will appear in a future bulletin — not a cabinet list.

## FAQs

**Has the cabinet approved a new Sainik School in Hardoi?**

Yes, among 22 proposals cleared on 27 October 2026. No further detail has been published.

**Can my child apply to the Hardoi school now?**

No — no notice, window, seat count or opening year exists yet. Do not pay anyone claiming to hold Hardoi seats.

**Will Hardoi admit through AISSEE or the UP exam?**

Not stated yet. UP has both routes, so this must wait for an official announcement.

**Is the Mainpuri school new?**

No. It has operated since 22 July 2019; this decision is land for expansion, not a new admission window.
