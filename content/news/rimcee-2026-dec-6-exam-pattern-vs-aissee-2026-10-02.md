## What's happening

The first-ever NTA-conducted Rashtriya Indian Military College entrance exam — **RIMCEE 2026** — now has its calendar locked in. The regular registration window closed on 30 September at 5 PM, but the **late application window is open right now, 1–7 October 2026**, with a **correction window on 9–11 October** (CONFIRMED — Telegraph India, 30 September 2026). The Stage-I written examination is scheduled for **6 December 2026** — a Sunday, in pen-and-paper mode, lasting four hours and thirty minutes, across **39 cities** (CONFIRMED — NDTV).

This is the exam for admission to **Class 8 at RIMC Dehradun**, the Indian Army's own school for boys who go on to the NDA. It is also a first: until this year, RIMC admissions ran through state-level commissions; **2026 is the first year NTA is conducting it** — the same body that runs AISSEE. And that is exactly why AISSEE parents keep asking: if NTA runs both, are the papers the same? They are not. Here is the honest comparison.

## RIMCEE vs AISSEE: the real differences

| | RIMCEE 2026 (RIMC Dehradun) | AISSEE 2027 (Sainik Schools) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry class | Class 8 only ✅ | Class 6 and Class 9 ✅ |
| Exam date | 6 Dec 2026 (Sunday) ✅ | 31 Jan 2027 (tentative) ✅ |
| Paper mode | Pen-and-paper ✅ | OMR-based ✅ |
| Maths & English | **Descriptive (written answers)** ✅ | **Fully MCQ** ✅ |
| GK / General Knowledge | MCQ ✅ | MCQ ✅ |
| Negative marking | NTA bulletin governs; check the official notice | None (no negative marking, per 2026 pattern) ✅ |
| Second stage | **Viva voce, tentatively April 2027** ✅ | None — admission via online counselling (AISSAC) ✅ |
| Application portal | exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee ✅ | exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society |

The single biggest difference is the one parents underestimate: **in RIMCEE, your child writes answers in words, not just bubbles circles.** A boy who is brilliant at MCQ tricks but has shaky handwriting, disorganised presentation, or weak spelling will find the RIMC paper far harder than the AISSEE. And the second difference is equally real — the **viva voce**. AISSEE ends at the OMR sheet; RIMCEE ends with a boy sitting across a table answering questions aloud. Confidence, communication, and general awareness decide the last leg.

## Who can apply, and what it costs

- **Eligibility:** studying in or passed Class 7 from a recognised school; aged 12.5–14 as on 1 July 2027 — that is, born between **2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015**, both dates inclusive (CONFIRMED — Telegraph India).
- **Fee (per NDTV's reporting of the NTA notice):** ₹1,000 for General, OBC-NCL and defence/ex-servicemen wards; ₹750 for SC/ST; plus a **₹500 late fee** in the 1–7 October window. One honest note: a couple of outlets have printed the SC/ST figure differently, so **confirm the exact fee table in the official information bulletin** before paying.
- **Apply only at:** exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee — never through an agent, a WhatsApp link, or a "coaching partner" form.

## What to do this week

1. If the Sept 30 deadline passed you by, the late window is your second chance — but don't wait for October 6. NTA portals jam in the last 48 hours, and the fee payment itself must complete by the window's closing time.
2. If your son is appearing, start the shift from MCQ drilling to **written-answer practice** now. Nine weeks to 6 December is enough to build presentation discipline — underline key steps, leave space between answers, keep handwriting legible.
3. For the viva voce (Stage-II), read the newspaper with him daily. A boy who can talk about what is happening in the country walks into a viva differently.

## FAQs

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 written exam?**

6 December 2026, pen-and-paper mode, 4 hours 30 minutes, across 39 cities in India — confirmed by NTA's official notice, reported by Telegraph India and NDTV.

**How is the RIMC paper different from AISSEE?**

RIMCEE requires descriptive (written) answers in Mathematics and English; only the GK section is MCQ. AISSEE is fully multiple-choice. RIMCEE also has a second stage — a viva voce tentatively in April 2027 — while AISSEE admission runs through online counselling (AISSAC) with no interview.

**Is the RIMCEE 2026 application window still open?**

Yes — the late application window with a late fee runs 1–7 October 2026, followed by a correction window on 9–11 October 2026. Apply only at exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee.

**Who can apply for RIMCEE 2026?**

Students studying in or who have passed Class 7 from a recognised school, born between 2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015 — that is, 12.5 to 14 years old as on 1 July 2027.
