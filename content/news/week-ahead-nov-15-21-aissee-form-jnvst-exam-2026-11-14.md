If your house has a child preparing for a Sainik School, a Navodaya school, or a military school, this week has three clocks running at once — and they do not wait for each other. The AISSEE form closes in 18 days. The JNVST exam is 14 days away. RMS CET follows 29 days from now. Here is the week, in order, and the five things to finish before Sunday.

## 1. AISSEE 2027 form: 18 days left, and the last weekend will be crowded

Applications close at **11:50 PM on Wednesday, 2 December 2026**. The fee is **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and **₹700** for SC/ST. If you miss 2 December, a late window runs **3 to 6 December** with an extra **₹500** — and after 6 December, no new applications are accepted at all. A correction window then runs **7 to 9 December**, but it only fixes particulars NTA permits; your registered mobile number and email stay locked after payment.

Do not plan to submit on the last weekend. Every year the portal slows down in the final 48 hours, and a failed payment at 11 PM on 2 December leaves you no buffer. Our morning guide on [submitting this weekend](/news/aissee-2027-18-days-left-submit-this-weekend-5-reasons-2026-11-14/) explains the five reasons to finish early — failed payments, photo rejections and the correction window not being a redo button among them.

## 2. JNVST 2027: 14 days to the 28 November exam

The JNVST Class 6 exam is on **28 November 2026** — two Saturdays from now. Admit cards have been available since **28 October**, so if your child is appearing, the card should already be downloaded and printed. This week, re-check the seven details on it — name spelling, photograph, exam centre, reporting time — because a mistake found now can still be fixed, and one found on exam morning cannot. Our [admit-card checklist](/news/jnvst-2027-admit-card-7-details-to-check-15-days-to-exam-2026-11-13/) walks through all seven.

## 3. RMS CET 2026: 29 days to the 13 December exam

The Rashtriya Military School entrance exam is on **Sunday, 13 December 2026** — a pen-and-paper OMR test of 2 hours 30 minutes for Class 6 and Class 9 admission in the five military schools. 🔶 **EXPECTED:** the city intimation slip is expected in the last week of November and the admit card around 8 December, based on the pattern of previous cycles — NTA has not announced those two dates yet. If your child is appearing for both RMS CET and AISSEE, note that the RMS exam falls right in the middle of the AISSEE final-11-weeks stretch, so plan the revision calendar around it rather than pausing everything for it.

## 4. The AISSEE exam itself is 78 days away — the final-11-week plans are already running

The AISSEE 2027 exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027** — Class 6 from 2:00 to 4:30 PM, Class 9 from 2:00 to 5:00 PM, offline OMR, no negative marking. With 78 days left, the final-11-week plans we published today — the [Class 6 GK plan](/blog/aissee-class-6-gk-final-11-weeks-plan-78-days/) and the [Class 9 plan](/blog/aissee-class-9-final-11-weeks-plan-78-days/) — are the right shape for this week: finish the form first, then settle into the weekly rhythm.

## What to finish this week: a 5-point checklist

1. **Submit the AISSEE form** — or at least complete it and pay the fee. Do not leave it to the last weekend.
2. **Re-check the photo, signature and thumb impression** against the bulletin's specs — rejections are the silent killer, and they surface only after submission.
3. **Download and verify the JNVST admit card** if your child is appearing on 28 November — all seven details, today.
4. **Note the RMS CET dates** (13 December exam; city slip expected late November) on the family calendar so they don't collide with AISSEE mock tests.
5. **Lock the weekly study rhythm** — one full paper every Sunday, at 2 PM, the same time as the real exam.

## FAQs

**When does the AISSEE 2027 application form close?**
The regular application window closes at 11:50 PM on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra fee of Rs. 500, and a correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026.

**When is the JNVST 2027 exam?**
The JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam is on 28 November 2026, which is 14 days from today. Admit cards have been available since 28 October 2026.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**
Sunday, 13 December 2026 — 29 days from today. It is a pen-and-paper OMR test of 2 hours 30 minutes, conducted by NTA for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 in the five Rashtriya Military Schools.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**
Sunday, 31 January 2027. Class 6 runs from 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM (150 minutes, 125 questions, 300 marks) and Class 9 from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM (180 minutes, 150 questions, 400 marks), in offline pen-and-paper OMR mode with no negative marking.
