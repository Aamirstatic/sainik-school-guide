> **Last verified: 30 September 2026, 7:15 AM IST.** This is a **September update**, not breaking news — the single biggest fact of the month is that the AISSEE 2027 notification is *still* not out. What follows is everything that did change this month, clearly labelled CONFIRMED or EXPECTED.

September ends today. If your child is targeting Sainik School admission in 2027, here is the state of play: **123 days** to go until the exam, one confirmed exam date, zero notification, and October — historically the month the form opens — starting tomorrow.

## The one confirmed fact: 31 January 2027

**CONFIRMED (tentative):** NTA's examination calendar for 2026–27 lists the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE 2027) on **31 January 2027**. The same calendar slots RIMC's entrance on 6 December 2026 and RMS CET 2026 on 13 December 2026. NTA is explicit that calendar dates are proposed and may change, but this is the date every serious plan should be built around.

## The one missing fact: the notification

**CONFIRMED (as of 8 September 2026):** the NTA portal was still carrying AISSEE 2026 updates, and no AISSEE 2027 application schedule had been announced. Nothing since has changed that status. **EXPECTED:** if the last cycle repeats — applications opened 10 October 2025 and ran just 20 days — October is the month to watch, literally starting tomorrow. That is pattern, not promise.

## What else moved this month

- **UP Sainik Schools:** the Society's 2027–28 admission window is already open and closes **25 October 2026** — the UP Sainik School entrance exam itself is on **20 December 2026**. If your child is eligible for Lucknow or Gorakhpur, that clock is already ticking.
- **RMS CET 2026:** applications for the Rashtriya Military Schools' December entrance are open; the exam is **13 December 2026**.
- **RIMC:** the December 2026 entrance (6 December) closed applications on 30 September — if you applied, admit-card season is next.
- **School list background:** in the 2026 cycle, 33 established Sainik Schools plus new Sainik Schools participated, with 3 additional schools added in October 2025. The 2027 list comes with the new bulletin.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released as of 30 September 2026?**

No. NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 notification or information bulletin as of today. The last published status update (8 September 2026) confirmed no application schedule had been announced.

**What is the confirmed exam date for AISSEE 2027?**

31 January 2027, listed in NTA's examination calendar for 2026–27. NTA labels these calendar dates as tentative, so the information bulletin will confirm the final date.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 notification expected?**

Expected in October–November 2026, based on the 2026 cycle pattern when applications ran 10 to 30 October 2025. This is a pattern-based expectation, not an NTA announcement.

**Which other defence-school exams have dates this season?**

RIMC's December 2026 entrance is on 6 December 2026, RMS CET 2026 on 13 December 2026, and the UP Sainik Schools entrance exam on 20 December 2026, per the NTA calendar and the UP Sainik Schools Society.
