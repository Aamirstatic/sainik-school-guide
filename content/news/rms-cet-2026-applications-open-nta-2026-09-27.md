> **🔄 Last verified: 27 September 2026** — ✅ **CONFIRMED**: NTA opened RMS CET 2026 applications on 23 September 2026. Last date 20 October 2026 (5:00 PM), fee deadline 11:50 PM the same day, late window 21–25 October 2026. Exam: 13 December 2026 (Sunday), pen-and-paper OMR, 63 cities. Fees: ₹1,000 (General/OBC), ₹750 (SC/ST/KIA). Two independent news sources report identical details.

For parents eyeing a defence-school seat for their child, this is genuinely good news — and it landed quietly. On **23 September 2026**, the National Testing Agency opened applications for the **Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026** for admission to Class VI and Class IX in the five Rashtriya Military Schools for the 2027-28 academic session.

If AISSEE 2027 is your main target, think of RMS CET as a serious parallel shot, not a distraction. Same audience, same exam style, similar syllabus DNA — and a three-week application window you should not sleep through.

## The dates that matter

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Online application window | 23 September – 20 October 2026 (up to 5:00 PM) |
| Last date for successful fee payment | 20 October 2026 (up to 11:50 PM) |
| Late application window (with ₹500 late fee) | 21–25 October 2026 |
| RMS CET 2026 written exam | **13 December 2026 (Sunday)** |
| Exam mode | Pen-and-paper, OMR-based MCQs |
| Exam cities | 63 across India |
| Apply at | exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet/ |

## Who can apply

- **Class VI**: aged 10–13 years as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2014 and 31 March 2017, both dates inclusive).
- **Class IX**: aged 13–16 years as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2011 and 31 March 2014, both dates inclusive).
- Class VI candidates should have passed or be studying in Class V; Class IX candidates should have passed or be studying in Class VIII at a recognised school. Instruction medium is English, so comfort with English matters.

## The fee structure

General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates pay **₹1,000**; SC, ST and wards of armed forces personnel killed in action pay **₹750**. Fee is paid online by credit/debit card, net banking or UPI.

## The five schools

Chail (Shimla Hills, Himachal Pradesh), Ajmer and Dholpur (Rajasthan), and Belgaum and Bengaluru (Karnataka). These are residential, English-medium schools run by the Ministry of Defence for Classes VI to XII — and founding members of the Indian Public Schools' Conference.

## What to do next

Apply on the official portal — not on aggregator sites that just link to it. Keep scanned copies of the birth certificate, photograph, signature and category documents ready in the prescribed formats *before* you start the form, because the medical-examination hospital you select during registration cannot be changed later. And do not wait for 20 October: NTA portals get congested in the last 48 hours.

One last thing — AISSEE 2027 (31 January 2027, per the NTA exam calendar) remains a separate exam with its own notification, which is still awaited. RMS CET and AISSEE are both defence-school routes, but you must apply for each separately.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is RMS CET 2026 the same as AISSEE 2027?**
No. RMS CET is the entrance for the five Rashtriya Military Schools; AISSEE is for the Sainik Schools. Both are conducted by NTA for the Ministry of Defence, but they are separate exams with separate applications, fees and timelines.

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**
20 October 2026 up to 5:00 PM, with fee payment accepted till 11:50 PM the same night. A late window runs 21–25 October 2026 with an additional ₹500 late fee.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**
Sunday, 13 December 2026 — a pen-and-paper OMR test with MCQs, held across 63 Indian cities.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 application fee?**
₹1,000 for General/OBC/OBC-NCL candidates and ₹750 for SC/ST candidates and wards of armed forces personnel killed in action, payable online.
