Fee figures and exam-duration details below are single-source reports — treat them as the source's account and confirm on the official portal at `exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee/` before paying.

**Three days.** That's how long is left to apply for one of the most prestigious school admissions in the country. The National Testing Agency will close the online application window for the **Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination (RIMCEE) 2026** on **30 September 2026**, for admission to Class 8 at RIMC Dehradun for the July 2027 term.

If your child is in Class 7 and you've been circling this form "later," later just arrived.

![Examination hall desks ready for the RIMCEE written exam](/images/inbody/rimcee-2026-registration-closes-september-30-2026-09-27.webp)

## What happened

NTA has, for the first time, taken over the RIMC entrance examination — it used to be run by the state commissions, and everything now goes through NTA's online portal. The registration link is live at `exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee/`. Here's the schedule, straight from the official notice:

| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| Application window closes (no late fee) | **30 September 2026, till 5 PM** |
| Late-fee application window | **1–7 October 2026, till 5 PM** |
| Written exam (Stage-I) | **6 December 2026 (Sunday)** |
| Viva voce (Stage-II, tentative) | Around April 2027 |

The Stage-I written exam is pen-and-paper and runs for **4.5 hours** (reported by Careers360). Students who clear Stage-I move on to the viva voce — and yes, you need to qualify both stages.

## Why RIMC matters to Sainik School families

Here's the thing — if you're reading this site, you're probably preparing for AISSEE 2027 too. RIMC isn't a rival to that plan; it's the sibling plan. RIMC Dehradun is the country's oldest military boarding school, and its alumni list reads like a Who's Who of the Armed Forces. The entrance exam tests written English and Mathematics (descriptive papers) plus General Knowledge — skills that overlap heavily with AISSEE preparation.

The one big difference worth knowing: **RIMC selects purely on merit — there is no reservation system**, and admissions run on state-wise/zone-wise merit lists. And unlike AISSEE's single written stage followed by e-counselling, RIMC adds an in-person viva voce round. A child who's preparing for both gets more chances, not divided effort.

## Check eligibility before you fill the form

This is the part people get wrong. RIMCEE 2026 has narrow eligibility:

- **Age:** your child must be **born between 2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015** — in plain terms, more than 12 years and 6 months but not more than 14 years old as on 1 July 2027.
- **Education:** must be **studying in Class 7 or have passed Class 7** from a recognised school.
- **Fee:** Careers360 reports ₹1,000 for General and defence wards, and ₹750 for OBC-NCL/SC/ST — but this comes from a single report, so verify the exact amount on the official portal before you pay.

Don't guess on the date of birth. If your child's birth certificate falls outside that window by even a day, the form gets rejected. Measure twice.

## FAQs

**Is RIMCEE 2026 the same as the AISSEE?**
No. AISSEE is NTA's exam for Sainik Schools (Class 6 and 9, exam 31 January 2027). RIMCEE is a separate NTA exam for RIMC Dehradun (Class 8 only). A child can apply to both — the exams don't clash. [Read how RIMC compares with Sainik Schools]({{< relref "sainik-school-vs-rimc-vs-rashtriya-military-school.md" >}}).

**What is the last date to apply for RIMCEE 2026?**
✅ **CONFIRMED:** 30 September 2026 (till 5 PM) without late fee, and 7 October 2026 (till 5 PM) with late fee.

**Who can apply for the RIMC entrance exam?**
Children born between 2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015 who are studying in (or have passed) Class 7 from a recognised school. The NTA is also accepting this year's exam fully online — no hard-copy applications.

**How is the RIMCEE conducted?**
Stage-I is a pen-and-paper written exam on 6 December 2026, reported at 4.5 hours. Qualifiers face a Stage-II viva voce, tentatively in April 2027.
