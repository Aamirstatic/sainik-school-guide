The one date you can trust right now is **31 January 2027**. Everything else about AISSEE 2027 is still "expected".

The National Testing Agency published its examination calendar for 2026–27 on **16 September 2026**, and the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination is listed on **31 January 2027** — a one-day paper. That's the calendar talking. The calendar, NTA stresses, is *not* a notification: detailed bulletins with registration dates, eligibility and admit-card schedules come separately, and the tentative date can still move.

## Why this matters more than the rumours

Some education pages are already publishing contradictory claims — one well-known page says the notification "was released on 13 October 2026" in one section while calling it "Soon" in another. Ignore that noise. Our portal check on **21 October 2026** found no information bulletin on the official NTA AISSEE portal, and that remains the ground truth: **no bulletin, no registration link, no official application dates yet.**

What NTA's own calendar *does* give you is a fixed finish line to plan backwards from. That's worth more than any rumour.

## The 101-day reverse timeline

Counted from today, 22 October 2026, to the calendar date:

| Exam city intimation slip | ⏳ EXPECTED | Mid-January 2027 |

| **AISSEE 2027 (written)** | ✅ CONFIRMED date, ⏳ tentative | **31 January 2027** |
| Result | ⏳ EXPECTED | February 2027 — last year: 27 February 2026 |
| E-counselling (AISSAC) | ⏳ EXPECTED | March–April 2027 |

Two things to note. First, the calendar date is tentative by NTA's own admission — administrative or logistical reasons can shift it. Second, the application window was roughly 30 days last year, and the last date was *extended* (from 30 October to 9 November 2025). Plan for the window, not for an extension.

## What to do with the next 30 days

1. **Stop refreshing for the notification daily — but keep documents ready.** The bulletin can drop any day; our [7-documents application checklist](/blog/aissee-2027-application-documents-checklist-2026-10-06/) walks through what's usually asked for.
2. **Plan the calendar backwards.** 101 days to the exam means roughly 14 weekends of mock tests. If the notification lands in early November, the application eats a week of attention — finish the syllabus bulk before that.
3. **Watch only official channels.** The NTA AISSEE portal and nta.ac.in — not forwarded screenshots. NTA explicitly warns against relying on social media forwards.

The exam date is ink on the calendar. The starting gun hasn't fired yet — but 101 days is short enough that preparation should be running already.

## FAQs

**What is the AISSEE 2027 exam date?**
NTA's official exam calendar (released 16 September 2026) lists AISSEE 2027 on 31 January 2027. The date is tentative — NTA says calendar dates may change, and the exam bulletin will confirm the final date.

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No. As of our last portal check on 21 October 2026, the NTA AISSEE portal lists no information bulletin for the 2027 cycle. Claims that the notification released on 13 October 2026 are contradicted by the official portal.

**When will the AISSEE 2027 application form come out?**
Expected in October or November 2026, based on the 2026 cycle (notification 10 October 2025, last date 9 November 2025). NTA has not announced the dates yet — this is an expectation, not a confirmation.

**How many days are left for AISSEE 2027?**
From 22 October 2026 to the calendar date of 31 January 2027, there are 101 days. The countdown assumes the calendar date holds, which is tentative until the notification confirms it.
