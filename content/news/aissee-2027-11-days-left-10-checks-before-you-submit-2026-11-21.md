## Eleven days is plenty — if the form is right the first time

The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. Eleven days sounds comfortable. It is — unless the form goes in with a mistake that cannot be fixed later. The correction window from **7 to 9 December** covers only permitted fields, and the fee, once paid, is generally non-refundable. So sit with the form for ten quiet minutes tonight, while the portal is calmer than it will be on 2 December, and run these checks.

## The 10 checks

**1. The name matches the birth certificate, letter for letter.** Read the name on the certificate aloud while you look at the form — spellings, initials, middle names. That certificate is what the school checks at admission time.

**2. The date of birth is inside the age window.** Class 6: born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017**. Class 9: born between **1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014**. There is no age relaxation for any category, and birthdays on 31 March or 1 April decide a child's class — check them twice.

**3. The photo, signature and thumb impression follow the bulletin.** The bulletin sets the format and size for each upload, and files that do not match are rejected by the portal. Our [photo and signature specs guide](/blog/aissee-2027-photo-signature-specs-size-background-rejections/) covers the common rejection reasons.

**4. The school preferences are in the right order.** You are choosing from the 33 established Sainik Schools plus 80 approved New Sainik Schools for Class 6 (40 for Class 9). Counselling allots the highest preference your child's merit clears — so put the school you genuinely want first. Our [guide to ranking school choices](/blog/aissee-2027-form-which-sainik-schools-to-list-rank-order/) explains how.

**5. The exam cities are reachable.** The city is announced on **11 January 2027** and the centre only with the admit card on **25 January**. Pick cities you can actually reach on a Sunday morning in January.

**6. The category certificate is valid — if you claim one.** SC, ST and OBC-NCL candidates compete in their category quota only with a valid certificate uploaded. An expired or wrong-format certificate is one of the quietest ways a form fails. If you claim no category, leave it blank rather than guessing.

**7. The domicile certificate is ready.** In the 33 established schools, 67% of seats go to candidates domiciled in the school's home state. The domicile certificate decides the quota — not the city where your child writes the exam. Keep it ready for counselling even if the form does not ask for it today.

**8. The fee payment actually went through.** The fee is **Rs 850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and **Rs 700** for SC/ST. Pay by card, net banking or UPI, then check the payment status shows success before closing the page. A payment that hangs halfway is not a submitted form — and if a payment fails, read our [fee-payment failure guide](/news/aissee-2027-fee-payment-failed-13-days-left-2026-11-19/) before paying again, so you do not pay twice.

**9. The mobile number and email are yours — and working.** Every later message from NTA goes to the contacts on the form. Use a number you will still have in January, and check the email spelling. One wrong digit means silence for two months.

**10. Save the confirmation page.** Download it, note down the application number somewhere safe, and keep the fee receipt. You will need the application number for the city slip on 11 January and the admit card on 25 January 2027. A screenshot is good; a printed copy in a folder is better.

## If you miss 2 December

Missing the deadline is not the end — but it costs money. The late window runs from **3 to 6 December** with an extra **Rs 500** on top of the regular fee, closing at 11:50 PM on 6 December. After that there is no further window. And the late window does not fix a bad form; it only gives you more time to submit a good one. Read our [correction-window guide](/news/aissee-2027-correction-window-7-9-december-audit-confirmation-page-2026-11-18/) so you know what you can still change between 7 and 9 December — and what you cannot. The exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**.

## FAQs

**When does the AISSEE 2027 application form close?**
Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM. The fee must also be paid by the same time. Candidates who miss it can still apply from 3 to 6 December with a late fee of Rs 500.

**Can I correct my child's name or date of birth after submitting the AISSEE 2027 form?**
The correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026, but it covers only permitted fields. Do not count on being able to fix a wrong name or date of birth later — check both against the birth certificate before you submit.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**
Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates. The fee is paid online by card, net banking or UPI, and is generally non-refundable once paid.

**What should I save after submitting the AISSEE 2027 form?**
Download and save the confirmation page, note down the application number, and keep the fee payment receipt. You will need the application number for the exam city slip on 11 January 2027 and the admit card on 25 January 2027.
