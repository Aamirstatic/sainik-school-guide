## Where the notification actually appears (CONFIRMED)

There is exactly one source that matters: the official NTA AISSEE portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**, with a parallel notice on **nta.ac.in**. That is where the 2026 bulletin, application form, correction window, admit cards and results all lived, and where the 2027 cycle will begin.

A practical routine while you wait:

1. **Bookmark the portal, not a news page.** The homepage of the AISSEE portal gets a "New" banner the moment the cycle opens.
2. **Check once a day, 10 AM–6 PM.** NTA publishes during working hours; midnight refreshes buy you nothing.
3. **Follow the NTA helpline route, not rumours.** NTA lists **011-40759000 / 011-69227700** and **aissee@nta.ac.in** for queries (Tier-2 source: MoneyControl's AISSEE desk piece). The helpline confirms portal-level facts, like whether a cycle is open.
4. **Ignore YouTube "OUT 🔥" thumbnails** until you can download the official PDF bulletin yourself. The bulletin — eligibility, dates, fee, syllabus, participating schools, reservation rules — is the notification, not a video about it.

## Fake-notification red flags

Some education sites have already published 2027 "dates" as if announced. One claims the application last date is **16 December 2026** and that the notification PDF is released — but links no official NTA PDF and contradicts every pattern the 2026 cycle set. Treat such claims as unverified until the portal itself changes.

Quick tests before you believe a claim:

- **Does it link the official bulletin PDF?** No link, no belief.
- **Does a second independent source confirm it?** One Tier-1 source or two independent Tier-2 sources — that is our own standard, and it works for readers too.
- **Does it match the NTA exam calendar?** Anything calling the exam date anything other than **31 January 2027 (tentative)** contradicts the calendar NTA published on 16 September 2026.
