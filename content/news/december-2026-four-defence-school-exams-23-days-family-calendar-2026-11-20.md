## Four exams, 23 days, one family calendar

Here is the honest picture for any family with a child in Class 5, 6, 7 or 8 this winter. Between Saturday, 28 November and Sunday, 20 December 2026, four different entrance exams fall in 23 days — and the AISSEE 2027 application form closes right in the middle of them, on 2 December.

Nobody planned it this way on purpose. JNVST belongs to Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti, RIMCEE and RMS CET sit on the NTA calendar, the UP Sainik School test is run by the UP Sainik Schools Society, and AISSEE is NTA's Sainik Schools exam. Four different bodies, four different portals — and one child, one family, one December.

So print this page, or save it. These are the dates that matter, in the order they arrive.

## The calendar, in order

|------|------|------------------|
| **JNVST 2027, Class 6** | **Sat, 28 Nov 2026, 11:30 AM–1:30 PM** | Admit card out since 28 Oct; 80 questions, 100 marks, no negative marking |
| **AISSEE 2027 form closes** | **Wed, 2 Dec 2026, 11:50 PM** | Late-fee window 3–6 Dec (+₹500); correction window 7–9 Dec |
| **RIMCEE (RIMC, Class 8)** | **Sun, 6 Dec 2026** | NTA calendar date; admit card expected a few days before the exam |

| **UP Sainik School (Class 6 & 9)** | **Sun, 20 Dec 2026** | OMR paper, 200 marks, 2 hours 30 minutes; admit card after 25 Nov |

The AISSEE 2027 exam itself is further out — **Sunday, 31 January 2027**, 72 days from today — but the form is the December job. Miss 2 December and the late-fee window is your only safety net, and after 6 December there is no second one.

## This week: two things to finish before the rush

**First, the JNVST admit card.** It has been downloadable since 28 October from navodaya.gov.in, using the registration number and the child's date of birth. If you have not printed it yet, do it this weekend — not on 27 November. Check three things on it: the child's name and photo, the exam centre address, and the medium of the question paper. Centres cannot be changed, and any error must be reported to the Principal of the district's Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya before the exam. Children must be seated by 11:00 AM on 28 November, and they need the printed admit card plus the child's Aadhaar card or the parent's residence certificate that was uploaded with the form.

**Second, the AISSEE form, if it is still pending.** Twelve days sounds comfortable. It is not, if the photograph needs retaking or a payment hangs on the last evening — which is exactly what happened to families last year. [Our step-by-step order for first-time applicants]({{< relref "aissee-2027-12-days-left-havent-applied-yet-start-now-2026-11-20.md" >}}) takes under an hour if the documents are ready before you start.

## What each exam actually asks of your child

The four December exams do not test the same thing, and that matters for the last weeks of preparation:

- **JNVST (28 Nov):** 80 objective questions, 100 marks, two hours. Mental Ability and EVS carry 40 questions and 50 marks; Arithmetic 20 questions and 25 marks; Language 20 questions and 25 marks. To qualify, a child must score at least 14 in Mental Ability and EVS, 7 in Arithmetic and 7 in Language. No negative marking.
- **RIMCEE (6 Dec):** the RIMC Dehradun entrance for Class 8, July 2027 term — written papers in English, Mathematics and General Knowledge, followed by a viva and medical for those who clear the written stage.
- **RMS CET (13 Dec):** the Rashtriya Military Schools' common entrance test for Class 6 and Class 9, run by NTA for the 2027-28 session.
- **UP Sainik School (20 Dec):** a single OMR paper of 200 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes, for Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow and Sainik School, Gorakhpur. Lucknow takes boys only in Class 6 and boys and girls in Class 9; Gorakhpur takes boys and girls in both classes.

If your child is sitting more than one of these, do not try to prepare for all four at once in the last week. The syllabuses overlap heavily in Maths and reasoning. The week before each exam, revise only that exam's pattern — [our JNVST final-week plan]({{< relref "jnvst-2027-9-days-left-final-week-plan-class-6.md" >}}) is the model for how to do it.

## The week after: what to watch

Once the exams start coming, the dates to watch shift. The UP Sainik School answer key is tentatively due on 24 December, with the written result around 30 December. The RMS CET result and the JNVST result follow their own schedules — the JNVST result is 🔶 **EXPECTED** by the end of March 2027, based on the NVS cycle. And the AISSEE correction window, 7 to 9 December, is the last chance to fix the form before the exam city is announced on 11 January 2027 and admit cards arrive on 25 January.

One warning, because December brings it every year: no agent, coaching centre or WhatsApp forward can get your child a seat, a centre change or an early result in any of these exams. Admit cards come only from the official portals — navodaya.gov.in, exams.nta.nic.in and upsainikschool.org. Anyone promising otherwise is selling something that does not exist.

## FAQs

**Which defence-school entrance exams are held in December 2026?**

Four in 23 days: JNVST Class 6 on 28 November 2026, RIMCEE on 6 December, RMS CET on 13 December, and the UP Sainik School entrance exam on 20 December. The AISSEE 2027 form also closes in this window, on 2 December 2026.

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam and what time does it start?**

Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM. Children must be seated by 11:00 AM. The admit card has been available since 28 October 2026 on navodaya.gov.in.

**Is the AISSEE 2027 form still open?**

Yes, until Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM. A late-fee window runs from 3 to 6 December with an additional Rs 500, and corrections can be made from 7 to 9 December 2026.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam admit card available?**

Admit cards will be generated after 25 November 2026 on upsainikschool.org, using the same login created during registration. The exam is on Sunday, 20 December 2026.
