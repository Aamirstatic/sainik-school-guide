## The slip is out — and it is not the admit card

If your child is sitting RIMCEE 2026, this morning brings the first concrete document of the final stretch. The National Testing Agency released the **advance exam city intimation slip on 17 November 2026** for the Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination. The written exam is on **Sunday, 6 December 2026** — seventeen days from today — for admission to Class 8 at RIMC Dehradun for the July 2027 term.

Here is the distinction every parent needs to get right, because NTA itself has stressed it: the city slip tells you **which city** your child's exam centre is in. That is all it does. It is not the admit card, it carries no centre address and no reporting time, and it will not get your child through the exam-hall gate. Its whole purpose is to let you plan travel and stay in advance, instead of scrambling in the final week.

## How to download the city slip

The slip is live on the official RIMCEE portal. Go to **exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE**, open the Advance City Module, and log in with the **application number and password** used for the application. View the allotted city and download the slip for reference.

Do this today, not next week. The city on the slip decides whether you are booking a train or just driving across town — and December trains fill fast. If the login fails, use the "forgot password" option on the portal rather than creating a fresh application; a duplicate application is the one mistake that can quietly disqualify a candidate.

The admit card is the document that matters at the gate. When it arrives — in the final week, going by the bulletin — check the centre name and address, the reporting time, your child's name spelling and photograph against the application. Any mismatch should go to NTA immediately; the week before the exam is not the time to discover a wrong photograph.

## A quick reminder of the paper — and its trap

Those who clear the written papers move to a **50-mark viva voce**, followed by a medical fitness test. Final selection rests on the combined merit of the written exam and the viva.

## What to do this week

First, download the slip and note the city. Second, if the city is away from home, book travel and stay now — refundable where possible, since the exact centre only comes with the admit card. Third, check the [AISSEE 2027 form deadline](/news/aissee-2027-14-days-left-apply-2-december-deadline-2026-11-18/) if your child is also applying to Sainik Schools; the two December–January exams share many of the same families, and the AISSEE form closes on 2 December. And finally, keep the preparation steady. The city slip changes logistics, not the syllabus — the papers on 6 December are the same ones your child has been preparing for all along.

## FAQs

**Is the RIMCEE 2026 exam city slip released?**
Yes. NTA released the advance exam city intimation slip on 17 November 2026. Registered candidates can check their allotted examination city at exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE using their application number and password.

**Is the RIMCEE city slip the same as the admit card?**
No. The city slip only tells you which city your exam centre is in, so you can plan travel. It is not valid for entry into the exam hall. The admit card, with the exact centre and reporting time, will be released separately.

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 admit card be released?**
No date has been announced yet. As per the NTA information bulletin, the admit card comes 5 to 7 days before the exam, which points to roughly 29 November to 1 December — treat that as an expected pattern, not an announced date, and keep checking the official portal.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 exam and what is the pattern?**
Sunday, 6 December 2026. The Stage-I written exam is 400 marks across three papers: English (125 marks, descriptive, 2 hours), Mathematics (200 marks, descriptive, 1 hour 30 minutes) and General Knowledge (75 marks, MCQ, 1 hour). At least 50 percent in each paper is needed to reach the viva voce.
