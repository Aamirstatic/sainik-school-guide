## The card comes tomorrow — be ready tonight

If your child is sitting the RIMCEE on Sunday, 6 December, tomorrow is the day the paperwork moves. The NTA releases the admit card **five to seven days before the exam**, which puts the window at **29 November to 1 December**. It will not arrive by email. It will not arrive by post. It will appear, quietly, on the NTA RIMCEE portal — and the families who download it the day it appears are the ones who sleep well the night before the exam.

Do the preparation tonight, while you have time. Keep the **application number and your child's date of birth** on a piece of paper next to the computer. Make sure you know the login — the same application number and password or date of birth you used for the form. And check the portal once tomorrow morning and once in the evening. NTA does not announce the exact hour a document goes live.

## How to download it

1. Open **exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE** — the official NTA RIMCEE portal, and nowhere else.
2. Look for the admit-card download link for the December 2026 exam.
3. Log in with the application number and date of birth (or password, whichever the page asks).
4. Download the PDF and **print two copies** — one for the exam hall, one kept safe at home.
5. Read every line on the card before you put it in the bag.

One warning that bears repeating every year: if a message, a WhatsApp forward or a phone call offers you a download link, it is not from NTA. The only source is the portal above.

## The 7 things to check the moment it appears

Do not just print it and file it. Sit down with your child and check these, one by one:

1. **Name spelling** — exactly as on the birth certificate and the application form.
2. **Photograph** — your child's photo, clear and recognisable.
3. **Roll number and application number** — note them down separately; you will need them for the result.
4. **Exam centre name and full address** — not just the city. Work out the route tonight, not on exam morning.
5. **Reporting time and paper timings** — the paper runs 4 hours 30 minutes; plan to reach early, not on time.
6. **Date of birth and category** — a mismatch here can cause trouble at the gate.
7. **The instructions printed on the card** — what to carry, what is not allowed, and whether a self-declaration or a photograph needs to be pasted.

If anything is wrong, contact NTA through the helpline on the portal the same day. Do not assume a small error will be overlooked at the centre — and do not wait until 5 December to discover it.

## The city slip should already be with you

Before the admit card comes the **city intimation slip**, which NTA issues 12 to 15 days before the exam — that window was **21 to 24 November**. The slip tells you only the exam **city**, so families who need to travel can book tickets and rooms in advance. It does not admit your child to the centre; only the admit card does that.

If you have not downloaded the city slip yet, do it tonight, in the same login. If the city it names surprises you — a different city from the ones you chose in the form — that is the moment to plan travel, not the morning of 6 December. The centre itself, with its exact address and reporting time, comes only on the admit card.

## What this exam is, in one paragraph

RIMCEE is the entrance examination for **Class 8 admission to the Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun**, for the term beginning July 2027. It is a single pen-and-paper sitting of **4 hours 30 minutes carrying 400 marks**, conducted by NTA across **39 cities**. The bulletin gives a tentative intake of **25 boy cadets and 10 girl cadets**, subject to approval. After the written exam, the **Stage-I result is declared by 20 February 2027**, and the **Stage-II viva voce (50 marks)** is tentatively scheduled for **April 2027**.

And the date that matters most to a family reading this tonight: the exam is **nine days away**. The admit card is the last piece of administration. Once it is downloaded, printed and checked, there is nothing left to file — only the revision your child has already been doing. Download it tomorrow, check it properly, and then let the exam be about the exam.

## FAQs

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 admit card be released?**

The admit card is expected in the window from 29 November to 1 December 2026 — five to seven days before the 6 December written exam, as the NTA RIMCEE-2026 Information Bulletin provides. Download it only from the official NTA portal, exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE.

**Where do I download the RIMCEE 2026 admit card?**

Only from the NTA RIMCEE portal at exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE, using your application number and date of birth. NTA does not email admit cards and does not send them by post.

**What should I check on the RIMCEE admit card?**

Check the candidate's name and photograph, roll number, exam centre name and address, reporting time, paper timings, and the instructions on the card. Report any error to NTA immediately — do not wait until exam morning.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 exam and what happens after it?**

The Stage-I written exam is on Sunday, 6 December 2026 — pen-and-paper, 4 hours 30 minutes, 400 marks, across 39 cities. The Stage-I result is declared by 20 February 2027, and the Stage-II viva voce is tentatively scheduled for April 2027.
