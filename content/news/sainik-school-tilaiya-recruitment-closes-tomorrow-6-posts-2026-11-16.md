If you have been meaning to apply to Sainik School Tilaiya, today is the day to stop meaning to. The recruitment for six teaching and non-teaching posts closes **tomorrow, 17 November 2026** — and this one is offline only. There is no apply-online button to save you at 11:50 PM. Your envelope has to physically reach the school in Koderma district, Jharkhand, by tomorrow, and the school has said plainly that it will not be responsible for postal delays.

That is the whole story in one line: if your application is not already in the post, send it today by the fastest reliable service you can find, and keep the proof of dispatch.

## The six posts

| Post | Category | Vacancies | Type |
|------|----------|-----------|------|
| TGT (Social Science) | SC | 1 | Contractual |
| TGT (Maths) | OBC | 1 | Contractual |
| Music Teacher | OBC | 1 | Contractual |
| Craft & Workshop Instructor | SC | 1 | Contractual |
| Medical Officer | UR | 1 | Contractual |
| Quartermaster | UR | 1 | Regular |

The five contractual posts are initially for 11 months, with extension possible on the school's requirements. Only the Quartermaster post is regular. The advertisement appeared in Employment News (24–30 October 2026), and the school is run by the Sainik Schools Society under the Ministry of Defence.

## Who can apply

Age limits are calculated as on **1 October 2026**. In brief:

- **TGT (Social Science):** Graduate with any two of Geography, History, Economics and Political Science — one of them must be Geography or History — with at least 50% marks, plus B.Ed. or equivalent. Age 21–35.
- **TGT (Maths):** Bachelor's degree in Mathematics with two of Physics, Chemistry, Electronics, Computer Science or Statistics, plus B.Ed. and CTET Paper-II. Age 21–35.
- **Music Teacher:** Higher Secondary plus a degree or diploma in Music from a recognised institution. Age 18–50.
- **Craft & Workshop Instructor:** Higher Secondary plus a two-year trade certificate from a recognised Industrial Training Centre. Age 18–44.
- **Medical Officer:** MBBS from a recognised institution. Age 21–35.
- **Quartermaster:** B.A. or B.Com., plus at least five years of experience as UDC (Stores), Quartermaster or an ex-serviceman — preferably a JCO with at least 10 years of experience handling and accounting stores.

Apply only for the post you actually fulfil the eligibility for, and write the post applied for clearly on the envelope. The school reserves the right to shortlist candidates, and test and interview dates will be notified later on the school website.

## Pay, fee and how to send it

The remuneration reported from the advertisement: TGT posts **₹44,900 fixed** per month, Music Teacher and Craft & Workshop Instructor **₹29,200 fixed**, Medical Officer **₹70,000 fixed**, and the Quartermaster on **Pay Level-5 under the 7th CPC plus allowances**. Contractual posts carry fixed remuneration with no other allowances during the contract period.

The application fee is **₹400 for General/OBC** and **₹250 for SC/ST**, non-refundable, paid only through an account-payee Demand Draft in favour of **Principal, Sainik School Tilaiya**, payable at **State Bank of India, Sainik School Tilaiya Branch (Branch Code 3502)**.

The envelope must contain: the filled prescribed form, self-attested copies of educational and professional certificates, experience certificates, date-of-birth proof and category certificate if applicable, the Demand Draft, and a self-addressed envelope bearing a **₹55 postage stamp**. Send it to the Principal, Sainik School Tilaiya, Tilaiya Dam, Koderma, Jharkhand. Keep a copy of everything and the proof of dispatch.

## What to do today

1. **Download the form now** from sainikschooltilaiya.org and check it against the notification PDF on the same site. If the fee or any detail differs from what is reported here, the school's notification wins.
2. **Get the Demand Draft made today.** Banks take their time, and a draft is the one thing you cannot fix at the post office.
3. **Post by Speed Post or a reliable courier today**, addressed exactly as above, with the post written on the envelope. Do not wait for tomorrow morning — tomorrow is the last date for *receipt*, not for posting.
4. **Watch the school website** for the test and interview schedule, which will be notified there later.

This is one of three Sainik School deadlines we flagged in this week's roundup — Sainik School Nagrota's three posts close on 20 November, and that one is also Speed Post only, so the same warning applies there. Tilaiya simply runs out first.
