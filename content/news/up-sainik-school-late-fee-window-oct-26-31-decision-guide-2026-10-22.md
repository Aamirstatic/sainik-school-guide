Sunday, 25 October, 5 PM — that is when the regular UP Sainik Schools application window closes at ₹1,000. From Monday morning, the same form costs **₹2,000 in total**, and that price holds till 31 October, 5 PM. After that, the door shuts for the 2027-28 session.

This piece is not another "hurry up" reminder. It is for the parent doing the real math tonight: *Can I still make Sunday? And if I can't — is the late window worth ₹2,000, or should I treat it as a trap?* Here is the honest answer.

## What the late window actually is

Think of it as a paid extension, not a second chance with new rules. Everything about the process stays identical — same portal ([upsainikschool.org](https://upsainikschool.org)), same one-mobile-one-registration rule, same eligibility (UP domicile for both applicant and father; Class 6 born 2 July 2015 – 1 January 2018; Class 9 born 2 July 2012 – 1 January 2015), same correction window of **3–5 November**, same tentative exam date of **20 December 2026**. The only thing that changes is your wallet: ₹1,000 becomes ₹2,000.

One thing worth saying plainly: the late window is a facility, not a penalty designed to punish you. The Society knows documents get stuck — a caste certificate that hasn't come back from the tehsil, a domicile proof with a spelling error. The extra ₹1,000 buys six more days to sort real problems. That is a fair trade when the problem is real.

## Who should use it — and who shouldn't

**Use it if:** a document is genuinely stuck outside your control, you only discovered the exam this week, or a payment/OTP failure ate your Sunday attempt. In these cases the late window is exactly what it's for. Apply on **26 or 27 October** — early in the window, not on the 31st — so you keep a buffer before the correction window.

**Don't use it for:** procrastination. And here is why this matters beyond the money. Every day you push the application back is a day you lose from the other end — the correction window (3–5 November) doesn't move, and the tentative exam (20 December) doesn't move. A form filed on 31 October gets the same three correction days as one filed on 20 October, but with zero slack for a rejected photo or a bounced payment. The ₹1,000 you "save" by waiting is the most expensive saving you'll make all year.

Last year, a parent we spoke to filed on the final evening of a similar late window, the payment gateway timed out at 4:40 PM, and he spent the last twenty minutes refreshing a frozen page. He got through — barely. Don't be that parent. If you can file this weekend, file this weekend.

## If you do use the late window: the checklist

- **Prepare documents before Monday.** Photo, signature, birth certificate, domicile, caste certificate if applicable — scanned, correct size, correct format. The late window gives you days, not a reason to start from zero.
- **One mobile, one registration.** This rule doesn't relax in the late window. A second registration on another number will get flagged, not merged. Our [one-mobile explainer](/blog/up-sainik-school-one-mobile-one-registration-2026-10-16/) covers why.
- **Save the confirmation page.** The application number on it is your key to the admit card and the correction window. Screenshot it, print it, email it to yourself.
- **Mark 3–5 November now.** Registration-stage details (mobile number, name, class) can't be edited even in the correction window. Get them right the first time.
- **Keep 20 December in sight.** The exam is tentatively 59 days after tonight. Our [post-application roadmap](/news/up-sainik-school-dec-20-exam-interview-medical-roadmap-2026-10-22/) maps what happens between the form and the final merit list.

The UP Sainik Schools at Lucknow and Gorakhpur run their own entrance exam, separate from the national AISSEE — which is exactly why this deadline doesn't appear on the NTA portal, and why so many parents hear about it late. If you're reading this on Thursday night with the form still unfilled: the weekend is your window, and Monday is your safety net. Use the window. Keep the safety net for a real emergency.

## FAQs

**What is the UP Sainik School late-fee window for 2027-28?**
Applications can still be submitted from 26 to 31 October 2026 (till 5 PM) on upsainikschool.org, at a total fee of ₹2,000 instead of the regular ₹1,000.

**Is the UP Sainik late fee refundable?**
No. Application fees, including the late fee, are non-refundable. Pay it only when your form is complete and correct.

**Does applying in the late window change the exam or the correction window?**
No. The entrance exam stays tentatively scheduled for 20 December 2026 and the correction window stays 3 to 5 November 2026, regardless of when you applied.

**Can I edit my UP Sainik form after submitting in the late window?**
Only during the correction window (3–5 November 2026), and only the fields the portal allows. Registration-stage details like mobile number cannot be changed at all.
