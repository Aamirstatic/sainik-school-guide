## Six days left — and the late window is not the safety net it looks like

The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. That is six days from today. The fee — **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, **₹700** for SC/ST — has to be paid by the same hour.

And yes, there is a late window: **3 to 6 December**, with an extra **₹500** on top of the fee. Plenty of parents are quietly counting on it. Here is the honest truth: the ₹500 is the least of your worries. The late window is a rescue boat, not a plan — and rescue boats leak.

## What actually goes wrong in the late window

**A rejected photo or signature has nowhere to go.** The most common reason forms get stuck is an uploaded photo or signature that does not meet the bulletin's size or format. If that happens on 5 December, you have hours to re-scan and re-submit — with the portal at its busiest. If it happens on 27 November, you have five calm days.

**A failed payment at 11 PM on 6 December is final.** Payment gateways time out on the last evening of every national exam form. A payment that fails on a normal day can be retried the same afternoon. One that fails at 11 PM on 6 December cannot be fixed by 11:50 PM — and after that, there is no AISSEE 2027 for your child.

**The correction window cannot fix everything.** NTA opens a correction window from **7 to 9 December 2026**, but only certain particulars can be edited. It is not a second application, and it will not undo a wrong school preference or exam centre choice made in a rush. Assume whatever you submit is close to final.

**The portal itself is the risk.** Lakhs of families file in the last 48 hours. Pages load slowly, sessions time out, and the helpline is at its busiest exactly when you need it most. That is just what the last day of a national form looks like, every single year.

## The bottom line

Six days is plenty. The form takes less than an hour once the documents are ready. What costs families their child's AISSEE 2027 attempt is almost never the form — it is the decision to do it tomorrow. Do it today, do it by Friday, and the late window becomes something you read about, not something you depend on.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. The fee must also be paid by the same time — a form with an unpaid fee is not a submitted form.

**Can I still apply for AISSEE 2027 after 2 December?**

Yes, in the late-fee window from 3 to 6 December 2026, by paying an extra ₹500 over the regular fee. The last date for fee payment with the late fee is 6 December at 11:50 PM. After that, there is no way to apply for AISSEE 2027.

**How much is the AISSEE 2027 fee in the late window?**

₹1,350 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards (₹850 plus ₹500 late fee) and ₹1,200 for SC and ST candidates (₹700 plus ₹500).

**Can I correct mistakes in my AISSEE 2027 form after submitting it?**

NTA opens a correction window from 7 to 9 December 2026, but only certain particulars can be edited — it is not a fresh application. That is why the form should be filled carefully the first time, well before the deadline.
