## Two deadlines, one family

Here is the thing most AISSEE parents are quietly juggling right now: there are two clocks running at once. The first one is loud — the application form closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**, just 20 days away. The second one is quieter but bigger — the exam itself is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**, 80 days away.

The window opened on 3 November. Nine days are gone. If your child's form is already submitted and the confirmation page is saved, you can relax on the first clock and focus entirely on the second. But if the form is still sitting in the "we'll do it this weekend" pile, this week is the week to finish it — because the last days of any NTA window are exactly when portals slow down, payment gateways hiccup, and small document problems turn into missed deadlines.

## The form track and the prep track

| | Form track (finish by 2 Dec) | Prep track (runs to 31 Jan) |
|---|---|---|
| **This week** | Gather documents, fill the form, pay the fee, save the confirmation page | Keep the daily study rhythm; one full mock paper this Sunday |
| **7–9 Dec** | Correction window — fix errors only, nothing new can be added | Revision of weak chapters, not new topics |
| **11 Jan 2027** | Exam city slip — note the allotted city | Start planning travel and the exam-day routine |
| **25 Jan 2027** | Admit card — download, print, check every detail the same day | Final revision, OMR practice, early nights |
| **31 Jan 2027** | Exam day: Class VI 2:00–4:30 PM, Class IX 2:00–5:00 PM | — |

## Don't plan around the late-fee window

Yes, there is a safety net: applications submitted between **3 and 6 December 2026** are accepted with an additional late fee of **Rs. 500**. But treat it as a safety net, not a plan. You will be applying in the same last-minute rush, with the same portal load, and the same risk that a rejected photo or a failed payment leaves you no time to fix it. Apply in the regular window. The late-fee window exists for genuine emergencies, not for procrastination.

## Your "apply this week" checklist

1. **Documents ready before you log in:** photograph, signature and thumb impression in the sizes the Information Bulletin specifies, plus domicile and category certificates if they apply to you.
2. **School preferences decided:** list your schools in genuine order of preference — home-state quota and girls' seats make the order matter. Our guide to [which schools to list and in what order](/blog/aissee-2027-form-which-sainik-schools-to-list-rank-order/) walks through it.
3. **Exam cities chosen carefully:** pick four cities you can actually reach. The city slip on 11 January only tells you the city; the exact centre comes with the admit card on 25 January.
4. **Fee paid and confirmation saved:** Rs. 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and defence wards; Rs. 700 for SC/ST. Pay by card, net banking or UPI, then download and print the confirmation page the same day.
5. **Details double-checked:** name spelling against the birth certificate, date of birth, category — the correction window (7–9 December) fixes errors, but it cannot fix a form you never submitted.
6. **Only the official portal:** apply at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. No agent, no school website, no "admission guaranteed" service can file the form for you.

A parent in Lucknow told us last week that her son had finished three mock tests but the form was still untouched — "the exam feels real, the form feels like paperwork." She is right about the feeling and wrong about the risk. The form is the ticket. No form, no exam, no matter how good the mock scores are. Finish it this week, and the remaining 80 days belong entirely to your child. ## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. Applications are accepted only on the official NTA portal, exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society.

**Can I still apply for AISSEE 2027 after 2 December?**

Yes, but only in the late-fee window from 3 to 6 December 2026, with an additional late fee of Rs. 500. The correction window then runs from 7 to 9 December 2026.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs. 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and Rs. 700 for SC/ST candidates. The fee is paid online and is generally non-refundable.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam, and when do I get the admit card?**

The exam is on Sunday, 31 January 2027 — Class VI from 2:00 to 4:30 PM and Class IX from 2:00 to 5:00 PM. The exam city slip comes on 11 January 2027 and the admit card on 25 January 2027.
