If you teach — or want to — and a Sainik School is on your list, this is the week to act. Three schools have deadlines in the next eight days, and two of them close before the week is out. Here is all three, in deadline order, so you can see at a glance which one you can still make.

## The three deadlines, in one table

| School | Last date | Posts | How to apply |
|--------|-----------|-------|--------------|
| **Sainik School Tilaiya** (Jharkhand) | **Monday, 17 November 2026** | 6 | Offline, by post with demand draft |
| **Sainik School Nagrota** (Jammu) | **Thursday, 20 November 2026** | 3 | Offline, **Speed Post only** |
| **Sainik School Purulia** (West Bengal) | **Monday, 23 November 2026** (walk-in, 8:30 AM) | 3 | Walk in with documents |

## Tilaiya: 6 posts, closes Monday 17 November

Sainik School Tilaiya in Koderma district, Jharkhand, is hiring for six teaching and non-teaching posts, advertised in Employment News (24–30 October 2026): **TGT (Social Science), TGT (Maths), Music Teacher, Craft & Workshop Instructor, Medical Officer and Quartermaster**, on contractual and regular bases. The completed application form — downloaded from the school's official website, sainikschooltilaiya.org — along with documents and a demand draft, must **reach the school by 17 November 2026**. Contractual appointments are initially for 11 months, with extension possible. Test and interview dates will be notified later on the school website. Our full guide to the [Tilaiya recruitment](/news/sainik-school-tilaiya-recruitment-6-posts-nov-17-2026-11-01/) has the post-wise details.

Two days is not much time. If you are applying, post today — a form that arrives on the 18th is a form that gets rejected.

## Nagrota: 3 posts, Speed Post by 20 November

Sainik School Nagrota, Jammu and Kashmir, has invited applications for three posts under Advertisement No. EN 31/61 (Employment News, 31 October–6 November 2026): **TGT (Computer Science)** — one regular post, Pay Level 7 of ₹44,900–₹1,42,400 plus allowances, age 21–35 — plus **Art Master** and **Nursing Sister (Female)** on contract. The application fee is **₹500, non-refundable**, and applications must be sent by **Speed Post only** to reach the Principal by **20 November 2026**. The school warns that postal delay is the applicant's responsibility, so do not trust ordinary post for this one. Our [Nagrota recruitment guide](/news/sainik-school-nagrota-recruitment-3-posts-nov-20-2026-10-31/) has the eligibility and qualification details.

## Purulia: walk-in interview, Monday 23 November

The simplest of the three to act on — no postal deadline to miss. Sainik School Purulia will hold a walk-in interview at **8:30 AM on Monday, 23 November 2026**, for three ad hoc teaching posts: **TGT (Social Science), TGT (Hindi) and Art Master**. The consolidated pay is **₹25,000 a month** for the TGT posts and **₹21,000 a month** for Art Master. The age limit is 21–35 years as on 1 November 2026. Candidates should report with their application, bio-data, original certificates and attested copies of certificates and mark sheets. No TA/DA is paid. Our [Purulia walk-in guide](/news/sainik-school-purulia-walk-in-interview-nov-23-tgt-art-master-2026-11-05/) covers what to carry.

## One honest note before you apply

All three notices carry the same warning in different words: the school can cancel a vacancy for administrative reasons, and the official notification — not any job portal, and not this article — is what counts. Download the notice from the school's own website, check the eligibility against your own certificates, and keep a copy of everything you send. For parents reading this instead: the AISSEE 2027 form is still open until 2 December — our [notification explainer](/news/aissee-2027-notification-out-applications-open-exam-jan-31-2026-11-03/) has the dates.

## FAQs

**What is the last date for Sainik School Tilaiya recruitment 2026?**

Monday, 17 November 2026. The completed offline application, with documents and demand draft, must reach Sainik School Tilaiya by that date. Six posts are open: TGT Social Science, TGT Maths, Music Teacher, Craft and Workshop Instructor, Medical Officer and Quartermaster.

**What is the last date for Sainik School Nagrota recruitment 2026?**

Thursday, 20 November 2026. Applications for three posts — TGT Computer Science, Art Master and Nursing Sister (Female) — must reach the school by Speed Post only, with a non-refundable application fee of 500 rupees.

**When is the Sainik School Purulia walk-in interview?**

Monday, 23 November 2026 at 8:30 AM at Sainik School Purulia, West Bengal. Three ad hoc teaching posts are open — TGT Social Science, TGT Hindi and Art Master — and candidates should carry their application, bio-data, original certificates and attested copies. No TA or DA is paid.

**Where do I get the Sainik School recruitment application form?**

From the school's own official website — sainikschooltilaiya.org for Tilaiya, sainikschoolnagrota.com for Nagrota, and sainikschoolpurulia.com for Purulia. Always verify the post, eligibility and fee in the official notification before applying.
