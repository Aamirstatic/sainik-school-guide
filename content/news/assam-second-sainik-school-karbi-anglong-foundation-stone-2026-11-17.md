## A second Sainik School for Assam

Assam is getting a second Sainik School. Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma laid the foundation stone for the new school at **Langvoku in Karbi Anglong district on Tuesday, 17 November 2026**, as part of the district's Grand Silver Jubilee celebrations.

Until now, Assam's only Sainik School has been at Goalpara. For families in Karbi Anglong and the surrounding hill districts, that meant sending a ten-year-old hundreds of kilometres away — or not applying at all. A second school in the state changes that maths, at least for the next generation of applicants.

## The project, in numbers

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Location | Langvoku, Karbi Anglong district, Assam |
| Foundation stone | 17 November 2026, by CM Himanta Biswa Sarma |
| Cost | ₹336 crore |
| Funded by | Ministry of Defence |
| Built by | Assam State Public Works Department (PWD) |
| Campus area | 44,000 square metres |
| Capacity | 640 cadets |
| Construction time | 30 months |

The Chief Minister said the school will have modern infrastructure to prepare young students for careers in the armed forces and national service. The state PWD has been given 30 months to complete construction.

## Why this matters for AISSEE parents

Here's the honest part: **this school will not take a single admission this cycle, or next.** Thirty months of construction from November 2026 runs to roughly mid-2029, and a school needs staff, affiliation and an AISSEE listing before it can admit cadets. None of that exists yet.

So what should a parent in Assam actually do with this news?

First, **don't let anyone sell you a seat.** A new school announcement is exactly the moment agents and coaching centres start promising admissions. There is no admission process for Langvoku today — no form, no list, no quota. The only real route into any Sainik School remains the AISSEE, and the [AISSEE 2027 application window](/news/aissee-2027-notification-out-applications-open-exam-jan-31-2026-11-03/) closes on 2 December 2026 for the existing schools.

Second, **keep Goalpara on your list.** For the AISSEE 2027 cycle, Sainik School Goalpara remains Assam's Sainik School in the bulletin, and the [new Sainik Schools in the 2027 bulletin](/news/aissee-2027-new-sainik-schools-80-class-6-40-class-9-explained-2026-11-06/) are the approved private-partner schools already listed — Langvoku is not among them.

Third, **file this away for the future.** If your child is in Class 4 or 5 today, Langvoku could realistically be an option by the time they reach Class 6 — if construction stays on schedule. We will track the school's progress and report when it first appears in an AISSEE bulletin.

## What happens next

The PWD begins construction now, and the 30-month clock is the number to watch. The next real milestone is not a ceremony — it is the school's name appearing in an NTA information bulletin with a seat count. Until then, this is a foundation stone, not an admission opportunity. Good news for Assam's future cadets, but no shortcut for this year's applicants.

## FAQs

**Where is Assam's second Sainik School being built?**

At Langvoku in Karbi Anglong district, Assam. Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma laid its foundation stone on 17 November 2026.

**How much will the new Sainik School in Karbi Anglong cost, and who is funding it?**

₹336 crore, funded by the Ministry of Defence. The Assam State Public Works Department will construct the campus.

**How many cadets will the Karbi Anglong Sainik School hold?**

640 cadets. The campus will be built across 44,000 square metres, with modern infrastructure for military-oriented education.

**When will admissions open at the new Sainik School in Karbi Anglong?**

No admission date has been announced. Construction alone is scheduled to take 30 months, so admissions will only begin once the campus is ready — families should watch for the school to appear in a future AISSEE bulletin, and should not trust any agent promising a seat now.
