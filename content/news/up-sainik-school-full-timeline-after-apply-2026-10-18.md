Note the rhythm: this is a five-month funnel. The correction window (Nov 3–5) matters more than it looks — the portal states that registration-stage details **cannot be edited after successful registration** (TOI), so use that window rather than discovering a wrong date of birth in January.

## The eligibility fine print parents miss

The prospectus carries three rules that surprise families every year:

1. **Double domicile.** Not just the child — the applicant *and* their father must both be domiciles of Uttar Pradesh ✅ (edexlive, citing prospectus).
2. **Age cutoffs are tight.** Class 6: born **2 July 2015 – 1 January 2018**; Class 9: born **2 July 2012 – 1 January 2015** ✅. Both dates inclusive.
3. **Boys/girls rules differ by school.** At the Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow, Class 6 is **boys only**; Class 9 takes boys and girls. The Gorakhpur school is co-ed for both classes ✅.

Academic bar: the child must pass Class 5 (or Class 8) from a recognised school by 20 May 2027 ✅. Reservations per the prospectus: 27% OBC, 21% SC, 10% EWS-General, 2% ST ✅.

## The exam itself

The written test is OMR-based and objective: **English, General Knowledge, Mathematics and Intelligence — 200 marks, two hours and thirty minutes** ✅ (edexlive, prospectus). Shortlisted candidates then face a medical examination and an interview; final selection rests on written score, medical fitness, interview and reservation rules. Our guide on [UP Sainik School vs the national AISSEE route](/news/up-sainik-school-vs-aissee-which-form-to-fill-2026-10-07/) helps if you are filling both forms this month.

One caution straight from TOI's report: the official portal also hosts a **sample OMR sheet and sample question paper** — download those, and make payments only on upsainikschool.org. Anyone demanding money "to guarantee admission" is running a scam.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for UP Sainik School admission 2027-28?**
October 25, 2026 (5 PM) without a late fee, and October 31, 2026 (5 PM) with the late fee. The regular fee is Rs. 1,000; applying in the late window costs Rs. 2,000 total.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam 2027-28?**
Tentatively December 20, 2026. The exam is OMR-based, 200 marks, two and a half hours, covering English, General Knowledge, Mathematics and Intelligence.

**Who can apply for UP Sainik School 2027-28?**
The applicant and their father must both be domiciles of Uttar Pradesh. For Class 6 the child must be born between July 2, 2015 and January 1, 2018; for Class 9 between July 2, 2012 and January 1, 2015. Lucknow's Class 6 is boys-only; Gorakhpur is co-ed for both classes.

**When do UP Sainik School 2027-28 final results come out?**
Tentatively March 15 to 20, 2027, after the written exam (Dec 20), answer key (Dec 24), written result (Dec 30), and interviews plus medicals in February 2027. Confirm every date against the official portal, upsainikschool.org.
