## The form opened on 3 November. You still have 20 days — use them properly

If you only heard about AISSEE 2027 this week, you are not late. You are exactly the parent this piece is for. Applications opened on 3 November and close on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. That is 20 days from today — more than enough, if you do the steps in the right order and stop treating the form like a weekend project you will get to eventually.

Here is the order that works. Do not skip step one.

## Step 1 (tonight): check the age rule before anything else

Age is the one condition NTA enforces without exceptions. There is no relaxation for any category — not SC/ST, not defence wards. Check it against the birth certificate, not against memory.

- **Class 6:** your child must be **10 to 12 years old as on 31 March 2027** — born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017** — and studying in Class 5 (for New Sainik Schools: passed or appearing in Class 5).
- **Class 9:** your child must be **13 to 15 years old as on 31 March 2027** — born between **1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014** — and must have passed Class 8 from a recognised school. Students already in Class 9 or above are not eligible.

If the age fits, everything else is fixable. If it doesn't, no document in the world saves the application — and it is better to know that tonight than on 2 December.

## Step 2 (this weekend): gather four things, then sit down once

The form itself takes under an hour. What eats days is hunting for documents mid-form. Get these ready first:

1. **A fresh photograph** — taken on or after **1 July 2026**, JPG, 10 to 200 KB, white background, 80% face coverage, no cap or goggles. Last year's school photo does not qualify.
2. **Signature and thumb impression** — the child's signature, and the **left-thumb impression in blue ink** on white paper (JPG, 10 to 50 KB). A right-thumb impression is allowed only if the left thumb is unavailable.
3. **Domicile certificate** — needed for the 67% home-state quota in the 33 existing schools. If yours is not ready, apply for it this week; do not leave it for the last days.
4. **Category certificate, if applicable** — SC/ST/OBC-NCL or defence-ward proof, in the format the bulletin asks for.

Check the exact file sizes in the Information Bulletin before you scan anything. A file that is too large is the most common reason the portal rejects an upload — and the fix is a two-minute resize, not a second attempt at midnight.

## Step 3: fill the form and pay — then keep the confirmation page

Apply only at the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**. The fee is **₹850** for General, OBC (NCL) and defence wards, and **₹700** for SC/ST, paid online by card, net banking or UPI. The fee is generally non-refundable once paid, so check every entry before you pay — especially the name spelling, date of birth and category, which must match the certificates exactly.

If something goes wrong after submission, there is a **correction window from 7 to 9 December** — three days, and NTA does not reopen it. And if you miss 2 December entirely, the **late window runs 3 to 6 December with an extra ₹500**. Treat both as safety nets, not plans: the late window costs money, and the correction window cannot fix everything.

## Step 4 (from tomorrow): a first study week that a real child can actually do

The exam is on **31 January 2027** — a pen-and-paper OMR test, multiple choice, **no negative marking**, so your child should attempt every question. Class 6 is 150 minutes, 125 questions, 300 marks; Class 9 is 180 minutes, 150 questions, 400 marks. Mathematics carries half the marks in both papers, so it gets half the study time.

For a child starting now, the first week is not about finishing the syllabus. It is about building the habit: one hour of Maths drills a day, 20 minutes of reading, and one short mock on Sunday at 2 PM — the same time as the real exam. That is it. A child who does that for four weeks arrives at December far ahead of the child who bought five guidebooks and opened none of them.

## What happens after you apply

The exam-city slip comes on **11 January 2027** and the admit card on **25 January 2027**. The result date has not been announced yet. After the exam, school-wise merit lists are followed by a medical examination, document verification and e-counselling. None of that needs your attention tonight — tonight needs the age check and the documents.

Twenty days is plenty. But only if the first step happens tonight.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. If you miss it, there is a late window from 3 to 6 December with an extra fee of Rs. 500 — but do not plan around it. Apply in the regular window.

**What is the age limit for AISSEE 2027 Class 6?**

Your child must be 10 to 12 years old as on 31 March 2027 — born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017. There is no age relaxation for any category, including SC/ST and defence wards.

**Can girls apply for AISSEE 2027?**

Yes. Boys and girls are both eligible for Class 6 in the 33 existing Sainik Schools and the approved New Sainik Schools. For Class 9, girls are eligible subject to availability of seats.

**What documents do I need before filling the AISSEE 2027 form?**

A recent photograph taken on or after 1 July 2026, the child's signature, a left-thumb impression in blue ink, plus the domicile certificate and category certificate if applicable. Check the exact file sizes in the Information Bulletin before you scan anything.
