## The official date is 2 December. Your date should be 30 November

There are **13 days** left to apply for AISSEE 2027. The National Testing Agency's window closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**, and the exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. Those are the official dates, and they are not going to move.

But the last day of a form is the worst day to fill it. Education Times, reporting on the NTA notice, puts it plainly — applicants "should not wait for the final day, as portal congestion and payment-gateway delays are common close to deadlines." That is not a warning about other people's children. It is about yours, on the night of 2 December, watching a payment spinner at 11 PM.

So make a small change to your plan. Keep 2 December on the calendar, but treat **Monday, 30 November** as your real deadline.

## Three things that go wrong on the last day

**1. The payment fails, and the fix takes a day or two.** If the fee leaves your account but no confirmation page appears, the correct move is to wait 24 to 48 hours and check your candidate login before paying again. On 30 November, that wait still ends before the deadline. On 2 December, it ends after it. We explained the exact steps in [our fee-payment guide]({{< relref "aissee-2027-fee-payment-failed-13-days-left-2026-11-19.md" >}}).

**2. A photo or signature gets rejected, and there is no time to re-scan.** The portal rejects files that do not match the bulletin's size and format specifications. Re-scanning a thumb impression or getting a fresh passport photo of a fidgety 10-year-old is a one-hour job on a normal day — and an impossible one at 10 PM on deadline day. The five upload mistakes that reject forms most often are listed in [our 17 November guide]({{< relref "aissee-2027-15-days-left-5-upload-mistakes-that-reject-forms-2026-11-17.md" >}}).

**3. A small error gets submitted because nobody re-read the form.** Tired parents at midnight mistype a date of birth or pick the wrong exam city. The correction window from **7 to 9 December** can fix some fields — but not everything, and it cannot rescue a form that was never submitted or a fee that never went through. Audit your confirmation page the moment it is generated, using the checklist in [our correction-window guide]({{< relref "aissee-2027-correction-window-7-9-december-audit-confirmation-page-2026-11-18.md" >}}).

## What to finish this weekend (21–22 November)

This Saturday and Sunday are the last calm weekend before deadline week. Use them for the boring jobs, so the final days are only for checking:

- **One folder, everything in it.** Birth certificate, Class 5 report card (Class 6 applicants) or Class 8 certificate (Class 9), domicile certificate, category certificate if applicable, and the child's Aadhaar. If any certificate is missing, Monday is the day to chase it — not 2 December.
- **Photo, signature and thumb impression, scanned to spec.** Check the exact dimensions and file sizes in the information bulletin on exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society before you scan, not after the portal rejects them.
- **Fee ready in the account you will pay from.** ₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards; ₹700 for SC/ST. The fee is non-refundable, so confirm the amount and the category before paying.
- **A second pair of eyes.** Have someone else read the form before you hit submit — date of birth, spelling of the name, school preferences, exam city. The person who filled it is the worst person to proofread it.

## If you genuinely cannot finish by 30 November

Do not panic, and do not skip applying. The late window from **3 to 6 December** exists precisely for this — ₹500 extra, same exam, same schools. What you must not do is drift past 6 December, because after that there is no window at all.
