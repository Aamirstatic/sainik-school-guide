## What happened

Sainik School Kalikiri in Annamayya district, Andhra Pradesh, has posted its **best-ever performance** in the UPSC National Defence Academy written examination: **50 cadets qualified for Course No. 158** ✅.

The headline inside that number: **three of them are girls**, from the school's **first-ever batch of female cadets** ✅. Sainik Schools only began admitting girls in recent years — so this is not just a Kalikiri milestone. It is proof, from a school that got its first girl cadets not long ago, that the path from AISSEE to the NDA now runs fully open for girls.

Of the 79 students in the school's current Class XII batch, **41 cleared** — a success rate of nearly **52%** ✅. Nine more cadets from the previous batch also qualified, taking the overall tally to 50.

## Why this matters to AISSEE 2027 parents

Parents ask me all the time: "Does a Sainik School *actually* lead to the NDA, or is that just the brochure?" Kalikiri's numbers are the answer. Since its first batch graduated in 2021, **34 former cadets have joined the NDA and other defence academies**, and **five were commissioned as officers earlier this year** ✅.

And for parents of daughters considering AISSEE 2027: three girls clearing the NDA written in the school's very first girls' batch tells you the training machinery there doesn't treat girl cadets as an experiment. It treats them as cadets.

## What comes next for the 50 qualifiers

The written exam is stage one. The 50 cadets now face the **Services Selection Board (SSB) interview**, which tests leadership, mental aptitude, personality and overall suitability for military service, followed by medicals ✅. Principal Group Captain C.S. Pardesi credited the school's teaching and training staff for their systematic preparation ✅.
