## A fresh notice, two days old

Sainik School Rewari, in village Gothra of Haryana's Rewari district, has issued a short recruitment notice dated **13 October 2026** for **5 teaching and non-teaching posts**. This is one of the fresher Sainik School hiring announcements this month — and the clock on it is genuinely short: offline applications are accepted only between **17 October and 6 November 2026 (5:00 PM)**.

This is a *receipt* deadline, not a posting deadline. Your application must be in the school's hands at Gothra by 5 PM on 6 November. Given postal delays, speed post from outside Haryana realistically needs 4–5 working days — so treat **31 October** as your personal last day to post.

October has been unusually busy for Sainik School hiring — compare with the [Sainik School Kunjpura recruitment (15 posts, apply by 31 October)](/news/sainik-school-kunjpura-recruitment-2026-16-days-left-2026-10-15/), the [Sainik School Mainpuri recruitment (2 posts, apply by 25 October)](/news/sainik-school-mainpuri-recruitment-2026-10-days-left-2026-10-15/) and the [Sainik School Goalpara recruitment (13 posts, apply by 12 November)](/news/sainik-school-goalpara-recruitment-2026-2026-10-14/). Rewari's list is smaller but has an interesting spread — a doctor, a physical-education teacher, a nursing assistant, a matron and a counsellor.

## Fee, age and selection

- **Application fee:** ₹500 for General and OBC candidates, paid by **bank draft** drawn in favour of the Principal, Sainik School Rewari, payable at Rewari. **SC and ST candidates pay no fee.**
- **Age limit:** teaching posts are reported as 21–35 years and non-teaching posts as 18–50 years, with relaxation as per government norms. Note a small discrepancy between portals on the PGT (Physical Education) upper age limit (one lists 21–50) — 🔶 EXPECTED: confirm the exact band for your post in the official notice.
- **Selection process:** shortlisting of applications, followed by a written exam and/or interview, skill test where applicable, medical examination and document verification. No TA/DA is admissible.

## How to apply (offline only)

Download the prescribed application form and the full notification from **ssrw.org** (available from 17 October 2026). Fill it carefully, attach self-attested copies of your certificates from matriculation onward, a passport-size photograph, the bank draft, and send the complete packet to: **The Principal, Sainik School Rewari, Vill – Gothra, Distt – Rewari, Haryana – 123102.** Write on the envelope: **"APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF ……………"** (filling in the post name). Keep a photocopy of everything you send, and get a postal receipt as proof of dispatch.

One practical note: this is an *offline* process, so there is no confirmation SMS or application number. Your postal receipt is your only proof that you applied on time — file it safely until the selection list is out.
