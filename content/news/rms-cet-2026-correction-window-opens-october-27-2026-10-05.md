> **Last verified: 5 October 2026.** Correction-window dates and the fee schedule are read from NTA's official RMS CET 2026 bulletin. Field-level correction rules are NTA standard practice — marked EXPECTED — until the portal publishes this year's editable-fields list on 27 October.

You have two days. That is the entire correction window NTA gives RMS CET 2026 applicants: **27 and 28 October 2026** (CONFIRMED — NTA bulletin). After that, your form is final.

![Student reviewing the RMS CET 2026 application form before the correction window](/images/inbody/rms-cet-2026-correction-window-opens-october-27-2026-10-05.webp)

## Where things stand right now

The regular application window closes on **20 October 2026 at 5 PM** (fee by 11:50 PM the same night), and the late window — with an extra Rs. 500 — runs **21 to 25 October 2026** (CONFIRMED). The correction window on 27–28 October covers all submitted applications.

## What you can typically fix — and what you can't

Corrections run on the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet** (CONFIRMED), using your application number and password — nothing accepted by email, phone or post. NTA's correction windows follow a consistent pattern. The exact editable-fields list for RMS CET 2026 will appear on the portal when the window opens, but based on standard NTA practice (EXPECTED):

| Usually correctable | Usually locked |
|---|---|
| Photograph and signature (re-upload) | Registered mobile number |
| Exam city choices | Registered email ID |
| Category (with balance fee if the slab changes) | Candidate's name |
| Academic details (class, school, board) | Date of birth |
| Father's/mother's name spelling | Application number |

Read the editable-fields list on the portal on 27 October itself — do not assume this year's list is identical. If a correction moves you from a lower fee slab to a higher one (for example, SC to General), NTA's standard practice is that the **balance fee must be paid during the window** (EXPECTED). Corrections within the same slab carry no extra charge.

## Check these five things in your form this week

Do not wait for 27 October to discover a problem. Open your submitted application PDF now and verify:

1. **Photo and signature** — the most common rejection reasons. The bulletin specifies size, format and background; a casual selfie-style photo is the classic failure.
2. **Category selection** — a wrong category changes both your fee and your merit-list eligibility. Match it against your caste certificate, not memory.
3. **Name spelling** — must match school records and birth certificate. Fix it only if the window allows, with supporting documents ready.
4. **Exam city order** — your four preferences decide where the centre lands. Rank them by real travel convenience.
5. **Class and school details** — the class you are in now, and whether you are applying for Class 6 or Class 9 across the five schools (Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum, Bengaluru).

Check the form now, keep your login credentials and the confirmation page saved, and when the window opens on the 27th, log in on day one — portal traffic on the last evening is the one stress you can avoid. ## FAQs

**When is the RMS CET 2026 correction window?**
The application correction window runs on 27 and 28 October 2026 on the official NTA portal at exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet. It covers candidates who submitted their forms in both the regular window (till 20 October) and the late window (21 to 25 October).

**What can I change in the RMS CET 2026 form?**
Per NTA's standard correction practice, you can typically update your photograph and signature, exam city choices, category, and academic details. Registered mobile number, email and the candidate's name are usually locked. NTA publishes the exact editable-fields list on the portal when the window opens.

**Will I be charged extra for corrections?**
If a correction moves you to a higher fee slab — for example, changing category from SC to General — NTA's standard practice is that the balance fee must be paid during the window itself. Corrections within the same slab carry no extra charge.

**What happens if I don't correct a mistake by 28 October?**
NTA accepts no changes after 28 October. A wrong category, a mismatched name or a rejected photograph can follow the candidate to the admit card and counselling, so this two-day window is the last safety net.
