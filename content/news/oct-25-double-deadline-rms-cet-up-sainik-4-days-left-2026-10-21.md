## If you are still applying to RMS CET 2026

The regular window closed on 20 October; the late window that opened on 21 October is the last chance. During 21–25 October you pay the normal fee **plus ₹500**: ₹1,500 total for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates, ₹1,250 for SC, ST and wards of personnel killed in action.

Get these two right: a bank debit alone does not prove submission — download the confirmation page. And the 5 PM cut-off on 25 October is for the form; fee payment runs till 11:50 PM, but do not use those hours as a buffer. Then keep 27–28 October free — the correction window, where a typo in name, category or photo can still be fixed.

## If you are still applying to UP Sainik School

The normal-fee window closes on 25 October at 5 PM for both the Lucknow and Gorakhpur schools. Miss it and you can still apply until 31 October — at ₹2,000 extra. The correction window is 3–5 November, admit cards are expected after 25 November, and the written exam is on 20 December.

Eligibility recap: Class 6 applicants must be born between 2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018; Class 9 applicants between 2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015. Near a boundary date? Verify against the official prospectus before paying.

## Why this week decides the December season

RMS CET's written exam is 53 days away (13 December); UP Sainik School's is 60 days away (20 December). The families who file this week get the correction window as a safety net and two full months of undistracted preparation. The families who miss 25 October either pay the penalty or lose the cycle entirely.

## What to do today (21 October)

1. If either form is pending, complete it today — not on the 25th, when portals slow down.
2. Keep scanned photo, signature, birth, domicile and category certificates ready before logging in.
3. Pay the fee right after submitting; download and print the confirmation page.
4. Mark the correction windows (27–28 Oct for RMS CET; 3–5 Nov for UP Sainik) now.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**
The late-fee application window closes on 25 October 2026 at 5 PM. Fee payment can be completed until 11:50 PM the same day.

**What is the last date for UP Sainik School admission 2027-28?**
Applications without a late fee close on 25 October 2026 at 5 PM. With a late fee of Rs 2,000, applications are accepted until 31 October 2026 at 5 PM.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 late fee?**
Candidates pay Rs 500 extra over the normal fee: Rs 1,500 total for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates, and Rs 1,250 for SC, ST and wards of personnel killed in action.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 correction window?**
The online correction window is scheduled for 27 to 28 October 2026, after the late application window closes.
