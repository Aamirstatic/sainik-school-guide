## Five posts, one tight deadline

Sainik School Chhingchhip, the Sainik Schools Society school in Mizoram's Serchhip district, is hiring for **5 support-staff posts**: 4 Ward Boys (male only) and 1 GE (Ayah, female only). The notification went up in the first week of October 2026, and the last date for receipt of applications is **23 October 2026** — seven days from today.

October has been the busiest hiring month I've seen across Sainik Schools this year: compare the [Sainik School Rewari recruitment (5 posts, apply by 6 November)](/news/sainik-school-rewari-recruitment-2026-5-posts-2026-10-15/), the [Sainik School Kunjpura recruitment (15 posts, apply by 31 October)](/news/sainik-school-kunjpura-recruitment-2026-16-days-left-2026-10-15/) and the [Sainik School Mainpuri recruitment (2 posts, apply by 25 October)](/news/sainik-school-mainpuri-recruitment-2026-10-days-left-2026-10-15/). **Age limit:** 18 to 50 years, calculated as on the last date for receipt of applications. 🔶 EXPECTED: age relaxation for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwBD) follows the usual government recruitment guidelines, as reported by one portal — the official notification will spell out the exact relaxations.

**Engagement and pay:** 🔶 EXPECTED — the posts are reported as contractual engagements on a consolidated pay of **₹28,800 per month** (the same figure for Ward Boys and the GE Ayah). If you need a permanent posting, verify the engagement type in the official notification before applying.

## Fee and how to apply

- **Application fee:** **₹500, non-refundable**, payable through a **crossed bank draft** in favour of the Principal, Sainik School Chhingchhip, or through an **online payment receipt**. The reported school account details are: Account No. **36367969391**, State Bank of India, Serchhip Branch, IFSC **SBIN0005826** — but verify these in the official notification before transferring anything.
- **Mode:** strictly **offline**. Download the prescribed application format from the school website's notice board (or collect it from the school), fill it completely, and attach self-attested copies of certificates and testimonials showing percentage of marks from matriculation onwards.
- **Send to:** The Principal, Sainik School Chhingchhip, Chhingchhip Village, District Serchhip, **Mizoram – 796161**.
- **Deadline:** applications must **reach** the school by **23 October 2026**. This is a receipt deadline, not a posting deadline. Speed post from outside Mizoram realistically takes 4–7 working days — so treat **today or tomorrow as your last day to post** if you are applying from another state.

## Selection process

🔶 EXPECTED: the reported selection process is a **written examination followed by an interview** for shortlisted candidates. The exact pattern, syllabus and interview weightage are only in the official notification — do not prepare off guesses.

## A word of caution before you pay

This is a legitimate-looking school recruitment, but fake "Sainik School job" scams are common: nobody from the school will call you asking for money on a personal phone number or a UPI ID. Pay the ₹500 fee **only** through the bank draft or the official account named in the school's own notification, and keep the receipt. If any detail above differs from the official notice on sschhingchhip.mizoram.gov.in, the official notice wins.
