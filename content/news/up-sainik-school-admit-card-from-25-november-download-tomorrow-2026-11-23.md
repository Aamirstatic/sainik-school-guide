## Tomorrow is the day to check the dashboard

If your child applied to the UP Sainik Schools — Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School in Lucknow or Sainik School in Gorakhpur — tomorrow, **25 November**, is the date to put in your diary. The schools' official prospectus says admit cards for the 2027-28 entrance exam will be generated **after 25 November 2026**, which means they start appearing on the portal from tomorrow onwards.

Do not wait for a message. The school does not send the admit card by post or email. It appears on your candidate dashboard at **upsainikschool.org**, and you download it by logging in with the **username and password created during registration**. Check tomorrow morning — and if it is not there yet, check again the next morning. "After 25 November" means the release rolls out from that date; the card is yours to fetch, not theirs to send.

Here is the thing most parents miss: this admit card has **two sections** — your child's copy and the school's office copy. The school copy must be handed to the invigilator during the exam. Print both, and do not leave the school copy at home on 20 December.

## The 5 checks to run the evening you download it

Our [detailed 5-checks guide](/news/up-sainik-school-admit-card-after-25-nov-5-checks-20-dec-exam-2026-11-14/) covers this in full. The short version, for tonight:

1. **Name, photograph and date of birth** — against the original documents. The prospectus is unusually specific here: the admit card must carry the **same recent photograph you uploaded while filling the form**.
2. **Exam centre and reporting time.** The exam is held at centres in **Agra, Ayodhya, Bareilly, Ghaziabad, Gorakhpur, Jhansi, Meerut, Prayagraj, Varanasi and Lucknow**. Know which one is yours before exam week.
3. **The school's office copy** — printed, in the same folder as your child's copy.
4. **The identity document.** Along with the admit card, your child must bring the **student identity card or bonafide certificate with photograph, attested by the head of the institution** of the school they currently attend.
5. **Login credentials.** If the login fails, check the password you set during registration first — lost credentials are your responsibility. The helpline is **7052777795, 9 AM to 2 PM on working days**, and it will ask for the application ID, registered mobile number and the child's name.

One deadline has already passed and cannot be recovered: the correction window ran from **3 to 5 November 2026** and is now closed. What the admit card says now is, for most fields, final — which is exactly why the checks above matter.

## 25 days to the exam — and the road after it

The exam itself is on **Sunday, 20 December 2026** — 25 days from tomorrow. It is a single OMR-based objective paper: **200 questions for 200 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes**. Mathematics carries 75 of those marks and English 50, with Intelligence and General Knowledge making up the rest. Lucknow admits boys only in Class 6 and boys and girls in Class 9; Gorakhpur admits boys and girls in both classes.

The written test is only the first gate. The prospectus schedule after it runs like this:

|---|---|---|

Applications for this cycle closed on **31 October 2026**, so this piece is for the families whose child is already registered — and for Class 5 and Class 8 parents watching this cycle to plan the next one. If your child is also in the AISSEE 2027 race, remember the national form closes on **2 December** — our [late-fee explainer from this evening](/news/aissee-2027-9-days-left-late-fee-window-not-extension-2026-11-23/) explains why 2 December, not the 3–6 December late window, is the real deadline.

## FAQs

**When will the UP Sainik School admit card be available?**

Admit cards will be generated after 25 November 2026, according to the schools' official Instructions and Guidelines for 2027-28. Download it from the candidate dashboard at upsainikschool.org using the username and password created during registration.

**When is the UP Sainik School entrance exam?**

Sunday, 20 December 2026. It is a single OMR-based paper of 200 questions for 200 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes, for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 at Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow and Sainik School, Gorakhpur.

**What should I check on the admit card the day I download it?**

Check your child's name, photograph, date of birth, exam centre and reporting time against the original documents. The admit card has two sections — your child's copy and the school's office copy, which must be handed to the invigilator during the exam. Print both.

**What happens after the UP Sainik School exam on 20 December?**

The tentative answer key is due on 24 December 2026 and the written result on 30 December 2026. Shortlisted candidates download their interview letter on 7 January 2027, and the medical examination and interview run from 1 to 28 February 2027, with the final result expected between 15 and 20 March 2027. These are tentative dates from the prospectus schedule.
