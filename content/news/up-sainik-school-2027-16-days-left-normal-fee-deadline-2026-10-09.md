## The fee doubles in 16 days

If your child is applying to the Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School in Lucknow or the UP Sainik School in Gorakhpur for the 2027-28 session, this is the deadline that matters most right now. The normal-fee application window closes on **25 October 2026 at 5 PM** — that is 16 days from today, a Sunday.

Miss that, and you don't lose your chance entirely — but you pay double. The late-fee window runs from **26 to 31 October 2026 (till 5 PM)**, and the application fee jumps from **Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,000**. There is no partial penalty here; one day late costs the same as six days late. Applying this weekend instead of next week is the simplest Rs 1,000 saving in this whole admission cycle.

Two things deserve attention. First, the correction window is only three days — 3 to 5 November — so re-check name spellings and birth dates before submitting, because it is a short safety net. Second, the entrance exam is just 72 days away, on 20 December. For families doing both this exam and AISSEE 2027 (expected January), the two tests will now land within weeks of each other.

## Who can apply — the UP-only rule

This is where many parents slip up. The UP Sainik School exam is **not** the AISSEE, and it is not open to everyone. Both the candidate **and** the father must hold a valid Uttar Pradesh domicile certificate — if the father's domicile is from another state, the child is ineligible regardless of where the family currently lives.

**Age limits (both dates inclusive):**
- **Class 6:** born between 2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018
- **Class 9:** born between 2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015

Note these differ from the AISSEE age bands — do not apply one exam's rule to the other. On the girls question: Lucknow's Class 6 takes boys only; Gorakhpur takes both boys and girls for Class 6; and both schools take boys and girls for Class 9.

## The exam itself (**EXPECTED** pattern)

The entrance test is a **2.5-hour offline OMR exam carrying 200 marks**, with four sections — Mathematics, English, General Knowledge, and Intelligence. The Class 6 paper is set at the Class 5 syllabus level and the Class 9 paper at the Class 8 level, so your child is tested on what they are already studying in school. Sample papers for both classes are available on the school's admission page — worth downloading this week rather than in December.

After the written test, roughly three times the number of available seats are shortlisted on merit for the medical exam and interview, which is held at the Lucknow campus. One serious warning from the prospectus: any attempt to influence the selection process — "source" or recommendation — leads to immediate rejection. The schools are explicit about this.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for UP Sainik School 2027 without a late fee?**
25 October 2026, till 5 PM. Applications submitted after that are accepted only under the late-fee window, which runs from 26 to 31 October 2026 till 5 PM.

**How much is the late fee for UP Sainik School 2027?**
The normal application fee is Rs 1,000. With the late fee added, applications from 26 to 31 October 2026 cost Rs 2,000 — double the normal amount.

**When is the UP Sainik School 2027 entrance exam?**
The entrance examination for the 2027-28 session is scheduled for 20 December 2026 (Sunday). It is a 2.5-hour offline OMR test carrying 200 marks.

**Who can apply for UP Sainik School admission 2027?**
Only UP domiciles — both the candidate and the father must hold a valid UP domicile certificate. Class 6 applicants must be born between 2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018; Class 9 applicants between 2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015.
