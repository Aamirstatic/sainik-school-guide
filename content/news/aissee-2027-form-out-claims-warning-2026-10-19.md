> **Last verified:** 19 October 2026 · **Status:** notification NOT released (CONFIRMED via fresh press check today) · Exam date: 31 January 2027 (CONFIRMED tentative, NTA calendar) · Fee: ₹850/₹700 (EXPECTED, 2026 rates)

If you have been Googling "AISSEE 2027 application form" this week, you may have seen pages claiming the form is already out. Here is the straight answer: **it is not.**

As of 18 October 2026, no AISSEE 2027 notice has been posted on the official NTA portal, exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. That check comes from a fresh update today by Aglasem's education desk, which also flags the problem plainly: "Some education websites have reported that the AISSEE 2027 form is already out. This could not be confirmed from NTA."

![Father and daughter checking a document together at a desk — parents should verify AISSEE 2027 claims against the official NTA portal](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-form-out-claims-warning-2026-10-19.webp)

## Why the confusion is spreading

The AISSEE 2027 information vacuum is the root cause. Last year, NTA opened the AISSEE 2026 form on 10 October 2025. Parents therefore expected the 2027 form around the same week this month — and some education portals rushed ahead with "form is out" style headlines before any bulletin existed.

The result is visible on the pages themselves. One widely-read portal currently claims its dates are "drawn from the official AISSEE 2027 notification released on October 13, 2026" — while on the very same page listing the notification release as still "Soon". Two claims, one page, opposite stories. That is exactly how rumours turn into panic calls to schools.

What is actually verifiable:

- **CONFIRMED:** No AISSEE 2027 information bulletin on the NTA portal as of 18–19 October 2026 (Aglasem check, today).
- **CONFIRMED (tentative):** Exam date 31 January 2027, from NTA's 2026-27 exam calendar published 16 September 2026.
- **EXPECTED:** Application window in October–November 2026, based on the last three cycles (2024: 7 Nov 2023, 2025: 24 Dec 2024, 2026: 10 Oct 2025).
- **EXPECTED:** Fee ₹850 for General/OBC/Defence wards, ₹700 for SC/ST — the 2026 rates, to be confirmed in the bulletin.

## How to verify the truth yourself (takes 2 minutes)

1. Open **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** — this is the only official portal.
2. Look for a link labelled "Information Bulletin" or "Application Form for AISSEE 2027". In the 2026 cycle, the bulletin PDF appeared here before the registration link activated.
3. If there is no bulletin, the form is not out — no matter what any coaching ad, WhatsApp forward, or education website says.
4. Bookmark the portal and check it once a day. We also publish an update the day NTA releases it.

## What to do next

Do not pay anyone who claims to "reserve a seat" or "fast-track" the AISSEE form — the application is online-only through NTA and needs no middleman. Instead, use the waiting time productively: keep the birth certificate, domicile certificate and category certificate ready, arrange one valid email ID and mobile number for registration, and check our [what to do while waiting for the AISSEE 2027 notification](/blog/what-to-do-while-waiting-aissee-2027-notification-2026-10-19/) checklist.

## FAQs

**Is the AISSEE 2027 application form out?**
No. As of 19 October 2026, NTA has not published any AISSEE 2027 notification on the official portal exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. Claims elsewhere could not be confirmed from NTA.

**How do I check the real AISSEE 2027 notification status myself?**
Open exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society and look for an official Information Bulletin PDF under the AISSEE section. If there is no bulletin there, the form is not out — no matter what other websites say.

**What is the expected AISSEE 2027 exam date?**
31 January 2027, marked tentative in NTA's 2026-27 exam calendar published on 16 September 2026. The date will be confirmed in the information bulletin when it releases.

**What should I do while waiting for the AISSEE 2027 form?**
Keep the birth certificate, domicile certificate and category certificate ready, arrange a valid email ID and mobile number, and continue preparation. Apply only on the official NTA portal once the bulletin is live.
