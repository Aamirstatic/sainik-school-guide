## Twelve days is plenty — if you start today

Here is the honest picture. The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. Today is Friday, 20 November. That is 12 days.

Twelve days is more than enough to fill one form. It is not enough to fix a rejected photo, a failed payment and a wrong date of birth all at once — which is exactly what happens to the families who wait until 2 December. Every year, the same story repeats: the portal slows down on the last evening, a payment hangs, and a parent discovers at 11 PM that the photograph they uploaded does not meet the specs.

So this piece is for one specific reader: the parent who has not started the form yet. Not the parent who submitted in the first week — you are done, and [our confirmation-page checklist]({{< relref "aissee-2027-form-submitted-5-things-to-check-confirmation-page-2026-11-17.md" >}}) is your next stop. This is for the parent who keeps meaning to do it. Start today, in this order, and it takes under an hour.

## Step 1: Register first, fill later

Go to the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**, and complete the registration with a working email ID and mobile number. You will get an application number and a password. Write them down on paper, not just in your head — you will need the same login for the admit card in January and the result later.

Do this step today even if you plan to fill the rest tomorrow. Registration takes five minutes, and it separates the "I have started" families from the "I will do it on the last day" families.

## Step 2: Keep the documents in one folder before you type a word

The form asks for your child's details, a photograph, a signature and supporting documents. Gather them before you open the form, not during it:

- A recent passport-size photograph of your child and their signature, in the size and format the bulletin specifies. Photo and signature rejections are the single most common reason forms get stuck — [our upload-mistakes guide]({{< relref "aissee-2027-15-days-left-5-upload-mistakes-that-reject-forms-2026-11-17.md" >}}) lists the five that actually happen.
- Date of birth proof that matches the school record exactly. Class 6 applicants must be born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017**; Class 9 applicants between **1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014**.
- Domicile and category certificates, if you are claiming the home-state quota or a reservation benefit. The home-state share is 67% of seats at the 33 established schools, so a missing domicile certificate is not a small detail.
- Your child's Aadhaar number and the school details (Class 5 for Class 6 applicants; Class 8 passed for Class 9 applicants).

One folder, everything inside, phone charged, stable internet. That is the whole preparation.

## Step 3: Fill the form, then stop and re-read it

Fill in the details slowly. The two fields parents get wrong most often are the date of birth and the school preference order — and both are expensive to fix later. The correction window (7 to 9 December) exists, but only some fields can be edited in it. Check the name spelling against the school record, check the date of birth digit by digit, and check the exam-city preferences before you move on.

## Step 4: Pay, then download the confirmation page the same minute

Pay the fee online — **Rs 850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, **Rs 700** for SC/ST — by card, net banking or UPI. The moment the payment succeeds, download the confirmation page and save it as a PDF. Do not close the tab first and "download it later". Later is when payments show as failed and parents panic. If a payment fails, [do not pay twice immediately]({{< relref "aissee-2027-fee-payment-failed-13-days-left-2026-11-19.md" >}}) — check the payment status first.

## The safety net, and why you should not plan to use it

If 2 December passes, there is a late window from **3 to 6 December** with an additional **Rs 500** late fee. After that, a correction window runs from **7 to 9 December**. Then the exam city is announced on **11 January 2027**, admit cards come on **25 January**, and the exam is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027** — pen-and-paper OMR, no negative marking.

Treat 2 December as the deadline and the late window as an emergency exit, not a plan. Our standing advice has not changed all month: treat **Monday, 30 November** as your real deadline, so a failed payment or a rejected photo still leaves two working days to fix it.

Twelve days. One hour of work. Start with registration today.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. The application window opened on 3 November 2026, and both the form and the fee payment close at 11:50 PM on 2 December.

**What happens if I miss the 2 December deadline for AISSEE 2027?**

You can still apply from 3 to 6 December 2026 by paying an additional late fee of Rs 500 over the regular fee. After 6 December, the window closes — there is no second extension announced.

**How much is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates. The fee is paid online by card, net banking or UPI.

**Can I correct my AISSEE 2027 form after submitting it?**

Yes, in the correction window from 7 to 9 December 2026. Only the fields NTA allows can be edited, so check the confirmation page carefully the day you submit — do not wait for December.
