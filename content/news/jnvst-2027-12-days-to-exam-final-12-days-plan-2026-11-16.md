## 12 days left — and the admit card is already in your hands

Twelve days. That is what stands between your child and the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam on **Saturday, 28 November 2026, 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**. If you have been following our countdown, you will know the admit card has been available since **28 October** — three weeks already. If you still have not downloaded and printed it, do it tonight, not this weekend. Everything after that is revision, and revision in the last 12 days is a different job from revision in the last two months. No new chapters. No new books. Just speed, accuracy, and calm.

The paper itself is short and sharp: **80 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours** — Mental Ability, Arithmetic and Language — with **no negative marking**. That last fact matters more than it sounds. In the final days, your child should practise attempting every question, because a guess costs nothing and a blank definitely scores nothing. Our [exam-day guide from yesterday](/news/jnvst-2027-13-days-to-exam-exam-day-guide-2026-11-15/) covers what to carry and the three mistakes that cost a seat; this piece is the day-by-day plan that gets your child to the hall ready.

## The 12-day calendar: 17 to 28 November

| Days | Dates | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 12–10 | Tue 17 – Thu 19 Nov | Two full 2-hour mock papers, one on Tuesday and one on Thursday, sat at 11:30 AM in exam conditions. Review every wrong answer the same evening and write the mistake in one notebook. |
| 9–7 | Fri 20 – Sun 22 Nov | Mental Ability speed drills — 40 questions in 40 minutes, every day. This is the section where slow children lose the most marks. No new topics. |
| 6–4 | Mon 23 – Wed 25 Nov | Arithmetic accuracy: one 20-question set a day, untimed, with every step written out. Then one final full mock on Wednesday. |
| 3–1 | Thu 26 – Fri 27 Nov | Light revision only — formula sheet, the error notebook, and one Language reading passage a day. Pack the exam-day bag on Thursday evening. |
| Exam day | Sat 28 Nov | Reach the centre by 10:30 AM. Seating closes at 11:00 AM. |

Honestly, the middle of that table is where most families go wrong. The temptation in the last week is to buy one more guidebook or start one more chapter. Don't. A child who has finished the syllabus needs repetition, not new material — and a child who has not finished it will not finish it in 12 days. What moves the score now is fewer silly mistakes, and silly mistakes only fall with timed practice and same-evening review.

## The last 72 hours: what actually changes

From Thursday 26 November, stop timed papers entirely. The last three days are for confidence, not for discovering new weaknesses. Read the error notebook once a day. Re-do the five arithmetic questions your child got wrong most often. Read one Language passage aloud, slowly, and answer its questions without rushing. And sleep — a 10- or 11-year-old who sleeps properly the two nights before the exam will outscore a tired child who revised until midnight, every single time.

One practical point: the exam is a morning paper, 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, and the child must be seated by 11:00 AM. If your child has been studying in the evenings, shift the last two mock papers to the morning slot so the brain is awake at the right hour. And visit the exam centre route once this week if you have not already — a wrong turn on 28 November morning is the one mistake no revision can fix.

## Admit card: check it tonight, not on the 27th

The admit card has been downloadable since 28 October from the NVS admission portal at **cbseitms.rcil.gov.in/nvs**, linked from **navodaya.gov.in**, using the registration number and the child's date of birth. Print two copies tonight — one for the bag, one kept at home. Check the seven details on it against our [7-details checklist](/news/jnvst-2027-admit-card-7-details-to-check-15-days-to-exam-2026-11-13/): name spelling, date of birth, exam centre, reporting time, photograph, signature and the instructions printed on the card. If anything is wrong, raise it through the NVS portal immediately — do not wait until exam week, when there is no time left to correct it.

## What to do this week

- **Print the admit card tonight** — two copies — and verify every detail against the registration form.
- **Sit two full mock papers this week** at 11:30 AM, in exam conditions, and review every error the same evening.
- **Drill Mental Ability daily** — 40 questions in 40 minutes — because speed, not knowledge, is what leaks marks there.
- **Stop new material after 25 November** — the last three days are for the error notebook, light revision and sleep.

## FAQs

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam?**

Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM. Children must be seated by 11:00 AM — latecomers are not permitted.

**How do I download the JNVST 2027 admit card?**

Download it free from the NVS admission portal (cbseitms.rcil.gov.in/nvs), linked from navodaya.gov.in, using your child's registration number and date of birth. The admit card has been available since 28 October 2026.

**What is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 paper pattern?**

80 questions for 100 marks in 2 hours — Mental Ability, Arithmetic and Language sections — with no negative marking. Divyang candidates get 40 minutes extra.

**When will the JNVST 2027 result come?**

The result is expected by the end of March 2027, as stated in the NVS prospectus. NVS will announce the confirmed date on the same portal.
