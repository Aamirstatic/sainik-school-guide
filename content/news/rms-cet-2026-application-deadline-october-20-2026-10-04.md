> **Last verified: 4 October 2026.** Every fact marked CONFIRMED was read today in NTA's official RMS CET 2026 information bulletin (via the government CDN hosting it) and cross-checked on duupdates.in and unilist.in, all reporting the same schedule.

If Rashtriya Military School is on your family's admission list this year, tonight's message is simple: **the regular application window closes on Tuesday, 20 October 2026, and you have 16 days left**. The form itself must be submitted by **5 PM** on that day, and the fee transaction must succeed by **11:50 PM** (CONFIRMED — NTA's official RMS CET 2026 bulletin).

Miss that, and the only way in is the late window from **21 to 25 October 2026**, which costs an **extra Rs. 500** on top of the regular fee (CONFIRMED — NTA bulletin). The exam itself is on **13 December 2026 (Sunday)** — a 2-hour-30-minute pen-and-paper Stage-1 written test for admission to **Class 6 and Class 9** across the five Rashtriya Military Schools (CONFIRMED — NTA bulletin).

## Fees and eligibility

**Application fee:** Rs. 1,000 for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates; Rs. 750 for SC, ST and wards of personnel Killed in Action (CONFIRMED). Payable online only — credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI.

**Eligibility — Class 6:** must have passed Class 5 or be studying in Class 5 (must pass before admission); age 10 to 13 years as on 31 March 2027, i.e. born between **1 April 2014 and 31 March 2017** (CONFIRMED).

**Eligibility — Class 9:** must have passed Class 8 from a government or recognised school; age 13 to 16 years as on 31 March 2027, i.e. born between **1 April 2011 and 31 March 2014** (CONFIRMED).

Applications are online only at **exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet** (CONFIRMED). These details cannot be changed after the correction window — once it closes on 28 October, NTA accepts no further changes.

## Why this deadline matters more than the others

Families following the defence-school circuit are juggling three calendars right now. Our [morning countdown](/news/rms-cet-2026-70-days-to-december-13-exam-2026-10-04/) pegged 70 days to exam day; the more urgent number now is the 16 days to the application cutoff.

Practical advice from the pattern NTA itself warns about: **do not plan for the late window as a backup**. The extra Rs. 500 is the small cost; the real risk is server congestion in the final days and mistakes you can no longer fix after the correction window. Apply this week, not on 20 October. ## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**
The regular application window closes on 20 October 2026 — the form must be submitted by 5 PM and the fee paid by 11:50 PM the same day. A late window runs from 21 to 25 October 2026 with an additional late fee of Rs. 500.

**When will the RMS CET 2026 exam be held?**
The Stage-1 written examination for RMS CET 2026 is scheduled for 13 December 2026 (Sunday). It is a 2-hour-30-minute pen-and-paper test for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 in the five Rashtriya Military Schools — Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum and Bengaluru.

**What is the application fee for RMS CET 2026?**
General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates pay Rs. 1,000. SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel Killed in Action pay Rs. 750. The fee is payable online only, by credit/debit card, net banking or UPI.

**Is there a correction window for RMS CET 2026?**
Yes. NTA will open an application correction window on 27 and 28 October 2026 on the official portal for candidates who need to fix details in their submitted forms.
