Forty-four days. That's what stands between your child and the RIMC Dehradun entrance exam — one of the hardest school-level papers in the country, and the only doorway to Class 8 at the Rashtriya Indian Military College for the July 2027 term.

With applications, late fees and the correction window all behind you (those closed on 30 September, 7 October and 11 October respectively), nothing administrative remains except two documents: the city intimation slip and the admit card. Here's exactly when to expect each — and what to do with the 44 days you have left.

## The timeline that matters now

| Milestone | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Application deadline | 30 Sep 2026 | Closed |
| Late-fee window (₹500) | 1–7 Oct 2026 | Closed |
| Correction window | 9–11 Oct 2026 | Closed |
| 🔶 City intimation slip | ~21–24 Nov 2026 | Expected (12–15 days before exam) |
| 🔶 Admit card download | ~29 Nov–1 Dec 2026 | Expected (5–7 days before exam) |
| ✅ Stage-I written exam | 6 Dec 2026 | Confirmed — pen-and-paper, 39 cities |

A quick warning about those two expected dates: NTA announced day-counts, not dates. If the slip appears a day or two early or late, that's normal — check [exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee](https://exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee) regularly from the third week of November instead of refreshing the page hourly.

## What the city slip and admit card actually do

Parents often confuse the two. The **city intimation slip** only tells you which city your exam centre is in — useful for booking travel early, especially since the exam spans 39 cities and some families have to travel the night before. The **admit card** carries the centre name, address, reporting time and your roll number. You cannot enter the hall without a printed admit card; a phone screenshot will not do.

## Your 44-day checklist

1. **Fix the written paper first.** The Stage-I paper (English, Mathematics, General Knowledge) is 4 hours long. If your child hasn't attempted a full-length timed paper yet, this weekend is the deadline to start — every Sunday till the exam.
2. **English writing speed matters as much as knowledge.** RIMC's English paper rewards handwriting under pressure. One unseen passage + one essay, timed, every week.
3. **Keep the documents folder ready now.** Photograph printouts, birth certificate and domicile certificate copies — the same set you uploaded. Lost documents discovered on admit-card day are a panic you don't need.
4. **Plan travel when the city slip drops.** If the centre is in another city, book immediately in late November. December trains and buses fill fast.
5. **Viva voce comes after the written.** Only written-qualified candidates face Stage-II (viva voce, expected April 2027). We covered what that stage tests [here](/news/rimcee-2026-viva-voce-after-written-2026-10-18/) — don't spend October on interview prep; the written paper is the filter.

Look, RIMC takes a tiny batch each term. But the written paper is a known quantity — past papers, fixed pattern, no surprises if prepared. Forty-four focused days are worth more than four distracted months. Use them.

## FAQs

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 city intimation slip be released?**
NTA's official information bulletin says the slip comes 12 to 15 days before the exam. Counting back from 6 December 2026 gives roughly 21 to 24 November 2026 — our calculation from the bulletin rule, not a separate announcement.

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 admit card be released?**
Five to 7 days before the exam — so around 29 November to 1 December 2026. Download it from the NTA RIMCEE portal and take a printout; phone screenshots are not accepted at the gate.

**What is the RIMCEE 2026 exam date and mode?**
The Stage-I written examination is on 6 December 2026 in pen-and-paper mode, conducted across 39 cities for admission to Class 8 at RIMC (July 2027 term).

**Can I still apply for RIMCEE 2026?**
No. Regular registration closed 30 September 2026, the late-fee window ended 7 October, and the correction window closed 11 October. The next admission cycle will be announced next year.
