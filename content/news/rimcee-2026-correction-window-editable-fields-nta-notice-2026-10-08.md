The editable and non-editable field lists below are from that notice. The 6 December 2026 written-exam date and paper pattern are from earlier verified reporting.

## What changed overnight: the field list is now official

When we covered the correction window yesterday, the exact list of editable fields was not public yet — the portal notice had not been issued. That changed this morning. NTA issued its formal public notice on **7 October 2026**, and it spells out precisely which fields are locked and which can be edited. If your child applied for the Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination (RIMCEE) 2026 — admission to the July 2027 term at RIMC Dehradun — this is the authoritative list.

The correction facility will be available **9 to 11 October 2026, up to 11:50 PM**, only on the official RIMCEE website.

## What you CAN edit (per NTA's 7 October notice)

- **Any ONE of:** candidate's name, father's name, mother's name — only one of these three, not all
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Category
- Signature
- Class VII equivalent details
- Examination city selection (subject to NTA's conditions)
- Aadhaar details — only if you registered using an identity document other than Aadhaar

## What is LOCKED — the window will not change these

- Mobile number
- Email address
- Present or permanent address
- Emergency contact details
- Photograph

An honest word here: if any of these locked fields is wrong on your form, no correction can fix it. Do not waste the window hoping otherwise — and do not trust anyone who promises to "unlock" them for a fee.

## The three rules that matter most

1. **One-time facility.** NTA is explicit: after 11 October at 11:50 PM, no further correction chance will be given. Once you submit the corrected details, the form is frozen — no second round.
2. **A correction can change your fee.** If a correction (say, a category change) alters the examination fee applicable to you, the difference is payable online by credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI. Fee already paid is **not refunded**, even if the correction reduces the applicable fee.
3. **Portal only.** Corrections are accepted on the official website alone. No emails, no helpdesk visits, no agent can do it for you.

## Do this tonight — the window opens tomorrow

1. **Print your confirmation page** (if you have not already) and compare every field against the birth certificate and Class VII records, line by line.
2. **Decide on the name field now** — you may change only one of candidate/father/mother name. Check the spelling against the official documents; this is the correction most parents need.
3. **Keep your login ready** — application number, password or date of birth, and the registered mobile number for OTPs — before 9 October, not during.

## FAQs

**When does the RIMCEE 2026 correction window open and close?**

It opens on 9 October and closes on 11 October 2026 at 11:50 PM. NTA calls it a one-time facility, so no further correction chance will be given after the window closes.

**Which fields can I edit in the RIMCEE 2026 application form?**

You may change any one of candidate name, father's name or mother's name; plus date of birth, gender, category, signature, Class VII equivalent details, exam city selection, and Aadhaar details if you registered with a non-Aadhaar identity document.

**Which fields cannot be changed in the RIMCEE 2026 correction window?**

Mobile number, email address, present or permanent address, emergency contact details and photograph are locked and cannot be edited in the correction window.

**Is there a fee for correcting the RIMCEE 2026 application form?**

An additional fee applies only if your correction changes the examination fee applicable to you, payable by card, net banking or UPI. Fee already paid is not refunded even if the correction lowers the applicable fee.
