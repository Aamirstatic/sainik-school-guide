## How AISSEE registration works, step by step

The process below is the CONFIRMED AISSEE 2026 sequence, reported by the Times of India from the NTA bulletin. The 2027 flow is expected to be identical.

**Step 1 — Go to the right portal.** The application runs on `exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society` (CONFIRMED for 2026). Ignore lookalike sites; official links only appear on NTA's own domains.

**Step 2 — New registration.** Register with the child's name, email ID and mobile number. Keep both active — OTPs land here. The registered email and mobile typically **cannot be changed later**, so triple-check.

**Step 3 — Fill the form.** Personal, academic and exam-city details. Last year the exam ran across **190 cities** (CONFIRMED, TOI). Pick cities you can genuinely reach.

**Step 4 — Upload documents.** Scanned photo, signature and certificates in the specified format. This is where most forms die — blurry signatures and mismatched name spellings are the classic rejections. Our [correction-window guide](/blog/aissee-correction-window-what-you-can-cannot-edit/) explains what can and cannot be fixed afterwards.

**Step 5 — Pay the fee.** Debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI. Last year's fee was **₹850 for General/OBC-NCL/Defence/Ex-Servicemen and ₹700 for SC/ST** (CONFIRMED for 2026; EXPECTED to repeat). Save the payment receipt — no receipt, no proof of application.

**Step 6 — Submit and print.** Review the preview page once, submit, and download the confirmation page. Keep the application number safe; you will need it for admit-card downloads and corrections.

Do not wait for the bulletin. Keep scanned copies of the child's photo and signature in the right sizes, birth proof, category certificate if applicable, and defence-ward documents if applicable. ## FAQs

**Is the AISSEE 2027 application form out?**
Not yet. As of the portal check on 2 October 2026, NTA had not released the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin. Registration is expected to open in October 2026, following last year's pattern.

**Where do I apply for AISSEE 2027?**
On the NTA portal at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. The entire 2026 process ran on this portal, and the same is expected for 2027.

**What is the AISSEE application fee?**
For 2026 it was Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL, Defence personnel and Ex-Servicemen, and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates. The same fee is expected for 2027 until the new bulletin says otherwise.

**What documents do I need for the AISSEE form?**
Last year candidates needed a scanned photo, signature, and relevant certificates such as birth proof, category certificate and defence-ward documents where applicable. Keep digital copies ready now so you are not rushing when the portal opens.
