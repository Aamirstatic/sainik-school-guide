# RIMCEE 2026: After the 30 September Deadline — Correction Window, Admit Card & the 6 December Plan

> **🔄 Last verified: 28 September 2026** — Registration and late-window dates ✅ **CONFIRMED** across Careers360, Times of India/Education Times and Education Post. The correction window (9–11 Oct), city-intimation and admit-card timelines below are reported from the official NTA information bulletin via Education Post — treat them as the reported schedule and verify on `exams.nta.nic.in/rimcee/` when NTA activates each window.

**Forty-eight hours.** That's all that separates you from the close of NTA's registration window for the **RIMCEE 2026** — the entrance exam for Class 8 admission to RIMC Dehradun for the July 2027 term. But here's what most families miss: the deadline isn't the end of the story. It's the beginning of a very specific calendar — a late-fee window, a correction window, an admit card, and then the big day on 6 December.

If your child is appearing for both RIMC and the AISSEE, this timeline runs in parallel with the Sainik School calendar. Pin it up.

![Students writing in a pen-and-paper examination hall](/images/inbody/rimcee-2026-after-deadline-what-happens-next-2026-09-28.webp)

## What happens, step by step, after 30 September

| Step | Dates | What to do |
|------|-------|------------|
| Registration closes (no late fee) | **30 Sep 2026, 5 PM** ✅ CONFIRMED | Submit tonight if you haven't — NTA portals slow down on the last day |
| Late-fee window (₹500 extra) | **1–7 Oct 2026, 5 PM** ✅ CONFIRMED | Fallback only; don't plan for it |
| Correction window | **9–11 Oct 2026** (reported) | Fix name spelling, DOB, category, uploads — one pass, carefully |
| City intimation slip | ~12–15 days before exam (reported) | Check which of the 39 cities you're allotted |
| Admit card | ~5–7 days before exam (reported) | Download, print, read every instruction |
| Stage-I written exam | **6 Dec 2026 (Sunday)** ✅ CONFIRMED | Pen-and-paper, 4.5 hours |
| Stage-II viva voce | Tentatively April 2027 | Only for Stage-I qualifiers |

The exam itself has descriptive (written-answer) papers in **Mathematics and English** plus **MCQs in General Knowledge**, conducted across 39 cities in India.

## Why the correction window matters more than you think

NTA's correction facility opens just two days after the late window closes — 9 to 11 October. That's a 3-day window, and it is the *only* chance to fix mistakes. RIMC has brutally narrow eligibility (born between 2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015), and mismatches between the form and the birth certificate are the single most common reason forms get held up. When the correction window opens, check the name spelling, date of birth, category, photograph and uploaded certificates against the originals — once, slowly. Don't resubmit the form.

## What to do this week

1. **If the form isn't in, put it in tonight.** Late fees are avoidable costs; portal traffic on 30 September is not.
2. **Keep the confirmation page and fee receipt** — print two copies. You'll need the application number for corrections and the admit card.
3. **Start December prep now.** English descriptive answers and Maths working steps can't be crammed in November. The GK paper rewards daily reading.
4. **Watch the RIMCEE portal in early October** for the correction-window link rather than news blogs — NTA activates it silently.

For Sainik School families doing both exams: RIMCEE is a separate exam from AISSEE (31 January 2027, per NTA's calendar). The calendars don't clash — which is exactly why doing both is smart, not overloaded.

## FAQs

**When does RIMCEE 2026 registration close?**
30 September 2026, till 5 PM, without late fee. A late-fee window (₹500 extra) runs 1–7 October 2026, also till 5 PM.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 correction window?**
NTA will open a correction facility from 9 to 11 October 2026, so you can fix errors in the submitted form. Use it — a wrong date of birth or uploaded document can get the form rejected.

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 admit card be released?**
As per the official information bulletin as reported by Education Post, city intimation slips come about 12–15 days before the exam and admit cards about 5–7 days before, so expect the admit card around end-November 2026.

**What is the RIMCEE 2026 exam pattern?**
Stage-I is a pen-and-paper written exam on 6 December 2026 (4.5 hours) with descriptive papers in Mathematics and English plus MCQs in General Knowledge, held across 39 cities. Qualifiers advance to a viva voce tentatively in April 2027.
