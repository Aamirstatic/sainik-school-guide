Nineteen days. That is all that stands between your child and the closing of the AISSEE 2027 application form — **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM**. The exam is still 79 days away, on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**, so it is easy to treat the form as something for later. Don't — parents discover a wrong photograph or a failed payment on the last evening every year, when there is no time left to fix it.

The four checks that catch almost every rejection take about ten minutes each. Run them before you hit submit, not after.

## Check 1: Names and dates must match, letter for letter

Enter your child's name, father's name and mother's name exactly as they appear in the school records, and the date of birth exactly as on the birth certificate. This sounds obvious — until a form says "Kumar" while the birth certificate says "Kumarr". One extra letter can get a form rejected.

Class 6 candidates must be 10–12 on 31 March 2027 and studying in Class 5; Class 9 candidates 13–15 and studying in Class 8.

## Check 2: Photo, signature and thumb impression in the right size

This is the single most common reason forms bounce. The bulletin is specific:

- **Photograph:** JPG, **10 KB to 200 KB**. Use a recent passport-size photo — the same photo will appear on the admit card.
- **Signature and left-hand thumb impression:** JPG, **10 KB to 50 KB each** (right thumb if the left is not available).
- **Birth certificate:** PDF, **50 KB to 300 KB**.

Upload, then actually look at what you uploaded — zoom in and check it is sharp and the right way up. A blurred photo is a rejection waiting to happen.

## Check 3: Four exam cities, and the paper medium — both hard to undo

You choose **four exam cities in order of preference**. NTA tries to give you one of them, but it can allot another city. Put the city you can genuinely travel to first. [The city slip arrives on 11 January 2027](/news/aissee-2027-exam-city-slip-jan-11-admit-card-jan-25-what-to-check-2026-11-13/), and the admit card on 25 January.

Just as important: choose the **medium of the question paper** carefully. Class 6 offers English, Hindi and several regional languages; Class 9 is English only. The medium **cannot be changed after the form is submitted**.

## Check 4: Pay the fee — and prove it with the confirmation page

The fee is **₹850** for General, OBC (NCL), and wards of Defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and **₹700** for SC/ST candidates. Pay online by debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI. Two things parents get wrong here:

- **The form is not complete until the fee is paid and the confirmation page is generated.** Money deducted from your bank account alone is not proof of payment. Download and print the confirmation page.
- **The fee is not refundable, and a submitted application cannot be withdrawn.** Only **one application per candidate** is allowed — submit more than one form for the same class and all of them can be rejected.

Save your **application number and password** the moment they are generated — you will need them for the admit card, the answer key and the result. Use a mobile number and email that will stay active for three months: they **cannot be changed after payment**.

## If you miss 2 December, it costs you — literally

A late window runs from **3 to 6 December 2026** with an extra fee of **₹500**, and a correction window from **7 to 9 December 2026** lets you fix most details. [We explained last week whether the late-fee window is worth paying](/news/aissee-2027-late-fee-window-dec-3-6-worth-paying-2026-11-08/) — short version: it is a safety net, not a plan. The correction window cannot fix your mobile number, your email or your paper medium.

Do the four checks tonight — ten minutes now beats a rejected form in December.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. Applications opened on 3 November 2026. If you miss it, a late window runs from 3 to 6 December 2026 with an extra fee of Rs. 500, and the correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026.

**What photo and signature sizes does the AISSEE 2027 form need?**

The passport-size photograph must be a JPG of 10 KB to 200 KB. The candidate's signature and left-hand thumb impression must each be a JPG of 10 KB to 50 KB. Blurred or wrongly sized uploads are a common reason forms get rejected.

**Can I correct mistakes in my AISSEE 2027 form after submitting?**

Most details can be corrected in the correction window from 7 to 9 December 2026. But some things cannot be changed — your mobile number and email cannot be changed after payment, and the medium of the question paper cannot be changed after submission. Get those right the first time.

**How do I know my AISSEE 2027 application is complete?**

Your form is complete only when the fee is paid and the confirmation page is generated. Money leaving your bank account is not proof on its own. Download and print the confirmation page, and keep your application number and password safe — you will need them for the admit card, answer key and result.
