Now the exam owns the calendar. Here's the official road map from today to the viva voce, straight from the information bulletin — so you know exactly what's coming and when.

## What just ended

The correction window ran from **9 to 11 October 2026**, a one-time facility on the website only. NTA was clear: no further correction opportunity will be given. If you corrected in time, your form now reflects the fixes. If something small is still wrong, don't panic — correction fields were limited anyway, and minor errors in non-critical fields don't decide selection. Just make sure the essentials (name, date of birth, category, photograph) are right on the confirmation page you saved.

Save that confirmation page somewhere you won't lose it. Screenshot it, email it to yourself, print one copy. It's your proof of registration and your reference for every stage that follows.

The exam is **55 days** from today. That number should shape every study decision from here on.

## What the 55 days should look like

**This week:** the rule that matters most is the qualifying bar — **at least 50% in each of the three papers** (English, Mathematics, General Knowledge) to reach the viva. A brilliant maths score cannot rescue a failed English paper. So the revision plan has to feed all three papers every week, not just the favourite one.

**Mid-November:** watch for the city intimation slip (12–15 days before the exam) — it tells you the exam city, so plan travel if needed. The admit card follows 5–7 days before the exam. Download it immediately, check the photo and the centre address, and keep two printed copies.

## After the written exam

The result comes **by 20 February 2027**. Those who qualify move to the **Stage-II viva voce**, tentatively in **April 2027** — roughly four to six weeks after the result. The viva is the personality-and-confidence stage: clear speech, general awareness, honest answers. You can't cram for it in a week, which is exactly why general knowledge should be a daily habit right now, not a December panic.

## FAQs

**Has the RIMCEE 2026 correction window closed?**

Yes. The one-time correction window ran from 9 to 11 October 2026 and closed at 11:50 PM on 11 October. No further correction opportunity will be given.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 written exam?**

The Stage-I written examination is on 6 December 2026, in pen-and-paper mode at 39 cities across India, per the official information bulletin.

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 admit card be released?**

Admit cards will be available 5 to 7 days before the exam — expect them around the end of November 2026. The city intimation slip comes 12 to 15 days before the exam.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 result declared?**

The result is expected by 20 February 2027, and the Stage-II viva voce is tentatively scheduled for April 2027, four to six weeks after the result.
