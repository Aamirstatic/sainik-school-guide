## The Full Timeline, From the Official Portal

Most parents tracking UP Sainik School admission 2027-28 have been working off second-hand schedules — trackers, coaching posts, forwards. The school's own admission portal has now published a clear milestone list for the session, and the first hard deadline is closer than many families realise: **18 days away**.

Here is the timeline as the portal lists it:

| Milestone | Date (✅ official portal) |
|---|---|
| Application form opens | 10 September 2026 |
| Last date without late fee | **25 October 2026 (till 5:00 PM)** |
| Last date with late fee (Rs 2,000) | 31 October 2026 (till 5:00 PM) |
| Correction window | **3–5 November 2026 (till 5:00 PM)** |
| Entrance exam | 20 December 2026 (Sunday) — tentative |
| Admit card | ❌ Not yet published |

The normal application fee is ✅ **Rs 1,000**; miss the 25 October deadline and it becomes ✅ **Rs 2,000** total (Buddy4Study, cross-checked with the portal's late-fee notice). Paying double for a form you could have submitted today is the easiest saving on this list — treat 25 October, not 31 October, as your real deadline.

## Two Things the Portal Confirms That Most Parents Miss

**First: the correction window exists — and it is short.** The portal gives exactly three days, 3–5 November, to fix form errors. Compare that with the 20 December exam date: NTA and other boards often allow longer windows. Three days is enough if you prepare, and nowhere near enough if you discover the error on day three. Save a print of your submitted form on the day you apply, and keep birth certificates, the domicile documents and a recent photograph within reach for the first week of November.

**Second: the admit card date is genuinely not out.** The portal's "What's New" section and the admission page carry the opening date, deadlines, correction window and exam date — but nothing on admit cards. ❌ **Do not** trust forwarded messages claiming a specific admit card release date; as of 7 October 2026, no such date has been officially published.

## What to Do This Week

If your child is applying for Class 6 or Class 9 at Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School (Lucknow) or UP Sainik School (Gorakhpur), the portal's own dates give you a short, fixed to-do list:

1. **File before 25 October** at upsainikschool.org — the entrance exam link for session 2027-28 is under the admission section.
2. **Verify both domiciles now.** This is the UP-specific rule that rejects the most applications: the candidate *and* the father must both hold valid UP domicile certificates. Sorting this out after the correction window is a gamble.
3. **Print and store your confirmation page** the day you apply — it is what you compare against during the 3–5 November correction window.
4. **Aim at 20 December.** The portal marks the date tentative, but planning backwards from it gives you ten weeks of prep time from today.

Note that this timeline is **separate from the NTA's AISSEE 2027 schedule** — UP's two state-run schools run their own exam, while Sainik Schools in Mainpuri, Jhansi and Amethi admit through NTA's all-India test, whose notification is still awaited.

## FAQs

**What is the last date for UP Sainik School admission 2027-28 without a late fee?**
The last date without a late fee is 25 October 2026, till 5:00 PM, as per the official UP Sainik School admission portal. Applications submitted by this deadline pay the normal fee of Rs 1,000.

**What is the last date with a late fee for UP Sainik School 2027-28?**
31 October 2026, till 5:00 PM. A late fee of Rs 2,000 applies in total, so filing after 25 October costs an extra Rs 1,000.

**When is the UP Sainik School 2027 entrance exam?**
20 December 2026 (Sunday). The official portal marks this date as tentative, so recheck it closer to November.

**Is the UP Sainik School 2027 admit card date announced?**
No. As of 7 October 2026, neither the admission portal nor the school homepage lists an admit card date. It is expected to be announced after the correction window closes in November.
