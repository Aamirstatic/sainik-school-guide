# All India Sainik Schools National Games 2026 Conclude: What the Ghorakhal Finale Shows AISSEE Aspirants

> **🔄 Last verified: 28 September 2026** — Group A results at Nagrota ✅ **CONFIRMED** (JammuBeat reporting Greater Kashmir; Daily Excelsior; The Tribune). Group B dates and disciplines ✅ **CONFIRMED** (SSBCrack; official announcement on Sainik School Ghorakhal's website). The Group B overall trophy outcome below is 🔶 **EXPECTED/REPORTED** — early reports from the closing ceremony suggest host Ghorakhal kept the trophy, but the school's official results sheet has not been published yet.

**Six days, five disciplines, and a closing ceremony on 26 September.** The All India Sainik Schools National Games (AISSNG) 2026 Group B — hosted at **Sainik School Ghorakhal** in Uttarakhand from 21 to 26 September — has concluded, closing out this year's national games season for the Sainik Schools Society.

This matters to you even if your child is sitting the AISSEE in January. Because these games are the clearest window parents ever get into what Sainik Schools *actually* train — and it's wider than any syllabus chapter.

![Cadets at a Sainik School morning assembly](/images/inbody/aissng-2026-national-games-conclude-ghorakhal-2026-09-28.webp)

## What happened: two meets, two hosts, two champions

**Group A** ran at Sainik School Nagrota in J&K and closed on **5 September 2026**. Host Nagrota lifted the overall championship — winning junior boys' basketball, junior boys' volleyball, junior girls' basketball and the English debate — with Royal International Residential School (RIRS), Fatehabad as runners-up and Sainik School Kunjpura third. Eight contingents competed: Nagrota, Kapurthala, Kunjpura, Sujanpur Tira, RIRS Fatehabad, Kesari Devi Lohia Jagram Public School Kurukshetra, Woods Park Hamirpur and Dayanand Public School Nabha.

The games were scored across **basketball, volleyball, athletics, debate and quiz** — in boys' and girls' sections and in two age bands, sub-junior (Classes VI–VIII) and junior (Classes IX–XI). The Tribune reported that Woods Park Sainik School, Bani brought home five medals from the Nagrota meet, including a silver in the 4×100m relay and a bronze in the girls' 100m sprint — a reminder that girl cadets compete on exactly the same stage now.

**Group B** then took over at Ghorakhal, 21–26 September 2026, with the same five disciplines. Early reports from the ground suggest host Ghorakhal kept the overall trophy at home, but 🔶 treat that as unconfirmed until the school publishes its results.

## Why this matters to an AISSEE 2027 family

Three things worth absorbing:

1. **The exam is the door, not the house.** AISSEE (tentatively **31 January 2027** ✅ CONFIRMED, NTA calendar) tests Maths, language, intelligence and GK — but once inside, your child competes in athletics, basketball, debate and quiz *at national level*. Schools like Nagrota pour serious resources into this: it inaugurated new synthetic courts just a month before hosting Group A.

2. **Fitness is part of selection, not an extra.** AISSEE's medical round after e-counselling screens physical fitness — cadets who spend their prep years sedentary can clear the written test and stumble at the very next gate. A child who plays an hour a day isn't "distracted"; they're preparing for the school, not just the paper.

3. **Debate and quiz count towards championships.** This is the point parents miss. The National Games score the debating table alongside the basketball court. Reading habits, general awareness and the ability to speak clearly are being scored nationally — which is exactly what the GK and language sections of AISSEE reward anyway.

## What to do next

- **Protect one hour of play daily in the prep schedule.** Physical activity improves concentration for study hours — it is not competing with them. (See the [perfect daily timetable for AISSEE Class 6](/blog/aissee-daily-timetable-class-6/) for how to fit it in.)
- **Add debate-style reading.** A daily newspaper habit builds both GK for the paper and the quiz/debate culture the school rewards later.
- **If you missed the games season:** the next cycle of inter-school and inter-group tournaments — football, pre-Subroto fixtures — runs through the academic year. ## FAQs

**What is the All India Sainik Schools National Games (AISSNG)?**

It is the annual national sports meet of the Sainik Schools Society, where cadets compete in basketball, volleyball, athletics, debate and quiz. Schools compete in groups — the 2026 edition had Group A at Nagrota and Group B at Ghorakhal.

**Who won the AISSNG 2026 Group A title?**

Host Sainik School Nagrota won the overall championship when the Group A meet closed on 5 September 2026, with Royal International Residential School Fatehabad as runners-up and Sainik School Kunjpura third.

**Where and when was the AISSNG 2026 Group B meet held?**

At Sainik School Ghorakhal in Uttarakhand, from 21 to 26 September 2026, with competitions in basketball, volleyball, athletics, debate and quiz.

**Do Sainik Schools really score debate and quiz alongside sports?**

Yes. Both the 2026 Group A and Group B meets included debate and quiz as championship disciplines alongside basketball, volleyball and athletics — they count towards the overall title.
