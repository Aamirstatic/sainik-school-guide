## 20 days — and the form deserves an evening, not a rush

The AISSEE 2027 application window has been open for nine days now, since **3 November 2026**. It closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM** — that is 20 days from today. The exam itself is **Sunday, 31 January 2027**, which is 80 days away.

Here's the thing: the parents who run into trouble are rarely the ones who apply late. They are the ones who apply in a hurry on the last evening, with a photo that gets rejected or a name that doesn't match the birth certificate. Give the form one careful evening.

## The five checks

**1. Name and date of birth, exactly as on the records.** The name on the form must match your child's birth certificate and school records, letter for letter — including the spelling of the surname and any middle name. A mismatch here is the single most common reason parents end up fighting the correction window later. Read our guide on [what to do about a name spelling mistake](/blog/aissee-2027-name-spelling-mistake-what-to-do/) before you type a single letter.

**2. Photo, signature and thumb impression in the prescribed format.** The application asks you to upload a scanned photograph, signature and thumb impression, along with the other prescribed documents. Upload them in the format the portal specifies — a wrong-sized or blurry scan is one of the few things that can get a form flagged after submission.

**3. Category and domicile certificates, ready before you start.** If your child claims SC, ST, OBC-NCL or a defence-ward quota, the applicable category certificate must be submitted with the form — and the domicile certificate decides the home-state quota in counselling. Keep the full list from our [AISSEE 2027 documents checklist](/news/aissee-2027-application-documents-checklist-2026-10-06/) open beside you while you fill the form, and don't start until every document on it is in hand.

**4. School preferences and exam centres, chosen with care.** The form asks you to indicate your preferred Sainik Schools or New Sainik Schools and your examination centres. This is not a throwaway field — preferences shape the counselling allotment later. Talk it through as a family before you click, and pick exam centres your child can actually reach on a January afternoon without a heroic journey.

**5. Fee paid, confirmation page downloaded and saved.** The fee is **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence personnel wards, and **₹700** for SC/ST candidates, payable online by card, net banking or UPI. After payment, download and print the confirmation page and the fee receipt, and keep them safe — you will need them for every later stage, including the admit card and counselling. A payment made is not the same as a form submitted: check that the confirmation page actually exists before you close the tab.

## What if you miss 2 December?

There is a safety net, but it costs you. Applications can still be submitted from **3 to 6 December 2026** with an additional late fee of **₹500** on top of the regular fee. After 6 December, the window is shut. Then comes the correction window, **7 to 9 December 2026** — your only chance to fix mistakes in the submitted form. The exam city will be announced on **11 January 2027** and admit cards will be released on **25 January 2027**.

Apply only through the official NTA portal. No school website, no agent, no WhatsApp link takes AISSEE applications. If anyone offers to fill the form for a fee, walk away — the NTA helpline (1800-180-4747, aissee@nta.ac.in) answers the questions agents charge for.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**
2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM, including the fee payment. Applications opened on 3 November 2026 on the official NTA portal.

**What happens if I miss the 2 December deadline for AISSEE 2027?**
You can still apply from 3 to 6 December 2026 by paying an additional late fee of Rs 500 over the regular fee. After 6 December there is no further window.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**
Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence personnel wards, and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates. The fee is paid online by card, net banking or UPI.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**
Sunday, 31 January 2027, in pen-and-paper OMR mode. Class 6 runs from 2:00 to 4:30 PM and Class 9 from 2:00 to 5:00 PM.
