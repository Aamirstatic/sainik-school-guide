## 7 posts, one deadline: 6 November

Sainik School Kodagu, at Kudige in Kushalnagar taluk of Karnataka, has opened recruitment for **7 posts** under advertisement number **07/OCT 2026**. The vacancies mix regular teaching posts with contractual support posts, and the window is short — the completed application must **reach the school by 6 November 2026**, just eight days from today.

This is the third Sainik School recruitment drive this month that we have covered — Chittorgarh (5 posts, last date 7 November) and Rewari (5 posts, last date 6 November) came earlier — but Kodagu is the one with the widest mix: four teaching posts, one nursing post and two support posts. If you are a trained teacher or a nursing professional looking for a residential-school posting, this is the one to check first.

## Who can apply

The bar differs sharply by post, so check yours before you spend money on a demand draft:

- **PGT Physics:** An integrated postgraduate course in Physics with at least 50% marks including B.Ed., or a Master's degree with at least 50% marks plus a B.Ed. from an NCTE-recognised institution, with proficiency in teaching through English medium. Age 21–40 years.
- **TGT English:** A four-year integrated degree course with at least 50% marks, or a Bachelor's/Honours degree with at least 50% marks plus B.Ed.; English as an elective or main subject in at least three years or six semesters, and competency to teach in English. Age 21–35 years.
- **TGT Hindi:** The same degree and B.Ed. pattern, with Hindi as a subject in all three years of the degree course. Age 21–35 years.
- **TGT Social Science:** A degree with at least 50% marks covering any two of History, Geography, Economics and Political Science, with Geography or History studied in all years of graduation, plus B.Ed. and English-medium competency. Age 21–35 years.
- **Nursing Sister:** A nursing diploma or degree; ex-servicemen of the Medical Assistant trade are also eligible. At least 5 years of experience is required. Age 18–50 years.
- **Ward Boy:** Matriculation or an equivalent examination from a recognised board. Age 18–50 years.

## How to apply — offline only, and the post matters

There is no online form. Download the prescribed application format from the school website, fill it carefully, attach self-attested copies of your photograph, signature, ID proof and certificates, and enclose the demand draft. Then send it by **Registered Post or Speed Post** to:

> The Principal, Sainik School Kodagu, Village & Post: Kudige, Taluk: Kushalnagar, Kodagu District, Karnataka – 571 232

Two traps to avoid. First, **applications delivered by hand are not accepted** — the notification says so explicitly, so do not plan a personal visit to drop the form. Second, post it early: the deadline is the date the form must *reach* the school, not the date you post it. With 6 November falling on a Friday, posting on 4 November is the sensible last safe day for most of India.

The fee is **Rs. 500 for General/OBC** and **Rs. 350 for SC/ST**, paid by demand draft drawn in favour of The Principal, Sainik School Kodagu.

## What happens after you apply

Selection involves a **written examination, followed by a skill test and interview, and a class demonstration or practical test** as applicable to the post. Dates for the test and interview are intimated to candidates, so keep the contact details on your form current and watch the school website after the deadline.

The official PDF on [sainikschoolkodagu.edu.in](https://www.sainikschoolkodagu.edu.in/) is the final authority on eligibility, reservation and the application format — if anything here differs from the PDF, the PDF wins.
