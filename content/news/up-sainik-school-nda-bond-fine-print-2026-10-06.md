Nineteen days. That is all that stands between UP families and the closing of the UP Sainik School 2027-28 application window — 25 October at 5 pm, with the normal ₹1,000 fee, or 31 October with a ₹2,000 late fee. Thousands of parents will fill the form this month. But before you do, there is a page in the prospectus most families only discover at admission — and it can shape the next seven years of your child's life.

It is the **agreement bond**.

## The fine print: an NDA clause

The official prospectus lists, among the documents required at admission, an **agreement bond on a ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper** (plus an indemnity bond and an affidavit). What the bond actually says is the part that matters: according to Buddy4Study's review of the 2027-28 prospectus, every cadet's parent or guardian must sign an agreement stating that **if the student does not appear for the NDA entrance exam conducted by UPSC, or clears it and does not join the NDA, the parents must refund the entire amount the school spent on the student**. The same applies if the student leaves the school midway.

Why does the school ask this? It is not subtle about its purpose. The UP Sainik Schools Society exists to prepare boys and girls for the Armed Forces — the state fully finances the schools precisely so that cadets become NDA officers. The bond is the mechanism that keeps that mission honest. It is not a punishment clause; it is the price of a heavily subsidised education.

This is the conversation every family needs to have **before** applying, not after the merit list: is your child genuinely inclined toward a defence career? If they are dreaming of medicine, engineering, or civil services instead, this school — and this bond — will fight them every step of the way. The admission form is ₹1,000. The wrong decision costs far more.

## Three more things that disqualify families every year

**1. The father-child domicile rule.** Both the applicant *and* the father must hold a valid Uttar Pradesh domicile certificate. If the father's domicile is from another state, the child is ineligible — regardless of where the family currently lives. This single rule quietly kills more applications than any other.

**2. The school-and-class gender grid.** At Captain Manoj Kumar Pandey UP Sainik School, Lucknow, Class 6 entry is **boys only** (boys and girls for Class 9). UP Sainik School, Gorakhpur admits boys and girls in both classes. If you are applying for your daughter in Class 6, Gorakhpur is the only option of the two — choose the wrong combination and the form is wasted.

**3. The travel cost is yours.** Shortlisted candidates travel to the Lucknow campus for medical and interview — roughly three times the number of vacancies get called — and the family bears all travel and accommodation costs. No TA/DA, no allowance. And one strict warning from the prospectus: if anyone tries to approach officials for "recommendation" on behalf of the child, the candidature is rejected immediately.

## FAQs

**What is the UP Sainik School NDA agreement bond?**
At admission, parents must sign an agreement bond (on a ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper, per the official prospectus) with an NDA clause: if the student does not appear for the UPSC NDA exam, or clears it but does not join NDA, the parents must refund the full cost of the child's schooling. The same applies if the student leaves midway. Always verify the exact wording in the current prospectus at upsainikschool.org.

**What is the last date for the UP Sainik School 2027-28 form?**
25 October 2026 (till 5 pm) with the normal ₹1,000 fee; 26 to 31 October 2026 (till 5 pm) with a ₹2,000 late fee. There is no offline form — apply only at upsainikschool.org.

**Who is eligible for UP Sainik School admission?**
Only UP domiciles: both the applicant and the father must hold a valid Uttar Pradesh domicile certificate. Class 6 needs DOB between 2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018; Class 9 needs DOB between 2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015. Lucknow's Class 6 entry is boys only; Gorakhpur takes boys and girls in both classes.

**When is the UP Sainik School 2027 entrance exam?**
20 December 2026 (Sunday) — a single 200-mark OMR paper of 2 hours 30 minutes covering Mathematics, English, General Knowledge and Intelligence, with no negative marking.
