## Nine days left — and the safety net has a price tag

The AISSEE 2027 application form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM**. Nine days from today. If your child is applying for Class 6 or Class 9 in a Sainik School, that is the date that matters — and if you are reading this in the evening, after a day of work and school, this is the conversation to have at home tonight: submit the form this week, not on the last night.

Here is the thing most parents get wrong about the deadline. Yes, there is a window after 2 December. From **3 to 6 December**, NTA will still accept forms — but only with a **late fee of Rs 500** on top of the regular fee. That window is new this year, and it is easy to read it as an extension. It is not one.

## What waiting actually costs

| Route | General / OBC-NCL / Defence wards | SC / ST |
|---|---|---|
| On time (by 2 Dec, 11:50 PM) | **Rs 850** | **Rs 700** |
| Late window (3–6 Dec, 11:50 PM) | **Rs 1,350** (850 + 500) | **Rs 1,200** (700 + 500) |
| After 6 December | No form at all | No form at all |

The late fee has to be paid by **6 December, 11:50 PM**, the same as the form itself. A form submitted in the late window without the extra Rs 500 is not a submitted form.

And after 6 December, the door closes. Last year there was no late window at all — when the 2026 deadline passed, NTA extended it instead. This year the late window *is* the safety net, built into the schedule from the start. **No further extension has been announced.** Anyone telling you the dates will move again is guessing, and a guess is a bad thing to bet your child's exam year on.

## The correction window is a repair shop, not a second deadline

After the late window comes the **correction window, 7 to 9 December** — one time only. It exists so you can fix a mistake on a form you have already submitted: a spelling, a photo, a school choice. It is not a second chance to apply, and it cannot create a certificate that does not exist yet.

Two honest limits, both worth knowing before you submit:

- Your **registered email ID and mobile number** — the fields that identify your application — typically stay locked in the correction window, based on how NTA has run these windows in past cycles. Get them right the first time; everything else flows from them, including the admit card and the result.
- The window is **one-time-only**. Once it closes on 9 December, the form is frozen until the exam-city announcement on 11 January 2027 and the admit card on 25 January.

Our [correction-window guide](/blog/aissee-correction-window-what-you-can-cannot-edit/) explains what the window can and cannot fix in detail — but the short version is this: the less you need it, the better your form is.

## What to do tonight

This is a short list, and it fits in one evening:

1. **Submit the form and pay the fee by 2 December, 11:50 PM.** Do not aim for the last night — the portal slows down, payment gateways fail, and a failed payment at 11:40 PM is a panic no family needs. Our [fee-payment guide](/news/aissee-2027-16-days-left-fee-payment-rules-confirmed-2026-11-16/) explains the one rule that matters: the form is complete only when the fee is paid **and the confirmation page is generated**. Download that page as a PDF and keep it safe.
2. **Check the three certificates first.** Domicile, caste and birth-certificate fixes need a government office visit and working days — our [certificates piece from this morning](/news/aissee-2027-9-days-left-certificates-need-office-visit-2026-11-23/) explains why this is the last full working week to get them.
3. **If you genuinely cannot submit by 2 December**, use the late window on 3 or 4 December — not on the 6th. The extra Rs 500 is annoying; losing the whole year is not comparable.
4. **After 9 December, stop worrying about the form.** The next dates are the exam-city announcement on **11 January 2027**, the admit card on **25 January**, and the exam itself on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**.

The form is the easy part of AISSEE 2027. The hard part is the exam. Do not let the easy part become the reason the hard part never happens.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. The fee must also be paid by then. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December 2026 with an extra fee of Rs 500.

**How much is the AISSEE 2027 late fee?**

Rs 500 extra, on top of the regular fee. That means Rs 1,350 in total for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards (Rs 850 plus Rs 500), and Rs 1,200 for SC and ST candidates (Rs 700 plus Rs 500). The late fee must be paid by 6 December 2026, 11:50 PM.

**What happens if I miss the late-fee window on 6 December?**

There is no further window. No extension has been announced, and the form cannot be submitted after 6 December 2026. The next chance is the AISSEE 2028 cycle — so treat 2 December as the real deadline and the late window as an emergency exit, not a plan.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 correction window, and what can it fix?**

The correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026 and is one-time-only. It lets you fix details on a submitted form, but your registered email ID and mobile number — the fields that identify your application — typically stay locked, based on how NTA has run correction windows in past cycles.
