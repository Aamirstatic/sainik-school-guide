Everyone is talking about the 25 October form deadline — and rightly so, because missing it costs you an extra ₹1,000 or the seat entirely. But the application is only *step one* of a five-month selection journey. Your child won't set foot in the school until March — if everything goes right. Walk through the whole road with your child tonight. It makes the preparation real.

## The complete roadmap: 25 October 2026 → March 2027

| Stage | Dates | What happens |

## The interview and medical: what most parents underestimate

The written exam filters; the interview and medical *select*. Roughly four months after you submit the form, shortlisted children face a panel interview and a medical fitness examination through February 2027.

Two honest points. First, don't buy a coaching industry around it — the interview checks what the school is actually buying: confidence, clarity of thought, basic general awareness, and the temperament to live in a residential military-style school. A composed child who can explain why they want to join beats one drilled on 500 "expected questions."

Second, the medical is not a formality. Sainik Schools run a physically demanding routine — morning PT, drill, games — so it genuinely screens fitness. If your child has a known condition (eyesight, hearing, orthopaedic), get it checked now. That leaves time for correction or an honest conversation about expectations.

## Three things to do this week

**1. Save the login credentials somewhere permanent.** The registration username and password unlock the admit card, the interview letter — everything downstream. Screenshot it, print it, stick it on the fridge.

**2. Mark 3–5 November in red.** The correction window is three days. After it, a wrong date of birth or a bad photograph becomes a permanent problem.

**3. Start the 20 December syllabus now.** The written exam is eight weeks away. The admit-card machinery runs on its own — your child's preparation doesn't need to wait for it.

## One last note for AISSEE families too

UP Sainik School's process is separate from NTA's AISSEE — different form, exam and counselling — but the rhythm is the same: written exam in Dec/Jan, interviews in February, results by March. Track both without mixing up portals: upsainikschool.org vs exams.nta.nic.in.

## FAQs

**What happens after the UP Sainik School form deadline on 25 October 2026?**
The correction window runs 3–5 November 2026, admit cards come after 25 November, the written exam is on 20 December 2026, interview letters are downloadable from 7 January 2027, interviews and medicals run through February 2027, and the final result is expected 15–20 March 2027.

**Is there an interview and medical after the UP Sainik School written exam?**
Yes. Candidates who clear the 20 December written exam download interview letters from 7 January 2027, then attend the interview and medical examination between 1 and 28 February 2027, per the official schedule.

**When is the UP Sainik School 2027-28 final result expected?**
The tentative final result window is 15–20 March 2027, after the February interview and medical stage. Keep checking upsainikschool.org.

**When does the UP Sainik School admit card come out?**
Admit cards are available for download after 25 November 2026 from the official portal upsainikschool.org, using the registration login credentials.
