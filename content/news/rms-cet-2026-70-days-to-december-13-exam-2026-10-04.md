## What's happening

Today is 4 October 2026. The RMS CET 2026 — the entrance exam for the five Rashtriya Military Schools — is on **13 December 2026, a Sunday** — exactly **70 days** away. Ten weeks: enough to build real skill, short enough that every week counts.

Note the RMS exam comes **first**: AISSEE 2027 is on 31 January 2027 (CONFIRMED tentative, NTA exam calendar) — so the next 70 days ARE the RMS sprint.

## The 10-week plan

Not an official schedule — practical advice from years of watching entrance cycles.

| Phase | Weeks | The one priority |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| 1. Fix weaknesses | 1–3 | Weakest section first: one concept a day, 20 questions nightly. No full mocks yet. |
| 2. Build speed | 4–6 | Timed 30-question MCQ drills. Confirm negative marking from the bulletin first. |
| 3. Full papers | 7–9 | One full paper every Sunday morning, exam-hour conditions; review every wrong answer on Saturday. |
| 4. Taper | 10 | No new chapters. Revise formulas, re-solve the error log, sleep properly. |

A calm child on 13 December beats a crammed one.

## Why RMS CET matters for AISSEE families

Both exams test Maths, English and reasoning at the same level, so RMS prep now builds exactly the muscles AISSEE needs in January. Think of 13 December as a dress rehearsal graded by strangers — but don't let RMS eat January's AISSEE revision. On 13 December, shut the RMS books and switch to AISSEE-only prep.

## What to do this week

1. **Apply now.** The regular window closes 20 October at 5 PM — 16 days away; the late window costs Rs 500 extra. Only use exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet.

## FAQs

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**

13 December 2026, a Sunday — pen-and-paper MCQs across 63 cities, for Class 6 and Class 9 admission to the five Rashtriya Military Schools for 2027-28.

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**

20 October 2026 (5 PM; fee till 11:50 PM). Late window: 21–25 October with a Rs 500 late fee. Apply only at exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 application fee?**
