## Fourteen days — and the last week will not be calm

Here is the honest picture. The AISSEE 2027 form has been open since 3 November, and today, 18 November, exactly 14 days remain before it closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. That sounds comfortable — until the last three days, when the portal slows, payments fail, and parents refresh the page at 11:40 PM.

If the form is already submitted, this is a two-minute check. If not, treat 2 December as if it were 28 November, and finish it this week while the portal is fast.

The late window is real but not a safety net to plan around — it costs ₹500 extra and closes on 6 December with nothing after it. And the correction window does not fix everything: some fields cannot be changed even then. The cheapest correction is the one you never need.

## What the form costs — and the one fee trap

The application fee is ✅ **CONFIRMED**: **₹850** for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and **₹700** for SC/ST candidates. Payment is online only — card, net banking or UPI — and generally non-refundable. Some third-party websites list a lower fee (₹800/₹650); the NTA bulletin figure is ₹850/₹700, so trust the bulletin and the payment page, not the aggregator.

One warning matters more than the fee: fake "AISSEE help" pages and paid agents promise guaranteed seats or faster forms for a fee. NTA takes no offline applications and no agent can change a merit list. Our guide to the [scams targeting parents while forms are open](/blog/aissee-2027-scams-targeting-parents-forms-open-30-second-checks/) has a 30-second check to run on any link before you trust it.

## Five checks before you hit submit

1. **Date of birth vs the school certificate.** Class 6: born 1 April 2015–31 March 2017. Class 9: born 1 April 2012–31 March 2014. Age is counted as on 31 March 2027, with no relaxation for any category. A mismatch can mean disqualification at document verification.
2. **Photo, signature and thumb impression.** The bulletin specifies exact formats and sizes; wrong files are the most common reason forms get stuck. Read the specifications before scanning.
3. **Domicile and category certificates.** The 67% home-state quota runs on the domicile certificate; the SC/ST/OBC (NCL) shares run on the category certificate. Fix an expired or wrongly formatted certificate now, not in the correction window.
4. **School choices, in the right order.** Admission covers Class 6 and Class 9 in the 33 existing Sainik Schools, Class 6 in 80 approved New Sainik Schools, and Class 9 in 40 New Sainik Schools. The order matters later in counselling — see our [age-window guide](/news/aissee-2027-16-days-left-age-window-birthday-decides-class-2026-11-16/).
5. **The confirmation page.** After payment, download and save the confirmation page and the application number — you will need it for the admit card, the result, and e-counselling registration.

## After the form: what the next 74 days look like

Submitting the form is the easy part. The exam is on **31 January 2027** — 74 days from today — in pen-and-paper OMR mode, with no negative marking, so every question is worth attempting. The qualifying bar is 25% in each section and 40% in aggregate (not applicable to SC/ST candidates in existing Sainik Schools). Our [worked examples of the 25% rule](/blog/aissee-2027-qualifying-marks-25-percent-rule-worked-examples/) show exactly how many questions that means per section.

Watch for the exam-city intimation around 11 January and the admit card around 25 January — both on the same portal, [exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society), and nowhere else. No admit card comes by post.

## What to do tonight

If the form is not yet submitted, open the official portal tonight and submit with the documents from the checklist beside you. If it is submitted, check the name spelling and date of birth against the school certificate once, and file the application number somewhere you will find it in January. Fourteen days is enough — but only if the last three of them are not the plan.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM, at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. Do not wait for the last evening — the portal slows down badly near every NTA deadline.

**Can I still apply for AISSEE 2027 after 2 December?**

Yes, but it costs extra. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December 2026 with an additional late fee of Rs. 500. After 6 December, the form closes completely.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 correction window?**

7 to 9 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. Use it to fix spelling mistakes, a wrong date of birth, a wrong category or a wrong school choice. Some fields cannot be changed even in the correction window.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**

Sunday, 31 January 2027, from 2:00 PM — 150 minutes for Class 6 and 180 minutes for Class 9. The exam-city intimation is expected around 11 January 2027 and the admit card around 25 January 2027.
