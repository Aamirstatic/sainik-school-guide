## Eight days left — and then the clock keeps ticking

If you have not filled the AISSEE 2027 form yet, the headline is simple: **the window closes on 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**, and the fee has to be paid by the same time. Eight days. That is the part every parent knows.

Here is the part most parents miss: 2 December is not the end of the story. It is the first of six dates that run all the way to the exam on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. Knowing the full timeline now — instead of discovering each step the night before — is the difference between a calm application and a panicked one.

## The full AISSEE 2027 timeline after 2 December

| Date | What happens | What you should do |
|---|---|---|
| **2 December 2026, 11:50 PM** | Regular application window closes; fee payment deadline | Submit the form and pay the fee — Rs 850 (General/OBC-NCL/defence wards) or Rs 700 (SC/ST) |
| **3–6 December 2026** | Late window with an extra Rs 500 fee | Only if you missed 2 December — do not plan to use this |
| **7–9 December 2026** | Correction window | Fix the mistakes NTA allows you to fix — check your confirmation page first |
| **11 January 2027** | Exam city announced | Check which city your child is allotted and start planning travel |
| **25 January 2027** | Admit cards available to download | Download it, print two copies, and check every detail on it |
| **31 January 2027** | **AISSEE 2027 exam** (pen-paper OMR) | Class 6: 150 minutes, 300 marks. Class 9: 180 minutes, 400 marks |
| **Result** | Not announced yet | NTA says it will be intimated later on its website |

That gap between 9 December and 11 January is the quiet month. No forms, no portals — just preparation. Sixty-eight days stand between today and the exam, and the families who use December well are the ones whose children walk into the hall calm.

## Three things to do this week

**First, do not treat the late window as a plan.** The 3–6 December window exists, and the extra Rs 500 is not the problem — the problem is that a form filled in a rush on 5 December is the form most likely to carry a wrong photo, a wrong date of birth, or a wrong school preference. Apply before 2 December.

**Second, save your confirmation page the moment you submit.** The correction window (7–9 December) only helps if you know what is wrong. Download the confirmation page, read it line by line against the birth certificate and the school records, and note anything that needs fixing. Our [correction-window audit guide](/news/aissee-2027-correction-window-7-9-december-audit-confirmation-page-2026-11-18/) walks through exactly what to check.

**Third, know your numbers.** The bulletin lists [3,160 Class 6 seats and 731 Class 9 seats across the 33 Sainik Schools](/news/aissee-2027-3160-class-6-seats-731-class-9-seats-2026-11-24/) for 2027-28. Class 6 is where the real opportunity is — more than four times the Class 9 intake. If your child is in Class 5, this is the entry point the system is built around.

## What not to worry about yet

The exam city (11 January) and the admit card (25 January) are two months away. Parents sometimes spend November worrying about which city they will get — but you cannot change the allotment, and the city list is fixed by the choices you already made on the form. Our [exam-city explainer](/news/aissee-2027-exam-city-slip-jan-11-admit-card-jan-25-what-to-check-2026-11-13/) covers what to check when the slip actually appears. For now, the only date that matters is 2 December.

One honest note: the result date has not been announced. NTA's own notice says it "will be intimated later on website." If any video or portal gives you a result date today, it is guessing. We will publish it the day NTA announces it.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. Both the form and the fee payment must be completed by then.

**I missed the 2 December deadline. Can I still apply for AISSEE 2027?**

Yes, for four more days. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December 2026 with an extra fee of Rs 500 on top of the normal fee. After 6 December, the window closes.

**When can I correct mistakes in my AISSEE 2027 form?**

During the correction window from 7 to 9 December 2026. Only the fields NTA allows can be changed, so check your confirmation page carefully before that window opens.

**When will I know my AISSEE 2027 exam city and admit card date?**

NTA will announce the exam city on 11 January 2027, and admit cards will be available to download from 25 January 2027. The exam is on Sunday, 31 January 2027.
