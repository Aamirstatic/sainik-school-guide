If you teach Social Science, Hindi or Fine Arts — and you can reach Purulia on a Monday morning — this is one of the simplest Sainik School openings you will see all year. There is no online form to fill, no fee mentioned in the notice, and no long wait for an admit card. You walk in with your papers, and the interview happens the same day.

Sainik School Purulia, in West Bengal, will hold a walk-in interview at **8:30 AM on Monday, 23 November 2026**, for three teaching posts on an ad-hoc basis. Candidates attend in person at the school campus with their application, bio-data, original certificates and attested copies. The notice describes no written test or further stages.

## The three posts at a glance

| Post | Vacancies | Age limit | Pay per month |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| TGT (Social Science), ad hoc | 1 | 21–35 years as on 1 Nov 2026 | Rs. 25,000 (consolidated) |
| TGT (Hindi), ad hoc | 1 | 21–35 years as on 1 Nov 2026 | Rs. 25,000 (consolidated) |
| Art Master (UR), ad hoc | 1 | 21–35 years as on 1 Nov 2026 | Rs. 21,000 (consolidated) |

The age limit is 21 to 35 years as on 1 November 2026 for all three posts, and the notice does not mention any age relaxation. The Art Master post is marked UR; no category is printed for the two TGT posts.

## Who can attend

**TGT (Social Science):** A graduate with History and Political Science as two subjects, plus B.Ed from a recognised university or institute. Or a B.A. B.Ed with Social Science (History and Political Science) from a Regional College of Education. Teaching experience in an English-medium residential school, knowledge of computer applications, and at least 50% marks in graduation and B.Ed are desirable.

**TGT (Hindi):** A bachelor's degree with Hindi studied as an elective or main subject for at least three years (six semesters), plus B.Ed from a recognised university or institute. Or a four-year integrated degree from an NCTE-recognised institution with the required subject combination. The same desirable qualifications apply — English-medium residential school experience, computer knowledge, and 50% marks in graduation and B.Ed.

**Art Master:** B. Fine Arts, or a five-year recognised diploma in Drawing and Painting, Sculpture or Graphic Art — or an equivalent degree from a recognised university. Past teaching experience in an English-medium residential school is desirable, and proficiency in games and sports, NCC and co-curricular activities like music, dance or drama is preferred.

## What to carry on 23 November

The notice does not include an application form and does not prescribe a format, so prepare your own application and bio-data. Carry these:

- Your application and bio-data
- Original certificates and mark sheets
- Attested copies of certificates and mark sheets
- Experience certificates and registration documents, where applicable

Reach the campus well before 8:30 AM — walk-in interviews run on a first-come queue, and latecomers risk missing the day entirely. No TA or DA will be paid for attending.

## The honest caveats

Three things to be clear-eyed about before you travel. First, all three posts are **ad hoc, not regular appointments** — this is a temporary engagement, not a permanent government teaching job. Second, the school administration reserves the right to cancel the vacancy for administrative or policy reasons. And third, the notice does not describe the selection process beyond the walk-in interview itself, so go prepared for a same-day interaction rather than expecting a written exam call later.

That said, for a B.Ed graduate in West Bengal or a neighbouring state, this is a genuine foot in the door of the Sainik Schools system — and unlike most teaching recruitments, the whole thing is decided in a single morning, ten days from now.

## FAQs

**When is the Sainik School Purulia walk-in interview?**

On Monday, 23 November 2026. Candidates must report at 8:30 AM at Sainik School Purulia, PO Sainik School-723104, District Purulia, West Bengal.

Three ad-hoc teaching posts: one TGT (Social Science), one TGT (Hindi) and one Art Master (UR). All three are on an ad-hoc basis, not regular appointments.

TGT (Social Science) and TGT (Hindi) carry a consolidated salary of Rs. 25,000 per month each, and Art Master carries Rs. 21,000 per month. The notice does not mention any allowance on top of the consolidated salary.

Your application and bio-data, original certificates, and attested copies of certificates and mark sheets. There is no prescribed application form — you bring your own application. No TA or DA is paid for attending, and the notice does not mention an application fee.
