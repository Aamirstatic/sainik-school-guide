## The next deadline is not the exam — it is the city slip

If your child applied for RMS CET 2026, the exam is three weeks away — **Sunday, 13 December 2026**, a single pen-and-paper OMR paper of two and a half hours for Class 6 and Class 9 entry into the five Rashtriya Military Schools. But the next thing on your calendar is not the exam. It is the **city intimation slip**, due in the **last week of November 2026** — which means, in practice, some day this coming week.

Here is why it matters more than parents think. The city slip is the first time you learn *where* your child is sitting the exam. If the centre city is two hours from home, you need to book travel and think about where the child sleeps the night before. If you only discover that on admit-card day, around 8 December, you have five days to sort it out — in December, when trains and rooms are already filling up. The city slip buys you a week of calm. Use it.

## What the slip tells you — and what it does not

The city slip carries exactly one useful piece of information: the **city** of the exam centre. It does not carry the centre's name, its address, or the reporting time. That is the admit card's job, and the admit card comes later — five to seven days before the exam, which the portals tracking RMS CET expect to be **around 8 December 2026**.

So when the slip appears, do not panic if it looks thin. It is supposed to look thin. The three checks to run the day it lands:

| Check | What to do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **City** | Note the city and work out travel time from home, honestly — not the optimistic version | A centre city 200 km away needs a night-before plan, not a 4 AM start |
| **Login** | Confirm your application number and password still work on exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet | The same login downloads the admit card; a forgotten password on 8 December is a crisis, on 29 November it is a five-minute fix |
| **Details** | Check the child's name, photograph and date of birth on the slip against the school ID | Any mismatch must be raised with NTA now, while there is still time |

One warning that catches families every year: NTA does not send the slip by post, by email, or by SMS. If you are waiting for a message, you will wait forever. The slip appears on the portal, and only on the portal.

## FAQs

**When will the RMS CET 2026 city intimation slip be released?**
A: In the last week of November 2026, according to the NTA RMS CET-2026 Information Bulletin. Admission portals tracking the exam expect it around 29 November 2026, but NTA has not fixed a single date — check the portal every day from 26 November.

**Is the city slip the same as the RMS CET admit card?**
A: No. The city slip only tells you which city your child's exam centre is in, so you can plan travel. The admit card, with the centre name, address and reporting time, comes separately, five to seven days before the exam — expected around 8 December 2026.

**How do I download the RMS CET 2026 city slip?**
A: Go to exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet and log in with the application number and password created during registration. The slip is not sent by post, email or SMS — you have to download it yourself.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**
A: Sunday, 13 December 2026. It is a single pen-and-paper OMR written test of two and a half hours for Class 6 and Class 9 entry into the five Rashtriya Military Schools.
