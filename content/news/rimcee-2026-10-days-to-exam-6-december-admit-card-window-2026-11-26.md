## Ten days. One document still to come

If your child is sitting RIMCEE 2026, the countdown is now short enough to count on your fingers — and the next three days matter more than the last four did. The written exam for admission to Class 8 at the Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun, is on **Sunday, 6 December 2026**, ten days from today, and the term it leads to begins in July 2027.

One of the two documents before the exam is already in your hands, or should be. NTA released the **advance exam city intimation slip on 17 November**, and it is live on the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE**. If you have not downloaded it yet, do it today — we covered the release in detail on 19 November, including [what the slip does and does not do](/news/rimcee-2026-city-slip-out-check-exam-city-2026-11-19/). The slip tells you the city — and only the city. The exam runs in 39 cities, and for many families the city decides whether this is a drive across town or a train booked the night before.

The second document is the one that matters at the gate, and it is not out yet. The **admit card** arrives 5 to 7 days before the exam, which works out to roughly **29 November to 1 December**. NTA has said it will be released "in due course" and has announced no separate date — so check the portal every evening from Sunday 29 November rather than waiting for a message. No admit card is sent by post, and none arrives by SMS or WhatsApp. If a message offers you a download link, it is not from NTA.

## The paper your child is walking into

Here is the part parents underestimate, because RIMCEE does not look like the other December exams. This is a **400-mark written exam** in three papers, and the trap is built into the scoring: your child needs **at least 50 percent in each paper** to reach the viva voce. A brilliant Maths score cannot rescue a weak English paper.

| Paper | Marks | Format | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 125 | Descriptive | 2 hours |
| Mathematics | 200 | Descriptive | 1 hour 30 minutes |
| General Knowledge | 75 | MCQ | 1 hour |

Those who clear move to a **50-mark viva voce**, then a medical fitness test; final selection rests on the combined merit of the written exam and the viva. The Stage-I result is expected by **20 February 2027**, and the viva voce is tentatively scheduled for **April 2027**. English is the quiet trap here: a descriptive paper with a 50-percent floor of its own. If Maths has had the last month, give English the next ten days — one essay and one comprehension passage a day, and no new grammar rules after 3 December.

## The last ten days: what to do, day by day

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| **26–28 Nov** | Download the city slip if you have not. Book travel and stay now if the city is away from home — refundable where possible, since the exact centre only comes with the admit card. Two timed papers only, marked strictly. |
| **29 Nov–1 Dec** | Admit-card watch: download it the day it appears, print two copies, and check the centre, reporting time, name spelling and photograph. |
| **2–5 Dec** | Light revision only: formulas, GK one-liners, one English essay outline a day. Pack the admit card, ID proof, pens and a water bottle. Sleep by 9 PM on 5 December. |
| **6 Dec** | Reach the centre early — the exam will not move even if 6 December is declared a public holiday; the bulletin says so plainly. |

If the login fails, use the "forgot password" option on the portal rather than creating a fresh application — a duplicate application can quietly disqualify a candidate. For help, NTA's helpline is **011-40759000 / 011-69227700**, or email **rimc@nta.gov.in**.

## FAQs

**Is the RIMCEE 2026 admit card released?**

Not yet. NTA has announced no admit-card date. As per the information bulletin, the admit card comes 5 to 7 days before the exam, which points to roughly 29 November to 1 December 2026. Check the official portal, exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE, every evening from 29 November.

**Is the RIMCEE 2026 exam city slip out?**

Yes. NTA released the advance exam city intimation slip on 17 November 2026. Registered candidates can check their allotted examination city at exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE using their application number and password.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 exam and what is the paper pattern?**

Sunday, 6 December 2026. The Stage-I written exam is 400 marks across three papers: English (125 marks, descriptive, 2 hours), Mathematics (200 marks, descriptive, 1 hour 30 minutes) and General Knowledge (75 marks, MCQ, 1 hour). At least 50 percent in each paper is needed to reach the viva voce.

**Is the city slip the same as the admit card?**

No. The city slip only tells you which city your exam centre is in, so you can plan travel. It carries no centre address and no reporting time, and it will not get your child through the exam-hall gate. Only the admit card does that.
