## A committee, not a vacancy notice

Here is the first thing to get straight: this is not a job advertisement. The Jammu and Kashmir Government has constituted a four-member committee to examine and update the Recruitment Rules of J&K Sainik School, Manasbal, and to propose the amendments needed. The order — Government Order No. 470-JK (Edu) of 2026, issued by the School Education Department — was reported by Kashmir Life on 9 November 2026.

The committee is headed by the Director of School Education, Kashmir. Its other members are the Additional Secretary of the School Education Department, the Principal of Sainik School Manasbal, who will serve as Member Secretary, and the department's Under Secretary. The chairman has also been authorised to co-opt any other representative or member considered necessary for the work.

The order was issued in supersession of all previous orders on the matter, and the committee has to examine the existing rules and submit its recommendations, along with the proposed amendments, within two months of its constitution. That points to a report around early January 2027 — 🔶 **EXPECTED**, calculated from the two-month window. The decision follows the minutes of the 38th meeting of the school's Executive Committee.

## Why the rules need revising now

Manasbal is not one of the 33 Sainik Schools run by the Ministry of Defence. It is run by the Jammu and Kashmir government, and its staffing troubles have been public for a while. A Greater Kashmir report from August 2026 described a school struggling to keep its academic strength: enrolment of around 350 students against a total capacity of about 480, regular faculty recruitment not keeping pace with requirements, and School Education Department teachers deputed to the residential school to fill the gaps.

The same report noted that the school had no permanent principal — the charge was held by Kupwara's Chief Education Officer as an additional assignment — and that the Education Minister had promised a permanent appointment soon. Recruitment rules are the unglamorous paperwork underneath all of that: they decide who can be appointed, to which posts, with what qualifications and age limits. Outdated rules are one reason posts stay vacant or get challenged.

For context, the school's last recruitment drive was Advertisement Notification No. 1 of 2026, issued in March 2026 for 14 teaching and non-teaching posts, with applications closing on 31 March 2026. That drive is long over. Nothing in the new order reopens it.

## What this means for job aspirants

If you are a teacher or graduate in Jammu and Kashmir watching Sainik School Manasbal for openings, this order matters — but only as groundwork. Revised rules come first; fresh advertisements follow later, once the rules are approved. So the practical advice is dull but correct: **do not pay anyone** claiming a form or shortcut linked to this order — there is no application attached to it; **keep your documents ready** (degree, B.Ed, CTET/STET where applicable, domicile and category certificates) so a real advertisement does not catch you scrambling; and **watch the school's own channels** for the next notification rather than forwarded messages.

For parents, this is a staff matter, not an admissions matter. It does not change the AISSEE 2027 application window, open until 2 December 2026, or admission to Manasbal, which remains open to domicile holders of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.

## What to watch next

The honest test of this order is the deadline. If the committee reports on time, the revised rules should reach the public domain around early January 2027, and any fresh recruitment advertisement after that will run under them. If it slips, Manasbal's staffing gaps continue as they are. We will update this story when the committee's recommendations are published or a new advertisement appears.

## FAQs

**Does the Manasbal recruitment-rules order announce any teaching or non-teaching vacancies?**

No. The order only sets up a committee to revise the recruitment rules. No new vacancies have been announced under this order.

**Who is on the committee revising Sainik School Manasbal's recruitment rules?**

A four-member committee headed by the Director of School Education, Kashmir, with the Additional Secretary of the School Education Department, the Principal of Sainik School Manasbal as Member Secretary, and the department's Under Secretary.

**When will the revised recruitment rules for Sainik School Manasbal be ready?**

The committee has to submit its recommendations and proposed amendments within two months of its constitution, which points to around early January 2027.

**Does this order affect admission to Sainik School Manasbal?**

No. This order is about staff recruitment rules only. Admission to the school is a separate process, open to domicile holders of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.
