## A new roof over Nagrota's sporting life

If you are filling the AISSEE 2027 form this week and Sainik School Nagrota is on your list, the school changed today — in a small way that matters more than it looks. On **Wednesday, 25 November 2026**, Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor **Manoj Sinha** inaugurated a newly constructed **multi-utility indoor sports arena** at Sainik School Nagrota, in Jammu.

The arena was built by the **ICICI Foundation** through corporate social responsibility support, and the Lieutenant Governor congratulated the Foundation at the ceremony. He also urged the school administration and coaching staff to make sure the facility is used to its full potential — a pointed remark, because a new building only helps if cadets actually train in it every week.

## What the arena actually changes for cadets

Here is the practical bit. The arena will provide **uninterrupted training throughout the year** and let cadets pursue sporting excellence in **badminton, basketball and table tennis, regardless of weather conditions**. That last phrase is doing real work: Jammu's summers are harsh and its winters bite, and outdoor courts sit empty for weeks at a stretch.

It is also the second sports upgrade at Nagrota in four months. In August 2026 the school inaugurated new synthetic tennis, volleyball, basketball and badminton courts — so the arena lands on top of an already-improving sports setup. Nagrota also hosted the All India Sainik Schools National Games this year and won the Group A title on home turf.

## Five principles, and a line about the uniform

The Lieutenant Governor used the occasion to give cadets five guiding principles: **dream big, work hard with honesty, embrace teamwork and sportsmanship, maintain physical and mental well-being, and always regard the military uniform as a symbol of service and sacrifice**.

He was blunt about what sport is for in a military school. Sports foster teamwork, resilience, discipline, humility and leadership, he said — and in the military tradition, physical training has always carried special importance, because a soldier is expected to stay calm in adverse circumstances, make quick decisions, trust comrades and stand firm when things get difficult. "All these qualities are developed on the sports field," he told the cadets.

On the uniform, his words are worth quoting exactly: *"The uniform is not merely a symbol of authority and honour; it is a symbol of service, responsibility and sacrifice."* He added that some of the cadets in front of him could one day represent India on the Olympic stage.

Earlier in the visit, he laid a wreath at the **Shaurya Smarak** and paid homage to the soldiers who died for the unity, sovereignty and integrity of the nation.

## Why this matters if Nagrota is on your form

Nagrota is the only Sainik School in Jammu and Kashmir run by the Sainik Schools Society under the Ministry of Defence, on a 108-acre campus on the banks of the River Tawi, about 18 km from Jammu Tawi station. It was founded on 22 August 1970, and it carries the nickname "Gateway to NDA" in the state — it won the **Raksha Mantri Trophy in 2025** for sending the highest percentage of cadets to the NDA, with 11 cadets clearing the SSB.

The form closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. For 2027-28, Nagrota has **75 Class 6 seats — 65 for boys and 10 for girls — and 10 Class 9 seats, all for boys**; there are no Class 9 seats for girls this year. Our full [parents' guide to Sainik School Nagrota](/blog/sainik-school-nagrota-complete-parents-guide-2027-2026-11-25/) has the seat split, the four houses and the fees picture. If Nagrota is going on your child's preference list, the arena is one more reason to put it there — but fill the form for the school, not the building. Seven days is enough time to fill it carefully. It is not enough time to fill it twice.

## FAQs

**What was inaugurated at Sainik School Nagrota on 25 November 2026?**

A newly constructed multi-utility indoor sports arena, inaugurated by Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha. It was built by the ICICI Foundation through corporate social responsibility support.

**Which sports can cadets train for in the new arena at Sainik School Nagrota?**

Badminton, basketball and table tennis. The indoor arena lets cadets train through the year regardless of weather, which matters in Jammu's summer heat and winter cold.

**How many seats does Sainik School Nagrota have in AISSEE 2027?**

75 seats in Class 6 — 65 for boys and 10 for girls — and 10 seats in Class 9, all for boys. There are no Class 9 seats for girls at Nagrota this year.

**When does the AISSEE 2027 form close?**

2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra fee of Rs 500, and corrections can be made from 7 to 9 December. The exam is on 31 January 2027.
