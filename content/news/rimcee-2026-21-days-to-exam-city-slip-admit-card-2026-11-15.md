If your child applied for RIMC Dehradun, the waiting is nearly over. The RIMCEE 2026 written exam is on **Sunday, 6 December 2026** — 21 days from today. Applications closed weeks ago (the late window ended on 7 October), so nothing is left to file. What is left is a short, fixed sequence: city slip, admit card, exam. Miss the first two and the third gets harder than it needs to be.

## The three dates that matter now

| What | When |
|------|------|
| City intimation slip | **21–24 November 2026** (12–15 days before the exam) |
| Admit card download | **29 November–1 December 2026** (5–7 days before the exam) |
| Stage-I written exam | **Sunday, 6 December 2026** |
| Stage-I result | **By 20 February 2027** |
| Stage-II viva voce | **Tentatively April 2027** |

Both documents come only from the NTA portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE**. The admit card is never sent by post, and no duplicate is issued at the centre — download it the day it appears and print two copies.

One honest warning: the bulletin says the exam schedule will not change even if 6 December is declared a public holiday. Do not plan around a postponement that is not coming.

## The paper: 400 marks, and every subject must be passed

Here is the thing parents most often get wrong about RIMCEE. It is not one paper with one cut-off. It is three papers, and your child must clear **50 percent in each subject separately**:

| Paper | Questions | Marks | Time |
|-------|-----------|-------|------|
| English (descriptive) | 45 | 125 | 2 hours |
| Mathematics (descriptive) | 30 | 200 | 1 hour 30 minutes |
| General Knowledge (MCQ) | 75 | 75 | 1 hour |
| **Total** | **150** | **400** | **4 hours 30 minutes** |

Mathematics alone is half the total — 200 of 400 marks — so it decides most outcomes. But the 50-percent rule means a child who aces Maths and fails English still goes home. If your child's English is the weak paper, these 21 days belong to it: one descriptive English paper a week, written by hand, timed at two hours.

There is **no negative marking**, and the paper is bilingual in English and Hindi except for the English paper itself. If a question's translation is ambiguous, the English version is treated as final.

## What happens after the written exam

Clearing the written paper is only stage one. Shortlisted candidates face a **viva voce worth 50 marks** — at least 25 to qualify — testing intelligence, personality and communication skills. Then comes a medical examination at a military hospital. Only candidates found medically fit, in merit order and within the vacancies, are offered admission.

The bulletin gives a tentative intake of **25 boy cadets and 10 girl cadets** for the July 2027 term, subject to approval. RIMC is a small, fiercely competitive school — it exists to feed the National Defence Academy — so treat every mark in these 21 days as if it decides the outcome. It probably does.

## What to do this week

Do not wait for the city slip to start preparing. Check the NTA RIMCEE portal twice a week from 21 November, keep the application number and password from the original form within reach, and run one full 4½-hour mock under real exam timing — Maths last, when the mind is tiredest, because that is when the real paper will test it. ## FAQs

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 exam?**
Sunday, 6 December 2026. It is the Stage-I written examination for Class 8 admission to RIMC Dehradun for the July 2027 term, conducted by NTA in pen-and-paper mode across 39 cities.

**When will the RIMCEE 2026 city slip and admit card come?**
The city intimation slip comes 12 to 15 days before the exam, so around 21 to 24 November 2026. The admit card follows 5 to 7 days before the exam, around 29 November to 1 December 2026, on exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE.

**What is the RIMCEE 2026 paper pattern and qualifying mark?**
The written exam is 400 marks over 4 hours 30 minutes: English 125 marks, Mathematics 200 marks and General Knowledge 75 marks. A candidate must score at least 50 percent in each subject separately — a high score in one subject cannot cover a fail in another.

**How many seats does RIMC Dehradun offer in the July 2027 term?**
The bulletin gives a tentative intake of 25 boy cadets and 10 girl cadets per term, subject to approval by the competent authority. Admission is by merit through the written exam, viva voce and medical examination.
