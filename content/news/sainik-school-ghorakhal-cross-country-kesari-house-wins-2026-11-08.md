## What happened

Sainik School Ghorakhal held its annual Inter-House Cross-Country Race on 7 November 2026, and Kesari House came out on top.

The school's Principal, Group Captain Madhu Sanger, flagged off the race. Cadets ran in three groups over distances matched to their age: the senior group — 134 students — covered about 10 kilometres, the junior group — 209 students — ran about 5 kilometres, and Class 6 cadets had their own shorter race of about 2.4 kilometres. Fifty-one girl cadets also took part.

In the end, Kesari House took the overall title, ending Singh House's run as last year's champions. The individual honours went to Cadet Gaurav Negi, first in the senior group; Cadet Umang, first in the junior group; and Cadet Srishti Gangwar of Bachendri House, first in her category.

## A race that tells you what the school is

If you are a parent weighing Sainik School for your child, a cross-country result might look like a small thing. It isn't — not at Ghorakhal.

The school sits at about 6,000 feet in the Kumaon hills near Nainital, and its cross-country course is not a flat track. It is hilly trails, the kind of running that tests stamina in a way a playground sprint never will. That is the point. Sainik Schools exist to prepare boys and girls for the armed forces, and endurance is part of the job description — the same reason the [All India Sainik Schools National Games](/news/all-india-sainik-schools-national-games-2026-winners-announced-2026-11-08/) closed at Bijapur only yesterday, with Ghorakhal itself taking the Junior Boys' Basketball title there.

The house system matters too. Houses compete against each other all year — academics, sports, drill, debate — and the cross-country trophy is one of the big ones. A house win like Kesari's is not decided by one fast runner; it is decided by how many cadets in the house finish well. That is a deliberate design: it teaches cadets that the team's result matters more than any one person's.

## The bigger picture for parents

Ghorakhal is one of the 33 established Sainik Schools, founded in 1966. Like the others, it admits through [AISSEE 2027](/news/aissee-2027-notification-out-applications-open-exam-jan-31-2026-11-03/) — applications are open until 2 December 2026 and the exam is on 31 January 2027.

When parents ask us what life inside a Sainik School is actually like, the honest answer is that it is mornings like this one: early starts, physical training before classes, and competitions that the whole school takes seriously. [Sainik School Imphal held its own inter-house cross-country](/news/sainik-school-imphal-cross-country-555-cadets-2026-10-26/) just two weeks ago, with 555 cadets running. It is not an extra. It is the routine.

That routine is also why the schools produce the NDA results they do — Ghorakhal itself had 44 cadets clear the NDA written examination this year. The running, the discipline and the house competition are not separate from the academics. They are the same training.

## What to watch next

The next big date on the school calendar for parents is not a race — it is the AISSEE 2027 application deadline on 2 December 2026. If Ghorakhal is on your child's list, the form is open now on the NTA portal. And if your child does get in, tell them this: the cross-country course at 6,000 feet does not get easier. The cadets who finish it well are the ones who trained for it all year.
