## Two clocks, one deadline day

Sunday, 25 October 2026 is the busiest single day of the defence-school form season. The UP Sainik School entrance exam closes its normal-fee window at 5 PM, and the Rashtriya Military Schools Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) closes its late-fee window at the same hour. If either form is still unfinished in your house, today is the day to finish it — not tomorrow, because tomorrow is too late for one of them and twice as expensive for the other.

## What closes today

| Form | What closes at 5 PM today | Fee | Portal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UP Sainik School entrance exam 2027-28** (Classes 6 and 9, Lucknow and Gorakhpur) | Normal-fee application | Rs 1,000 | upsainikschool.org |
| **RMS CET 2026** (Classes 6 and 9, five Rashtriya Military Schools) | Late-fee application form | Rs 1,500 (General/OBC) or Rs 1,250 (SC/ST and wards of personnel killed in action), including the Rs 500 late fee | exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet |

One difference matters for planning: for RMS CET, the form must be in by 5 PM but fee payment stays open till 11:50 PM tonight. For UP Sainik, form and fee both end at 5 PM.

## The morning checklist: finish before 5 PM

Work through this list in order, and do not leave payment to the last hour — portals slow down on deadline days.

1. **Keep the confirmation page, not just the payment receipt.** For RMS CET, a bank debit alone does not prove submission. For UP Sainik, download the completed form and receipt.
2. **Check the photograph and signature before you pay.** These are the fields families most often need to fix later, and fixes are only possible inside the correction window.
3. **One application per candidate for RMS CET.** A second registration for the same child creates trouble the correction window cannot undo.
4. **For UP Sainik, check domicile first.** Applicants and their fathers must be domiciled in Uttar Pradesh; Class 6 applicants must be born between 2 July 2015 and 1 January 2018, Class 9 between 2 July 2012 and 1 January 2015.
5. **Submit by 4 PM if you can.** That leaves an hour for a failed payment retry.

## If you miss today: two very different futures

Missing the UP Sainik normal-fee window costs money, not the year: the late window opens tomorrow, 26 October, and runs till 31 October at 5 PM with a total fee of Rs 2,000. The correction window follows on 3 to 5 November, admit cards arrive after 25 November, and the exam is on 20 December 2026.

Missing the RMS CET window costs the year. Today is the final day of the late window and no further window has been announced. The correction window runs on 27 and 28 October, the city intimation slip is expected in the last week of November, admit cards come five to seven days before the exam, and the written test is on 13 December 2026.

## The December countdown, and the AISSEE watch

From today: the RIMCEE written exam is 42 days away (6 December), RMS CET is 49 days away (13 December), and the UP Sainik exam is 56 days away (20 December). The AISSEE 2027 exam is 98 days away on 31 January 2027 (EXPECTED).

Until a bulletin appears on the official NTA portal, treat every "form out" claim as unverified.

## FAQs

**What exactly closes at 5 PM today, 25 October 2026?**

The UP Sainik School entrance exam application at its normal fee of Rs 1,000, and the RMS CET late-fee application form.

**Can I still pay the RMS CET fee after 5 PM today?**

Yes, for RMS CET only: the form must be submitted by 5 PM, but fee payment stays open till 11:50 PM. For UP Sainik, form and fee both close at 5 PM.

**What happens if I miss today's UP Sainik deadline?**

You can still apply in the late window from 26 to 31 October at Rs 2,000 total, closing at 5 PM on 31 October, with the correction window on 3 to 5 November.

**Is there any window after today for RMS CET?**

No. Today is the final day of the late window, and no further window has been announced — missing it means waiting for the next admission cycle.
