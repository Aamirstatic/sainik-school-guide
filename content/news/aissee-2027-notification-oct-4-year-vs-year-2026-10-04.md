> **Last verified: 4 October 2026.** This is a **status explainer, not breaking news** — the AISSEE 2027 notification has not been released. Figures marked **CONFIRMED** are from last year's official cycle; everything about the 2027 cycle is marked **EXPECTED** until NTA publishes the information bulletin.

It is Sunday, 4 October 2026, and if you have been checking for the AISSEE 2027 notification every morning, here is the calm answer: **it is not out yet** — and that is completely normal.

This time last year, on 4 October 2025, the AISSEE 2026 form was also not out. It dropped six days later, on Friday, **10 October 2025** (CONFIRMED, via Buddy4Study and multiple news reports). So early October with no notification in sight is not a delay — it is the schedule.

## AISSEE 2026 vs AISSEE 2027: the comparison that matters

| Milestone | 2026 cycle (CONFIRMED) | 2027 cycle (EXPECTED) |
|---|---|---|
| Notification / form opens | 10 Oct 2025 | October 2026 |
| Initial application window | 10–30 Oct 2025 (20 days) | Oct–Nov 2026 (pattern) |
| Extended deadline | 9 Nov 2025 | Unknown until bulletin |
| Correction window | Nov 2025 | Nov 2026 (pattern) |
| Application fee | ₹850 Gen/OBC, ₹700 SC/ST | ₹850 / ₹700 (2026 rates) |
| Exam date | 18 Jan 2026 | **31 Jan 2027** (tentative, NTA calendar) |
| Result | 27 Feb 2026 | Feb 2027 (pattern) |

Notice the pattern: last year gave parents barely **20 days** in the initial window before the extension was announced. Do not assume the extension will happen again this year — plan as if the window stays short.

## What this week probably looks like

Last year's release fell on a Friday. This year's equivalent Friday is **9 October** — but that is pattern-spotting, not a prediction. NTA does not follow a fixed calendar date for the release (EdexLive's three-year analysis makes this clear). The honest version of the story: it could come any day this month, so stay ready rather than trying to guess the date.

## Use this week to get form-ready

![A study planner and calendar on a student's desk](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-notification-oct-4-year-vs-year-2026-10-04.webp)

While you wait, finish the boring work now, because the form window rewards the prepared:

1. **Photograph and signature** — last year the specs were 10 KB–200 KB (photo) and 10 KB–50 KB (signature), JPG format. Get fresh scans done at a studio, not a phone photo of a photo.
2. **Birth certificate and domicile** — check the name spelling and date of birth match the school records exactly. DOB mismatches are the single most common correction-window headache.
3. **Category certificate** — if applicable, and issued by the competent authority.
4. **One working email and mobile number** — the OTPs and all future communication (admit card, answer key, result) go here.
5. **Ignore coaching-centre "inside dates"** — nobody outside NTA knows the release date. EdexLive specifically warns parents not to rely on dates circulated by unofficial websites.

## FAQs

**Is the AISSEE 2027 notification out?**

No. As of 4 October 2026, NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or opened the application form. The notification is expected sometime in October 2026, based on last year's pattern.

**Should parents worry that the AISSEE 2027 form is late?**

No. Last year the AISSEE 2026 form opened on 10 October 2025, so early October with no form out is exactly on schedule, not behind. Nothing is delayed yet.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam expected?**

31 January 2027, tentatively, as listed in the NTA exam calendar 2026-27 released on 16 September 2026. This will be confirmed in the information bulletin when it releases.

**What should parents prepare while waiting for the AISSEE 2027 form?**

Keep the birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate if applicable, the child's photograph and signature in the required JPG sizes, and an active mobile number and email ID ready. Last year's window was only 20 days initially, so early preparation matters.
