> **Last verified: 22 October 2026.** School-wise figures below are CONFIRMED from two independent Tier-2 publications (The Tribune, The Hans India). The 242 running total is our arithmetic on publicly reported school figures — clearly a tally of media reports, not an official UPSC count.

Two more schools have reported their NDA written-exam results this week, and the national picture is getting clearer — and more impressive. **Sainik School Korukonda in Andhra Pradesh has recorded the highest number of NDA/NA written qualifiers of any Sainik School in the country**, while **Sainik School Kunjpura in Haryana** has reported a strong batch of its own. Together, they push the reported Sainik School tally this cycle past 240.

## Korukonda: 54 qualifiers, the country's best (CONFIRMED)

According to The Hans India, Korukonda produced **54 successful candidates** in the UPSC NDA/NA written examination for **158 Course** — the most of any Sainik School nationally. The numbers behind it are striking:

- **41 out of 78 cadets** from the current Class XII batch qualified — a **52.56% success rate**, meaning more than half the batch cleared one of the country's toughest school-leaver exams.
- **13 cadets from the previous batch** also cleared, taking the total to 54.
- This is the **second consecutive year** that more than half of Korukonda's passing-out batch has qualified.

Principal Group Captain SS Shastri congratulated the cadets and credited the training team led by Wing Commander Kiran V., plus the school's faculty, for the "sustained guidance, systematic preparation and mentorship" behind the result.

## Kunjpura: 45 qualifiers, announced this morning (CONFIRMED)

The Tribune reports that **45 cadets from Sainik School Kunjpura** have qualified in the NDA written examination — **32 from the current Class XII batch** and **13 from the previous batch**. Principal Captain (IN) Gurbir Singh praised the cadets' "hard work, discipline and perseverance" and expressed confidence ahead of the SSB interviews.

## The running scoreboard: 242 and counting

Our [national scoreboard on 14 October](/news/nda-ii-2026-sainik-school-scoreboard-2026-10-14/) stood at 143 reported qualifiers across the schools that had announced by then — including [Nalanda's 23](/news/sainik-school-nalanda-nda-written-2026-results-2026-10-12/), [Kalikiri's 50](/news/kalikiri-sainik-school-50-cadets-nda-written-result-2026-10-12/), [Ghorakhal's 44](/news/ghorakhal-44-cadets-clear-nda-written-2026-10-13/) and [Punglwa's 9](/news/sainik-school-punglwa-9-cadets-clear-nda-written-exam-2026-10-14/). Korukonda (54) and Kunjpura (45) reported after that tally was published, so there is no double-counting. The running total of publicly reported Sainik School qualifiers is now **242**.

That figure will keep moving. More schools are still announcing, and the number that matters next is not the written tally but how many convert through the [five-day SSB stage](/news/nda-written-qualifiers-ssb-stage-explained-2026-10-14/) — which is where the real attrition happens.

## Why parents should care

If your child is eyeing AISSEE 2027, this is the endgame you are buying into. A Sainik School's NDA record is the most honest measure of what it does with six or seven years of your child's life. Korukonda's 52.56% written-qualification rate from a single Class XII batch is not luck — it is daily, relentless preparation, and that is what you are choosing when you choose a Sainik School.
