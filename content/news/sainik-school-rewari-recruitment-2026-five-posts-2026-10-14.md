## Five posts, all contractual — what Sainik School Rewari is hiring for

Sainik School Rewari, Haryana, has released a short recruitment notice (published 13 October 2026) inviting applications for **five contractual teaching and non-teaching posts** for 2026. Applications open **17 October** and close **6 November 2026 (5:00 PM)**, and — importantly — the school is accepting them **offline only**: by India Post or by hand at the school office. There is no online application link.

Before parents of our usual audience tune out: yes, this is a jobs update, not an admission update. But thousands of readers of this site are defence-family and teacher-family households, and contractual roles like these are exactly the kind of opening people ask us about. So here is the complete, verified picture.

## The five posts and who can apply

All five vacancies are on a **contractual basis** — an initial one-year engagement (11 serving months plus a one-month break period), extendable up to three years subject to satisfactory performance and institutional requirements. No TA/DA is admissible for candidates called for test or interview.

| Post | Posts | Qualification required |
|------|-------|------------------------|
| Medical Officer | 1 | MBBS degree |
| PGT (Physical Education) | 1 | Degree in Physical Education |
| Nursing Assistant (Male) | 1 | Nursing diploma / GNM / B.Sc Nursing with 5 years' experience |
| PEM/PTI-cum-Matron (Female) | 1 | Class 10 (matriculation) passed |
| Counsellor | 1 | Graduate/PG in Psychology, or PG in Child Development, or Graduate/PG with diploma in Career Guidance and Counselling |

**Age limits (as on the advertisement date):** Medical Officer, PEM/PTI-cum-Matron, Nursing Assistant and PGT (Physical Education): **18–50 years**. Counsellor: **21–50 years**. Age relaxation applies as per Central Government rules. (Note: the job portals flag these qualification and age details as per the notice; treat the official notification on ssrw.org as the final word.)

The selection process follows the standard Sainik School pattern: **shortlisting of applications → written test/interview → skill test (where applicable) → medical examination → document verification**.

## Dates, fee and how to send your application

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Notification published | 13 October 2026 |
| Application window | **17 October – 6 November 2026 (till 5:00 PM)** |
| Mode | Offline only — by post or by hand |
| Fee | **Rs. 500** (General/OBC); **nil** for SC/ST — by bank draft in favour of "Principal, Sainik School Rewari", payable at Rewari |
| Send to | The Principal, Sainik School Rewari, Vill–Gothra Ahir, Distt–Rewari, Haryana – 123102 |
| Official website | ssrw.org |

Superscribe the envelope with the **name of the post applied for** ("APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF …"). Download the prescribed application form from the school website, attach attested copies of certificates from matriculation onwards, a passport-size photograph, and your mobile number and email ID. Applications must **reach** the school by the last date — postal delays are the applicant's risk, so post early or deliver by hand if you are nearby.

## What this means for you

If you are a qualified candidate looking for a teaching or support role in a disciplined, defence-oriented school environment, the window is short — **21 days**, and it opens this Saturday. Get the application form ready now, prepare the bank draft (Rs. 500 for General/OBC), and don't wait for November.

One caution from our side: **never pay anyone claiming to "guarantee" a Sainik School job.** Selection is through the school's own shortlisting and test/interview process. Applications go only to the address above. If an agent asks for extra money, walk away.

## FAQs

**How many posts are open in Sainik School Rewari Recruitment 2026?**

Five posts on a contractual basis: Medical Officer (1), PGT Physical Education (1), PEM/PTI-cum-Matron Female (1), Nursing Assistant Male (1) and Counsellor (1). The short notice was published on 13 October 2026.

**What is the last date to apply for Sainik School Rewari jobs?**

Applications are accepted from 17 October to 6 November 2026 (up to 5:00 PM), by post or by hand, addressed to the Principal, Sainik School Rewari, Vill-Gothra Ahir, District Rewari, Haryana – 123102.

**What is the application fee for Sainik School Rewari Recruitment 2026?**

Rs. 500 for General and OBC candidates, payable by bank draft drawn in favour of Principal, Sainik School Rewari, payable at Rewari. SC and ST candidates pay no fee.

**Are the Sainik School Rewari 2026 posts permanent?**

No. The posts are contractual for an initial period of one year (11 serving months plus a one-month break), extendable up to three years subject to satisfactory performance.
