## An evening off, on campus

Sainik School Nagrota organised its Annual Nagrotian Mela 2026 on 29 October at the Maj Somnath Sharma PVC Stadium, giving cadets an evening of fun, food and entertainment away from their demanding academic and military training schedules. The mela was inaugurated by Principal Captain (IN) Shibu Devasia. The report describes a full fairground atmosphere rather than a formal function: food stalls, games, live music, rides and raffles, ending with fireworks.

## What the evening included

- **Food stalls** — cadets enjoyed a variety of food items, including those served at a special counter by Simbly South.
- **Cadet-run game stalls** — the games were run by the cadets themselves, which the report notes showcased their creativity, teamwork and organisational skills. That detail matters: in a Sainik School, even the fun is partly a leadership exercise.
- **Horse and camel rides** — a crowd favourite that added to the excitement of the evening.
- **Live music** — the school's musical band, the Decibels, entertained the gathering with a lively performance.
- **Raffle draws** — several prizes kept cadets engaged through the evening.
- **Firecracker finale** — the celebrations culminated in a firecracker display that lit up the night sky before the event concluded.

## Why this small story matters to AISSEE parents

Parents researching Sainik Schools often see only the exam, the discipline and the routine. A mela like this is the other half of the picture: the Daily Excelsior report frames it explicitly as a chance for cadets to relax amid demanding schedules while building friendship, camaraderie and teamwork. Those are not soft extras — they are the qualities the schools say they are trying to build.

Nagrota has had a busy year on this front: the school hosted the Group A meet of the All India Sainik Schools National Games in 2026 and won the overall championship. A school that can stage a national meet in September and a campus mela in October is showing parents something useful: the calendar has room for celebration alongside competition. Read our earlier report on the [National Games finale at Ghorakhal](/news/aissng-2026-national-games-conclude-ghorakhal-2026-09-28/) for the wider picture.

One honest limit: the report describes the mela as a campus celebration for cadets. It does not mention public or parent entry or timings beyond the evening itself, so families should check with the school directly rather than travelling on the strength of this report.
