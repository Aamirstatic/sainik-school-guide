## The clock is real now: 20 days to 2 December

The AISSEE 2027 application window opened on 3 November 2026, and it closes on 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM. Counting from today, 12 November, that leaves 20 days — three weekends.

Our [evening report on the notification](/news/aissee-2027-notification-out-applications-open-exam-jan-31-2026-11-03/) covered the full schedule, and our [form-mistakes guide](/news/aissee-2027-form-mistakes-to-avoid-before-you-submit-2026-11-04/) covered what goes wrong at the last minute. This piece is the simpler job: what to finish this week, while there is still time to fix a problem.

## This week's checklist

**1. Confirm the fee for your category.** As reported from the NTA bulletin, the fee is Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and Defence candidates and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates, payable online by UPI, debit or credit card or net banking. Our [fee and age-limit guide](/news/aissee-2027-fee-eligibility-age-limit-2026-11-03/) has the full breakdown. A form without a successful payment is not a form at all.

**2. Gather the documents before opening the form.** Birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate if applicable, the child's current-school details, a recent photograph and a signature. If any certificate needs issuing or correcting — a spelling mismatch, an old-format caste certificate — start that process this week, not in the last week.

**3. Check the age rule against the birth certificate, not memory.** Class 6 needs age 10–12 as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017, currently in Class 5); Class 9 needs age 13–15 as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014, currently in Class 8).

**4. Shortlist schools in order before you fill choices.** The form asks for school preferences. Decide the order as a family, calmly, this week — not at 11 PM on 2 December.

**5. Submit early, keep the correction window as a safety net.** The correction window runs from 7 to 9 December 2026, but some fields may be locked, so do not plan to fix a serious error there. And if you do miss 2 December, the late window (3–6 December) costs an extra Rs 500 — an emergency exit, not a plan.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**
2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM, for both the form and the fee payment. A late window with an extra Rs 500 fee runs from 3 to 6 December 2026.

**How many days are left to apply for AISSEE 2027?**
20 days remain after 12 November 2026 before the 2 December deadline. That is three weekends — enough time to apply calmly, but not enough to keep postponing the documents.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam?**
31 January 2027, in pen-and-paper OMR mode. The exam city is scheduled to be announced on 11 January 2027 and admit cards on 25 January 2027.

**What should I finish this week for AISSEE 2027?**
Gather the birth certificate, domicile and category certificates, a recent photograph and signature in the required format, confirm the fee for your category, and shortlist the schools in order of preference before you start the form.
