**The AISSEE 2027 notification has not been released.** Let me say that plainly, because WhatsApp groups and YouTube thumbnails are already screaming "FORM OUT!" — and they are wrong, or at best guessing.

But here is what we *do* know, and it is genuinely useful: the last three AISSEE cycles have followed an almost identical rhythm, and that rhythm points to **October 2026** as the month the notification drops. This article is not breaking news — it is a pattern-based watch guide, clearly labelled, so you know exactly what to expect and where to look first.

## What the past cycles actually show

For AISSEE 2026, NTA opened the application window from **10 October to 30 October 2025** (fee payment till 31 October), followed by a correction window of **02–04 November 2025**. That is ✅ **CONFIRMED** — reported by multiple outlets citing the official NTA notice. Earlier cycles landed in the same October slot.

The pattern, then, is simple: NTA announces in early-to-mid October, gives roughly three weeks to apply, then a short correction window in early November. If 2027 follows suit — and every signal says it will — the notification should appear within the next two to four weeks.

## Where to check — and where not to

When the notification lands, it will appear **first** on NTA's official channels: `nta.ac.in` and the AISSEE portal (`exams.nta.ac.in` / `exams.nta.nic.in`). Check those before believing anything else.

A word of caution from experience: every year, fake "AISSEE form released" posts circulate on WhatsApp and Facebook weeks before the real thing. The test is simple — **if it is not on an nta.ac.in domain, it is not the notification.** No coaching institute, no YouTube channel, and no job portal publishes the form. They only report it afterwards.
