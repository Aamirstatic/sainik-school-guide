# RMS CET 2026: 21 Days Left to Apply — Fees, Age Limits, Exam Pattern Explained

> **Last verified: 29 September 2026.** This is a mid-window status update and applicant checklist — not a breaking-news report. The window opened on 23 September 2026; all dates and fees below are CONFIRMED from the NTA schedule as reported by multiple national dailies.

The Rashtriya Military Schools Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026 is now exactly one week into its application window. If your child is aiming for one of the five Rashtriya Military Schools — Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum or Bengaluru — the countdown has begun in earnest: **21 days** remain until the regular application deadline of **20 October 2026**, and **75 days** until the exam on **13 December 2026**.

Here is everything a parent needs to know, in one place.

## The dates that matter

![Students writing an entrance examination in a hall](/images/inbody/rms-cet-2026-21-days-left-checklist-2026-09-29.webp)

| Event | Date (CONFIRMED) |
|---|---|
| Application window opens | 23 September 2026 |
| Regular last date (form) | **20 October 2026, up to 5:00 pm** |
| Fee payment last date | **20 October 2026, up to 11:50 pm** |
| Late-fee window | **21–25 October 2026** (₹500 late fee) |
| Correction window | **27–28 October 2026** (EXPECTED — reported by exam listing sites; confirm in the Information Bulletin) |
| Exam date | **13 December 2026 (Sunday)** |

The late-fee window is a genuine safety net, not a second chance to be casual about: apply now, because portal congestion on the last day is the single most common reason applications get rejected or left unpaid.

## Who can apply

RMS CET 2026 admits students to **Class 6 and Class 9** in the five Rashtriya Military Schools, which are English-medium residential schools run under the Ministry of Defence.

**Age limits (as on 31 March 2027)** — check your child's date of birth before anything else:

- **Class 6:** 10 to 13 years — born **1 April 2014 to 31 March 2017** (both dates inclusive)
- **Class 9:** 13 to 16 years — born **1 April 2011 to 31 March 2014** (both dates inclusive)

A six-month relaxation in the upper age limit applies to wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action.

**Academic eligibility:** For Class 6, the child must have passed Class 5, or be studying in Class 5 now and pass it before the date of admission. For Class 9, the child must have passed Class 8 from a government or recognised school. Since English is the medium of instruction, a working knowledge of English is preferred for Class 9 applicants.

## Application fee (CONFIRMED)

- **General / OBC / OBC-NCL:** ₹1,000
- **SC / ST / wards of personnel killed in action (KIA):** ₹750
- **Late fee (21–25 October):** ₹500 extra

Payment is online only — credit/debit card, net banking or UPI. Keep the transaction proof safe; a surprising number of parents discover a failed payment only after the window closes.

## Exam pattern (CONFIRMED)

- **Mode:** Pen-and-paper, OMR-based
- **Question type:** Multiple-choice questions (MCQs)
- **Exam cities:** 63 across India (check the Information Bulletin for the full list)

Candidates apply through the official NTA RMS CET portal at `exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet`. Do not trust any third-party "apply here" link that asks for money beyond the official fee.

## What to do this week

1. **Verify the date of birth** against the age cut-offs above — this is where ineligible applications die.
2. **Register today**, even if the form is not complete; a registered account beats a last-minute rush.
3. **Scan documents now** — photograph, signature and certificates in the specified sizes (check the Information Bulletin for exact dimensions).
4. **Read the Information Bulletin** end-to-end once. The 20-minute read saves days of confusion later.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**
20 October 2026, up to 5 pm for the form and 11:50 pm for fee payment. A late-fee window runs 21 to 25 October 2026 with a ₹500 late fee.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 application fee?**
₹1,000 for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates; ₹750 for SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action. A ₹500 late fee applies in the 21–25 October window.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**
13 December 2026, Sunday — a pen-and-paper, OMR-based test with multiple-choice questions, for admission to Classes 6 and 9 in the five Rashtriya Military Schools.

**What is the age limit for RMS CET 2026 Class 6 admission?**
10 to 13 years as on 31 March 2027 — born between 1 April 2014 and 31 March 2017, both dates inclusive. Class 9 is 13 to 16 years (born 1 April 2011 to 31 March 2014).
