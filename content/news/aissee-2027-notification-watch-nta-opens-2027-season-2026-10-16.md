## NTA's 2027 season has officially begun

The National Testing Agency flipped the switch on its 2027 examination season this week. On **Tuesday, 13 October 2026**, NTA released the **NEET UG 2027 notification** — the application window opened the same day at neet.nta.nic.in, and the last date to apply is **15 November 2026**. On the very same day, NTA also released the **CMAT 2027 notification**, opening registrations for the management entrance at exams.nta.ac.in/CMAT.

Two major national exams notified in a single day. That is the clearest signal yet that NTA's exam machinery for 2027 is fully in motion — and it puts every Sainik School parent's favourite question squarely back on the table: **where is the AISSEE 2027 notification?**

## Where AISSEE 2027 stands right now

As of this morning, the answer is unchanged: the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin has not been released. NTA's AISSEE portal is still carrying the previous cycle's content, and there is no confirmed application schedule. We have been tracking this on our [notification watch page](/news/nta-portal-no-aissee-2027-link-evening-check-2026-10-09/) — one portal after another keeps reporting the same status: expected, but not announced.

What we do know with confidence:

- **Exam date (tentative):** NTA's 2027 exam calendar lists AISSEE 2027 on **31 January 2027**. The calendar also keeps 31 January as a buffer day for JEE Main Session 1, so small shifts are possible — but 31 January is the date to plan around.
- **Last year's timeline:** The AISSEE 2026 form opened on **10 October 2025** and closed on 9 November 2025; the exam was held on **19 January 2026**. That rhythm is why most education portals place the AISSEE 2027 form in the **October–November 2026** window.
- **The gap:** AISSEE 2026's form opened on 10 October 2025. As of 16 October 2026, there is no AISSEE 2027 form. The longer the wait stretches into late October, the tighter the window between application and the tentative January exam becomes.

None of this is a prediction from us — it is the arithmetic of the calendar. A January exam with a month-long application window means the notification cannot be delayed much beyond the next few weeks without compressing the preparation timeline further.

## Why this matters more than it looks

Every year, parents discover during the form-filling stage that a name on the birth certificate does not match the school record, or that a category certificate is expired, or that the photograph fails the upload specifications. Those discoveries cost days — and the NTA correction window is typically only a few days long. For a full check of what a mismatched document costs, read our piece on [how form rejections actually happen](/news/up-sainik-school-form-rejection-checks-2026-10-10/), and keep your [category and certificate validity](/news/obc-ncl-ews-certificate-validity-aissee-2027-2026-10-11/) in order.

## What to keep ready this weekend

Do not wait for the notification to do this. If any of the following is wrong, fixing it takes weeks — not days:

1. **Birth certificate:** the child's name, date of birth and father's name must match the school records and Aadhaar. If they don't, start the correction process at the Registrar of Births and Deaths **this week**.
2. **Photograph and signature:** keep a recent passport-size photo (white background) and the child's signature scanned, following the file-size norms NTA has used in past cycles.
3. **Category certificate:** if you plan to claim OBC-NCL, SC, ST or EWS, confirm the certificate is valid for the 2026–27 financial year.
4. **Mobile number and email:** use an active, dedicated email ID — NTA communicates almost entirely by email and SMS, and you cannot change these mid-cycle.

The notification, when it lands, will arrive on exams.nta.ac.in — not on WhatsApp forwards. Check the official portal directly, and beware of fake "application started" messages doing the rounds in parent groups.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No. As of 16 October 2026, NTA has not released the AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or application schedule. NEET 2027 and CMAT 2027 notifications came out on 13 October 2026, but the Sainik School form follows its own timeline and is still awaited.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 exam expected?**
NTA's official 2027 examination calendar places AISSEE 2027 on 31 January 2027, listed as tentative. The calendar also marks 31 January as a buffer date for JEE Main, so treat it as the working date while waiting for the official AISSEE bulletin to confirm.

**When did AISSEE 2026 registration open last year?**
The AISSEE 2026 application window opened on 10 October 2025 and closed on 9 November 2025, with the exam held on 19 January 2026. That pattern is why most portals expect the AISSEE 2027 form in October–November 2026, though nothing is confirmed until NTA announces it.

**What should parents do while waiting for the AISSEE 2027 form?**
Get every document scan-ready now: birth certificate, domicile and category certificates if applicable, a recent photograph and signature in the usual NTA specifications, and the child's Aadhaar details. Fix any name or date-of-birth mismatches between documents immediately, because the correction window after the form opens is short.
