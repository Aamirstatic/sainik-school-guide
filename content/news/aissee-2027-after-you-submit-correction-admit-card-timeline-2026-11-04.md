## Submitting the form is the halfway mark, not the finish line

Thousands of families have already filled the AISSEE 2027 form since the National Testing Agency (NTA) opened applications on 3 November 2026. If yours is one of them, it is tempting to relax until January. Don't — not quite yet. Four milestones sit between your submission and the exam hall, and two of them need action from you: the correction window and the admit card download. Miss either, and a perfectly good application can still cause trouble on exam day.

## The full post-submission timeline

| Milestone | Date | What you must do |
|---|---|---|
| Regular application window closes | 2 December 2026, 11:50 PM | Apply and pay the fee before this — not on it |
| Late application window | 3–6 December 2026 | Only if you missed 2 December; costs an extra Rs 500 |
| Correction window | 7–9 December 2026 | Fix permitted details; your only editing chance |
| Exam city announcement | 11 January 2027 | Check which city you have been allotted |
| Admit card download | 25 January 2027 | Download, print and verify every detail |
| **Examination** | **31 January 2027 (Sunday)** | Pen-and-paper, OMR-based, Class 6 and Class 9 |
| Result | To be announced later | Watch the official portal only |

## The correction window (7–9 December): your one editing chance

The correction window opens the day after the late window closes, and it runs for just three days. This is the only opportunity NTA gives you to fix details in a submitted form — so treat it as a deadline, not a reminder.

When it opens, log in and check the form against your documents, field by field: the child's name and date of birth against the birth certificate, the school and class details, the category and reservation claim, and the uploaded photograph and signature. If something is wrong, correct it inside the window and save the updated confirmation. If everything matches, leave it alone — unnecessary edits in a hurry are how correct forms become incorrect ones.

## Exam city (11 January) and admit card (25 January): plan travel early

On 11 January 2027, NTA will announce the exam city allotted to each candidate. That tells you where you are going, but not the exact centre — that comes with the admit card on 25 January, six days before the exam.

If your allotted city is far from home, use the 11 January announcement to start planning travel, and use the admit card to confirm the centre address and reporting time. If anything on the admit card looks wrong — a blurred photograph, a misspelt name — contact NTA immediately through the helpline (1800-180-4747) or aissee@nta.ac.in, rather than hoping it will be sorted at the gate.

## The result: no date yet, and that is normal

NTA has not announced a result date for AISSEE 2027. That is not a delay — it is simply how the schedule works at this stage. Ignore social-media posts claiming a result date: they are guesses.

## Your post-submission checklist

1. **Save the confirmation page now.** Keep a copy with your documents — you will need it through admit card, exam and counselling.
2. **Diarise 7–9 December.** Set a reminder for the correction window the day it opens, not the day it closes.
3. **Diarise 11 and 25 January.** Exam city first, then admit card — check both on the day, not the week after.
4. **Use the wait to prepare.** The exam is 31 January 2027. The form is done; the preparation is the part that still moves the score.

## FAQs

**When is the AISSEE 2027 correction window?**
From 7 to 9 December 2026. That is the only chance to fix permitted details in your submitted form, so check every field against your documents before the window closes.

**When will I know my AISSEE 2027 exam city?**
On 11 January 2027, when NTA announces the allotted exam city. The admit card, with the exact centre address, follows on 25 January 2027.

**When is the AISSEE 2027 admit card released?**
On 25 January 2027, six days before the exam. Download it from the official NTA AISSEE portal and check your photograph, signature and centre details the same day.

**Has the AISSEE 2027 result date been announced?**
No. NTA has not announced a result date yet. The result will be declared later on the official website, after the 31 January 2027 exam.
