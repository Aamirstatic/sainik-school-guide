> **Last verified: 15 October 2026 (Thursday, ~11 AM IST).** All dates, fees and school details below are CONFIRMED from the NTA announcement as reported independently by the Education Times and Moneycontrol this week: applications close **20 October 2026 at 5 PM**, fee payment runs to **11:50 PM** the same day, the late window is **21–25 October** with a **Rs 500** late fee, and the exam is on **13 December 2026**. This is a **deadline reminder**, not a fresh announcement — the registration window opened on 23 September 2026. Apply only through the official NTA RMS CET portal.

## Five days, five schools, two deadlines — what you are racing

The Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026 application window enters its final five days today. The headline date is **20 October at 5 PM** — but there are actually two deadlines, and confusing them is the single most common way families end up in the late-fee window.

| Deadline | Time | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Application submission | 20 Oct, 5:00 PM | Form + document upload |
| Exam fee payment | 20 Oct, 11:50 PM | ₹1,000 (Gen/OBC/OBC-NCL) or ₹750 (SC/ST/KIA wards) |
| Late window (extra Rs 500) | 21–25 Oct | Both form and fee |

That 5 PM / 11:50 PM split matters: you can still pay after the form deadline passes on the final day, but you cannot submit a fresh form after 5 PM. If you are reading this on the 20th evening and haven't submitted the form yet, the late window (21–25 October, Rs 500 extra) is your only option.

## Five schools, five checks

The Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test is the gateway to the five RMS schools — **Chail (Himachal Pradesh), Ajmer (Rajasthan), Dholpur (Rajasthan), Belgaum (Karnataka) and Bengaluru (Karnataka)** — English-medium residential public schools run by the Ministry of Defence, from Class 6 to Class 12. The exam is pen-and-paper, OMR-based, all multiple-choice questions, across **63 cities**. NTA handles the application, the written test and the Stage 1 result; the schools take over the later selection stages.

Five checks most applicants still get wrong:

**1. The age bands are exact to the day.** Class 6: 10–13 years as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2014 – 31 March 2017, inclusive). Class 9: 13–16 years (born 1 April 2011 – 31 March 2014). A birthday one day outside the window means rejection — no exceptions.

**2. Class 9 applicants need a recognised school.** Class 6 needs a Class 5 pass (or about to pass). Class 9 needs Class 8 from a government or recognised school — and English familiarity helps, since English is the medium of instruction.

**3. Don't mix up the two exams.** Many families apply for both RMS CET and AISSEE in the same season — perfectly legitimate, and they are 49 days apart (13 Dec 2026 vs 31 Jan 2027). But keep two separate records: application numbers, fee receipts and exam cities, so you don't pick the wrong class on the wrong form.

**4. The Rs 500 late fee is real money.** The 21–25 October window exists so last-minute applicants aren't shut out, but it costs Rs 500 on top of the normal fee. No correction window has been announced — fill the form carefully the first time.

**5. Read the NTA notice, not the summaries.** Portals (including ours) report the NTA announcement; the official notice on the NTA RMS CET portal is the authority on photo specs, documents and the fee-payment flow.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**
Applications close on 20 October 2026 at 5:00 PM. The exam fee can still be paid until 11:50 PM the same day. A late window runs 21 to 25 October with a Rs 500 late fee.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**
13 December 2026, a Sunday. It is a pen-and-paper, OMR-based MCQ test conducted across 63 cities in India.

**Which schools admit through RMS CET 2026?**
Five Rashtriya Military Schools: Chail in Himachal Pradesh, Ajmer and Dholpur in Rajasthan, Belgaum and Bengaluru in Karnataka — all English-medium residential schools under the Ministry of Defence.

**Can a child apply for both RMS CET and AISSEE 2027?**
Yes. The two exams are 49 days apart — RMS CET on 13 December 2026 and AISSEE on 31 January 2027 — and NTA conducts both. Eligibility overlaps heavily for Class 6 and Class 9 applicants.
