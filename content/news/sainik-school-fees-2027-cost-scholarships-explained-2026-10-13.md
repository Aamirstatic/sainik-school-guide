> **Last verified: 13 October 2026.** This is an explainer, not breaking news: the AISSEE 2027 notification is still **not released** (EXPECTED Oct–Nov 2026). The fee figures below are CONFIRMED from official school fee notices for 2026-27, opened and read today. They are the best guide to 2027-cycle costs until schools publish fresh notices — fees vary by school and rise every year.

With the AISSEE 2027 application window expected to open this month, parents usually ask one question after "what is the exam date": *what will it actually cost me if my child gets in?* The honest answer: a Sainik School seat costs real money — but less than it looks at first, and there are scholarships most families never hear about until it is too late.

![A mother helping her child study at home for the Sainik School entrance exam](/images/inbody/sainik-school-fees-2027-cost-scholarships-explained-2026-10-13.webp)

## The headline number: roughly ₹1.8–2.1 lakh a year (CONFIRMED, 2026-27 notices)

Take **Sainik School Korukonda** (Andhra Pradesh), whose official "New Admissions Fee Structure for the Year 2026-27" is published on the school website. For a General/OBC/Defence-category cadet, the table totals **₹2,06,375**, with advance payments for books, horse-riding and the like taking the amount payable to **about ₹2,14,000** (SC/ST families pay marginally less: about ₹2,12,500). Tuition alone is ₹1,28,625; the rest is diet charges (₹44,250 — computed at ₹150 × 295 days), clothing and washing (₹26,000), pocket money (₹3,000), incidental charges and a security deposit.

Two independent data points confirm the range. **Sainik School Kapurthala** (Punjab) lists an annual fee of **about ₹1,82,143** — tuition ₹1,16,922, diet ₹41,300, clothing, pocket money, horse-riding ₹5,400 and incidental charges. The **New Sainik School at Amritsar** (a PPP-model school) publishes a 2025-26 structure totalling **₹2,00,000**: a one-time ₹10,000 admission charge, ₹80,000 annual dues and tuition, ₹50,000 hostel, ₹45,000 diet, ₹15,000 kitting, ₹5,000 pocket money and a ₹5,000 refundable caution deposit, paid in two instalments.

Across schools, third-party compilations put the 2025-26 range from about **₹1,16,924** (Amaravathinagar) to **₹2,26,881** (Imphal). The takeaway: budget **₹2 lakh a year** for a mid-tier estimate, then adjust once your allotted school publishes its notice.

## The part parents miss: fees rise 10% every year (CONFIRMED)

Korukonda's notice carries a line worth reading twice: *"There will be 10% hike on Tuition Fee every year as per direction of Sainik Schools Society."* So do not budget six years of Class 6 at the admission-year figure. A ₹1,28,625 tuition becomes roughly ₹1,41,500 in year two and ₹1,55,700 in year three, compounding. Ask the school office for the projected six-year schedule at the time of admission — it is a reasonable question, and schools are used to it.

## The part parents should not miss: scholarships (CONFIRMED, official schemes)

**1. Ministry of Defence defence scholarships.** The MoD pays a full scholarship of **₹32,000 a year** to children of NCOs and Other Ranks, and a half scholarship of **₹16,000** to children of JCOs. This is published in the Sainik Schools' own scholarship pages (Imphal's, for instance), alongside an MoD "sharing of burden" contribution of roughly **₹11,000–12,000 a year** toward enhanced fees and diet charges for each cadet.

**2. State government scholarships.** Many states run their own schemes on top. Manipur's page shows the pattern: merit scholarships covering 50–100% of tuition and diet charges, scaled to marks in the annual exam or the AISSEE itself, with relaxed thresholds for SC/ST/OBC cadets.

**3. SC/ST concessions.** Beyond the defence scholarships, SC/ST candidates pay reduced security deposits and the AISSEE application fee is lower (₹700 vs ₹850, expected for 2027). Some schools also list fee concessions specifically for SC/ST cadets.

The catch: scholarships are **not automatic**. Admission offices expect families to apply through the school or state portal with income, category and service documents after admission. Put it on your to-do list the week your child joins.

## Two things to get straight this October

First, the **application fee is not the school fee**. Paying ₹850 to fill the AISSEE 2027 form only buys your child the entrance exam on **31 January 2027** (CONFIRMED tentative, NTA calendar). The annual fee is paid later, in instalments, after e-counselling allots a seat.

## FAQs

**What is the annual fee of a Sainik School for 2027 admission?**
Roughly ₹1.8–2.1 lakh a year at most established schools, per official 2026-27 notices. Expect the 2027-cycle figures to be confirmed after the NTA bulletin releases.

**Do Sainik School fees go up every year?**
Yes — tuition rises 10% annually under the Sainik Schools Society's standing direction. Budget for the rise, not just year one.

**Are there scholarships or fee concessions?**
Yes: MoD defence scholarships (₹32,000 full for NCO/Other-Rank wards, ₹16,000 half for JCO wards), MoD fee-burden sharing of about ₹11,000–12,000 a year per cadet, state merit/income scholarships, and SC/ST concessions. Apply after admission; they are not automatic.

**Is the AISSEE application fee the same as the school fee?**
No. The ₹850/₹700 application fee (expected for 2027) only covers the entrance exam. The school fee of about ₹2 lakh a year is paid in instalments after your child secures a seat.
