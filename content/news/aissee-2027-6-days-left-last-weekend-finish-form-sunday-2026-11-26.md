Six days. That is what is left for the AISSEE 2027 form, and this weekend is the last full one before the deadline. The form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM** — and the fee must be paid by the same hour, or the form does not count as submitted.

Here is the thing most families miss: the last two days of a deadline are the worst days to fill a form. Servers slow down, payment gateways fail, and a rejected photograph at 11 PM leaves no time to fix it.

## Saturday 28 November: JNVST day comes first

Saturday belongs to the JNVST children. The Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test for Class 6 runs on **Saturday, 28 November, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM**, in offline mode at centres across the country. Children must be seated by **11:00 AM** — latecomers are not allowed in. Carry a printed admit card, an Aadhaar card or residence certificate, and a blue or black ballpoint pen. The paper is 80 questions, 100 marks, two hours, with no negative marking.

If your child is sitting JNVST on Saturday, do not touch the AISSEE form that morning. Get the exam done, come home, rest. Then, in the evening, do one quiet job: gather the AISSEE documents and check the file sizes — photo 10 to 200 KB, signature and thumb impression 10 to 50 KB each, certificates 50 to 300 KB as PDF. A wrong-size file is the most common reason a form stalls, and it takes five minutes to fix on Saturday evening and five hours of panic on Wednesday night.

If your child is not sitting JNVST, Saturday is your form day — fill it, pay, and download the confirmation page the same evening.

## Sunday 29 November: finish it, whatever it takes

Sunday is the deadline you set for yourself. Not 2 December — **Sunday, 29 November**. Sit down after lunch, fill the form end to end, pay the fee, and download the confirmation page. Then check three things before you close the laptop: the child's name is spelt exactly as on the birth certificate, the date of birth matches, and the school preferences are in the order you actually want.

Why Sunday and not Monday? Because Monday to Wednesday is your safety net, not your plan. If the payment fails on Sunday, you have three days to retry. If you fill the form on Wednesday and anything goes wrong, there is no time left — only the late-fee window, which costs an extra ₹500 and, as our [late-fee warning](/news/aissee-2027-6-days-left-late-fee-window-risks-2026-11-26/) explains, brings its own risks.

## Monday to Wednesday: the safety net, not the plan

If the form is already submitted by Sunday, Monday 30 November to Wednesday 2 December is for one job only: re-read the confirmation page calmly and keep it safe. Do not re-open the form to tinker, and do not change school preferences at the last minute on a hunch.

If the form is still not done by Monday morning, treat Monday as the last real day. Fill it before evening, pay before night, and download the confirmation page. Tuesday is for fixing whatever Monday broke. Wednesday is the day the portal closes at 11:50 PM — plan to be nowhere near it by then.

## What happens after 2 December

The late window runs from **3 to 6 December** with an extra ₹500. The correction window runs from **7 to 9 December**, for permitted changes only. The exam city is announced on **11 January 2027**, admit cards come on **25 January**, and the exam itself is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**.

But none of that matters if the form is not in. Six days is plenty — if the documents are ready today and the form is finished this weekend. Sunday night, confirmation page in hand. That is the whole plan.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. The application fee of Rs 850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates must also be paid by the same time.

**When is the JNVST 2027 Class 6 exam?**

Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, in offline mode. Children must be seated in the exam room by 11:00 AM and must carry a printed admit card, an Aadhaar card or residence certificate, and a blue or black ballpoint pen.

**Can I still apply for AISSEE 2027 after 2 December?**

Yes, in the late-fee window from 3 to 6 December 2026, by paying an extra Rs 500 over the regular fee. The last date for fee payment with the late fee is 6 December at 11:50 PM. After that, there is no way to apply.

**What documents should be ready before I start the AISSEE form?**

The child's photograph (10 to 200 KB, JPG), signature (10 to 50 KB, JPG), left thumb impression (10 to 50 KB, JPG), and birth certificate, domicile certificate and category or defence certificate (50 to 300 KB, PDF). Keep them scanned and checked before you sit down to fill the form.
