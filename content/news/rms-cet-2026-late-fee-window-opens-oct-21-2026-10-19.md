## Missed today's 5 PM deadline? You have five more days

The regular RMS CET 2026 window — the NTA entrance test for Classes 6 and 9 at India's five Rashtriya Military Schools — closed today, 20 October, at 5:00 PM. If you missed it, a late-fee window opens **tomorrow, 21 October**, running through **25 October (5:00 PM)**.

This is the second and final chance for the 2027-28 session. See [our deadline mechanics piece](/news/rms-cet-2026-final-48-hours-deadline-mechanics-2026-10-18/) for how the closing hours played out.

## What you pay in the late window

Late fee: a flat **₹500** on top of your category's regular fee. Pay online — the form isn't complete until payment goes through. Payment deadline: **11:50 PM, 25 October**.

| Category | Regular fee | Late-window total |
|---|---|---|
| General / OBC / OBC-NCL | ₹1,000 | ₹1,500 |
| SC / ST | ₹750 | ₹1,250 |
| Wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action (KIA) | ₹750 | ₹1,250 |

## Who can still apply: age rules

Age is measured **as on 31 March 2027** — both limits are **CONFIRMED**:

| Class | Age on 31 March 2027 | Born between |
|---|---|---|
| VI | 10–13 years | 1 April 2014 – 31 March 2017 |
| IX | 13–16 years | 1 April 2011 – 31 March 2014 |

Wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action get a **six-month upper-age relaxation**. Check the birth certificate against these dates first — age mismatch can't be fixed after submission.

## How to apply, step by step

1. Go to the official portal: **exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet** — only this address accepts applications.
2. Register with the child's name, a valid email ID and mobile number — OTPs and the admit card notice go here.
3. Fill the form — personal details, class applying for, choice of exam city (63 cities available).
4. Upload photograph, signature and certificates in the specified format — blurry scans get forms held up.
5. Pay the fee (regular category fee + ₹500 late fee) before **11:50 PM on 25 October**.
6. Download and save the confirmation page. Print one copy for document verification later.

## Prepare these tonight

Gather these before the window opens: birth certificate, recent passport-size photograph, signature scan, caste/category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL) if claiming the concession, and the KIA service certificate where applicable.

## 55 days to exam day

The exam is on **13 December 2026** (Sunday), a pen-and-paper OMR test with MCQs — **55 days from tonight**, enough for one solid revision cycle if preparation starts the moment the form is filed. ## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026 with the late fee?**

The late-fee window runs from 21 October to 25 October 2026. Submit by 5:00 PM on 25 October, and pay the fee (category fee plus Rs 500 late fee) by 11:50 PM the same night.

**How much is the RMS CET 2026 late fee?**

The late fee is Rs 500, added to the category fee. In the late window, General/OBC candidates pay Rs 1,500 and SC/ST or wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action pay Rs 1,250.

**Can I still apply if I missed the regular RMS CET 2026 deadline?**

Yes — the late-fee window runs 21 to 25 October for Class VI and IX admissions to the five Rashtriya Military Schools. Apply at exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet before 25 October, 5 PM.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**

The exam is on 13 December 2026, a Sunday — a pen-and-paper OMR test with MCQs, held in 63 exam cities across India for the 2027-28 session.
