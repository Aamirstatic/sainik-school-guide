## Seven days is plenty — if you stop treating the form as one job

Here is the honest picture. The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. That is seven days from today. And yet every year, a chunk of parents end up paying the ₹500 late fee — or worse, missing the form entirely — not because the form is hard, but because they treated it as one big job to do "sometime this week" and then life happened.

So stop treating it as one job. It is five small jobs, and each one takes less than an hour. Do one a day, and you will be done by Friday — five days early, with the whole weekend as buffer.

## The plan, day by day

| Day | Do this | Time needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Wed, 25 Nov (today)** | Gather the documents: birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate if any, the child's Class 5 (or Class 8) school details, and a scanned photo and signature in the sizes the bulletin asks for | 30–45 min |
| **Thu, 26 Nov** | Fill the form — registration, personal details, school preferences. Save, do not submit yet. Read every field back once | 45 min |
| **Fri, 27 Nov** | **Submit the form and pay the fee.** This is your real deadline, not 2 December | 20 min |
| **Sat, 28 Nov** | Download and save the confirmation page. Check the application number, the child's name spelling, and the school choices on it | 15 min |
| **Sun, 29 Nov – Tue, 1 Dec** | Buffer. If anything went wrong — a failed payment, a wrong photo — this is when you fix it, calmly | As needed |
| **Wed, 2 Dec, 11:50 PM** | The real last date. If you followed this plan, you are already done and watching other parents panic | — |

One rule inside the plan: **the fee is part of the form, not an afterthought.** A form submitted without a successful fee payment is not a submitted form. Pay on Friday, and check that the payment shows as successful on the confirmation page — not just that money left your account. If a payment fails, retry the same day, not the next one. We explained this trap in detail in our [fee-deadline myth-buster](/news/aissee-2027-fee-deadline-2-december-not-3-december-2026-11-24/): several portals print 3 December as the fee date, and trusting them costs ₹500 — or the form.

## Why Friday, 27 November, and not 2 December

Because the last day of any national exam form is the worst day to be on the portal. Servers slow down, payment gateways time out, and the NTA helpline is at its busiest. A payment that fails at 11 PM on 2 December cannot be fixed at 11:50 PM. A payment that fails on Friday morning can be retried on Friday afternoon. That is the whole logic: move your deadline five days earlier and the risk disappears.

And if something genuinely goes wrong and you do miss 2 December, you are not finished. The late-fee window runs **3 to 6 December** with an extra ₹500, and the correction window runs **7 to 9 December**. But treat those as rescue boats, not as the plan. The plan is Friday.

## After the form: what happens next

Once the form is in, the next dates are fixed. The exam city is announced on **11 January 2027**, the admit card comes on **25 January 2027**, and the exam itself is on **Sunday, 31 January 2027**. We laid out the full post-2-December timeline — late window, correction window, city slip, admit card, exam day — in our [after-2-December guide](/news/aissee-2027-after-2-december-what-happens-next-timeline-2026-11-24/). For now, though, only one date matters: Friday, 27 November. Finish the form, save the confirmation page, and then get back to the preparation. The exam is 67 days away, and that is where the real work is.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**
Wednesday, 2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. The fee must also be paid by the same time — a form with an unpaid fee is not a submitted form.

**What happens if I miss the 2 December deadline?**
You can still apply in the late-fee window from 3 to 6 December 2026 by paying an extra ₹500 over the regular fee. After 6 December at 11:50 PM, there is no way to apply for AISSEE 2027.

**How much is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**
₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and ₹700 for SC and ST candidates. In the late window, add ₹500 — so ₹1,350 or ₹1,200.

**Should I submit the AISSEE form on 2 December itself?**
No. Aim to submit by Friday, 27 November. The portal slows down and payment gateways fail on the last day, and a failed payment at 11 PM on 2 December leaves you no time to fix it.
