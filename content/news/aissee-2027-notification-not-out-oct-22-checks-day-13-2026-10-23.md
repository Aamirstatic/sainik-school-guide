## Two independent checks, one week apart, same answer

AISSEE 2027 is now **13 days past** the date NTA released the 2026 notification (10 October 2025). And the answer is still the same: nothing official.

EntranceZone refreshed its AISSEE 2027 tracker on **22 October 2026**, and its verdict is blunt: the cycle "remains in the pre-notification stage, with no official application form or dates released yet by the National Testing Agency." It tells students, plainly, to "treat the cycle as pending and avoid relying on any unconfirmed date."

A day earlier, CollegeDunia updated its AISSEE page (21 October 2026) with the same finding from its own check: as of **16 October 2026**, no AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or registration link was listed on the official NTA AISSEE portal.

This matches our own [portal fact-check of 21 October](/news/aissee-2027-notification-fact-check-nta-portal-no-bulletin-2026-10-21/). Three sources, three dates, one ground truth.

## The contradiction that keeps tripping parents

One well-known education page is *still* carrying the claim that the AISSEE 2027 notification "was released on 13 October 2026" in one article, while its other articles call the notification "Soon". EntranceZone's 22 October check directly contradicts the 13 October claim — the cycle "remains unpublished" as of yesterday.

The rule that never fails: if the bulletin isn't on **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**, the notification isn't out. Everything else is noise. Bookmark that portal page and check it yourself; no third-party headline can replace it.

## What the calendar still gives you: a 100-day finish line

The one fixed point remains NTA's own calendar: AISSEE 2027 is listed on **31 January 2027**. Counted from today, that is exactly **100 days**. Our [101-day countdown piece](/news/aissee-2027-january-31-nta-calendar-101-day-countdown-2026-10-22/) mapped the reverse timeline — the logic still holds: work backwards from the exam date, not forwards from a notification that hasn't arrived.

The 2026 cycle shows the pace NTA keeps once the bulletin drops: 10 October 2025 notification → 18 January 2026 exam → 27 February 2026 result, with e-counselling (AISSAC) after that. 🔶 EXPECTED: the 2027 cycle follows a similar shape — notification October/November 2026, form fee around ₹850 (General/OBC/Defence) and ₹700 (SC/ST), correction window after the deadline, admit cards a couple of weeks before the exam.

## While you wait: keep these documents ready

The one productive use of this delay is document readiness. Based on the file specs previous NTA bulletins required (🔶 EXPECTED for 2027 — treat as guidance, confirm against the actual bulletin):

| Document | Typical spec |
|---|---|
| Candidate photo | White background, JPG, 10 KB – 200 KB |
| Signature | Running handwriting, JPG, 4 KB – 30 KB |
| Left-hand thumb impression | Blue ink on white paper, JPG, 10 KB – 50 KB |
| Birth / caste / domicile certificates | JPG, 100 KB – 500 KB |

The same passport-size photo is uploaded in the form *and* pasted on the exam-day attendance sheet — use one recent photograph for both. The moment the bulletin drops, NTA gives roughly three weeks; the families who struggle are the ones hunting for a thumb impression scan at the last hour.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**
No — as of 22 October 2026, the cycle is still pre-notification. No bulletin, no form, no official dates.

**Why is it late this year?**
Nobody outside NTA knows. The 2026 notification came on 10 October 2025; the 2027 one is now 13 days past that mark with no announcement. Treat any "expected this week" claim as rumour until the portal itself updates.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 exam date?**
31 January 2027 per NTA's official examination calendar — confirmed on the calendar, tentative until the bulletin locks it in.

**What should I check daily?**
Only one page: exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. When the "Information Bulletin" link for AISSEE 2027 appears there, apply within the window. Nothing else counts as an announcement.
