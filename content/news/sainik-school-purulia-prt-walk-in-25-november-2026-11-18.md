If you teach primary classes and hold a D.El.Ed, there is a walk-in interview a week from today — no online form, no written test first, just turn up with your certificates.

Sainik Shiksha Niketan, Purulia, has announced **4 posts of Primary Teacher (PRT)** on a contractual basis. The walk-in interview is on **Tuesday, 25 November 2026, at 8:00 AM**, at Sainik School Purulia on Ranchi Road, NH-32, Purulia, West Bengal.

## The post at a glance

| Detail | What the notice says |
|---|---|
| Post | Primary Teacher (PRT) — 4 posts, all unreserved |
| Appointment | Contractual basis (not a permanent post) |
| Salary | ₹16,000 a month, consolidated |
| Walk-in date | 25 November 2026, reporting at 8:00 AM |
| Venue | Sainik School Purulia, Ranchi Road, NH-32, PO: Sainik School — 723104, Dist. Purulia (WB) |
| Age limit | 21 to 45 years as on 1 November 2026 |
| Application fee | None mentioned in the notice |

## Who can attend

The essential qualifications are straightforward, and worth checking honestly before you make the trip:

- **Graduation**, with at least **50% marks in Higher Secondary**.
- **D.El.Ed** from an institution recognised by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE).
- **Proficiency in writing and teaching in English** — this is an essential condition, not a preference.

A B.Ed, knowledge of computers, teaching experience, and proficiency in games, sports, NCC and co-curricular activities are listed as **desirable** — they will help at the interview, but the notice does not make them compulsory. The notice does not mention any age relaxation.

## What to carry on 25 November

Candidates must attend with the **filled application** in the format printed in the notice (addressed to the Chairman, Sainik Shiksha Niketan, Purulia), plus:

- Educational certificates, in original, with attested copies
- Bio-data
- Work experience certificates, if any

The notice also asks for details that are easy to forget — Aadhaar number, two references with contact numbers, and a signed declaration about any disciplinary, vigilance or criminal case. Fill the form at home the night before; do not try to complete it at the gate at 8 AM.

Two practical warnings from the notice itself: **no TA or DA** will be paid for attending, and the school administration **reserves the right to cancel the vacancy or change the terms** for administrative reasons. If you are travelling from outside Purulia district, keep that in mind.

## Don't confuse this with the other Purulia walk-in

Purulia has had more than one recruitment notice this month. This PRT walk-in on **25 November** is a separate notice from the Sainik School Purulia walk-in for TGT (Social Science), TGT (Hindi) and Art Master posts on **23 November** — different posts, different qualifications, different day. Check which notice matches your qualification before you travel, and carry the right application format for the right post.

## Why this matters

Four contractual primary-teacher posts will not make headlines. But for a trained primary teacher in West Bengal looking for work inside a residential school system, a walk-in is the shortest route there is — no waiting for a written-test admit card, no months-long selection calendar. The trade-off is equally plain: ₹16,000 a month on contract is modest pay, and the post carries no claim to a permanent appointment. Go in with your eyes open, your certificates in order, and a clear sense of what the job actually offers.
