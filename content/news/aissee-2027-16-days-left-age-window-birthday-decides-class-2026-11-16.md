## Sixteen days left — and one date matters more than all the rest

The AISSEE 2027 form closes at **11:50 PM on Wednesday, 2 December 2026**. Sixteen days. In that time, parents will worry about the photograph size, the fee payment and the exam-city choices — and all of those matter. But there is one detail that decides everything before any of them: your child's date of birth.

Age is the only eligibility rule in AISSEE with no flexibility at all. A wrong photo can be re-uploaded. A failed payment can be retried. A wrong birth date cannot be argued with — the computer checks it against the window, and the window does not move.

## The two windows — check yours now

| Class | Age on 31 March 2027 | Date of birth (both days inclusive) |
|---|---|---|
| Class 6 | 10 to 12 years | **1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017** |
| Class 9 | 13 to 15 years | **1 April 2012 – 31 March 2014** |

That is the whole rule. And here is the part parents of March and April babies need to read twice — the boundary dates are inclusive, and one day either way changes everything:

| Your child's birthday | Class 6 | Class 9 |
|---|---|---|
| 31 March 2017 | ✅ Eligible (youngest possible) | ❌ Too young |
| 1 April 2017 | ❌ Too young by one day | ❌ Too young |
| 1 April 2015 | ✅ Eligible (oldest possible) | ❌ Too old |
| 31 March 2015 | ❌ Too old by one day | ❌ Too old |
| 31 March 2014 | ❌ Too old | ✅ Eligible (youngest possible) |
| 1 April 2014 | ❌ Too old | ❌ Too young by one day |
| 1 April 2012 | ❌ Too old | ✅ Eligible (oldest possible) |

A child born on 1 April 2017 misses Class 6 by a single day. There is no rounding, no "almost 10", no discretion at the school gate. The age is counted as on **31 March 2027**, not on the exam date, not on the admission date — 31 March 2027, full stop.

## No relaxation — for anyone

This is the part that surprises most families. The bulletin gives **no age relaxation for any category** — not SC, not ST, not OBC-NCL, not wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen. Reservation changes the seat you compete for; it does not change the birthday window. If your child falls outside the window, the category certificate does not help, and the correction window of 7–9 December cannot fix a birth date that is simply outside the range.

Class 9 has one extra condition on top of age: the child must have **passed Class 8 from a recognised school by the time of admission**. A 14-year-old still sitting in Class 8 this year is fine — the pass needs to be complete by admission, not by the form date. But a child who has not yet reached Class 8 at all is not eligible for Class 9, whatever their birthday says.

## The birth certificate is the only proof that counts

Do not fill the date of birth from memory, from the school diary, or from an Aadhaar card that was made in a hurry years ago. Open the **birth certificate issued by the competent authority** and copy the date exactly as printed — day, month, year. That is the document you will upload with the form (as a PDF of 50 to 300 KB), and it is the document NTA will match against at verification.

If the birth certificate and the school records disagree, stop and sort it out **before** you submit. Getting a certificate corrected takes days; the form closes in sixteen. A mismatch discovered at document verification — after the exam, after the merit list — is the cruellest way to lose a seat your child earned.

## FAQs

**What is the age limit for AISSEE 2027 Class 6?**

10 to 12 years as on 31 March 2027 — born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017, both days inclusive. There is no age relaxation for any category.

**What is the age limit for AISSEE 2027 Class 9?**

13 to 15 years as on 31 March 2027 — born between 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014, both days inclusive. The child must also have passed Class 8 from a recognised school by the time of admission.

**Is there any age relaxation for SC, ST, OBC or defence wards in AISSEE 2027?**

No. The bulletin gives no relaxation in the age limit for any category, including SC, ST, OBC and defence wards. The date-of-birth window is the same for everyone.

**What if the date of birth on my child's certificate differs from the school records?**

Enter the date exactly as it appears on the birth certificate issued by the competent authority, and upload that certificate as a PDF of 50 to 300 KB. If the two records disagree, get the certificate corrected before you submit — a mismatch at document verification can cost the seat.
