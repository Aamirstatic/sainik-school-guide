## Today's the Last Day

If your child is studying in (or has passed) Class 7 and you have been putting off the RIMCEE form, this is it. The Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination (RIMCEE) 2026 — the gateway to Class 8 at RIMC Dehradun for the July 2027 term — shuts its late application window today, 7 October 2026.

The regular window closed on 30 September. The late window (1–7 October) carries an additional fee of Rs 500, but it is the last chance to apply for the December cycle. There is no indication of any extension, so treat today as final.

## What to Do in the Next Few Hours

If you are applying today, do it now, not tonight. Servers slow down on last-day evenings, and a failed payment at 11:40 PM is a nightmare nobody needs.

1. **Keep documents ready first:** photograph, date of birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), left-hand thumb impression and the school studying certificate — scan them before you open the form, because a failed upload mid-session can lose progress.
2. **Register and fill carefully:** one-time registration, then personal, parent, domicile, category and education details. Read the Information Bulletin before you begin — it is the final word on eligibility and fees.
3. **Preview before paying:** check the name spelling, date of birth and category — these are the fields that hurt most when wrong.
4. **Pay online and save the confirmation page:** the fee goes through credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI. Keep your registration ID, password and the confirmation page safe — you need them for the admit card.
5. **Apply only through the official NTA portal:** third-party "apply" sites have no role in this process.

## What Comes Next

Once the window closes, the **correction window (9–11 October)** lets you fix mistakes in the submitted form. After that, the countdown is 60 days to the **6 December written exam** — Mathematics and English in descriptive format, General Knowledge as MCQs, with no negative marking. Qualifiers go on to the Stage-II viva voce, tentatively in April 2027, then medicals.

## FAQs

**What is the last date for RIMCEE 2026 late application?**
The late application window closes on 7 October 2026, with the fee transaction permitted until 11:50 PM. Submit the form itself well before the evening, since portal traffic is heaviest on the last day.

**What is the RIMCEE 2026 late fee?**
A late fee of Rs 500 applies on top of the examination fee during the 1–7 October window, so General, defence wards and OBC-NCL candidates pay Rs 1,500 in total and SC/ST candidates Rs 1,250.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 written exam?**
The Stage-I written examination is scheduled for 6 December 2026 (Sunday), exactly 60 days from 7 October. It is pen-and-paper based and will be held in 39 cities across India.

**Is there a correction window for RIMCEE 2026?**
Yes. NTA opens an application correction window from 9 to 11 October 2026, where you can edit details in the submitted form on the website only.
