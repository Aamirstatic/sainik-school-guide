## 20 days left — and Class 6 is where most families apply

The AISSEE 2027 application window closes on **2 December 2026 at 11:50 PM**. Today, 12 November, that leaves 20 days. The exam itself is on Sunday, **31 January 2027** — 80 days away.

Class 6 is the big entry point: it is the class most families target, it admits both boys and girls in every Sainik School, and its paper is shorter and more forgiving than the Class 9 paper. But the form still rejects applications every year over the same small mistakes — a wrong photo size, a date of birth that falls outside the age window, a domicile certificate uploaded in the wrong format. This checklist covers the five things to get right before 2 December.

## The Class 6 checklist

**1. Age — check it first, because there is no flexibility.** Your child must be 10 to 12 years old as on 31 March 2027, which means born between **1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017**, both days inclusive. The bulletin is explicit that there is no age relaxation for any category. Check the date of birth on the birth certificate, not your memory — and check that the certificate itself is ready to upload as a PDF of 50–300 KB.

**2. Know the paper you are signing up for.** Class 6 is a single paper of **150 minutes (2:00 to 4:30 PM)**, 125 questions, **300 marks**: Language 25 questions for 50 marks, Mathematics 50 questions for 150 marks, Intelligence 25 for 50, and General Knowledge 25 for 50. There is **no negative marking** — every question is worth attempting. Unlike Class 9, which is English only, Class 6 candidates can choose from 13 languages, including English, Hindi, Marathi, Odia, Bengali, Assamese, Punjabi, Tamil, Gujarati, Telugu, Kannada, Urdu and Malayalam. To qualify, a candidate needs 25% in each section and 40% in aggregate; SC/ST candidates are exempt from the minimum marks in the existing Sainik Schools.

**3. Fee and documents.** The fee is **Rs. 850** for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and **Rs. 700** for SC/ST candidates, payable online. Keep ready: a recent photograph (JPG, 10–200 KB, taken on or after 1 July 2026, white background, 80% face coverage), signature (JPG, 10–50 KB, running hand, black ink) and left-thumb impression (JPG, 10–50 KB, blue ink), plus the date-of-birth, domicile and category certificates as PDFs (50–300 KB). Defence wards need the service certificate or PPO.

**4. Four exam cities, in order.** The form asks you to pick four exam cities in order of preference. Choose with care — it is one of the hardest things to change later. The city intimation slip comes on **11 January 2027** and the admit card on **25 January 2027**.

**5. Girls can apply for Class 6 everywhere.** The bulletin confirms girls are eligible for Class 6 in all Sainik Schools, with 10% of Class 6 strength or 10 seats, whichever is higher, reserved for girl candidates across all categories.

Twenty days is enough for a Class 6 application — if this week is the week the documents get ready. The families who struggle are rarely the ones who started late. They are the ones who started on the last day.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027 Class 6?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. Applications are accepted only online at exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. A late window runs from 3 to 6 December with an extra fee of Rs. 500.

**What is the age limit for AISSEE 2027 Class 6?**

10 to 12 years as on 31 March 2027 — born between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017, both days inclusive. Girls are eligible for Class 6 in all Sainik Schools.

**What is the AISSEE Class 6 exam pattern?**

One paper of 150 minutes (2:00 to 4:30 PM), 125 questions for 300 marks: Language 25 questions, Mathematics 50, Intelligence 25 and General Knowledge 25. There is no negative marking. Class 6 candidates can choose from 13 languages, including English and Hindi.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs. 850 for General, OBC (NCL) and wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and Rs. 700 for SC/ST candidates. The fee is non-refundable and is paid online.
