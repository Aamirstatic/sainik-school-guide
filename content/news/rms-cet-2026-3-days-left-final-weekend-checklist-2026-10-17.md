Three days. That is all that stands between your child's RMS CET 2026 form and a closed portal. The regular application window for the Rashtriya Military Schools Common Entrance Test — admission to Classes 6 and 9 for the 2027–28 session — closes on **Tuesday, 20 October 2026** (CONFIRMED — Education Post, Career India, SarkariResult). This weekend is the real deadline; everything after it costs extra.

## The two cutoffs on 20 October (not one)

Most parents remember "20 October" as one date. There are actually two different timings, and the distinction has bitten applicants in previous cycles:

| What | Deadline | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form submission | 20 Oct, **5:00 PM** | CONFIRMED — Education Post, Career India |
| Fee payment | 20 Oct, **11:50 PM** | CONFIRMED — Education Post, Career India |

Your form is not complete until the money lands. A submitted-but-unpaid form at 11:51 PM is a rejected form. Submit by the evening of **Sunday, 19 October** at the latest, so any payment hiccup still has Monday to be sorted.

## What the late-fee window really costs

Missed 20 October? There is a safety net — a late application window from **21 to 25 October 2026**, with an additional **Rs 500 late fee** (CONFIRMED — Education Post, Times of India). The regular category fees are (CONFIRMED — Education Post):

- **General / OBC / OBC-NCL:** Rs 1,000
- **SC / ST / wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action:** Rs 750

Do not treat the late window as a second deadline to aim for. Portals slow down on last days, payment gateways fail, and you pay Rs 500 for the stress. Treat 19 October as your deadline and the late window as emergency-only.

## This weekend's 6-step checklist

1. **Check the age eligibility first.** Class 6: 10–13 years as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2014 – 31 March 2017). Class 9: 13–16 years as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2011 – 31 March 2014). Both ranges inclusive (CONFIRMED — Education Post, Times of India).
2. **Keep documents ready before you start.** Photograph, signature, birth certificate, caste/category certificate if applicable — pre-scanned in the prescribed formats so the form does not stall mid-session.
3. **Apply on the official portal only:** exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet (CONFIRMED — Education Post).
4. **Pay online** via credit/debit card, net banking or UPI, and **save the confirmation page** — screenshot and PDF both.
5. **Expect a correction window around 27–28 October** (EXPECTED — reported by admission trackers; exact editable fields will be in NTA's official correction notice). It is for small errors, not for changing your class or category.
6. **Start the December plan now.** The written exam is on **Sunday, 13 December 2026** — 57 days from today (CONFIRMED — Education Post, Career India). Pen-and-paper OMR mode; NTA runs the written stage, while interview and medical are handled by the schools afterwards.

## Why this deadline matters more than it looks

RMS CET is not a backup exam you squeeze in — it is admission to five of India's most established military residential schools: Chail, Ajmer, Dholpur, Belgaum and Bengaluru (CONFIRMED — Education Post). ## FAQs

**When does the RMS CET 2026 application window close?**
The regular window closes on Tuesday, 20 October 2026 — the form must be submitted by 5 PM and the fee payment must succeed by 11:50 PM the same day.

**Is there a late fee option for RMS CET 2026?**
Yes. A late application window runs 21–25 October 2026 with an additional Rs 500 late fee on top of the regular category fee.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 application fee?**
Rs 1,000 for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates; Rs 750 for SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 correction window?**
The correction window is expected on 27–28 October 2026, after the late window closes. Exact field lists will be in the official correction notice.
