## The deadline is real this time: 10 days left

The National Testing Agency opened the Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026 on 23 September, and the window closes on **20 October 2026** — ten days from today. Fee payment must go through by **11:50 pm on 20 October**. Miss that, and the only route left is the late-fee window: **21 to 25 October (up to 5 pm)**, with an extra **Rs 500 late fee**.

This is the last full weekend before the form closes. Servers get busy on the final two days — parents who waited for the notification to "settle" should treat this weekend as the deadline.

RMS CET 2026 decides admission to **Class 6 and Class 9** in the five Rashtriya Military Schools — Chail (Himachal Pradesh), Ajmer and Dholpur (Rajasthan), and Belgaum and Bengaluru (Karnataka). These are English-medium residential schools run by the Ministry of Defence, open to both defence wards and civilians. The written test is on **13 December 2026** — a pen-and-paper OMR test with multiple-choice questions, conducted across **63 cities**.

## The three things to get right this weekend

**1. The age windows are wider than AISSEE.** Class 6 candidates must be **10 to 13 years** as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2014 to 31 March 2017) — note that a 13-year-old who is too old for AISSEE Class 6 can still apply here. Class 9 candidates must be **13 to 16 years** as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2011 to 31 March 2014).

**2. Keep documents ready before you log in.** The form needs the usual set: birth certificate, a government photo ID, a recent photograph and signature, and the previous class marksheet. Class 6 applicants must have passed (or be studying in) Class 5; Class 9 applicants must have passed Class 8 from a government or recognised school. Class 9 applicants should also note that English is the medium of instruction in Rashtriya Military Schools.

**3. Pay the right fee once.** General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates pay **Rs 1,000**; SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action (KIA) pay **Rs 750**. Payments go through the online gateway on the NTA RMS portal — complete it the same day you fill the form, and save the confirmation page.

A brief correction window is EXPECTED after applications close (NTA ran one on 27–28 October in its announced calendar — see our [earlier coverage](/news/rms-cet-2026-correction-window-opens-october-27-2026-10-05/)), but it is meant for small fixes, not for finishing a half-filled form.

## Why RMS matters even if you're chasing AISSEE

For many families, RMS CET is the parallel track to AISSEE 2027: five schools, the same December exam season (RIMC on 6 December, RMS on 13 December, UP Sainik School on 20 December), and a defence-run residential education. If your child is preparing for AISSEE, the RMS paper uses the same OMR-MCQ muscle — preparing for both is not double work. ## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**

20 October 2026 without late fee (fee payment up to 11:50 pm). A late-fee window of Rs 500 runs from 21 to 25 October 2026 (up to 5 pm).

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**

13 December 2026 (Sunday). It is a pen-and-paper OMR test with multiple-choice questions, held across 63 exam cities.

**What is the application fee for RMS CET 2026?**

Rs 1,000 for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates; Rs 750 for SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action (KIA).

**What are the age limits for RMS CET 2026 Class 6 and Class 9?**

Class 6: 10 to 13 years as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2014 and 31 March 2017). Class 9: 13 to 16 years as on 31 March 2027 (born between 1 April 2011 and 31 March 2014).
