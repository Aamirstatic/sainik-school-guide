> **Last verified: 21 October 2026 (Wednesday).** ✅ CONFIRMED: no AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or registration link on NTA's official portal as of 18 October 2026 (Aglasem, updated 19 October); nothing was listed as of 16 October 2026 either (Collegedunia). ✅ CONFIRMED: the AISSEE 2026 notification was released on 10 October 2025. 🔶 EXPECTED: the 31 January 2027 exam date per NTA's calendar (reported by Collegedunia) — not yet in an official bulletin.

Twenty-one October, and the most-watched page in Indian school admissions still shows nothing. The National Testing Agency has not posted any AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or registration link on its official portal — exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society — for the 2027-28 admission cycle. That puts us **eleven days past** last year's release date: the AISSEE 2026 notification had come out on **10 October 2025**.

## Why this matters more than a calendar quirk

Last year's timeline is the reference parents should keep in hand:

| Event | AISSEE 2026 (actual) | AISSEE 2027 (status) |
|---|---|---|
| Notification / registration start | 10 October 2025 | ❌ Not out (as of 21 Oct 2026) |
| Original last date to apply | 30 October 2025 | To be announced |
| Extended last date | 9 November 2025 | To be announced |
| Exam date | 18 January 2026 | 🔶 31 January 2027 (expected) |
| Result declared | 27 February 2026 | To be announced |

The exam date, at least, is not a guess: **31 January 2027** is the date shown in NTA's published exam calendar for AISSEE 2027. From today that is **102 days away**. Every day the notification slips, the window between release and exam compresses — which means preparation has to carry on regardless of the form.

## Ignore the "form is out" claims — verify it yourself

Aglasem noted on 19 October that "some education websites have reported that the AISSEE 2027 form is already out" — and explicitly said this **could not be confirmed from NTA**. (We debunked a specific October-13-release claim on [18 October](/news/aissee-2027-notification-not-out-oct-13-claim-false-2026-10-18/).)

The verification takes thirty seconds and settles it every time:

1. Open **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society** — the only official portal.
2. Look for the **Information Bulletin** section. If the 2027 bulletin is not there, registration is not open — no matter what any third-party site says.
3. Do not apply or pay through any other website. NTA accepts AISSEE applications online only, on its own portal.

## What to prepare this week

The form typically opens with roughly a month to apply (last year: 10 October to 9 November after the extension). Keep these ready so nothing stalls you on day one:

- Birth certificate (proof of date of birth)
- Domicile certificate
- Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL) if claiming a reservation
- Recent passport-size photograph and scanned signature
- Last year's fee for budgeting: ₹850 (General/OBC-NCL/Defence), ₹700 (SC/ST) — the 2027 fee will be in the new bulletin

And study like the exam is on 31 January — because it almost certainly is.

## FAQs

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**

No. As of 18 October 2026, no AISSEE 2027 information bulletin or registration link was listed on NTA's official portal, and no notice has appeared since. The only official source is exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society.

**When was the AISSEE 2026 notification released?**

The AISSEE 2026 notification was released on 10 October 2025. The 2027 bulletin has not yet appeared, eleven days past that corresponding date.

**What is the expected exam date for AISSEE 2027?**

AISSEE 2027 is expected on 31 January 2027, as per NTA's exam calendar — this date is expected, not yet confirmed in an official bulletin.

**What should parents keep ready while waiting for the AISSEE 2027 form?**

Keep the birth certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), a recent photograph, and the signature scan ready, so the form can be filled the day the window opens.
