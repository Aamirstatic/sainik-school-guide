## What's happening

While AISSEE 2027 families wait for NTA's notification, one defence-school window is open right now and quietly closing: **RMS CET 2026**, the entrance test for the five Rashtriya Military Schools, shuts its regular application window on **20 October 2026 at 5 PM** — **17 days from today** (CONFIRMED — Times of India, Education Times, 24 September 2026).

The exam is on **13 December 2026 (Sunday)**, for admission to **Class 6 and Class 9** in the 2027-28 session. Applications have been open since 23 September on NTA's RMS CET portal (CONFIRMED — EdexLive, NDTV).

Seventeen days sounds comfortable until you remember the documents: birth certificate, domicile, caste certificate, photograph, signature. The families who wait until 19 October will find the NTA server is not their friend that night.

## Deadline rules (CONFIRMED)

| Event | Date | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Regular application closes | **20 Oct 2026, 5 PM** ✅ | Fee payment accepted till 11:50 PM the same day |
| Late application window | 21–25 Oct 2026, 5 PM ✅ | **Extra Rs 500 late fee** |
| Exam | 13 Dec 2026 (Sunday) ✅ | Pen-and-paper OMR, MCQs, 63 cities |
| Fee | Rs 1,000 Gen/OBC; Rs 750 SC/ST/wards of KIA ✅ | As notified by NTA |

The late window exists, but it is a penalty you pay for procrastination, not a backup plan. Rs 500 on top of the exam fee, for nothing. Apply in the regular window.

## Who can apply

- **Class 6:** **10 to 13 years as on 31 March 2027** — born between 1 April 2014 and 31 March 2017 (both dates inclusive). The child must have passed Class 5, or be studying in Class 5 and pass it before admission (CONFIRMED — TOI, Jagran Josh).
- **Class 9:** **13 to 16 years as on 31 March 2027** — born between 1 April 2011 and 31 March 2014 (both dates inclusive). The child must have passed Class 8 from a recognised school. English is the medium of instruction, so working knowledge of English is expected (CONFIRMED — NDTV).

## What to do this week

1. **Check age eligibility first.** Open your child's birth certificate and match it against the ranges above — five minutes, and it eliminates the most common rejection reason.
2. **Gather the documents now.** Photograph, signature, birth certificate, domicile, caste certificate if claiming reservation. Scan them this weekend — do not discover a missing paper on 19 October.
3. **Apply by 15 October, not 20 October.** Portals slow down near deadlines. Finish early, then use the extra days for preparation, not paperwork.
4. **Pick the nearest exam city while registering.** The test runs in 63 cities across India (CONFIRMED — EdexLive) — choose now rather than hoping for a change later.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for RMS CET 2026?**
20 October 2026, up to 5 PM for the application form; fee payment is accepted until 11:50 PM the same day. A late window runs 21–25 October 2026 with a Rs 500 late fee.

**When is the RMS CET 2026 exam?**
13 December 2026 (Sunday), pen-and-paper OMR mode with multiple-choice questions, across 63 cities in India.

**What is the RMS CET 2026 application fee?**
Rs 1,000 for General, OBC and OBC-NCL candidates; Rs 750 for SC, ST and wards of Armed Forces personnel killed in action. Applications from 21–25 October carry an additional Rs 500 late fee.

**What is the age limit for RMS CET 2026 Class 6 and Class 9?**
Class 6: 10 to 13 years as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2014 to 31 March 2017). Class 9: 13 to 16 years as on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2011 to 31 March 2014).
