# RIMCEE 2026: NTA Registration Closes at 5 PM Today — Last-Hour Checklist

> **Last verified: 30 September 2026 (2:15 PM IST).** The 5 PM deadline today and the 1–7 October late-fee window are ✅ **CONFIRMED** across the Times of India, Shiksha, Education Times and the Daily Jagran. The correction window (9–11 October) below is reported from the official NTA bulletin via Education Post — verify on the NTA RIMCEE portal when it opens.

If your child is aiming for Class 8 at Rashtriya Indian Military College, Dehradun, this afternoon is the moment of truth. The National Testing Agency closes the **RIMCEE 2026** online application window **today, 30 September, at 5 PM** — for admission to the July 2027 term. Reading this at lunch break? You still have a few hours. Use them well.

This is the first RIMC cycle running fully through NTA's online system — no hard-copy form to post, everything on the portal. That is convenient, but it also means the 5 PM cutoff is enforced by a server, not a post office. When the window shuts, it shuts.

![Alarm clock next to study materials signalling the RIMCEE 2026 deadline](/images/inbody/rimcee-2026-apply-by-5pm-today-2026-09-30.webp)

## Your last-hour checklist (before 5 PM)

- [ ] **Confirm eligibility first.** Date of birth between **2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015** (both dates inclusive), and the child must be studying in — or have passed — **Class VII** from a recognised school. ✅ CONFIRMED via Shiksha and the Times of India.
- [ ] **Apply on the official portal only**: `exams.nta.nic.in/RIMCEE` (the NTA examination portal). Shiksha also lists `rimc.edu.in` as the application route. Do not trust lookalike links forwarded on WhatsApp.
- [ ] **Keep scanned documents ready** before you begin — photograph, signature, birth proof and category certificate in the formats the portal specifies. A rejected upload at 4:55 PM is a nightmare you can avoid at 2:30 PM.
- [ ] **Pay the fee today.** Outlets differ on the exact payment cutoff (one reports 11:50 PM, another says 5 PM), so the safe move is simple: submit the form *and* complete the payment before 5 PM. Save the confirmation page and the application number.
- [ ] **Expect a slow portal.** Last-day traffic is real on NTA portals. If the page hangs, wait and retry rather than refreshing aggressively or starting a second application.

## Missed 5 PM? The late-fee window opens tomorrow

Do not panic — NTA has built in a safety net. A **late-fee window runs from 1 to 7 October 2026 (till 5 PM)**, with an additional fee of **₹500** over the regular application fee. ✅ CONFIRMED across Shiksha, Education Times and the Daily Jagran.

Treat it as a fallback, not a plan. Apply in this window only if today genuinely slipped away — and still aim for 1 or 2 October, not the 7th.

The written paper carries **descriptive questions in Mathematics and English** plus **MCQs in General Knowledge** — a pattern that rewards clear, practised writing, not just ticking boxes. Qualifiers move to the viva voce stage next year. Our [after-the-deadline timeline](/news/rimcee-2026-after-deadline-what-happens-next-2026-09-28/) walks through each of these stages in detail.

One honest note for parents juggling multiple exams: the RIMC written test lands on **6 December**, exactly one week before the RMS CET (13 December) and under two months before AISSEE 2027 (31 January). If your child is attempting more than one, [October's four-week plan](/news/october-4-week-aissee-2027-game-plan-2026-09-29/) is worth pinning up tonight.

## FAQs

**What time does RIMCEE 2026 registration close today?**

Today, 30 September 2026, at 5 PM — confirmed by the Times of India, Shiksha and Education Times. Submit the form and complete the fee payment before 5 PM to be safe.

**What if I miss the 30 September RIMCEE deadline?**

You get a second chance: NTA opens a late-fee window from 1 to 7 October 2026 (till 5 PM) with an additional fee of ₹500. Do not plan for it — apply today if you can.

**Who is eligible for the RIMCEE December 2026 exam?**

Children born between 2 July 2013 and 1 January 2015 who are studying in (or have passed) Class VII from a recognised school. The exam is for Class 8 admission to RIMC Dehradun for the July 2027 term.

**When is the RIMCEE 2026 written exam and what is the pattern?**

6 December 2026 (Sunday), pen-and-paper mode across 39 cities. The paper has descriptive questions in Mathematics and English plus multiple-choice questions in General Knowledge.
