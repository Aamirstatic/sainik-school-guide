## Five days on the calendar, one day that matters

The AISSEE 2027 form closes on **Wednesday, 2 December 2026, at 11:50 PM**. Count it out and you get five days. But here is the thing — five days on the calendar is not five days of working time. Saturday and Sunday are the weekend, and Wednesday is the last day, when every parent who waited will be on the portal at the same time.

That is why our day-by-day plan, published on 25 November, set **today, Friday 27 November**, as the real deadline. Not because the rules changed — they have not — but because a form submitted today leaves you four calm days to fix anything that goes wrong. A form submitted on 2 December leaves you nothing.

If your child's form is ready — documents gathered, photo and signature scanned in the sizes the bulletin asks for, school choices decided — submit it today. It takes about twenty minutes. Then get back to the preparation, because the exam is 65 days away and that is where the real work is.

## What breaks on the last day

We have watched this cycle play out every year, and the failures are always the same three:

**The payment fails.** A UPI transaction times out, a card is declined, the portal shows "payment pending". On 27 November, you retry in an hour and it goes through. On 2 December at 11 PM, you are refreshing a page and praying.

**The photo gets rejected.** Wrong background, wrong size, a scan that is too dark. The portal tells you at the upload step, and you need a fresh scan. That is a ten-minute job on a Friday afternoon and a crisis on a Wednesday night.

**The portal slows down.** Thousands of parents submit on the last day. Pages time out, sessions expire, half-filled forms vanish. NTA has never extended an AISSEE deadline because the website was slow, and there is no reason to expect it to start now.

None of this is a warning from NTA. It is our honest reading of how the last days of every application window actually go — and it is the reason yesterday's piece spelled out [what the ₹500 late fee really costs you](/news/aissee-2027-6-days-left-late-fee-window-risks-2026-11-26/). The money is the smallest part. The lost weekend is the real price.

The fee is **₹850** for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and **₹700** for SC/ST candidates. In the late window, add ₹500 — so ₹1,350 or ₹1,200. Apply only on the official portal, **exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society**. NTA says it has no agents, and anyone offering to "fill the form for you" for a fee is not doing you a favour.

## After you submit today

Do not just close the browser. Download and save the **confirmation page** — it carries the application number you will need for everything that follows. Check four things on it before you file it away: the application number, the child's name spelling, the date of birth, and the school choices in the order you meant them. Our [five-point confirmation-page check](/news/aissee-2027-form-submitted-5-things-to-check-confirmation-page-2026-11-17/) walks through each one.

If something is wrong, do not panic — the correction window runs from **7 to 9 December**, and it exists precisely for this. But note what we explained on 18 November: the window lets you correct permitted particulars; it is not a fresh application, and it cannot fix everything. The safest correction is the one you never need.

Then the weekend is yours. Use it the way our [December study plan](/news/aissee-2027-66-days-to-exam-december-study-plan-2026-11-26/) suggests: one full 150-minute paper on Sunday morning, phone in another room, and an honest look at which section cost the most marks. The form is admin. The paper is the exam.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

Wednesday, 2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM. Both the form and the fee payment must be completed by then. We recommend submitting by today, Friday 27 November, and keeping the weekend as a buffer.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

₹850 for General, OBC-NCL and defence wards, and ₹700 for SC and ST candidates. In the late window from 3 to 6 December, add ₹500 — so ₹1,350 or ₹1,200.

**Can I still apply after 2 December 2026?**

Yes, in the late-fee window from 3 to 6 December 2026, by paying an extra ₹500 over the regular fee. The last date for fee payment with the late fee is 6 December at 11:50 PM.

**What should I check on the AISSEE confirmation page after submitting?**

The application number, the child's name spelling, date of birth, category, and the school choices in order. Download and save the confirmation page — it is your proof of submission and the reference for the correction window from 7 to 9 December.
