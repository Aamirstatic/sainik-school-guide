## The window is open — and so is the window for mistakes

The National Testing Agency opened AISSEE 2027 applications on 3 November 2026 for Class 6 and Class 9 admission to Sainik Schools and New Sainik Schools. The form stays open until 2 December, up to 11:50 PM. That sounds like plenty of time. It isn't, if you discover a problem on the last evening.

Here's the thing — most rejected or stuck forms are not rejected for lack of merit. They stall on small, boring details: a photograph that doesn't meet the specification, a name spelt differently from the school record, a fee payment that never completed. Every one of those is avoidable if you slow down for ten minutes before hitting submit.

## The 7 mistakes to check before you submit

**1. A photograph, signature or thumb impression that doesn't match the specification.** The application asks for scanned copies of the photograph, signature and thumb impression. Check the size, format and background rules in the Information Bulletin before uploading — a blurry phone photo cropped in a hurry is the classic first-week failure.

**2. A name or date of birth that differs from the school record.** The form asks for personal and academic details — Class 5 details for Class 6 admission, Class 8 for Class 9. Type the name exactly as in the school record, and check the date of birth against the birth certificate, not from memory. Age limits are strict: Class 6 applicants must be 10–12 on 31 March 2027 (born 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2017) and Class 9 applicants 13–15 (born 1 April 2012 – 31 March 2014).

**3. Skipping the category or domicile certificate.** Candidates in specific categories must submit the applicable category or domicile certificates wherever required. If your child claims a category benefit, keep that certificate ready before you start — hunting for it after submission is how deadlines get missed.

**4. Rushing the school and exam-centre choices.** The form asks for your preferred schools and examination centres. Don't pick the first name on the list — discuss the choices as a family first, because changing them later depends on what the correction window allows, and that list of editable fields is not yet confirmed in the reports we checked.

**5. Paying the fee but not downloading the confirmation page.** The fee is Rs 850 for General, OBC and Defence category candidates and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates, payable by debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI. After payment, download and keep the confirmation page and fee receipt. A payment that "went through" but produced no confirmation page is not a completed application — check it the same day, while there is still time to fix it.

**6. Leaving it to 2 December.** Portals slow down on deadline days, and a failed payment at 11:40 PM cannot be rescued after the 11:50 PM cut-off. The late window (3–6 December, extra Rs 500) is a safety net, not a plan — and it costs you money for a delay you could have avoided.

**7. Trusting anyone other than the official portal.** NTA takes applications only through exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society. Anyone offering to fill the form for a fee, promising a school of choice, or sharing a "direct link" outside the portal is not helping you — they are the risk.

## Your pre-submit checklist

- [ ] Photograph, signature and thumb impression match the bulletin specifications
- [ ] Name and date of birth match the school record and birth certificate
- [ ] Category or domicile certificate ready, if applicable
- [ ] School preferences and exam centres chosen deliberately, not by default
- [ ] Fee paid (Rs 850 or Rs 700) and confirmation page downloaded and saved
- [ ] Form submitted well before 2 December, 11:50 PM

One last point: the correction window runs 7–9 December. Use it if you must, but treat it as a last resort. The cleanest form is the one you checked properly the first time.

## FAQs

**What is the last date to apply for AISSEE 2027?**

2 December 2026, up to 11:50 PM, including the fee payment. A late window with an extra fee of Rs 500 runs from 3 to 6 December 2026.

**What documents do I need to upload for the AISSEE 2027 form?**

As per the NTA application process reported by the Times of India, you need a scanned photograph, signature and thumb impression, plus category or domicile certificates wherever applicable.

**What is the AISSEE 2027 application fee?**

Rs 850 for General, OBC and Defence category candidates and Rs 700 for SC and ST candidates, payable online by debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI.

**Can I correct my AISSEE 2027 form after submitting it?**

Yes, NTA provides a correction window from 7 to 9 December 2026 for permitted changes. Do not rely on it for mistakes you can avoid now — some fields may not be editable.
