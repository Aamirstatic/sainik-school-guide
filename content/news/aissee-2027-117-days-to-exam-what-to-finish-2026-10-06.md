> **Last verified: 6 October 2026.** This is a milestone explainer, not breaking news. The AISSEE 2027 notification is still not out — but the countdown to the expected exam date (31 January 2027) is real, and what you finish this month decides February's outcome.

One hundred and seventeen days. That is how far the AISSEE 2027 exam sits from this morning — close enough to feel urgent, far enough to still change everything. The NTA's exam calendar of 16 September 2026 lists **31 January 2027** as the date (CONFIRMED as tentative — the NTA itself flags it as subject to confirmation), and while the notification hasn't appeared yet, the syllabus won't change. Parents who wait for the bulletin to begin serious prep are, every single year, the ones scrambling in January.

So here is the honest question: by 31 January, will your child's preparation be *finished*, or still *in progress*? What you do between now and mid-December decides the answer.

![Student writing answers at a school exam desk](/images/inbody/aissee-2027-117-days-to-exam-what-to-finish-2026-10-06.webp)

## October: finish the syllabus (days 117–87)

This is the last genuine learning month. Every topic your child hasn't touched yet needs its first honest attempt by the end of October. For Class 6, that means the four sections carrying the full 300 marks: Maths (50 questions, 150 marks), Language, Intelligence and General Knowledge (25 questions, 50 marks each) — all EXPECTED to repeat the 2026 pattern. For Class 9: Maths at 200 marks, plus English, Intelligence, General Science and Social Science at 50 each, English-only paper, 180 minutes.

The qualifier rule that trips up aspirants: **25% in every section and 40% aggregate** (not applicable to SC/ST) — straight from the 2026 bulletin. A child scoring brilliantly in Maths but ignoring GK is not "almost through"; they are out. October's job is to leave no section untouched.

## November: mock-test season (days 86–56)

Once the notification drops — expected this month — the application takes an hour; preparation shouldn't change for it. November belongs to full-length OMR mocks under timed conditions. Two per week minimum. Track section-wise scores against the 25% qualifying line, not just the total.

## December: revision and weak-point surgery (days 55–24)

By mid-December, new learning stops. December is for re-working every question your child got wrong in mocks, drilling OMR speed (Class 6 gets 150 minutes for 125 questions — barely 72 seconds per question), and locking exam-day routine: sleep, breakfast, reaching the centre early.

## January: taper and confidence (days 23–0)

The last three weeks are about staying sharp, not cramming. Light revision, one mock a week, and trusting the base built since October. Admit cards are EXPECTED mid-January based on the last cycle — when they arrive, verify the exam city and reporting time immediately.

## What the notification watch adds

Yes, the bulletin is still awaited — [we tracked the October pattern yesterday](/news/aissee-2027-notification-october-pattern-what-parents-should-prepare-2026-10-05/). But treat the notification as an administrative event, not a preparation event. The only things it will confirm are dates, fees (EXPECTED ₹850/₹700 based on last year), and eligibility — not the syllabus your child should already be finishing. And stay alert while waiting: [fake websites and guaranteed-seat scams peak exactly in this window](/news/aissee-2027-fake-websites-guaranteed-seat-scams-alert-2026-10-05/).

## Your October checklist

- [ ] Syllabus 100% covered at least once by 31 October — no untouched chapters
- [ ] Weak sections identified with mock scores, not feelings
- [ ] OMR practice started (bubbling technique, not just solving)
- [ ] Documents folder ready for the form (birth certificate, domicile, studying certificate)
- [ ] Official portal bookmarked; no reliance on forwards

117 days is not a lot. But it is enough — if October is used properly.

## FAQs

**How many days are left for the AISSEE 2027 exam?**

As of 6 October 2026, 117 days remain until 31 January 2027, the exam date tentatively given in the NTA exam calendar of 16 September 2026. The NTA itself marks the date as tentative, so final confirmation waits on the official notification.

**Has the AISSEE 2027 notification been released?**

No. As of 6 October 2026 the notification is still awaited, with registration expected in October 2026 based on the previous cycle. Do not trust claims that the form is live — check only the official NTA AISSEE portal.

**What should a Class 6 aspirant finish by December 2026?**

Complete the full syllabus — Maths, Language, Intelligence, GK — by mid-December, then move entirely to mocks and revision. The 2026 pattern suggests 150 minutes, 125 questions, 300 marks, no negative marking, with 25% per section and 40% aggregate needed to qualify.

**Can preparation start seriously before the notification is out?**

Yes — and it should. The syllabus, pattern and qualifying marks come from the established AISSEE framework; only the application window is moving. Waiting for the bulletin to start studying is the most common preparation mistake.
