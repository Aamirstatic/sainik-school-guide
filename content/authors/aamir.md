# About Aamir Raza

Aamir Raza is the founder and SEO strategist behind Sainik School Guide India. With over 8 years of experience in search engine optimization and digital marketing, he built this platform to bridge the information gap that exists around Sainik School admissions in India.

## Background

Aamir specializes in education-sector SEO, helping students and parents find accurate, actionable information online. He noticed that most Sainik School content on the internet was either outdated, incomplete, or buried under coaching center advertisements. That realization led him to create a comprehensive, free resource that covers everything from AISSEE preparation to NDA career paths.

His expertise lies in understanding what parents and students actually search for, and creating content that directly answers those questions. He applies the latest SEO strategies — including Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and Search Experience Optimization (SXO) — to ensure the right information reaches the right people at the right time.

## What He Covers

**SEO & Content Strategy** — Aamir oversees the content strategy for Sainik School Guide India, ensuring every article is optimized for search engines while remaining genuinely useful for readers. He writes about AISSEE admissions, school comparisons, fee structures, and scholarship opportunities.

**Sainik School Admissions** — He provides data-driven insights into the admission process, covering eligibility, documents, timelines, and common mistakes. He tracks changes across all 33+ Sainik Schools and 69 new PPP model schools.

**NDA and Defense Careers** — His articles cover the complete pathway from Class 6 admission to NDA selection and commissioned officer careers, backed by real data from official sources.

## Approach

Aamir's writing is data-driven, practical, and SEO-optimized. Every claim is backed by official sources — NTA notifications, Sainik Schools Society data, and verified statistics. He believes that good SEO and good content are not opposites; the best content is both search-friendly and genuinely helpful.

He sources his information from official NTA websites, Sainik Schools Society notifications, Ministry of Defence publications, and direct conversations with school administrators and alumni.
