# About Rifaul Hasan

Rifaul Hasan is the **Principal of Jai Govind Public School (JGPS)**, a position he has held with distinction for over 25 years. With a quarter-century of hands-on experience in school administration, student development, and academic leadership, he brings unparalleled practical knowledge to Sainik School Guide India.

## Background

As the head of JGPS, Rifaul has directly overseen the education and development of thousands of students. His 25+ years in education have given him deep insight into what works and what does not in preparing young students for competitive entrance exams like AISSEE, as well as the challenges of residential school life.

His expertise is not theoretical — it comes from decades of working directly with students, parents, and teachers. He has seen firsthand how the right guidance at the right time can transform a student's trajectory, and how common mistakes during the admission process can cost deserving students their seats.

## What He Covers

**Student Development & Counseling** — Rifaul writes about the practical aspects of student life in Sainik Schools — daily routines, hostel life, academic pressure, homesickness, and personal development. His advice is grounded in real experience, not textbook theory.

**Parent Guidance** — Having interacted with thousands of parents over 25 years, Rifaul understands their concerns, fears, and aspirations. He writes about how parents can support their children through the Sainik School admission process and beyond.

**Academic Excellence** — His articles cover study strategies, exam preparation, time management, and building sustainable academic habits. He draws from his experience leading JGPS to provide actionable advice.

**Sainik School Comparisons** — With his deep knowledge of the education landscape, Rifaul provides honest, data-driven comparisons between Sainik Schools, RIMC, Rashtriya Military Schools, and other defense-preparatory institutions.

## Approach

Rifaul writes with the authority of someone who has spent 25+ years in education. His articles are practical, empathetic, and direct. He does not sugarcoat challenges — if a particular aspect of Sainik School life is difficult, he says so, and then provides strategies to handle it.

His unique perspective as a school principal allows him to see the bigger picture — how admissions, academics, student welfare, and career preparation all fit together. This holistic view is what makes his articles particularly valuable for parents making this important decision.
